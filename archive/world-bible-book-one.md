## `02-World-Bible/magic.md` — as it stood

## MAGIC (system canon — public and page-facing layers; deep layer in 00)

### The model
- Six elements — FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIGHTNING, NATURE — the known world's MODEL of magic, not necessarily its truth; origin and nature stay a world-level mystery (00). The mother's way is pedagogy beneath the elements — perception-first, threads, structure, cost — deniably closer to the deeper grammar.
- The Temple teaches a particulate theory: magic moves through THE MINIMA — the smallest grains of the world, present in all matter ("as a struck string wakes its silent neighbor" — Priscian's delivery). "The minima" is a COMMON word. The Temple's science is old, learned, honest — and incomplete (00; the "smallest grains" claim is its honest error — Kael shelves it untested, Ch. 10). Neris may use the word from Ch. 10 on; she never handed it to Kael in Part I — vocabulary withheld is the rationing ("the word was not hers," Ch. 8).
- THE LIE (public doctrine): only select bloodlines can be Resonants. False — an old imperial lie preserving the royal-noble power dynamic (00).
- Enforcement — the pedagogy monopoly: attunement stays dormant untaught; flickers are dismissed as luck or superstition. The empire does not suppress commoner Resonants — it is simply the only teacher. Anomalies absorb as *noble blood somewhere in the line*; the lie self-seals.

### Structure
- Proficiency: every Resonant works all elements at varying talent and is extremely more proficient in ONE (proficiency = far more capacity there); most work almost nothing outside it. Rare: two or three proficiencies. Kael's many-element small-scale creativity is a future weapon because the world does not fight that way.
- Capacity vs. control: separate axes — raw output vs. precision, threading, creativity (mechanics in 00).
- Declaration: the dominant element settles with maturity and training (soft window ~12–14; "when it settles it settles"). Early magic is small and formless for most, but high capacity TINTS early (Aurelian's wax-sheet fire, Ch. 9) — capacity-worship's pageantry is built on this.
- Drain (public; taught Ch. 9 in Ruthen's cost register — spending, the purse, a grain): the Art exhausts and is never infinite; deep drain is painful; drain near the bottom whitens the hair PERMANENTLY ("witch-white" in folk talk; "The body kept the receipt"); full drain kills, every time, however beloved the house. An old Resonant's hair is a readable ledger. **THE MARK'S REGISTER IS PURE QUARTZ WHITE** (author, s33 — the page's image for Ruthen's seam, *like quartz through rock*). **The Vessantine silver is NOT a mark** — hereditary house colouring (00): silver-fair is blood, quartz-white is a receipt; folk talk garbles the two and the empire lets it. The two body-marks in their clean registers: white hair = taught science by day; red eyes = fireside legend by dark; village talk garbles both.

### Laws on the page
- THE DISTANCE LAW — the proximity law's page face (Ch. 11; mechanism 00 §THE PROXIMITY LAW): the Art is spent FROM the body — anywhere on it; hands are the habit. Distance and disconnection raise the cost; remote manifestation is an advanced mark. A continuous medium counts as connection (Kael's boot-thread through the ground).
- **THE THREADING (author, s25).** NOT one resonant thread laid into a blade: **MANY** lightning threads held in tight parallel, **accelerated and CONTAINED**, run the length of the steel and kept there under load. The difficulty is holding the whole bundle exact through impact, out of a purse that will not fill a cup. **Page-facing:** *"spend a thread"* stays the general idiom for one act of spending, in narration and every mouth; the page renders the OBSERVED thing — *a thread*, *the stitch*, *a stitch of white down its length* (Ch. 14) — and the bundle is never enumerated, counted or explained. **THE ROUTE (Ch. 21, Vask):** shoulder → arm → hand → steel, fast and tight to the body the whole way with nothing coming off it — hence total awareness of the body at once, and why a man who has lost track of his own elbow cannot hold it. **Vask's pedagogy:** steel down, thread with bare hands, take up the blades only once both hands hold and the feet are known — by then it runs out into the steel because there is nowhere else for it to go. **WHAT IT DOES, AND WHAT IT IS NOT (author, s37).** The threading **augments the cutting power of whatever it coats** — steel or the bare hand — and **cuts workings**. It is **NOT an electrical effect on a body**: it does not shock, convulse or seize a man, and no page may render it that way. **IT IS VISIBLE** — white, running the length of the medium (*a stitch of white down its length*, Ch. 14; the whole yard saw the public flash) — so a page that hides it is wrong; the thing Kael controls is whether he holds it or lets it go at the knock. **Consequences:** the BURST is proportionally worse (the whole bundle comes home when exactness fails); holding **two** threadings is a categorically larger problem than one, not a doubled one. **CAPACITY IS NOT THE EXPLANATION FOR THE GAP (author, s25 — do not write it as one):** Vask is a program-forged elite and far better than Kael at BOTH axes; his capacity means only that his errors would cost nothing, and he does not make them. Never stage an adult master's superiority over Kael as raw purse alone; Kael's control is exceptional *for a boy of thirteen in this world*, never in absolute terms.
- THE BURST (Ch. 13 — lightning-threading failure mode): a thread that loses exactness under load (impact, shock, divided attention) does not die — it discharges INTO ITS SOURCE. Margin is a function of capacity: a large purse absorbs the slop; a small purse pays in its own flesh; burn scales with thread size. The speckle-burns are ORDINARY burns — never a magical body-mark. Page canon, Vask: "I can be wrong by a finger's width. The purse forgives me. Nothing forgives you."
- INTERNAL REINFORCEMENT (Ch. 14 — advanced): resonance spent INWARD into the Resonant's own body — nothing leaves it; limbs strengthened and hardened, weight, force and speed multiplied (a flagstone split under one step). Capacity-hungry. **NOT A VEL SECRET (author, s24):** a KNOWN earth technique, available to any earth Resonant with the capacity and schooling; what the Vel own is the DEGREE — eleven generations of breeding and drilling — and that gap is real and not closeable by cleverness. The bloodline fence is intact (motion still from muscle). Page render observational only ("Whatever she had spent, she had spent it inward"); no doctrine delivered. **TWO SCALES, DO NOT CONFLATE (author, s37).** HERS is a whole limb or a whole body — capacity-hungry, and what lets a thirteen-year-old girl throw grown men about. **KAEL'S IS A LINE**: he cannot buy an arm, so he buys the exact line the force folds along, about two fingers wide, and **the constraint is AREA, not cost** — at that size it is cheap and repeatable, and it does NOT empty him (the s24 *empties him every time / not twice in a day* clause is RETIRED). The untrained flood a region; the technician takes the one point (why is in 00). Never write his version as a weaker flood; it is a different move at a different scale.
- **THE MEDIUM LAW — METAL TAKES THE THREAD (Ch. 15):** lightning enters METAL far more readily than wood. Wood must be persuaded and holds a thread only under the Resonant's own exactness; steel accepts one unasked and carries it WHOLE, hilt to point, no thinner for spreading. Gift and cost are one property: THE BURST through steel arrives everywhere at once and burns proportionally worse — "the same size thread in a better road." Page canon, Vask: "Wood forgives you. It bounces, it bends, and it lies to you about what you did. This does not lie." Deep-layer consistency: substances differ in minima-nature, readable by feel (00). Banked: the endgame kit's lightning-through-steel is an AFFINITY DISCOVERED, not a difficulty overcome.
- **THE EVOLVED THREADING — THE INWARD ART IN LIGHTNING (author, s39; SEALED MECHANISM, page-facing observation only).** Vask's whole-body form is not more threading; it is **the threading combined with the inward Art** — the same move as internal reinforcement (Valeria's earth, hardening and speeding a limb), run in LIGHTNING through an entire body at once. **The mechanism is in 00 (bible-only, never on any page of any book until the world can hold it). The toll is enormous** and scales with how long it is held. **THE REGISTER FENCE IS ABSOLUTE:** this world has no physiology for it, so no page may reach for impulse, nerve, muscle-signal or any term implying an apparatus this world has not built (`curriculum.md` §Register rule — the one permanent ban). **WHAT THE PAGE GETS (Ch. 30, and it is Kael's whole reading):** the white in the whole of him, held, *not coming off*; *It was not going along him. It was going into him*; the same shape as a girl's Art spent inward, in the wrong element, in a whole body at once; and the speed rendered as ABSENCE — *Not blurred. Not quick. Absent* — *a third thing, made out of two he already knew, and there was no name anywhere in him to put on it.* **CONTROL, NOT PURSE** (s25 fence): never stage it as a large reserve. Ch. 30 gives Kael half of it, and he wants the rest (where that goes is in 00).
- **CONTACT-SPENT FIRE (Ch. 30 — the coalition general).** Fire spent at the POINT and MOMENT of contact — off the knee, the heel, the knuckles — a burst at the instant of every blow, *the way sparks come off a wheel*, and the instant is the only warning there is. Legal under the proximity law (the Art is spent from the body, hands are only the habit) and the coalition's answer to the empire's one-handed grammar: nothing is reserved for the Art because everything is the Art. **Not a named school on any page**; nobody theorises it.
- **A THREADING CUTS A WORKING (Ch. 26; author, s30).** A blade carrying the threading, driven through a fire working, **divides it** — not extinguished, not absorbed: *it did not go out. It broke* — split along the line the steel took, the halves closing on empty air behind the man who went through them. **Scope, held narrow:** the threading (the contained bundle, never a single thread), through a medium that carries it whole, against a working being *paid for continuously* rather than one that has landed. **THE HAND IS ALSO A MEDIUM:** blades sheathed, the threading runs shoulder → arm → hand and stops there, *because there was no blade on the end of it any more*, and the fire comes apart on the hand as on the blade. **The page NEVER explains why it works** (06: *the causal chain stops at the first why*); nobody theorises it; Priscian must not be handed it. **The cost is total:** this AND internal line-reinforcement in the same instant (Ch. 26's *two at once*) empties Kael of every grain in about a fifth of a second, and it must never become cheap.
- **MENDING OUT OF PROFICIENCY, AND THE FIRST WHITENING ON THE PAGE (Ch. 32).** Neris is an AIR Resonant and mends her son's broken ribs with nature anyway. It works because she carries the other half in the same head — the anatomy, the herb-lore, the butchery table — and *the Art does what you understand*; it works BADLY because the element is not hers, and **the difference is rendered as contrast, not explained**: the First Master's mending two mornings earlier showed nothing, and hers shows everything. **She goes deep enough to take the drain-mark, and the manuscript's first whitening happens as an EVENT rather than as an established feature** — pure quartz white, out of the roots, watched by a boy lying under her hands who was taught the cost doctrine in a Temple yard and can price it to the grain. Nobody on the page says what it means.
- **NATURE IS THE ART OF MENDING (Ch. 19):** the healing Art — most of what a healer does badly with a hot iron and a needle, a skilled artist does properly. **The Art alone is worth very little:** a Resonant who does not know which way the bone goes will push it the wrong way; healing needs the Art AND the medical knowledge in one head, and very few carry both. Severin does (03/00). Unremarked rhyme: *the Art does what you understand* (Ch. 10).
- **VALERIA — EARTH BOTH WAYS (Ch. 16/17).** Super-advanced in earth spent OUTWARD as well as inward; internal reinforcement is her SPECIALTY — degree, not possession. Ch. 14/15's "nothing left her body" is Kael's observation of one duel, not a law about her. **FENCE (hard):** the threading is a LIGHTNING technique and is not hers to have — she has taken it on the arm a dozen times and watched for it slower, and none of that is a road to doing it — seeing a technique is not acquiring one; proficiency is where the capacity lives. Do not let her acquire it, here or later — the same rule as AIR, below.
- **THE GROUND-SENSE (Ch. 16 — the Vel school, taught not gifted):** a reading, never a projection. Connected to the stone she stands on, she reads what it carries — weight, gait, position. Nothing leaves her body; the bloodline fence is untouched (perception only). **FENCES (hard):** SHORT RANGE · INTENTIONAL ONLY, never passive · EXPENSIVE — paid in slower guards and lost meals. A fighting instrument, not surveillance: Kael watched her fifteen mornings from the treeline unfound and was caught only when he came inside it while she was drilling.
- **THE BLIND SPOT (Ch. 16 — Kael's counter, UNANSWERED):** the sense depends on continuity, so the counter breaks it — a thread of earth into the stone as his weight lands — not to move it, only to trouble the grain under the boot — so the step dies where it stands. Costly to the point of collapse (six threads in six seconds out of the smallest purse on the island), good for a few strides. She diagnosed it in four days and could not answer it: the floor can only report what the floor is doing. Kael's first win off her; it stands through the autumn.
- **AIR — THE SECOND SENSE (unbuilt — do not build it without a session):** the answer to the blind spot is a medium that is not the floor — air touches everything, and a moving body leaves a shape in it. Both senses together would make her a far better reader than either alone. Valeria named it and cannot have it — "years and a school that does not exist and an element that is not mine." Not on any page beyond her naming it; do not let her acquire it casually.
- **OPEN — THE BIND (Kael's untested hypothesis, Ch. 15):** if metal is a road rather than a container, the road need not stop where his blade stops; in a bind, steel touches steel, and a continuous medium is a connection. Written in the blank book, never attempted. NOT canon until spent on the page.
- **THE LAZY PLACE AT MOUNTAIN SCALE (Ch. 33 — Severin).** Nothing gets lifted. He reaches along the ground into the one place under an entire flank where all of it is already leaning, and **takes it away** — four hundred feet of terrace goes down with everything standing on it, and it opens the only road out. **This does not contradict Ch. 27's *he could hold one. He could not hold two*: HOLDING is the expensive direction and RELEASING is the cheap one**, and nobody on the page says so. It costs him his body rather than his hair — **no new white can show on a man already grey all through, and the never-confirm rule on his grey is untouched and unremarked.** *Everything heavy is lazy* is a literal magical law (00) and this is its largest page.

Consistent with Ch. 26's hand-as-medium and with the threading's augment-what-it-coats rule above. **The page explains none of it and nobody theorises it.** Consequence, never stated: the sword he gave away was never the weapon.

Not explained on the page; Kael reads it a second after Valeria has already done it.

Rendered through Kael with the anatomy his mother taught him at a butchery table when he was eight. **THE WORKING MAKES NO NOISE; THE MEN DO** — nothing comes off her, nothing crosses the air, nothing on the shelf moves, and the screaming that follows does not stop, because most of them are not dead yet (author, s48: *silence does not match the violence*). **FENCES:** the page never shows her face during it · nobody theorises it and nobody names it · **this is her ELEMENT and not the bloodline** — do not let a page imply the two are the same thing · **it is NOT her ceiling**: Kael prices her to the grain and finds no bottom, and what she is is Book Two's (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md`).

It drives what GROWS: root, vine, thorn and whole standing wood, up through solid rock, laced and thickening at the crossings and putting out fresh growth on the far side of itself. Severin closed a cut with eleven feet of it in half a minute, then sent a dozen standing pieces out through the gap. **Mending is the same element turned on flesh** — which is why the man who knows which way the bone goes is also the man who can do this, and *the Art does what you understand* (Ch. 10) governs both. **The page explains none of it and nobody theorises it.** It costs him: he gets up crooked with the nose bleeding again.

Then Neris's wind picks all four hundred up and makes them one front. **Technique over purse, twice, and then scale on top of it** — and Kael, who can read every one of the four workings on that shelf perfectly, can perform none of them, because line-reinforcement is two fingers wide and his capacity is empty. **The deficit is real and the page does not soften it** (`../06-Style-Sheet.md` §KAEL'S DEFINING AXIS, and its counterweight).

Nothing comes off her and nothing crosses the air. **The scale of the problem, not the size of the working, is what escalates:** she answers them one at a time while there is a second between them, and past that *she was not choosing any more* — whatever arrives, in the order it arrives. **FENCES:** her face is never on the page during it (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md`) · nobody theorises it · **it is still not her ceiling**, and what she is remains Book Two's.

## THE TEMPLE
- **THE THREE SCHOOLS:** the empire keeps three great Resonant schools — the TEMPLE in the southern archipelago (the isles' rich merchant-noble houses), **THE COLLEGIUM** in the CAPITAL (named on the page Ch. 22; red and black), and one in the NORTHWEST, name unminted. Three schools, one fence — the pedagogy monopoly runs through all three. Open axis: archipelago money vs. capital blood vs. northwest frontier as student-status geography.
- **Head:** SEVERIN — the family's secret patron (00/03). On-page mostly "the master"; public title THE FIRST MASTER.
- **Students:** the empire's golden children — multi-part names, house pride, casual cruelty toward the charity case. Cohorts by age, fixed rooms; the YARD takes the young cohorts together — the one shared arena; war-training scales where classrooms split.
- **THE BOARDS:** twice a year the Temple writes its children in order — midwinter and spring examinations (four days: letters, sums, the chronicle, natural philosophy, a day in the yard), standings nailed to the colonnade of the first court, every name in earned order. The machine that converts Kael's excellence into public standing whether he wants it or not (his record lives in 07).
- **JURISDICTION (Ch. 18 — hard):** the Temple is the First Master's; **the tournament is NOT.** The empire holds the games; the three schools convene; the island hosts because Severin asked for it four years out. Entries are made TO THE GAMES, under the games' seal, by men the games appoint, and are lodged in the capital. **THE HOUSELESS PROVISION:** a judge of the games may stand for a competitor who has no house — a small, ancient, twice-a-generation clause written so the empire never loses a boy off a farm whose father cannot write. Severin can refuse a boy a room and the yard; he cannot refuse him the games, and cannot strike a name off a document that is not his.

### Tournament structure (author rulings, s25–s32)
- **THE GOLD LEAGUE — 16, from three schools: 6 TEMPLE / 5 CAPITAL / 5 NORTHWEST.** The host seats six; the extra seat is a small political fact nobody remarks on. **Bracket: 16 → 8 → SEMIFINALS → FINAL.** No play-in, no byes.
- **SELECTION:** the autumn entry sheet (Ch. 18) is the ISLAND'S ENTRY LIST — everyone entered from here, in earned order off the Temple's standings, Valeria at its head because she is resident for the year. **It is NOT the Gold six.** The six are picked off the MIDWINTER BOARDS — how a boy ninth or tenth in autumn is in the Gold league by spring: he climbed over the winter while two northwest-house boys were called home (Ch. 21). Never let a later chapter treat the autumn sheet as the bracket.
- **AFFILIATION vs RESIDENCE:** **Valeria competes for the CAPITAL**, not the Temple, though resident on the island a year — her coaches are the capital's grey and her house answers to the crown.
- **TEMPLE SIX:** Lucan Drusane · Kael · Elarine Sedrane · Aurelian Corvane · **MELORAN** · one further name (unassigned, available to mint). **MELORAN** (s26): the surviving senior-cohort entrant — line 4 of Ch. 18's sheet; seventeen, a spear, the man Aurelian trains against four days a week to be *enormously better* than him; NOT one of Halden's yard pair (Talrane's second son and the Iscane sit four lines below Kael). He took Aurelian out in the round of 16 (Ch. 24) and sits in the other half of the bracket. **LEDRON**, the sheet's other senior name, is gone — a northwest house sent a boat for him in the turn toward spring, no letter to the First Master (Ch. 21). **TAVRIAN**, a lower-ranked northwest entrant, went in the cold term — **do not resolve whether his mother's illness was the reason.** Neither departure is a mystery to be solved; both are the war arriving as an absence.
- **THE SILVER LEAGUE — the Temple's remaining students against each other.** **HARD RULE, scope STUDENTS ONLY:** the visiting schools send their five competitors and **no other students** — no reserves, no younger cohorts, no schoolmates along to watch. The competitors' curve is four hundred Temple children and **ten** visitors, all Gold. **This does NOT thin the island or the crowd:** houses, retinues, banners, officials, traders and spectators pour in for weeks beforehand (Ch. 12's *biggest market this island will ever see*); the arena seats eleven thousand; the village lets rooms it has never had to let. Oss is here. **A HOUSE DOES NOT COME TO AN ISLAND (author, s50).** A student's house is overwhelmingly NOT at the games: a parent or two travels, the seat and the rest of the name stay on the continent. So the island's dead are the schoolchildren and the parents who came, and the houses themselves survive the raid substantially intact — Corvane is at the capital and at Corvane, Sedrane four hundred miles off. **The consequence the page uses: the survivors are bereaved, not orphaned of a house**, and the machine that disposes of them is untouched. **Prize: a purse of gold, and the glory of winning** — set against the Gold league's city; the empire's whole class structure is in that gap, and nobody on the page needs to say so.
- **THE HOUSELESS PROVISION AND THE PRIZE (author, s25 — door CLOSED):** the prize goes to the winner's HOUSE; for a boy with none, **the crown holds it in his name until he has a house to receive it.** He can win a city and be unable to take delivery of it. Consequences: **Halden gets nothing material from a Kael victory** — only that a judge who stood for a winner was right about something in front of every house; the entry stays as clean as 00 s21 requires; the SURNAME THREAD stays owed — *Kael of what?* has a legal answer, and it is *nowhere to put a city*. **Do not route the prize to Halden, the Temple, or Severin.**
- **THE RULES ARE LIBERAL — SEVERIN IS THE ONLY SAFETY (author, s25).** The games permit **SHARP BLADES** and **FULLY UNLEASHED RESONANCE**; the single restriction is **"NO MORTAL INTENT"** — a clause that can only be weighed afterwards, and that most fighters disregard. **The enforcement is a man, not a rule: the FIRST MASTER sits above the sand for the whole of the games and (A) BLOCKS directly fatal attacks with EARTH, (B) MENDS even fatal wounds with NATURE** — his sealed dual proficiency (00 s14) and Ch. 19's *nature is the Art of mending*, at scale, in public (spend-state: 03/severin). **Contrast, never stated:** the Temple's own duels are fenced hard — nothing above the shoulders, nothing the healer cannot mend by supper, Ruthen standing them — and the empire's games have none of that: the school protects its children; the empire posts one old man to catch what falls. Pre-tests Severin's LIMIT (00) a week before the fire; six days of it deepen the never-confirmed second reading of his grey. **The chair is why he is above the sand, six miles away, when Neris goes into labour** (00 s26) — and **nobody fetches him** (author, s42): he reaches the house hours late and of his own accord, at the head of the district's escape party.
- **WHAT THE GAMES MEASURE (author, s26):** **MARTIAL PROWESS AND NOTHING ELSE** — arms as carried, resonance without limit, one boy left standing. No trial of letters or wits, no scored discipline beside the fighting: the games are the empire's self-portrait, and what it takes its own strength to be is the officer corps it is about to inherit (00 s8). **Never stated on the page:** the Temple's BOARDS already measure the real axis twice a year, and the empire that sailed here to price its children does not read them — 06 §KAEL'S DEFINING AXIS is served by the ABSENCE; the quiet true thing has to win inside the fight (*fights are puzzles*) or not at all. The layer that prices more than swordwork is the nobles' market (Ch. 17: *they are not choosing a champion, they are choosing an ORDER*), and it is not scored.
- **THE DAY-MAP (author, s28).** One round a day: **D1 round of 16 · D2 round of 8 · D3 SEMIFINALS · D4 break · D5–6 the final and the crowning, which never happen.** The games print six days (Ch. 22: *six days above that sand*) and **die on the third**, half the programme unspent. ### THE HONEST MEN (Ch. 48 — the crew)
- **A BYWORD, NOT A NAME — AND IT IS A FAIRY TALE (author, s72).** What people call them, in Ivrit and in the Koine alike, **because they are supposed never to have broken into anywhere in their lives**: everything they have ever taken walked out of a gate in daylight with the paper in order and the man on the gate holding the door for it. *It is a joke. It stopped being funny about six years ago and people still say it.* **THE TRUTH IS THAT THEY BREAK IN, AND ARE EXTREMELY GOOD AT IT** (Ch. 50). **The reputation is the security, not the method:** a crew the whole continent agrees never breaks into anywhere is a crew nobody looks for when a thing has been broken into, and six years of being told where they were not is the best cover money cannot buy. **Nobody on any page says this**, and Kael — who assembled them at Ch. 48 out of three facts — was right about *why* they were in Ishareth and wrong about *how* they work, and **has never been corrected by anybody.**
- **WHAT THE PAGE HAS OF THEM.** A house on the upstream side of Ishareth taken since the turn of the month, with two men on the door day and night who carry nothing and watch nothing; a load of copper gone out of a locked shed with the lock still on it and the owner's mark still on the door, which the watch came and looked at and went away from; and **every boat on that bank refusing money at any price for a month**. And the Solvenna robbery two years ago that nobody has explained — **a whole marble statue**, out of a yard two hundred men stood round for a month, on a cart with the owner's own seal on it. **They are not passing through.**
- **THE LEADER IS BORREN — named at Ch. 52, his own mouth; no other member is named on any page** (`naming.md`). **Kael used a small version of their entire method at Ishareth's gate nine days earlier — arriving inside something the gate had already decided to let through — and no page says so.**
- **MET AT CH. 49, AND THE TRADE IS STRUCK. THE INFERENCE WAS RIGHT AND NOBODY EVER SAID SO.**
- **THE HOUSE ON THE UPSTREAM SIDE IS A FAKE-OUT.** Two on the door of a room with bedding for two, one pot, ash raked flat and no rubbish in the corners — nothing behind them worth two men. Anyone who comes up that street and says *bridge-house* is answered by men who have been sitting on a rehearsed answer long enough to be bored of it, then relieved of his steel, searched, hooded and walked somewhere else: turns doubled back so the same three strides of loose board are crossed twice, then twelve minutes upriver by boat with a bank close on the left. **The real place is a working ROPE-WALK** — a long low building open to the river on posts, with men actually making rope in it and a small room off it holding paper, wax and seals and nothing anybody could be arrested for.
- **FIFTEEN OF THEM, AND NOT ONE LOOKS LIKE ANYTHING.** A man of about sixty in a good coat; two who are plainly brothers and plainly not of this country; **a woman of about thirty with a basket**, greeted by more of them than anybody else and the sharpest questioner in the room; four who are of Ishareth and could not be anything else. They stop the day's work in that rope-walk without one word about the cost of it.
- **THE LEADER IS ON THE PAGE — unnamed until Ch. 52, then BORREN** (`naming.md`). Past fifty, thin, grey, going bald from the front, with a close grey beard, and **a plain oval of dark leather over the right eye on a cord that goes up under the hair** (author, s72) — the rest of the face *the sort you are given directions by*. **He is the one member of that crew who can be described**, against a whole building of people who *could have been anybody at all*, and nobody on any page remarks on it; a pen in his hand and a sheet he finishes before he looks up. He asks **what do you want** and **what have you got**, and never once **who are you**. He overrules a correct objection from his own man without arguing it (*Go and get on with the rope*), clears the room for a boy, hands a child a knife, and laughs once, genuinely, at an answer he did not expect. **His limit is on the page inside twenty words:** he asks about Valeria in front of Valeria in the tone of a man asking about a mule, is answered by her, and never looks at her again. He tells two children he will find out for himself why they cannot be seen.
- **THE ONE RULE, AND IT BINDS EVERYBODY:** nobody who knows how a thing is going to be done leaves the building until it is done — the leader and the woman with the basket included. *It is not about you. It has never once been about anybody.*
- **THEIR PLAN WAS COMPLETE FOR A MONTH EXCEPT FOR ONE HOLE.** Papers, a seal, a bank, and **the boats bought before the turn of the month at a price he does not enjoy thinking about** (which is why no hull on that bank would take money — Ch. 48). What was missing was the inside of the bridge-house, and it walked up the street and asked for a knife. **Kael did not bring them a job; he filled the hole in theirs.**
- **THE TERMS (Ch. 49):** Kael gives sixty-two days of the governor's arrangements and two Resonants' hands; **the princess is held out of the trade entirely** and the crew are told so; the price is **one part in ten of what comes out of that building**, plus **Kael inside it when the door opens.** He is offered a fifth and does not take it. **The job is set for the night after the meeting.**
- **HOW THEY DID IT (Ch. 50) — THEY WALKED IN THE FRONT CARRYING THE THING HE WANTED MOST.** Kael's own recommendation: say you have found the ones the governor is hunting, and put two of them in irons. **The children are the paper.** Plain iron with no black in the grooves, so that if it comes apart the two of them can use the Art; **every weapon in the room walks in on the boy**, in the five places the crew themselves search a man; the captors are searched at the gate and are clean, and nobody touches the merchandise. Then five guards contained in four seconds, **choked out and left alive** — hands and mouths, nobody shouting, nobody killed — the leader taking the governor with nothing in either hand. **The chest walks out of the gate in the open as the reward for the boy**, in front of fifty men who watched two children walk in, and nobody says a word about it.
- **THE ONE RULE HELD ON THE NIGHT.** The leader went out with the job and was in the room; so was the woman with the basket, who carried her basket up a stair, through a search and into that room, and **nobody once asked her about it.**
- **HE CONCEDES TO A CHILD AND DOES NOT ASK WHY.** Twice now: the room cleared over a correct objection (Ch. 49), and the governor left conscious over another correct one (*"He can shout." / "Then see that he cannot."*), with three prepared reasons never asked for. **Unexplained on the page both times.**
- **THEY ARE GONE, AND THE LEADER SAYS SO (author, s73; Ch. 51).** ***"You will not see any of us again."*** Not in that city after tomorrow; **they are making their way to SOLVENNA**, which is where the trio is going; *if you hear a thing about us it will not be true*, and in a street both sides walk past. **The one rule expired with the job** and nobody was kept. **He knows Kael has his face and raises it first** — *"I knew it before you sat down"* — and his reason is not trust: *a description is not worth the paper until somebody wants it enough to pay for it.* He prices the boy instead: *"I have watched you for two days and I know what you are for. You are for one thing and it is not money. That is not a compliment. It is the most expensive way there is to live."*
- **THE TENTH WAS PAID, AND IT IS SHORT.** Small, heavier than it looks, and already turned by somebody who knows how *into something two children can carry through a gate*. Priced out loud on his own fingers — a winter in a town where nobody asks, boats twice, a man on a border who does not look, bread at four times what bread costs — and stopped at the fourth: *"It does not go thirteen months. I told you in that room that it might be short. It is short."* **Ch. 49's refused fifth comes home and nobody remarks on it.**
- **HE FOUND OUT WHY THEY CANNOT BE SEEN AND DID NOT SAY IT.** *I said I would find out why you cannot be seen and I have found out most of it tonight, and I am going to do you the courtesy of not saying the rest of it out loud in a street* … *Get her off this river.* **The governor never said what the third child was, in front of people who would have paid for it, and the leader assembled her anyway — and no page joins those two facts.**
- **THE REFUSAL, AND WHAT AN HOUR OF THEM COST.** He grants Kael's whole argument for another night with the governor and refuses it anyway — *"That is all true … No."* — then prices it: *there are fourteen people of mine in this city, and not one of them came into it for you*, and the hour was bought *because you paid for it and because I wanted to hear the answers.* **AND HE LEAVES THE MARK ALIVE, AND IT IS NOT MERCY:** *we do not leave bodies. A body is a hunt, and a hunt has a name in it by the third week* — the man must account to sixty men he is behind on paying, and *he is worth more to me sitting against that wall than he is in the river, and he knows that as well as I do, which is why he offered.*
- **THE NEVER-AGAIN STOOD ONE NIGHT, AND THE TRIO UNDID IT BY WALKING IN (Ch. 52).** *"It was true when I said it."* They find the rope-walk uninvited, in daylight, off the hooded count — the doubled loose board, the turns kept and the distances thrown away, twelve minutes of oars — and the work stops one pair of hands at a time to look at three children on a bank. Asked how, Kael keeps the secret for four seconds (*"That's a secret … it'll cost you."*) and then gives it away correcting Borren's own count of his wasted turns: *"I counted eight."* The second genuine laugh the page has given him.
- **THE TRIO ARE INSIDE (Ch. 52), AND THE TERMS ARE BORREN'S SHAPE, NOT KAEL'S.** Passage to Solvenna inside the crew, all three. Kael's hands for any work the road asks; the two girls with the camp — and what the third one is stays unsaid by everybody, the leader included. Kael offered his **whole share** for the girls staying back and was refused the shape: *a man on a job who is owed nothing is a man with nothing under his feet* — their bread comes out of his share and **the remainder is his, and he will take it.** **The one rule is extended:** *nobody leaves, not at the first town, not at the worst one* — thirteen months, eighteen people, no door that opens partway along. Consent asked of all three (*"Is it your own choice to be standing on my bank."*); obedience priced to Kael's face — *on my road, being right is not a rank* — with the admission made in the room that some evenings Borren will be wrong and Kael will do it anyway. **The standing up was the signature**, the name came at the door, and the chapter ends on the first order: *"There is rope to carry."* Departure at first light is off the page.
- **THE FIRST DAY INSIDE THE CREW, AND THE CREW'S TRAVELLING SHAPE (Ch. 53).** Up the river in the dark before first light in the three boats, the bank close on the left, the bridge downstream and never passed; landed mid-morning on a gravel shelf where a track came down, **six mules with a boy holding them and nobody said whose**; the boats went back down with two men Kael had not seen before; then tracks that go where the road does not, the sun on their backs. **The camp:** a fold that cannot be seen from anywhere a man would stand; canvas; three fires, small, **dug down with flat stones on the side they came from** (Kael's father's rule, kept by somebody else for the first time in his life); the mules to water and then a line; everyone eats at once out of the pot and *nobody counted anything onto anybody's plate*. Tirzah gives Kael the one-hand job (the hobbles) and says nothing about the hand.
- **THE ROUND (Ch. 53) — FIVE OF THEM NAMED, AND WHERE THEY CAME FROM.** Borren opens it (*"You know what they are paid … You should know what they are called."*) and tells no story himself. **TIRZAH**, the woman with the basket: of the coast three days west of Qeshet, a stall of cloth and thread; her husband hanged at the gate by the evening for going after a governor's son who took *a length of blue ribbon* off her stall; the basket is *what there was of it that I could carry*. **SELIS**, eighteen perhaps, hair cut short at the jaw: of AMMARETH; her father carried the old lord's cup for thirty years, and when *the new top went on* the men came to every house that had carried anything for the old one. **ORAN and TALO**, twins from across the water: their own side emptied the valley ahead of the empire's column — grain, animals, every man who could walk, fire in the rest — and left their mother in the house; Talo does not talk, *he did once*. **AMBROS**, the man of sixty in the good coat: steward forty-one years of a house forty miles north of the capital whose land *a cousin of the Emperor* wanted; a charge was found, the head to the wall with his sons, the people sold with the furniture; the coat is the house's. Also round the fire: a man of Ishareth who lost a warehouse, one of the four who would not speak and was not made to, the talker with a wife somewhere. **Kael gave his real name and *The island*, and nobody asked.** The tent at the camp's edge is the trio's alone, on Tirzah's word.
