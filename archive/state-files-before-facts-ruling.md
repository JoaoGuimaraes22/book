## `02-World-Bible/magic.md` — as it stood

## MAGIC (system canon — public and page-facing layers; deep layer in 00)
Laws, not a ledger: how each reached the page is in the chapter records and `../archive/world-bible-book-one.md`. Kael's own profile has one home — `../03-Character-Bible/kael.md` (STATIC → Abilities).

### The model
- Six elements — FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIGHTNING, NATURE — the known world's MODEL of magic, not necessarily its truth; origin and nature stay a world-level mystery (00). The mother's way is pedagogy beneath the elements — perception-first, threads, structure, cost — deniably closer to the deeper grammar.
- The Temple teaches a particulate theory: magic moves through THE MINIMA — the smallest grains of the world, present in all matter ("as a struck string wakes its silent neighbor" — Priscian's delivery). "The minima" is a COMMON word. The Temple's science is old, learned, honest — and incomplete (00; the "smallest grains" claim is its honest error — Kael shelves it untested, Ch. 10). Neris may use the word from Ch. 10 on; she never handed it to Kael in Part I — vocabulary withheld is the rationing ("the word was not hers," Ch. 8).
- THE LIE (public doctrine): only select bloodlines can be Resonants. False — an old imperial lie preserving the royal-noble power dynamic (00).
- Enforcement — the pedagogy monopoly: attunement stays dormant untaught; flickers are dismissed as luck or superstition. The empire does not suppress commoner Resonants — it is simply the only teacher. Anomalies absorb as *noble blood somewhere in the line*; the lie self-seals.

### Structure
- Proficiency: every Resonant works all elements at varying talent and is extremely more proficient in ONE (proficiency = far more capacity there); most work almost nothing outside it. Rare: two or three proficiencies. Kael's many-element small-scale creativity is a future weapon because the world does not fight that way.
- Capacity vs. control: separate axes — raw output vs. precision, threading, creativity (mechanics in 00).
- Declaration: the dominant element settles with maturity and training (soft window ~12–14; "when it settles it settles"). Early magic is small and formless for most, but high capacity TINTS early (Aurelian's wax-sheet fire, Ch. 9) — capacity-worship's pageantry is built on this.
- Drain (public; taught Ch. 9 in Ruthen's cost register — spending, the purse, a grain): the Art exhausts and is never infinite; deep drain is painful; drain near the bottom whitens the hair PERMANENTLY ("witch-white" in folk talk; "The body kept the receipt"); full drain kills, every time, however beloved the house. An old Resonant's hair is a readable ledger. **THE MARK'S REGISTER IS PURE QUARTZ WHITE** (author, s33 — the page's image for Ruthen's seam, *like quartz through rock*). **The Vessantine silver is NOT a mark** — hereditary house colouring (00): silver-fair is blood, quartz-white is a receipt; folk talk garbles the two and the empire lets it. ### Laws that govern every element
- **THE DISTANCE LAW** — the proximity law's page face (Ch. 11; mechanism 00 §THE PROXIMITY LAW): the Art is spent FROM the body — anywhere on it; hands are the habit. Distance and disconnection raise the cost; remote manifestation is an advanced mark. A continuous medium counts as connection (Kael's boot-thread through the ground). Fire spent at the point and moment of contact — off knee, heel and knuckle — is legal under it, and is the coalition's answer to the empire's one-handed grammar: nothing is reserved for the Art because everything is the Art (Pellan, Ch. 30/33; `factions.md`).
- **THE ART DOES WHAT YOU UNDERSTAND** (Ch. 10). The Art alone is worth very little: a Resonant who does not know which way the bone goes will push it the wrong way. Healing needs the Art AND the medical knowledge in one head, and very few carry both (Severin does — `../03-Character-Bible/severin.md`; why is in 00). The same law lets a working be run OUT OF PROFICIENCY by someone who carries the other half in the same head — it works, and it works BADLY, and the difference is rendered as contrast, never explained (Neris mending ribs with nature, Ch. 32, against the First Master's mending two mornings earlier that showed nothing).
- **CAPACITY IS NOT THE EXPLANATION FOR THE GAP (author, s25 — do not write it as one).** A program-forged elite is better than Kael at BOTH axes; a large purse means only that the master's errors would cost nothing, and he does not make them. Never stage an adult master's superiority over Kael as raw purse alone; Kael's control is exceptional *for a boy of thirteen in this world*, never in absolute terms. **THE DEFICIT IS REAL:** on a shelf where four Resonants held a hillside he read every working perfectly and could perform none of them — line-reinforcement is two fingers wide and his capacity was empty — and the page did not soften it (`../06-Style-Sheet.md` §KAEL'S DEFINING AXIS, and its counterweight).
- **SCALE AND COMBINATION.** Workings combine across Resonants: one lights every separate thing on three hundred strides of hillside at once, exactly where the fuel is, and another's wind makes the four hundred fires one front (Ch. 34) — **technique over purse, twice, and then scale on top of it**, which is the book's thesis in one page. The scale of the PROBLEM, not the size of the working, is what escalates: at saturation a Resonant stops choosing and takes whatever arrives, in the order it arrives (Ch. 35).
- **NOBODY THEORISES.** No page explains WHY a working works (`../06-Style-Sheet.md`: *the causal chain stops at the first why*); no technique below is named as a school on any page; Priscian must not be handed any of it; Kael reads what he sees and joins nothing up. Where a page renders an absence — a Resonant who cannot reach — it renders a body standing still and says nothing whatever about why.

### Lightning — the threading family
- **THE THREADING (author, s25).** NOT one resonant thread laid into a blade: **MANY** lightning threads held in tight parallel, **accelerated and CONTAINED**, run the length of the steel and kept there under load; the difficulty is holding the whole bundle exact through impact, out of a purse that will not fill a cup. **Page-facing:** *"spend a thread"* stays the general idiom for one act of spending, in narration and every mouth; the page renders the OBSERVED thing — *a thread*, *the stitch*, *a stitch of white down its length* (Ch. 14) — and **the bundle is never enumerated, counted or explained.** **WHAT IT DOES, AND WHAT IT IS NOT (author, s37):** it **augments the cutting power of whatever it coats** — steel or the bare hand — and **cuts workings**. It is **NOT an electrical effect on a body**: it does not shock, convulse or seize a man, and no page may render it that way. **IT IS VISIBLE** — white, running the length of the medium; a page that hides it is wrong; the thing Kael controls is whether he holds it or lets it go at the knock. **Consequences:** the BURST is proportionally worse (the whole bundle comes home when exactness fails); holding **two** threadings is a categorically larger problem than one, not a doubled one.
- **THE ROUTE, AND HOW IT IS TAUGHT (Vask, Ch. 21):** shoulder → arm → hand → steel, fast and tight to the body the whole way with nothing coming off it — hence total awareness of the body at once, and why a man who has lost track of his own elbow cannot hold it. Steel down first; thread with bare hands; take up the blades only once both hands hold and the feet are known — by then it runs out into the steel because there is nowhere else for it to go.
- **THE BURST** (Ch. 13 — the failure mode): a thread that loses exactness under load (impact, shock, divided attention) does not die — it discharges INTO ITS SOURCE. Margin is a function of capacity: a large purse absorbs the slop; a small purse pays in its own flesh; burn scales with thread size. The speckle-burns are ORDINARY burns — never a magical body-mark. Page canon, Vask: "I can be wrong by a finger's width. The purse forgives me. Nothing forgives you."
- **THE MEDIUM LAW — METAL TAKES THE THREAD (Ch. 15):** lightning enters METAL far more readily than wood. Wood must be persuaded and holds a thread only under the Resonant's own exactness; steel accepts one unasked and carries it WHOLE, hilt to point, no thinner for spreading. Gift and cost are one property: THE BURST through steel arrives everywhere at once and burns proportionally worse — "the same size thread in a better road." Page canon, Vask: "Wood forgives you. It bounces, it bends, and it lies to you about what you did. This does not lie." Deep-layer consistency: substances differ in minima-nature, readable by feel (00). Banked: the endgame kit's lightning-through-steel is an AFFINITY DISCOVERED, not a difficulty overcome.
- **A THREADING CUTS A WORKING (Ch. 26; author, s30).** A blade carrying the threading, driven through a fire working, **divides it** — not extinguished, not absorbed: *it did not go out. It broke* — split along the line the steel took, the halves closing on empty air behind the man who went through them. **Scope, held narrow:** the threading (the contained bundle, never a single thread), through a medium that carries it whole, against a working being *paid for continuously* rather than one that has landed. **THE HAND IS ALSO A MEDIUM:** blades sheathed, the threading runs shoulder → arm → hand and stops there, and the fire comes apart on the hand as on the blade. **The page NEVER explains why it works**; nobody theorises it; Priscian must not be handed it. **The cost is total:** this AND line-reinforcement in the same instant (Ch. 26's *two at once*) empties Kael of every grain in about a fifth of a second, and it must never become cheap.
- **THE EVOLVED THREADING — THE INWARD ART IN LIGHTNING (author, s39; SEALED MECHANISM, page-facing observation only).** Vask's whole-body form is not more threading; it is **the threading combined with the inward Art** — the same move as internal reinforcement, run in LIGHTNING through an entire body at once. **The mechanism is in 00 (bible-only, never on any page of any book until the world can hold it). The toll is enormous** and scales with how long it is held. **THE REGISTER FENCE IS ABSOLUTE:** this world has no physiology for it, so no page may reach for impulse, nerve, muscle-signal or any term implying an apparatus this world has not built (`curriculum.md` §Register rule — the one permanent ban). **WHAT THE PAGE HAS, AND ALL IT MAY HAVE:** the white in the whole of him, held, *not coming off*; *It was not going along him. It was going into him*; the same shape as a girl's Art spent inward, in the wrong element, in a whole body at once; the speed rendered as ABSENCE — *Not blurred. Not quick. Absent* (Ch. 30). **AT FULL SCALE THE HANDS CUT** (Ch. 33): steel does not slow it and mail does not slow it — a flat hand through the side of a helmet, an open palm taking an arm off at the shoulder — consistent with the hand-as-medium and augment-what-it-coats rules above; the sword he gave away was never the weapon, and nobody says so. **CONTROL, NOT PURSE** (s25 fence): never stage it as a large reserve. **Kael got half of it and wants the rest**; where that goes, and the road to it, are in 00 (the fence on him: `../03-Character-Bible/kael.md` §FORWARD).

Written in the blank book, declined twice, never attempted. NOT canon until spent on the page.

## THE TEMPLE (the empire's schools; the island's fell at Ch. 27)
The institution's law is world canon — it is how an imperial school works, and it is what the Temple was for the four years of the page. The chapter-by-chapter history of the games is in the chapter records and `../archive/world-bible-book-one.md`; the day-by-day clock is `../07-Story-Ledger/timeline.md`.

### The three schools
- The empire keeps three great Resonant schools — the TEMPLE in the southern archipelago (the isles' rich merchant-noble houses), **THE COLLEGIUM** in the CAPITAL (named on the page Ch. 22; red and black), and one in the NORTHWEST, name unminted. Three schools, one fence — the pedagogy monopoly runs through all three. Open axis: archipelago money vs. capital blood vs. northwest frontier as student-status geography.

### The Temple's law
- **Head:** SEVERIN — the family's secret patron (00/03). On-page mostly "the master"; public title THE FIRST MASTER.
- **Students:** the empire's golden children — multi-part names, house pride, casual cruelty toward the charity case. Cohorts by age, fixed rooms; the YARD takes the young cohorts together — the one shared arena; war-training scales where classrooms split.
- **THE BOARDS:** twice a year the Temple writes its children in order — midwinter and spring examinations (four days: letters, sums, the chronicle, natural philosophy, a day in the yard), standings nailed to the colonnade of the first court, every name in earned order. The machine that converts a boy's excellence into public standing whether he wants it or not.
- **DECLARATION IN PRACTICE — THE DISH (Ch. 11):** declaration formalized empirically — the iron dish with its half-ring of plain media (candle, water, sand, sprig, copper rod); each student spends ONE grain and the element shows itself; the clerk enters it in the roll; held when a cohort's oldest reach the window ("What settles, settles. The dish only asks early."). Post-ceremony, resonance duels enter the yard: the taught one-handed stance; the three rules — nothing above the shoulders, nothing the healer cannot mend by supper, and the yard-master standing them.
- **Pre-declaration cohorts:** theory, attunement, and COST — nobody hands fire to ten-year-olds; casting lives in the older precincts a young cohort never enters, so magic surrounds a first-year as distance-texture. First resonances (Ch. 9) are candle-bench exercises under the yard-master, taught only after cost doctrine ("before I let any of you spend a grain, you will learn what spending is"); a formless near-nothing at the bench is absorbed by the lie ("someone's bastard, then").
- **JURISDICTION (Ch. 18 — hard):** the Temple is the First Master's; **the tournament is NOT.** The empire holds the games; the three schools convene; the island hosted because Severin asked for it four years out. Entries are made TO THE GAMES, under the games' seal, by men the games appoint, and are lodged in the capital. **THE HOUSELESS PROVISION:** a judge of the games may stand for a competitor who has no house — a small, ancient, twice-a-generation clause written so the empire never loses a boy off a farm whose father cannot write. A First Master can refuse a boy a room and the yard; he cannot refuse him the games, and cannot strike a name off a document that is not his. **There is no such clause for a wall** (`geography.md` §The gate): the empire has a provision for a houseless boy's ambition and none for his life.
