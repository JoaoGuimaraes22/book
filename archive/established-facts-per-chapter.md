- **CH. 38 ENDS IN THE DARK:** the same night as the table; Kael under in the governor's house and his mother unable to rise, the other six behind their doors; the sleep unnamed; the governor's carriage promised for the morning.
- **CH. 40: TWO MONTHS EAST, AND THE COLUMN IS FIFTY.** Sixty-two days of road; **the escort is fifty men** — eight of them the box's own (two on the seat, six who work the door in threes) plus the governor, and the rest a number that never comes within twenty feet of the door; Kael keeps the day off the slot's light with the moon as a second witness and the direction off the sun; the governor stays with the box the whole way; the country rises to a long high back, cold at night, with standing water at the ninth week. On the sixty-second day the box comes off the road and they make a camp, and that night **Kael breaks and skins his own left hand to get it out of the band** — silently, hours early — and unties the ring-rope and the ankles with the ruin doing the holding. **The Art is still gone** (the right band stays on). The chapter ends with the bar coming off the outside of the door; nothing after it is on any page. **Wren is three months old and on no page.**
- **CH. 41: THE DOOR, AND OUT.** On the sixty-third morning the three men who open the box are killed inside it — Kael takes the one on the rope with his teeth and puts that man's knife into Valeria's hands; **the princess stands up**, which turns the doorway; Valeria kills all three in irons, with a hand's width of chain, and takes the bar across the ribs. The door is barred again from outside on three dead men, and **the escape is unseen: nobody in the camp of fifty knows at the chapter's end.** They go out on the blind side of the box and up grey stone that takes no track, uphill and away from the water. **The iron stays on all three of them** (five wrists; Kael's left hand is out of its band) — the pin is peened over, there is no key and nothing to cut it with, so **the Art is unanswered for all three.** Kael's ruined left hand holds a mouth shut and is bitten through it. The gag comes off over the shoulder, and **the princess's first word in two months is *Kael*, not *Lucan*** (author) — followed by *I did not do what you told me.* Kael's count on the pursuit is **ten or twelve, mounted, with a tracker**, built off the governor's looking back, and nothing corrects him. **KESIR is dead; Rashim is alive.** Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine are still on no page.
- **CH. 42: THE SEARCH PARTY, AND THE QUESTION.** The same day, continuous. The trio flees north off the road into trees on the cold side of the KHAROUN, to the first cover and running water in two months. **The governor's search party — twelve, seven in on foot behind a tracker — is fought and killed by the three of them together**; **Aeliana stabs a man's leg to get Kael out from under another** and does not let go, and that man is kept alive; **six dead, one prisoner.** Each of the three now carries a **soldier's sword** (Kael's own pair gone since Ch. 39; the longsword he cannot use) and keeps the knives. **Kael tortures the bound man** — feverish, in massive pain, keeping the count on the man's own pulse to stay clinical — for **where they are (the KHAROUN) and the nearest village (DORESH, half a day north, down the water, a smith)**; he oversteps once onto the house and his mother, reads that the man truly knows nothing (he was on the road, not in it), and stops. **Valeria kills the prisoner, unasked, and Kael lets her.** The plan is on the page: find where they are · a village · a smith to cut the iron · a healer. **The iron is still on all three of them** (five wrists; Kael's left hand out of its band) and nothing in the party's kit opens it; the Art is unanswered. **Kael's left hand is a fever** — the red line up the forearm, priced at days without a healer. The three go down toward Doresh at dusk. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 43: DORESH TOOK THEM IN.** The night of the sixty-third day into six days in the village. **The cover story is war orphans who were taken** — no name and nobody to write to, and the iron on their wrists is the part of it a village believes. Kael's approach works and picks the house off a banked forge fire; **he collapses at the door**, and the two girls, with no Ivrit between them, ask for help without a word — a deliberate knock on wood and two open hands held up in iron, neither of which Kael can attribute. **He wakes in a bed on the second morning.** The house is DAMIR the smith's. **An ordinary healer mends them with no Art** — the hand opened, drained, packed and splinted, ten days needed; the arm decided in two or three; the hand *you will find out in the spring*. The headman takes the story and the village has done this before. **The princess is hidden under a scarf and begins learning Ivrit from Kael.** **ISHARETH is spoken.** The iron is still on all three; the Art is unanswered. The chapter ends with the three of them under the storeroom floor and the boards going down.
- **CH. 44: FORTY DOORS, AND THE IRON OFF.** Continuous. **The pursuit arrives *slower and bigger*** — ten horses at least, working the villages with a list, describing all three (including *silver, like a thing that has been made*), naming a price worth every roof in Doresh, and naming **any house** that helps. **The cover story is destroyed by that description** and forty houses are asked and **not one opens its mouth**; Kael counts the three quarters of an hour from under the floor. In the exhale afterwards **the child pulls the scarf off and the hair is out for about four seconds**, and **TARIM sees it** from the forge door; Kael reads the family right and the person wrong. **Damir cuts the iron off all three over two nights** — the peen cut, the pin drifted, an hour a band — with forty houses lying in the dark listening and nobody coming out; he takes the iron as the fee and buries it under his forge floor as evidence. **The Art answers for all three, the first since Ch. 39**, and what the black in the grooves is is still on no page. Tarim is not in the forge on the second night and Kael misreads it. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 45: THE GROUND.** On the seventy-first morning Damir says his son is gone up the water in the night. **Kael does not stop Tarim — he buys him** (author): he agrees with Tarim's arithmetic, corrects the one term in it (*there is no sentence in the world where you give us up and Doresh is not in it*), shows him **the Art** as proof (a thread the length of a soldier's sword, at the cost of a hard breath), tells him *she is stronger than I am, and so is the other one* — true of Valeria, a lie about Aeliana — and promises the princess's father's reward for the village. In return Tarim confesses the truth, brings them to a place Kael names, and gives him **a day**; he carries one of the bands back out from under the forge floor as proof. **Damir rules for Doresh** (author): *Doresh stands by children. We are all in this together and there is nothing to decide.* The headman is on no page of Ch. 45 or Ch. 46 and nobody in the village leaves. A day of preparation: **188 caltrops** off Damir's forge, **three pits** opened by Valeria (*My Art is still returning to me Kael*), and the litter laid over them by the princess, who **speaks Ivrit on the page for the first time** and is corrected by Damir. **Kael says *Val*** (author). Kael's promise: *not one of those men rides back up that water.* Ends with the horses coming past Tarim at the split rock.
- **CH. 46: NINETEEN COME DOWN AND NONE GO BACK UP.** Continuous. The caltrops stop the column, the ground divides it, and **all nineteen die in that fold** (author): 1 under his own horse, 9 down the water side (4 of them taken alive in the pits), 3 at Kael's mouth, 6 held at the top including the officer — **14 dead, 5 taken alive, and all five killed on the page.** The princess's count of nineteen against Kael's eighteen sends him two hundred strides down the shallows, where he finds the last man **breathing** and kills him there in front of Tarim. **Valeria fights at less than full Art, visibly slows, and takes both spears through her own left arm; Kael cannot hold the thread past a count of six** and finishes his last man kneeling on his chest with none. **The killing of the helpless is explicit** (author) and the first one goes wrong. **The men had been bought** — no two coats the same, a child's tooth on a cord, coins sewn in three hems, a letter about a roof — and Kael reads all of it with his hands; the sum still comes out right and **the weight does not stay down**, which is the first failure of the one thing he knows how to do with a thing (`../03-Character-Bible/kael.md`). **THE GOVERNOR IS AT ISHARETH, BUYING MEN** off a letter, with one bridge, and has told nobody anything since the day he shut that door. **Doresh keeps thirteen horses**, priced by Damir at one at a time down the water over four years; Damir gives the iron back — *it is not evidence here*. **THE DECISION IS ISHARETH** (author), settled by the map — west is two months of open road with a description of her hair at every well — with Kael's true reason said out loud and Valeria saying once that she does not like it. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 47: THE CROSSING.** Eleven days east of Doresh (two more than Kael said), then six days inside ISHARETH. They hunt and forage the road and are not hungry; **Kael explains the count out loud for the first time in the manuscript** and believes it is ordinary. **The description they are hiding from is *three***, so the scarf stays on and they do not come through the gate together. **NALAR** is named at the lip. **ISHARETH IS NOT THE EMPIRE'S CITY** — the wall, the watch and the toll gate are the city's own, there is no white and blue anywhere on it, and the one building the empire is standing in is the bridge-house, so *there is one bridge and it is his* is the whole of the governor's holdings. They come in inside thirty-eight mules through a gate that counts animals and loads. **ZAHIR's yard**; a room paid for out of coin cut from three dead men's hems above Doresh. **The princess rules herself indoors and Valeria refuses to leave her, and the princess overrules her** (author). **Valeria's ground-sense is a projection** (author). The search: **sixty-three men at the bridge-house and rising**, paid off a table three days a week out of a chest fed by the tolls, nine of them the box's, **Rashim on the yard gate**; and a sum with no door in it. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 48: THE HONEST MEN.** Three facts that are not about each other — a load of copper out of a locked shed with the lock still on it; **no boat on that bank will take anybody at any price**, which the princess gets off four mouths from a window she is not allowed to open; and a house on the upstream side with two on the door day and night. **THE HONEST MEN** is minted, as a byword, in Zahir's mouth, with the Solvenna robbery (a whole marble statue off a yard two hundred men stood round for a month, on a cart with the owner's own seal). Kael works out that there is **one reason to be in that city this month** and that it is the chest he wants the man beside, so **he is trading and not begging**: sixty-two days of the governor's arrangements, a Vel, three Resonants, and a girl whose face is the reason the whole thing exists. **The part of him that would have argued did not say one word, and he noticed that it had not.** **Kael and Valeria go to the door together** on the princess's order. Ends on the approach: *"I want to talk to somebody about the bridge-house."* **No member of the crew is on the page; the leader is unnamed.** Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 49: THE PAPER.** The seventh day in Ishareth, continuous from Ch. 48. **The house on the upstream side is a fake-out**: the steel goes on a table, the two of them are searched (the sleeves back to the elbow, the seventy-day iron-marks seen and not mentioned) and hooded, walked a doubled route (the same three strides of loose board at 341 and again at 509) and taken **twelve minutes upstream by boat** to a working **rope-walk**, where the crew's business is done in a small room full of paper, wax and seals. **The leader is on the page and is UNNAMED, and so is every other member of the crew** — past fifty, thin, grey; he asks *what do you want* and *what have you got* and never *who are you*. Kael answers with **the rate** (fifty-one, then sixty-three two days later, one in three turned away) and gives it away free. The room is cleared over a correct objection, and **the Art is shown to one man**: Kael's threading up a borrowed pen-knife, twice; **Valeria raises a column of the beaten floor to her shoulder** with no noise and nothing coming off her. **THE ART IS FULLY BACK ON BOTH** (author). The leader prices them — *You are the ones out of his box* — and **Kael does not deny it**, and is made to say aloud what the three of them are worth. **THE TERMS:** information and two Resonants' hands for **one part in ten** of what comes out of the building, **plus Kael inside it when the door opens**; **the princess is held out of the trade entirely.** **Thirteen months to Solvenna from Ishareth** (author), and hiding makes that road dearer, slower and more dangerous — Ch. 38's *five months* was said at Qeshet and is untouched. **Kael is offered a fifth and does not take it.** The band is called — **fifteen, none of whom looks like anything** — and the job is set for **the night after the meeting**, because their plan has been complete for a month except the inside of that building and **the boats were bought before the turn of the month**. **NOBODY WHO KNOWS HOW IT IS GOING TO BE DONE LEAVES THAT BUILDING UNTIL IT IS DONE** (author), the leader included, so **Kael gives up the princess's address** — Zahir's yard, the outside stair, the last room — to buy her a word, with the token *nineteen boards, eleven long and eight short over the joist.* Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 50: THE REWARD.** The eighth day in Ishareth, dusk; the night and the day of planning are off the page. **The honest men break into the bridge-house, and the byword is cover rather than method** (`../02-World-Bible/factions.md`). **Kael and Valeria go in as the merchandise** — plain iron with no black in the grooves, because if it comes apart those two have to be able to use the Art — and **every weapon in the room walks in on Kael**, in the five places the crew searched him in Ch. 49. **The story is Kael's own sentence**, said by one of the crew: *We have two of them. We want the price on these two, and we want the work of getting the third.* The captors are searched at the gate and are clean; nobody touches the boy. **RASHIM identifies him** — off the walk before the face — and hurries, which Kael has never once seen him do. Upstairs: five guards and the governor. **The governor does not know Kael's face**, having never looked at it properly, so he goes to the wrist instead — and **stops on the iron, because there is nothing cut into it for the black to sit in.** **Nobody in that room, Kael included, had worked that out** (author); the crew's leader reads the shift in the man's face and calls it. **Six move at once and not one of them moves toward a weapon**; the five are contained and **choked out, and nobody is killed**; Valeria takes the man nearest the door with her fists and her Art spent inward, so nothing leaves her body and the job is not signed. **The leader takes the governor with his hands.** Kael asks for him to be left awake and **is not asked why**. **The chest walks out of the gate in the open as the reward for the boy**, in front of fifty men who watched two children go in, and nobody says anything about it. **The room does not empty** — Valeria, the leader, the woman with the basket and others stay — and the chapter ends on Kael in the governor's own chair asking *The people who were with us in Qeshet. Where are they?* Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 51: THE SHIPS.** The same night, continuous. **The governor answers, and the first answer is nothing** — *I do not know where they are.* **What the page now has of that house, and it is the first since Ch. 39:** sixteen men in, four to a door and four doors (Valeria: *"Four came into my room"*), six out carrying three children, **ten who never left**, and the people in it still fighting when the box went out of the yard. **No name, no outcome, and nobody left alive who knows.** Kael converts *I could still hear it at the bend above the town* into **twenty-six minutes** and says it to nobody. **THE BETRAYAL IS IN HIS OWN MOUTH:** a man twice in the winter with nothing in his hands, who wanted **the coast and not the harbour** — the watch on the wall on three nights and nothing about them in any letter going north — and **thirty-one hulls, counted by the governor himself.** His reason: *they were going to have Qeshet*, and what he could arrange was *how it was done, and when, and to whom*; *"I did not ask what was on it. I did not ask what they would do."* He has kept the city seventeen years and his father before him, and **the name was given to the family when the empire took the city.** **QESHET HAS BEEN TAKEN by the same men who took the island**, on a day he does not know, and **nobody in Ishareth has heard it.** **The coalition is *a resistance* on the page and nothing more**; the great war is named — the island the first spark, Qeshet the second — and **the crew's leader does not disagree.** **THE SLEEP IS STILL UNNAMED:** a thing he was sent, told what it would do and how much to use, *did not ask what it was and was not told*, and *it did not do all of it*; *"I have no name for him, or a face to give."* **Kael loses it, gets a fistful of the man's cloth and the chain onto his throat, and Valeria does not move**; the crew's leader says *"Come back."* **The third concession is refused and he is asked why for the first time** — *"That is all true … No."* **The governor is left alive and it is not mercy** (a body is a hunt). **His only question all night is *"Is she alive?"*** and he never says what the third child is. They walk out on the same lie, past **forty-nine men**, and *It held.* **THE CREW ARE GONE — *"You will not see any of us again"* — bound for SOLVENNA**, with the tenth paid, small, and **short of thirteen months**. **The princess opened the door**, and **Aeliana takes hold of Valeria for twenty-three seconds** before she is through it. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 52: THE JOINING.** The small hours into the ninth day in Ishareth, continuous. **The council on the three roads** — alone (no top on the time), an imperial ally (*the last imperial hand we took put us in a box*), or the honest men — with the rally doctrine in Valeria's mouth as family teaching (*I was taught it before I was taught anything with an edge on it*), **the letter raised by Kael and distrusted by the princess** (*by the man who had us in a box before morning*; whether it ever went north is on no page), and **the ruling hers**: *"Then I have ruled. We go."* The morning walk as not-a-three; **the rope-walk found off the hooded count** and the secret kept four seconds (*"I counted eight"*); **the trio joins**: Kael's hands for any work, the two girls with the camp, their bread out of Kael's share and the remainder his, the one rule extended thirteen months (*nobody leaves*), consent asked of all three. **BORREN is named, his own mouth, at the door**; the chapter ends on the first order — *"There is rope to carry."* A crowd at the bridge-house since first light and no table, through Zahir, unfollowed. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 53: THE ROUND.** The first day on the crew's road, continuous from Ch. 52. **Up the river in the dark** in the three boats, the bridge downstream and never passed (*the second leaving of his life that nobody had watched* — author); a gravel landing mid-morning, six mules with a boy and nobody says whose, the boats back down with two strangers; tracks, not the road, the sun on their backs. **The camp** in a fold, canvas, three small fires dug down with stones on the side they came from, one pot and nothing counted onto plates; Tirzah gives Kael the hobbles, one-handed. **The round:** Borren's *you should know what they are called*; ***Kael***, nothing after it; **Valeria as *Tamar*, Aeliana as *Adah***, sister and cousin, *our people are dead*; then **Tirzah** (a husband hanged at a gate over a length of blue ribbon a governor's son took), **Selis of Ammareth** (a cupbearer's house when the new top went on), **Oran and Talo** (a valley emptied and fired by their own side, their mother left in the house), **Ambros** (a steward of forty-one years, a house sent to the wall for land *a cousin of the Emperor* wanted), a man of Ishareth's warehouse, the talker's laugh; **Kael's turn is *The island*** and nobody asks; **Borren tells nothing.** The princess's back goes straight at *a cousin of the Emperor* and stays. **The tent is the trio's** (Tirzah's word): Valeria across the door; *He did not name the house.* / *No.* / *I know which one it was.* **Kael keeps Avestan from the crew.** Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine still on no page.
- **CH. 39: THREE IN A BOX, EAST.** The day after Qeshet's night Kael woke in a moving box in painted iron with Valeria and Aeliana and nobody else; the governor of Qeshet opened its door at dusk a day east. His mother, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine are on no page from Ch. 39; whether they live is unknown to Kael and unsaid. The Art does not answer in the painted iron (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`). Nothing after the door is on any page.
