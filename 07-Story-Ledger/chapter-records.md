- **Ch. 40 — "The Hands" (Kael = 13, not re-pinned; continuous from Ch. 39's door at dusk, through sixty-two days of road, to the camp and the door opening the morning after).** **I — THE DOOR.** The governor looks at the princess for forty-one seconds and never at the other two; one instruction over their heads, in Ivrit: *Her hands. Oil and a strip. She is not to be marked.* Three men, and the method — one on the bar, one on the rope, one with the oil, and *the man on the rope watched Valeria and not the girl he was holding*; the gag checked with two fingers and left. **Kael prices the three of them the way the man in the doorway would have to:** the girl worth more than anything else on the road and *it was not close*; *a Vel is not a thing you come by*; and himself, because a princess told a governor to write him down a son of House Drusane — *Maybe if she had not, he would not yet be alive.* *So they would be fed.* Outside, the governor's voice in Ivrit: the fires are too big. **II — THE ROAD.** One rule — *He was not going to lose the day*: the days off the light in the slot with **the moon as a second witness**; the direction off the sun and the sky; east, and up more than down. Fed twice a day at a box that barely stops — *a little over four hours of looking at the world* in two months, and he spends every second of it on ground, growth, stone, tracks, and at the wells a string of loaded animals whose men do not look at a black box going past. **THE NINE.** The governor is still there in the sixth week: canopy and chair gone, thinner in the face, sleeping badly, riding at the back, and *every day, at some point in the afternoon* turning to look at the road behind him. **Kael's answer — the goods are too valuable to hand to anybody — is wrong**, and *it would be a long time before anybody told him.* The other eight give themselves up under eight weeks of watching: **RASHIM** and **KESIR** on the box seat; Kesir priced as cruel on the fourth day and **the verdict taken back on the eighteenth** — he cannot stand to look at her, and shutting the door is the fastest way to stop — *He could not remember the last time that had happened.* **The ninth voice is the governor's**, and Kael has it by the second week — the level Koine with the coast under it that came through the boards on the first day; Ivrit to his own men, the empire's tongue for what he wants done, *and it was done, and nobody thought about it first.* In the fifth week the governor opens the door between meals, begins a sentence, looks at the cloth across her mouth *as though he had not until then thought about what it did*, and shuts it; Kael makes it mean the man wants to be told he will be paid. **III — THE THREE OF THEM.** All going down. **The watch divided:** *"Valeria. You should sleep."* … *I'll keep track of all of it. If I'm ever caught lacking, I'll rely on you, but I'll need your full strength when the time comes to go.* She does not argue, says *"Very well,"* and sleeps — *He filed it, and knew that something had happened, and did not have the size of it.* She sleeps, eats everything, spends nothing, *went thin the slow way instead of the fast one*; *He never once checked on her.* He takes the toll. **THE PRINCESS GOES:** the back, in the fourth week; the water, and *"Highness, please. Aeliana. Drink it,"* which becomes Valeria's, twice a day, for the rest; the talking — *"My father will surely pardon you and reward you with anything you wish, if you free me. Please. Anyone."* in the second week and **again, word for word, in the fourth**; and the laugh through the cloth in the sixth, small, going on slightly too long. Kael files it as the heat, then the wrists, then an illness he has no training to name, and **never gets to the truth of it.** His count turned inward: grip, forearm, thumb and finger round the arm above the elbow; every number down; and in the seventh week one gets better — **the band goes further up his hand and stops in exactly the same place**, *because what was stopping it was not the part of him that was going away.* **IV — THE TWO ROADS.** The sum on his mother built twice and abandoned — taken, or not taken, and no way to choose: *A sum with two ends and no way to choose between them is not a sum. It is a hole.* **WREN IS THREE MONTHS OLD, to the day** — a baby of three months takes milk from somebody, so either he is with his mother, or with somebody who found a way, or he is not anywhere at all; *The last of him he had was a weight inside his coat in a passage, asleep, with his fists shut.* **V — THE COUNTRY.** Brown, then grey-green in the low places, then thorn in the wadis, then a long back of the world, high and cold at night; **standing water at the start of the ninth week.** Four terms line up for the first time: a stop that stays stopped, ground that will keep three people alive, a moon four days from nothing, and a body still able. **On the sixty-second day the box comes off the road** and they make a camp. **VI — THE PLAN.** *"Tonight."* The morning door is always three; *when the door is open it stays open*; and the three preparations said out loud — the ankles, the rope off the ring, the band held back over the hand — *If any one of the three of them is looking properly, we are finished.* Valeria: *They have not looked properly since the fourth week*; her part is to be awake and not appear to be. To the princess: do nothing, do not stand, do not run, *do not fight whoever takes you* — and **he cannot tell whether one word went in**, and prices her as a body that will have to be carried. **VII — THE HAND.** *"Highness. Look away now."* — **she does not.** The thumb, the decision made twice, a twist of straw in the teeth, the heel of the right hand driven through the joint: *the joint at the base of the thumb stopped being a joint and became two pieces with a hinge of skin over them.* The first pull moves half of what it needs to and stops on bone, the band taking the skin off the knuckles *rolling it ahead of itself the way cloth rolls up an arm.* Then the bracket's quarter-inch gap and **pressure, not a blow, because a blow is a noise**: the bone below the little finger folds inward with the ends sliding past each other, and a second splinters. *"There will be another night."* / *"No. No."* / *"You may not be able to use it again."* / *"I know. I will do it. I must do it."* — and Valeria prices the whole thing in about four seconds, finds no fault in his arithmetic, and stops. The last pull is **not enough**, and he pulls harder, and the whole back of the hand comes off *the way a glove comes off inside out.* **He does not scream.** No waves and no top to it, held down with teeth, weight and thirty feet of sleeping men; *breathing like someone had removed his lungs*; then the shaking. **No name for it** — *He had a name for every kind of pain his father's school had ever handed him … He had none at all for this.* **Valeria counts him back**, *"One."*, to about sixty, three times, picking him up from the last number he said without comment. **VIII — THE WORK, AND THE DOOR.** *A knot wants two hands: one to hold the thing still, one to work it* — his right is in iron on the rope he has to untie, so **the holding is the ruin's**: an hour and a half, let go four times, out once.
