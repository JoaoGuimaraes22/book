- **Ch. 37 — "The Still" (Kael = 13, not re-pinned; thirty days, from the second morning of the drift to the night of landfall — the spring gone into summer). BOOK TWO OPENS.** **OVERTURE** — the sixth slip, four sentences in the telling voice: thirst, and *"It is a month, and I am counting it again."* **I — THE EMPTY HORIZON.** The island gone in the night; the routine of the hull — the cup, the bailing, twenty words, the coats on a sweep for the night breeze; **Kael puts the boat on the sky** (position by stars, taught at eight on the flat rock) and Severin has it off the swell: north and a little west, a walking pace, further than nine days. **II — NERIS SAYS IT.** *"We must boil the sea."* … *"The salt stays."* — to the two fire Resonants and nobody else. **III — THE STILL:** a ballast stone, the stone jar, the box lid tilted, the sail-cloth wrung cold; **Aurelian's fire on an open palm** (*"the most useful thing my house has done in a generation"*), Elarine's smaller, steadier and hotter; a gallon on a good day for eight, and it is what they live on. **IV — THE PRINCESS AND THE CLOTH** (a scene, at the author's ask): she takes the cloth, wrings it wrong, is told, does it right — *"Show me."* — and tells him why: his mother said she would be good at this, and *"I should like to know which."* He takes it as an experiment. **V — THE MONTH:** the figs gone, the biscuit counted aloud, one fish in twelve days; thirst as a body; **Wren crying thinner**, his mother's cup doubled and then trebled, **the princess's cup down the boat** on the nineteenth day; Valeria never off the girl; the seam that will not close; a day in it, perhaps two. **VI — LAND**, smelled first; the current along the coast; the sand chosen over the harbour; the hull opens fifty strides out and stops being a boat; Valeria carries the princess; **Kael's father's sword on his back on the length of cord.** **VII — THE WATCH:** six spears and a captain; **the eight get ready, badly** — both of Valeria's blades, Kael's pair with the sword left on his back, Neris's child into the old man's arm and her hands loose, Severin's hand flat on the sand; Aurelian gives the name; **the watch kneels to the name** with their eyes on the blades — *"Forgive the spears"* … *"the sea brings us a great many things that say they are what they are not"* — and Valeria drinks first from the skin. **VIII — QESHET:** the mile, the brown, the trees; his mother walking inside six spears as a woman with a baby; **water running at the gate**, which Kael cannot walk past; the roofed street, the hands doing nothing, the market through a wall. **IX — THE GOVERNOR** comes out to them: Hazoun Sabrane, fifty, one band of blue; told the Temple is nowhere he **does not ask how**; the square turns to a princess doing nothing; **her hair — silver, once** — and the whole chain, and a knee to the girl where the watch gave one to the name; *"Get up, Governor."* **X — THE MENDING:** the house's healer, oils and a needle and no Art — Kael's ribs found wrong in four seconds; *"She is good,"* says Severin; the healer reaches for the child and Neris refuses: *"Nobody here is qualified to but me."* At the door: *"You are your mother's. It is not always a compliment."* **XI — THE TABLE.** A rider saddled for the capital and every name wanted; **Neris goes still** (*"the stillness the village did not make"*) and Kael has no second word to give; **the princess gives Drusane** — Lucan, the Lady, and the child — *"Write it so."* Aurelian backs it with true sentences (*"Of the capital"* … *"neither of them came down"*), Elarine with *"In my cohort also."*; the governor looks at a herder's hands and writes. The four give their own; Severin's Temple, *"which is gone."* The governor asks about the cloth gently; Neris gives the eyes as damaged at the birth — *"Anybody who takes the cloth off him will do him harm."* — *"Nothing will touch him in this house."* A horse leaves the yard. Kael eats *"under a name that was not his"* and does not count anything. **FACTS:** the sixth first-person slip (the overture; feeling only; no future fact) and no other departure; the map on the page (north to the continent; west is the road; *east is theirs*); thirty days; no rain, no water Resonant, **no Art of Neris's**, no mark on either fire Resonant; **Aeliana's hair spent — silver**; the eyes' colour still unspent; **QESHET and HAZOUN SABRANE minted**; the household's gesture; the watch and the governor kneel; **the Drusane letter is on the road**; Kael misreads the princess's why (pride) and is uncorrected; the knife stayed in the coat; the longsword rides on his back; the hull is gone and whose it was is on no page; the deferral came due off the page and stands; the three wants untouched and the man unnamed; *general* still on no page; no coalition name spoken; the Ghost, the Phantom, Halden and Oss not mentioned. **NEW NAMES:** Qeshet · Hazoun Sabrane. **NEW WORLD FACTS:** the south's register and material culture (`../02-World-Bible/geography.md`, `naming.md`); a fire Resonant as a stove (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`). - **Ch. 38 — "The Watch" (Kael = 13, not re-pinned; the same night as Ch. 37's table, continuous from its last line, to the drugged dark).** **I — THE ROOMS.** The governor rises — *a carriage to the capital* in the morning, for all of them — and goes out; eight doors, a light in each, and nobody moves; two men of the house at the passage who stand like the watch. Aurelian says it (*I have wanted a bed with my whole body for a month, and I find that I cannot walk to one*), Elarine confirms it, the princess rules it — *Nobody is to be by himself who does not wish to be. It is a house. It has walls. It will bear it.* Valeria checks two rooms — *This one*; Severin takes the far door alone (*for longer than any of you has been alive*); Aurelian and Elarine end behind one door — *The floor. You will have the floor.* / *Naturally.*; Kael has the child. From her doorway: *Goodnight, Lucan* — he is two strides past it before he knows it was for him. **II — THE BED.** The third door: a latch and no bar; the sword along the foot of the door, grip toward the bed, the pair by his head; the first bed of his life. **III — THE LANGUAGES.** He understood the healer, the captain, the household — *the language you called Traders*; why she taught him the tongue of the men who burned the island: *It is not theirs* — older than those men, *spoken by all for thousands of years, until the empire spread just a few hundreds of years ago*; the child-names retired and the true names given — **Ivrit** (the south), **Avestan** (the east, the one she sings in), **the Vessantine Koine** (the empire's); why all of them — *So that there would be nowhere on this continent you could not go.* *I was never going to leave the island.* / *No.* **IV — THE EYES.** *Perhaps it's time. Ask your questions* — the refusal ends. Oss's story off the sea-wall in Kael's mouth; *I saw one of his eyes*; her answer: a story that *changes and conforms to what people want to hear rather than the truth* — *These red eyes, are something in my blood. Even I know little about them … it came to him through me. It is in him now.* *What about me?* — *I'm not sure, Kael*: she hoped not, prayed to the gods of the East, his eyes were *simply eyes* at birth, her blood *not as strong in you as it is in Wren*. **And her own:** *My eyes are just like his … ways, with great will and control, to hide them.* Sworn: *Nobody. Say it.* / *Nobody.* — from Valeria, the princess, the two next door; nothing added to what the old man knows. The rest deferred: *not under a roof that writes letters.* **V — THE WATCH.** *I am going to stay awake tonight … There's something off about this place, these people. Or perhaps there's something off in me.* Kael says the enemy aloud — the invaders the enemies, the empire's people *the victims. They'll help* — and gets *my truth*: *There are no enemies. There are no allies. All we can be sure of, is family.* *I don't sleep well. Not since father—* / *Look at you.* The weight that is not tiredness; *Something is wrong.* / *I know.*; *Count for me*; she holds something in her body and it is not enough; *Stay awake, Kael. I'll get up, I'll go to you* — *her legs failed her*; *Six never came out loud*; his name in her voice, *then just, darkness.* **FACTS:** no departure of any kind; the same night — no new day on the page; the governor's carriages promised, not seen; the sleep is never named (no *drug*, no *poison*); the three true language names minted in Neris's mouth, the birds and the fourth unnamed; the empire's spread dated to *a few hundreds of years ago* and Avestan to *thousands of years* before it; *the gods of the East* minted (she prayed to them); **Kael has been TOLD his mother's eyes are red and hidden — he has still never seen them**; whether the blood is in him is open in her mouth; Neris says she knows little of it; the bloodline's name still on no page; the bundles and the leaving NOT raised; the Severin door not on the page — the deferral stands unasked; the sword, the pair and the coat last placed in the third room; *Lucan* said to his face for the first time, by the princess; Kael's count failed a fourth way — from inside; the Phantom, Halden, the coalition's names and *general* not mentioned; the linen unexplained beyond the table's lie. **NEW NAMES:** Ivrit · Avestan · the Vessantine Koine · the gods of the East. **NEW WORLD FACTS:** `../02-World-Bible/lexicon.md` §Languages · `factions.md` §The Vessantine Empire (its age) · `bloodline.md` (the page ceiling).
