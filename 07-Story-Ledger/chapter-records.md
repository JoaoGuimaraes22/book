- **Ch. 37 — "The Still" (Kael = 13, not re-pinned; thirty days, from the second morning of the drift to the night of landfall — the spring gone into summer). BOOK TWO OPENS.** **OVERTURE** — the sixth slip, four sentences in the telling voice: thirst, and *"It is a month, and I am counting it again."* **I — THE EMPTY HORIZON.** The island gone in the night; the routine of the hull — the cup, the bailing, twenty words, the coats on a sweep for the night breeze; **Kael puts the boat on the sky** (position by stars, taught at eight on the flat rock) and Severin has it off the swell: north and a little west, a walking pace, further than nine days. **II — NERIS SAYS IT.** *"We must boil the sea."* … *"The salt stays."* — to the two fire Resonants and nobody else. **III — THE STILL:** a ballast stone, the stone jar, the box lid tilted, the sail-cloth wrung cold; **Aurelian's fire on an open palm** (*"the most useful thing my house has done in a generation"*), Elarine's smaller, steadier and hotter; a gallon on a good day for eight, and it is what they live on. **IV — THE PRINCESS AND THE CLOTH** (a scene, at the author's ask): she takes the cloth, wrings it wrong, is told, does it right — *"Show me."* — and tells him why: his mother said she would be good at this, and *"I should like to know which."* He takes it as an experiment. **V — THE MONTH:** the figs gone, the biscuit counted aloud, one fish in twelve days; thirst as a body; **Wren crying thinner**, his mother's cup doubled and then trebled, **the princess's cup down the boat** on the nineteenth day; Valeria never off the girl; the seam that will not close; a day in it, perhaps two. **VI — LAND**, smelled first; the current along the coast; the sand chosen over the harbour; the hull opens fifty strides out and stops being a boat; Valeria carries the princess; **Kael's father's sword on his back on the length of cord.** **VII — THE WATCH:** six spears and a captain; **the eight get ready, badly** — both of Valeria's blades, Kael's pair with the sword left on his back, Neris's child into the old man's arm and her hands loose, Severin's hand flat on the sand; Aurelian gives the name; **the watch kneels to the name** with their eyes on the blades — *"Forgive the spears"* … *"the sea brings us a great many things that say they are what they are not"* — and Valeria drinks first from the skin. **VIII — QESHET:** the mile, the brown, the trees; his mother walking inside six spears as a woman with a baby; **water running at the gate**, which Kael cannot walk past; the roofed street, the hands doing nothing, the market through a wall. **IX — THE GOVERNOR** comes out to them: Hazoun Sabrane, fifty, one band of blue; told the Temple is nowhere he **does not ask how**; the square turns to a princess doing nothing; **her hair — silver, once** — and the whole chain, and a knee to the girl where the watch gave one to the name; *"Get up, Governor."* **X — THE MENDING:** the house's healer, oils and a needle and no Art — Kael's ribs found wrong in four seconds; *"She is good,"* says Severin; the healer reaches for the child and Neris refuses: *"Nobody here is qualified to but me."* At the door: *"You are your mother's. It is not always a compliment."* **XI — THE TABLE.** A rider saddled for the capital and every name wanted; **Neris goes still** (*"the stillness the village did not make"*) and Kael has no second word to give; **the princess gives Drusane** — Lucan, the Lady, and the child — *"Write it so."* Aurelian backs it with true sentences (*"Of the capital"* … *"neither of them came down"*), Elarine with *"In my cohort also."*; the governor looks at a herder's hands and writes. The four give their own; Severin's Temple, *"which is gone."* The governor asks about the cloth gently; Neris gives the eyes as damaged at the birth — *"Anybody who takes the cloth off him will do him harm."* — *"Nothing will touch him in this house."* A horse leaves the yard. Kael eats *"under a name that was not his"* and does not count anything. **FACTS:** the sixth first-person slip (the overture; feeling only; no future fact) and no other departure; the map on the page (north to the continent; west is the road; *east is theirs*); thirty days; no rain, no water Resonant, **no Art of Neris's**, no mark on either fire Resonant; **Aeliana's hair spent — silver**; the eyes' colour still unspent; **QESHET and HAZOUN SABRANE minted**; the household's gesture; the watch and the governor kneel; **the Drusane letter is on the road**; Kael misreads the princess's why (pride) and is uncorrected; the knife stayed in the coat; the longsword rides on his back; the hull is gone and whose it was is on no page; the deferral came due off the page and stands; the three wants untouched and the man unnamed; *general* still on no page; no coalition name spoken; the Ghost, the Phantom, Halden and Oss not mentioned. **NEW NAMES:** Qeshet · Hazoun Sabrane. **NEW WORLD FACTS:** the south's register and material culture (`../02-World-Bible/geography.md`, `naming.md`); a fire Resonant as a stove (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`).
