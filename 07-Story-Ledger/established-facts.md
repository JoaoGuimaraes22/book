### Story facts
- The bloodline descends through NERIS (00): she was the program's harvested ASSET, Vask a forged INSTRUMENT. Kael and Wren carry it through her.
- Kael: social blindness flaw; technician talent — low raw output, exceptional control and tactics.
- The father teaches the body; the mother teaches the mind and magic.
- The parents were not seen fighting until the fire. The father died in it.
- The mother gives birth to the brother at the end of Act One. **She, the newborn and the master are ABOARD at the end of Book One** — the extraction and the parting are Book Two's (author, s49; `standing-rules.md`).
- **BOOK ONE ENDS AT CH. 36**, on a becalmed hull one day out, with the island still on the horizon and no lights on it.
- **A student's HOUSE is not at the games** — a parent or two travels and the seat stays on the continent (`../02-World-Bible/temple.md`), so the Temple children who got off the island are bereaved and not houseless. Corvane and Sedrane both survive the raid.
- **Eight get off the island alive:** Kael, Neris, Wren, Severin, Valeria, Aeliana, Elarine Sedrane, Aurelian Corvane. The hull loses its mast on the way out and drifts. The trio still crosses the continent together; Book One no longer ends at the moment it becomes three.
- The raiders are COALITION forces. Kael's initial truth: they are the enemy.
- The master placed the parents on the island, and brought Kael to the Temple as "the master's charity case."
- **BOOK TWO OPENS AT CH. 37** in `manuscript/book-two/`: thirty days on the hull, no rain, the still; landfall at Qeshet on the south coast; all eight ashore; the hull gone. The map: the island south of the continent, the empire west, the coalition east (author, s55).
- The longsword rides on Kael's back on a length of cord; he fights with the mismatched pair (author, s55). **At Ch. 38's end it lies along the foot of the third room's door in the governor's house, the pair at the head of the bed.** **From Ch. 39 both are on no page — *He did not need to look for his blades.***
- **CH. 38 ENDS IN THE DARK:** the same night as the table; Kael under in the governor's house and his mother unable to rise, the other six behind their doors; the sleep unnamed; the governor's carriage promised for the morning.
- **CH. 40: TWO MONTHS EAST.** Sixty-two days of road; Kael keeps the day off the slot's light with the moon as a second witness and the direction off the sun; the governor stays with the box the whole way; the country rises to a long high back, cold at night, with standing water at the ninth week. On the sixty-second day the box comes off the road and they make a camp, and that night **Kael breaks and skins his own left hand to get it out of the band** — silently, hours early — and unties the ring-rope and the ankles with the ruin doing the holding. **The Art is still gone** (the right band stays on). The chapter ends with the bar coming off the outside of the door; nothing after it is on any page. **Wren is three months old and on no page.**
- **CH. 39: THREE IN A BOX, EAST.** The day after Qeshet's night Kael woke in a moving box in painted iron with Valeria and Aeliana and nobody else; the governor of Qeshet opened its door at dusk a day east. His mother, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine are on no page from Ch. 39; whether they live is unknown to Kael and unsaid. The Art does not answer in the painted iron (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`). Nothing after the door is on any page.
