## Standing Rules (from author)
**LAW CORE.** Story-side STATE: what has been spent, what is now fixed, what may not be contradicted. The RULE side — how prose is written — is `../06-Style-Sheet.md`.

- **FATHER-GLIMPSE LEDGER (ration hard).** Sanctioned glimpses of Vask's true capacity spent: (1) the slate, Ch. 1; (2) rising out of the nothing, Ch. 2; (3) the wrist catch, Ch. 3. Ch. 4 and Ch. 5: zero spent. EXCEPTION EXECUTED (Ch. 6): the rescue rendered ONLY as fragments through half-conscious POV (sound stopped mid-note; absence; hands; "a quiet the size of several men") — Kael could never testify to what happened; no comparison frame built (deliberately: the reader never sees Vask do what Kael couldn't). The account holds at three-plus-fragments and is now VERY expensive. No new glimpses until the "never seen them try" beat (mid-book). The fire remains the only full answer.
- **DEPARTURE LEDGER (POV departures — rules in 06).** **Pan-aways are not counted (author, s32)** — the device is established; which chapters take the camera off Kael is stated in each chapter's record (Ch. 12 · Ch. 23 · Ch. 26). The craft fences bind every use: dialogue and observable action only · NO interiority for secret-holding heads (Severin, Vask, Neris, the Gardener) · cut before the answer · the native seal available · machinations MOVING, never sealed answers · roman, never block-italic (06 reserves the italic for the slips) · a chapter's micro-cuts are ONE departure. Interludes and first-person slips keep their per-use argument, because there the count IS the rule. **First-person slips: 2 spent, 1 reserved.** (1) The Ch. 1 micro-overture — five sentences of future feeling/judgment ("I was happy...") ahead of "Before anything else, there was the fire"; zero future facts; frame unresolved. Retroactively legitimizes Ch. 6's "later, much later" as the same voice surfacing. Deliberate echo logged: "both were true" runs adjacent to "both things were real". (2) The Ch. 13 close — the coat block ("the wearer pays for it... both things were true. Both things were always true.") — the overture's dark sibling; "heavier coats since" = feeling/judgment, zero future facts. (3) THE TRIPTYCH: happiness claimed (Ch. 1) -> cost named (Ch. 13) -> **the voice's return AT THE FIRE**. **RATION AMENDED (author, s37): the third panel is the voice's return at the fire, and INSIDE THE FIRE it may surface more than once, at genuine turning points, argued per use.** Outside the fire the per-use argument is unchanged and the count still IS the rule. **SPENT INSIDE THE FIRE: Ch. 28 only** (the first kill — *"you may remember deciding"*). **Ch. 29 takes none** — argued and declined: the gate's power is in the plainness of its machinery, and a slip there would editorialise what the scene already does. Vask's death must not repeat its instrument: Ch. 28's is about what was built into his hands, so the father's has to be about the man who built them.
- **THE KYNEMIR ABSORBED-STATE RULE (sealed in 00 — page-facing consequence):** the blood surfaces from the absorbed/flow state, NEVER from panic or fear. Ch. 6's beating therefore correctly shows no surfacing. Any future scene of Kael in mortal terror must NOT flicker; any future absorbed-state scene is live territory. The unleashing gate (00 s20) keeps this as its first term: flow is necessary and insufficient. **The fire is no longer the unleashing — Kael unlocks in BOOK TWO**; what the fire carries is the FLICKER at Vask's death, rendered as him passing THROUGH terror into the cold place, never as panic.
- **GROUP LAUGHTER IS NOW A CHARGED REGISTER FOR KAEL (Ch. 6 onward, unmarked).** Every future scene where people laugh around him — the Temple yards, feasts, the golden children — has the settlement under it. Never labeled; render as his small physical tells only. Its term of art on the page: **"a crop of laughter"** (a deliberate coinage — logged in 02's lexicon; four instances Ch. 8–10; further uses deliberate-only). The register's origin runs the full night of Ch. 6 in three strikes (the spotting, their own man down, "someone was laughing still").
- **THREE STACKED SYSTEMS (Part II engine).** Kael arrives at the Temple carrying three systems at once: ignorance + settlement fear + armor; the golden children read the composite as strangeness. His threat-calibration (set in the yard) makes their worst fail to register as danger; his calm under mockery enrages; escalation spiral. Runs unlabeled under every Temple-yard scene.
- **CRACKED WASTER — INERT FOREVER.** The waster with the seated crack (Ch. 9, Lucan's rig) is spent: the crack-up return WAS the resolution. Do not bring it back as a device.
- **THE WEAPONS SCHOOL (opened Ch. 7 · STEEL SPENT Ch. 15, author-ratified session 19).** Sword, spear, bow — on WASTERS (Vask-carved wood; species never named on the page — "ash" retired session 19) through Ch. 14. **From Ch. 15 the school is in STEEL:** Vask puts blunted training blades in Kael's hands one year out, provenance unremarked and unasked; the Temple issues its own to the cohorts at midsummer, so the mountain is ahead of the empire and no one in that yard knows it. This spends the *steel is banked — the Temple's, or the fire's* clause on the TEMPLE'S side only. **THE FIRE'S STEEL IS UNTOUCHED AND STILL BANKED: the CHEST (00) is inheritance, not issue** — the two must never be confused, and the throw's weight depends on Kael already knowing steel when it comes. Runs at home in parallel with the Temple years (mornings stay Vask's), powering the concealment thread from both ends: the Temple never sees what the mountain teaches; the mountain never asks what he shows the Temple. Vask's Ch. 2 register holds (beside him, longer, better; corrections one stroke). At-capacity rule untouched.
