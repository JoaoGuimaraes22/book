Observables only, no interiority, roman not italic, no valve moved. **COUNTING CONVENTION (s29):** a chapter's micro-cuts count as one spend however many there are — the ration is on the CHAPTER taking the camera off Kael, not on the number of cuts. **THE DEVICE IS ESTABLISHED (author ruling, s29) — the per-use argument is DROPPED for pan-aways.** The camera may pan away when a chapter wants it; it no longer owes a heavy-canon argument first, and this ledger records rather than authorises. **The craft fences are UNTOUCHED and still bind every use:** dialogue and observable action only · NO interiority for secret-holding heads (Severin, Vask, Neris, the Gardener) · cut before the answer · the native seal available · machinations MOVING, never sealed answers. **Scope is pan-aways ONLY** — interludes and the first-person slips keep their per-use argument, and slip 3 stays firmly reserved. **First-person slips: 2 spent, 1 reserved.** (1) The Ch. 1 micro-overture — five sentences of future feeling/judgment ("I was happy...") ahead of "Before anything else, there was the fire"; zero future facts; frame unresolved. - **THE KYNEMIR ABSORBED-STATE RULE (sealed in 00 — page-facing consequence):** the blood surfaces from the absorbed/flow state, NEVER from panic or fear. Ch. 6's beating therefore correctly shows no surfacing. Any future scene of Kael in mortal terror must NOT flicker; any future absorbed-state scene is live territory. The unleashing gate (00 s20) keeps this as its first term: flow is necessary and insufficient. **The fire is no longer the unleashing — Kael unlocks in BOOK TWO**; what the fire carries is the FLICKER at Vask's death, rendered as him passing THROUGH terror into the cold place, never as panic.

Never labeled; render as his small physical tells only. The register's origin runs the full night of Ch. 6 in three strikes (the spotting, their own man down, "someone was laughing still").

His threat-calibration (set in the yard) makes their worst fail to register as danger; his calm under mockery enrages; escalation spiral. Runs unlabeled under every Temple-yard scene.
