### THE PARENTS AT CAPACITY, AND THE STEEL
- **THE AT-CAPACITY RULE (06's for Book One; rolled to `../archive/style-sheet-book-one.md`, s56) IS PAID ON VASK'S SIDE.** Vask has been seen at true capacity — Ch. 30 (the whole-body threading, the longsword, the exchanges with Pellan) and Ch. 33 (his hands) — rendered through a boy on the ground with broken ribs who could see only part of it and knew exactly what the part was worth; the narration compared him to nothing and explained nothing. The pre-fire glimpse ledger (three glimpses, Ch. 1–3, and the Ch. 6 fragments) is closed history. **Neris's ceiling is Book Two's** (below).
- **VASK IS DEAD; THE SWORD WAS GIVEN, NOT THROWN (Ch. 33; author, s43)** — blade taken halfway down in his left hand, the grip turned over into his son's right, the boy's fingers closed on it by his own; then he walked into four hundred men with nothing in either hand and cut with them. It was staged as the MONUMENT every earlier Vask beat pointed at (the tally-marks, the carved wasters, the audit, the seed-bread, the carried-across-ice hand; the rule is in 00). **KAEL SAW THE DEATH, ALL OF IT, AND WAS NOT KNOCKED OUT** — he was spared none of it.
- **VASK NEVER CLAIMED THE BLOOD IN WORDS** — only *"Mine as well."* (Ch. 11) and one palm-crack; Kael, the Temple and the world inferred it. Spent; the rule behind it is in 00.
- **THE WEAPONS SCHOOL, AND ITS STEEL.** Wasters (Vask-carved; the wood's species is unnamed on the page — *ash* retired, s19) through Ch. 14; from Ch. 15 the school is in STEEL — blunted training blades one year out, provenance unremarked and unasked; the Temple issued its own at midsummer, so the mountain was ahead of the empire and nobody in that yard knew it. **The fire's steel was inheritance, not issue:** the longsword reached the page in Vask's hand (Ch. 30) and went into his son's (Ch. 33). Kael's own pair came out of the shed at Ch. 32 — two blades that do not match, his father's answer to the twin-shorts prohibition, put down inside his reach with not one word about the arena. **The prohibition is answered and dead, and nobody has adjudicated it.**
- **THE CHEST (planted Ch. 19, locked) WAS NEVER OPENED ON ANY PAGE.** Its steel reached the page before it did — Vask arrived already carrying the longsword (Ch. 30), so the man was the signal that the ceiling was off, not the chest — and the *board up, the empty hole* archaeology designed for the house (s39) was cut from Ch. 32 by the author (s42): the archaeology and the subtraction moved forward whole and are unspent. **Nobody on the page has explained the sword.** Neris's dagger travels a different road (00; `../03-Character-Bible/neris.md`).

### WHAT THE FIRE LEFT ON THE PAGE

- **THE CONCEALMENT ENDED IN THE HOUSE (Ch. 32).** 06's *love is embedded in the teaching, never stated* held for thirty-one chapters and is deliberately over from there: the parents behave like people who know — the mother used the Art in front of her son and took a permanent mark for it; the father sat down on his own floor, asked a question with no use in it, and armed the boy he forbade. **Nobody has said why.** A spend first (s43); 06's rule was rescoped to match at s56 — *love is embedded, not stated*, and a stated line is a spend, argued.
- **THE CH. 32 REGISTER STANDS, AND IT IS THE POINT (author, s43).** Severin's *"You fool"* and *"I'll go check on her, do take care of these children behind me"*, Valeria's *"I hope you will let us stay by you, you know"*, Kael's *"Yes, well… I'm glad you're fine too, Valeria"*: their raw selves, in that house, that night, with the concealment ending inside it — the author's scope for it: that house, not a general loosening of three of the book's most controlled mouths.
- **NERIS'S ART IS SPENT AT MASS SCALE AND IT IS NOT HER CEILING (Ch. 34–35; author, s48).** Air that cuts, on the shelf; the same instrument turned to defence at saturation, from the bow. Kael priced her both times and found no bottom — *a number he could not even estimate the shape of.* **What she is is Book Two's to show**; what remains open on her is a MOUTH, not a scale — the word in a raider's mouth and her face, and the Phantom. The old *steel and skill and nothing else* clause is retired.
- **HER FACE WAS TURNED AWAY FOR THE WHOLE OF IT, BOTH TIMES (Ch. 34, Ch. 35).** The page did not get round in front of her during either working; she did not turn round afterwards for a long time and nobody said her name; nobody remarked on the not-turning. **Kael has never seen his mother's eyes go red**; Severin and the princess have.
- **KAEL'S COUNT HAS FAILED THREE WAYS** — it returned nothing at the gate (Ch. 28; the wall did not make it so, the wall made it visible), worked and returned something worse at the house (Ch. 32), and on the water returned answers faster than they were worth, losing the terms one at a time until *he did not know what number he was on* (Ch. 35). **None of the three was panic: the machine did not fail, its output stopped being usable.**

### THE BOAT, AND WHERE THE BOOK ENDS
- **THE SEPARATION IS BOOK TWO'S (author, s49).** Neris, Wren and Severin are aboard the hull at the end of Book One; the parting, its no-farewell and its instrument are Book Two's to spend — nothing on any Book One page arranges it, and nobody is in a second boat. The word in a raider's mouth, the mother's face and **the Phantom asked and not answered** travel with the mouth that is still aboard.
- **HE DID NOT ASK (Ch. 36).** *Ask me on the water* came due; he had the question finished in his mouth, priced what it would cost her, and put it down; she felt him take the breath and offered him nothing. **The Phantom is unasked and unanswered; the promise is neither broken nor kept.**
- **HE BLAMES HER, AND HE HAS NOT BLAMED HIMSELF (Ch. 36).** Kael told the princess to her face that the fleet came for her and that his father died of it — *they came up my father's path, four hundred of them, all night, because you were standing in his yard … and you are not worth it.* **Nobody confirmed it and nobody denied it**, and the narration joined nothing up. **His own hands are in the chain and he has not noticed them** (`../00-Author-Canon-SEALED.md` §True causality): his reconnaissance put that house in the party's road; his arithmetic chose the ground. **He is wrong at the level that matters, and Book One ends with him wrong (author, s50).**
- **VALERIA STOPPED THE HAND AND NOT THE MOUTH (Ch. 36; author, s50).** She did not move while he said it; she was in front of him before the arm was anywhere, and it was his MOTHER's voice that ended it, not hers. **This is not the crown-vs-Kael choice** — she stopped the body so as never to have to choose, which cost her nothing anybody can call her on. **The choice is STILL not made.** Her *Please* is on the page twice in Book One — Ch. 27 (twice on that page) and here.
- **THE HULL IS STILL UNEXPLAINED (Ch. 36).** Asked nothing, Severin said only *"It was not stocked for eight."* — true, and it answers nothing — **and nobody asked what it was stocked for.** The stores were laid in for four (Kael, Vask, Neris and the child); **that number is not on the page and Kael has not derived it**; nobody on any page knows it is the family's hull but the reader. His *ask me the rest of it on the other side of that water* is still unspent, because they are not.
- **THE THREE WANTS ARE BOOK ONE'S LAST BEAT (author, s50):** his mother and brother alive; to know what his parents were; to kill the man who killed his father — the first wants of his life with a *because* under them (04's purpose thread pays here), and the page never says so. **No vow was sworn (author, s50):** a want, not a promise; nothing said aloud; nobody to swear it to; **he has not named the man on any page.**
- **THE HULL DRIFTED, AND IS GONE (Ch. 37; author, s49).** Thirty days, north and a little west, to the south coast of the continent; it opened on the strand a mile from Qeshet's mole. §BOOK TWO — QESHET below.

### BOOK TWO — QESHET (Ch. 37)
- **THE MAP IS ON THE PAGE (author, s55):** the island south of the continent; the empire its west, the capital with it; the coalition's countries its east; the road from the south coast *north and then west along the shoulder*; *east is a month of nothing and then it is theirs* (Ch. 36, turned at s55 to match). `../02-World-Bible/geography.md`.

- **THE GOVERNOR DID NOT ASK *HOW*.** Told the Temple was not anywhere at all, the next thing out of him was not the question; Kael filed the absence. Whether the news of the island beat them to the continent is on no page.
- **THE THREE WANTS ARE UNTOUCHED** — not a word of them on the Ch. 37 page; the man who killed his father is still unnamed. Neris's face was not an issue: she worked nothing. Kael's count returned when there was water in him, and at the end of the meal he did not count anything at all.

It ran unlabeled under every Temple-yard scene, and it is still the composite.
