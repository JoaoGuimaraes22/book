## Standing Rules (from author)
**LAW CORE.** Author-ratified rules that govern how the prose is written.

- **Love is embedded in the teaching, never stated.** Every stern lesson must carry the signature of protection in its stitching (the hand before the ground; the lie that makes a horizon). If a teaching scene reads stern without detectable love, revision flag.
- **FATHER-GLIMPSE LEDGER (ration hard).** Sanctioned glimpses of Vask's true capacity spent: (1) the slate, Ch. 1; (2) rising out of the nothing, Ch. 2; (3) the wrist catch, Ch. 3. Ch. 4 and Ch. 5: zero spent. EXCEPTION EXECUTED (Ch. 6): the rescue rendered ONLY as fragments through half-conscious POV (sound stopped mid-note; absence; hands; "a quiet the size of several men") — Kael could never testify to what happened; no comparison frame built (deliberately: the reader never sees Vask do what Kael couldn't). The account holds at three-plus-fragments and is now VERY expensive. No new glimpses until the "never seen them try" beat (mid-book). The fire remains the only full answer.
- **THE KYNEMIR ABSORBED-STATE RULE (sealed in 00, session 5 — page-facing consequence):** the blood surfaces from the absorbed/flow state, NEVER from panic or fear. Ch. 6's beating therefore correctly shows no surfacing. Any future scene of Kael in mortal terror must NOT flicker; any future absorbed-state scene is live territory. The fire candidate depends on him being past panic, into something else.
- **GROUP LAUGHTER IS NOW A CHARGED REGISTER FOR KAEL (Ch. 6 onward, unmarked).** Every future scene where people laugh around him — the Temple yards, feasts, the golden children — has the settlement under it. Never labeled; render as his small physical tells only. Its term of art on the page: **"a crop of laughter"** (ratified session 13 as deliberate coinage — logged in 02's lexicon; four instances Ch. 8–10; further uses deliberate-only). Ch. 6 revision (session 13) extended the register's origin: the men's laughter now runs the full night in three strikes (the spotting, their own man down, "someone was laughing still").
- **SCIENCE REGISTER — AMENDED TO A GRADIENT (session 6; full rule in 02).** The permanent ban is on OUT-OF-WORLD vocabulary only (no modern terms, no formulas as notation). Knowledge, method, and precision deepen WITHOUT CEILING across the series. Part I = craft-lore (holds as written). Part II+ = the HONESTY PIVOT (canonized Ch. 7): the parents teach with precision where they can afford it; Kael's language matures into genuine period natural philosophy; he coins his own terms where the world has none (far-counting precedent — the lexicon habit is a canonized trait, see 03); the EXPERIMENTAL TURN begins (his own trials — "asking twice with one thing changed"). Sealed irony (00): honesty in five schools, rationing in three (their past, history, magic) — the pivot draws the map of their secrets in negative space; Kael will notice the asymmetry (filed unknowing Ch. 7 — Part II beat). If a lesson reads MODERN, revision flag; if it reads merely precise, that is now correct.
- **THREE STACKED SYSTEMS (Part II engine, session 6, canon — promoted from 04 at the session-18 addendum 5 (the outline compaction)).** Kael arrives at the Temple carrying three systems at once: ignorance + settlement fear + armor; the golden children read the composite as strangeness. His threat-calibration (set in the yard) makes their worst fail to register as danger; his calm under mockery enrages; escalation spiral. Runs unlabeled under every Temple-yard scene.
- **CRACKED WASTER — INERT FOREVER (ruled session 12; promoted from 04's superseded brief at the session-18 addendum 5 (the outline compaction)).** The waster with the seated crack (Ch. 9, Lucan's rig) is spent: the crack-up return WAS the resolution. Do not bring it back as a device.
- **THE WEAPONS SCHOOL (opened Ch. 7).** Sword, spear, bow — on WASTERS (Vask-carved ash; steel is banked — the Temple's, or the fire's). Runs at home in parallel with the Temple years (mornings stay Vask's), powering the concealment thread from both ends: the Temple never sees what the mountain teaches; the mountain never asks what he shows the Temple. Vask's Ch. 2 register holds (beside him, longer, better; corrections one stroke). At-capacity rule untouched.
- **THE MENTOR THREAD (opened Ch. 7 — develop gradually).** Kael–Severin now has a private channel (the walk down): the rehearsal (teaching concealment as curriculum — the family trade in a third teacher's hands, never labeled), question-doors, the one-inch doctrine tell ("Hold that. It is the only thing I am worried you'll lose."). Severin's register: doors with nothing written on them; kindness in valve-form (the fish-and-mutton line — an exit given without acknowledging a door). Grow by inches across Part II; the valve family (liar's knot, winter-feast fact) never tips.
- **DO NOT SOFTEN THE HARSHNESS (counterweight).** The parents are not good parents. Their teaching is at times result-oriented to the point of genuine, reader-discomforting harshness — procedural, calm, total; never rage, never cruelty for its own sake. Kael normalizes and defends it on the page (he is proudest when the harshness "works"), which makes it worse, not better. The love is real AND the harm is real; neither cancels the other. Occasionally a parent glimpses what they are doing — and does not change. They rebuilt the shape of the thing that unmade them. If every harsh scene resolves into reassurance, revision flag. (Ch. 5 instance: "She never noticed that he stopped" — the harm running the other direction now; the concealment they built arriving in him, aimed at them.)
- **MODEL-TIC SELF-AWARENESS (author-ratified 2026-08-20).** The prose is drafted with Claude, and Claude has default rhythms it falls into when not watching itself: standalone em-dashes, "not X but Y" constructions, sentence fragments deployed for gravity, triadic lists. None are banned — the book's voice already uses all of them — but every instance must be a CHOICE, not a default. While drafting, Claude self-flags passages where its own patterns are steering the sentence instead of the voice; revision passes report per-chapter tic counts (dashes, not-buts, fragments, triads — and, added session 12: AND-CHAINS ["and x and y because z" accumulations; and/1k as the gauge] and SIMILE DENSITY ["the way…"/"like" per 1k]) alongside the word count. The two session-12 additions are AWARENESS instruments, not thresholds (author ruling): chain-cadence and simile-dense passages are legitimate where chosen — the audit found them steering by default (37–58 and/1k, ~1 simile per 100 words, everywhere). Upward drift without intent = revision flag. The test is always: would this sentence survive being read aloud in the author's voice, or does it sound like a model being writerly?
- **MAGIC ON THE PAGE (Part I ceiling):** perception-first, non-lexical, craft register only. No mechanics, no terms ("the minima" arrives at Temple enrollment, Part II). Every magic lesson is double work — teaching AND leashing (00, Kynemir); the relief-tell is now SPENT — do not respend.
