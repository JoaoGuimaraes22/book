Vask's death must not repeat its instrument: Ch. 28's is about what was built into his hands, so the father's has to be about the man who built them.

- **THE FLICKER IS SPENT AND STAYS UNRESOLVED (Ch. 33).** Reached THROUGH terror into the cold place, never as panic — the absorbed-state rule is intact and was the constraint the scene was built around. - **THE CONCEALMENT ENDS IN THE HOUSE (Ch. 32).** 06's *love is embedded in the teaching, never stated* held for thirty-one chapters and is **deliberately over from here**: the parents behave like people who know. The mother uses the Art in front of her son and takes a permanent mark for it; the father sits down on his own floor, asks a question with no use in it, and arms the boy he forbade; there are hands, warmth and plain words all through it. **Nobody says why, and nobody may.** This is a SPEND, not a rule change — 06 is untouched, and nothing retroactive follows.
- **NERIS'S ART IS SPENT, AT MASS SCALE, AND HER TRUE CAPACITY IS BOOK TWO'S (Ch. 34; author, s48).** The *steel and skill and nothing else* clause from s42/s43 is **RETIRED**: she was mended by Severin and had recomposed some, and on the shelf above the bowl she spent AIR at a scale the manuscript had never shown — and **it cuts** (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md` §AIR AT MASS SCALE). What the page establishes is that **Kael priced her and found no bottom** — *a number he could not even estimate the shape of.* **She is nowhere near what she is, and that is Book Two's to show.** What remains owed on her is not a scale but a MOUTH: the word in a raider's mouth and her face, and the Phantom.
- **HER FACE IS TURNED AWAY FOR THE WHOLE OF IT, AND THAT IS THE RULE, NOT THE STAGING (Ch. 34).** Severin's hand stops Kael four strides behind her; the page never gets round in front of her while she works; **she does not turn round afterwards for a long time and nobody says her name**, and *when she did turn round she was his mother.* **Kael has never seen his mother's eyes go red and must not.** No page may show her face during a working of that size, and nobody remarks on the not-turning, then or ever.
