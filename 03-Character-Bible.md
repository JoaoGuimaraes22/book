## KAEL (protagonist)
- **Role:** POV character; hidden child of an ex-instrument (Vask) and an ex-asset (Neris) of the program; carrier of the rare KYNEMIR bloodline — through his mother (kinetic magic; dormant, leashed by his own training; the Ch. 4 knot was its first raw surfacing — he does not know; see 00). Surname unknown even to him — he doesn't learn surnames EXIST until the Temple ("Kael of what?").
- **Want:** (childhood) to touch the village lights; to understand; to be measured. Evolves. **Ch. 5 turn:** the want stops waiting — "he had traded every promise for every fact." **THE ACKNOWLEDGMENT HUNGER (author-ratified session 12):** he WANTS success and fame — to be acknowledged, measured, remarked — the starved hunger of a boy parented through preparation ("you did not get remarks for doing what was expected"; "Again tomorrow" held like treasure). He climbs; he NEVER sandbags. THE COAT HIDES WHAT HE IS, NEVER THAT HE IS GOOD — an unstable split by design: his own want keeps poking holes in his own coat (the counter-drawer he cannot name yet: what NOT being seen costs). Success feeds both dangers — noble eyes (attention, later the harvest's screening) and peer resentment (the spiral, the Oss cooling, Lucan's arithmetic). The want-thread pays off in Valeria: "the first to be his measure" — the acknowledgment he starved for, in the one language he speaks.
- **Wound:** raised in loving secrecy; the world forbidden; parented through preparation instead of presence. **Ch. 5 deepening:** the discovery that the founding promise was a lie ("a door painted on a wall") — absorbed silently, never confronted; the first wound he chooses to carry alone. **Ch. 6 — THE SETTLEMENT (SEALED SCAR CANON, executed):** his first impression of humanity is the atrocity — he never entered the feast (treeline only); the first humans he meets at ground level are the rot-men. Witnessed the assault in full with no categories for it; the trauma is foundational and series-long: distrust of strangers, of crowds, of touch, of GROUP LAUGHTER (charged register, unmarked, from Ch. 6 on — render as small physical tells only). This sits UNDER the innate social blindness as a second engine — Pt II awkwardness is now ignorance PLUS fear; combat-as-only-social-language becomes CAUSAL (the one contact with rules); the Valeria thaw and all future intimacy must dig through this. **THE MISFILED DEFEAT:** he files the night as "not enough" — his failure — when the sealed truth is the machinery worked and only the operator broke (mind stable, he wins; canon). This wrong verdict is a driver: it feeds the Temple hunger and the mid-book "never seen them try" beat. **THE UNFILED FACULTY (on-page, never labeled):** his conscience — the nameless hating with no drawer — is the one faculty in him nobody built ("the first thing his body had ever known before he did that no one had put there"). The series' moral spine; the reason the Requiem is love and not doctrine. **THE PHYSICAL SCAR:** left forearm, blade slash, stitched by Neris — same site, near-same length as Vask's tally-marks (rhyme unmarked, never explain; banked reveal door).
- **Defining trait (childhood especially): CURIOSITY.** Voracious love of learning — "his questions had questions"; a day with a new thing in it is a better day. On-page canon (Ch. 3): "the itch that cannot be out-waited" — the one urge his stillness training cannot defeat. Ch. 5: the itch "changed registers" — it drives the year and the crossing. Design: parent-cultivated (they fed it deliberately — see 00, Doctrine) and rule-breaking (it is the engine of the Part I transgression and points DOWN the mountain). It is also exactly what the Temple/harvest prizes.
- **The concealment inheritance (begun Ch. 5, canon):** his first secret kept FROM his parents — "a room of his own now, with a shut door in it" (her own image, inherited). "Both things were real... the family trade." This is the seed of the Part II self-chosen concealment and the series-long "becoming them" thread. He deploys their own schools against the house (the count, the third board, the door's holding, the night-watch pattern cracked) — lovingly, proudly, in their voice.
- **Flaw:** SOCIAL BLINDNESS — cannot read cues, hierarchy, mockery, alliances; answers rhetorical questions literally; doesn't understand why excelling makes enemies. Combat is the only social language he's fluent in. **WHAT/WHY SPLIT (ratified session 13 — full rule in 06):** he detects the WHAT (anger, a lie, a turned room) and misreads the WHY; the gap closes at series scale — people as not-patterns is a late discovery, and PAST it his social reading becomes extremely proficient (arc, not fixed trait; the rule governs pace, not destination).
- **Ch. 7 ripples (Part I close):** THE PUNISHMENT THAT NEVER CAME — guilt with tools and no names; consequence arrived as permission (formative strangeness, never resolved). The audit FED the misfile ("The body held. We will build the rest." — sealed double reading, 00). Weapons school begun (something to swing — "did not tell anyone, least of all himself" that some of the gladness was that). Received concealment as curriculum on the road (the coat — worn gratefully; the family trade in a third teacher's hands). Declined the eavesdrop (the cost of doors — the shut-door room maturing into ethics-shaped caution, unlabeled). AT THE GATE — THREE STACKED SYSTEMS (Pt II engine, canon): ignorance (never socialized) + fear (the settlement under everything; group laughter's first strike fired Ch. 7) + armor (training that renders both invisible — "None of it showed"). Consequence for the golden children: they read the composite as STRANGENESS; his threat-calibration was set in a yard they can't imagine, so their worst may not register as danger; his calm under mockery enrages; escalation spiral armed.

Continuity: the knife first (Ch. 3, and it told the truth at the settlement); wasters rule the present.

His empiricism + survival pragmatism vs. the nobles' capacity-worship = a named Part II engine. Sealed (00): his method is the deep layer's native epistemology — invisibly correct all along.

The lexicon grows across the series — characterization, archaeology, worldbuilding in one habit. "I don't know" held as a TOOL (hers → his → Severin's blessing on it).
- **Talent profile (AUTHOR EMPHASIS ratified session 11 — see 06: keep the low-capacity fact heavily on-page, and the empire/Temple's capacity-worship loudly, visibly wrong about him; his true defining axis is intelligence + technique + practical fieldcraft + exceptional martial skill — balance, hand-to-hand, weapons, his parents' teaching in his body; technique and control remain his greatest asset as magic's importance grows):** technician, NOT powerhouse. Proficiency: LIGHTNING (declaration = reserved Part II beat); unusually even generalist spread across all six elements beneath it. LOW raw capacity; exceptional control, technique, tactics, decision-making, threading, creativity — his lightning is small, exact, fast (sparks, timing, nerve-and-thread work), never bolts. Wins the way water wins. His style is his parents' synthesis — father's economy of motion through mother's economy of power. When he moves, they're alive. (Deep-layer note, sealed: under the true mechanics of magic, his profile is the deep-truth-favored one — see 00.)

The beat now lands against two years of established blindness.
