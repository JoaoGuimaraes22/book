## KAEL (protagonist)
- **Role:** POV character; hidden child of an ex-instrument (Vask) and an ex-asset (Neris) of the program; carrier of the rare KYNEMIR bloodline — through his mother (kinetic magic; dormant, leashed by his own training; the Ch. 4 knot was its first raw surfacing — he does not know; see 00). Surname unknown even to him — he doesn't learn surnames EXIST until the Temple ("Kael of what?").
- **Want:** (childhood) to touch the village lights; to understand; to be measured. Evolves. **Ch. 5 turn:** the want stops waiting — "he had traded every promise for every fact." **THE ACKNOWLEDGMENT HUNGER (author-ratified session 12):** he WANTS success and fame — to be acknowledged, measured, remarked — the starved hunger of a boy parented through preparation ("you did not get remarks for doing what was expected"; "Again tomorrow" held like treasure). He climbs; he NEVER sandbags. THE COAT HIDES WHAT HE IS, NEVER THAT HE IS GOOD — an unstable split by design: his own want keeps poking holes in his own coat (the counter-drawer he cannot name yet: what NOT being seen costs). Success feeds both dangers — noble eyes (attention, later the harvest's screening) and peer resentment (the spiral, the Oss cooling, Lucan's arithmetic).
