- **MODEL-TIC SELF-AWARENESS (author-ratified 2026-08-20).** The prose is drafted with Claude, and Claude has default rhythms it falls into when not watching itself: standalone em-dashes, "not X but Y" constructions, sentence fragments deployed for gravity, triadic lists. None are banned — the book's voice already uses all of them — but every instance must be a CHOICE, not a default. While drafting, Claude self-flags passages where its own patterns are steering the sentence instead of the voice; revision passes report per-chapter tic counts (dashes, not-buts, fragments, triads — and, added session 12: AND-CHAINS ["and x and y because z" accumulations; and/1k as the gauge] and SIMILE DENSITY ["the way…"/"like" per 1k]) alongside the word count. The two session-12 additions are AWARENESS instruments, not thresholds (author ruling): chain-cadence and simile-dense passages are legitimate where chosen — the audit found them steering by default (37–58 and/1k, ~1 simile per 100 words, everywhere). Upward drift without intent = revision flag. The test is always: would this sentence survive being read aloud in the author's voice, or does it sound like a model being writerly?
- **MAGIC ON THE PAGE (Part I ceiling):** perception-first, non-lexical, craft register only. No mechanics, no terms ("the minima" arrives at Temple enrollment, Part II). Every magic lesson is double work — teaching AND leashing (00, Kynemir); the relief-tell is now SPENT — do not respend.
