# 06 — Style Sheet
**THE RULE SIDE — how the prose is written.** Invariant; amended in place. Everything here is a drafting instruction: **what the page HAS is `07-Story-Ledger/`, what a word MEANS is `02-World-Bible/`, what the book IS is `01-Premise-and-Pitch.md`** — 06 points at those, it never restates them. Genre: epic, gritty, historical-flavoured fantasy; intimate over spectacular, earned over destined. Spend state — slips, interludes and pan-aways used — is `07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md` §DEPARTURE LEDGER. Superseded text: `archive/style-sheet-book-one.md`.

## POV
- **Default, and the vast majority of every book:** close third limited, single POV (Kael). Inside a Kael-scene **the reader never knows more than he does, except by inference.**
- **Three sanctioned departures.** The count is the rule and it lives in the departure ledger.
  1. **PAN-AWAY** — external camera, established, exempt from the per-use argument and not counted. A short scene without Kael: **dialogue and observable action only, no interiority for anyone.** The cut-before-the-answer is the device — the reader overhears to the cliffhanger and never past it, and characters shifting into Avestan seal it in-world. Machinations MOVING, never sealed answers. Roman, never block-italic.
  2. **INTERLUDE / split-group bit** — argued per use, every time, and whose head it is and what it may hold are settled in that argument. A chapter or a bit, third limited in another head, adult-cold register from the first line, roman; flashbacks allowed for other characters. May be time-displaced if the reader is anchored in its first lines.
  3. **FIRST-PERSON SLIP, Kael only** — argued per use. A retrospective *I* at a critical emotional turn, **sentences and not scenes**, or a short block of them as a book's overture. **Set in italics.** It may carry future feeling, judgment and fact.
- **THE CONTENT FENCE, binding slips and the forward reach alike: it may not name an event, a place or a date the story has not yet paid for.** It may say *I looked for years*; it may not name what happens.
- **The narration may reach forward** and say that a thing was wrong, or would not be known for a long time. **This is not a slip** — no *I*, no italics, not counted. Sparing; the author's ear governs the rate.
- The telling-frame the slips imply stays DELIBERATELY UNRESOLVED. Do not commit to one without a dedicated session.

## The voice
**The gradient.** Early childhood: short sentences, concrete senses, literal child-logic. Middle: questions sharpen, sentences lengthen, pattern-noticing. Adolescence on: complexity, irony, self-awareness. **THE BLOOMING** — from Part II his wits bloom audibly as he grows: quicker in dialogue, drier and faster in thought, pattern-jumps arriving sooner. Intelligence made audible, not merely longer sentences.

**The instruments** — what the prose DOES, so a draft reaches for it on purpose instead of arriving as competent narration that then has to be stripped back. **Instruments, not a checklist: over-use of any one is itself a tic, and a paragraph built out of three at once is louder than the voice.**
- **THE FLAT DECLARATIVE THAT REFUSES ITS OWN DRAMA.** The biggest beats land in the shortest, plainest sentence available, and nothing follows — *He was paid.* · *Nothing after it.* The moment is left larger than the sentence.
- **DESCRIPTION BY ABSENCE** — *not broken into, not forced, gone* · *not one of them looked like anything.* Reach for the negative observation before the adjective.
- **THE NUMBER WHERE THE ADJECTIVE GOES** — *nine times the money* · *one word in four.* The narration's unit of measure.
- **THE UNATTRIBUTED COLLECTIVE** — *The fire moved. Nobody said anything.* How the book renders a crew, a village, a crowd.
- **THE GNOMIC PRESENT INSIDE PAST NARRATION** — *It came the way things come in a yard, which is sideways and in pieces.* The telling-frame briefly audible. Sparing.
- **DIALOGUE STRIPPED TO THE EXCHANGE** — tagless and adverbless where the speakers are obvious; the social fact carried by what is said, never glossed after.
- **THE REACTION RENDERED AS NON-REACTION** — *Kael's hands went on doing what they were doing*; or name the absence and stop.
- **REPETITION AS RHYTHM** — a clause returned to with one term changed, where another book would reach for an intensifier.
- **THE SCENE ENDS ON THE LINE.** Stop on the last spoken or observed thing and add nothing to land it.

## Never explain
**The engine of the book: the reader assembles it, and the narration does not help.**
- **Menace is what Kael notices and does not weigh.** What is wrong is presented as normal; the wrongness exists only in the reader's head, and the narration never names it.
- **THE WHAT/WHY SPLIT.** His physical reading is extraordinary; his social reading is not. He may detect the WHAT correctly — he's angry, she's lying, the room turned — and misread the WHY entirely: the motive, and whether a lie is cruel, protective, embarrassed, playful or kind. **Let him make real mistakes, on the page, uncorrected.** It is an ARC and not a fixed trait, and its pace is the author's, scene by scene. **Shown, never diagnosed** — the narration does not label it.
- **The causal chain stops at the first why.** Links that re-derive established canon get cut; trust the plant.
- **A naked statement of feeling is NOT banned.** A line naming what a character feels, in his own register, at a moment the book has already paid for, is allowed and is sometimes the right line. What is banned is an emotion-label standing in for the scene that would have earned it. Weigh each instance; never auto-fail one on a rule's name.

## What gets cut
**Two lists, one job: what comes out before the author sees it.**

**MY DEFAULT RHYTHMS.** Standalone em-dashes · *not X but Y* · fragments deployed for gravity · triadic lists · AND-CHAINS · default similes (*the way…* / *like*). **None are banned — the voice uses all of them — but every instance must be a CHOICE, not a default.** Self-flag while drafting wherever the pattern is steering the sentence instead of the voice. **Known default: first drafts run 51–55 and/1k uniformly across every section** — check and/1k PER SECTION at the end of every first draft, not per chapter. And-chains and simile density are awareness instruments, not thresholds; upward drift without intent is a flag.

**THE SUBTRACTIVE STANDARD** — named off the author's own passes, which have cut in one direction every time: narrated conclusions out, the observable action left.
- **THE GLOSS.** A stated fact is not followed by its meaning. **If the second sentence says what the first one meant, the second sentence is the cut.**
- **THE CALLBACK.** Narration reaching back to an earlier chapter for weight, parallel or irony is cut to a clause or to nothing. **Write the moment so it lands on its own**; the reader who remembers is paid without being told they remembered.
- **THE INSTRUMENT NAMED FOR FLAVOUR.** Kael's count, his pricing and his people-reading appear only when they do work in the scene.
- **THE INVENTORY.** Lists restating possessions or facts the reader is already holding.
- **THE CLEVER DEFERRAL AT THE PEAK.** When the thing the character most wants is on the table, **he asks for the thing itself.** Technique-flex at the emotional peak is the cut.
- **THE RITUAL ECHO.** A line answered by its own restatement, for ceremony.
- **THE COY WITHHOLDING.** A speaker declining to say a thing in order to be interesting.

**Metrics.** Revision passes report per-chapter tic counts beside the word count, plus **narration words and words-per-paragraph.** `wc -w` is a budget instrument and not a reading-experience one — a dialogue-heavy chapter carries far less prose bulk per word, so check narration bulk before calling a chapter long, and **never cut fast dialogue to protect a total.**

**The test, always:** would this sentence survive being read aloud in the author's voice, or does it sound like a model being writerly?

## People on the page
- **KAEL'S AXIS.** Intelligence, technique, fieldcraft and exceptional fighting — all of it his parents' teaching in him. **The deficiency is ONE axis:** keep a heavy, persistent emphasis on his lack of capacity, and on the world wrongly treating capacity as the measure. The world keeps saying the loud wrong thing about him; the page keeps showing the quiet true thing. **COUNTERWEIGHT — the critique is of capacity-WORSHIP as the sole measure, never of capacity, mass, leadership or social gravity themselves.** Those are real powers, honestly priced by Kael himself, and his lack of them is a REAL deficit, not a hidden virtue. Render the complementarity.
- **LOVE READS IN THE STITCHING OF THE WORK, not in declarations** — the hand before the ground; the lie that makes a horizon. A teaching scene that reads stern without detectable love is a revision flag.
- **AND THE HARSHNESS STANDS.** The teaching was at times result-oriented to the point of reader-discomforting harshness — procedural, calm, total; never rage, never cruelty for its own sake — and Kael normalised and defended it on the page, which made it worse rather than better. **The love is real AND the harm is real; neither cancels the other, and nothing resolves into reassurance** — grief does not sand the harm off the dead father, and a lesson remembered keeps its stitching and its cost. **If it resolves, revision flag.**
- **THE PARENTS' PAST REACHES THE PAGE AS ARCHAEOLOGY** — objects, scars, reflexes, other people's fear, other people's mouths — and Kael reads it the way he reads everything, as evidence, across the whole series.
- **RELATIONSHIP TIME IS LOAD-BEARING.** Every chapter carries at least one scene whose only job is a relationship moving — warmth, friction, play, pride, not plot and not clue-work — and it is allowed to BREATHE: real dialogue, real laughter that is not threat. **The fix is TIME, not sentiment.** A loss devastates in proportion to the warmth banked beforehand.
- **DIALOGUE AND INTERIORITY, MIXED.** Lessons in the teacher's actual voice, conflicts in the room's actual words, emotional conflict on the surface of scenes as well as under them. **Interiority is the seasoning and scene is the meal** — favour dialogue and real-time action over narrated summary wherever both can carry the beat. **Not a quota; an ear.** The instruction is HOLD, not push: a chapter that wants to be interior is allowed to be.
- **THE SUPPORTING CAST SHINES.** Every significant character gets moments that are THEIRS — voice, body, presence — and Kael is sometimes the camera, not the subject. Show, don't tell, with some tell allowed. Physical description comes from `03-Character-Bible/appearance-ledger.md`, filtered through his eye.
- **FIGHTS.** Clinical arithmetic and visceral reality at once, never heroic romanticism. **Kael wins by legible technique and decision-making, never by power-ups — the reader should understand every victory.** Immersive, fast cuts, the reader in it; relative distances, never surveyed ones. He knows what he is looking at: **write that, in the present** — not a justification of it, and not a callback to the boy he was.

## Register — the rule is elsewhere; 06 only points
- **SCIENCE:** a gradient, full rule in `02-World-Bible/curriculum.md`. **The drafting test — if a lesson reads MODERN, revision flag; if it reads merely precise, that is correct.** The ban is what a term DRAGS IN (notation, units, an apparatus this world has not built), never how modern the word sounds.
- **MAGIC:** perception-first — what Kael feels, prices and counts, in his own register, before any doctrine; the world's model reaches the page **in the mouths of the people who hold it, never as narration explaining.** `02-World-Bible/magic.md`, `lexicon.md`.
- **NAMES** carry sociology; commoners lack surnames. `02-World-Bible/naming.md`; blocked and available names: `07-Story-Ledger/established-facts.md`.
- **Sealed truths on the page are STATE, not staging:** how a spend reached the page is 07's, and what a page may not yet point at is 00's.

## Things to avoid
Info-dumps · POV breaks · characters as plot functions (01 §Character Law) · hidden-puppet-master vibes for the leader, who stays human, limited and doctrine-driven.
