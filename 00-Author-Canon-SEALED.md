## THE ENDING (series destination)
- **The Requiem:** The everlasting darkness is not a faction — it is the WAR ITSELF, the self-feeding cycle of atrocity between empire and coalition (and the rot of power behind both). Kael ends it by becoming the final enemy of the world: he deliberately becomes something so monstrous that a divided world unites against him, and in uniting, remakes itself. He sacrifices himself. The world never learns the truth of what he did.
- **The witness:** Valeria Vel is the one person who knows the truth. The reader knows; the world does not.
- Reference tone: Code Geass — Zero Requiem. Bittersweet; the world becomes as he envisioned; he is remembered as the monster.
- The series' central question — "can he become dangerous without becoming them?" — is answered: he becomes WORSE than them, on purpose, as the final act of love.

## THE PARENTS

### Their true history
- **THE ASYMMETRY (sealed):** the bloodline descends through the MOTHER. She was an ASSET — a harvested bloodline child, both livestock and weapon. The father was an INSTRUMENT — a soldier the program forged. He was made; she was TAKEN. The program's return is aimed at her lineage — and now her sons.
- Both were instruments of an ancient imperial PROGRAM that breeds/harvests children of a rare bloodline.
- **Names as rebellion:** instruments and assets had DESIGNATIONS, not names. "Vask" and "Neris" are names they chose when the master placed them on the island — possibly the first things they ever chose. There are no "real" names beneath them for Kael's archaeology to find. Naming their sons was therefore an enormous act. (Reserved beat: someone from the program addressing one of them by designation.)
- **"Neris" (sealed nuance):** passes as village-plain but belongs to no register; villagers assume it foreign. It may be a sound from wherever the bloodline actually comes from — the one thing she kept. Deliberately unresolved; braids toward the far-continent/origin-of-magic thread. Do not explain.
- They did not merely flee — they REBELLED: killed their handlers, burned the program's apparatus, and escaped believing they had destroyed it entirely.
- Their island life was lived in the belief that the enemy was DEAD. The rules and drills were residual discipline, not active fear. This is why they dared to have children.
- They were wrong. The program rebuilt itself in secret during their years of peace.
- Their shame is double: what they did as instruments, and that they believed it was over and stopped watching.
- Thematic rhyme: their uprising was the failed first draft of Kael's Requiem — they destroyed an institution; he will destroy the CONDITIONS that grow institutions.

### The Doctrine of Cultivated Inheritance
- The program holds that capability passes BOTH by blood and by rearing: a bloodline child raised by cultivated minds becomes more than either alone. Partially true (genetics + upbringing) — but the program holds it as doctrine.
- Consequence: assets and instruments were EDUCATED, not merely trained — sciences, languages, letters. **Neris's erudition is program-made.** Her learning is itself something that was done to her.
- The dark rhyme: teaching Kael, out of love, she unknowingly completes the program's own design — cultivated parent producing cultivated child. "They rebuilt the academy that unmade them" is literal.
- Kael is the doctrine's PROOF: bred from their finest asset, raised by two of their finest minds. This sharpens the harvest logic — the program doesn't just want his blood, it wants what the blood-plus-rearing produced.
- Kael's defining curiosity (canonized Ch. 3) is the trait the doctrine prizes and the Temple will recognize.
- **Ch. 5 enactment (the tail eaten):** her curriculum killed her own cover story ("what fire eats" → the sea can never freeze), and his training built his escape (stillness, count, standing game, star-navigation all deployed at the crossing). The cultivated inheritance eats its own tail — on the page now, unmarked.

## MAGIC — DEEP LAYER (sealed)
*The mechanical truth of magic. Never on-page in Part I; surfaces in fragments from Part II (Temple teaching = the empire's approximation); full truth is series-scale.*

- **The particle:** matter is composed of ordinary substance AND a second, finer order of particle — the particles of magic, present in all matter, ambient in the world.
- **Empire/Temple scholarly term (CANON): "the minima"** — the smallest grains of the world. Register: real pre-modern natural philosophy (minima naturalia); the Temple's science is honest, ancient, learned — and incomplete. On-page from Part II enrollment (Kael learns the word at the Temple). NEVER in Part I.
  - Term released for collision: "magicule" (established vocabulary in *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*). Mechanic retained in full; word replaced.
  - Banked: "vessence" (Vessantine + essence) — NOT the scholars' term; available later as courtly/propaganda flourish ("the vessence of the blood") if wanted.
  - Neris's craft register never uses any scholarly term — hers is felt-only vocabulary (the carrying, the lazy place); Part I stays entirely non-lexical.
- **The mechanism — INFECTION:** every human carries a personal reserve of these particles. Magic is the act of infecting the ambient particles around you with your own, which changes the nature of ordinary matter — air becomes fire, lightning is spawned, earth is summoned and shaped. All elemental magic is one mechanism: transmutation via infection.
- **The elements are a taxonomy of OUTCOMES, not of magic:** "fire mage" = someone whose infection most readily produces the fire-transmutation. The six elements classify the six common RESULTS, mistaken by the known world for six kinds of magic. The model works — predicts, trains, wins wars — and is wrong about what magic is. The empire's political lie (bloodline doctrine) sits ON TOP of a deeper honest scientific error. Whoever understands the mechanism rather than the taxonomy plays a different game (far-continent thread).
- **Attunement:** the particles can be FELT if one is trained and tuned to them, with varying degrees of sensitivity. Attunement is trainable in nearly everyone but almost never self-discovers — untrained, the sense stays dormant; flickers are dismissed as luck or superstition. This is the mechanical truth beneath the pedagogy monopoly (02): the empire doesn't suppress mages, it owns the only tuning school. Neris's perception-first curriculum teaches the TRUE layer — feel before frame.
- **Capacity = the reserve.** The size of one's personal store of particles determines raw potential (bigger fire, larger air slash). **Control = economy of infection** — how few particles you spend, how exactly you place them. The untrained flood a region to force a change; the technician infects the one point that cascades. "Everything heavy is lazy" is a LITERAL magical law: every structure has its load-bearing lazy place, and exact address there is nearly free. The standing game was always part of the magic curriculum; Neris knows this.
- **Drain (the cost):** magic spends the reserve; it is never infinite. The reserve replenishes (rate = a hidden axis; keep vague). The gradient: spending is effort → deep spending is PAIN → spending near the bottom MARKS you: **the hair whitens, permanently, in proportion to how close to empty the mage went** → true zero is DEATH.
  - The whitening never grows back dark. The body keeps the receipt. Any old mage's hair is a readable ledger: how many times, how deep. (Severin's grey acquires a second possible reading — never confirm.)
  - **The world's two body-marks of magic:** RED EYES (Kynemir unleashing — legend known, lineage lost) and WHITE HAIR (drain — the mark of having gone too far down). The populace knows and garbles both. Seedable cheaply: a white-streaked master nobody asks about; village talk of witch-white. **THE VESSANTINE SILVER (author ruling, s33) — a THIRD register, and it is NOT a mark.** The ruling house runs to a hereditary silver-fair; it is blood, not drain. **The drain-mark's register is PURE QUARTZ WHITE**, distinct in kind — the image the page already planted in Ruthen's seam, *like quartz through rock*. The folk registers garble all three, which is the point: the empire's own house wears something adjacent to the mark the folklore fears, and nobody says so where it can be heard. **CONSEQUENCE, undesigned:** a Vessantine is identifiable on sight, hereditarily and permanently — including one who needs not to be.

### Minima vs. the deeper structure (sealed)
