## MAGIC — DEEP LAYER (sealed — decided 2026-08-09)
*The mechanical truth of magic. Never on-page in Part I; surfaces in fragments from Part II (Temple teaching = the empire's approximation); full truth is series-scale.*

- **The particle:** matter is composed of ordinary substance AND a second, finer order of particle — the particles of magic, present in all matter, ambient in the world.
- **Empire/Temple scholarly term (CANON): "the minima"** — the smallest grains of the world. Register: real pre-modern natural philosophy (minima naturalia); the Temple's science is honest, ancient, learned — and incomplete. On-page from Part II enrollment (Kael learns the word at the Temple). NEVER in Part I.
  - Term released for collision: "magicule" (established vocabulary in *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*). Mechanic retained in full; word replaced.
  - Banked: "vessence" (Vessantine + essence) — NOT the scholars' term; available later as courtly/propaganda flourish ("the vessence of the blood") if wanted.
  - Neris's craft register never uses any scholarly term — hers is felt-only vocabulary (the carrying, the lazy place); Part I stays entirely non-lexical.
- **The mechanism — INFECTION:** every human carries a personal reserve of these particles. Magic is the act of infecting the ambient particles around you with your own, which changes the nature of ordinary matter — air becomes fire, lightning is spawned, earth is summoned and shaped. All elemental magic is one mechanism: transmutation via infection.
- **The elements are a taxonomy of OUTCOMES, not of magic:** "fire mage" = someone whose infection most readily produces the fire-transmutation. The six elements classify the six common RESULTS, mistaken by the known world for six kinds of magic. The model works — predicts, trains, wins wars — and is wrong about what magic is. The empire's political lie (bloodline doctrine) sits ON TOP of a deeper honest scientific error. Whoever understands the mechanism rather than the taxonomy plays a different game (far-continent thread).
- **Attunement:** the particles can be FELT if one is trained and tuned to them, with varying degrees of sensitivity. Attunement is trainable in nearly everyone but almost never self-discovers — untrained, the sense stays dormant; flickers are dismissed as luck or superstition. This is the mechanical truth beneath the pedagogy monopoly (02): the empire doesn't suppress mages, it owns the only tuning school. Neris's perception-first curriculum teaches the TRUE layer — feel before frame.
- **Capacity = the reserve.** The size of one's personal store of particles determines raw potential (bigger fire, larger air slash). **Control = economy of infection** — how few particles you spend, how exactly you place them. The untrained flood a region to force a change; the technician infects the one point that cascades. "Everything heavy is lazy" is a LITERAL magical law: every structure has its load-bearing lazy place, and exact address there is nearly free. The standing game was always part of the magic curriculum; Neris knows this.
- **Drain (the cost):** magic spends the reserve; it is never infinite. The reserve replenishes (rate = a hidden axis; keep vague). The gradient: spending is effort → deep spending is PAIN → spending near the bottom MARKS you: **the hair whitens, permanently, in proportion to how close to empty the mage went** → true zero is DEATH.
  - The whitening never grows back dark. The body keeps the receipt. Any old mage's hair is a readable ledger: how many times, how deep. (Severin's grey acquires a second possible reading — never confirm.)
  - **The world's two body-marks of magic:** RED EYES (Kynemir unleashing — legend known, lineage lost) and WHITE HAIR (drain — the mark of having gone too far down). The populace knows and garbles both. Seedable cheaply: a white-streaked master nobody asks about; village talk of witch-white.
  - Banked door (not a plan): a late-saga scene where Kael's hair whitens in the open — the cost arriving before anyone speaks, for every reader who has held the rule since Book One.
- **KYNEMIR — the mechanism (sealed):** ordinary magic infects to TRANSMUTE — it changes what things are. Kynemir infection does not transmute anything: it commands MOTION directly — it moves the particles themselves, nature unchanged. Everyone else changes what things are; Kynemir changes WHERE things are.
  - This is why it fits no element (the elements classify transmutation outcomes; there is no transmutation), why the taxonomy cannot hold it, and why it reads as world-breaking: no medium, no negotiation — force applied to matter as such.
  - The knot (Ch. 4): nothing changed its nature. It simply MOVED, a whole breath early. The purest first expression.
  - DELIBERATELY UNSEALED: whether Kynemir blood carries a different particle or a different KIND of infection. Origin-of-magic territory; braids into the far continent.
- **Kael's profile under the model:** a small reserve spent with near-perfect economy. His low-capacity/high-control technician style is the DEEP-TRUTH-FAVORED profile in a capacity-worshipping world. His whole style is the truth of magic, hiding in plain sight.
- **Deniable adjacency (logged, not sealed):** Neris's language pedagogy (attention, sound, "the ends of the languages") may sit near magic's grammar. Never explain. Gives the pale-making bird-word somewhere to live.
