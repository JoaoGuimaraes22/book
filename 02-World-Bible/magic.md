## MAGIC (system canon — public and page-facing layers; deep layer in 00)
Laws, not a ledger: how each reached the page is in the chapter records and `../archive/world-bible-book-one.md`. Kael's own profile has one home — `../03-Character-Bible/kael.md` (STATIC → Abilities).

### The model
- Six elements — FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIGHTNING, NATURE — the known world's MODEL of magic, not necessarily its truth; origin and nature stay a world-level mystery (00). The mother's way is pedagogy beneath the elements — perception-first, threads, structure, cost — deniably closer to the deeper grammar.
- The Temple teaches a particulate theory: magic moves through THE MINIMA — the smallest grains of the world, present in all matter ("as a struck string wakes its silent neighbor" — Priscian's delivery). "The minima" is a COMMON word. The Temple's science is old, learned, honest — and incomplete (00; the "smallest grains" claim is its honest error — Kael shelves it untested, Ch. 10). Neris may use the word from Ch. 10 on; she never handed it to Kael in Part I — vocabulary withheld is the rationing ("the word was not hers," Ch. 8).
- THE LIE (public doctrine): only select bloodlines can be Resonants. False — an old imperial lie preserving the royal-noble power dynamic (00).
- Enforcement — the pedagogy monopoly: attunement stays dormant untaught; flickers are dismissed as luck or superstition. The empire does not suppress commoner Resonants — it is simply the only teacher. Anomalies absorb as *noble blood somewhere in the line*; the lie self-seals.

### Structure
- Proficiency: every Resonant works all elements at varying talent and is extremely more proficient in ONE (proficiency = far more capacity there); most work almost nothing outside it. Rare: two or three proficiencies. Kael's many-element small-scale creativity is a future weapon because the world does not fight that way.
- Capacity vs. control: separate axes — raw output vs. precision, threading, creativity (mechanics in 00).
- Declaration: the dominant element settles with maturity and training (soft window ~12–14; "when it settles it settles"). Early magic is small and formless for most, but high capacity TINTS early (Aurelian's wax-sheet fire, Ch. 9) — capacity-worship's pageantry is built on this.
- Drain (public; taught Ch. 9 in Ruthen's cost register — spending, the purse, a grain): the Art exhausts and is never infinite; deep drain is painful; drain near the bottom whitens the hair PERMANENTLY ("witch-white" in folk talk; "The body kept the receipt"); full drain kills, every time, however beloved the house. An old Resonant's hair is a readable ledger. **THE MARK'S REGISTER IS PURE QUARTZ WHITE** (author, s33 — the page's image for Ruthen's seam, *like quartz through rock*). **The Vessantine silver is NOT a mark** — hereditary house colouring (00): silver-fair is blood, quartz-white is a receipt; folk talk garbles the two and the empire lets it. The two body-marks in their clean registers: white hair = taught science by day; red eyes = fireside legend by dark; village talk garbles both. **A whitening may happen on the page as an EVENT** (Neris, Ch. 32 — the manuscript's first, out of the roots, watched by a boy who can price it to the grain); nobody on the page says what it means. **No new white can show on a man already grey all through** — Severin's large workings cost him his body instead (crooked, the nose bleeding), and the never-confirm rule on his grey protects itself, unremarked.

### Laws that govern every element
- **THE DISTANCE LAW** — the proximity law's page face (Ch. 11; mechanism 00 §THE PROXIMITY LAW): the Art is spent FROM the body — anywhere on it; hands are the habit. Distance and disconnection raise the cost; remote manifestation is an advanced mark. A continuous medium counts as connection (Kael's boot-thread through the ground). Fire spent at the point and moment of contact — off knee, heel and knuckle — is legal under it, and is the coalition's answer to the empire's one-handed grammar: nothing is reserved for the Art because everything is the Art (Pellan, Ch. 30/33; `factions.md`).
- **THE ART DOES WHAT YOU UNDERSTAND** (Ch. 10). The Art alone is worth very little: a Resonant who does not know which way the bone goes will push it the wrong way. Healing needs the Art AND the medical knowledge in one head, and very few carry both (Severin does — `../03-Character-Bible/severin.md`; why is in 00). The same law lets a working be run OUT OF PROFICIENCY by someone who carries the other half in the same head — it works, and it works BADLY, and the difference is rendered as contrast, never explained (Neris mending ribs with nature, Ch. 32, against the First Master's mending two mornings earlier that showed nothing).
- **CAPACITY IS NOT THE EXPLANATION FOR THE GAP (author, s25).** A program-forged elite is better than Kael at BOTH axes; a large purse means only that the master's errors would cost nothing, and he does not make them. An adult master's superiority over Kael is control, not raw purse; Kael's control is exceptional *for a boy of thirteen in this world*, not in absolute terms. **THE DEFICIT IS REAL:** on a shelf where four Resonants held a hillside he read every working perfectly and could perform none of them — line-reinforcement is two fingers wide and his capacity was empty — and the page did not soften it (`../06-Style-Sheet.md` §KAEL'S DEFINING AXIS, and its counterweight).
- **SCALE AND COMBINATION.** Workings combine across Resonants: one lights every separate thing on three hundred strides of hillside at once, exactly where the fuel is, and another's wind makes the four hundred fires one front (Ch. 34) — **technique over purse, twice, and then scale on top of it**, which is the book's thesis in one page. The scale of the PROBLEM, not the size of the working, is what escalates: at saturation a Resonant stops choosing and takes whatever arrives, in the order it arrives (Ch. 35).
- **NOBODY THEORISES.** No page has explained WHY a working works (`../06-Style-Sheet.md`: *the causal chain stops at the first why*); no technique below has been named as a school on any page; Priscian has none of it; Kael reads what he sees and joins nothing up. Where a page renders an absence — a Resonant who cannot reach — it renders a body standing still and says nothing whatever about why.
- **PAINTED IRON STOPS THE ART (Ch. 39).** Iron cuffs with black *put there* in the grooves, worn and renewed — *like ink in the cuts of a letter*; in them the smallest thread Kael has gets no answer, *for the first time in his life* — and Valeria the same (*I thought it was the blow*). **Kael's inference is the page's whole theory:** *Iron alone does not stop the Art. His own Art went into iron better than into anything. It must be that black paint.* — the medium law run backwards in his own head. Nobody names it, nobody says what it is or where a southern governor got it; the box was *built for Resonants* and had carried people before. What the black is: 00. **IT COMES OFF WITH A CHISEL, AND THE ART COMES STRAIGHT BACK (Ch. 44):** the pin is peened both sides, so a smith cuts the peen off the head, drifts the pin out and the band opens — about an hour a band with a living arm inside it, because he cannot swing and must tap and set and tap, and there is no quiet way to do it. **The moment the iron is off, the Art answers** — for all three of them, after seventy days. **The iron itself is very good iron**, better than a village smith can make, and **he does not know what the black in the grooves is either**; it is not rust and not any paint he knows. Nobody theorises it. **WHAT SEVENTY DAYS WITHOUT IT WAS, IN THE ONE MOUTH THAT SAID SO (Ch. 45):** for Valeria the ground-sense returning is *the whole of that wall … every stone in it, where the weight of it went, and which one of them the whole thing is standing on* — and its absence was *as if the Art was something like my eyesight, or my sense of smell, that I suddenly had lost. As if I had lost a part of the world. And perhaps a part of myself too.* Kael's answer is his mother's — that knowledge makes you see the world differently, and some ways of seeing are one person's only. **Nobody else has said what the seventy days were, and the page does not.**

### Lightning — the threading family
- **THE THREADING (author, s25).** NOT one resonant thread laid into a blade: **MANY** lightning threads held in tight parallel, **accelerated and CONTAINED**, run the length of the steel and kept there under load; the difficulty is holding the whole bundle exact through impact, out of a purse that will not fill a cup. **Page-facing:** *"spend a thread"* stays the general idiom for one act of spending, in narration and every mouth; the page renders the OBSERVED thing — *a thread*, *the stitch*, *a stitch of white down its length* (Ch. 14) — and **the bundle is not enumerated, counted or explained on the page.** **WHAT IT DOES, AND WHAT IT IS NOT (author, s37):** it **augments the cutting power of whatever it coats** — steel or the bare hand — and **cuts workings**. It is **NOT an electrical effect on a body**: it does not shock, convulse or seize a man. **IT IS VISIBLE** — white, running the length of the medium, and the page shows it; the thing Kael controls is whether he holds it or lets it go at the knock. **Consequences:** the BURST is proportionally worse (the whole bundle comes home when exactness fails); holding **two** threadings is a categorically larger problem than one, not a doubled one.
- **THE ROUTE, AND HOW IT IS TAUGHT (Vask, Ch. 21):** shoulder → arm → hand → steel, fast and tight to the body the whole way with nothing coming off it — hence total awareness of the body at once, and why a man who has lost track of his own elbow cannot hold it. Steel down first; thread with bare hands; take up the blades only once both hands hold and the feet are known — by then it runs out into the steel because there is nowhere else for it to go.
- **THE BURST** (Ch. 13 — the failure mode): a thread that loses exactness under load (impact, shock, divided attention) does not die — it discharges INTO ITS SOURCE. Margin is a function of capacity: a large purse absorbs the slop; a small purse pays in its own flesh; burn scales with thread size. The speckle-burns are ORDINARY burns — never a magical body-mark. Page canon, Vask: "I can be wrong by a finger's width. The purse forgives me. Nothing forgives you."
- **THE MEDIUM LAW — METAL TAKES THE THREAD (Ch. 15):** lightning enters METAL far more readily than wood. Wood must be persuaded and holds a thread only under the Resonant's own exactness; steel accepts one unasked and carries it WHOLE, hilt to point, no thinner for spreading. Gift and cost are one property: THE BURST through steel arrives everywhere at once and burns proportionally worse — "the same size thread in a better road." Page canon, Vask: "Wood forgives you. It bounces, it bends, and it lies to you about what you did. This does not lie." Deep-layer consistency: substances differ in minima-nature, readable by feel (00). Banked: the endgame kit's lightning-through-steel is an AFFINITY DISCOVERED, not a difficulty overcome.
- **A THREADING CUTS A WORKING (Ch. 26; author, s30).** A blade carrying the threading, driven through a fire working, **divides it** — not extinguished, not absorbed: *it did not go out. It broke* — split along the line the steel took, the halves closing on empty air behind the man who went through them. **Scope, held narrow:** the threading (the contained bundle, never a single thread), through a medium that carries it whole, against a working being *paid for continuously* rather than one that has landed. **THE HAND IS ALSO A MEDIUM:** blades sheathed, the threading runs shoulder → arm → hand and stops there, and the fire comes apart on the hand as on the blade. **The page does not explain why it works**; nobody theorises it; Priscian has not been handed it. **The cost is total:** this AND line-reinforcement in the same instant (Ch. 26's *two at once*) empties Kael of every grain in about a fifth of a second, and it has never been cheap.
- **THE EVOLVED THREADING — THE INWARD ART IN LIGHTNING (author, s39; SEALED MECHANISM, page-facing observation only).** Vask's whole-body form is not more threading; it is **the threading combined with the inward Art** — the same move as internal reinforcement, run in LIGHTNING through an entire body at once. **The mechanism is in 00 (sealed — author, s39: bible-only until the world can hold it). The toll is enormous** and scales with how long it is held. **THE REGISTER RULE APPLIES IN FULL:** this world has no physiology for it, so the page has no impulse, nerve or muscle-signal in it, and no term implying an apparatus this world has not built (`curriculum.md` §Register rule — the one permanent ban). **WHAT THE PAGE HAS:** the white in the whole of him, held, *not coming off*; *It was not going along him. It was going into him*; the same shape as a girl's Art spent inward, in the wrong element, in a whole body at once; the speed rendered as ABSENCE — *Not blurred. Not quick. Absent* (Ch. 30). **AT FULL SCALE THE HANDS CUT** (Ch. 33): steel does not slow it and mail does not slow it — a flat hand through the side of a helmet, an open palm taking an arm off at the shoulder — consistent with the hand-as-medium and augment-what-it-coats rules above; the sword he gave away was never the weapon, and nobody says so. **CONTROL, NOT PURSE** (author, s25): it is not a large reserve. **Kael got half of it and wants the rest**; where that goes, and the road to it, are in 00 (his state: `../03-Character-Bible/kael.md` §FORWARD).
- **OPEN — THE BIND (Kael's untested hypothesis, Ch. 15):** if metal is a road rather than a container, the road need not stop where his blade stops; in a bind, steel touches steel, and a continuous medium is a connection. Written in the blank book, declined twice, never attempted. NOT canon until spent on the page.

### Earth
- **INTERNAL REINFORCEMENT** (Ch. 14 — advanced): resonance spent INWARD into the Resonant's own body — nothing leaves it; limbs strengthened and hardened, weight, force and speed multiplied (a flagstone split under one step). Capacity-hungry. **NOT A VEL SECRET (author, s24):** a KNOWN earth technique, available to any earth Resonant with the capacity and schooling; what the Vel own is the DEGREE — eleven generations of breeding and drilling — and that gap is real and not closeable by cleverness. The bloodline fence is intact (motion still from muscle). Page render observational only ("Whatever she had spent, she had spent it inward"); no doctrine delivered. **TWO SCALES (author, s37).** HERS is a whole limb or a whole body — capacity-hungry, and what lets a thirteen-year-old girl throw grown men about. **KAEL'S IS A LINE**: he cannot buy an arm, so he buys the exact line the force folds along, about two fingers wide, and **the constraint is AREA, not cost** — at that size it is cheap and repeatable, and it does NOT empty him (the s24 *empties him every time / not twice in a day* clause is RETIRED). The untrained flood a region; the technician takes the one point (why is in 00). His version is not a weaker flood; it is a different move at a different scale.
- **EARTH OPENS GROUND BY SUBTRACTION, NOT BY DIGGING (Ch. 45).** Both hands flat on the ground and left there; then the grass goes all at once over an area about the size of a door, and the earth under it **does not fly anywhere and does not lift** — it goes down and sideways at the same time, *the way sand goes when you pull the bottom out of it*, packing itself into the sides of the hole it is making. It makes almost no noise, it takes a long time, and **there is no spoil anywhere**; the sides come out harder than the ground around them. The same law as the terrace (holding is the expensive direction, releasing the cheap one), turned to making a hole a man's height deep. **It is capacity-hungry:** at less than full strength Valeria priced herself at *three properly, or four badly and no use to you tomorrow*, and sat down for a quarter of an hour after the third. Nobody theorises it.
- **VALERIA — EARTH BOTH WAYS (Ch. 16/17).** Super-advanced in earth spent OUTWARD as well as inward; internal reinforcement is her SPECIALTY — degree, not possession. Ch. 14/15's "nothing left her body" is Kael's observation of one duel, not a law about her. **She cannot have the threading:** it is a LIGHTNING technique — she has taken it on the arm a dozen times and watched for it slower, and none of that is a road to doing it; seeing a technique is not acquiring one; proficiency is where the capacity lives (the same law as AIR, below).
- **EARTH RAISED, NOT ONLY TAKEN AWAY (Ch. 49).** The same outward earth run the other direction: a hand flat on a beaten floor, and **a column about as thick through as a man comes up out of the ground** and stops at her shoulder. **No noise, nothing flying anywhere, nothing coming off her**, and the earth for three feet round the foot of it goes down by about the width of a thumb — the ground it is made of is the ground it came out of. Nobody theorises it. **BY CH. 49 THE ART IS FULLY BACK ON BOTH OF THEM (author, s70):** Ch. 45's *my Art is still returning to me Kael* is discharged, the pillar costs her nothing the page shows, and Kael holds a threading down a borrowed pen-knife twice in a row at about what a hard breath costs.
- **THE GROUND-SENSE (Ch. 16 — the Vel school, taught not gifted): IT IS A SORT OF PROJECTION (author, s69).** Art spent out of her into the stone she is standing on and read back off it — the same family as Kael's boot-thread into the ground, and legal under the DISTANCE LAW, where a continuous medium counts as connection. She reads what it carries — weight, gait, position. **The old *a reading, never a projection · nothing leaves her body · perception only* clause is STRUCK by the ruling**; Ch. 14's *nothing left her body* was Kael watching one duel of internal reinforcement and is untouched. **The bloodline fence still holds, for a different reason:** this is ordinary earth work under the distance law, available to any earth Resonant with the schooling, and what the Vel own is the degree. **ITS LIMITS ARE CONSEQUENCES OF THE SPENDING:** SHORT RANGE · INTENTIONAL ONLY, never passive · EXPENSIVE — paid in slower guards and lost meals. A fighting instrument, not surveillance: Kael watched her fifteen mornings from the treeline unfound and was caught only when he came inside it while she was drilling. **ON THE PAGE AT CH. 47:** a hand flat on the boards of a room over a mule yard — *Her Art flowed through the floor and walls* — giving two bodies above her and what each of them was doing; not long, not while asleep, and she would rather nobody in that city knew she could do it at all.
- **THE BLIND SPOT (Ch. 16 — Kael's counter, UNANSWERED):** the sense depends on continuity, so the counter breaks it — a thread of earth into the stone as his weight lands — not to move it, only to trouble the grain under the boot — so the step dies where it stands. Costly to the point of collapse (six threads in six seconds out of the smallest purse on the island), good for a few strides. She diagnosed it in four days and could not answer it: the floor can only report what the floor is doing. Kael's first win off her; it stands.
- **AIR — THE SECOND SENSE (unbuilt):** the answer to the blind spot is a medium that is not the floor — air touches everything, and a moving body leaves a shape in it. Both senses together would make her a far better reader than either alone. Valeria named it and cannot have it — "years and a school that does not exist and an element that is not mine." Not on any page beyond her naming it; she does not have it.
- **HOLDING IS THE EXPENSIVE DIRECTION; RELEASING IS THE CHEAP ONE.** *Everything heavy is lazy* is a literal magical law (00). Its two page faces are one law: an earth Resonant who could hold one burning stone in the air over four hundred children and could not hold two (Ch. 27) can reach along the ground into the one place under an entire flank where all of it is already leaning and **take it away** — four hundred feet of terrace goes down with everything standing on it, and nothing gets lifted (Ch. 33). **Nobody on the page says so.**

### Air
- **AIR AT SCALE CUTS; IT IS NOT CONCUSSIVE (author, s48).** A column throws a man into a wall and a dish sweeps dust (Lucan, the ceremony); at mass scale the same element **cuts** — ground taken bald for eighty strides *as clean as a scythe cut*, trunks split down the grain into strips that carry through a hundred men without slowing, and then, spent directly, men coming apart at the joints of the body (Neris, Ch. 34) — and, turned the other way, **takes things down instead of apart**: what is thrown at a boat is caught and put into the sea, each piece taking a piece of the sea up with it (Ch. 35). **THE WORKING MAKES NO NOISE; THE MEN DO** — nothing comes off her, nothing crosses the air, nothing on the shelf moves (author, s48: *silence does not match the violence*). **ON THE PAGE:** her face is not shown during a working of that size · nobody theorises it and nobody names it · **this is her ELEMENT and not the bloodline**, and nothing on the page joins the two · **it is NOT her ceiling (author, s48)**: Kael prices her to the grain and finds no bottom, and what she is is Book Two's (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md`). A killing volume of air at ten feet, with no visible part to it, is a FIRE Resonant's (Thiro; `factions.md`), and its one counter is that he cannot spend it on air he is standing in the middle of.

### Nature
- **NATURE IS THE ART OF MENDING (Ch. 19)** — the healing Art: most of what a healer does badly with a hot iron and a needle, a skilled artist does properly, and only in a head that also holds the medicine (the law above). **NATURE'S OTHER FACE IS LIVING WOOD (author, s48):** the same element drives what GROWS — root, vine, thorn and whole standing wood, up through solid rock, laced and thickening at the crossings and putting out fresh growth on the far side of itself (eleven feet of it closing a cut in half a minute, Ch. 34). **Mending is the same element turned on flesh** — which is why the man who knows which way the bone goes is also the man who can do this. **The page explains none of it and nobody theorises it.** Large nature workings cost the body (Severin gets up crooked with the nose bleeding) or the hair (Neris's two marks, Ch. 32).

- **THE SOUTH MENDS WITHOUT THE ART (Ch. 37):** oils, a needle, strapping — an old woman's hands that know where things are on a person without looking, and the First Master's *She is good.* Kael's ribs, mended wrong out of the wrong element, were found wrong in four seconds and strapped without a question.
### Fire
- **FIVE ARTS, NO TWO THE SAME, NONE A SCHOOL.** The coalition's five fires — contact-spent (PELLAN) · mass in standing sheets (GALLOS) · fire put INTO ground so that ground can never be crossed again (DUNMAR) · a finger-thick line carried out of one hand that DIVIDES a working rather than opposing it (FENNA) · a volume of air at ten feet with no visible part to it (THIRO) — have their one home in `factions.md` §The Coalition and `../03-Character-Bible/coalition-generals.md`. **None is named as a school and none is theorised on any page.** Fenna's division is the same law as the threading's — a line through a working paid for continuously splits it — and she is the only one of the five built the way Kael is built; nothing on any page says so. **Over water two of them can reach a hull and two cannot** (the ground-fire has no ground; the volume has no ten feet), and **no page explains either half** (`../03-Character-Bible/coalition-generals.md` §THE WATER).
- **A FIRE RESONANT IS A STOVE (Ch. 37).** Fire held in an open palm under a stone jar — on nothing, feeding on nothing, the size of a fist and the colour of a forge — two hours at a stretch, daily for a month, on biscuit and figs: Aurelian in the mornings, Elarine in the evenings, hers smaller, steadier and hotter. It empties them for the day and they lie down; **no mark on either** (author, s55). A gallon on a good day for eight people, and it kept them alive. Nobody theorises it.
