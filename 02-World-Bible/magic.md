## MAGIC (system canon — public and page-facing layers; deep layer in 00)

### The model
- Six elements — FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIGHTNING, NATURE — as far as the known world knows. The taxonomy is the world's MODEL of magic, not necessarily its truth; origin and nature stay a world-level mystery (far-continent braid, 00). The mother's way is pedagogy beneath the elements — perception-first, threads, structure, cost — deniably closer to the deeper grammar.
- The Temple teaches a particulate theory: magic moves through THE MINIMA — the smallest grains of the world, present in all matter ("as a struck string wakes its silent neighbor" — Priscian's delivery). "The minima" is a COMMON word, the world's everyday name for the fine grains. The Temple's science is old, learned, honest — and incomplete (00; the "smallest grains" claim is its honest error — Kael shelves it untested, Ch. 10). Neris may use the word from Ch. 10 on; she never handed it to Kael in Part I — vocabulary withheld is the rationing ("the word was not hers," Ch. 8).
- THE LIE (public doctrine): only select bloodlines can be Resonants. False — an old imperial lie preserving the royal-noble power dynamic (00).
- Enforcement — the pedagogy monopoly: magic almost never surfaces untrained; attunement stays dormant without teaching; flickers dismiss as luck or superstition. The empire doesn't suppress commoner Resonants — it is simply the only teacher; the Temple is the fence around fire. Anomalies absorb as "noble blood somewhere in the line" — the lie self-seals.

### Structure
- Proficiency: every Resonant works all elements at varying talent and is always extremely more proficient in ONE (proficiency = much more capacity in that element); most work almost nothing outside it — Kael's many-element small-scale creativity is a future weapon precisely because the world doesn't fight that way. Rare: two or even three proficiencies.
- Capacity vs. control: separate axes — raw output vs. precision, threading, creativity (mechanics in 00).
- Declaration: the dominant element settles with maturity and training (soft window ~12–14; "when it settles it settles"). Early magic is small and formless for most, but high capacity TINTS early (Aurelian's wax-sheet fire, Ch. 9); capacity-worship's pageantry is built on exactly this.
- Drain (public; taught on-page Ch. 9 in Ruthen's cost register — spending, the purse, a grain): the art exhausts and is never infinite; deep drain is painful; drain near the bottom whitens the hair PERMANENTLY ("witch-white" in folk talk; "the body keeps the receipt"); full drain kills, every time, however beloved the house. The whitening never reverses — an old Resonant's hair is a readable ledger. The world's two body-marks in their clean registers: white hair = taught science by day; red eyes = fireside legend by dark; village talk garbles them.

### Laws on the page
- THE DISTANCE LAW (Ch. 11): the art is spent FROM the body — anywhere on it; hands are merely the habit. Distance and disconnection raise the cost; remote manifestation is an advanced mark. A continuous medium counts as connection (Kael's boot-thread through the ground). Mechanism in 00.
- THE BURST (Ch. 13 — lightning-threading failure mode): a thread that loses exactness under load (impact, shock, divided attention) does not die — it discharges INTO ITS SOURCE. Margin is a function of capacity: a large purse absorbs the slop of an inexact thread; a small purse pays in its own flesh; burn scales with thread size. The speckle-burns are ORDINARY burns — never a magical body-mark (the two body-marks remain red eyes and white hair). Page canon, Vask: "I can be wrong by a finger's width. The purse forgives me. Nothing forgives you."
- INTERNAL REINFORCEMENT (Ch. 14 — advanced use): resonance spent INWARD into the Resonant's own body — nothing leaves it; limbs strengthened and hardened, weight, force, and speed multiplied (a flagstone split under one step). Capacity-hungry — a big-purse technique; the Vel line's guarded school, bred across eleven generations. Kynemir fence intact: reinforcement is transmutation-mediated — her own tissue infused via her element; motion still comes from muscle. Page render observational only ("Whatever she had spent, she had spent it inward"); no doctrine delivered yet.
- **THE MEDIUM LAW — METAL TAKES THE THREAD (Ch. 15):** lightning enters METAL far more readily than wood. Wood must be persuaded and holds a thread only under the Resonant's own exactness; steel accepts one unasked and carries it WHOLE — end to end, hilt to point, no thinner for having spread. The gift and the cost are the same property: a medium that takes a thread that easily gives it back the same way, so THE BURST through steel arrives everywhere at once and burns proportionally worse — "the same size thread in a better road." Page canon, Vask: "Wood forgives you. It bounces, it bends, and it lies to you about what you did. This does not lie." Deep-layer consistency: substances differ in their minima-nature and the difference is readable by feel (00). Consequence banked: the endgame kit's lightning-through-steel is an AFFINITY DISCOVERED, not a difficulty overcome.
- **OPEN — THE BIND (Kael's untested hypothesis, Ch. 15):** if metal is a road rather than a container, the road need not stop where his blade stops; in a bind, steel touches steel, and the distance law already counts a continuous medium as a connection. Written in the blank book, never attempted. NOT canon until it is spent on the page.
