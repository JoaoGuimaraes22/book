## MAGIC (system canon — public and page-facing layers; deep layer in 00)

### The model
- Six elements — FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIGHTNING, NATURE — the known world's MODEL of magic, not necessarily its truth; origin and nature stay a world-level mystery (far-continent braid, 00). The mother's way is pedagogy beneath the elements — perception-first, threads, structure, cost — deniably closer to the deeper grammar.
- The Temple teaches a particulate theory: magic moves through THE MINIMA — the smallest grains of the world, present in all matter ("as a struck string wakes its silent neighbor" — Priscian's delivery). "The minima" is a COMMON word. The Temple's science is old, learned, honest — and incomplete (00; the "smallest grains" claim is its honest error — Kael shelves it untested, Ch. 10). Neris may use the word from Ch. 10 on; she never handed it to Kael in Part I — vocabulary withheld is the rationing ("the word was not hers," Ch. 8).
- THE LIE (public doctrine): only select bloodlines can be Resonants. False — an old imperial lie preserving the royal-noble power dynamic (00).
- Enforcement — the pedagogy monopoly: attunement stays dormant untaught; flickers are dismissed as luck or superstition. The empire does not suppress commoner Resonants — it is simply the only teacher. Anomalies absorb as *noble blood somewhere in the line*; the lie self-seals.

### Structure
- Proficiency: every Resonant works all elements at varying talent and is extremely more proficient in ONE (proficiency = far more capacity there); most work almost nothing outside it. Rare: two or three proficiencies. Kael's many-element small-scale creativity is a future weapon because the world does not fight that way.
- Capacity vs. control: separate axes — raw output vs. precision, threading, creativity (mechanics in 00).
- Declaration: the dominant element settles with maturity and training (soft window ~12–14; "when it settles it settles"). Early magic is small and formless for most, but high capacity TINTS early (Aurelian's wax-sheet fire, Ch. 9) — capacity-worship's pageantry is built on this.
- Drain (public; taught Ch. 9 in Ruthen's cost register — spending, the purse, a grain): the Art exhausts and is never infinite; deep drain is painful; drain near the bottom whitens the hair PERMANENTLY ("witch-white" in folk talk; "The body kept the receipt"); full drain kills, every time, however beloved the house. An old Resonant's hair is a readable ledger. **THE MARK'S REGISTER IS PURE QUARTZ WHITE** (author, s33 — the page's image for Ruthen's seam, *like quartz through rock*). **The Vessantine silver is NOT a mark** — hereditary house colouring (00): silver-fair is blood, quartz-white is a receipt; folk talk garbles the two and the empire lets it. The two body-marks in their clean registers: white hair = taught science by day; red eyes = fireside legend by dark; village talk garbles both.

### Laws on the page
- THE DISTANCE LAW — the proximity law's page face (Ch. 11; mechanism 00 §THE PROXIMITY LAW): the Art is spent FROM the body — anywhere on it; hands are the habit. Distance and disconnection raise the cost; remote manifestation is an advanced mark. A continuous medium counts as connection (Kael's boot-thread through the ground).
- **THE THREADING (author, s25).** NOT one resonant thread laid into a blade: **MANY** lightning threads held in tight parallel, **accelerated and CONTAINED**, run the length of the steel and kept there under load. The difficulty is holding the whole bundle exact through impact, out of a purse that will not fill a cup. **Page-facing:** *"spend a thread"* stays the general idiom for one act of spending, in narration and every mouth; the page renders the OBSERVED thing — *a thread*, *the stitch*, *a stitch of white down its length* (Ch. 14) — and the bundle is never enumerated, counted or explained. **THE ROUTE (Ch. 21, Vask):** shoulder → arm → hand → steel, fast and tight to the body the whole way with nothing coming off it — hence total awareness of the body at once, and why a man who has lost track of his own elbow cannot hold it. **Vask's pedagogy:** steel down, thread with bare hands, take up the blades only once both hands hold and the feet are known — by then it runs out into the steel because there is nowhere else for it to go. **WHAT IT DOES, AND WHAT IT IS NOT (author, s37).** The threading **augments the cutting power of whatever it coats** — steel or the bare hand — and **cuts workings**. It is **NOT an electrical effect on a body**: it does not shock, convulse or seize a man, and no page may render it that way. **IT IS VISIBLE** — white, running the length of the medium (*a stitch of white down its length*, Ch. 14; the whole yard saw the public flash) — so a page that hides it is wrong; the thing Kael controls is whether he holds it or lets it go at the knock. **Consequences:** the BURST is proportionally worse (the whole bundle comes home when exactness fails); holding **two** threadings is a categorically larger problem than one, not a doubled one. **CAPACITY IS NOT THE EXPLANATION FOR THE GAP (author, s25 — do not write it as one):** Vask is a Sanctum-forged elite and far better than Kael at BOTH axes; his capacity means only that his errors would cost nothing, and he does not make them. Never stage an adult master's superiority over Kael as raw purse alone; Kael's control is exceptional *for a boy of thirteen in this world*, never in absolute terms.
- THE BURST (Ch. 13 — lightning-threading failure mode): a thread that loses exactness under load (impact, shock, divided attention) does not die — it discharges INTO ITS SOURCE. Margin is a function of capacity: a large purse absorbs the slop; a small purse pays in its own flesh; burn scales with thread size. The speckle-burns are ORDINARY burns — never a magical body-mark. Page canon, Vask: "I can be wrong by a finger's width. The purse forgives me. Nothing forgives you."
- INTERNAL REINFORCEMENT (Ch. 14 — advanced): resonance spent INWARD into the Resonant's own body — nothing leaves it; limbs strengthened and hardened, weight, force and speed multiplied (a flagstone split under one step). Capacity-hungry. **NOT A VEL SECRET (author, s24):** a KNOWN earth technique, available to any earth Resonant with the capacity and schooling; what the Vel own is the DEGREE — eleven generations of breeding and drilling — and that gap is real and not closeable by cleverness. Kynemir fence intact: reinforcement is transmutation-mediated (her own tissue infused via her element; motion still from muscle). Page render observational only ("Whatever she had spent, she had spent it inward"); no doctrine delivered. **TWO SCALES, DO NOT CONFLATE (author, s37).** HERS is a whole limb or a whole body — capacity-hungry, and what lets a thirteen-year-old girl throw grown men about. **KAEL'S IS A LINE**: he cannot buy an arm, so he buys the exact line the force folds along, about two fingers wide, and **the constraint is AREA, not cost** — at that size it is cheap and repeatable, and it does NOT empty him (the s24 *empties him every time / not twice in a day* clause is RETIRED). This is the deep layer's own economy of infection: the untrained flood a region, the technician infects the one point. Never write his version as a weaker flood; it is a different move at a different scale.
- **THE MEDIUM LAW — METAL TAKES THE THREAD (Ch. 15):** lightning enters METAL far more readily than wood. Wood must be persuaded and holds a thread only under the Resonant's own exactness; steel accepts one unasked and carries it WHOLE, hilt to point, no thinner for spreading. Gift and cost are one property: THE BURST through steel arrives everywhere at once and burns proportionally worse — "the same size thread in a better road." Page canon, Vask: "Wood forgives you. It bounces, it bends, and it lies to you about what you did. This does not lie." Deep-layer consistency: substances differ in minima-nature, readable by feel (00). Banked: the endgame kit's lightning-through-steel is an AFFINITY DISCOVERED, not a difficulty overcome.
- **THE EVOLVED THREADING — THE INWARD ART IN LIGHTNING (author, s39; SEALED MECHANISM, page-facing observation only).** Vask's whole-body form is not more threading; it is **the threading combined with the inward art** — the same move as internal reinforcement (Valeria's earth, hardening and speeding a limb), run in LIGHTNING through an entire body at once. **The mechanism (BIBLE-ONLY, never on any page of any book until the world can hold it):** interior lightning overrides the body's own governing impulses and drives the muscle far past the rate it can be asked for. **The toll is enormous** and scales with how long it is held. **THE REGISTER FENCE IS ABSOLUTE:** this world has no physiology for it, so no page may reach for impulse, nerve, muscle-signal or any term implying an apparatus this world has not built (`curriculum.md` §Register rule — the one permanent ban). **WHAT THE PAGE GETS (Ch. 30, and it is Kael's whole reading):** the white in the whole of him, held, *not coming off*; *It was not going along him. It was going into him*; the same shape as a girl's art spent inward, in the wrong element, in a whole body at once; and the speed rendered as ABSENCE — *Not blurred. Not quick. Absent* — *a third thing, made out of two he already knew, and there was no name anywhere in him to put on it.* **CONTROL, NOT PURSE** (s25 fence): never stage it as a large reserve. **KAEL'S FAR-FUTURE ROAD (author, s39):** he eventually does this. Ch. 30 is the taste, and he is given half of it and wants the rest.
- **CONTACT-SPENT FIRE (Ch. 30 — the coalition general).** Fire spent at the POINT and MOMENT of contact — off the knee, the heel, the knuckles — a burst at the instant of every blow, *the way sparks come off a wheel*, and the instant is the only warning there is. Legal under the proximity law (the Art is spent from the body, hands are only the habit) and the coalition's answer to the empire's one-handed grammar: nothing is reserved for the Art because everything is the Art. **Not a named school on any page**; nobody theorises it.
- **A THREADING CUTS A WORKING (Ch. 26; author, s30).** A blade carrying the threading, driven through a fire working, **divides it** — not extinguished, not absorbed: *it did not go out. It broke* — split along the line the steel took, the halves closing on empty air behind the man who went through them. **Scope, held narrow:** the threading (the contained bundle, never a single thread), through a medium that carries it whole, against a working being *paid for continuously* rather than one that has landed. **THE HAND IS ALSO A MEDIUM:** blades sheathed, the threading runs shoulder → arm → hand and stops there, *because there was no blade on the end of it any more*, and the fire comes apart on the hand as on the blade. **The page NEVER explains why it works** (06: *the causal chain stops at the first why*); nobody theorises it; Priscian must not be handed it. **The cost is total:** this AND internal line-reinforcement in the same instant (Ch. 26's *two at once*) empties Kael of every grain in about a fifth of a second, and it must never become cheap.
- **MENDING OUT OF PROFICIENCY, AND THE FIRST WHITENING ON THE PAGE (Ch. 32).** Neris is an AIR Resonant and mends her son's broken ribs with nature anyway. It works because she carries the other half in the same head — the anatomy, the herb-lore, the butchery table — and *the Art does what you understand*; it works BADLY because the element is not hers, and **the difference is rendered as contrast, not explained**: the First Master's mending two mornings earlier showed nothing, and hers shows everything. **She goes deep enough to take the drain-mark, and the manuscript's first whitening happens as an EVENT rather than as an established feature** — pure quartz white, out of the roots, watched by a boy lying under her hands who was taught the cost doctrine in a Temple yard and can price it to the grain. Nobody on the page says what it means.
- **NATURE IS THE ART OF MENDING (Ch. 19):** the healing art — most of what a healer does badly with a hot iron and a needle, a skilled artist does properly. **The Art alone is worth very little:** a Resonant who does not know which way the bone goes will push it the wrong way; healing needs the Art AND the medical knowledge in one head, and very few carry both. Severin does (03/00). Unremarked rhyme: *the Art does what you understand* (Ch. 10).
- **VALERIA — EARTH BOTH WAYS (Ch. 16/17).** Super-advanced in earth spent OUTWARD as well as inward; internal reinforcement is her SPECIALTY — degree, not possession. Ch. 14/15's "nothing left her body" is Kael's observation of one duel, not a law about her. **FENCE (hard):** the threading is a LIGHTNING technique and is not hers to have — she has taken it on the arm a dozen times and watched for it slower, and none of that is a road to doing it — seeing a technique is not acquiring one; proficiency is where the capacity lives. Do not let her acquire it, here or later — the same rule as AIR, below.
- **THE GROUND-SENSE (Ch. 16 — the Vel school, taught not gifted):** a reading, never a projection. Connected to the stone she stands on, she reads what it carries — weight, gait, position. Nothing leaves her body; the Kynemir fence is untouched (perception only). **FENCES (hard):** SHORT RANGE · INTENTIONAL ONLY, never passive · EXPENSIVE — paid in slower guards and lost meals. A fighting instrument, not surveillance: Kael watched her fifteen mornings from the treeline unfound and was caught only when he came inside it while she was drilling.
- **THE BLIND SPOT (Ch. 16 — Kael's counter, UNANSWERED):** the sense depends on continuity, so the counter breaks it — a thread of earth into the stone as his weight lands — not to move it, only to trouble the grain under the boot — so the step dies where it stands. Costly to the point of collapse (six threads in six seconds out of the smallest purse on the island), good for a few strides. She diagnosed it in four days and could not answer it: the floor can only report what the floor is doing. Kael's first win off her; it stands through the autumn.
- **AIR — THE SECOND SENSE (banked, UNBUILT):** the answer to the blind spot is a medium that is not the floor — air touches everything, and a moving body leaves a shape in it. Both senses together would make her a far better reader than either alone. Valeria named it and cannot have it — "years and a school that does not exist and an element that is not mine." Not on any page beyond her naming it; do not let her acquire it casually.
- **OPEN — THE BIND (Kael's untested hypothesis, Ch. 15):** if metal is a road rather than a container, the road need not stop where his blade stops; in a bind, steel touches steel, and a continuous medium is a connection. Written in the blank book, never attempted. NOT canon until spent on the page.
- **THE LAZY PLACE AT MOUNTAIN SCALE (Ch. 33 — Severin).** Nothing gets lifted. He reaches along the ground into the one place under an entire flank where all of it is already leaning, and **takes it away** — four hundred feet of terrace goes down with everything standing on it, and it opens the only road out. **This does not contradict Ch. 27's *he could hold one. He could not hold two*: HOLDING is the expensive direction and RELEASING is the cheap one**, and nobody on the page says so. It costs him his body rather than his hair — **no new white can show on a man already grey all through, and the never-confirm rule on his grey is untouched and unremarked.** *Everything heavy is lazy* is a literal magical law (00) and this is its largest page.
- **THE HAND AS THE MEDIUM, AT FULL SCALE (Ch. 33 — Vask).** The evolved whole-body form run through bare hands: **his hands cut.** Steel does not slow it and mail does not slow it — a flat hand through the side of a helmet, an open palm taking an arm off at the shoulder, an edge of hand through a spear haft and the man behind it. Consistent with Ch. 26's hand-as-medium and with the threading's augment-what-it-coats rule above. **The page explains none of it and nobody theorises it.** Consequence, never stated: the sword he gave away was never the weapon.
- **THE FIVE FIRES (Ch. 33 — the coalition's five; full entry `factions.md`).** All fire, no two the same, **none of them named as a school, none theorised on any page.** Contact-spent (PELLAN) · mass in standing sheets (GALLOS) · **fire put INTO ground so that ground can never be crossed again** (DUNMAR — terrain taken by subtraction, almost no noise) · **a finger-thick line carried out of one hand along the ground, which DIVIDES a working rather than opposing it** (FENNA) · **a volume of AIR at about ten feet with no visible part to it at all** (THIRO — men reach for their own armour, then cannot breathe, and there is no mark on them afterwards). **THIRO'S ONE COUNTER, and it is the only one there is: he cannot spend it on air he is standing in the middle of** — which is why closing to contact is survivable and standing at ten feet is not. Not explained on the page; Kael reads it a second after Valeria has already done it.
