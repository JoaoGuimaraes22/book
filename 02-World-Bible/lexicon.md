## CONTROLLED LEXICONS (coined register terms)
- **Rule:** coined terms for seasons, calendar, institutions, and the Art are CONTROLLED vocabulary — each term consistent (never improvise a synonym for a logged term) and OWNED by a place and culture; new locales (Book Two's continent, coalition lands, the far continent) mint their own rather than inheriting the island's. New coinages are logged here at chapter commit.
- **Village register (island):** the dark of the year (longest night) · the winter feast / feast night.
- **Temple register:** the cold term · the turn toward spring · the boards · first resonances · the dish (the proficiency ceremony) · the roll · the yard / the yard-master · storm-weeks (the drowned-yard weeks of deep winter; children's vernacular, used unglossed).
- **Mountain / Kael's narration register:** winter's turn (the seam of the year) · "Evening" (the daily deadline — Vask's word) · the long game · **"science"** (NERIS'S word, taught on-page Ch. 10: "knowing built by trial, and tested until it holds"; nobody in the village says it; the Temple says "natural philosophy." World-canon: in the empire, science is SERVANT-CLASS knowing — scientists (smiths, hedge-doctors, milldam-menders, empirics) rank BENEATH Resonants; "natural philosophy" is the same thing in the prestige register — "Wear the better coat." The word AND the status-map are deniable Sanctum-fingerprint carriers) · his personal lexicon: far-counting, the standing game, what fire eats, **proofing** (Ch. 15 — his own name for the mode his father's school turns into one year out: not teaching but coming at him at speed, over and over, to find what breaks; nothing explained after) ("what being seen costs" is BANKED ONLY — not on any page; the drawer stays unnamed and live) · **"a crop of laughter"** (the group-laughter register's term of art; instances Ch. 8 ×2 / Ch. 9 / Ch. 10; CAPPED — further uses deliberate-only, like any protected echo).
- **Magic register (THE controlled vocabulary for the Art — when these words appear, magic is on the page):** **the minima** (the fine grains in all things; common word) · **resonance** (the CONNECTION between person and minima; count-noun "a resonance" = an instance) · **resonate** (the verb) · **Resonant** (a person capable of the Art; per element by prefix: fire Resonant, water Resonant) · **resonant thread** (a controlled connection — the formal term behind the "spend a thread" idiom; the full phrase reserved for scholars' mouths) · **the threading** (Kael's signature technique — NOT a single thread but a tight, accelerated, contained BUNDLE of them run through steel; mechanic in `magic.md`. Keep the two words distinct: *a thread* = one act of spending, general idiom, any mouth; *the threading* = the technique. The page never enumerates the bundle) · **Resonance Theory** (the scholarly field — Priscian's discipline) · **reach / push / spend / the purse / a grain** (Ruthen's teaching-and-cost register; "reach"/"push" live ONLY in a teacher's quoted voice — narration and generic magic-talk always use the resonance system; spend/grain/thread remain general) · **capacity** (the formal term for the reserve — **FIRST ON THE PAGE Ch. 21, in Kael's mouth**. **REGISTER SHIFT, author ruling s25:** from here **KAEL says *capacity*, not *the purse***. The purse is the yard's craft word — Ruthen's, Vask's, the benches' — and those mouths keep it; the boy has moved to the scholars' term. In Ch. 21 the two words stand side by side in one exchange, a son and a father naming the same thing differently, and **nothing on the page remarks on it**. Part of the wider rise: `curriculum.md` §Register rule) · **proficiency · declaration** (structural terms; the domains are always "the elements," never "provinces" — that word stays geographic). Kael has NO private jargon for the Art; plain English "ask/question" stays plain. **THE ART — CAPITALISATION (author ruling, s28; retro pass executed same session across all 23 chapters).** **"the Art" takes a capital when it names magic as a DISCIPLINE** — the field itself, the thing a school teaches and a doctrine is written about: *a school of the Art · the Art runs in the blood · the elements of the Art · the Art does what you understand · the Art is spent from the body.* **It stays lowercase as a COUNT NOUN or a POSSESSED INSTANCE** — one person's practice of it, or one kind of it: *a rare art · two arts · her art was fire · his own art · your art will come · the healing art · an internal art · art hand* (the attributive compound). The same distinction English draws between Law and laws, Medicine and a medicine. **WHY THE FENCE MATTERS:** every other coined magic term on the page is lowercase (*the minima · resonance · a resonant thread · the threading · capacity · the purse · a grain*), and so is every one of Kael's coinages (*the coat · far-counting · the standing game · proofing · the lazy place*). The Art is the ONE abstract noun licensed to capitalise, and it is licensed because **Resonant** already does — the practitioner and the discipline take the capital together, and nothing else follows them. Do not let this spread to *the boards · the dish · the yard · the games · the coat*. **Scope note:** the capital is inaudible, so no character's register changes and Neris's felt-only vocabulary is untouched; and `check-echoes.sh` lowercases before matching, so no protected phrasing can break on it. The retro pass moved no word count in any chapter. Retired from page vocabulary: mage · wright · channeling · "working" (ordinary labor only).
