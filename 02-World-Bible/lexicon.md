## CONTROLLED LEXICONS (coined register terms)
- **Rule:** coined terms for seasons, calendar, institutions, and the Art are CONTROLLED vocabulary — each term consistent (never improvise a synonym for a logged term) and OWNED by a place and culture; new locales (Book Two's continent, coalition lands, the far continent) mint their own rather than inheriting the island's. New coinages are logged here at chapter commit.
- **Village register (island):** the dark of the year (longest night) · the winter feast / feast night.
- **Crowd register (the games):** **"the Ghost"** — what the standing ground named Kael after the first bout (Ch. 23), for never being where the air went. **The PEOPLE's word, not the Temple's and not the nobles'** — minted in the cheap seats, carried round the arena in under a minute; not a compliment about skill but a description of an absence. **Said to his face on the road (Ch. 26)** — *"Go on, Ghost"* from a man with a coil of rope, hat off, and the two words in whitewash on a bed sheet with a rope through the hem — so it is now what the island calls him. Do not let the Temple or the nobles adopt it as their word; do not let a herald or an official confer it. Requiem rhyme, never remarked: the world puts its first name on him years before he chooses the one it will remember (00).
- **Temple register:** the cold term · the turn toward spring · the boards · first resonances · the dish (the proficiency ceremony) · the roll · the yard / the yard-master · storm-weeks (the drowned-yard weeks of deep winter; children's vernacular, used unglossed).
- **Mountain / Kael's narration register:** winter's turn (the seam of the year) · "Evening" (the daily deadline — Vask's word) · the long game · **"science"** (NERIS'S word, taught on-page Ch. 10: "knowing built by trial, and tested until it holds"; nobody in the village says it; the Temple says "natural philosophy." World-canon: in the empire, science is SERVANT-CLASS knowing — smiths, hedge-doctors, milldam-menders, empirics rank BENEATH Resonants; "natural philosophy" is the same thing in the prestige register — "Wear the better coat." The word AND the status-map are deniable Sanctum-fingerprint carriers) · his personal lexicon: far-counting, the standing game, what fire eats, **proofing** (Ch. 15 — his own name for what his father's school turns into one year out: not teaching but coming at him at speed, over and over, to find what breaks; nothing explained after) · *what being seen costs* — BANKED ONLY, not on any page; the drawer stays unnamed and live · **"a crop of laughter"** (the group-laughter register's term of art; instances Ch. 8 ×2 / Ch. 9 / Ch. 10; CAPPED — further uses deliberate-only, like any protected echo).
- **Magic register (THE controlled vocabulary for the Art — when these words appear, magic is on the page):** **the minima** (the fine grains in all things; common word) · **resonance** (the CONNECTION between person and minima; count-noun "a resonance" = an instance) · **resonate** (the verb) · **Resonant** (a person capable of the Art; per element by prefix: fire Resonant, water Resonant) · **resonant thread** (a controlled connection — the formal term behind the "spend a thread" idiom; the full phrase reserved for scholars' mouths) · **the threading** (Kael's signature technique — a tight, accelerated, contained BUNDLE of threads run through steel; mechanic in `magic.md`. *A thread* = one act of spending, general idiom, any mouth; *the threading* = the technique; the page never enumerates the bundle) · **Resonance Theory** (the scholarly field — Priscian's discipline) · **reach / push / spend / the purse / a grain** (Ruthen's teaching-and-cost register; "reach"/"push" live ONLY in a teacher's quoted voice — narration and generic magic-talk always use the resonance system; spend/grain/thread remain general) · **capacity** (the formal term for the reserve — first on the page Ch. 21, in Kael's mouth. **Kael says *capacity*, not *the purse*** (author, s25); the purse is the yard's craft word — Ruthen's, Vask's, the benches' — and those mouths keep it; nothing on the page remarks on the split. Part of the wider rise: `curriculum.md` §Register rule) · **proficiency · declaration** (structural terms; the domains are always "the elements," never "provinces" — that word stays geographic). Kael has NO private jargon for the Art; plain "ask/question" stays plain.
- **THE ART — CAPITALISATION (author, s28).** **"the Art" takes a capital when it names magic as a DISCIPLINE** — the field itself, the thing a school teaches and a doctrine is written about: *a school of the Art · the Art runs in the blood · the elements of the Art · the Art does what you understand · the Art is spent from the body.* **Lowercase as a COUNT NOUN or a POSSESSED INSTANCE** — one person's practice of it, or one kind of it: *a rare art · two arts · her art was fire · his own art · your art will come · the healing art · an internal art · art hand.* (The distinction English draws between Law and laws, Medicine and a medicine.) The Art is the ONE abstract noun licensed to capitalise, because **Resonant** already does — practitioner and discipline take the capital together, and nothing else follows: every other coined magic term (*the minima · resonance · a resonant thread · the threading · capacity · the purse · a grain*) and every one of Kael's coinages (*the coat · far-counting · the standing game · proofing · the lazy place*) stays lowercase. Do not let it spread to *the boards · the dish · the yard · the games · the coat*. Neris's felt-only vocabulary is untouched.
- **Retired from page vocabulary:** mage · wright · channeling · "working" (ordinary labor only).
