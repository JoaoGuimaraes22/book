## CONTROLLED LEXICONS (coined register terms)
- **Rule:** coined terms for seasons, calendar, institutions, and the art are CONTROLLED vocabulary — each term consistent (never improvise a synonym for a logged term) and OWNED by a place and culture; new locales (Book Two's continent, coalition lands, the far continent) mint their own rather than inheriting the island's. New coinages are logged here at chapter commit.
- **Village register (island):** the dark of the year (longest night) · the winter feast / feast night.
- **Temple register:** the cold term · the turn toward spring · the boards · first resonances · the dish (the proficiency ceremony) · the roll · the yard / the yard-master · storm-weeks (the drowned-yard weeks of deep winter; children's vernacular, used unglossed).
- **Mountain / Kael's narration register:** winter's turn (the seam of the year) · "Evening" (the daily deadline — Vask's word) · the long game · **"science"** (NERIS'S word, taught on-page Ch. 10: "knowing built by trial, and tested until it holds"; nobody in the village says it; the Temple says "natural philosophy." World-canon: in the empire, science is SERVANT-CLASS knowing — scientists (smiths, hedge-doctors, milldam-menders, empirics) rank BENEATH Resonants; "natural philosophy" is the same thing in the prestige register — "Wear the better coat." The word AND the status-map are deniable Sanctum-fingerprint carriers) · his personal lexicon: far-counting, the standing game, what fire eats ("what being seen costs" is BANKED ONLY — not on any page; the drawer stays unnamed and live) · **"a crop of laughter"** (the group-laughter register's term of art; instances Ch. 8 ×2 / Ch. 9 / Ch. 10; CAPPED — further uses deliberate-only, like any protected echo).
- **Magic register (THE controlled vocabulary for the art — when these words appear, magic is on the page):** **the minima** (the fine grains in all things; common word) · **resonance** (the CONNECTION between person and minima; count-noun "a resonance" = an instance) · **resonate** (the verb) · **Resonant** (a person capable of the art; per element by prefix: fire Resonant, water Resonant) · **resonant thread** (a controlled connection — the formal term behind the "spend a thread" idiom; the full phrase reserved for scholars' mouths) · **Resonance Theory** (the scholarly field — Priscian's discipline) · **reach / push / spend / the purse / a grain** (Ruthen's teaching-and-cost register; "reach"/"push" live ONLY in a teacher's quoted voice — narration and generic magic-talk always use the resonance system; spend/purse/grain/thread remain general) · **proficiency · declaration** (structural terms; the domains are always "the elements," never "provinces" — that word stays geographic). Kael has NO private jargon for the art; plain English "ask/question" stays plain. Retired from page vocabulary: mage · wright · channeling · "working" (ordinary labor only).
