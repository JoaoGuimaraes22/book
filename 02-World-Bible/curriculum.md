## THE MOUNTAIN CURRICULUM (the parents' schools)
- **Register rule (a gradient): THE BAN IS ON APPARATUS, NOT ON WORDS THAT SOUND MODERN IN ENGLISH (author, s69).** *This is not the real world; a word the author thinks fits is a word this world has* — *heist* was queried and ruled in (Ch. 48). What stays banned is **notation, units, and any term implying an apparatus or a discipline this world has not built** — the thing the term drags in, never how recent the word feels to an English ear. English is the medium, not the setting; the register is the author's ear and not a word-list. (With `naming.md`'s s57 ruling that real-world words are not barred in any register, the *modern terminology* clause is retired.) Knowledge, method, and precision deepen without ceiling. Part I = craft-lore (appetite and keeping, the lazy place, far-counting). Part II on = the honesty pivot: precision where they can afford it; Kael's register matures into genuine period natural philosophy — substance, principle, proportion, trial; the Temple's terms arrive where honest; Kael coins his own where the world has none. The experimental turn: from receiving method to running it ("ask twice, with one thing changed"). Revision flags: reads MODERN = fail; reads merely precise = correct.
- **PART IV — THE REGISTER RISES (author, s25).** From the tournament season Neris moves Kael off the coined child-words (far-counting, what fire eats, the standing game — she names all three and retires them to his face) into the formal scientific vocabulary she was schooled in; his register climbs with her, and he leaves the yard's craft word behind of his own accord (*the purse* → **capacity**, `lexicon.md`). **The Sanctum fingerprint ceases to be deniable and the seal is untouched:** no name, no source, no doctrine — she never says where the words come from and he never asks; he knows only that they are not the village's and not the Temple's, and he can tell. **Scope is hers and his:** the rise happens in her teaching and in Kael's own head and mouth, never in the world's — the Temple keeps "natural philosophy," the yard keeps the purse, the village keeps everything it had. **Precise nouns for real phenomena are licensed** where the phenomenon is on the page and the boy has tested it (*conductivity*, *conductors*, Ch. 21). Still banned outright: notation, units, and any term implying an apparatus or a discipline this world has not built (and see the s69 ruling above — the test is what the term drags in, not how modern it sounds). 06's counterweight applies to her here — she glimpses what she is doing, and does not change.
- **Father (body):** scree in the dark, breath, snares, the knife (the lesson is everything the cutting is not), stillness, terrain; loaded scree with counts; the grammar of falling; knife-work to the body ("where a man carries; where he is lazy"). THE WEAPONS SCHOOL (from Ch. 7): sword, spear, bow on Vask-carved wasters; mornings at home in parallel with the Temple years — the concealment thread powered from both ends (steel state: `../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md`). THE HAND-TO-HAND SCHOOL: the school with no first day (arm-snare play before memory, folded into work); escape-facing by design — every lesson begins with the boy CAUGHT; the one lesson "Get loose" (never explained on-page; points at the program by inference only); the tap-sign (two taps = instant release, always honored; "The hand talks").
- **Mother (mind) — subject names are Kael's own or hers:** far-counting = mathematics (heights by shadow, position by stars) · the standing game = mechanics ("Everything heavy is lazy") · "what fire eats" = chemistry (spent Ch. 5: transformation as appetite — fire the picky eater, cold the other mouth, salt the KEEPER; kills the promise) · herb-lore / mending = biology and anatomy, incl. reagent-craft ("things that cannot lie make the best witnesses"); butchery is the anatomy classroom · languages: four by age 8 — bird (first), stone-click ("Traders" — **IVRIT**, the south's, Ch. 38), the soft downhill one (strictest in it; sings in it — **AVESTAN**, the east's, Ch. 38), one unspecified; **the reason, given at last (Ch. 38): *so that there would be nowhere on this continent you could not go*** — the child-names retired to his face in a governor's bed, the true names in `lexicon.md` · the new school (magic — from Ch. 4): unnamed by her design; perception-only, rationed in drops, lessons deliberately small; she answers until SHE stops — the leash (00) · history: RATIONED — scraps, never on asking; the Temple carries real history education; their secrecy about history is the boat-counting reflex — every thread of the world's story leads back to them.

- **SPENT ON THE PAGE IN BOOK TWO (Ch. 37):** *position by stars* — taught at eight on the flat rock, on a mountain he was never going to leave; he put a drifting hull on the sky two nights running and got the line the old man had off the swell. **The kitchen chemistry is hers on the page** — *We must boil the sea … The salt stays* — given in the formal register to two fire Resonants and to nobody else, the child-word only in his head; he had been shown it at nine with a pot and a cold knife on a winter morning, and sat five days on a boat without taking it out.
