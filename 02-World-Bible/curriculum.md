## THE MOUNTAIN CURRICULUM (the parents' schools)
- **Register rule (a gradient):** the permanent ban is on OUT-OF-WORLD vocabulary only — no modern terminology, no notation. Knowledge, method, and precision deepen without ceiling. Part I = craft-lore (appetite and keeping, the lazy place, far-counting). Part II on = the honesty pivot: precision where they can afford it; Kael's register matures into genuine period natural philosophy — substance, principle, proportion, trial; the Temple's terms arrive where honest; Kael coins his own where the world has none. The experimental turn: from receiving method to running it ("asking twice with one thing changed"). Revision flags: reads MODERN = fail; reads merely precise = correct. **PART IV — THE REGISTER RISES (author ruling, s25).** From the tournament season Neris deliberately moves Kael off the coined child-words (far-counting, what fire eats, the standing game — she names all three and retires them to his face) and into formal scientific vocabulary: the register she was schooled in. Kael's own register climbs with her, and in the same chapter he leaves the yard's craft word behind of his own accord (*the purse* → **capacity**, `lexicon.md`). **THIS IS THE SANCTUM FINGERPRINT CEASING TO BE DENIABLE — and the seal is untouched:** no name, no source, no doctrine; she never says where the words come from and he never asks. He knows only that they are not the village's and not the Temple's, and he can tell. **SCOPE IS HERS AND HIS:** the rise happens in her teaching and in Kael's own head and mouth, never in the world's — the Temple keeps "natural philosophy" and its own vocabulary, the yard keeps the purse, and the village keeps everything it had. **THE MODERN FLAG IS NARROWED, NOT LIFTED:** precise nouns for real phenomena are licensed where the phenomenon is on the page and the boy has tested it (*conductivity*, *conductors*, Ch. 21). Still banned outright: notation, units, and any term implying an apparatus or a discipline this world has not built. **06's counterweight applies to her here** — she glimpses what she is doing, and does not change.
- **Father (body):** scree in the dark, breath, snares, the knife (the lesson is everything the cutting is not), stillness, terrain; loaded scree with counts; the grammar of falling; knife-work to the body ("where a man carries; where he is lazy"). THE WEAPONS SCHOOL (from Ch. 7): sword, spear, bow on Vask-carved wasters (steel banked); mornings at home in parallel with the Temple years — the concealment thread powered from both ends. THE HAND-TO-HAND SCHOOL: the school with no first day (arm-snare play before memory, folded into work); escape-facing by design — every lesson begins with the boy CAUGHT; the one lesson "Get loose" (never explained on-page; points at the Harvest by inference only); the tap-sign (two taps = instant release, always honored; "The hand talks").
- **Mother (mind) — subject names are Kael's own or hers:** far-counting = mathematics (heights by shadow, position by stars) · the standing game = mechanics ("Everything heavy is lazy") · "what fire eats" = chemistry (spent Ch. 5: transformation as appetite — fire the picky eater, cold the other mouth, salt the KEEPER; kills the promise) · herb-lore / mending = biology and anatomy, incl. reagent-craft ("things that cannot lie make the best witnesses"); butchery is the anatomy classroom · languages: four by age 8 — bird (first), stone-click ("Traders"), the soft downhill one (strictest in it; sings in it), one unspecified; ends never explained · the new school (magic — from Ch. 4): unnamed by her design; perception-only, rationed in drops, lessons deliberately small; she answers until SHE stops — the leash (00) · history: RATIONED — scraps, never on asking; the Temple carries real history education; their secrecy about history is the boat-counting reflex — every thread of the world's story leads back to them.
