## THE TEMPLE
- **THE THREE SCHOOLS:** the empire keeps three great Resonant schools — the TEMPLE in the southern archipelago (serving the isles' rich merchant-noble houses), one in the CAPITAL, one in the NORTHWEST; the other two names reserved. Three schools, one fence — the pedagogy monopoly runs through all three. Open axis (undecided): archipelago money vs. capital blood vs. northwest frontier as student-status geography.
- **Head:** SEVERIN — the family's secret patron (00/03). On-page mostly "the master"; public title THE FIRST MASTER.
- **Students:** the empire's golden children — multi-part names, house pride, casual cruelty toward the charity case. Cohorts by age, fixed rooms; the YARD takes the young cohorts together — the one shared arena; war-training scales where classrooms split.
- **THE BOARDS:** twice a year the Temple writes its children in order — midwinter and spring examinations (four days: letters, sums, the chronicle, natural philosophy, a day in the yard), standings nailed to the colonnade of the first court, every name in earned order. The machine that converts Kael's excellence into public standing whether he wants it or not (his record lives in 07).
- **THE GRAND TOURNAMENT:** INTER-SCHOOL — the three schools convene: why the island fills, why a royal princess attends, and why the harvest screens there (00); the raid strikes the empire's future officer corps in one building. Students compete for a great prize (TBD). Announced on-page Ch. 12: two springs out (~Kael 13; Part IV); the island as host; "You have two years... Spend them well."
