## THE TEMPLE
- **THE THREE SCHOOLS:** the empire keeps three great Resonant schools — the TEMPLE in the southern archipelago (serving the isles' rich merchant-noble houses), one in the CAPITAL, one in the NORTHWEST; the other two names reserved. Three schools, one fence — the pedagogy monopoly runs through all three. Open axis (undecided): archipelago money vs. capital blood vs. northwest frontier as student-status geography.
- **Head:** SEVERIN — the family's secret patron (00/03). On-page mostly "the master"; public title THE FIRST MASTER.
- **Students:** the empire's golden children — multi-part names, house pride, casual cruelty toward the charity case. Cohorts by age, fixed rooms; the YARD takes the young cohorts together — the one shared arena; war-training scales where classrooms split.
- **THE BOARDS:** twice a year the Temple writes its children in order — midwinter and spring examinations (four days: letters, sums, the chronicle, natural philosophy, a day in the yard), standings nailed to the colonnade of the first court, every name in earned order. The machine that converts Kael's excellence into public standing whether he wants it or not (his record lives in 07).
- **THE GRAND TOURNAMENT:** INTER-SCHOOL — the three schools convene: why the island fills, why a royal princess attends, and why the harvest screens there (00); the raid strikes the empire's future officer corps in one building. Students compete for a great prize (TBD). Announced on-page Ch. 12: two springs out (~Kael 13; Part IV); the island as host; "You have two years... Spend them well." **THE PRIZE + THE MARKET LAYER (Ch. 17):** the prize is the conquered city of AMMARETH, to the winner's HOUSE. Beneath the pageant, and openly known to anyone whose family writes letters, the tournament is where the nobility PRICE the empire's children and fix their standings — three schools in one place, one week, every house that matters in the stands with a list, written once and carried for life. THREE FLOORS, keep the registers distinct: public glory · the market (cynical-political, spent Ch. 17) · the HARVEST (predatory-institutional, sealed in 00, unspent). The market layer shelters the harvest: the program never had to build a net.

### Declaration in practice
- THE PROFICIENCY CEREMONY — the dish (Ch. 11): declaration formalized empirically — the iron dish with its half-ring of plain media (candle, water, sand, sprig, copper rod); each student spends ONE grain and the element shows itself; the clerk enters it in the roll; held when a cohort's oldest reach the window ("What settles, settles. The dish only asks early."). Post-ceremony, resonance duels enter the yard: the taught one-handed stance; the three rules — nothing above the shoulders, nothing the healer cannot mend by supper, and Ruthen standing them.
- Pre-declaration cohorts (design): the young cohorts learn theory, attunement, and COST — nobody hands fire to ten-year-olds; casting lives in the older precincts Kael's cohort never enters, so magic surrounds him as distance-texture. The tournament's reserve is a POV-access rule, not a world rule: beat 18 = first magic seen close and whole, as spectacle.
- First resonances (Ch. 9): candle-bench exercises under the yard-master, taught only after cost doctrine ("before I let any of you spend a grain, you will learn what spending is"). Kael's public result (sealed): formless near-nothing — the lie self-seals around weakness ("someone's bastard, then"); the declaration's revelation stays protected; under the deep layer his profile is the favored one.
