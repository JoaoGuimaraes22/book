## NAMING REGISTERS (sociology of names)
- **Village/common:** short, worn-smooth, sea-and-stone — Bren, Joren, Task, Mira. Kael reads as this register, deliberately. Canon: Vask, Wren. "Neris" PASSES as it but belongs to none (00).
- **Noble/imperial:** long, formal, Latin-Byzantine. Canon: Vessantine (the ruling dynasty); Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine. The -ANE ending is the imperial-house sound — Corvane, Sedrane, Drusane — giving the register coherence and making new houses cheap to mint.
- **Imperial place names (Ch. 38):** cities on the empire's OWN ground take the Latin-Byzantine register **without the -ANE ending**, which belongs to houses. Canon: **SOLVENNA**. Conquered places keep their own tongue's name instead (rule below) — Ammareth, Qeshet.
- **Soldier-worn:** army-ground names sitting between village and guard registers without belonging to either — commoners or minor-house men risen through war; one hard closed syllable + a worn ending. Canon: RUTHEN; HALDEN (the register fits his cover whether or not the cover is true).
- **Royal-guard lineages:** old, hard, inherited like weapons — Vel, Kaeth, Sarn. (Valeria Vel: ornate given name against a blunt house name — a line so long in service it absorbed some royal gravity.)
- **Scholarly:** real pre-modern learning vocabulary — "the minima".
- **THE COALITION / THE DOWNHILL TONGUE (author, s43 — minted at Ch. 33).** The coalition's own register, and it inherits nothing from the island or the empire. **Shape:** two syllables, stress on the first; liquid consonants (l, r, m, n) and open vowels; endings in -an, -ar, -o, -a, -is. It is a tongue that **sings** — the one Neris goes slower in than she goes in anything else. **Its name on the page is AVESTAN** (Ch. 38, Neris's mouth; `lexicon.md`). **Canon:** ROLAN · TREMAR (Ch. 30, in raiders' own talk) · **PELLAN · DUNMAR · THIRO · FENNA · GALLOS** (Ch. 33, the five). **HARD:** no -ANE, no Latin-Byzantine ornament, no house name, **no second name at all** — one name each, and the contrast with the empire's three-names-and-a-house is a political fact nobody on any page remarks on. New coalition names are minted in this shape or not at all.
- **THE SOUTH / QESHET (author, s55 — minted at Ch. 37).** A conquered country's register, neither the island's nor the empire's: hard-to-soft consonants (q, kh, z, s, r, m, d), long open vowels, endings in *-et, -ah, -oun, -ir, -esh, -im*. **Real-world words are not barred in any register (author, s57)** — the languages' true names on the page are *Ivrit*, *Avestan* and *the Vessantine Koine* (Ch. 38; `lexicon.md`). **The south's tongue is IVRIT**, and the whole coast speaks it. **Canon:** QESHET (the city) · HAZOUN (the governor's given name). **A NATIVE RULER KEPT IN PLACE CARRIES AN IMPERIAL HOUSE NAME TAKEN AT THE CONQUEST** — *Hazoun Sabrane*: a first name out of the south and a second out of the same box as Corvane and Sedrane, worn one after the other, and nobody on the page remarks on it — the mirror of the conquered-places rule below: the empire did not rename the city, and the man renamed himself. **The household's form** is a hand flat to the breast and the head going down over it; the empire's is the knee, head bowed. SABRANE is taken.
- **Conquered places (rule, s20):** a conquered city keeps its OWN tongue's name — the empire does not rename what it takes. Canon: AMMARETH. The register is therefore deliberately un-imperial (no -ANE, no Latin-Byzantine ornament); the empire handing out a city that still carries somebody else's name is itself a small political fact, never remarked on.
- Commoners effectively lack house names; Kael does not know surnames EXIST until the Temple.
