## THE VESSANTINE EMPIRE · THE COALITION · THE PROGRAM

### The Vessantine Empire
- Ruling dynasty: House Vessantine. Once great — generations ago it used its power for good; it has rotted generation by generation into greed, pride, blood, and lust. The powerful rule over the weak.
- Corrupt to the core at the royalty level; good people and good rulers still exist within it (Character Law applies to civilizations).
- Conquered much of the continent; its subjugated nations have suffered for generations.
- **THE PROTECTION TIERS (author, s25 — keep them distinct).** **(1) The imperial guard** — white and imperial blue; the empire's ordinary armed men, drilled and everywhere, unremarkable to anyone who prices bodies. **(2) The ROYAL GUARD** — a specialised body that accompanies royalty; competent and loyal, chosen and drilled; they die where they stand without needing to discuss it. Dark grey with nothing on it; they arrange themselves across the DISTANCES rather than in front of the body, each already standing where a thing would have to come from. **(3) THE ROYAL-GUARD LINEAGES — VEL, KAETH, SARN** — a **different kind entirely**, not a better grade of the same thing: old houses inherited like weapons, bred and drilled across generations. **They serve the royal LINEAGE, wherever it goes** — a Vel escorting a daughter is ordinary. **The apex: the STRONGEST VEL ALIVE stands over the KING or QUEEN**, always has, and that posting is the entire content of being the best one. The Line is small enough that **its members know each other on sight** — by a walk, at distance, in bad light. **On-page discipline:** never write it deterministically — never say a Vel *cannot* guard a lesser royal — and never let an ordinary escort read as the Line's best; render the LADDER and where a given Vel sits on it, never a caste bar; keep the comparison LIGHT — one beat, not a meditation. **CANON (Ch. 22):** the princess's twelve are royal guard and **none of them is Vel** — Valeria would have known any of them across the arena and never turns her head. **They die in the invasion** when imperial security collapses inward around her (04 beat 19), which is why she reaches the boat with two children and no guard at all. **It starts on the mountain (Ch. 32):** four of the twelve are already gone between the district's far gate and the house, along with every other body in that party — twenty-five leave the district and fifteen arrive. **HOW THE LINEAGES ARE RAISED (author, s37):** they remove the first time — years of WATCHING killing, executions mostly, before a child is given anything sharp, so that a Vel has no first one and no number. ### The Coalition
- Recent alliance of the continent's remaining and conquered forces, formed to resist the empire.
- Founding grievances: subjugation, exploitation — and one more, unrevealed (00).
- Carries out the raid on the island at the end of Book One. To Kael, at first, THEY are the enemy — his first truth, written in fire; the saga slowly rotates the compass.
- Contains good and desperate people; commits atrocities. Neither side of the war is clean.
- **MOSTLY FIRE RESONANTS (author, s39).** The coalition's Resonants skew heavily to FIRE. Recorded as given; **why a coalition of many nations skews one element is an OPEN QUESTION** (`open-questions.md`) and is not to be explained on the page or invented here.
- **THE COLUMN THAT COMES UP THE MOUNTAIN IS ORDINARY MEN (author, s42; amended s46).** The force that follows the escape party to the house is not Resonant; the Art on that ground is the five's alone (Ch. 33 — §FIVE GENERALS below). Not a contradiction of the skew above — that was said of a force that took a whole island, and this is one column of it. Nobody on the page remarks on it.
- **FIVE GENERALS (author, s39) — NAMED ON THE PAGE FROM CH. 33 (author, s43), AND NOTHING ELSE ABOUT THEM IS.** The coalition fields five, and **all five stand in the mountain yard at Ch. 33** because a princess of the empire is standing in it (`../00-Author-Canon-SEALED.md` §True causality). **PELLAN · DUNMAR · THIRO · FENNA · GALLOS.** **THE FENCE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE LICENCE:** the names arrive **in their own mouths**, in the downhill language, in Kael's hearing — **the word *general* appears on no page of Book One**, no rank is given, the number five is never stated, and no structure, command relation or seniority is ever drawn. Kael reads five bodies and never once a command. **They carry ONE NAME EACH and no house** — against an empire whose children carry three names and a house — and nobody on any page remarks on it. **PELLAN DIES at Ch. 33** (Vask kills him); the other four survive the island. Sheet: `../03-Character-Bible/coalition-generals.md`. Sealed material: `../00-Author-Canon-SEALED.md` §THE COALITION'S GENERAL.
- **THEIR FIRE IS FIVE DIFFERENT ARTS (author, s43), and no page theorises any of them.** **PELLAN** — spent at the point and moment of contact, off knee, heel and knuckle, the instant the only warning (Ch. 30/33). **GALLOS** — mass: standing sheets the width of open ground, thrown casually, the coalition's reputation made flesh. **DUNMAR** — fire put INTO ground rather than onto anything: he does not burn men, he makes ground nobody can cross again, and he takes terrain by subtraction. **FENNA** — a finger-thick line carried out of one hand and drawn across things; she divides a working rather than opposing it, and she is the only one of them built the way Kael is built. **THIRO** — a volume of air, at ten feet, with no visible part to it at all: men reach for their own armour and then cannot breathe, and there is no mark on them afterwards. **His counter is the one Valeria found and it is the only one there is:** he cannot spend it on air he is standing in the middle of.
