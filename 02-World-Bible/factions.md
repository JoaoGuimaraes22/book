## THE VESSANTINE EMPIRE · THE COALITION · THE PROGRAM

### The Vessantine Empire
- Ruling dynasty: House Vessantine. Once great — generations ago it used its power for good; it has rotted generation by generation into greed, pride, blood, and lust. The powerful rule over the weak.
- Corrupt to the core at the royalty level; good people and good rulers still exist within it (Character Law applies to civilizations).
- Conquered much of the continent; its subjugated nations have suffered for generations.
- **THE PROTECTION TIERS (author, s25 — keep them distinct).** **(1) The imperial guard** — white and imperial blue; the empire's ordinary armed men, drilled and everywhere, unremarkable to anyone who prices bodies. **(2) The ROYAL GUARD** — a specialised body that accompanies royalty; competent and loyal, chosen and drilled; they die where they stand without needing to discuss it. Dark grey with nothing on it; they arrange themselves across the DISTANCES rather than in front of the body, each already standing where a thing would have to come from. **(3) THE ROYAL-GUARD LINEAGES — VEL, KAETH, SARN** — a **different kind entirely**, not a better grade of the same thing: old houses inherited like weapons, bred and drilled across generations. **They serve the royal LINEAGE, wherever it goes** — a Vel escorting a daughter is ordinary. **The apex: the STRONGEST VEL ALIVE stands over the KING or QUEEN**, always has, and that posting is the entire content of being the best one. The Line is small enough that **its members know each other on sight** — by a walk, at distance, in bad light. **On-page discipline:** never write it deterministically — never say a Vel *cannot* guard a lesser royal — and never let an ordinary escort read as the Line's best; render the LADDER and where a given Vel sits on it, never a caste bar; keep the comparison LIGHT — one beat, not a meditation. **CANON (Ch. 22):** the princess's twelve are royal guard and **none of them is Vel** — Valeria would have known any of them across the arena and never turns her head. **They die in the invasion** when imperial security collapses inward around her (04 beat 19), which is why she reaches the boat with two children and no guard at all. **HOW THE LINEAGES ARE RAISED (author, s37):** they remove the first time — years of WATCHING killing, executions mostly, before a child is given anything sharp, so that a Vel has no first one and no number. Full entry and its rendering rules: `../03-Character-Bible/valeria.md` §THE DEATH-EDUCATION.

### The Coalition
- Recent alliance of the continent's remaining and conquered forces, formed to resist the empire.
- Founding grievances: subjugation, exploitation, and — unrevealed — the children taken by the program's harvests.
- Carries out the raid on the island at the end of Book One. To Kael, at first, THEY are the enemy — his first truth, written in fire; the saga slowly rotates the compass.
- Contains good and desperate people; commits atrocities. Neither side of the war is clean.
- **MOSTLY FIRE RESONANTS (author, s39).** The coalition's Resonants skew heavily to FIRE. Recorded as given; **why a coalition of many nations skews one element is an OPEN QUESTION** (`open-questions.md`) and is not to be explained on the page or invented here.
- **FIVE GENERALS (author, s39) — BIBLE-ONLY.** The coalition fields five. **Nothing in Book One says so**, and the word *general* does not appear on any page; one of them is on the island and Kael meets him at Ch. 30 without a name, a rank or a number. Sheet-less by design. Full entry: `../00-Author-Canon-SEALED.md` §THE COALITION'S GENERAL.

### The Program (mostly unrevealed — 00)
- On-page in Book One: fragments only — an insignia the father recognizes with horror; a forbidden-language word in a raider's mouth; the mother's face when she understands what has come back.
- Reader's takeaway at the end of Book One: the parents thought something was dead, and it isn't.
- **HALDEN IS THE HARVEST (author, s38 — 00 owns it; nothing on any page of Book One).** The judge is the program's collection arm, post-rebellion generation, and his imperial standing is real rather than a costume — which is the market layer sheltering the harvest in one body. **At this point its business on the island is hunting VASK and NERIS**, with Kael only the road to them; it is not collecting a child. He is genuinely strong, body and Art, and that is never remarked on (`../06-Style-Sheet.md` §Staging). Sheet: `../03-Character-Bible/halden.md`.
