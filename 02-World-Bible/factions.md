## THE VESSANTINE EMPIRE · THE COALITION · THE PROGRAM

### The Vessantine Empire
- Ruling dynasty: House Vessantine. Once great — generations ago it used its power for good; it has rotted generation by generation into greed, pride, blood, and lust. The powerful rule over the weak.
- Corrupt to the core at the royalty level; good people and good rulers still exist within it (Character Law applies to civilizations).
- Conquered much of the continent; its subjugated nations have suffered for generations.
- **THE PROTECTION TIERS (canon, s25 — keep them distinct).** **(1) The imperial guard** — white and imperial blue; the empire's ordinary armed men, drilled and everywhere, and read as unremarkable by anyone who prices bodies. **(2) The ROYAL GUARD** — a specialised body that accompanies royalty; **competent and loyal**, chosen and drilled, and they will die where they stand without needing to discuss it. They escort princes and princesses. Rendered in dark grey with nothing on it; they arrange themselves across the DISTANCES rather than in front of the body, each already standing where a thing would have to come from. **(3) THE ROYAL-GUARD LINEAGES — VEL, KAETH, SARN** — a **different kind entirely**, not a better grade of the same thing. Old houses inherited like weapons, bred and drilled across generations. **They serve the royal LINEAGE, wherever it goes** — deeply loyal, wholly purpose-filled, and they guard princes and princesses often; a Vel escorting a daughter is ordinary. **What is NOT ordinary is the apex: the STRONGEST VEL ALIVE stands over the KING or QUEEN**, always has, and that posting is the entire content of being the best one. So the Line is everywhere the blood is, and its top is fixed. The Line is small enough that **its members know each other on sight** — by a walk, at distance, in bad light. **On-page discipline:** do not write this deterministically — never say a Vel *cannot* guard a lesser royal — and never let an ordinary escort read as the Line's best. The page renders the LADDER and where a given Vel sits on it, never a caste bar; and keep the comparison LIGHT — one beat, not a meditation. **CANON (Ch. 22):** the princess's twelve are royal guard and **none of them is Vel** — Valeria would have known any of them across the arena and never turns her head. **They die in the invasion** when imperial security collapses inward around her (04 beat 19), which is why she reaches the boat with two children and no guard at all.

### The Coalition
- Recent alliance of the continent's remaining and conquered forces, formed to resist the empire.
- Founding grievances: subjugation, exploitation, and — unrevealed — the children taken by the program's harvests.
- Carries out the raid on the island at the end of Book One. To Kael, at first, THEY are the enemy — his first truth, written in fire; the saga slowly rotates the compass.
- Contains good and desperate people; commits atrocities. Neither side of the war is clean.

### The Program (mostly unrevealed — 00)
- On-page in Book One: fragments only — an insignia the father recognizes with horror; a forbidden-language word in a raider's mouth; the mother's face when she understands what has come back.
- Reader's takeaway at the end of Book One: the parents thought something was dead, and it isn't.
