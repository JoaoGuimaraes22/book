## CONSTRAINTS — a re-cut may not break these

- **THE PANEL'S GARMENT IS NOT CANON (author, s33).** The adopted panel wears an ornate pauldron. **She does not wear armour.** Book One's Aeliana is the girl at the tournament in the gown — *blue so dark it reads black until the light moves on it; gold at shoulder and hem in a quantity with no function except to be a quantity.* **The face panel owns the face and nothing else; a plate takes her clothing from a full figure, never from here.** Author ruled the face perfect and clothing fixable later.
- **A portrait PLATE of her is illegal.** Her sheet's hard rendering rule forbids a narrator verdict on her beauty. The ANCHOR is legal because anchors show the person. Her chapter plates arrive through THE RADIUS (what a room does) or through Kael's instruments failing.
- **Her hands do nothing** — the sheet has Kael looking for *where the hands go unoccupied* and getting nothing. Applies to any full figure.
- **Silver, warm and bright, never cold or ashen** — visibly distant from the drain-mark's pure quartz white (00). It is achromatic and therefore free against the colour law.
- **Hair loose by default.** The pinned construction is TOURNAMENT state: a Ch. 22 plate puts it up off the neck.
- **She sits on the WARM ground** — the capital's register (02/geography, style-anchor). Kael and Valeria sit on the cool one.
- **Her eyes are the hardest case in the set.** Pale blue on pale skin under near-white hair has almost no local dark available; the lashes and brows carry the colour alone.

## PROMPT — face panel (author's, as run)

Prepend **THE SHARED STYLE BLOCK** (`../style-anchor.md`), then:

*+ warm aged parchment ground, tea-stained and softly foxed*

*Subject: a sixteen-year-old girl.*

*Clear pale blue eyes — the only colour in the image, luminous and light in value, framed by dark lashes and brows.*

*Long wavy silver-white hair falling loose past the shoulders, almost colourless, rendered mostly as bare paper and the palest greys with fine ink strands defining the locks. Loose flyaway strands, uneven edges, naturally settled. The hair is the lightest mass in the picture.*

*Light freckles across the nose and cheeks.*

*Face slightly rounded rather than elongated, jaw softly defined but not sharp, cheeks with slight remaining fullness, small rounded chin, straight nose, short philtrum, visible slender neck.*

*Expression: quiet and slightly unguarded, gaze not fully composed, returning to the viewer. Lips closed and soft.*

*Dark embroidered high-collar garment with ornate fitted pauldron, scrollwork filigree.* — **the garment clause is NOT canon; see constraints.**
