  - **RUN 1 REJECTED (author, s64: *not great. this should be a illustration of kael among people, not him gazing into the cammera*), and the fault is the block's.** What came back is a chest-up portrait of the boy filling the frame with a village and its people behind him as background, his eyes on the lens. The block asked for exactly that without meaning to: it put the camera down the road ahead of him, turned him three-quarters toward it, and hung the crowd behind his shoulder, so the only thing the frame could be about was his face. **THIS IS THE THIRD INSTANCE OF ONE ERROR** — `ch20/the-cooper` and `ch22/the-hand-at-the-gate` are the other two, and all three put a subject's face at the camera when the block's argument was somewhere else. **The rule the three of them make: where the picture is about ground, people or a distance, the camera must not be placed where the face is aimed.** Run 1 also came back 1024×1536, a 2:3 canvas that is not one of the tool's five, where every other run this session was exactly 1086×1448.
  - **REBUILT AT s64 AND NOT YET RUN.** The camera now stands in the crowd at the side of the road at a grown person's eye height, side on, so the road runs across the frame instead of at the viewer; the nearest bystanders are cut by the frame edges with their faces away; the boy is one figure among many at about a third of the frame's height, seen from behind his shoulder in profile; and the gaze is nailed down in both directions — nobody looks at the viewer and no part of his face is turned toward it. The subject is stated as the ground rather than the boy: one lane of empty road with a moving figure in it, closed on both sides by stopped bodies.
