## CANDIDATES

- ★ **Go on, Ghost.** The road up: a man with a coil of rope over his shoulder, hat off in both hands, stepped back into the ruts — and the boy going by along the top of the ditch, a cart behind with a painted bed sheet roped to the rail and illegible. Four years of nobody turning round, reversed in one cart-length. **The safest thing in the chapter and the only one with no injury anywhere in it.**
- **I am asking anyway.** Elarine alone at her mark before the bell — hair bound, sword in the right hand, the left open and empty and held out from the body because doctrine keeps one hand for the Art. **The empty hand is the subject, and the chapter ends with it empty for good.** Must be close on her: two figures at distance on this sand is `ch23/empty` one day earlier.
