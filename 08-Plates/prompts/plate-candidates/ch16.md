# Candidates CH16 — The Terms

## CHOSEN

- **The ear on the brick.** The cistern court: the heir of House Corvane face down on wet brick with his cheek in a puddle and his hair full of moss, Elarine on the wall with a coin of flame standing unmoved on her palm, and Kael mid-circuit. *"I can feel where you are with my face. That is revolting. Do it again."* **The only comic plate the book has**, and the trio working at the same time. Ch. 16 canon: *did not look up from the flame standing on her palm, which had not moved in four minutes and was the only tidy thing in the court.* → `../plates/ch16-the-ear-on-the-brick.md` · adopted s47 `../../images/plates/ch16-the-ear-on-the-brick.png`
- **Nine strides.** Framed over her shoulder so her back is the picture, which is what the page states twice. The gorse above the old sea court: a boy standing up out of it with his hands where she can see them, and forty feet away a girl alone in the middle of the flagstones who has not turned around. *"You are too light to be one of mine,"* Ch. 16 canon: *She set her blades against the base of a column and walked out to the middle of the stones and stood there.* … *"Nine strides," Valeria Vel said.* … *She had not turned around.* … *"You are too light to be one of mine," she said, to the empty court in front of her. "They come in at forty and they land on their heels. Stand up."* → `../plates/ch16-nine-strides.md` · adopted s45 `../../images/plates/ch16-nine-strides.png`
- **The sill.** *(s63 — the revisiting session's extra plate; the pick handed back: author, s63 «pick one for each, go»).* The ninth week of the terms, and the one attempt in them that is a picture: he goes up the outside of the guest range, hangs off the sill of a vacant room two doors from hers with his boots off the building altogether, and stays there for a quarter of an hour — and she says nothing at all, because he is not on her floor. **The chapter's whole argument carried by one body**, and the set's second vertical-depth staging after `ch18/the-man-at-the-second-bend`; the camera is written on both of §Light's channels, as a high angle and as the surfaces it exposes. **3:4.** *Two doorway compositions were priced and left:* the door opening on *Where are you* restages `ch17/her-own-hands`' doorway one chapter later, and the page declines to name what her face did there; and the held `hand on the door` puts no face in a chapter whose extra should carry one. ## CANDIDATES

- **The hand on the door.** — **HELD (s45):** a hand and an object, which is the crop the author ruled oversubscribed (`../README.md`). A palm flat on the wood, the corridor empty behind it, nothing coming through. Eleven weeks in one object, no face in the frame — and the latch about to lift under the hand.
- *Alternate:* sitting on the floor of her room with his head back against the wall, waiting, because she finishes her own sentences.
