  - **THE BOY IS NOT ON THE SHADOW, AND THAT IS THE ONE REAL LOSS.** The block put him out along it at about two thirds of its length, walking its line heel to toe; he is walking on the open ground beside it, and no foot is set against another. **So the measuring is not visible** — he reads as a boy coming up the yard rather than as a boy pacing off a shadow, and the picture leans on the sentence beside it to say what he is doing. The shadow also came back nearer twice her height than the three the block asked for.
  - **SHE READS FOND, AND THE THREE NEGATIONS DID NOT HOLD IT.** The block said *not fond, not maternal, not tender* against a stack of positives all pointing the other way — laughing, going along with it, her eyes on her son — and §AGE's rule held: one negation cannot beat a stack of positives, and three did not either. **This is the folder's first plate of Neris looking warm at her child**, where `03` has her *not warm, not maternal, not soft*. **Adopted as drawn (author, s61)**; the sheet does not move, and the page it draws is a page where she is laughing in her own yard with her arms out.
