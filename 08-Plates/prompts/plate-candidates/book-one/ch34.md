# Candidates CH34 — The Run

*Written at s53, at design time. **Both plates are the mother, which is the chapter.***

  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s65, on the debloated block; 1086×1448, exactly 3:4).** Both faces came back wet all the way through, which is the whole beat, and neither mouth is doing anything about it. The boy has both hands on the grip of a sword far too big for him with the blade going out through the bottom edge. **Her arms landed better than they were written:** the block gave her one forearm across the coat and the other back and open, and the picture folds BOTH arms across the front of it, gripping it closed — so the thing inside the coat is more completely hidden and more completely held than the block asked for.
  - **THE EYE CONTACT DID NOT LAND, AND IT WAS THE DECLARED SUBJECT.** The block carried §Gaze's caps construction verbatim — *THE TWO ARE LOOKING DIRECTLY AT EACH OTHER … This eye contact is the subject of the image* — and the picture has his eyes up on her and hers down and past him. **That instrument is not reliable: it held on `ch21/the-doorway-at-the-end` and failed here and on `ch31/the-eight-breaths` in one session.** **BUT AS DRAWN IT MAY BE TRUER THAN AS WRITTEN**, which is why it stands: the page gives them *something under a second*, and a held meeting of eyes is a longer moment than the chapter has. What is on the plate is the instant on either side of it. **RULED AT s65: it stands as drawn and is not re-run.**
  - **THE RUN DID NOT LAND EITHER.** *Both are mid-stride with the weight forward, the hair and the loose cloth carried back off them* returned two people upright and moving at a walk, with only her hair carrying any of it. **§WRITE THE STATE, NOT THE ACTION got the state named and still did not get the motion**, where `ch32/out-of-the-door` — back foot off the ground, front foot down and taking weight, both feet in frame — got it completely. **The difference is the feet, and this block had cropped them out at the waist.** So a run is not a state written above the waist; the stride has to be in the frame.
  - **AND THE REGISTER COST GENERALISES: NIGHT COSTS IT, NOT ONLY A WARM SOURCE IN FRAME.** §Limits counts lamp-lit and fire-lit runs; this one had no lamp, no fire and no near source at all — an orange sky overhead and nothing else — and came back dense and dark with almost no bare paper. **So the design choice is daylight or not, and *keeping the flame out of the frame* buys nothing.** Eight runs now; the ink held again.
  - **DRIFTED, three, none owed.** The two quartz-white marks read as one, and it sits at the top of the head rather than as two near each other — the same *below the scale at which it renders* problem the s53 list already carries for a small mark on a face · his cloth is dirty and earthy where the block said *more red than cloth now* · and the crop went wider than the waist again, the fifth time in five.

## CANDIDATES

- **Elarine lighting the bowl.** Nothing coming off her hands, nothing crossing the air, and three hundred strides of hillside catching at once exactly where the fuel was. **Passed over:** the same picture as candidate 2 with a different woman in front of it.
- **Aurelian talking.** One good hand out and not touching a princess, correct in every particular, for two miles, for nobody. *"Put your foot flat, do not go on the toe of it, flat, all of it, like a horse — that's it."* **The warmest thing in Part IV. Proposed at s65 and not taken; live.**
- **Eleven feet of live wood.** Root and thorn driven up through solid rock across the mouth of a cut in half a minute, with three-inch thorns standing off it, by an old man bleeding out of the nose.
- **I have got you. I love you.** **Passed over at s53:** a hand on the side of a head for the third time in four chapters.
- **The terrace stood up.** Valeria one-armed on the lip below the shelf with her feet set — *the right one forward and turned out, the left one back and flat, which is not a fighting stance, which is a stance for putting something into the ground* — and three columns the thickness of a man's chest coming up through the terrace wall under her. **The folder has no picture of her Art. Proposed at s65 and not taken; live.**
