  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s63; the block ran as the file holds it after the 3:4 re-cut, at 1086×1448).** The camera sits at the side of the table at seated height and close in, the boards run across the lower third, the two of them fill the upper two thirds facing each other across it, and the eye contact over the offered knife is plainly the subject. The man leans in with both forearms over the boards, the boy sits back with both hands resting on the table and neither raised, the lamp is the only light, and his sleeves are down to the wrist so the tally-marks are not spent a second time in one chapter.
  - **THE CUT HELD, FIRST TIME, AND IT IS THE SESSION'S CLEANEST PROOF OF A RULE THE FOLDER ALREADY HAD.** `../../prompt-system.md` §The cut says name the landmark **and** say what it puts out of frame. Five blocks this session named only the landmark and went wide five times (`ch12`, `ch13`, `ch15`, `ch17`, `ch18`); this one says *the bottom edge crosses the near edge of the tabletop, so that no laps, no knees, no chairs and no floor are in the frame at all*, and the frame does exactly that. **Five failures and one success in one session, one variable.** The rule needed no amendment — it needed following.
  - **BUT THE OFFER REVERSED, AND IT IS A DEPARTURE FROM THE PAGE AND NOT ONLY FROM THE BLOCK.** Ch. 19 has *held it out across the table hilt first*, and the block spelled the hold out — *the man's right hand holds it flat by the blade with the grip turned toward the boy*. The run gave the ordinary grip-in-fist hold with the point toward the boy, so the picture offers it blade first. **Adopted as drawn (author, s63)**, and recorded here because it is a page contradiction rather than a drift: it is the same class as `ch15`'s blunting, where a described hold lost to the default way of holding the object, and it is the second instance of that in this session.
  - **AND THE LAMP-LIT SPLIT IS NOW THREE FOR THREE.** Like `ch11/mine-as-well` and `ch15/the-lamp-on-the-woodpile`, this one came back one warm brown with the paper tooth as a hard reticulated texture and almost no bare unpainted paper, against the five daylight runs that all held palette and medium. **Eight runs, one variable, no exceptions in either direction.** Still recorded rather than promoted: no correction has been attempted, and the s42 test wants one that is wanted every time.
  - **DRIFTED, three more, none a correction owed.** The lamp stands centred between them rather than at the far edge off to one side, which works · the blade reads longer than the block's *about the length of a hand* and brighter than *cleaned back to its own dull weather* · and the boy reads mid-teens against a twelve-year-old chapter, the recorded age drift continuing.
