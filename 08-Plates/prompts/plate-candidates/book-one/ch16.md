  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s63; the block ran as the file holds it, at 3:4, 1086×1448).** Everything the block asked of the body arrived: both hands hooked over the outer edge of the sill with the knuckles whitened, the arms straight and locked, the shoulders pushed up round his ears by the weight, the body flat to the wall, the boots clear of the stone and hanging free, and the head turned in and up with the cheek almost against it. He is not climbing and not looking down. The shutter above him is closed and there is no face at it.
  - **THE CAMERA LANDED EXACTLY, AND IT IS THE SESSION'S CLEANEST INSTRUMENT RESULT.** The block wrote the high angle on both of §Light's channels — in the picture-maker's own words, *high-angle view, the camera out from the wall and a little above him*, and as the surfaces it exposes, *the top of his head and his shoulders are seen from above, the top surface of the sill is visible with his fingers hooked over its outer edge, and the drop under him is open all the way down to the flagstones* — and the run gave back every one of those surfaces. **Set that against the three cut failures of the same session** (`ch12`, `ch13`, `ch15`), where the landmark was named and what it excludes was not. Same session, same tool, two instruments: **the one written on two channels held and the one written on one channel failed three times.** That is the strongest evidence the folder has for §The cut's second half.
  - **AND HE READS YOUNGER HERE THAN IN ANY OTHER PICTURE OF THIS SESSION**, with no age clause written in the block. The difference is not a word: it is that the whole small body is in frame at distance — narrow shoulders, a small head against a wall of dressed blocks — where a face near the camera has drifted older in every other run. Recorded as an observation, not a method; one run proves nothing on its own.
  - **DRIFTED, two, neither a correction owed.** **The raking light did not arrive as hard shadows** — the block put a low sun on the left throwing the sill and every joint hard to the right, and the light came back soft and ambient from above; **what did land is the one clause that mattered**, the thin warm line along the top edge of the sill and along his forearms, which is the only warmth in a cool grey picture · and the sill reads as a long ledge running the width of the wall rather than one window's own sill, which changes nothing about the hold.
