  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s64; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4).** The eye contact is the subject and it arrived as one: the two of them square to each other at close range, the house and its doorway out of the frame entirely, the slope standing pale and cool behind them, and cold flat pre-dawn light with no sun in it and no hard-edged shadow anywhere. The man's hands have come away and hang half curled at the bottom of the frame, and the boy's arm carries its turns of cloth. **And it is the best medium in the session** — generous bare unpainted paper, granulation, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, the ink linework strongest. **The daylight side of the lamp-lit split is now nine for nine.**
  - **THE SIZE CONTRAST DID NOT ARRIVE.** The block wrote *a head and a half shorter and narrow beside him* and their heads are nearly level, so the picture is two people of very nearly one size rather than a man a boy has to look up at. The clause was stated once, beside the pair it had to hold for, exactly as §Hands prescribes, and it still did not carry — **the first recorded failure of a size contrast written correctly**, and the note for the next block is that height may need a landmark in the frame to be measured against rather than a comparison between the two bodies.
  - **AND THE FATHER CAME BACK LIGHTER AND LESS WORN THAN HIS OWN REFERENCE.** Identity holds off `vask.png` — the same bone, the same wave and length in the hair, the same stubble — but the hair carries warm highlights where the reference and `canon-brief.md` have iron-dark, and the weathering has softened, so *a face like used tools*, the most weathered in the book, is not what arrived. Recorded, not corrected.
  - **DRIFTED, three more, none a correction owed.** The crop is loose again, at the hip where the block said the lower ribs — third instance this session · the track running away downhill is not in the picture, the background being slope alone · and the boy reads above thirteen.

## CANDIDATES

- **His father was sitting down.** From just inside the door over the boy's dark near shoulder: his mother at the table with her hand flat on a sheet of paper, and his father *in a chair*. **Passed over at s51** — the subject is a posture that has never happened, and a picture cannot say *never*; it would lean entirely on twenty-four chapters the viewer has to be carrying. Candidate 3 gets the man into the chair anyway, without asking the frame to argue it.
- **The pot of mint.** Chest-up across the table: the father back from the fire and pouring, the steam coming up between them, the boy's hands closing round the cup, the bound arm on the boards. No dialogue anywhere in it, and **the only picture in the set of Vask giving something.** Kept on the shortlist; candidate 3 covers the same table one beat later with all three faces in it.
- **Everything is by the door.** Two roped canvas bundles at the dark edge of the lamp's reach and the boy turned in his chair to look at them, the count finally arriving somewhere. The one image in the chapter about what happens after it; **an object at its own distance with a face at another** is the shape `ch19/the-third-board` and `ch24/the-ice` were both rejected for, and this one only survives because the face is in the frame with it.
- ★ **What has she not got?** The father's half of the door, and the block that would pair with candidate 5 instead of 3: the arm just let go of, one turn of cloth freshly tightened, thumb well below the cut, and the boy who has just answered him. **Unspent, and deliberately left so** — the dawn is two plates' worth and only one was taken.
