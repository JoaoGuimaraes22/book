  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s64; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4).** **The best depth in the session, and the stack came back in the exact order it was written:** the top of a dry-stone terrace wall nearest and lowest with the boy standing on it, back to the camera, small, arms down and hands hanging · the terraces going up in bands to a broken olive line · four miles of open field ground with the columns of smoke standing off it, thin and straight in still air, some low where the ground is wet and some strung along the run of a track · small groups of men, two and three and four at a time, going along the walls between them · the mountain at the top with its summit lost in cloud · and a strip of open water down the right-hand edge with boats standing in toward the shore. No face and no feature of a face appears anywhere in the picture, the bundle and the two short blades ride across his back on their cord, and the hardest edges in the frame belong to the smoke and to him.
  - **THE SMOKE READS AS STEAM RATHER THAN AS HOUSES BURNING.** The columns are thin, white and cold, with no glow and nothing dark in them, so a reader who has not read the chapter sees vapour off wet ground rather than eleven roofs alight. **Recorded as the one thing the block did not buy**: it named the columns and their placement and never named what is at the foot of them. The next block of this kind gives the base of at least one column something to be — a dull glow, or a dark head on the smoke where it leaves the ground.

## CANDIDATES

- **She let herself be seen.** Valeria alone in the strip of open ground in front of the spear line, covered to the elbows, hair out of its binding on one side, two blades, saying nothing, with the wall above and thirty thousand people behind. **Passed over at s53:** a solo Valeria one chapter after `ch28/rearranging-the-road`.
- **Elarine on the wall.** A hundred and forty strides out and below: forty men on a stretch of parapet, two of them children, one of them stopped. *They were in and he was out.* No face resolved at that distance, which is the entry's whole point.
- **The count.** Thirty-one thousand at the low end, taken off a strip and checked against a second strip. **Priced at s53 and left standing:** it is `ch27/the-sky`'s shape — a wash of thousands and one small figure.
- **The mountain is not empty.** From the high ground east: eleven fires spread across four miles of ground that has thirty holdings on it, men going along the field walls the way water goes along a gutter, boats on the eastern water, and the mountain standing in the haze with its top in cloud. **The widest thing in Part IV** and the strongest unbuilt candidate on this chapter.
- **Go back to your place.** The old woman putting the bottle away, working four steps back into the press, and standing in it — after being given the only thing he owned that was worth anything, correct to the minute and checked twice.
