# Candidates CH44 — The Doors

*Written at s68, at design time. **The seventh day in Doresh and the two nights after it** — Kael thirteen, the princess a year above him, Valeria of his age; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian, Elarine and Hazoun Sabrane are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it. Damir, Miresh, Tarim and Neshah have no references and no `03` sheets and are framed rather than resolved.*

**Everything decisive in this chapter happens where it cannot be photographed** — under a floor, behind forty shut doors, and inside a boy's arm — so the three chosen take the one thing each of those has that is visible: the four seconds in the sun that undo the hiding, the fold at night with one door open, and two fingers on an anvil. **The register runs cold-day, cold-night, dark-interior**, which is the widest spread any chapter in this run has had, and `ch43/the-beans` and `ch43/the-table` between them say how the two lit ones should be built: a warm source keeps the register when it is small, far and mostly out of frame, and costs it when it stands near the subject inside the picture.

## CHOSEN

- **The scarf.** Four seconds in the yard: Neshah up on her hip with both fists in the cloth, the princess laughing and holding the front of it and losing, and the whole of it coming down — two months of it, unwashed and grey with dust, with the sun straight into it and the dust making no difference whatsoever. **The only unguarded, joyful face this person has anywhere in the book**, and it is also the moment that ends them: three or four men in the yard all facing away, and Tarim at the mouth of the forge shed twenty feet off with a bucket, facing this way, the only one who can see it. **The picture carries the joy and the catastrophe in the same frame and does not choose.** 3:4. ADOPTED AS DRAWN.** **The frame carries the joy and the catastrophe at once, which is what it was built to do.** The tug of war is exact — the child's two fists shut on the cloth pulling it away and up, her own two hands caught in the front of it, and already lost — and both of them are laughing, which is the only unguarded face this person has anywhere in the book. The hair is the brightest and lightest thing in the frame by a distance and the sun is straight into it. Behind, four grown men in the yard with every one of them facing away; and at the mouth of the forge shed, twenty feet off with a bucket in his hand and facing this way, the one person who can see it. The rings and the short chain read on both her wrists. Daylight, and the register came with it.
- **Forty doors.** The fold at night from up above: forty low houses strung along the water down the length of it, every window and every doorway shut and dark, and one thing lit in the whole village — the open front of the smith's forge at the near end with a low red fire in it, laying one small patch of light on its own dirt and no further. **The chapter's title, and the only way to draw what the chapter is about**, which is forty people who were offered every roof in Doresh and did not open their mouths. Nobody is in it. **No reference attached**, so the block takes the STYLE Medium paragraph, and the warm source is built to `ch43/the-beans`'s successful shape: small, far, and spilling nowhere. 3:4. - **Two fingers.** The forge after everyone has gone: the iron off all three of them and swept into a leather bucket, both his wrists bare and paler where the bands were, and a boy sitting on the anvil block with two fingers flat on the anvil, asking a very small question and being answered for the first time in seventy days. **Nothing glows and the block says so outright** — the Art is not drawn here, and the whole picture is a face that has stopped holding itself, which no plate of him has ever shown. 3:4. He rests the whole hand on the anvil face rather than two fingers.

## CANDIDATES

- **Hold her arm.** Damir at the block with the chisel set on the peened pin, Kael kneeling on the far side holding her arm down on the horn with his one hand, the princess's arm on the iron. *She is not an orphan off a road. / No. / And you knew what you were telling me. / Yes.* … *My father shod for the man who held this road before the empire had it, and for the empire after. It makes no difference to the mule. / Hold her arm.* **The best human beat in the chapter and the first line in line for a fourth.** Held back only because Ch. 43 spends the family twice already (`the-table`, `the-beans`), and `Forty doors` says the same thing at the scale of a village, which the folder has never drawn.
- **Under the floor.** The three of them in the space where the boards sit up off the rock, too shallow to sit in, with boots crossing above and a voice in the yard naming a price. **Passed over as the third hiding-under-something picture in four chapters**, after `ch41/four-feet` and this chapter's own opening.
- **Oh. Thank you.** The second band off, and the princess sitting with both hands in her lap looking at them, and saying it in the empire's tongue to a man who does not have it. Very close in kind to `ch41/she-was-waiting` — the same person, the same lap, the same hands.
- **Valeria walks out.** Her second band away, standing up and walking out of the forge into the yard to do nothing at all for a while, and Kael coming out after her. The best thing Valeria does in the chapter and it is a person standing in a dark yard with her back to us.
- **Tarim at the shed door.** The bucket in his hand, a second and a half of being looked at, and then the bucket going down and Tarim going into the shed after it. **A whole plot turning on a face, and there is no reference for that face** — and the beat is already inside `The scarf`, where it belongs, at twenty feet.
- **The pile on the block.** Six bands, one of them still shut on nothing, and the chain, black in the grooves, on a smith's block. An object study in `ch40/the-holding`'s register, and it fails for the reason `ch39/the-black-in-the-iron` failed twice: §Limits says a substance in the grooves of metal does not render, and this picture is about that substance.
