# Candidates CH10 — The Asking

## CHOSEN

- **The waterer.** *The hollowed half-log came back a flat plank and the counterstone hangs from a ring; the author adopted it and both stand as drawn.* **Kael is deliberately out of frame and the viewer stands where he stands** — with the boy in it the picture is about approval. The split-log channel, the trough hung a little off its middle on an iron pin, the counterstone at the short end, water arriving in the cuts between the rows in fair portions with nobody there — and a man crouched over it, stopping the trough with one finger to test the pin's seat. *It will work itself loose at the pin by summer.* The longest he has ever stood in front of a thing his son made. Ch. 10 canon: *A split-log channel from the runnel above the plot; a trough on an iron pin, hung a little off its middle; a counterstone lashed at the short end. The trough filled, and passed its balance, and tipped — one measured pour down the cuts between the rows, the same to each — and the stone stood it back up to fill again.* … *He stood over the plot with his arms folded and watched the trough fill, and tip, and right itself with a knock of wood on wood, and fill again — the whole round of it, three times, saying nothing. - **The honey and the bread** *(s43)* *The wrists were the reason for the crop and are still not what arrives — the block asked for sleeves ending at the wrist and bare forearms in one breath; the hands carry the fact instead.* The study, close across the corner of the table: the jar set down beside the loaf with not one word said about it, the boy's eyes down on the jar, and the master's eyes on the boy watching him look. Two faces, a one-sided gaze, neither mouth open. **Four chapters plant on that jar and none of them explains it** — Ch. 2's honey in the milk, Ch. 4's *you're the reason the honey's always gone*, Ch. 7's wall speaking *what the honey said*, Ch. 18's *jar of honey that was never mentioned* — and one picture pays all of it. *Promoted out of the alternate below, which had the jar as set dressing beside the empty book.* It is also the crop that finally spends Severin's wrists, which `ch04.md` lost at full length. Ch. 10 canon: *The study was a room with too many rooms in it. Books to the ceiling on two walls; a table of instruments — a balance, glass, a thing of nested brass rings he had no name for; and on the sill, in a row of clay pots, green things growing that had no business being green in that light. The master set out bread, and took down a jar of honey and put it by the bread without one word being said about it, and Kael looked at the jar, and the master looked at him looking, and neither of them said the thing, and it got said anyway.* → `../plates/ch10-the-honey-and-the-bread.md` · adopted s43 `../../images/plates/ch10-the-honey-and-the-bread.png`

## CANDIDATES

- **The fern.** — **NOT TAKEN (s43), and the ★ moves off it.** *Nothing in the room said a resonance had happened* is the beat, so a fern that has lifted draws as a fern: the only way to show the lift is the boy's eyes going fern, hands, fern, which is a diagram of a sightline rather than a picture. **The fern is kept as set dressing in `the-honey-and-the-bread` instead**, on the sill behind them, which establishes the room Ch. 18 comes back to at the cost of one clause. The study: books to the ceiling, a table of instruments, clay pots on the sill — and the tired fern lifting, all its small leaves turned together like a bird waking, holding a breath in the thin light. The master's hands have not left his cup. Nothing in the room says a resonance has happened.
- **Three rows.** Summer's end, one bed, three rows out of one hand of seed: the fed row half again its neighbours, the plain row, and the threaded row that woke days early and finished no bigger. The Art and the world doing one another's work, drawn as a bed of beans.
- **The mist off the palm.** — **HELD, not rejected (s43).** Cupped water in a palm, a thread of fire spent into the top of it, and a ghost of mist standing off his hand for one breath. The chapter's idea-beat — *the Art does what you understand* — and a proper close-up. **Held only because a near warm source under a face is `ch09-aurelians-candle` one chapter back**, and the set can afford it later better than it can now.
- **Neris in the doorway.** — **REJECTED (s43).** *One hand on the frame, and she looked at him a moment longer than the doorway needed, and what was in her face was pride, whole, and under the pride, folding itself away as he came near, something else.* A fine moment and a near-duplicate composition of the adopted `ch02-the-doorframe.png`, which is the same woman, the same door and the same hand. **Recorded so it is not proposed again**; if the beat is ever wanted it has to be found somewhere other than the doorway.
- *Alternate:* the empty book open to its first blank page. *Full books answer. Empty ones ask.* **The honey jar left this entry at s43** and is candidate 4.
