## CHOSEN

- **Will I be allowed to hold it.** Two faces by the fire, the one warm minute in a cold chapter: a boy of twelve who has just asked something in the voice he used at five, and a mother in whose face *something came up … and stood there a moment, and went.* **The only plate in the book where Kael's face is open.** Ch. 19 canon: *"I'm going to have a brother. Or a sister."* … *"One or the other," his mother agreed.* … *"Will I be allowed to hold it."* … *He heard it come out of his own mouth and could not think where it had come from. It was not a count and it priced nothing and nobody in that room needed it, and he was twelve years old* … *and he had asked it in the voice he had asked things in when he was five.* … *His mother looked at him for some time.* … *Something came up into her face that had not been anywhere in it since he came through the door, and stood there a moment, and went.* → `../plates/ch19-will-i-be-allowed-to-hold-it.md` · adopted s48 `../../images/plates/ch19-will-i-be-allowed-to-hold-it.png`
- **We were owned.** Taken when candidate 1 was rejected. Both forearms down on the boards the way he does in the yard when he is going to explain a thing once, and the tally-marks lying there where they have lain in front of Kael for twelve years. **The viewer sits where Kael sits and the boy is not in the frame**, so the marks are put in front of the reader on the terms the page uses — *where they had lain in front of Kael for twelve years.* `ch03-the-back` spent them as something a son reads off a body without permission; here the father lays them down on purpose. *Alternates, both recorded so they are not re-proposed blind:* **the parents disagreeing** — *"It may not be them." / "It is them."* — the chapter's largest fact and the first disagreement their son has ever seen, ruled out at s48 because it is three people at that table by that light, which is `ch12/the-law-on-the-table` exactly. And **the palm on a bar already down**, Neris going to the door rather than the window because she does not go to the window when she is frightened: her hands occupied, her fear stated by a gesture and never by her face — and the composition `ch10` rejected as a near-duplicate of the adopted `ch02-the-doorframe`, same woman, same door, same hand. Ch. 19 canon: *Now he put both forearms on the table, the way he did in the yard when he was going to explain a thing once, and the tally-marks came up out of the sleeve and lay there where they had lain in front of Kael for twelve years.* … *"Three sentences," Vask said. ## CANDIDATES

- **The third board.** — **REJECTED AT DESIGN (author, s48): *the third board is not good, i dont like just a boot and kael at the end, does not move me.* Composed, never run, block deleted; reasoning on `ch19.md`. Do not re-propose.** Low across the floorboards at the foot of the loft ladder: the father's boot flat on the board with his whole weight down, the board sunk a fingernail's thickness end to end, and in the black seam a line of iron for as long as it takes to blink — with the boy beyond it at the table, hands flat, mouth shut. **The house's secret and the boy's whole training in one frame**, and the only person in the picture who sees it says nothing for years.
- **The knife, hilt first.** Across the table at the end of the night, the father turning it over twice the way he turns over everything before he gives it to anybody. *It is a tool. It has been a tool your whole life and it stays a tool, and if you ever have it in your hand on that road it is because everything I taught you before it has already failed.* Two faces and one object.
