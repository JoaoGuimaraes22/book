# Candidates CH42 — The Question

*Written at s68, at design time. **The sixty-third day, from first light to the going down off the Kharoun** — Kael thirteen, the princess a year above him, Valeria of his age; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian, Elarine and Hazoun Sabrane are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it.*

**The chapter is a day of decisions with a fight in the middle of it**, and the decisions are the drawable half. Six men die in forty feet and the seventh takes a spear in the back, and none of the seven is a picture — `../../prompt-system.md` §Injury's *draw the men, not the stroke*. **The three chosen are one distance apart each**: a wide country with one small body in it, a two-hander close at the water, and two faces a foot apart under the roots. They also run Kael alone, Kael with Valeria and Kael with the princess, which Ch. 41's three never did.

## CANDIDATES

- **He did not bring it down.** The soldier with the spear-butt up over the princess on her knees with both her hands still round the knife in his thigh — and not bringing it down, because some part of him looked at her hair and her face and knew what he was not allowed to do to it. **The hinge of the whole fight and the chapter's best single idea.** **Blocked, not passed over:** it is a grown man with a raised weapon over a kneeling girl, which is the one shape this folder has never got past a guard.
- **The wood took him.** The gap between the two close trees seen from the bank, the needle-dark past it, and a tracker bending to come through — with Valeria in the frame and not findable, which is the page exactly: *once she had stopped moving Kael, who knew exactly where she was, could not find her.* The best idea on the list after the one above, and the hardest to prove worked: a picture whose subject is that you cannot see something is a picture that looks like a picture of trees.
- **Boots on her cut feet.** Valeria sitting the princess down and putting a dead man's boots on her cut feet without a word, and saying nothing about any of it. Hands, care, quiet — very close in kind to `Leave it in`, and it would be the second held-hands-in-the-wood picture in one chapter.
- **The thumb.** Kael and the man on the ground, and the one second he would have back. The chapter's moral bottom and the most important thing in it. Not proposed as a picture: it is a child hurting a bound prisoner, and drawing it puts the reader in front of the act rather than inside the decision, which is where the prose keeps them.
- **She had watched the whole of it.** The princess on her knees in a dead man's coat with her hands in iron in her lap, having watched from the first lie to the last breath and not looked away, now looking at Kael with something in her face he cannot read. **Held back only because `ch41/she-was-waiting` is exactly this crop, one chapter earlier** — the same coat, the same iron in the same lap, the same look held on him.
- **Down the water.** The three of them going down off the Kharoun by the stream into the shadow the sun has not reached all day, the princess walking on her own feet in a dead man's boots, ten strides and then twenty and then nearly a hundred. Closes the chapter and is the only picture of the three of them moving. Passed over as a second wide landscape beside `The question`.
