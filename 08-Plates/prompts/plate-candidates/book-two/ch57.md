# Candidates CH57 — The Breath

*Written at s77, at design time. **The job, in one night** — the shutter, the man in the lane, the yard given away, the stair, the key that would not turn, the iron, and two officers raising their hands at each other in the first grey. Kael fourteen; the fourteen-year-old reference anchors, and the rung went into the two blocks with a young face in frame — **then the author cut it out of `and found nothing` before the run**, which is §Age in a plate's own instruction beside iron, and the reference carried the age alone.*

**THE FILTER IS HANDLED BY WHAT THE SUBJECT IS.** §Injury's hardest recorded case is restraint apparatus declared as the subject of a picture that also holds a child (`ch39`, refused twice). Here the child is the one closing the band, not the one wearing it — but the block still does not make the iron the subject: **the subject is a face that has just reached for something and found nothing**, and the band is written as a placement low in the frame, in the words that pass (*a plain iron band closed round his left wrist, with a pin through it*), with no category word anywhere and no hand over anybody's mouth. The knife in the lane is written the same way: a point *laid up under the angle of the jaw, resting there and not pressed in*.

**THE OFFICER HAS NO REFERENCE AND IS DRAWN ANYWAY**, because his face is the whole payoff and §A face with no reference blocks only a resolved face where the picture cannot afford one. He is described plainly and his face takes the finish. The other officer at the gate has no reference and is handled the other way — seen from behind, his raised hand large in the near frame and no features on him at all.

**Three registers on purpose:** a black lane with one far lamp, a stone cellar with two close lamps, and cold flat first-grey with no sun. No margin clause in any of them.

## CANDIDATES

- **It did not turn.** The stone stair, two locks a hand apart, the warehouse man's face not changing because a face that does not change is a face working, and an officer a hand away with a lamp saying *is there a difficulty* — and then the question that comes to the boy holding the light. **The chapter's tightest thirty seconds** and the strongest thing passed over; the crop is three faces at a door and it lost the slot to the moment the same stair pays off.
- **Take it.** Ambros in the good coat with the empire's blue at his collar, telling a commander kindly that he has earned a night off the wall — and six men walking across a yard toward a warm room with nineteen bodies on the floor of it. **The masterstroke of the whole book so far**, and blocked on Ambros having no reference: his face is the instrument and it cannot be resolved yet.
- **He did not give it back.** The officer folding the order and putting it inside his own coat instead of handing it over, and a boy in the dark along a wall filing a wrongness he cannot open. Small, ominous, and unresolved on the page — which is exactly why it is hard to draw and worth keeping on the bench.
- **That'll smoke.** The boy at the shutter making it his own idea, with a soldier looking up from a table. Passed over on the rhyme: `ch56/nine-strides-by-seven` is the same room from the same angle one chapter back.
- **Two men to a chest.** The turn of the stair, the boy with the lamp counting thirty-eight chests going past him on other men's shoulders. A fine picture of work and a thin one of anything else.
- **Looking the wrong way.** The last beat: three wagons of six years of a province coming over the lip, the whole camp looking at them, and one woman with her basket already on a mule looking back east at nothing. *I do not know yet. That is what I do not like about it.* **The chapter's closing dread and first in line for a fourth**, on `tirzah`, who has a reference now.
- **A report is a thing you get.** Valeria coming down off the line before the wagons have stopped, to see the boy come off the box with her own eyes. Her post has not moved in nine months and this is the one time it does.
