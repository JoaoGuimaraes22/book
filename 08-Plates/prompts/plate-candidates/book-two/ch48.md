# Candidates CH48 — The Honest Men

*Written at s71, at design time. **The sixth day and the morning after it, in Ishareth** — Kael thirteen, the princess a year above him, Valeria of his age; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor. Nobody else in the cast is on this page. Zahir and the two men on the bench have no references and no `03` sheets and are authored out of the southern register rather than resolved.*

**The chapter is 1,640 words and almost nothing happens in it.** Three pieces of information arrive sideways, out of mouths that are not talking to anybody in particular, and then a boy walks to a door. So the plates are about people **receiving** things rather than doing them, and the one action in the chapter is two children standing in front of a bench. Each of the three takes one of the four movements — the window, the gate, the bench — and the fourth, Kael on the floor putting it together, is under CANDIDATES and blocked on light.

**Two rulings.** **The head-cloth instruction is doubled on purpose** — once in the figure block and once in the preserve line. It ran single on `ch47/the-shape` and her hair came through; `ch44/nobody-said-anything` carried it twice and held. This is the block that tests it. And **no age rung goes in anywhere**: `ch46/the-strip` carried one and drifted, `ch47/the-shape` carried none and drifted the same amount, so it is not buying anything at this scale.

It is the best moment she has had in the book and it is made entirely of listening, which the folder has never drawn: a girl who was carried up six shelves of stone in irons two months ago, pleased with herself about a piece of work. 3:4, close. ## CANDIDATES

- **Back against the door.** Kael on the floor of the room with his back against the door, not sleeping, putting the three pieces together — and *the part of him that would have argued about it did not say one word. / He noticed that it had not.* **The chapter's true centre**, and the thread `03` now has open twice with nothing resolving it. **Blocked on light:** a bare room at night with no source, and the folder is five for five that a warm source costs the palette and the bare paper while nothing else lights a face. **First in line for a fourth** if it is taken at first grey instead of at night.
- **Twenty strides.** The house from where he stopped — a long low house at the end of a street that does not go anywhere, the plaster off it in two places, the shutters up on the upper floor in the middle of the day, and a bench outside the door with two men on it doing nothing at all. The establishing shot of the most dangerous door in the book; held because `The boots off` is standing inside it and has faces in it.
- **The lock still on the door.** A locked shed on the upstream side with the lock shut and the man's own mark still on the door and nothing behind it. An object study, and the joke of the whole chapter in one picture — passed over because it depicts a thing nobody in the chapter ever sees.
- **He is not selling them anything.** The three of them in the room in the morning, Kael telling them all of it including the part where it is a guess, Valeria hearing the whole thing without moving, and the princess looking at her hands. Passed over: `ch47/the-shape` is the same three people sitting and talking, one chapter back.
