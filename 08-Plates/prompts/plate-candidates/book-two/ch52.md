## CANDIDATES

- **Then I have ruled.** The council in the dark: the lamp burned out, nobody lit another, Kael's back against the door, the princess sitting back against the wall with the frame ticking under her, and the ruling given. **Her largest beat in the chapter and passed over on the page's own terms** — *The dark was good for this. Nobody had to do anything with their face* — so a lit version spends what the scene withheld and an unlit one is the faceless wide picture ruled against at s71 (*im much more of a fan of seeing a face, an emotion, an action, a close up*). Available on the author's word, framed at the shutter seams at the end of the scene instead, where there is light and the night has gone without him noticing.
- **The work stops.** *…the whole long building stood there holding rope and looking at three children on a bank.* The camera down the length of the rope-walk as at `ch49/the-rope-walk`, and everything in it stopped — the strands slack in the men's hands, every head turned to the near end, the wheel still. **The best composition the chapter has**, and the exact picture the s71 ruling swapped out: no face, no close range, and the block for its first half is written and still unrun.
- **It is. Entirely.** Asked last and straight at her, the one eye up from the stool, and the outlaw's procedure doing what no court ever did. Passed over because the stair already gives her the chapter; the crop is the two of them across the table, her standing.
- **The standing was the signature.** The man up on his feet behind the table with the stool pushed back, the boy reading what has just happened to him a second late. The chapter's cleverest beat and a hard one to draw as a state rather than a movement.
- **The finger on the table.** *A man on a job who is owed nothing is a man with nothing under his feet* — one hand, bare wood, the stone that used to weight the paper, the finger moving once as though striking a line through something. A hand-and-object crop, and the ledger register in one gesture.
- **The stone moved an inch.** The emptied room: the paper gone, the two seals gone, the third gone, and the stone sitting on bare wood — the crew already packed, told entirely by what is missing from `ch49/the-laugh`'s room. A thing picture, no face.
- **Zahir and the hoof.** The news of their going taken at length and to nobody in particular, a mule's near hind in his lap, in the voice of a man discussing weather. No reference and no face; a pure texture picture of the yard they are leaving.
- **The two sisters.** Valeria in front and not waiting, the princess spaced well behind with the water skin, and Valeria not turning her head once — the cost of it readable only off the set of her shoulders, which is where Kael reads it from forty strides ahead. A fine sentence and a diffuse picture.
