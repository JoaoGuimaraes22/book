# Candidates CH55 — The Job

*Written at s77, at design time. **Two dark tents, a winter town, and the fire where the job is named** — Kael turns fourteen in this chapter and nobody says so, Valeria is of his age, Aeliana fifteen; the fourteen-year-old references still anchor and the rungs move up one.*

**THE CHAPTER IS MOSTLY DARK AND MOSTLY TALK**, so the spread was built for register before anything else: flat cold winter daylight at the trough, a fire gone down to almost nothing at the crouch, and cool spring light in a shed. **Only one of the three is a face-to-face**, because two of this chapter's three big scenes are three people lying in a tent in the dark, where a plate would have to light what the page keeps unlit — the same objection that benched Ch. 52's council.

**THE THIRD BLOCK TAKES THE HANDS' OWN VIEW**, the folder's second deliberate use of it after `ch46/the-roof` (`../../../prompt-system.md` §The hands' own view: it costs the face outright, so it is only for a picture whose subject is the work). Here the subject is the work: **the hand that was destroyed at Ch. 40 has closed on a rope by itself.** It also keeps this chapter's third plate from being a second boy-looking-down-at-his-own-hand after `ch54/they-were-clean`.

**The bare-paper margin is asked for by name in the fire block** — two for two so far, both first asking.

## CANDIDATES

- **Nobody got up.** The fire where the job is named, and the circle not moving, one face at a time — Tirzah with the basket between her feet, Ambros with the coat buttoned, Oran with a hand on his brother's shoulder and Talo looking at the fire, the talker not talking, the princess with her sleeves down and her hands in her lap. **The chapter's largest moment and its hardest picture**: fifteen people, four of whom have references and most of whom have no face anywhere. The version that works is the circle at middle values with one or two faces resolved; **first in line for a fourth.**
- **He did not have a cup.** Borren sitting down last at the fire with his hands empty, looking round the circle one face at a time before he speaks. The tell that opens the chapter's second half, and passed over only because the crouch is the same man in the same firelight and pays more.
- **She laughed behind her hand.** The princess laughing properly in the summer tent, the sound going out of the open door into the camp, her hand up over her mouth. **The set still has no picture of her happy** and this is the second chapter running to offer one; it needs the tent's dark and the open door as its only light.
- **The shape across the door.** Valeria lying across the tent's opening with the stars behind her, not having moved the whole time — *if anybody ever asked her what was said in that tent she would say that nothing was.* A faceless plate by construction, which is what benched it.
- **Ambros keeps the house.** A steward of forty-one years running eighteen thieves in a shed as though the house had never fallen down, and the princess watching him from the kitchen door with a look Kael could not price. Blocked on Ambros having no reference and the beat being in two faces at once.
- **The frost.** Three shapes under blankets in a tent with the cold coming up through the ground, and the question said into the dark. The chapter opens here and the page keeps it unlit on purpose.
- **The fingers on his chest.** The last line — *he lay with his shut hand on his chest and felt it hold nothing.* The mirror of the chosen third plate and the wrong half of it: this one is the hand doing nothing, in the dark, where the other is the hand working in daylight.
