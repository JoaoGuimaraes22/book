# Candidates CH51 — The Ships

*Written at s75, at design time. **The governor's room, the street at dusk, and the room over the mule yard** — Kael and Valeria thirteen, Aeliana fourteen; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor, and no block carries an age rung: the room picture has the chain in frame (§Age in a plate — beside restraint let the reference carry the age), and the other two hold the line without it. The governor is featureless in every frame he enters — no reference — and carries his identifier instead: the one narrow band of imperial blue, the only bright colour on him, established in `ch50/where-are-they`.*

**A parallel writing session is on the book.** Every chapter quote below is pinned to the committed text of Ch. 51; **if a line moves, the quote on that entry has to move with it** (`../../../README.md` §The files carries the two constraints).

**The ch50 filter lessons are built into these blocks from the start:** no *iron*, no *bands*, no *children*, no *bound*, no *throat*, no weapon named in a ban; the one chain in the set is written as a placement — where it lies, not what it is for. The chain got its bands back unasked at `ch50/where-are-they` and can be trusted to do it again.

## CANDIDATES

- **Aeliana hears it.** *Say the last part again.* — sitting on the edge of the bed with her hands in her lap, her back doing the thing it had done at the governor's table, hearing that her father's south has fallen. A close, still picture of the princess register returning in a mule-yard room; **first in line for a fourth** — the embrace took her slot in the spread.
- **The survey.** The governor doing the whole of it — hair, mouth, shoulders, hands, chain, iron, then the face — *sixty-two days, and this was the first of it.* Blocked: the beat is in the looking man's face, and he has no reference to resolve it with.
- **Thirty-one hulls.** A man alone on his own wall in the dark, counting a line of hulls going past his coast with his mouth shut. The chapter's title image — but narrated, not present: the page has it as the governor's telling and Kael's picturing, and a plate of it would be the book's first picture of a scene the reader was never in. Passed over on that line; if the author wants it, the framing is from behind, the sea holding the last light.
- **The slat.** The leader's finger moving the shutter slat down the width of an eye, the single eye at the gap, the lamplight behind him. Very him, and a clean close-up — passed over because the set has two crew-leader close pictures in its last three chapters and the bag gives him Ch. 51.
- **The pin comes out.** *Wrists.* — the direct sequel to `ch50/the-pin`, the band coming off the ridge of skin it had sat on since dusk. A deliberate diptych; passed over on the consecutive-chapter rhyme precedent (`ch49`'s *boots go on* ruling), and available on the author's word.
- **Nineteen boards, through the wood.** Valeria at the top of the outside stair speaking to a shut door, the bar still on. The token said to the wood; passed over — the folder has a door picture (`ch39/the-door-at-dusk`), and the beat pays better inside the room a page later.
- **The steel off the ground.** Six men picking their weapons up in front of the gate, one at a time, unhurried, *the way men do a thing they have every right to do.* A fine sentence and a diffuse picture; passed.
- **Back against the door.** Kael on the floor at the very end, back against the barred door, the bag on the boards — the chapter's quiet close; passed over for the embrace, which owns the same room with more in frame.
