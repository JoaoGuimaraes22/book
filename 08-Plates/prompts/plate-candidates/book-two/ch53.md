# Candidates CH53 — The Round

*Written at s77, at design time. **The river before light, the camp at last light, and the fire** — Kael thirteen, Valeria of his age, Aeliana fourteen; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor, and both blocks with a resolved young face carry the rung. **The chapter's new people have no references at all** (`../../../../03-Character-Bible/crew.md`): Tirzah, Selis, Oran, Talo and Ambros are five named faces the folder has never cut, so a plate either turns the face away or draws it and becomes the design by default. Both answers are used here, once each.*

**THE FIRE IS THE CHAPTER, AND THE FIRE IS THE ONE THING THE MEDIUM FIGHTS.** §Limits: every lamp-lit or fire-lit run comes back dense and warm with almost no bare paper, five for five, and no wording has moved it — until `ch51/come-back` vignetted to bare paper at the edges unprompted, and its record left the instruction to **ask for it by name in the next night block**. This is that block, twice: both fire prompts close on the picture falling away to bare unpainted paper at its edges rather than being painted out to the border. **The two fires are also lit from below and out of frame**, which is the page (a fire dug down into the ground) and keeps the source itself off the paper.

**The far bank came back imperial at `ch52/rope-to-carry` because the Setting left it unnamed.** Every setting here names what is in the distance or says there is nothing resolved in it.

**No fourth visual register was minted.** The canon-brief carries three — the island, the Latin-Byzantine capital, and the south — and the coalition's east has no appearance canon anywhere in 02. Ch. 49's *plainly not of this country* is therefore drawn as the absence of the south's apparatus (bare heads, no wound cloth, nothing pale or loose) and not as a look of its own. **Their age is unstated on the page** — fourteen when the column came and *walking since* — so the block took the youngest reading it allows.

**Her hair stays covered, and the page never says so.** Fifteen people who have just been given her false name would know that hair on sight (Ch. 44, 46, 49); the chapter is silent, so the plate answers with the cloth, said twice as §Before the run prescribes. Fourth plate running for the covering, and the sleeves are down over both wrists, which the chapter says twice in its own words.

## CHOSEN

- **Nothing to do.** The camp already made around him at the end of the light — canvas up, mules on a line, the fire pits dug down with the flat stones stood round the side they came from — and Kael standing square in the middle of it with his hands at his sides, the only person there without a job. Tirzah crosses in front of him mid-stride, not stopping, and puts half an armful of mule hobbles into his right hand; his left hangs open and empty, and his eyes are on the hobbles arriving. **The inverse of every picture the set has of this boy**, and the chapter's argument about him in one frame: he has bought thirteen months of being told, and this is the first hour of it. **Her face is turned away to where she is going, which is both the answer to a face with no reference and the beat itself** — she looked at the hand once on the river and said nothing about it then and does not now. The one cool plate of three: last daylight, no fire lit yet. 3:4. ## CANDIDATES

- **Kael. Nothing after it.** The boy giving his one true name to fifteen strangers across a fire, and the whole circle not asking — *he watched the whole circle not ask, one face at a time.* Passed over because the beat is the circle and the circle is fifteen faces the folder has not cut; the version that works is a close crop of him with the ring behind him unresolved, and **it is first in line for a fourth.**
- **The hand on the basket.** Tirzah talking with the basket between her feet and a hand on it *without seeming to know she was doing it* — the basket that is *what there was of it that I could carry*, and the direct payoff of `ch49/nineteen-boards`, where it came back the most finished object in the picture. **Unblocked at s77:** `../../portraits/tirzah.md` was cut and adopted first run on the author's word (*i want her portrait*), so her face is pinned and this picture is what it is for. **First in line for a fourth.**
- **Three boats before light.** *They went up the river in the dark, before there was any light to go by. Kael never saw Ishareth again.* Low boats, coils, people sitting on the coils, the bank close on the left, and the one building in that city somewhere behind them, never passed. The chapter's opening and a cool register the folder is short of — passed over as a faceless wide picture on the s71 ruling.
- **The backs of three heads.** The middle boat from the last one: the woman with the basket with her back to the water and a girl either side, *the way a woman sits with her daughters*, and Kael seeing nothing else of them. Kael's literal view, and the picture of where the two girls have gone that he cannot follow. No faces at all, by construction.
- **Hemp under his right hand and nothing under his left.** The deficit stated by the chapter in its own words — and passed over as too close a rhyme to `ch52/rope-to-carry`, one chapter back, on the same hand and the same rope.
- **Ambros and the coat.** The man of sixty with the house's coat buttoned to the throat, *the only part of that house that is still standing up.* A face the empire has never had on a page — its own commoner — and a good one; passed over because the princess's back is the same story told from the side that pays.
- **I know which one it was.** The three of them in the tent in the dark, the fires down to nothing, her voice not the one she uses for ruling things. The chapter's last movement, and unlit: the black-room problem that put Ch. 52's council on this same bench.
