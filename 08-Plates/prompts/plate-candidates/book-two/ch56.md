# Candidates CH56 — The Pieces

*Written at s77, at design time. **A week of preparation and no job in it** — the count off a hillside, the pieces each of them makes, and the one piece nobody has. Kael fourteen, Valeria of his age, Aeliana fifteen; the fourteen-year-old references still anchor.*

**THE AUTHOR'S CORRECTION AT CH. 55 IS THE DESIGN RULE HERE:** *a face, character interaction.* Every one of the three is a face doing something to or in front of another person, and the two strongest object-beats in the chapter — Talo's twenty pairs of iron laid out on a blanket, and Ambros's paper going inside the buttoned coat — are on the bench under it rather than in the spread.

**THE BLACK IN THE GROOVES IS DELIBERATELY NOT ASKED FOR.** §Limits: a substance lying in the grooves of metal does not render, twice tried, *below what the medium separates; no demotion fixes it.* The page's own iron is *the black still in the grooves, worn down at the edges where it had been on a wrist* — so the block asks for the half that renders, the inner edge rubbed bright where it sat on a wrist and nothing else on them worn at all, which says the same thing with metal instead of paint.

**THE COVERING IS SAID LAST AND ALONE**, which is the shape `ch55/the-trough` suggested when the double form let hair through in the one block whose hair channel was also assigning hair to somebody else. Here the cloth-ownership clause is about the white and the blue, and the covered heads get their own closing sentence.

**Two of the three are night pictures at a fire**, and the third is an interior lit by a shutter-gap and a low hearth — the chapter is a week of evenings and one afternoon indoors, and the spread does not pretend otherwise.

## CANDIDATES

- **Get up.** Valeria with two sticks on the flat below the camp, the first time the two of them have fought since the island, and the lesson underneath it: *you take my blade with the left and hold it there … you cannot hold a blade with that hand now.* Then the tenth exchange, the stick stopped an inch from his head, and *you are pleased, and it thinks it is a hand.* **The chapter's best action beat and blocked on a rhyme:** `ch54/she-did-not-stop` is the same woman with the same stick on the same flat below the same camp at the same hour, one chapter back. Available on the author's word, and the version that clears the rhyme is close and inside the exchange rather than the whole flat.
- **Twenty pairs.** Talo's blanket: twenty pairs of plain iron with the pin peened one side only, and a heap of cloth strips rolled beside them, and a boy standing over it who has worn that iron. **Blocked by the author's own correction at Ch. 55** — an object study with the beat outside the frame is exactly what was called dull.
- **The only thing I know how to do with an evening.** Ambros at the fire with a board across his knees, folding the night's page and putting it inside the coat against his chest, against the buttons. *Nobody has ever read it. That is not what it is for.* Blocked on Ambros having no reference; the strongest thing in the chapter that cannot be drawn yet.
- **He was laughing.** Kael coming round in the open air with his head on a saddle after poisoning himself on purpose, and Borren sitting on the ground beside him — *which Borren did not do* — laughing quietly with a hand over the one eye. **The man's third laugh and the only one that is not at a room**; passed over because the iron already gives these two the chapter.
- **The well.** The boy with the cup in the square, and the town going about the end of its afternoon around him — a woman shaking a cloth, two men arguing about a horse that is not interested — none of them knowing what is coming up the valley before the leaves are off, and him looking for the thing that should have been in him about it and finding the count. **Passed over on self-repetition:** `ch54/they-were-clean` is a boy looking inside himself for a thing that is not there, one chapter back, and the picture would say it a second time.
- **Nine in white and blue.** The crew dressed as the empire at dusk — Borren in an officer's coat with the one eye, Ambros buttoned to the throat with the empire's colour on him at last, Tirzah not in white because a woman with a basket is her own uniform. A group picture and the payoff of the sewing.
- **He went toward them.** The last line: nine of them going down out of the fold in the last of the light toward a valley of lamps coming on, and a boy who was told at four years old never to go toward those lights. The chapter's closing rhyme with Ch. 1, and a faceless wide picture by construction.
