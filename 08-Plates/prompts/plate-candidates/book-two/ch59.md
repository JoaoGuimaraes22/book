# Candidates CH59 — The Fire

*Written at s77, at design time. **The night everything is paid for** — three sides in one fold, the empire's own fire off an officer's empty hand, Thiro closed at last, Ambros's four seconds, the warehouse man with the spear still in him, and an old man standing in the fire so that two wagons can get over a lip.*

**THERE IS NO DAYLIGHT IN THIS CHAPTER AND NO PRETENDING OTHERWISE.** Ch. 58 could buy a cool register off its afternoon; this one cannot, so §Limits is paid three times over and the variety has to come from **where the fire is**. Two of the three put it close and take the dense warm palette knowingly. The third puts it **far below and behind** — the `ch55/behind-her-hand` mechanism, a source that lights almost nothing — so one plate in the chapter keeps the night, the cool and the paper.

**THE HELD-OVER BEAT IS SPENT HERE.** Valeria's hand on the ground was passed over at Ch. 58 on the grounds that Ch. 59 is the fight chapter and would want it; it is wanted, and it is the chapter's only action plate.

**AND ONE PLATE ANSWERS ANOTHER.** `ch58/he-never-said-it` was adopted with the look missing — the depth flattened and his eye went past her. **The look is not re-run; it is paid on the other side**, in the one frame where she finds out, and the geometry is vertical instead of across a fire.

## CANDIDATES

- **But you must understand me too.** Borren and Ambros at the top of the fold by the abandoned chests, the coat buttoned to the throat and the blue at the collar the princess sewed, the confession said *the way he said everything, as a thing that had come in and a thing that had gone out* — and *I am not going to tell you it is all right. It is not all right. But I know.* **The chapter's moral centre and the direct payoff of `ch58/the-blue-at-the-collar`**, which set the collar up one chapter earlier. **First in line for a fourth**, and out of the three only because it would be the chapter's third close two-face plate in firelight. The killing itself — *he held the old man against his own chest while it happened the way you hold a thing you are setting down* — is the harder and better half of it and reads as an embrace, which is either the best picture in the chapter or a refusal.
- **Do not make it for nothing.** Valeria on the moving wagon with both arms across Kael, holding him down among the Emperor's money while he comes apart, her mouth at his ear: *He is buying this. Do not make it for nothing.* Two references, two faces, contact, and **the same arm that was in front of him on a boat**. Passed over on the count of warm two-face plates, not on quality.
- **The fire was real.** The empire coming into the fold with fire on the front of it, off the empty left hand of the officer at the head of the torch-column, going into the coalition line in a sheet. **The empire's own Resonant, revealed**, and the largest image in the chapter. No face for the officer and no way to make one; it would be a picture of a sheet of fire, and §Limits says a lit fire costs the whole palette for it.
- **Go on, then.** The warehouse man with the spear through him, his shoulder still under Kael's arm, shoving the boy the last of it. *He never learned his name.* No reference, and a spear through a torso is the hardest thing anybody has asked this tool for; §Injury's two specimens say it could be built, at a cost of several runs. Kept, and not now.
- **Now carry it out.** Kael putting the reins in the princess's hands with his good hand flat on the mule's neck — *You have carried the water. Now carry it out* — and her face doing a thing and then stopping doing it. Two references and a fine quiet beat; it loses the slot to `say it`, which is the same shape one scale up.
- **One leg, for half a second.** Kael's threading into the back of Thiro's knee, the never-still man made still, with Thiro's hand still in Valeria's hair. Three figures inside each other's reach and the most complicated thing in the chapter; the chapter's action slot went to the cleaner picture.
- **Under the jaw.** Valeria going up with both blades and closing the island rematch — *there was a girl's blade in his brain.* The arc payoff, and the reason it is not chosen is that the moment cannot be composed without the blade at the throat being the declared subject, which is §Injury's hardest ground for a picture that also wants her face.
- **The last of him.** An old man alone between the fire and the lip with a plain soldier's sword and his one eye open. Held back because it is inside `the one who was told` already, small and far, and because a lone figure in fire is the picture this chapter would reach for by habit.
