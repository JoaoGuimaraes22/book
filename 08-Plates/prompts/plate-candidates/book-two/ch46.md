# Candidates CH46 — The Narrow

*Written at s71, at design time. **The ambush in the narrow, the count after it, and the leaving the next morning** — Kael thirteen, the princess a year above him, Valeria of his age; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian, Elarine and Hazoun Sabrane are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it. Damir, Miresh, Tarim, the officer and the soldiers have no references and no `03` sheets and are framed rather than resolved.*

**Two things run through the list.** This is the set-piece the folder has been short of — *we have little action shots and closeups* — and it is the first sustained fight in Book Two, so **one of the three is an action plate and it is Valeria's**, the first picture in the book of her at full work. And **the chapter is not about the fight**: it is about a thing done deliberately and correctly that was not fighting, and the bill arriving afterwards through a boy's hands. So the three take one moment from the narrow, one from the count, and one from the bill, and they are an action plate, a close-up and an object study — three registers, three characters, no two alike.

**Two framing rulings.** Valeria anchors on the **portrait** and not on `valeria-14-full`, though the body is in frame: the full figure carries the royal guard uniform, and a gorget and vambraces bleeding into a fold in the south is exactly the costume failure the ownership rule exists to stop. Her road clothing is authored, as it was at Ch. 41–45. And **the princess's hair is out**, knotted back and dust-dulled as in `ch45/covering-the-holes`, not under Miresh's scarf: the page is silent, every man who came to look for her is dead by that line, and the silver is the lightest mass in the frame.

## CHOSEN

- **The strip.** Valeria at full work on the flattened grass by the water — a soldier's sword in the right hand and a knife in the left, *both hands*, a grown man going over backwards with his spear jammed across her body and never got round, and one man already face down behind them. Three-quarter length, both figures grounded on the same grass, the `scene-tests.md` §`fight-kael-valeria` recipe: the count per hand, the knife sized against her own hand, the spear given a contact point instead of a trajectory, dry-brush drag and flicked spatter in the style line, and the crossing of sword and shaft sited hard in the middle so the two bodies do not read as one mass at thumbnail size. **The book's first picture of Valeria at full**, and the set's action gap. 3:4. The shaft is held rather than *trapped under her left arm*, though the head is out past her and pointing at nothing, which is what the clause was for. **The burn did not render** — the left side of her face is clean, and the left side is the side turned away from the camera.   - **The register is the drift, and it is the first miss on this route.** The three no-reference blocks before it held the STYLE Medium paragraph outright; this one holds the tooth and the granulation in the ground, the grass and the coat, and **loses the ink** — there is no scratchy linework anywhere, the hand is modelled smoothly in wash with soft gradients, and there is very little bare paper. Wash-dominant where the block asked for a dominant ink drawing. **One instance and the cause is not settled** — evidence for the next no-reference block, not a correction owed here. Second and smaller: the hand rests on top of the sheet's edge pinning it to the coat rather than *spread flat under the sheet*, which is what a boy with one working hand would actually do and is the better answer.
- **Nineteen.** The princess stopped on the track with the grey cloak folded over her arm, extremely white, one corner of the mouth not holding and the rest of the face doing nothing, and her two hands folded in front of her and absolutely still — *which he had learned to read a month ago and had filed under the wrong heading.* Chest-up and close, the folded hands just inside the bottom edge, because the hands are half the moment. **Her number against his eighteen is why the last man did not get away**, and the whole of what she was for that day is a face holding and one small part of it stopped. The age rung is deliberately left out: she is fourteen against a reference cut at fifteen, and a rung by habit is what bloats the block. 3:4.   - **RUN RECORD (first run, s71; 1086×1448, exactly 3:4). ADOPTED AS DRAWN.** The fold came whole: fast white water and its stones on one side, the loose rise with needle trees along the top of it on the other, the stony track between, flat cold winter daylight and no sun. **The hands did what they were written to do** — laced, held at the waist, no grip and no movement in them, and the heavy dark cloak folded over the left forearm, carried and not worn, with the weight of it pulling the arm down. The hair is knotted plainly back, colourless, dust-dulled with strands out of it; the freckles are across the nose and cheeks; the pale blue eyes are the only saturated point in the picture and they are level on somebody just off camera and not on the viewer. Register held: granulation through the ground, ink dominant in the hair and the seams of the dress, bare paper in the water.
  - **Three drifts, and the first one paid.** The crop widened past *chest-up, close, the head large in the frame* to waist-up and more — the known widening — and it cost the head size and bought both hands, the cloak and the whole fold, which is the better picture. **The face is the one that did not land:** *no colour in the face at all* and *one corner drawn tight and slightly down* both went, and it reads composed and tired rather than white with one small part stopped. And the dress reads better fitted than *far too big for her, borrowed*.

## CANDIDATES

- **She sat down on the horse.** Valeria finished — sitting on a dead cavalry horse in the narrow with the blood coming out of her sleeve and everything spent, later off each man each time until there was no next one. The aftermath close-up with no action in it at all, and the counterweight to `The strip`. **First in line for a fourth**, and the strongest thing passed over.
- **The binding.** The princess kneeling to Valeria's opened left arm with a strip off her own hem — wrong twice and then right — two faces and the hands at the lower edge, *the first thing the princess had ever done for her with her hands*, and a thank you afterward with nothing else said about it. Passed over on repetition, not on quality: her hands carried `ch45/covering-the-holes` and this would be a second hand-plate of hers in consecutive chapters.
- **Twelve feet.** Valeria coming off the rise onto the third man's back with everything she had spent inward, the man below with his arms out at the lip of the hole. Spectacular, and the one composition here that a still picture may not be able to hold: a figure in air reads as floating, and no wording in the folder has yet bought a body mid-fall.
- **The horse on three legs.** The second horse in a place eleven feet wide, one forefoot up and never going down again, turning and striking out, with men behind it who cannot get past it and cannot get away from it. **The six seconds where the whole thing might have gone the other way**, and no face needed anywhere in it.
- **You will hear the difference in how I ask them.** Kael crouched at the officer between the horses, watching the side of the man's throat for the count without having decided to. Two figures, close, and the coldest thing in the chapter — held back because the chapter already spends its close-up on a face that is holding, and this one is a face that is not.
- **The handshake.** Kael's right hand out to Tarim at the wall — *which is not a thing they do in that fold* — and Tarim looking at it before he takes it. Quiet, and the whole of what the two of them settled and did not settle.
- **The last look back.** Kael two hundred strides along the south skirt of the Kharoun, stopped and looking back once at the fold going down into the north with the smoke standing up out of it. Passed over as a closer: `ch45/something-to-his-boot` and `ch40/the-road-behind-him` have both already been that picture.
- **Two fingers on the side of the neck.** Making sure, which is a thing you do with your hands. **Blocked on collision:** `ch44/two-fingers` is the same gesture under nearly the same name, two chapters back.
