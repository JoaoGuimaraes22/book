*Written at s53, at design time. **The chapter's worst material is not on this list as a picture** — s51's dropped grim plates are the folder's guidance on which half of a chapter like this to take.*

## CHOSEN

- **The lamp.** *"The lamp. Bring it to my left, hold it at the height of my shoulder, and do not put it down."* A princess of the empire holding a light at the height of an old man's shoulder with a dried stain down her sleeve. **The mother, the child and the work are all out of the frame.** → `../plates/ch33-the-lamp.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch33-the-lamp.png`
- **The sword.** The blade taken in his left hand halfway down, the grip turned over into his son's right, the boy's fingers closed on it by his father's own, one press, and let go. *Both hands. It is not a knife. You will grow into it in a year.* Ch. 33 canon: *Then he turned round and put the longsword into his son's hands.* … *He did it the way he did everything, which was with no ceremony in it anywhere: he took the blade in his left hand halfway down and turned the grip over and put it into Kael's right hand and closed Kael's fingers on it with his own, and pressed once, and let go.* … *It was far too big. It was a grown man's war sword and it weighed what a grown man's war sword weighs and Kael's wrist dropped four inches under it before he got it up.* … *"Both hands," his father said.* … *"Both hands. It is not a knife. You will grow into it in a year."* → `../plates/ch33-the-sword.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch33-the-sword.png`

## CANDIDATES

- **Facing the wrong way.** Carried down the slot over an old man's shoulder, awake, arms swinging, the sword still in hands that would not open — watching the yard, the pens, the grey flat and the roof of the only house he has ever lived in take at one corner and go up. **The strongest unbuilt image left in Book One.**
- **Walking out with nothing in either hand.** The father crossing his own ground toward four hundred men, seen from behind, empty-handed, after the sword has changed owner.
- **The mountain going.** An old man on one knee at the eastern edge of the yard with both palms flat on the stone, and four hundred feet of terrace and olive line letting go and going down the way water goes down.
- **Kael holding his brother.** *He weighed about as much as a lamb. Less.* — a fingernail, a strip of linen, and four seconds of being completely and unspeakably happy. **Deferred at s53** to a chapter that can hold it without the mother's body or the newborn's eye in the frame; `ch36/the-finger` is where it went.
- **Nobody was surprised.** The mother watching her elder son's face and not the child's, from the floor. **Blocked by the seal**, not by the tool: what it draws is a reaction to a thing this sheet has kept out of frame.
