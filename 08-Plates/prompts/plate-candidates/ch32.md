# Candidates CH32 — The Mountain

*Written at s53, at design time.*

## CHOSEN

- **The white in her hair.** Firelight, two faces, and one loose piece of her hair hanging between them going white from the root the way frost runs across the top of a pail — with her hands on his chest and his father's hands on his shoulders. **The whole family in one crop and the father is two hands.** → `../plates/ch32-the-white-in-her-hair.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch32-the-white-in-her-hair.png`
- **The lights on the mountain.** From the top of the woodpile he has stood on since he was four: seven lights below the bend, nineteen strung along the terrace under the olive line, one mass of light at the bottom of the path. **The deliberate inverse of `ch01/fallen-stars`.** Ch. 32 canon: *Kael went up onto the woodpile.* … *From the top of it you could see the whole of the lower ground.* … *He had stood up here his whole life. He had stood up here at four years old and looked down at the lights in their corner and been certain they were something else entirely.* … *There were lights on the mountain.* … *They were below the second bend and off to the east of it. Four of them.* … *Then, further down and further east, on the ground above the runnel where the olive line ran, there was a string of them coming along the terrace one behind another, and he counted that string twice and got nineteen both times.* … *there is something down at the bottom of the path with a lot of light on it that I have not been able to count yet.* … *the cloud had come down onto the top of the mountain and stayed there, and the underside of it was orange from one end of the island to the other, and it moved the way water moves.* → `../plates/ch32-the-lights-on-the-mountain.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch32-the-lights-on-the-mountain.png`
- **The half-step.** Two boys four feet apart in a mountain yard at night with a year of silence between them and a stranger's shoulder already coming into the ground between them. **The last time they see each other, and the folder's only picture of the two of them. Unblocked at s65 by `oss-14.png`.** Ch. 32 canon: *And three back from him, in a coat that was too good and had never been the right coat, his face open and turned and everything on it, was Oss.* … *Oss saw him. Kael watched it arrive: the recognition, and the thing after the recognition, and the hand that came half up off his side and did not finish.* … *Oss got about half a step forward.* … *Then the line moved him on, the way a line does, and somebody behind him needed the ground he was standing on, and he went up past the woodpile with the others, and that was that.* → `../plates/ch32-the-half-step.md`

## CANDIDATES

- **The two short blades.** Put down on the boards beside his right hand where he can reach them lying down, unmatched, one with a nick dressed out of it by somebody who knew how. *Kael did not say thank you, because it would have made it a smaller thing.*
- **The briefing.** A boy of thirteen giving the ground to a captain of the royal guard, pointing with a short blade — the path, the two bends, the scree, the woodpile that will go with three men on it. *He has been telling us for four years that everything is measurable and we have all been extremely funny about it.* **Proposed at s65 and not taken; live.**
- **The princess in the yard.** In the shoes she wore on a raised floor, a soldier's cloak over a gown with gold along a hem ruined to the knee, her hair down on one side, standing where they put her and looking at the orange in the cloud and not at the house.
- **Valeria in front of the house.** *It was a small house. One room, a loft, a door that held.* She looks at it for a long moment and asks which way the water is. **A year, a treeline, a sea court, and she still does not ask.** **Proposed at s65 and not taken; live.**
