  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s64; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4).** The hollow, the undercut lip and the fallen wall all arrived, with the four of them packed into it and the boy crouched at the mouth with both hands on a long fall of dead bramble, half drawn across. **The bramble reads as a thing that fell and not a thing that was placed**, which is the beat the whole picture exists for. The woman is well back in the dark with the small one against her and its face turned into her neck, the cloth bundle stands upright in front of the smallest, and the two lit faces are the most finished things in the frame. **The red landed exactly as sited** — dried and gone brown to the elbow on his forearms, nothing bleeding anywhere, and nothing about his face acknowledging it.
  - **THE LOOK BETWEEN THEM ONLY HALF ARRIVED.** Hers is right — level, out of the dark, unconvinced, over the child's head — and his eyes go past her rather than onto her, so what the picture shows is a boy still watching the ground while she looks at him. **It is not the fault the earlier runs had** (`ch20/the-cooper`, `ch22`): the gaze did not come to the camera, it went to the danger. Better as drawn, and recorded so the next two-hander in a hiding place knows that a body doing fieldcraft will not give its eyes to a conversation.
  - **DRIFTED, two, neither a correction owed.** The older child's face came back fully drawn where the block put both children's faces out of resolution — no reference exists for either, and nothing about them is canon, so it costs nothing · and he reads fifteen or sixteen again.

## CANDIDATES

- **The fold under the terrace.** A woman and three children in a hole in the bank with dead bramble pulled across it so that it looks like it came down on its own. *"Stay until it's dark. Not dusk. Dark."* **His conscience acting, at a cost of eleven minutes he counted and carried** — and he never learns whether it held.
- **Nobody looks up.** Flat on the tiles eight feet above a man who has stopped at the wall to look along the terrace, close enough to count the back of his neck. The chapter's cleanest piece of fieldcraft.
- **The lamp on the tread.** A dark stair, a lamp caught before it landed and set upright on the tread still burning, and a boy going up past it. **A hand-and-object crop, and cold as anything in the book.**
- **I'm sorry, little one.** Fifteen feet apart on a threshing floor, the big man in a plain coat with nothing on it, saying it gently in Kael's mother's accent with the ends of the words where she puts them. **Blocked:** he has no portrait and he is not finished with this book.
- **Carried.** One arm under the back and one under the knees, the white running down the chest against the boy's cheek and going round him, the ground going past, the mountain black above with its top in the cloud. **Passed over at s53** only because it is the same figure in the same minute in the same light as candidate 6.
