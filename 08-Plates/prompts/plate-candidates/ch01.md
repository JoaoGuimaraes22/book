- **The fallen stars.** Night at the flat rock: Neris standing with Kael wrapped in the big wool, her hand turning his chin down — and far below, the town in a heap of warm light where the land meets the sea, doubled and moving in the black water. From behind and below, the two of them shapes against the spill. ## CANDIDATES

- **The catch.** Vask's hand up without looking, the slate already in it, his face turned away and still talking to the goat. The eye goes to the hand; the face is elsewhere — the honest way to draw a man whose eyes have nothing in them.
- **The room of true things.** The one room from the loft's height: the fire small and orange in its hole, the table, the chest, the wall where every tool hangs in its place. No figures, or one hand crossing to straighten one. The house as a set of rules before the book has any people in it.
- **The broken ice.** *(Promoted out of the alternate at s61 and priced.)* A hard morning at the pail: the boy holding the broken disc up between his face and the low winter sun, looking through it. **The book's method arriving at four years old** — a child running an experiment on a promise — and the picture `ch05/the-two-pails` exists to kill. The ice is reserved bare paper with the sharpest step at its edge, which is the instrument the folder is best at; one figure, `kael-4` anchored. A hand-and-object crop with a face in it, which the s45 ruling says has to earn itself.
- **She counted the none.** — **PASSED OVER AT s61, and recorded so it is not re-proposed:** the chapter's real secret (*There were no boats at night. She counted them anyway.*) and both compositions it wants are already owned — `ch02/the-doorframe` has this woman at that door and `ch04/blade-across-her-knees` has her at that window at night. A third woman-at-an-opening is what `ch10` and `ch19` each had a candidate rejected for. If the beat is ever wanted it has to be found somewhere other than the door and the window.
