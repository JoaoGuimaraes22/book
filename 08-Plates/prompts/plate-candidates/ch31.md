  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s65; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4 — the third named ratio and the third exact hit).** The whole street arrived and none of it is decoration: the gutter set down one side, the bay tree in its pot as the only strong green in the picture, the barred timber gate up the street with six men on the inside of it seen from behind, the woman crossing a doorway with the basin, and the servant standing over two children on a step where the street bends. **The argument landed, which is the thing this plate exists for — every other person in the frame has a job, and she is the only one not doing anything.** The register held completely on daylight: bare paper through the road and the walls, hard-edged blooms, the ink the strongest element. Her green is the most saturated point in the picture; the dried red is on the forearm by site, gone brown, and nothing is bleeding.
  - **THE CUT DRIFTED, AND THIS ONE IS EVIDENCE.** The block named the waist and listed four things to exclude — *her hands, her hips, her legs and the ground immediately under her* — which is precisely the construction s63 found held the cut five times to one. It came back at the hip, with a belt, a forearm and a hand in the bottom corner. **So the named-exclusion cut is not the settled instrument s63's count made it look; with the ratio ALSO named, both together did not hold it.** Recorded as evidence and not as a correction owed — the wider crop puts the marked forearm in the frame, which the block had thrown away.
  - **THE GAZE DRIFTED, ON THE CHANNEL THAT ALREADY HAS TWO INSTANCES** (`ch14`, `ch17`): sent up the street toward the bend, and told twice where it does not go, it came back at the camera or a hand's width past it. **The cause is named in §Scenes and plates and I did not apply it:** in a close crop the reference's own mood beats the prompt — and `valeria-14.png` is composed *gaze returning to the viewer* — so the fix is to put the eyes on an object, not to write a third ban. There was no object in this frame for them to be on.
  - **AND SHE READS WELL ABOVE THIRTEEN — sixteen or eighteen — with no age clause written**, the standing drift again, one run after `ch30/the-fold-under-the-terrace` recorded the same thing. It stands as drawn.
