# Candidates CH31 — The Line

*Written at s53, at design time. **Neither plate is of Valeria, in her own chapter** — she is the eye in both and appears in neither, which is what she does for the whole of it.*

## CHOSEN

- **The hand flat.** By the cistern head in the evening: a woman who has just put her own name to a wall gives her daughter instructions, and then puts her hand flat against the side of her head and leaves it there. **The mother's face is out of the frame and Elarine's is the picture.** Ch. 31 canon: *She found her in the corner of the yard by the cistern head, with her mother, and stopped where she was, because you do not walk into that.* … *Something moved in Elarine's face and did not get anywhere.* … *Then she put her hand flat against the side of her daughter's head and left it there, and neither of them said anything more.* … *There was a burn along her jaw that nobody had looked at yet.* → `../plates/ch31-the-hand-flat.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch31-the-hand-flat.png`
- **The shutter.** A man goes to the window himself rather than sending anybody, closes it, comes back and finishes his sentence — with nineteen people standing round a bare table in lamplight behind him and the last of a cold daylight going out of the gap. **No face is resolved anywhere in it.** Ch. 31 canon: *The room had been somebody's dining hall and there were nineteen people in it.* … *a long table down the middle with nothing on it, chairs pushed back, everybody standing.* … *Outside, past the shutters, the sound off the hill was still going.* … *It had been going since before she came in. It was not loud. It came in under the talking the way rain comes in under talking, and every so often a piece of it stood up out of the rest and had one person inside it, and then went back down again.* … *Nobody in that room had mentioned it once.* … *A man had begun a sentence about the north bastion when he stopped, went to the window himself rather than sending anybody, closed the shutter, came back, and finished the sentence.* → `../plates/ch31-the-shutter.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch31-the-shutter.png`

## CANDIDATES

- **Drusane's two hands.** *Then he took his son's face in both hands and held it, and Lucan stood and let him.* **Blocked:** no `images/portraits/lucan.png`.
- **You were always enough.** Oss's father holding him by both arms, not mentioning the name once. **Blocked** for the reason `ch13.md` gave in the first place: there is no portrait of Oss and a plate is not where a face gets decided.
- **Severin agreed with by the wrong man.** *There was nothing on his face at all, and she thought that a man who has just been agreed with does not usually look like that.* **Passed over at s53:** it asks the block for a face doing nothing, which §Gaze forbids and which renders as vacancy. Available to a session willing to solve it.
- **The princess's one contribution.** *"Then I do not see the difficulty,"* and the turn of a hand on the arm of a chair, with nineteen standing people and one seated girl. **Passed over:** it is `ch22/the-box`'s composition indoors.
