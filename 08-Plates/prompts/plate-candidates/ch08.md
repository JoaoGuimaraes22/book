## CANDIDATES

- ★ **The roll.** The book the size of a door, the clerk's hand with ink on it like a renewed scar, and the pen standing patient as a heron over the long empty stretch where the other names kept going — then the small line that makes the emptiness official. *Empty is true.* Page, pen, hands; no faces. **The spine of the whole book in one object.**
- **Aurelian crosses the hall.** Room arriving around him where nobody moved. Dark-gold hair, tall for the cohort and built a year ahead of it, four behind him, pleasant — *never sneering.* At the table he is coming to, one boy has not moved either, and is the only still thing in the picture.
- *Alternate:* Priscian's great slate with **the minima** written on it a hand high, the benches dozing on their fists, and two children in the room not dozing — one hungry, one pricing.
