# Candidates CH35 — The Range

*Written at s53, at design time.*

## CHOSEN

- **The white room.** Eight people on the boards of a boat inside a room made of steam, the walls of it standing on the sea and going up past the mast, lit from underneath through the water, and the sky above it open and black. **The best thing in Book One that nobody had drawn.** → `../plates/ch35-the-white-room.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch35-the-white-room.png`
- **The spotter.** A boy kneeling at the strake with a day-old child up on his shoulder and his hand flat on its back, mouth wide open on a shouted word, calling a man's feet for his mother. **Two open mouths and neither of them is heard.** → `../plates/ch35-the-spotter.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch35-the-spotter.png`
- **The sail going over the side.** Valeria up on the thwart with a wet coat and Aurelian taking the bottom of it one-handed, the two of them walking a burning sail over the side while it is still going. **The one moment those two are a pair, and the first plate to carry her burn.** Ch. 35 canon: *Valeria Vel went up onto the thwart with the wet coat and got the burning sail down off the spar and Aurelian Corvane took the bottom of it one-handed and the two of them walked it over the side into the water while it was still going, and the sea put it out with a noise like an indrawn breath and it went away behind them, black and steaming.* → `../plates/ch35-the-sail-over-the-side.md`

## CANDIDATES

- **The mother in the bow.** Standing up in it facing the island with her hands loose at her sides, and then not choosing any more. **Passed over:** `ch34/the-bowl` with water in front of her instead of stubble.
- **Cut it.** Elarine getting up off the boards with nothing left in her and cutting the mast away. **Proposed at s65 and not taken; live.**
- **Getting smaller.** Lying along the strake watching the man who killed his father stand on a rock with his hands behind his back. **Blocked:** no portrait of him, and `ch13.md` says the work that unblocks that is a portrait.
