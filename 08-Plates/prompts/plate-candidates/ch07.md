- **The Temple from the road** *(s61 — the revisiting session's extra plate, chosen from Ch. 1–10 with the pick handed back: author, s61 «you choose, do choose from ch 1 to ch 10»).* The last shoulder of the coast, and the thing that has been a pale shape his whole life turning out to be full. **The set's one structural gap:** a third of Book One happens inside those walls and the folder has no picture of them from outside — `ch08` is a room, `ch09` a yard, `ch16` a court, `ch23` the arena sand. **Both of this chapter's other plates are mountain-register**, so this is its other half, and it is the same morning as `ch07/kael-of-what` an hour later, with the same cloth and the same bag on the same shoulder. Built on `ch18/the-man-at-the-second-bend`'s method — viewer placed first, the frame stacked bottom to top, the scale carried by a ratio and the age by one clause under the fourteen-year-old anchor. **No face is resolved anywhere in it**, his or the court's, which is what the page says of him: *None of it showed.* **The Nouns risk is the whole of the block** — *temple* returns columns and a pediment, so the place is written by construction throughout and the default killed once by name. The winner is the lit face of the tower and the rival demoted is the sky, which is why the sky is a flat pale wash and the reserved bare paper goes on the tower (`ch21/the-surge`: the reserve erases the object when it covers the object). **4:3.** The master is out of frame by choice: the page has him on the road alongside, and one small figure is the size of the thing.
