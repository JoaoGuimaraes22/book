# Candidates CH7 — The Opening

## CHOSEN

- **Kael of what?** The master waiting on the near side of the split rock (size fixed by `ch05.md`; **`ch06.md` carries the wording that stops it rendering as an obelisk**), leaning on a stick he does not need. **Plant this one deliberately** — the same question is answered at a wall, in wartime, later in the book, and the two pictures should rhyme on purpose. Ch. 7 canon: *The master was waiting at the split rock. He stood on the near side of it, on the path, leaning on a stick he did not need for leaning, and he watched Kael come down through the pens the way he watched everything, unhurried, and he did not say good morning. He said, "Who are you?"* … *"Of—" and there was nothing to put there. He had never needed anything to put there.* → `../plates/ch07-kael-of-what.md` · adopted s42 `../../images/plates/ch07-kael-of-what.png`
- **The wasters.** *The waster came back a flat plank; `ch07.md` carries the corrected object.* Winter evening, the drawknife, shavings coming off in whispers, one waster held out level on two fingers to see where it wants to tip — and across the room a boy healing by the same fire. The father building the things they are going to fight each other with. *Heavier at the tip than steel.* Ch. 7 canon: *Those weeks his father sat by the fire in the evenings with the drawknife and a stack of seasoned wood, and made swords* … *shavings taken off in whispers, the thing hefted, turned, hefted again, held out level on two fingers to see where it wanted to tip. Two of them, one to Kael's arm and one longer* … *The boy healed by the fire, and the father built the things they were going to fight each other with by the same fire, and the wind came to the door and pushed, and the door held.* → `../plates/ch07-the-wasters.md` · adopted s42 `../../images/plates/ch07-the-wasters.png`

## CANDIDATES

- ★ **The Temple from the road.** The last shoulder of the coast, and the thing that has been a pale shape his whole life turning out to be full: walls out of the headland, courts inside the walls, the tower with the morning sun on its face, and children crossing the outer court in twos and threes at the pace of belonging there. A small boy on the road with his hand on the strap of a bag, near where the knife is not.
- *Alternate:* the knife back on the wall, cleaned, in its place in the order of tools. *We know everything, and here is your knife.*
