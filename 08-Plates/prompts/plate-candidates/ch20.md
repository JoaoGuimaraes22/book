# Candidates CH20 — The Word

*Written at s48, at design time.*

## CHOSEN

- **You are my friend.** The cistern court twenty minutes before anybody has a reason to be there: Elarine turned round on the bench with the satchel set down and her chin up a fraction, and a boy who takes the word apart to see what it is made of because he has not got one. *"It is not a complicated word."* **Her face choosing to spend itself**, which no other plate of her has drawn. Ch. 20 canon: *She was in the cistern court twenty minutes before anybody had any reason to be, sitting on the bench nobody sat on, with her satchel on her knees and her hands on the satchel.* … *Elarine Sedrane turned round on the bench and looked at him properly for the first time that morning.* … *"I am going to say something now, and you are going to do something strange with it, and I have decided in advance not to hold that against you."* … *"You are my friend," Elarine said. "It is not a complicated word. I have been using it about you privately since the summer, and I did not say it, because I could see exactly what you would do with it." Her chin came up a fraction.* → `../plates/ch20-you-are-my-friend.md` · adopted s48 `../../images/plates/ch20-you-are-my-friend.png`
- **Val.** The sea court at the end of the cold term, the lamps coming up along the water one at a time: two people side by side on the cold flagstones, both looking out, and one word going out and not coming back. **The folder's first two-shot with no eye contact anywhere in it** — the register's one constant, broken because the page breaks it. Ch. 20 canon: *She sat down on the stones beside him with her forearms across her knees.* … *"Then do not use a word," said Valeria Vel.* … *It went on not being answered for long enough that he heard it going, the way you hear a stone going down a shaft and keep waiting after the sound should have come back.* → `../plates/ch20-val.md` · adopted s48 `../../images/plates/ch20-val.png`

## CANDIDATES

- **The emptying.** — **PRICED OUT (s48).** Flat on the stones after two seconds of a held line, the grey coming in from the corners, and Valeria crouched in front of him: *"What did you do." / "Wait."* A fine moment and a restaging of `ch14/one-finger` — him down, her over him — one chapter's worth of distance away from it in the reader's eye. Recorded so it is not re-proposed blind.
- **The cooper.** Dawn at the boat sheds: a man straightening off a hull and looking up the road, and a boy four minutes into proving him harmless off the wear on a sleeve and the way the shoulder comes round before the feet do. *His father had done this every day for eleven years.* **The only candidate in the chapter set in the village**, and the whole cost of turning a boy all the way up.
- *Alternate:* *"Then do not stand beside me. Stand in front of me. It is worth more."* — her chin up, the correction that is also the largest thing she has ever given anybody. Passed over at s48 only because candidate 2 is the same two people on the same stones in the same hour, and 2 is the chapter's last beat.
