# Candidates CH40 — The Hands

*Written at s64, at design time. **Sixty-two days east of Qeshet** — Kael thirteen, Aeliana a year above him, the fourteen-year-old references still the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it.*

**The chapter's title is plural and every strand in it is hands** — the princess's oiled and wrapped so she will not be marked, Kael's measured every morning until the thumb and the finger go past each other, his brother's shut, and the left one he takes apart in the dark to get it out of a band. **Two production facts run through the whole list.** The box is dark for the night the chapter turns on — the lantern went out in the evening and nobody came to see to it — so every picture of the act itself has no light in it; and the guard that refused `ch39/the-black-in-the-iron` twice reads restraint apparatus filling the frame as the declared subject of a picture with a child in it (`../../prompt-system.md` §Injury), so a close study of iron and a ruined hand is the one composition to keep away from. **Both chosen blocks answer both facts the same way**, on `ch39/the-iron-did-nothing`'s proven route: hold the distance, declare a subject that is not the apparatus, and let the mark sit by site and route inside a picture that is about something else.

## CANDIDATES

- **The hand out of the band.** The moment after the last pull — *what came out of the band into the cold of the box was wet and red all over and did not look like anything* — held in against his chest with the straw still in his teeth. The honest picture of the chapter's act, on §Injury's `ch23/the-horizon` method throughout. **Passed over on two counts, neither of them the moment:** the lantern is out and the box is as dark as it has been since Qeshet, so there is no light to draw it by; and it is the frame filled with a minor and an injury, which is the exact shape the guard refused twice at s60. The state it leaves is in `the-making-good`, in the light of the morning after.
- **Look away now.** He tells her twice and she does not, and a girl who has not stood up on her own account for five weeks sits up and watches the whole of it with her face wet and no sound in her at all. **Her most present moment in five weeks and nobody in the box remarks on it.** Unlit, like everything else that night, and it would be the same box for a third and fourth picture after ch39's two.
- **One.** A voice from the far wall picking him up off a dropped number, three times, *the way you pick up a dropped tool and put it back into the hand it fell out of.* The best thing Valeria does in the chapter and it is two people not moving in the dark.
- **The measuring.** Thumb and finger closed round his own arm above the elbow, going past each other where in the first week they would not meet. Two months in one crop, and it is about hands. **A hand-and-object exception that would have to earn itself against the same guard** — the band and the eleven links are on the wrist that does the measuring.
- **The door.** Six or seven breaths of the world, twice a day, and a boy spending every second of them on the ground, the growth, the stone, the tracks and the faces. The default chest-up with a face doing something, and the chapter's engine. **First in line for a third from this chapter.**
- **Tonight.** The lantern still on its hook, Valeria's head coming round, *Say it*, and behind them the princess awake and watching and having been for some time. Scene-test register exactly — three faces, one implied wall, a warm named source. Passed over as the box interior a third time.
- **The moon in the gap.** The fourth night, two fingers of sky, the second witness that keeps the days from drifting apart. Cheap and quiet, and close in kind to ch39's unused *blade of light*.
- **Kesir shuts the door.** The man Kael had down as cruel for two and a half weeks, caught in the one second that takes the verdict back: his eyes not on the cups but on her face, and going off it fast. **The chapter's best piece of reading and it cannot be read in a picture** — no reference, and the whole beat is where a stranger's eyes are for a quarter of a second.
