- **The unaimed laugh** *(added s45).* Run 1 floated; the fix was naming where the frame cuts the body instead of saying chest-up. The cistern court in the last of a summer afternoon: Aurelian with soot to the elbows laughing a real laugh, *surprised out of him, the first one Kael had ever heard from him that was not aimed at anybody* — and Kael at his shoulder giving nothing back, holding a breath where nobody can see him do it. **The first picture in the book of these two not fighting.** *Alternate, and ruled undrawable at s45:* the father not moving — standing over a boy on his hands and knees in the wet with the blade at his side, *looking at him. Not at the mistake. At him.* **The page names the face as the subject and then refuses to say what is in it**, and an image cannot decline to answer. The one framing that works — the man backlit, his face dark, the boy's face reading it — restages `ch14/one-finger` a chapter later. Reasoning on `ch15.md`. It was a real laugh, short, surprised out of him, the first one Kael had ever heard from him that was not aimed at anybody.* … *Kael's breath went to the long shape before he could stop it. ## CANDIDATES

- **Two blades on the block.** — **HELD (s45):** a pair of hands and an object, which is the crop the author ruled oversubscribed at s45 (`../README.md`). Before dawn, on the block by the woodpile: two blades *plain as spoons, blunted along both edges and rounded at the points.* Not new. A hand giving one over hilt first, and no explanation anywhere in the frame. **The single quietest object in the book's whole armoury.**
- **Nobody stands beside her.** — **HELD (s45):** wide staging of people walking away, and it would be Valeria's third plate in two chapters. The end of a morning at the sea court: the grey coats going up toward the town together, talking, and Valeria the other way, alone, winding her wrists with the case under her arm. The chapter's misread drawn as the chapter states it — and the reader gets to make it too.
