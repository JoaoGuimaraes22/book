# Candidates CH27 — The Fog

*Written at s51, at design time.*

**THE CHAPTER'S WORST MATERIAL IS DRAWABLE AND THE FIRST PASS SAID OTHERWISE.** Candidates 5 and 6 were proposed at s51 as moments the tool would refuse — **wrongly.** `ch21/dead` was refused and then ran on a rewording and was adopted, and `../prompt-system.md` §Injury says a refusal is usually a fault in the block. The real constraint is narrower: **do not draw the stroke, draw the men.** The framing that carries each is written into its entry.

## CANDIDATES

- **One hand out.** — **BUILT, NEVER RUN, DELETED (s51, author: *delete one-hand-out*). Do not re-propose.** The block is in `git log -p` on `ch27.md`. A burning stone stopped dead in the air over the north curve with a man's length to spare, the fire on it going nowhere, and an old man on a walkway below it with one arm up. *Nothing had shown. No light. No sound.* **The folder's second Art-with-no-effect**, after `ch26`'s unspent *both hands on her*, and both are Severin, which is the character.
- **Boats.** Through the seaward arch the grey moves and does not close: a line of hulls as far as the arch allows, another line behind it, and men already going over the side into the surf. **The hinge of the book**, and at that distance nothing violent is in frame.
- **The gate.** From the sand through drifting smoke: the mouth of the eastern gateway with thirty men standing square in it, seen from behind and side-on, no face resolved, **nobody mid-stroke**, the ground in front of them empty and the smoke a few feet from crossing them. The page's horror is that they are good and unhurried and reset and wait — **a picture of workmen in a doorway carries that**, and what they have made stays out of frame by framing, which is what the hard rule prescribes.
- **Marun.** *A woman said that one name about nine times while the crowd took her along the rail, and each time she said it exactly the same way.* **One resolved face inside a wash of moving small shapes**, mouth open on a word, carried past the rail — the inverse of the crowd instrument the folder already owns. Nobody down and nobody under.
- ★ **Oss.** Three rows up, hands already going, **turned the wrong way against the flow of the whole world** to get a smaller boy off the bench behind him, with the north curve about to stand up as one thing. Kael finds him in a second and a half and never sees him again. **The last time the reader sees him**, and a picture of one person doing the opposite of everyone else.
- **Eleven or twelve times.** The loft in the dark, a boy on his back with his hands out on the blanket, losing to somebody who is not there. The strangest and most private image in the chapter.
- **The arena out of the grey.** It arrives all at once, close enough to touch, full to the top rail, the noise standing straight up into the fog like smoke off a fire in still weather. The dark twin of `ch22/kael-of-the-island`.
- **Valeria out of the western gate**, hair bound, both blades, not looking at the crowd once — and the thing that goes across her face at her own name, *what a man's face does when a door shuts behind him in a corridor he has walked down a thousand times.* **Passed over at s51:** `ch14/both-hands-full` is already this girl with both blades full, and the face is the only new thing in it.
- **Tomorrow.** The table, the mint, and the boy who stopped, with his mother standing behind her chair instead of sitting in it. **Passed over at s51:** it is `ch25/four-seconds` two chapters later — same table, same lamp, same three people.
