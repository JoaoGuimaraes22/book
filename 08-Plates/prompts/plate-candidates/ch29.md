# Candidates CH29 — The Gate

*Written at s53, at design time. **The author handed the pick back** (s53: *you choose*), so the two ★ were taken.*

## CHOSEN

- **The eighteen inches.** Both his hands flat on the edge of the open leaf, his face turned up, *"Sir. Please. My mother is on the mountain"* — and the gap a tall slot of warm light with a broad shape filling it and no features on him. **Halden's face is not drawn and cannot be** until `images/portraits/halden.png` exists (`ch13.md`). Ch. 29 canon: *It opened the width of a person. Not a gate opening — a bar coming off and a leaf coming back about eighteen inches and stopping, with men behind it and their shoulders in it, and the whole weight of that hillside arriving against the front of the spear line in the same second.* … *Behind him, inside, there was light on stone and the ordinary sound of an ordinary street.* … *"Sir." He had never once called anybody sir in his life. It came out anyway. "Sir. Please. My mother is on the mountain."* … *the judge stood in the gap and gave Kael the whole of his attention, which is what he had always done.* → `../plates/ch29-the-eighteen-inches.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch29-the-eighteen-inches.png`
- **The shawl.** *"You're bleeding, love."* A woman past fifty crouched beside him with the corner of her own shawl against his shoulder and a stoppered bottle in the other hand, talking about somebody called Ilva. She is working and he is looking at her face. **The only warm picture in Part IV, and nothing in it is lit warm.** Ch. 29 canon: *She was somewhere past fifty. She had a shawl, one bundle, nothing else at all, and she was pointing at his shoulder with two fingers, at a cut he had taken at the wells and not looked at since.* … *She got down beside him with the noise people that age make getting down, and she took the corner of the shawl off her own shoulders, and she wet it out of a stoppered bottle that was the only thing in the bundle worth anything, and she cleaned it. She did it briskly and not very well. She talked the whole time about somebody called Ilva who was not there and who she was quite sure had gone to her sister's.* … *She did not know who he was.* → `../plates/ch29-the-shawl.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch29-the-shawl.png`

## CANDIDATES

- **She let herself be seen.** Valeria alone in the strip of open ground in front of the spear line, covered to the elbows, hair out of its binding on one side, two blades, saying nothing, with the wall above and thirty thousand people behind. **Passed over at s53:** a solo Valeria one chapter after `ch28/rearranging-the-road`.
- **Elarine on the wall.** A hundred and forty strides out and below: forty men on a stretch of parapet, two of them children, one of them stopped. *They were in and he was out.* No face resolved at that distance, which is the entry's whole point.
- **The count.** Thirty-one thousand at the low end, taken off a strip and checked against a second strip. **Priced at s53 and left standing:** it is `ch27/the-sky`'s shape — a wash of thousands and one small figure.
- **The mountain is not empty.** From the high ground east: eleven fires spread across four miles of ground that has thirty holdings on it, men going along the field walls the way water goes along a gutter, boats on the eastern water, and the mountain standing in the haze with its top in cloud. **The widest thing in Part IV** and the strongest unbuilt candidate on this chapter.
- **Go back to your place.** The old woman putting the bottle away, working four steps back into the press, and standing in it — after being given the only thing he owned that was worth anything, correct to the minute and checked twice.
