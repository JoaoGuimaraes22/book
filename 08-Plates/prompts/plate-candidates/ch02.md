# Candidates CH2 — Part of the Mountain

## CHOSEN

- **The long game.** One wide picture with three things in it: the boy at the foot of the fence post, knees to chest, hands quiet; the boat on the water with its oars folded up like a beetle's legs; and — hardest to find, which is the point — the shape by the woodpile that the eye slides off. The reader should have to look twice for the father. Distance solves the eyes entirely. Ch. 2 canon: the boy at the foot of the fence post, and the six-oared boat with its oars folded, far below. → `../plates/ch02-long-game.md` · adopted s40 `../../images/plates/ch02-long-game.png`
- **The doorframe.** Neris in the door, one hand up on the frame, the knuckles pale. She has been there some part of the afternoon. From the door you can see the pens, the woodpile and the sea. Her hands are where the book keeps her, and this is the first time they give her away. Ch. 2 canon: *One of her hands was up on the doorframe, the knuckles pale, and Kael thought she was holding the door up, and thought no more about it.* → `../plates/ch02-the-doorframe.md` · adopted s40 `../../images/plates/ch02-the-doorframe.png`
- **Rain falls on stones** *(s61 — the revisiting session's extra plate; the pick handed back: author, s61 «you choose»).* Close on his face at the foot of the post in the middle of the long game: the head lowered and the eyes raised under the brow, the face completely still, and the water running down it anyway. **The chapter's best line and its whole argument** — *water is not movement* — and it is a state and not an action, which is what `../../prompt-system.md` §WRITE THE STATE asks of a face. **It repeats neither adopted plate:** `long-game` is the same boy in the same fold at distance with his face down and no eyes, and this is him at conversational range with the eyes the subject; `the-doorframe` is his mother. **The wetting is out of frame by the crop and named nowhere in the block.** The posture is `scene-tests.md`'s `kneel-kael-aeliana` geometry — head down, eyes up under the brow — which is the one proven way to get an obedient body and an unbowed attention in the same figure. **The absence is the argument:** no other figure and no part of one at any edge, because the father is four steps away and does not come. **The boat is out of frame too** — `long-game` owns what the game watches, and this is what it costs. **3:4**, and **anchored on `kael-4.png`**: he is five here (`../../../07-Story-Ledger/timeline.md`), and a close crop is the one place the s44 fourteen-year-old default bites, since §Scale holds that the anchor wins an argument about age — `kael-4` is the same casting, so face consistency is not spent. **And it carries one saturated hue by name**, straw-gold grass and a patch of mustard-yellow lichen, which is what the s61 `ch07` run record says a next block should do. Ch. 2 canon: *He went down along the fence post and became the bottom of it, where the shadow pooled, knees to chest, hands quiet, face down but eyes up* … *his eyes went hot and spilled — but that was allowed, or not allowed, but not counted, because water is not movement.* … *He let his face rain without letting his face move, because the face is movement too, the face is the loudest thing you own.* → `../plates/ch02-rain-falls-on-stones.md` · **unrun**

## CANDIDATES

- **Done.** Dusk, the first bat out and stitching over the pens; a boy getting up in pieces the way the newborn goats get up, and a man standing close enough to catch him and not catching him. Ch. 2 canon: *He got up the way the newborn goats got up, in pieces, and went down, and his father stood near him — close enough to catch him, and did not catch him — while the legs remembered.* **THE BLOCK WAS DELETED AT s61** (author: *they're old with lesser system*) — an s40–s51 prompt that never made an image, written before the register the folder now uses. It is in `git log -p -- 08-Plates/prompts/plates/ch02-done.md`.
- **Being found.** The game when the game was little: his mother hunting round the pens in the pretend-worried voice, and a small boy folded up as a stone with his laugh already spilling out of him. *Being found was warm like bread.* **The only warm thing in the chapter**, and a deliberate rhyme against `long-game` — same yard, same fold, face up instead of down, and somebody coming for him. **Passed over at s61 only for the fold:** it would be the second picture of that pose in one chapter, and the third was spent on the same pose closer in.
- **Two fingers dropped.** The sign, *like something small being set down*, and the boy already gone still beyond it. **Passed over at s61, twice over:** the sign is a movement, which §WRITE THE STATE says a still picture cannot hold, and a near object with a far face is the crop `ch19/the-third-board` and `ch24/the-ice` were both rejected for.
- **His father's eyes came to him once.** *There was nothing in them that was angry. There was nothing in them that was anything.* **Passed over at s61 for `ch15`'s reason exactly:** the page names the face as the subject and then refuses to say what is in it, and an image cannot decline to answer.
- **The honey in the milk.** A reward nobody names, in a house that does not usually have honey. **Passed over:** `ch10/the-honey-and-the-bread` owns that jar and pays four chapters of planting with it.
