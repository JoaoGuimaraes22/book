# Candidates CH43 — The Water

*Written at s68, at design time. **Down off the Kharoun and six days in Doresh** — Kael thirteen, the princess a year above him, Valeria of his age; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian, Elarine and Hazoun Sabrane are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it. **Damir, Miresh, Tarim, Neshah and the old woman have no references and no `03` sheets**, so every one of them is framed rather than resolved: a back, a side of a face, a pair of hands.*

**This is the first chapter in four with no fight in it**, and the change of register is the design. Ch. 40 to 42 ran a box, a wood and a killing ground; this one is a lamp in a yard, a fire in a low room and a wall in the sun, and the three chosen are one of each. **The other production fact is that Ch. 40–42 spent four pictures on hands** — `the-holding`, `leave-it-in`, `say-it-back` and half of `four-feet` — which is why a fifth was dropped at design time and this chapter's night beat is shown from the family's side of the room instead.

## CHOSEN

- **The table.** *(s68 — author's ask: `Two hands` dropped, and something that shows the family.)* The one room in the evening, one pot on the plank table, and four of a family on the far side of it in the light with two dark strangers' heads cut by the near edge. Miresh leaning in to put a second piece of bread down and already turning back to the hearth without watching it land; Damir eating and looking at his own hands; Tarim mid-sentence about something else; Neshah kneeling up on the bench and looking straight out at the viewer because nobody has told her not to. **The ease is the subject** — no ceremony, no charity in anybody's face, nobody performing a kindness — against a pot that is small and sacks that are nearly down. **No reference attached** (the family have none), so the block takes the STYLE Medium paragraph. **3:4.** Ch. 43 canon: *Nobody at that table looked at what anybody else was given.* → `../plates/ch43-the-table.md`
- **The beans.** The fifth day, on the floor by the fire: a bowl of dried beans, two heaps sorted by a rule a four-year-old invented and is enforcing strictly, and the princess of the whole empire with her sleeves pushed back over the iron, being corrected at length in a language she does not have. Miresh at the fire with her back to them, not turning round. **The warmest picture in Book Two so far and the only funny one, and neither of them is smiling** — which is the whole joke and the whole tenderness. **3:4.** Ch. 43 canon: *the princess of the whole empire had her sleeves pushed back over the iron on her wrists and was being corrected, at some length, in a language she did not have.* → `../plates/ch43-the-beans.md`
- **Why are they doing this.** The third day in the yard: Kael on the wall in the sun with the bound arm up on his knee, Valeria at the gap in the wall watching the track, neither looking at the other, and the question between them that he has no mechanism for. **The chapter's argument, and the opposite composition to `ch42/leave-it-in`** — the same two people, the same care, and a clear width of empty sunlit wall between them instead of her hands on his arm. **3:4.** Ch. 43 canon: *the sun came round the corner of the shed and onto the wall, and they sat in it.* → `../plates/ch43-why-are-they-doing-this.md`

## CANDIDATES
- **Two hands.** Kael face-down in a stranger's yard at night with the plan finished and himself finished with it, and the only thing in the world he can see is two bound hands coming down into a lamp's light, held out flat and open and turned up, and staying there. **The hinge of the chapter and of her arc**: the princess of the empire asks for shelter with two empty hands and no word of the language, and it works, and nobody in the house ever says so. The camera is on the dirt at the height of his face; no face is in the picture and nothing above the wrists is shown, which is also what makes it drawable. **No reference attached**, so the block takes the STYLE Medium paragraph. **3:4.** Ch. 43 canon: *They were held out flat and open and turned up, with iron on the wrists above them.* **DROPPED AT DESIGN TIME (author, s68: *two-hands just a picture of some hands? nah, i want anything different*)**, and the block deleted; it is in `git log -p -- 08-Plates/prompts/plates/ch43-two-hands.md`. The moment stays on the page and is not re-proposed: what replaced it is `The table`, which shows the same kindness from the family's side of it rather than from the dirt.

- **Kaya.** The child at the end of the bed with her thumb out of her mouth, having decided what he is called and being satisfied with it. *Nobody had asked him that in two months.* The purest beat in the chapter, and it loses to `The beans`, which has the same child doing the same work with more in the frame.
- **The old woman's hands.** Forty years of it, going round and round and round his arm, quick and flat and unhurried — the third pair of hands he has ever watched do this and the first he could price. Would be the fifth hands picture in four chapters, and `Two hands` is the one that had to have the slot.
- **The iron in the last of the light.** Damir turning Kael's wrist over in both of his, close to his face, going along the band with his thumb and stopping at the pin — and his thumbnail in the groove where the black is that neither of them can name. **A live thread and a good two-hander**, held back because the black in the grooves does not render (§Limits) and the picture would be about a thing that cannot be seen.
- **The headman.** Both hands on the top of his stick and his chin on his hands, hearing four sentences and not asking a fifth. *Every one of them tells me about somebody who is coming for them… You did not.* One old face doing all the work, and no reference for it.
- **Under the floor.** The three of them flat in the space where the boards sit up off the rock, Valeria turned round in the dark to face the opening, the boards going down and feet crossing above. Passed over as the second hiding-under-something picture in three chapters, after `ch41/four-feet`.
- **The second piece of bread.** Miresh putting it down in front of him and going on with what she is doing, and after a while he eats it. The chapter's thesis in one object, and it is two hands and a piece of bread on a table, which is a smaller picture than it is a moment.
