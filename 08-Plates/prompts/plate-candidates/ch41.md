# Candidates CH41 — The Second

*Written at s68, at design time. **The sixty-third morning**, out of the box and up onto the stone — Kael thirteen, the princess a year above him, Valeria of his age; the fourteen-year-old references are the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian, Elarine and Hazoun Sabrane are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it.*

**The chapter is four minutes of killing and then two hours of not being seen**, and only the second half is drawable. The violence is the throat, the knee and the knife under the arm, and none of the three is a picture — `../../prompt-system.md` §Injury's *draw the men, not the stroke* points at everything on this list. **The other production fact is the box**: Ch. 39 spent two pictures inside it and Ch. 40 two more, so a fifth and sixth of the same six feet of boards would be the chapter's own worst habit. **Both chosen blocks answer it the same way** — one takes the box interior once and puts a standing figure and a doorway in it, which nothing before it has; the other goes underneath the thing and shoots out.

## CHOSEN

- **She stood.** The turn of the whole chapter, and there is no violence anywhere in it: a girl coming up off the floor of a box where nobody has stood in sixty-two days, back straight and chin level, in the grey before the sun with the smoke of a camp a quarter of an hour awake in it. **The folder's first Aeliana in the box**, and its first picture of her doing anything. The apparatus is on her and incidental — rings and a hand's width of chain at scene distance, the cloth across the lower half of her face — and the declared subject is a person standing up. **3:4.** Ch. 41 canon: *she came up off the floor of that box with her back straight and her chin level, and she stood.* → `../plates/ch41-she-stood.md`
- **Four feet.** The chapter's held breath and the exact opposite of an action plate: three people lying still on frozen ground under a cart while a man stands at the boards a hand's width from the princess's feet and looks out at the light coming onto the hills. **Nothing above his knee is in the frame and nothing needs to be** — the picture is three faces not moving. Visually unlike anything in the folder: the camera on the ground under a wagon, shooting out between the wheels at the light. **3:4.** Ch. 41 canon: *His feet were the width of a hand from the princess's.* → `../plates/ch41-four-feet.md`

## CANDIDATES

- **The sixth shelf.** The three of them flat in a line of thorn on grey stone, and below them the whole camp on the flat with its smoke going straight up in the cold — the box at the near end with its door shut, the fires, the picket line, the road going away east with the light coming along it. Fifty men, and nobody with a reason to look up. **First in line for a third from this chapter.** Passed over only because it stacks depth bottom to top from a high vantage with figures nearest, which is `ch40/the-road-behind-him` one chapter earlier. Ch. 41 canon: *Below them the camp had spread itself out on the flat and put its smoke straight up in the cold.*
- **Put the bar on.** Valeria seating the bar back on the door they have just come out of — two tries, and the second one seats it — so that from ten strides away there is a black box with its door shut, which is what there has been every morning for two months. Kael's mind on the page and the coldest thing he does in the chapter. Passed over as a third exterior of the same box after Ch. 40's two.
- **I did not do what you told me.** The last image in the chapter: the princess in a dead man's coat with her hands in iron in her lap, her hair down her back with two months in it, the corners of her mouth cracked into the flesh, looking straight at him and waiting. **The chapter's best face**, and it is held back only because `She stood` already spends her, and two plates of one character in one chapter is a heavier claim than this chapter can carry against `Four feet`.
- **The cloth coming off.** He works the knot with his right hand and his teeth; she brings her hands up in the iron and touches the corners of her own mouth, both sides, carefully, *the way you touch a thing to find out how much of it there is*. Hands and a face and nothing else. Close in kind to the entry above and to `ch40/the-holding`'s hands, and it would be the third hand study in two chapters.
- **Taking the ground.** Frost laid white and even over dust and holding a boot like wax; churned refrozen mud going off one way toward the water and the pickets; grey stone coming through in shelves the other way, taking nothing at all. Nobody in it — the ground itself as the decision, in the register `ch40/the-holding` proved. Cheap, quiet, and it asks the reader to read a choice out of terrain, which is a lot to ask of one picture.
- **The boots.** Getting them off the man in the doorway with one hand and his teeth in the laces, and they are still warm inside, and he does not look at that for very long. **Blocked, not passed over:** it is a body on the floor of the box with a child's hands on it, which is the one shape the guard has refused outright three times in this folder.
