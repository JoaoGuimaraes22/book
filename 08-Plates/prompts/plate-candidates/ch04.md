# Candidates CH4 — The Visitor

## CHOSEN

- **The blade across her knees.** Neris at the window in the dark, back straight, face to the sea, the unsheathed blade lying across her knees with her hands resting on it the way they rest on bread dough. **Her sheet says this plate is already composed by this chapter, and it is right.** Back, window, sea, blade, hands. Ch. 4 canon: *His mother sat at the window in the dark with her back straight and her face to the sea, and across her knees, unsheathed, lay a blade he had never been shown, long and thin and older than the tools on the wall, and her hands rested on it the way her hands rested on bread dough, at home.* → `../plates/ch04-blade-across-her-knees.md` · adopted s40 `../../images/plates/ch04-blade-across-her-knees.png`
- **The master comes up the path.** *Run 1 came back Dickensian; the garment was rewritten by construction rather than by name and run 2 took it.* Severin arriving in the yard, seen from a nine-year-old's height: tall, gone lean, grey all the way through, the plain coat, the wrapped bundle on its strap — and the borrowed stoop, with wrists too thick for a man whose work is paper. His first appearance, in the mountain register. The image must render the grey's **ambiguity** and never resolve it. Ch. 4 canon: *old the way the split rock was old — worn to what mattered. Tall, gone lean, grey all the way through, in a coat too plain to be a villager's and too well-made to be a villager's, carrying a wrapped bundle on a strap · a scholar's stoop that sat on him the way a borrowed tool sits in the hand · the wrists coming out of the sleeves were too thick for a man whose work was paper.* → `../plates/ch04-the-master-arrives.md` · adopted s42 `../../images/plates/ch04-the-master-arrives.png`
- **The knot.** The cord of dark silk on the table between them like a plate of food; a boy's fingers on it; a cup set down without being drunk from; a mother's hand come flat to the boards. Four hands and one knot, no faces at all. *He unties things.* Ch. 4 canon: *a cord of dark silk tied in a knot Kael had never met before.
