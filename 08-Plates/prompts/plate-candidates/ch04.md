- **The stillness pointed at the wall.** The first time he sets himself down inside the game unasked: down beside the woodpile in the shadow at the foot of it, knees to chest, hands quiet, the axe standing unused against the block, and all of it aimed at a wall that gives him nothing but shapes. **Passed over at s61 on the pose:** `ch02/long-game` and `ch02/rain-falls-on-stones` are both that fold already, and a third would be the same shape a third time in four chapters.

Cheap, and available.
