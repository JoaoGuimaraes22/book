  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s64; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4).** The argument is fully in the picture: two faces held still with a laugh going on at both edges of the frame, the jar stopped short of his mouth, her knife where it was in the cheese, and the two of them looking at each other. Her costume came off the full-figure reference whole — the dark sleeveless coat over grey-blue, the high collar with its small insignia, the vambrace — his is the mended undyed wool with no metal on him anywhere; brick stands on every side with the square of black sky above it, and the cut held with no legs, no feet and no ground in the frame.
  - **THE LAMP-LIT CORRECTION DID NOT TAKE, AND THAT IS WHAT THIS RUN WAS FOR.** The block reserved bare unpainted paper by name along the lit edge of each face, on the jar and along the brick rim, and forbade the warm light to soften the ink. **The ink held** — the linework is the strongest element and both faces are finely drawn — **and the palette did not**: dense warm brown throughout, the paper tooth arriving as a hard reticulated texture across the whole surface, and no bare paper anywhere except two flames and a painted cloth. **That is four for four on the lamp-lit split** (`ch19.md`), and the first correction ever attempted against it. `ch21/the-doorway-at-the-end` carries the same clause and is the second test; **if it fails the same way, the finding moves off the block and onto the tool**, as the black-in-the-iron finding did on two grounds with one result (`../../prompt-system.md` §Limits). The lever after that is not wording — it is not lighting a plate with a warm night source at all, which is a design decision and the author's.
  - **DRIFTED, three, none a correction owed.** The two laughing figures came back with partial faces — an eye and a cheek at one edge, a laughing mouth at the other — where the block said their faces were not to be resolved, and those two are Aurelian and Elarine, whose portrait references were deliberately not attached; nothing about them is decided by it, but it is nearer to deciding than the block wanted · the pair sit side by side rather than across the cloth, so the two lines of sight meet in near profile at short range, **which is better as drawn** — it puts them shoulder to shoulder inside the noise · and the jar is in one hand where the block said two, the light reads as ambient warm rather than as two small lamps from below, and both read above thirteen.

## CANDIDATES

- **The ice.** A wide silver bowl packed round a jug, sweating into the cloth, in a room with four braziers going. *There's no ice on this island in the spring.* **Inside candidate 1 rather than its own plate** — an object crop with no face at its own distance is what `ch19/the-third-board` was rejected for.
- **Kneel.** A boy kneeling on new boards in front of a carried chair, not knowing what the farewell is, while a room full of people work out that nothing is coming. **The scene system already has this composition** as `scene-tests.md`'s `kneel-kael-aeliana`, so it is known to render; a plate would be the moment rather than the test.
- **The cistern supper.** All four on the brick with two lamps on the rim and most of a Temple supper on a cloth — three of them laughing at nothing, Kael's flinch, and Valeria not laughing either, and their eyes meeting over the top of a jar. **Passed over at s48:** it is `ch17/who-lives-in-it` again, same four, same brick court, one chapter's distance apart. The beat is recorded here so it is not lost.
