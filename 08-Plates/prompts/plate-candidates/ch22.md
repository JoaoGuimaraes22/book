# Candidates CH22 — The Names

*Written at s48, at design time. Kael is thirteen from this chapter on.*

## CHOSEN

- **The box.** Closer to her: seated above the games behind a stone rail, hands doing nothing, eyes down on the sand, two grey guards turned outward at the frame's edges. **Legal because the subject is the arrangement, not the face** — a chest-up close on her would be the portrait plate `../README.md` forbids. Eleven thousand people standing up, and a girl of fourteen small in the middle of the ground with the gold at her shoulder and hem the only saturated colour in the frame. **The only plate in the book with a crowd in it**, and the one that spends Aeliana's Ch. 22 canon — the near-black blue, the gold, the hair up off her neck. **Her fence is kept by distance:** no face in the picture is resolved, so no verdict is delivered. Ch. 22 canon: *She came out onto the stone in a blue so dark it read black until the light moved on it, with gold at the shoulder and the hem in a quantity that had no function except to be a quantity, and her hair up off her neck in a construction that had plainly taken more than one person more than an hour.* … *He looked for what he always looked for* … *He got nothing at all. It was not concealment* … *She sat, and the arena sat, and the sound went out of it like water out of a yard.* → `../plates/ch22-the-box.md` · adopted s48 `../../images/plates/ch22-the-box.png`
- **Kael, of the Island.** Composed as a chest-up close and recut wide before it ran, on the author's note that a close-up throws away the arena. **The folder's first plate with no resolved face in it.** The herald's pause where a house should be, and then a boy walking out onto white sand with eleven thousand people over him. *He came out into the middle of a white floor … and heard the first sound the world ever made about him, and the sound was a question.* The face is the chapter's own finding: **not frightened, and the absence of it worth a look.** Ch. 22 canon: *"Kael," the herald read out, "of the Island."* … *Kael walked out onto the sand.* … *Kael discovered, standing in the north curve with his heart already going, that none of that frightened him, and that the not being frightened was itself worth a look.* → `../plates/ch22-kael-of-the-island.md` · adopted s48 `../../images/plates/ch22-kael-of-the-island.png`

- **The hand at the gate mouth.** *(s64 — the revisiting session's extra plate.)* *Promoted from the candidate below.* The competitors' gate at first light, the noise arriving bodily from above and on all sides, and a hand that has gone by itself to a knife while the face it belongs to has not caught up with it. **The chapter's only close human moment, and the set had no face in it anywhere** — `the-box`, `kael-of-the-island` and `the-box-from-behind` all resolve nobody, by design, so the extra is the one thing three plates of a crowd cannot give. The rhyme stays with the reader and is never drawn: at ten he came through the Temple gate with nothing on him and his hand went to the same place and found nothing there. Built on §Gaze's contradiction-as-one — *the hand has decided something the face has not* — with the light all through the gate mouth and the arena behind it as a wash with no faces in it. **3:4.** Ch. 22 canon: *He came in through the competitors' gate with a Temple steward's hand on his shoulder and the noise arrived at him bodily, from above and on all sides* … *and he stood in the mouth of the gate and found that his hand had gone by itself to his knife.* → `../plates/ch22-the-hand-at-the-gate.md` · adopted s64 `../../images/plates/ch22-the-hand-at-the-gate.png`
  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s64; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4 — the ratio token is four for four).** The block's whole argument arrived: the fist shut round the hilt with the arm tight in against the body, and a face that has nothing whatever to do with that hand. **The winner-and-rival instrument worked a fourth time** — his face is the only fully resolved thing in the picture, the sharpest step from light to dark sits inside the eye, and the crowd, the seating and the stone are all quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges. The daylight medium held again: the sand is bare unpainted paper and carries the lights, the wash blooms are there and the ink linework is the strongest element. The mending landed everywhere, with no metal and no ornament on him, the bundle across the back closed with nothing showing, and every bit of light in the frame coming in off the sand with the stone behind him going dark.
  - **THE KNIFE IS VISIBLE AND THE PAGE KEEPS IT INSIDE THE COAT. The fault is the block's.** It wrote *a plain knife at his belt*, and Ch. 23 says *the knife was where his father had put it, off the belt, inside the coat*, and Ch. 24 has the guard notice *the place on Kael's coat the knife had come out of — which was not on the belt, and was not anywhere a boy carries a knife to be seen carrying one*. The picture has the grip in his fist and the blade hanging below it, in the open. **Adopted as drawn (author, s64), with the departure named here**; the beat the picture is for — the hand arriving before the head does — is intact.
  - **AND THE GAZE CAME TO THE CAMERA, WHICH IS A GEOMETRY FAULT AND THE SECOND RECORDED INSTANCE.** The block asked for the eyes out and up at the packed seating and then put the seating behind his shoulder with his body three-quarters to the lens; the renderer resolved the contradiction toward the head, exactly as it did on `ch20/the-cooper`. **Two for two with a stated cause**: where a block names a thing to look at, the thing has to be on the side the face is turned toward.
  - **DRIFTED, two more, neither a correction owed.** The crop is loose again at about mid-thigh where the block said just below the belt — **and this is the first instance where the exclusion half held and the landmark still slipped** (no feet, no floor, no other body in frame) · and he reads above thirteen, with the hair fuller than a hacked crop, the recorded age drift continuing where no age clause was written.

- **The arrival.** — **BUILT, RUN AND REJECTED (author, s48: *not a fan*); the retired sheet, now `../../archive/plate-sheets.md` §ch22.** Do not re-propose. **Replaced by candidate 6, the box**, which is the author's own proposal. **THE BLOCK WAS DELETED AT s61** (author: *they're old with lesser system*) — an s40–s51 prompt that never made an image, written before the register the folder now uses. - **The twelve across the distances.** *They were arranged across the distances — the near ones close enough to take her weight off her feet, the far ones out at the edges of the ground, where a thing would have to come from, and every one of them was already standing where he was going to be standing before he got there.* **No separate plate needed** — twelve resolved figures is past §Four figures' ceiling, and they do their work inside candidate 1 as spread shapes, turned outward, not marching.
- **The pause.** The herald arriving at the place where a house goes and finding the clerk wrote what the clerk was given — and a boy's eyes going up to the western curve to find the First Master already looking at him, *from the one seat under that sky with nothing in front of it to watch but an empty floor.* **Two people in eleven thousand who know what the pause is.** Held: it is a sightline across sixty yards, which no block in the folder has yet had to build.
- **The hand at the gate mouth.** The noise arriving bodily from above and on all sides, and a hand going by itself to a knife and being taken away again — *at ten he had come through the Temple gate with nothing on him anywhere and his hand had gone to that same place and found nothing at all.* The whole of four years in one reflex.
