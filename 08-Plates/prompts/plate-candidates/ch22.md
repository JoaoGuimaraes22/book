# Candidates CH22 — The Names

*Written at s48, at design time. Kael is thirteen from this chapter on.*

## CHOSEN

- **The box.** Closer to her: seated above the games behind a stone rail, hands doing nothing, eyes down on the sand, two grey guards turned outward at the frame's edges. **Legal because the subject is the arrangement, not the face** — a chest-up close on her would be the portrait plate `../README.md` forbids. Eleven thousand people standing up, and a girl of fourteen small in the middle of the ground with the gold at her shoulder and hem the only saturated colour in the frame. **The only plate in the book with a crowd in it**, and the one that spends Aeliana's Ch. 22 canon — the near-black blue, the gold, the hair up off her neck. **Her fence is kept by distance:** no face in the picture is resolved, so no verdict is delivered. Ch. 22 canon: *She came out onto the stone in a blue so dark it read black until the light moved on it, with gold at the shoulder and the hem in a quantity that had no function except to be a quantity, and her hair up off her neck in a construction that had plainly taken more than one person more than an hour.* … *He looked for what he always looked for* … *He got nothing at all. ## CANDIDATES

- **The arrival.** — **BUILT, RUN AND REJECTED (author, s48: *not a fan*); the retired sheet, now `../../archive/plate-sheets.md` §ch22.** Do not re-propose. **Replaced by candidate 6, the box**, which is the author's own proposal.
- **The twelve across the distances.** *They were arranged across the distances — the near ones close enough to take her weight off her feet, the far ones out at the edges of the ground, where a thing would have to come from, and every one of them was already standing where he was going to be standing before he got there.* **No separate plate needed** — twelve resolved figures is past §Four figures' ceiling, and they do their work inside candidate 1 as spread shapes, turned outward, not marching.
- **The pause.** The herald arriving at the place where a house goes and finding the clerk wrote what the clerk was given — and a boy's eyes going up to the western curve to find the First Master already looking at him, *from the one seat under that sky with nothing in front of it to watch but an empty floor.* **Two people in eleven thousand who know what the pause is.** Held: it is a sightline across sixty yards, which no block in the folder has yet had to build.
- **The hand at the gate mouth.** The noise arriving bodily from above and on all sides, and a hand going by itself to a knife and being taken away again — *at ten he had come through the Temple gate with nothing on him anywhere and his hand had gone to that same place and found nothing at all.* The whole of four years in one reflex.
