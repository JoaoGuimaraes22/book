# Candidates CH38 — The Watch

*Written at s60, at design time. **The same night as Ch. 37** — Kael thirteen, Wren a month old, the fourteen-year-old references still the anchor.*

**The chapter is a night end to end**, so neither chosen block takes the register's soft daylight from one side: both name their lamps, and both are pictures with more dark in them than the folder has yet made. **Valeria constrains this list as it constrained Ch. 37** — Ch. 36's *half her face was burned red and shining down one side* never says which side — and the governor and the woman with the lamp have no portraits.

**Neris's eyes are told and never shown.** She says under this roof that her own are like Wren's, *hidden by great will and control*, and Kael has never seen them; `../../../03-Character-Bible/neris.md` carries it as told, never shown. The plate of her in this chapter draws dark eyes, which is what the page has and what her sentence is about.

## CHOSEN

The one dark the register normally has to buy is free here, and both ends of the value range sit inside the subject — ten lamps and a black court. ## CANDIDATES

- **The sword along the foot of the door.** Kael crouched at a door with a wooden latch on a cord and *no bar, nothing a bar could have gone into*, laying his father's longsword along the bottom of it on the stone, grip toward the bed, and the pair going down on the floor at the head of the bed on the side where his hand would fall. **The chapter's title beat, and the sword is far too big for him, which reads at any crop.** Passed over only because *the eight lights* already carries the house and this one repeats its argument at room scale. First in line if a session wants a third from this chapter.
- **The bed.** All three of them lying down: his arms stiff at his sides in the first bed of his life, the child on his back between them with his fists shut, her hand on him and her eyes on the door. **The rest and the warning in one frame**, and the folder has nothing like a family lying down. Passed over for company — it is the same bed, the same three heads and the same lamp as the chosen block, and the two would be one picture drawn twice.
- **Goodnight, Lucan.** The princess in her lit doorway with Valeria at her shoulder, and a boy stopped two strides down the passage with his back to the frame, his brother inside his coat, who did not turn round. **His face is out by the pose and not by a ban, and Valeria's side is solved by the doorframe rather than by a clause** — the cheapest handling of that constraint the folder has found. Passed over: four figures and a back, on a night when the two chosen blocks are already carrying unusual light.
- **Aurelian says it.** Chest-up, mid-word — *I have wanted a bed with my whole body for a month, and I find that I cannot walk to one* — with the lit doorways behind him and Elarine at the frame's edge looking at the basin instead of at him. **The only moment in the book where the golden boy admits to having a body**, and the chapter's one piece of comedy.
- **Oh, for—.** Elarine going past him into her own room and leaving the door open behind her; Aurelian standing in front of the wrong door, not going in. Two figures in a lamplit passage, and the whole of their arrangement is in who is standing where. **Available and cheap; nothing is spent by it.**
- **The far door.** Severin down the length of the passage, a hand on the wall, stopped in a lit doorway to say *Goodnight*, and the deferral standing unasked behind him. **Passed over for company:** `ch37/the-old-man-on-his-knees` is his first plate and it is one chapter old.
- **The fist.** *The child's fist came open in his sleep and shut again on nothing.* **Passed over for company:** the folder's hand-and-object crops are at three, and the last of them is two chapters back.
- **This one.** Valeria looking into the first room, coming out, looking into the second, and choosing it for the princess without being asked. **Passed over:** her face, and the beat is small at any crop that hides it.
- **The count going.** Her laid along the far side of the bed like a wall with her eyes on the door and her legs failing her, and a boy's numbers coming *without meaning anything*. **The chapter's climax, and the hardest thing on this list to draw honestly** — the page names nothing about what the sleep was, so the picture would have to be two states and no cause. Available; it wants its own session.
- **The table standing.** The governor's hand flat to his breast, the room getting up with him, a boy finding that he had done it too. **Blocked:** the governor has no portrait, and a plate is not where a face gets decided.
