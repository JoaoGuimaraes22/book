## CHOSEN

- **Furniture.** The long hall at midday: the kitchen boy's tray still on his hip, the middle tables turned and working the story, and at the bench-end a boy eating bread with his face at the room's temperature. *Kael was at the bench-end, being furniture, and furniture hears everything.* **The coat, drawn** — the only picture in the book where the reader knows something every other face in the frame does not. Four resolved figures at most, the rest summary shapes (§Four figures). Three faces and a fire eating what it is given, and the answer arriving faster than any answer that house has ever given. ## CANDIDATES

- **The hole through the tree.** — **REJECTED AS A CANDIDATE (author, s44):** *I just think it's a bad illustration to show, should pick something else from the chapter.* **Not an execution note** — the run was built, the reserve worked and the tally-marks came out right, and the moment was still not one worth spending a plate on. The block and the run record stay on `ch12.md` so the reasoning survives; do not re-propose it. *He threaded the lightning through his own hand, and put the hand through the trunk of a tree, wrist-deep, as if the wood were wool, and drew it out. The tree stood there with a hole through it.* The hand withdrawn, the hole, frost — and the boy reading *the holding, not the hole.* **Same Vask collision as Ch. 11's candidate 2**; one of the two, not both.
- *Alternate:* the barred door — iron into stone, the sound the house made when it meant held — with the three of them behind it and the boy asleep above.
