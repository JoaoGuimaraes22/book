# Candidates CH39 — The Wake

*Written at s60, at design time. **A day after Ch. 38** — Kael thirteen, the fourteen-year-old references still the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it.*

**EVERY MOMENT IN THIS CHAPTER IS MINORS IN IRON, AND THAT IS A PRODUCTION FACT.** `ch38/nobody-say-it` was refused on the adolescent-and-children guard the same day and ran only after the child's vocabulary was swapped for placement words (`../../prompt-system.md` §Injury). Every block here is built on that finding: the iron is described by construction — *a band a finger thick, closed with a pin whose end is peened flat, eleven short links* — and never by the category. **That is a way of writing the block, not a reason to pass a moment over** (author, s60, `../../README.md` §Standing rulings): *the princess pulls* was priced as a likely refusal and left unbuilt at design time, and the author put it back.

**Valeria constrains this list for the third chapter running.** Ch. 36 gave her half a burned face and never said which side; this chapter puts it in the lantern twice — *with the burned side of her face toward the lantern, shining* — so any plate that resolves her face has to pick one. Both chosen blocks keep her unresolved or out; the side is still the author's to settle.

## CHOSEN

- **The black in the iron.** A boy holds his own wrists up in a lantern's swing and reads them, and finds paint laid into the grooves and laid on again over the wear. **The chapter's new fact about the world:** somebody builds these for Resonants, paints them for it, and had one ready. The winner is the black and the rival demoted by name is his own face, on `ch28/the-hand-back`'s method, with the winner given a ratio to a body part — *both hands and the metal between them are wider in the frame than his whole head*. **Wide frame.** Ch. 39 canon: *It lay in the grooves like ink in the cuts of a letter, and where the band was worn the black was worn too, and somebody had put more on over it.* → `../plates/ch39-the-black-in-the-iron.md`
  - **REFUSED FIRST TIME, s60, before any image was made, and the fault was mine and not the moment's** (author: *you alluded A LOT to his adolescence and young here, why*). The block carried §AGE's adolescent rung — four minor-markers in one paragraph — next to iron on a boy's wrists, plus *lying on his side with his knees drawn up*, which is a curl, plus a body-part negation at the cut. **All three were instruments reached for by habit.** The rung had nothing to correct: `kael-14.png` carries apparent age, and §Scale holds that under that anchor no ratio has yet pulled an age down. The knees could not be seen under the cut at all, so that clause could not change a pixel. Reworded once per §Injury: the rung out whole, the curl out, the cut turned into a positive inventory of what is inside the frame. **The iron itself is untouched** — described by construction throughout, and `ch23/the-horizon` renders a bleeding thirteen-year-old, so the subject was never the barrier.
- **The door at dusk.** The bar comes up, the door opens on low red evening, and a square dark shape stands in it and looks at the Emperor's daughter. **Book Two's first antagonist plate, and it spends no face:** Hazoun Sabrane has no portrait, which blocked `ch37/the-name-at-the-table`, and here the page itself puts him against the light — *a man against the light decides nothing*, which is the framing answer the hard rule asks for. The depth is stacked bottom to top on §Light's method: near straw, the bar of red along the floor, the girl against the side wall, the shape in the doorway. **Tall frame.** Ch. 39 canon: *the last of the light came in low and red across the straw, and there was a man standing in it* → `../plates/ch39-the-door-at-dusk.md`
- **The iron did nothing.** A girl who has never once been contradicted puts everything she has into it twice, and the metal answers the same way it will answer every time. **The strongest single image in the chapter**, built at s60 after being wrongly passed over for refusal risk. Written on §Injury's `ch23/the-horizon` method throughout — the mark stated flat and given a shape and an edge (*a raw red band right round each wrist, its edges rubbed rather than cut*), the red given a route and an appearance and never named as a substance, and the face forbidden the injury — **but it inverts that specimen on one channel deliberately**: `the-horizon` gives its slash clean edges and this one holds the red at soft wash-bled edges with no outline, because there the wound is the subject and here the subject is her face. The picture is the state after, not the pulling: §WRITE THE STATE, and the page's own end-state is the better picture anyway. **Tall frame.** Ch. 39 canon: *and then she was still, shaking with it, and the iron had done nothing at all, and her wrists had opened over the oil.* → `../plates/ch39-the-iron-did-nothing.md`

## CANDIDATES

- **The blade of light.** The day crossing the floor — a thin edge along three bars, then a blade of it lying on the straw and lengthening toward a boy who is reading the country off it: the sun behind them, so east, and the land rising. **The folder has no picture that is about time**, and this one is nothing but a bar of light, some straw and a boy watching it. First in line for a third from this chapter.
- **Don't.** Two faces across a lantern-lit box: a boy saying one word low in the only tongue they have between them, and a girl looking back at him over the cloth, having just learned what pulling buys. **The default crop and the chapter's best two-hander.** Passed over because the iron already carries the close crop and this one would be the same box twice.
- **She looked at her own arms.** A Vel comes awake, checks the princess before anything else in the world, and then looks at her own wrists for a long time. **Her whole line in one image**, and the only picture where the iron means something different from what it means to Kael. Blocked at present by the burn side.
- **I slept.** Turning to him with the burned side toward the lantern, shining, to hand him a fact about her body he is not to make into anything. Same block.
- **The slot.** Three bars, a shutter pushed back a finger's width, grey day coming through, and a girl looking at it as if looking hard enough would do something to it. **No face need be resolved at all** if the crop takes her from behind; available and cheap.
- **Nothing answered.** He asks for the smallest thread he has, the one he spent on a candle at ten, and for the first time in his life nothing comes. **The chapter's real event and it cannot be drawn** — the state it leaves is a boy lying still, which is every other picture in this list. Recorded as the one that defeated the method.
- **The wet corner.** He uses it on his knees at the end of the rope and a princess looks at the slot the whole time, and he chooses to take it for a kindness. **The most human beat in the chapter and it is not a picture**; the framing that would make it legal would also make it unreadable.
