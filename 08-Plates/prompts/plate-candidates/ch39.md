# Candidates CH39 — The Wake

*Written at s60, at design time. **A day after Ch. 38** — Kael thirteen, the fourteen-year-old references still the anchor. Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine are on no page in this chapter and in no picture from it.*

**EVERY MOMENT IN THIS CHAPTER IS MINORS IN IRON, AND THAT IS A PRODUCTION FACT.** `ch38/nobody-say-it` was refused on the adolescent-and-children guard the same day and ran only after the child's vocabulary was swapped for placement words (`../../prompt-system.md` §Injury). Every block here is built on that finding: the iron is described by construction — *a band a finger thick, closed with a pin whose end is peened flat, eleven short links* — and never by the category. **That is a way of writing the block, not a reason to pass a moment over** (author, s60, `../../README.md` §Standing rulings): *the princess pulls* was priced as a likely refusal and left unbuilt at design time, and the author put it back.

**Valeria constrains this list for the third chapter running.** Ch. 36 gave her half a burned face and never said which side; this chapter puts it in the lantern twice — *with the burned side of her face toward the lantern, shining* — so any plate that resolves her face has to pick one. Both chosen blocks keep her unresolved or out; the side is still the author's to settle.

## CANDIDATES

- **The blade of light.** The day crossing the floor — a thin edge along three bars, then a blade of it lying on the straw and lengthening toward a boy who is reading the country off it: the sun behind them, so east, and the land rising. **The folder has no picture that is about time**, and this one is nothing but a bar of light, some straw and a boy watching it. First in line for a third from this chapter.
- **Don't.** Two faces across a lantern-lit box: a boy saying one word low in the only tongue they have between them, and a girl looking back at him over the cloth, having just learned what pulling buys. **The default crop and the chapter's best two-hander.** Passed over because the iron already carries the close crop and this one would be the same box twice.
- **She looked at her own arms.** A Vel comes awake, checks the princess before anything else in the world, and then looks at her own wrists for a long time. **Her whole line in one image**, and the only picture where the iron means something different from what it means to Kael. Blocked at present by the burn side.
- **I slept.** Turning to him with the burned side toward the lantern, shining, to hand him a fact about her body he is not to make into anything. Same block.
- **The slot.** Three bars, a shutter pushed back a finger's width, grey day coming through, and a girl looking at it as if looking hard enough would do something to it. **No face need be resolved at all** if the crop takes her from behind; available and cheap.
- **Nothing answered.** He asks for the smallest thread he has, the one he spent on a candle at ten, and for the first time in his life nothing comes. **The chapter's real event and it cannot be drawn** — the state it leaves is a boy lying still, which is every other picture in this list. Recorded as the one that defeated the method.
- **The wet corner.** He uses it on his knees at the end of the rope and a princess looks at the slot the whole time, and he chooses to take it for a kindness. **The most human beat in the chapter and it is not a picture**; the framing that would make it legal would also make it unreadable.
