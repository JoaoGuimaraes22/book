  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s64; the block ran as the file holds it, at 1086×1448, exactly 3:4).** The pose came back exactly as written — settled deep with the weight forward, elbows on the knees, **both fists together under the chin**, the shoulders down and nothing about her moving. The gaze held where it was sent: level, wide open, locked on one place below and outside the frame, and not on the viewer. Hers is the only face in the picture; every other body is a faceless summary shape cut by the frame. The vambraces landed on both forearms and the charcoal over grey-blue with the high collar came off the full-figure reference clean, the hair loose and well past the shoulders with no ornament, nothing in her hands and no blade anywhere in the frame.
  - **THE CROWD INSTRUMENT WORKS AND IT ATE THE SETTING.** The block asked for cut stone benches rising steeply and open sky above and behind, and none of it is in the picture — the background is bodies from edge to edge, so what arrived is a crowd rather than the north curve of an arena. **One instance, recorded not promoted:** where a block asks for a setting and a wall of bodies in the same frame, the bodies took every inch and the setting drew nothing. The standing figures also came back static and hooded rather than up and shouting, so the contrast is between her stillness and a mass, not between her stillness and a curve on its feet.
  - **DRIFTED, two, neither a correction owed.** She reads well above thirteen — **the strongest instance of the age drift in the folder so far**, and again no age clause was written · and the light lost its stated direction and came back flat, with no cold high source and no shadow under the cheekbone.

## CANDIDATES

- **Elarine writing.** Four hundred children of the empire's finest houses on their feet and shouting, and one of them with a slate flat on her knees and a stylus going. **Her whole character in a crowd shot**, and the only plate that would show the north curve.
- **Valeria not standing.** The only person in that curve still seated: elbows on knees, chin on her fists, **watching his feet and not his hands**, and somewhere in the last three seconds she stopped blinking.
- **The man on the rail.** Nine rows up, a man on his feet with his hands on the rail and *the face of somebody watching an expense* — and a boy on the sand looking up into the seats to find it. The cruellest beat in the chapter; the man needs no reference, being nobody.
- **The corridor.** A man in heavy dark grey standing in a cold corridor, not blocking it, already where he would need to be standing. *"Alone." / "Alone," said the man in grey.* His face need not be resolved at all, which is the point of him. *Ruled out at s48, and priced:* **standing over him waiting for the fourth time** is the chapter's most famous image and it is a child standing over a downed child — `ch21/dead` was refused with far less in the frame, and a refusal produces no picture. Not attempted.
