# Candidates CH36 — The Quiet

*Written at s53, at design time. **The last two plates of Book One.***

## CHOSEN

- **The finger.** A fist no bigger than two joints of his finger, closed on it and not letting go, and a boy sitting completely still on the boards of a drifting boat counting the breathing. **The first picture in the set where his hands are doing nothing at all.** Ch. 36 canon: *"Sit back. Put your arm along the top of your leg. No — flat. Like that."* … *She put the child into the space he made.* … *He weighed almost nothing and he was extremely warm* … *His head had no neck under it worth the name; the whole thing worked because Kael's hand was under it. His fists were shut up beside his face, the way they had been shut through the whole of a barrage.* … *His breathing was wrong. That was the first thing.* … *He counted it, because he could not do anything else.* … *He counted for four minutes and got the shape of it, and found that it repeated, and that it was not wrong at all* … *His brother's hand came open and shut on Kael's finger and stayed shut.* … *He did not have much grip.* … *and he held on with it anyway, and Kael sat completely still on the boards of a drifting boat and did not take his finger back, because he did not want to be the one who did that.* → `../plates/ch36-the-finger.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch36-the-finger.png`
- **No lights.** The island black on the horizon at about the width of two hands, the orange gone out of the cloud, a sword he cannot lift lying along the boards — and nothing lit anywhere on it. **The third picture in the `ch01/fallen-stars` — `ch32/the-lights-on-the-mountain` rhyme.** → `../plates/ch36-no-lights.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch36-no-lights.png`
- **The boat words.** The old man giving him twenty of them in an hour at the tiller and making him say them back in a different order, with his hands not entirely steady and the burnt strakes behind him. **The folder's first plate of Severin.** Ch. 36 canon: *"What is this called," Kael said, and put his hand on the piece of wood he was leaning against.* … *"That is a strake. All of them are strakes. That is the top one, so if you want to be understood you call it the gunwale."* … *"Which side is starboard?"* … *"Good," said Severin. "Tomorrow I'll teach you some more."* → `../plates/ch36-the-boat-words.md` · adopted s65 `../../images/plates/ch36-the-boat-words.png`
  - **RUN RECORD (first run, s65, on the debloated block; 1086×1448, exactly 3:4). THE FOLDER'S FIRST PLATE OF SEVERIN, after his portrait had been on file since s33.** The whole of it arrived: the forearm laid along the tiller, both hands loose and open, the boy back against the side with one hand flat on the top edge of the gunwale and the fingers spread, his mouth open on one short word. The burnt boat is in the picture and is doing work — strakes burned through in patches, a hole with cloth stuffed into it, char on the rope — so the wreck of the night before is under a lesson about the names of things.
  - **THE REGISTER PROOF.** Daylight, no lamp, no fire: bare unpainted paper across the whole sky, the sea a single pale wash, granulation and hard-edged blooms throughout, and the ink the strongest element. **Set beside `ch34/both-faces-wet`, which had no flame in the frame either and came back dense and dark, this settles the shape of the §Limits cost — it is daylight or not, and nothing else has ever moved it.**
  - **AND THE EYE-CONTACT INSTRUMENT LANDED HERE, WHICH SORTS ITS FAILURES.** Four post-register instances now: it holds on `ch21/the-doorway-at-the-end` and on this one, **both of them seated, still, and with nothing in either body to do**, and it fails or half-fails on `ch34/both-faces-wet` (running) and `ch30/the-fold-under-the-terrace` (hands working, his eyes went to the danger instead). **So a pair of eyes will meet when the body has no competing task and will go to the task when it has one** — which is not a failure of the wording but of asking a working body for its attention.
  - **The beard does what the folder said it would.** §SUBJECT's *a beard covers the mouth: move the expression to the eyes and brows* was written for a portrait and holds on a plate — *one corner of the mouth moved very slightly* is invisible under it, and what carries the moment is the eyes and the level brows.
  - **DRIFTED, two, neither owed.** The healed wrist-to-elbow burn on the boy's left forearm is not legible at this size, the same below-the-scale problem as a small mark on a face · and the crop went wider than the ribs, the seventh widening in seven.
- **The silence.** *(s65 — a close-up, on the author's ask for the emotional and damaged ones.)* She has been told to her face that she is not worth it, by a boy who then came at her, and it is the first contradiction of her life. **The page renders it only as duration — she looks at the water long enough that two other children have to find something to do with their hands — so a close-up is the only place that face can be seen at all.** The damage in the picture is all in the hair and the cloth: her hair down out of its construction and dried into ropes with the salt in it, the gold on the gown gone dull, and not one mark on her face, which is the point. Ch. 36 canon: *Her hair had come down in the night and salted into ropes, and her hem had been through six miles of goat ground* … *"And you are not worth it."* … *Nobody on that boat said anything at all.* … *Aeliana did not answer him. She sat with her back against the side of the hull and looked at the water going along it, and she looked at it long enough that Elarine Sedrane began examining her own hands and Aurelian Corvane found something to do with the sky.* → `../plates/ch36-the-silence.md`

## CANDIDATES

- **The loaf.** A day-old loaf broken by hand and sent down a boat from person to person, because *it will not be worth eating tomorrow.* **Wants eight resolved faces in a hull forty feet long.**
- **The princess holding him.** Sitting down on the wet boards in what is left of a gown with her ruined hands under a herder's child, getting the head wrong and correcting it before anybody said anything. **Passed over:** `ch34/the-elbow` one day later.
- **The inventory.** A knife, a length of cord and a flat grey stone laid out on the boards between them. *That is what I have. You have a knife, a bit of cord and a stone. And I have you, and I have him.*
- **The wrist.** Valeria arrived in front of him with his wrist in her one good hand, standing closer than she has ever stood, half her face burned red and shining and her left arm still carried in against her ribs. *"Kael, stop it. Please," said Valeria.* **Proposed at s65 and not taken; live** — and it would be the closest two faces in the set.
