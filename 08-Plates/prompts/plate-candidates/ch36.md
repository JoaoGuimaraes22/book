# Candidates CH36 — The Quiet

*Written at s53, at design time. **The last two plates of Book One.***

## CHOSEN

- **The finger.** A fist no bigger than two joints of his finger, closed on it and not letting go, and a boy sitting completely still on the boards of a drifting boat counting the breathing. **The first picture in the set where his hands are doing nothing at all.** Ch. 36 canon: *"Sit back. Put your arm along the top of your leg. No — flat. Like that."* … *She put the child into the space he made.* … *He weighed almost nothing and he was extremely warm* … *His head had no neck under it worth the name; the whole thing worked because Kael's hand was under it. His fists were shut up beside his face, the way they had been shut through the whole of a barrage.* … *His breathing was wrong. That was the first thing.* … *He counted it, because he could not do anything else.* … *He counted for four minutes and got the shape of it, and found that it repeated, and that it was not wrong at all* … *His brother's hand came open and shut on Kael's finger and stayed shut.* … *He did not have much grip.* … *and he held on with it anyway, and Kael sat completely still on the boards of a drifting boat and did not take his finger back, because he did not want to be the one who did that.* → `../plates/ch36-the-finger.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch36-the-finger.png`
- **No lights.** The island black on the horizon at about the width of two hands, the orange gone out of the cloud, a sword he cannot lift lying along the boards — and nothing lit anywhere on it. **The third picture in the `ch01/fallen-stars` — `ch32/the-lights-on-the-mountain` rhyme.** → `../plates/ch36-no-lights.md` · adopted s53 `../../images/plates/ch36-no-lights.png`

## CANDIDATES

- **The loaf.** A day-old loaf broken by hand and sent down a boat from person to person, because *it will not be worth eating tomorrow.* **Wants eight resolved faces in a hull forty feet long.**
- **The boat words.** The old man giving him twenty of them in an hour at the tiller and making him say them back in a different order. *"Good. Tomorrow I'll teach you some more."* **The strongest single thing left unbuilt in Book One, and the folder still has no plate of Severin.**
- **The princess holding him.** Sitting down on the wet boards in what is left of a gown with her ruined hands under a herder's child, getting the head wrong and correcting it before anybody said anything. **Passed over:** `ch34/the-elbow` one day later.
- **The inventory.** A knife, a length of cord and a flat grey stone laid out on the boards between them. *That is what I have. You have a knife, a bit of cord and a stone. And I have you, and I have him.* # BOOK TWO *Book One closed with thirty-six chapters and two plates each.
