- **The two pails.** A hard-frost morning, a bench, two pails found together: one wearing its lid of ice, one dark and unfrozen and moving when it is rocked. Two objects, no people. *The more salt the water carries, the deeper the cold must bite to hold it.* This is the picture that kills the promise. Ch. 5 canon: *two pails on the bench on a hard-frost night, one pail plain and one brined for the goat, set out together, found together in the iron morning* … *The plain pail wore its lid of ice.
