- **The two pails.** A hard-frost morning, a bench, two pails found together: one wearing its lid of ice, one dark and unfrozen and moving when it is rocked. Two objects, no people. *The more salt the water carries, the deeper the cold must bite to hold it.* This is the picture that kills the promise.
