Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and

Setting: a stone courtyard as loose wash suggestion — walls implied
behind them, background falling into shadow. Soft daylight.

Composition: chest-up. Both figures cropped at mid-torso, faces
filling the upper half of the frame, standing close and turned
three-quarters toward each other, not squared off, not facing the
viewer. Kael on the left, Valeria on the right, similar height in
frame. Relaxed distance between them — near enough to talk quietly.

The moment: mid-conversation, at ease. Nothing is happening. No
weapons drawn, no tension in either body.

Kael turned toward her, head slightly tilted, saying something.
Mouth open mid-word, eyebrows a little raised. Loose and unguarded
— none of the stillness he holds around strangers.

Valeria listening, head turned to him, weight settled on one hip,
one shoulder dropped. Her mouth is closed but the corner has gone
soft, and the tiredness around her eyes reads as amusement rather
than impatience. Not smiling — just not braced. She is looking
directly at him.

Both faces fully visible and rendered with equal finish.

Preserve both characters exactly as shown in the reference images —
faces, hair, colouring, apparent age, clothing. Kael's eyes stay
pale grey. Valeria's stay green. She wears her royal guard uniform:
charcoal sleeveless coat, grey-blue underlayer, high collar with
bronze insignia, steel vambraces, gorget. No gown, no gold
embroidery. Do not blend their features. Do not mix their clothing.
