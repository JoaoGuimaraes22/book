Image 1: reference portrait of Kael — pale grey eyes, dark uneven
hair, fifteen years old.
Image 2: reference portrait of Aeliana — pale blue eyes, silver-white
hair, sixteen years old.

Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. The ink linework remains the
strongest element; colour tints rather than fills. Large areas left
as bare paper. Warm living skin, soft ochre in the mid-tones — the
warmth belongs to the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth. Warm
aged parchment ground, no setting, no background elements.

Composition: two full figures on bare parchment. KAEL kneels in the
left foreground on one knee, body angled toward the viewer, head
bowed low. ALEIANA stands to the right, further back, facing him,
full-length. Clear space between them. Both faces fully visible and
rendered with equal finish.

Lighting: soft directional light from one side, gentle shadow along
the shaded side of both figures.

KAEL — a fifteen-year-old boy, lean and hard, compact and athletic.
Kneeling on one knee, the other bent, one hand flat on the raised
knee, the other loose at his side. Back straight, head bowed
forward and down in formal obeisance — but his eyes are raised,
looking up at her from under the lowered brow. The body is
deferential and the attention is not. Still, coiled, economical.
Eyes near-white neutral grey, cool and colourless, dark limbal
ring — the lightest point in the picture, made more visible by the
lowered head and upward gaze.
Dark hair, unstyled, cut short and uneven, falling forward over the
face as the head drops.
Weathered and wind-chapped, skin clean, no grime. Plain, ordinary
bone structure, faintly asymmetrical.
Expression flat and unreadable, mouth closed.
Plain dark clothing chosen to go unnoticed: close-fitting
long-sleeved tunic in charcoal grey, sleeves pushed to the forearm,
plain leather belt, dark fitted trousers, cloth wraps at the
calves, soft dark leather boots.

ALEIANA — a sixteen-year-old noble girl, slight and sheltered,
soft. Standing straight, weight even, chin level, shoulders back,
hands folded loosely in front of her. Formal and composed, taught
to stand still. Looking down at him.
Eyes pale blue, luminous.
Long wavy silver-white hair falling past the shoulders, partly
pinned at the crown, almost colourless, rendered mostly as bare
paper and the palest cool greys. Colourless silver-white, not
blonde — no yellow or gold in the hair.
Pale untouched skin, freckles across the nose and cheeks.
Expression quiet and composed, lips closed.
Court dress: a full-length gown in a blue so dark it reads black
until the light moves on it, high collar, long fitted sleeves,
skirt to the floor. Gold embroidery massed at the shoulders and
heavily worked around the hem — dense elaborate scrollwork in far
greater quantity than any garment needs. Bare dark fabric between
shoulder and hem.
