Image 1: full-body reference of Kael.
Image 2: full-body reference of Valeria.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights. Plus dry-brush drag and flicked spatter
for the movement.

Setting: a stone courtyard as loose wash suggestion — flagstones
underfoot, walls implied, background falling into shadow. Both
figures grounded on the same floor plane.

Composition: two figures mid-fight, three-quarter length, facing
each other in profile-to-three-quarter view, not facing the viewer.
Bodies overlapping at the point of contact.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
His right hand holds one short dagger and nothing else.
His left hand is empty and open.
Her right hand holds one longsword and nothing else.
Her left hand holds one longsword and nothing else.
Four hands, three blades, nothing else in any hand. Both her
scabbards are empty. No other blade, blade tip or hilt appears
anywhere in the frame.

The dagger is short — blade no longer than his forearm, less than
half the length of either of her swords.

THE ACTION — Kael has closed inside her reach. He is low, weight
dropped, driving in past the point of her blades.
His right dagger is crossed against her right-hand longsword, blade
against blade, the bind held high between them, both arms straining.
His left hand is empty and open, held low and out for balance.
Her left-hand longsword is extended low, the edge inches from his
ribs, the blade in front of his body and clearly aimed at him.

All four hands fully visible, each anatomically connected at the
wrist.

Kael dropped low, knees bent, torso twisted into the drive, weight
committed forward. Expression tight and focused.

Valeria rooted and braced against the bind, hips turned into the low
cut, taller with the longer reach. Expression flat and hard.
