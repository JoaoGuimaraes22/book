Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a stone hall as loose wash suggestion — walls implied
behind them, background falling into shadow. Soft light from one
side.

Composition: waist-up, two figures close together and overlapping.
AELIANA has stepped in and put her arms around KAEL, holding him
tightly, her face turned into his shoulder and mostly hidden — only
the side of her brow, her closed eye and the line of her cheek
visible. KAEL faces the viewer over her shoulder, his face fully
visible and the focus of the image.

Four hands total: both of hers around his back, fingers gripping
the fabric of his tunic, arms fully wrapped and connected at the
shoulders. His right hand is raised but not yet resting on her —
hovering just off her back, fingers open, caught between reaching
and not. His left hand hangs at his side. No weapons anywhere in
frame.

AELIANA holding on hard, weight leaning into him, shoulders
dropped, the composure gone. Silver-white hair loose and spilling
over both their shoulders, almost colourless, rendered mostly as
bare paper and the palest cool greys. Court gown in near-black
blue, high collar, dense gold scrollwork embroidery massed at the
shoulders. No armour.

KAEL not returning it, or not yet. Standing straight, still,
shoulders slightly back — braced rather than yielding. Eyes open
and looking past her at nothing, pale near-white grey, the lightest
point in the picture. Expression unreadable, mouth closed. Someone
who has never been held and doesn't know what to do with it.
Plain charcoal tunic, no ornament, no metal, no embroidery.

Preserve both characters exactly as shown in their reference images
— faces, hair, colouring, apparent age. Kael's eyes stay pale grey.
Do not blend their features. Do not mix their clothing — the gold
embroidery belongs only to Aeliana.
