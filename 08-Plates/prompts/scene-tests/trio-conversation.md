Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a stone hall as loose wash suggestion — walls implied
behind them, background falling into shadow. Soft daylight from one
side.

Composition: waist-up, three figures standing in a shallow arc,
angled toward each other but all three faces clearly visible to the
viewer. Left to right: KAEL, then AELIANA in the centre and
slightly forward, then VALERIA. Standing close, relaxed distance.
No weapons drawn, all hands empty and relaxed. Nothing is
happening — this is a quiet conversation.

AELIANA is speaking. Head turned slightly toward Kael, mouth open
mid-word, one hand lifted a little at her waist in a small
explanatory gesture. Composed but animated — more alive than her
usual court stillness. Silver-white hair, pale blue eyes, pale
untouched skin, freckles. She wears her court gown: near-black
blue, high collar, long fitted sleeves, dense gold scrollwork
embroidery massed at both shoulders. No armour, no vambraces, no
gorget.

KAEL listens, turned toward her, head slightly tilted, mouth
closed, eyebrows a little raised. Loose and unguarded. Pale grey
eyes, dark uneven hair, plain charcoal tunic with no ornament of
any kind — no collar plate, no metal, no embroidery.

VALERIA listens from the other side, arms loose, weight settled on
one hip, mouth closed but the corner gone soft, watching Aeliana
with something like patience. Green eyes, dark hair tied back. She
wears her royal guard uniform: charcoal sleeveless coat over
grey-blue underlayer, high collar with small bronze insignia, steel
vambraces, fitted gorget. No gown, no gold embroidery, no
scrollwork.

Three faces, three distinct characters. Kael's eyes pale grey,
Aeliana's pale blue, Valeria's green. Preserve each exactly as
shown in their own reference image. Do not blend any of their
features. Do not mix their clothing — the gold embroidery belongs
only to Aeliana, the armour only to Valeria, and Kael wears neither.
