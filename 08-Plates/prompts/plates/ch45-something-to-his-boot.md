Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.

Setting: the narrow place in a fold in high country on a bright cold
day — a loose steep rise going up on one side, fast water on the
other, and about eleven feet of dust and stones between them, running
straight for a long way. A large split rock stands at the side of the
track. Wind. Loose wash suggestion, no detail anywhere. Flat winter
daylight, no sun in frame.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is on the track, low and down at the
bottom of the narrow, looking back up its length. Nearest, in the
lower third: dust, stones and the foot of the rise. In the middle
distance, at the side of the track beside the split rock, A YOUNG MAN
of about twenty is down on one knee with his back three-quarters to
the camera, both hands at his own boot. Coming past him and on down
the track toward the camera, TWO HORSEMEN at a walk, unhurried, one
slightly ahead of the other. Behind them, further up and smaller, more
riders strung out down the narrow, and above them all the open ground
at the top of it.

HE IS TAKING HIS TIME AND HE IS NOT LOOKING UP. His head is down at
the boot, his shoulders in, the whole of him arranged around doing one
small ordinary job slowly. His face is not resolved and it is turned
away. Nothing about him is signalling to anybody.

THE HORSEMEN ARE NOT SUSPICIOUS AND NOTHING IS HAPPENING YET. They
are at a walk, loose in the saddle, one turned to say something to the
other. Plain dark military kit, spears carried upright and at rest,
nothing drawn. No faces resolved on any of them.

THE TRACK AHEAD OF THEM IS EMPTY AND ORDINARY. Nothing is visible on
it, nothing is disturbed, nothing is dug and nothing is scattered:
just dust and stones running down between the rise and the water, all
the way to the bottom of the frame.

The kneeling man and the two nearest horses carry the finished ink
drawing; the rise, the water, the far riders and the ground stay at
quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges and no hard edge in
them.
