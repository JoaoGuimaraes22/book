Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the boy.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the sand floor of a great arena on a bright morning, as loose
wash suggestion — pale raked sand under him, and behind him the banked
seating rising as a wash of small shapes with no face resolved
anywhere in it. Flat daylight, no sun and no hard-edged shadow.

Composition: one figure, close and filling the frame, coming forward
toward the viewer at a walk. The bottom edge of the picture crosses
him below the collarbone, so that no arms, no hands and nothing held
in them are anywhere in the frame. Nobody else is in the picture.

HE IS SMILING AND HE DOES NOT KNOW HE IS DOING IT: the mouth open a
little with the corners lifted and the teeth showing, the cheeks
lifted with it — and the rest of the face taking no part at all, the
brows level and unarranged, the chin neither raised nor dropped. It is
delight, plain and unguarded and turned toward nobody. Not a snarl,
not a grimace, not a grin of triumph, and nothing cruel or savage
anywhere in it.

His eyes are wide open and bright and completely engaged, the pale
grey clear in them.

A smear of red across his front teeth.

HE IS LOOKING DIRECTLY OUT OF THE PICTURE AT THE VIEWER and his are
the only eyes in the frame.

One eyebrow is gone and the hair above it on that side is singed short
and uneven, and the skin high on that cheek is reddened. Grey soot and
pale sand dust across the face and in the creases of the neck.

His hair is dark and straight, hacked short and uneven, falling over
the forehead and disordered. He wears undyed coarse working wool,
much mended, in dull oatmeal and dead olive, torn open at the
shoulder, with no ornament and no metal anywhere on him.

Preserve him exactly as shown in the reference image — face, hair,
colouring, apparent age. His eyes are pale near-white grey.
