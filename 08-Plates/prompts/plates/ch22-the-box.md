Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a stone box raised above the floor of a great open arena on
a bright cold morning, as loose wash suggestion — a low stone rail
across the front of it, and below and beyond the rail the white raked
sand of the arena floor and the near arc of banked seating, packed
with people drawn only as a wash of small shapes with no faces.
Bright flat winter daylight from above; a cold blue sky at the top of
the frame.

Composition: half figure, one seated girl, three-quarter view, near
enough that her gown and her hair are fully readable. She sits well
back in a high stone seat behind the rail, upright and entirely
still, her head level and turned a little down toward the arena
floor. The stone rail crosses the lower part of the frame in front of
her. At the far edges of the frame, two guards in heavy dark grey
with nothing on it stand apart from each other, turned outward and
away from her, drawn as plain shapes with their faces not detailed.
Nobody else is in the box.

HER HANDS DO NOTHING. Both hands rest in her lap, open and loose,
holding nothing at all, with no fan, no glove, no cup, no jewellery
and nothing in either of them.

She is looking down at the arena floor below the rail, and not at the
viewer. Her face is composed and gives nothing away: the mouth
closed, the brows level, the eyes steady on the sand. Not smiling,
not bored, not sad, not proud, not searching.

Her hair is silver-white, colourless, with no yellow or gold in it,
dressed up off her neck in an elaborate arrangement. Her gown is a
blue so dark it reads black until the light moves on it, with dense
gold worked at both shoulders and along the hem; the gold is the only
saturated colour anywhere in the picture. No armour and no weapon of
any kind is on her.

The gown and the gold carry the hardest edges and the sharpest step
from light to dark; the stone, the guards, the sand and the crowd
stay at quiet middle values with soft edges, and nothing in the crowd
is drawn sharply enough to read as a person.

Preserve her exactly as shown in her reference images — face, hair,
colouring, apparent age; the two images are the same girl and there
is one girl in the picture. Pale blue eyes.
