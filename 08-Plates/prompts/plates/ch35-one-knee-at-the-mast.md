Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of the girl.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the open deck of a small boat at sea at night, as loose
wash suggestion — the mast standing beside her, wet, with the deck
and the low gunwale falling away below. Behind her and far off, the
black shoulder of an island on the water. Coming across the water
toward her, out of frame and filling everything in front of her, the
light of a fireball wider than the boat is long: the whole of the
sea between the island and the hull has gone the colour of the sun,
and it is the only light in the picture.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is on the deck with her, close and
level with her face, a little to her left so that the LEFT side of
her face is toward it. VALERIA fills the upper two-thirds of the
frame, up on one knee, her body square to the light and her head up
and facing straight into it. The mast is beside her on the right and
her right hand is on it. The bottom edge crosses her at the waist.
The other people are flat on the boards below the frame and none of
them is in the picture.

She is the only person aboard who did not lie down.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
Her right hand is flat on the wet wood of the mast at about the
height of her own shoulder, the fingers spread, holding on to
nothing and steadying nothing.
Her left arm is carried in against her ribs and her left hand is
empty and closed.
Two hands, no blade in either. Twin blades in their scabbards at her
hips, both scabbards full.

VALERIA — up on one knee, the back straight, the shoulders square,
the head up and facing the thing coming. The eyes wide open and
steady on it, the mouth closed, the jaw set. Not braced, not
flinching, not afraid. The whole of the light is on her face and she
is looking straight into it.

THE LEFT SIDE OF HER FACE IS BURNED. It runs from the eyebrow down
across the cheekbone to the jaw, red and shining, a smooth shine on
the skin. It is hours old. Nothing is broken and nothing is
bleeding, and the right side of her face is clean.

Long black hair, wet, hanging heavy and pulled back off the face, no
ornament. Dark near-black clothing, well made, soaked through and
dirty and torn at one shoulder — good cloth with a bad night on it,
not ragged. Dried red on both forearms, dark and gone brown, and
none of it is hers.

Lighting: the fireball is off the front of the picture and does all
of it — a hard flat light straight into her face and down the front
of her, warm and enormous, with the mast, the deck, the water and
the island behind her going to a deep dark and a long shadow thrown
back off her along the boards.

Her face carries the only finished drawing in the picture and the
sharpest step from light to dark; the mast, the deck, the water and
the island have no hard edge anywhere in them.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Her eyes are emerald green.
