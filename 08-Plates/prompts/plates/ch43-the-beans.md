Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a small whitewashed village house, low and
warm, in the evening. A packed earth floor, a fire in a hearth at one
side with a pot near it, sacks against the back wall, a shuttered
window. Loose wash suggestion, no detail anywhere.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low, at floor height, a little way
back. THE PRINCESS sits on the floor with her legs folded under her,
turned three-quarters toward the camera. A SMALL CHILD of about four
kneels across from her, seen from behind and to one side, with only
the side of her face showing. Between them on the floor sits a wooden
bowl of dried beans and two small heaps of beans sorted out of it.
Behind them both, at the fire and with her back turned to the room, a
WOMAN is working and does not look round.

THE CHILD IS CORRECTING HER AND SHE IS TAKING IT SERIOUSLY. The
child's hand is out over the two heaps, mid-instruction, one bean held
up. The princess is holding a single bean out in front of her between
finger and thumb, looking down at it, her head tipped, her face
absolutely serious — being taught something by a four-year-old and
attending to it completely. There is no smile on either of them and
nothing about it is comic.

Her sleeves are pushed back over her forearms, and on each wrist a
plain closed ring of dark metal with no more chain between them than
the width of her own hand. A plain cloth is tied over her hair so
that none of it shows. Her face is thin, the skin over the cheekbones
tight, and at each corner of her mouth a small split, dark and dry
and healing. She is in a coarse village dress far too big for her,
lent to her, with the ruin of something finer under it.

Warm firelight from one side is the only light. The two faces and the
bowl between them carry the finished drawing; the woman at the fire,
the sacks and the walls stay at quiet middle values with soft
wash-bled edges and nothing resolved in them.

Preserve her face and colouring exactly as shown in the reference
image. Pale blue eyes. Her hair is covered and none of it is visible.
