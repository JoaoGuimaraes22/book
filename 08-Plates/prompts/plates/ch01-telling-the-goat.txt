Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. The ink linework remains the
strongest element; colour tints rather than fills. Large areas left
as bare paper. Warm living skin, soft ochre in the mid-tones — the
warmth belongs to the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth.

Image 1: portrait reference of the man.

Setting: the yard of a small stone-and-turf mountain house in winter,
as loose wash suggestion — a low roof-line, a woodpile, a fenced pen,
snow on the ground and the roof, the mountain beyond going up into
pale wash. Bare unpainted paper carries the snow. Flat cold overcast
daylight, no warm source anywhere.

Composition: full length, three subjects on the same ground plane,
reading left to right — THE MAN at a chopping block, mid-stroke; THE
BOY small in the middle distance, walking away from the viewer and
away from his father toward the pen; THE GOAT in the pen at the
right.

THE BOY IS FOUR AND MUST READ FOUR: the top of his head no higher
than the man's hip, the head large for the body, shoulders narrow and
soft, arms and legs short, the walk slightly wide the way a small
child walks in snow. Seen from directly behind — the back of his
head, his back and his heels, no feature of his face drawn. Plain
undyed wool, coarse and much mended and a little too big for him.

The man's two hands are together on the haft of one axe, raised above
and behind his head at the top of the stroke, nothing else in either
hand. He is looking down at the block, not at the boy — level, mouth
closed, unhurried, already back at work. Coarse undyed working cloth,
much mended, closed at the throat, THE INK CARRYING THE CLOTH at the
seams and the mended places.

ONE GOAT, side-on in the pen, ITS HEAD TURNED AWAY, wholly
indifferent — not curious, not friendly, not alarmed, not attentive.
