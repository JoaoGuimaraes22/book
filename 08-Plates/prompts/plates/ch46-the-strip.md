Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of Valeria.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights. Plus dry-brush drag and flicked spatter for
the movement.

Setting: a flat strip of winter grass between a stony track and fast
water, at the bottom of a narrow fold in high country. A loose steep
rise going up out of frame on one side, dark needle trees above it.
Loose wash suggestion, no detail anywhere. Flat cold winter daylight,
no sun in frame. Both figures grounded on the same flat grass.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures mid-fight, three-quarter length, inside
each other's reach, seen from the side and slightly below. VALERIA
drives in from the left, low and forward. A GROWN SOLDIER is turned
to her and already going over backwards with his weight off his feet.
Their bodies overlap at the point of contact in the middle of the
frame. Well behind them and much smaller, one man lies face down in
the grass and is not moving.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
Her right hand holds one plain soldier's sword and nothing else.
Her left hand holds one knife and nothing else.
His two hands are both on one spear and nothing else.
Four hands, two blades and one spear, and nothing else in any hand.
No other blade, blade tip or hilt appears anywhere in the frame.

The knife is short — the blade about the length of her own hand. The
sword is plain straight working steel with no ornament on it.

THE SPEAR NEVER GOT ROUND. The shaft is jammed down across her body
and trapped under her left arm, the head of it out past her hip and
pointing at nothing behind her. That contact is where the two bodies
meet.

SHE IS ALREADY THROUGH HIM. Weight fully committed forward off the
front foot, hips turned into it, both arms working at once, the sword
arm short and inside rather than swung wide.

HER FACE IS SET HARD AND NONE OF IT IS MOVING. The brows low and
straight, the mouth closed and pressed thin, the eyes on his throat
and not on the viewer. The left side of her face is smooth and
shining where it healed.

Age: thirteen. The eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head, the jaw
softly defined and not sharp.

VALERIA: long black hair, well past the shoulders, loose and coming
out of itself with the movement. Plain dark road clothing, coarse,
close-fitting, worn and travel-dirty, with no metal on it anywhere.

THE SOLDIER is a grown man, a head and a half taller than her and
heavier through the chest, and none of it is helping him. His face is
turned away and only half drawn. Plain dark military kit and a coat
with a mark at the shoulder: the military kit belongs only to him and
she wears none of it.

The crossing of the sword and the spear shaft in the middle of the
frame carries the sharpest step from light to dark and the most
finished ink drawing; her face comes next; the grass, the water, the
rise and the man behind stay at quiet middle values with soft
wash-bled edges.
