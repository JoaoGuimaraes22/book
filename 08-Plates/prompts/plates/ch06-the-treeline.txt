Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. The ink linework remains the
strongest element; colour tints rather than fills. Large areas left
as bare paper. Warm living skin, soft ochre in the mid-tones — the
warmth belongs to the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth.

Image 1: portrait reference of the boy.

Setting: the last of a pine wood on a steep slope at night, as loose
wash suggestion — dark trunks close on either side, needles and
frozen ground underfoot, everything but the boy going into deep cool
wash.

Composition: close on the boy, three-quarter from his front and
slightly to one side, cropped just below his hands. He is crouched
back on his heels at the edge of the trees, knees drawn up to his
chest, both hands resting on his shins.

THE ONLY LIGHT IS WARM AND COMES FROM BELOW AND AHEAD OF HIM — a
shore of small lamps far downhill and outside the frame. It lands on
his face from underneath: the underside of the jaw, the cheekbones,
the lower lip, the front of the throat, the backs of his hands. The
trees, his back and the top of his head fall away into deep cool
shadow.

Far below and behind him, small and loose at the edge of the frame, a
long line of warm lamps runs end to end along a waterline and is
doubled and trembling in black water. DARK FIGURES MOVE AMONG THE
LAMPS, many of them, small and featureless — shapes against the
light, with no face drawn on any of them.

HIS BODY AND HIS FACE DISAGREE, AND BOTH MUST SURVIVE. He is leaning
forward toward the light, his weight carried out onto the balls of
his feet, the whole body wanting to go — and his face is afraid: eyes
wide open and fixed on the lights, lips parted, the brows drawn up in
the middle. NOT gathered to spring, NOT curious, NOT delighted, NOT
crying. His hands stay quiet on his shins, fingers relaxed, holding
nothing and gripping nothing.

The lamps are the brightest thing in the picture and are soft-edged
and loose, with no detail in them. His lit face is the most finished
thing and carries the sharpest step from light to dark. The trees,
the ground and his clothing are held at quiet low values with no hard
edges.

Eyes near-white neutral grey, cool and colourless, catching the warm
light without taking its colour — no more colour in the iris than in
the whites around it. Dark hair cut short and uneven. Coarse undyed
mended wool, thick and layered for a winter night, the ink carrying
the cloth at the seams and the mended places.
