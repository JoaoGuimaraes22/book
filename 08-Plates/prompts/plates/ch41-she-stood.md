Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a plain board box on a cart at the grey before
sunrise, straw over the floor. The door stands open at the far end,
and the light coming through it is cold and grey and full of
woodsmoke, and it falls across the straw and stops short of the
camera.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low, at straw height, looking down
the length of the box at the open door. THE PRINCESS stands in the
middle of it, upright and small in the frame, turned three-quarters
toward the camera against the pale grey of the doorway. Nearest, low
across the bottom edge, the knees of someone lying against the near
boards, dark and unresolved. To one side, a man down on one knee with
his back to the camera, one dark mass with no features on him. In the
doorway behind her, a second man bent under the roofline, a
silhouette against the light.

SHE IS STANDING AND NOTHING ABOUT IT IS ASKING PERMISSION. The back
straight, the chin level, the shoulders down and square, and the head
square on them and not turned back over one of them. She is facing
straight down the length of the box, her eyes level and steady on the
person lying at the near end. Not defiant, not frightened — someone
who has stood up.

Her hands are together in front of her, a plain closed ring of dark
metal on each wrist, with no more chain between them than the width of
her own hand, so the two wrists are held close together. A strip of
pale cloth is wound across the lower half of her face and knotted
behind her head, so that only the eyes and the brow are showing. Her
hair is loose down her back, silver-white and colourless, and two
months in the box are in it: unwashed and heavy, hanging in ropes
rather than waves, darkened with grease at the parting. What is left
of a fine gown, the cut of it still readable, the hem black with two
months of floor and every light colour in it gone grey.

She is the only upright thing in the picture and the lightest thing
in it: her hair the lightest mass in the frame and her eyes above the
cloth the only saturated colour anywhere. The men, the boards and the
straw stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges.

Lighting: cold grey daylight out of the open door and nothing else.
No lamp and no flame anywhere.

Preserve her face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale blue eyes.
