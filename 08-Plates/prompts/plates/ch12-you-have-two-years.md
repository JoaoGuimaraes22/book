Image 1: severin.png — half-figure reference of the old man.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the first court of an old stone school on the coast, by day,
as loose wash suggestion — a broad flagstone court, a colonnade of
plain columns implied down one side, and across the far end a flight
of five shallow stone steps with a wall above them going up out of
the frame. Flat grey daylight from a high overcast, no sun anywhere
and no cast shadows.

Composition: 4:3. The camera stands inside the assembly, at the
height of a child's head, looking across it to the steps. Along the
bottom third of the frame, near and dark, the backs of the heads and
shoulders of the front rank of children, cut off by the bottom edge,
drawn as loose dark shapes with no faces in them. Beyond them an
empty stretch of flagstone. On the steps, alone and central, the old
man stands at half-figure size in the frame, and his is the only
resolved face in the picture.

Behind and to either side of the near rank the rest of the assembly
is suggested in receding blocks of grey and brown wash: a great many
children standing in ordered ranks, every one of them facing the
steps.

Nothing is happening. Nobody is moving anywhere.

THE OLD MAN IS SPEAKING AND HE IS NOT PERFORMING IT: standing square
and unhurried, his weight even, his mouth open mid-word, one hand out
from his side a little way at waist height with the palm turned down,
as though setting something on a table. The brows unmoved, the eyes
level and out over the ranks. Courteous, attentive, and giving out
nothing he has not priced. Not stern, not grand, not kindly.

Hair fully grey, the whole head, a true neutral grey, even
everywhere. Tall, gone lean, still broad across the shoulder. A
long-sleeved robe of good plain dark wool closed to the throat and
falling to the ankle, the sleeves back off the wrists, and the wrists
are too thick for a man whose work is paper. No ornament, no chain,
no badge.

Colour: cool grey stone throughout, the ranks in muted browns and
greys, and his robe the darkest mass in the picture.
