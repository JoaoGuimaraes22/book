Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the boy.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the middle of the floor of a great open arena on a bright
cold morning, as loose wash suggestion — raked white sand, and beyond
the far side of the arena the top of the banked seating, and past
that open sea with many ships standing on it, small with the
distance, banners at their mastheads. A flat sea horizon runs across
the upper part of the frame under a cold blue sky. Bright flat
winter daylight from above.

Composition: chest-up, one figure, near the viewer and turned
three-quarters away, his head turned out toward the sea so that his
face is seen in three-quarter view. The bottom edge crosses him below
the chest. Behind and past him, small and low in the frame, the far
seating with a wash of standing people on it, no faces drawn, and
then the sea, the ships and the horizon line.

HIS LEFT ARM IS CUT. A long slash runs along the outside of the
left arm above the elbow, its edges clean, and there is red down
the outside of the forearm and across the back of the hand, dark and
wet. The arm is bare and unbound and no cloth or wrapping is on it.
His clothing is torn where the cut is and grubby with sand.

HIS FACE IS OPEN AND HE DOES NOT KNOW IT. The eyes wide and out on
the far horizon, the mouth closed, the brows lifted a little, the
whole face unguarded and giving everything away — and no smile
anywhere on it. Not laughing, not grinning, not triumphant, not
dazed, not in pain.

He is looking out at the ships on the horizon and nowhere else, not
at the viewer.

Undyed coarse working wool, much mended, dull oatmeal and dead olive,
with no ornament, badge, colour or metal on him anywhere. Nothing is
in either hand and no weapon is anywhere in the picture.

His face carries the sharpest step from light to dark and is the most
finished thing in the frame; the sand, the seating, the ships and the
sky stay at quiet middle values with soft edges. The banners on the
far masts are the only saturated colour anywhere in the picture.

Preserve him exactly as shown in his reference image — face, hair,
colouring. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face. Dark hair
cut short and uneven.
