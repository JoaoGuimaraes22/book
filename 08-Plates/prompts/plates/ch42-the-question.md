Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.

Setting: the top of a stone shelf on a long high back of open
country, early on a cold morning with the sun just up. The frost has
gone off the tops of the stone and is still lying in the shadows in
long white seams. The country goes away east and south in grey backs
one behind another, thorn low in the cuts between them. Down on the
flat below, a road running through the middle of it, and beside the
road a camp: fires, a picket line, animals, small tents, and its
smoke standing dead straight up in the cold. At the near end of the
camp a black box on four wheels with its door shut.

Composition: 3:4. A high vantage, the viewer up on the shelf with
him. The frame stacks away downward. Nearest, the bottom third: bare
stone, thorn, and frost in the shadowed seams. Above it, small and
low in the frame, ONE FIGURE lying flat at the lip of the shelf on
his belly, seen from behind and a little to one side, looking down.
Beyond and below him the whole flat with the road, the camp and the
standing smoke, small and complete. At the top, more grey country
going away, and pale sky.

HE IS READING GROUND, NOT WATCHING A CAMP. Nothing is happening below
and nothing is about to. The picture is a great deal of country and
one small person at the edge of it who has to decide something about
all of it.

THE FIGURE IS A SHAPE. Small, lean, dark-haired, in undyed coarse
working wool much mended, with a dark coat over him and his left arm
folded in under his chest and out of sight. He is flat on the rock
and no part of him is raised against the sky. No face is resolved.

The standing smoke is the sharpest vertical in the picture and the
frost the whitest thing in it, carried on bare unpainted paper. The
camp is drawn small and exact; everything else — the stone, the
thorn, the far country — stays at quiet middle values with soft
wash-bled edges.

Lighting: low early sun from one side, cold. Long shadows running off
the stone. Nothing is warmly lit and there is no fire in the frame
big enough to give light.
