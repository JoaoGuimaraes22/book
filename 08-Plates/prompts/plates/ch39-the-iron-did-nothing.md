Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a small wooden box on a cart, by day. Old
boards, straw over the floor, the corners in shadow. A lantern hangs
on a short hook near the roof. In the door there is a slot at the
height of a standing man's eyes, a hand wide, with three bars across
it, letting in one thin bar of grey day.

Composition: 4:3. The camera is set across the width of the box, far
enough back that both of them are in frame at about half length,
with the straw of the floor between them. AELIANA is seated against
the boards on the right, upright, her back straight, her hands
together in her lap. KAEL is on the left, lying in the straw and
propped on one elbow, his head up and turned toward her. The bar of
grey day from the slot crosses the straw between them.

NOTHING IS HAPPENING NOW. She has finished trying and this is the
state she is left in: her shoulders up and tight, the tendons
standing along both forearms, her breath held, and the metal exactly
where it was. He has said one word across the straw and is watching
to see whether it landed.

AELIANA — sitting exactly as she sat at a governor's table, upright
and waiting and doing nothing whatever, with everything going on
behind her face. A strip of cloth that was white once passes across
her mouth and is knotted behind her head, so the whole expression is
in her eyes and her brows: the brows drawn hard together, the eyes
wide open and steady on him. Not weeping. There is a thin line of
red at each wrist, and a little of it down the back of one hand,
dark and wet; below that her palms are dressed and oiled and already
healing, and the new red sits above them, on top of the old work.
Long silver-white hair down her back and over one shoulder,
colourless, never blonde, no yellow or gold in it, salt still in it,
and it is the lightest mass in the picture. What is left of a court
gown: the dark blue gone grey and salt bleached, the gold at the
shoulders dulled and dirty.

KAEL — his head up off the straw, his mouth just closing on the word
he has said, his brows a little drawn, his eyes steady on her. Eyes
pale grey, near white. A month at sea is on his face and it is all
on the surface: the lower lip split and healing, the skin scoured by
sun and salt, and the face under it sound. Dark hair, short, cut
unevenly, with straw in it. A plain undyed light fawn tunic, much
mended, no ornament and no metal on him anywhere but a plain band of
iron at each wrist.

THE TWO ARE LOOKING DIRECTLY AT EACH OTHER across the width of the
box, the lines of sight meeting over the lit straw between them.
This eye contact is the subject of the image. Neither of them is
looking at the viewer.

Her face and his are held at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled
edges, and the sharpest step from light to dark in the picture
happens inside her eye, at the edge of the iris. The red does not
take it.

Lighting: the lantern is warm and above and does most of the work,
coming down on her hair and on the straw between them and leaving
the boards behind them to go ink dark. The bar from the slot is grey
and cold and lies across the floor.

Preserve each exactly as shown in their reference image — faces,
hair, colouring, apparent age. Do not blend their features.
