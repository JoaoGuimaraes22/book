Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of Aeliana.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a small wooden box on a cart, by day. Old
boards behind her as loose wash suggestion, straw on the floor, the
corners falling into shadow. A lantern hangs on a short hook near
the roof.

Composition: 3:4. AELIANA is
seated against the boards, upright, the camera close and level with
her. Her two hands are together in front of her at the height of her
chest, the wrists side by side and near the middle of the frame; her
face is above them and fills the upper half.

The bottom edge crosses just below her hands, so that nothing of her
below the chest is in the frame.

NOTHING IS HAPPENING NOW. She has finished, and this is the state
she is in afterwards: her shoulders up and tight, the tendons
standing along both forearms, her breath held, and the metal exactly
where it was.

HER WRISTS ARE MARKED. A raw red band runs right round each wrist
under the iron, its edges rubbed rather than cut. Red runs from
under each band down the back of the hand in two or three thin
lines, dark and wet. Below that the palms are dressed, oiled and
mended and already healing, and the new red sits above them, on top
of the old work. Hold the red at soft wash-bled edges with no hard
outline drawn anywhere along it.

A plain iron band a finger thick sits round each wrist, closed with
a pin whose end is peened flat; eleven short links of the same iron
run between the two bands and hang slack. The iron is unmarked and
unmoved and has nothing on it.

HER EYES ARE DOWN ON HER OWN HANDS and on nothing else. A strip of
cloth that was white once passes across her mouth and is knotted
behind her head, so the whole expression is in her eyes and her
brows: the brows drawn hard together, the eyes wide open and
absolutely steady on her own wrists. Not weeping. She is not looking
at the viewer.

Her face and everything around it are held at quiet middle values
with soft wash-bled edges, and the sharpest step from light to dark
in the whole picture happens inside her eye, at the edge of the
iris. The red does not take it.

Long silver-white hair down her back and over one shoulder,
colourless, never blonde, no yellow or gold in it, with salt still
in it. What is left of a court gown: the dark blue gone grey and
salt bleached, the gold at the shoulders dulled and dirty.

Lighting: the lantern is the only light, warm and from above, coming
down on her hair and her forearms and the tops of her hands, leaving
the boards behind her and the corners of the box to go ink dark.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image.
