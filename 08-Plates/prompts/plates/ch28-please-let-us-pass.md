Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a narrow village lane between low stone walls on a foggy
morning, as loose wash suggestion — packed earth underfoot, small
flat-roofed houses close on both sides, smoke lying along the lane at
knee height, and a warm glow of fire in a roof further up on the
left. The daylight is cold and grey and does nearly all the work; the
fire is the only warm thing in the picture and it lights nothing.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low in the lane and close. KAEL
stands nearest, on the left, three-quarters toward the camera, caught
half a step forward with his weight already on the front foot. A few
strides beyond him A MAN of the eastern troops stands in the lane
with his back mostly to the camera, turning round at the shoulders
toward the boy, his face away and not resolved, a short spear held
down at his side and not raised. Further up the lane, two more of
them at the walls, small, unresolved, still working at the doors and
not yet turned. The bottom edge crosses Kael at mid-thigh, so that no
feet, no road surface at his feet and nothing lying in the lane are
in the frame at all. Valeria is not in the picture.

HE IS ASKING. His right hand is out in front of him, open, the palm
showing and the fingers apart, held about chest high and offered to
the man; his left hand is empty and down and has no blade in it. His
mouth is open in the middle of a word and his eyes are on the man's
face. The brows are up a little and the whole body is forward and
unguarded — nothing about him is set to fight.

He wears undyed coarse working wool in dull oatmeal and dead olive,
much mended, no ornament, badge, colour or metal on him anywhere;
his left forearm is bare of any wrapping and the skin along it is
new, pink and clean. No blade is anywhere in his hands or in the
frame but the man's spear, which is down.

The man wears the eastern troops' plain dark working kit with a
narrow green cord at the shoulder.

His face carries the sharpest step from light to dark and is the only
resolved face in the picture; the man, the lane, the walls and the
smoke stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges.
