Setting: the floor of a great arena, as loose wash suggestion — pale
raked sand underfoot running away behind him, and beyond it the banked
seating rising as a wash of small shapes with no face resolved
anywhere in it. Flat daylight, no sun and no hard-edged shadow.

Composition: one figure, waist-up, three-quarter view, the body angled
away from the viewer and the head turned back toward it. He is
standing, his weight settled, both hands empty and at his sides.
Nothing is in either hand and there is no weapon anywhere in the
picture. Nobody else is in the frame.

HIS CLOTHING IS DESTROYED AND STILL ON HIM: coarse undyed working wool
in dull oatmeal and dead olive, torn open down the front and hanging
off one shoulder, the left sleeve ripped away entirely, the cloth
shredded and filthy. Chest and one shoulder bare through the tear. A
plain leather belt sits over the remains of it, holding the shirt
together at the waist.

HIS BARE LEFT FOREARM FROM WRIST TO ELBOW IS NEW PINK SKIN — smooth,
unmarked, and clearly a different colour from the weathered skin above
the elbow and from his other arm. It is uncovered and unbandaged and
it is the newest thing on his body.

Marked from the fight everywhere else: grey dust and soot across the
chest and both forearms, dark bruising over one shoulder and along the
ribs, a few short red slashes across the torso and upper arm, and
older pale scars on the upper arm and collarbone. One eyebrow singed
short. Nothing bleeding.

Lean, hard and wiry — the ribs showing, the build of someone trained
rather than grown.

HIS FACE IS EMPTIED OUT AND STILL WORKING: the mouth slightly open on
the breath, the eyes fully open and steady, the brows level, the whole
face tired all the way down. Not slumped, not defeated, not proud, and
not blank.

HE IS LOOKING DIRECTLY OUT OF THE PICTURE AT THE VIEWER and his are
the only eyes in the frame.

His hair is dark and straight, hacked short and uneven, disordered and
damp at the temples.
