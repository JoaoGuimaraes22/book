Image 1: aurelian-14.png — portrait reference of the fair-haired boy.
Image 2: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the dark-haired boy.
Image 3: elarine-14.png — portrait reference of the girl.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a disused cistern court on a grey afternoon, as loose wash
suggestion — a low brick wall across the back, the far side of the
court in cool shade. Soft flat daylight. The floor is old brick laid
in courses, warm dark grey-brown, matte and damp, the joints drawn and
mossed dull green, two or three small shallow puddles in its hollows
taking the grey of the sky; nothing in the picture is mirror-like.

Composition: the viewer is down at the height of the near boy's face.
The fair-haired boy is nearest and largest, lying face down on the
brick propped on both elbows, his face turned across the frame to the
viewer's left; the bottom edge crosses just behind his shoulders, so
his legs are out of frame. Behind him the dark-haired boy walks across
the frame mid-stride at full length, and further back the girl sits on
the low wall. One brick floor runs unbroken from the near boy to the
wall, and all three are on it.

The fair-haired boy is delighted: grinning, the mouth open and pulled
wide with the corners up, teeth showing, the eyes bright and creased
at the outer corners, the brows up. Not alarmed, not sneering. His hair
is full of green moss. His chest rests on the brick, both forearms lie
along it from elbow to hand with the elbows under his shoulders, both
palms down; a shallow puddle stands a hand's width in front of his
face and his cheek and jaw are wet from it, nothing running. Good plain
cloth, well cut, in deep umber and warm charcoal, soaked down the
front.

The dark-haired boy mid-stride, his leading boot flat on the brick,
hands empty at his sides. Undyed working wool, coarse and much mended,
in dull oatmeal and dead olive.

The girl sits with her left hand in her lap and her right open, palm
up, one small flame the width of a coin standing on it with a clear
gap of air beneath — no wick, no candle, nothing in her hand. It is the
one bright saturated point in the picture. Plain close-fitting dark
clothing of very good quality, ink-black and dark olive, no ornament.

Where each is looking: the fair-haired boy past the left edge of the
frame at nothing, at ground level; the dark-haired boy down at his own
feet; the girl at her own open hand, not up. None of them at the
viewer. Nobody else is in the court.

Preserve all three exactly as shown in their reference images — faces,
hair, colouring, apparent age. Do not blend their features. Do not mix
their clothing: the good cloth belongs only to the fair-haired boy, the
mended wool only to the dark-haired boy, the close dark clothing only
to the girl.
