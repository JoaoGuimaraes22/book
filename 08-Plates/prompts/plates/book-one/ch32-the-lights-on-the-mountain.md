Image 1: kael-14-full.png — full-figure reference of the boy, for his build and silhouette. His face is not resolved in this picture.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a mountainside at night, high up, as loose wash suggestion. In
the near ground a small stone house with a low roof stands at the left,
its open doorway throwing one patch of warm orange light out across the
stones of a bare yard. A stacked woodpile stands at the right of the
yard, chest-high, the split faces of the logs showing. Below and beyond
the yard the ground falls away in a long dark slope of stone walls,
empty pens and terraces, going down and away into black.

Above everything, low cloud sits on the mountain and fills the whole
upper third of the picture, and the underside of that cloud is lit dull
orange from below, end to end, moving and uneven like water. It is the
largest thing in the frame.

Composition: one boy standing on top of the woodpile at the right, seen
from behind and a little below, small in the frame and dark against the
lit cloud. He is upright and completely still, both arms hanging, his
head turned down and out toward the slope below. He is the only person
in the picture.

WHAT IS BURNING BELOW HIM, EACH IN ITS OWN PLACE: seven small points of
warm light close together on the dark slope in the middle distance, low
in the frame and to the left of him, not moving. A line of nineteen
more, further down and further to the right, strung out one behind
another along a level terrace line, evenly spaced. And at the very
bottom of the picture, a long way down where the path leaves the frame,
one larger mass of orange light with no separate flames drawn in it.
Those are all the lights on the slope, and there is nothing else lit
anywhere on it.

Each of the twenty-six small ones is a single carried flame: a point of
warm orange no bigger than a pinhead in the frame, with a little haze
around it. No figures are drawn on the slope anywhere — only the lights.

NO FACE IS VISIBLE ANYWHERE IN THE PICTURE and no eyes are drawn
anywhere in it. Do not turn the boy's head toward the viewer.

The picture is nearly black and its colour is entirely in three places:
the orange under the cloud, the doorway of the house, and the lights on
the slope. The stone, the walls, the pens and the boy are cold dark
greys and browns with bare paper left in the lights on the cloud.

Preserve his build and clothing as shown in the reference image — undyed
coarse working wool, much mended, in dull oatmeal and dead olive, with
no metal on him. A cloth-wrapped bundle is across his back with the
shape of two hilts in it, the hilts covered.
