Image 2: valeria-14-full.png — full-figure reference of the same girl, for her clothing. Both images are of the same girl and there is one figure in this picture.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a bank of arena seating on a bright cold winter morning, as
loose wash suggestion — plain cut stone benches rising steeply, open
sky above and behind them, the light coming from high up and cold.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low and close in among the benches,
level with her. VALERIA sits alone in the middle of the frame,
leaning forward with her elbows on her knees and her chin resting on
both fists, looking down and out of the frame at something below her.
All round her, packed on the same benches, other children of her own
age are on their feet — legs, hips, backs and lifted arms crossing
the frame on both sides and cut off by the top edge, drawn as summary
shapes with no faces anywhere and nothing in them sharp enough to
read as a person. Hers is the only face in the picture. The bottom
edge crosses her below the knee, so that no feet, no bench front and
no floor are in the frame at all.

EVERY OTHER BODY IN THE FRAME IS UP AND SHOUTING AND SHE HAS NOT
MOVED. She sits deep in the seat with her weight forward, quite
still, her shoulders down and both fists together under her chin.

Her face: the eyes level, wide open and locked on one place below and
outside the frame, the lids up, the brows down a little, the mouth
closed. She is doing a piece of work, not enjoying a crowd.

Her black hair is loose, well past the shoulders, no ornament. Her
clothing as in her full-figure reference: the charcoal sleeveless
coat over the grey-blue underlayer, the high collar, the steel
vambraces. Nothing is in her hands and no blade appears anywhere in
the frame.

Her face carries the sharpest step from light to dark and is the only
fully resolved thing in the picture; the standing bodies, the benches
and the sky stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve her exactly as shown in her reference images — face, hair,
colouring, apparent age.
