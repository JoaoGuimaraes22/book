Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a packed bank of arena seating in fog, plain cut stone
benches rising steeply, the rows above going up into grey and losing
their shape. Cold flat light with no sun in it, and the first smoke
coming along under the benches.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is in among the rows, close, a little
above and to one side, standing in the crowd rather than in front of
it. The whole picture is a crowd on its feet and moving one way, up
and to the right toward an arch — backs, shoulders, arms and heads
carried together, drawn as summary shapes with no faces anywhere and
nothing in them sharp enough to read as a person.

ONE BODY IS TURNED THE OTHER WAY AND THAT IS THE SUBJECT OF THE
IMAGE. In the middle of the frame OSS has turned right round against
the flow, his shoulders square to the bench behind him and his back
to everywhere the crowd is going. He is bent forward with both hands
already down and out and closed on the arms of a much smaller boy on
that bench, lifting him.

HIS FACE IS DOWN AND TURNED TOWARD THE CHILD, in three-quarter view
from the side, fully drawn and fully lit — and it is not turned
toward the camera at all and he is not looking at the viewer. The
laughing half of his face is gone and the watching half is doing all
the work: the eyes down on the child's face, wide open and quick, the
brows drawn together, the mouth open a little on a short word. He is
frightened and he is doing it anyway.

The smaller boy is on the bench with his feet not yet under him, both
arms up and taken, his face turned away and not resolved.

Soft-built, with no hardness anywhere in him — a round face, full
cheeks, sloping shoulders, and the strength in nothing but the grip.
His clothing is the school's, dark and good, and newer than anything
else in the frame: the collar still standing where it was pressed and
no wear anywhere on it.

He is the only figure in the picture drawn with any finish; every
other body stays at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges,
and the sharpest step from light to dark in the picture happens
inside his eye.

Nobody in the picture is on the ground, nobody is under anybody, and
no fire and no weapon is in the frame.
