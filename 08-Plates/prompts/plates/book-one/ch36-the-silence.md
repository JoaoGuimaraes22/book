Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the girl.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Composition: 3:4. A close-up of her face. Head and shoulders only,
the head large in the frame.

Setting: the side of a small open boat behind her, worn planking and
a low gunwale, and past it flat pale sea going out to nothing. Soft
daylight from one side.

Age: fourteen. The eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head, the
jaw softly defined and not sharp.

She has just been told to her face that she is not worth it, and she
has not answered, and she is not going to.

AELIANA — the face still, the eyes open and turned down and out to
the water going past the side, past the edge of the frame. The mouth
closed. Nothing in the face is moving. Not crying and not angry.

Long silver-white hair, colourless, the lightest mass in the
picture, defined in ink strands against the pale ground and never
dissolving into it — come down out of its construction and dried
into ropes with the salt still in it. Her skin is pale and untouched
and there is not one mark on her face. The collar of a good gown,
gold thread at the shoulder gone dull and the cloth stiff with salt,
and nothing else of it in frame.

Lighting: flat afternoon daylight off the water, coming up onto the
underside of her face as much as down onto it.

Her face carries the only finished drawing in the picture; the
planking, the gunwale and the sea have no hard edge anywhere in
them.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Her eyes are pale blue.
