Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the boy.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the flat stone working ground of a mountain smallholding at
first light in early spring, as loose wash suggestion — wet stone
underfoot, the yard falling away into cool grey wash behind him. Cold
flat grey daylight, no sun and no shadow with an edge anywhere.

Composition: chest-up and close, one figure. The boy stands with his
head up and his chin lifted a little. Into the frame at the height of
his collar comes a plain blade, held level and perfectly still, and
the hand and forearm holding it; the rest of that figure is outside
the frame and nothing above the elbow is in the picture. The blade
has come to a stop a finger's width clear of him and rests there,
level and motionless, in contact with nothing.

This is the family's morning practice and it has been stopped: the
blade is being held where it is so that the boy can see where his
guard was late. Nobody is moving. Nothing is happening.

HIS FACE IS THE SUBJECT AND IT HAS COME OUT THE FAR SIDE OF FRIGHT.
The eyes wide open and very clear, fixed and steady; the mouth
closed; the jaw set; the breath shallow and quick in the throat and
the nostrils. Everything in the face is working and nothing in it is
panicking. Not weeping, not pleading, not defiant, and not blank.

He is looking along the blade to the hand that holds it at the edge
of the frame, and nowhere else — not at the viewer, and his head does
not turn toward the viewer.

Undyed coarse working wool, much mended, dull oatmeal and dead olive,
the collar open at the throat; no ornament and no metal on him
anywhere.

The steel is the one hard bright thing in the picture: it and his
face carry the sharpest step from light to dark and are the two most
finished things in it, and they are a finger's width apart. The wet
stone and the yard behind stay at quiet middle values with soft edges
and no detail.

Preserve him exactly as shown in his reference image — face, hair,
colouring. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face. Dark hair
cut short and uneven.
