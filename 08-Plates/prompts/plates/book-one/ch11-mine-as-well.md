Image 1: vask.png — portrait reference of the man.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the single room of a small mountain house at night, as loose
wash suggestion — a rough plank wall behind him, the room falling
away into deep cool shadow. One oil lamp stands on the table just
outside the frame at the left and is the only light in the picture.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is close and level with him, from his
left side, at a table's width. He is seated on a bench, chest-up, the
body angled away and the head turned down toward his own hand. His
right hand is held up in front of his own chest, open and cupped,
the palm turned toward his face; the hand is nearer the camera than
he is and reads about as wide in the frame as his own head. On the
bench beside him, cut by the bottom edge, a mended leather harness
strap lies where he has just set it down, with a curved awl and a
length of waxed thread on the boards next to it.

A THIN WHITE CRACK OF LIGHTNING IS STITCHED ACROSS THE HOLLOW OF HIS
PALM AND IT IS THE SUBJECT OF THE PICTURE. One line only, about as
long as the hand is wide, hard-edged and forked once, standing clear
of the skin and touching nothing. It is reserved as bare unpainted
paper and it is the lightest thing in the frame by a long way. It is
one line and nothing else comes off the hand.

HIS FACE IS DOING ONE THING: he is looking down into his own palm the
way he looks at weather — the eyes steady on the hand, the brows
unmoved, the mouth closed. Not proud, not surprised, not warm. A man
checking something he has known about for thirty years.

Iron-dark hair of medium length with grey scattered thinly through it
at the temples, no streak, seam or band of white anywhere in it. A
heavily weathered face. Coarse undyed working wool, much mended, in
dull oatmeal, the sleeve pushed back off the raised forearm; no
ornament and no metal on him.

Lighting: the lamp is warm and low and off to the left, and it lights
the near side of his face, the forearm and the underside of the
cupped hand, and leaves the far side of him and the wall behind him
to go into deep cool shadow.

Colour: the lamplight is a deep warm orange and is the only saturated
colour in the frame; the wall, the bench and the wool are cool
grey-brown; the crack in the palm is a cold white with a little blue
in it and nothing else in the picture is that colour.

His face and the room are held at quiet middle values with soft
wash-bled edges and no hard-edged dark anywhere, and the sharpest
step from light to dark in the whole picture happens at the edge of
the white line lying in the palm.

He is alone. No other figure and no part of one is anywhere in the
frame.

Preserve him exactly as shown in his reference image — face, hair,
