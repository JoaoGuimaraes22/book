Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the boy.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the outside wall of a stone guest range at a school, in the
last of a winter afternoon, as loose wash suggestion — dressed grey
blocks with wide mortar joints, one plain stone window sill standing
a hand's width off the face of the wall, and a shuttered window above
it. Far below, small and going into shadow at the bottom edge, the
flagstones of an empty court. Low sun raking along the face of the
wall from the left, so that the sill and every joint throws a hard
shadow to the right.

Composition: 3:4. High-angle view, the camera out from the wall and a
little above him, looking down the face of it: the top of his head
and his shoulders are seen from above, the top surface of the sill is
visible with his fingers hooked over its outer edge, and the drop
under him is open all the way down to the flagstones at the bottom of
the frame, with nothing at all beneath his boots. He is in the upper
third of the frame, hanging by both hands from the sill, the arms
straight and locked, the body flat against the wall, the boots clear
of the stone altogether and hanging free. His head is turned in to
the wall and up, the cheek almost against the stone, so that his face
is in three-quarter view from where the camera is.

NOTHING IS HAPPENING AND HE HAS BEEN THERE A LONG TIME. He is not
climbing, not reaching for anything and not looking down.

HIS FACE IS DOING ONE THING, WHICH IS WAITING. The mouth closed, the
jaw set but easy, the brows a little drawn, the eyes open and steady
on the stone a foot in front of him. Not frightened, not straining,
not pleased with himself.

The work is in the hands and the forearms: the fingers hooked over
the lip of the sill and gone white at the knuckles, the tendons
standing along the backs of the hands, the forearms corded, the
shoulders pushed up round his ears by the weight hanging off them.

Coarse undyed working wool, much mended, in dull oatmeal and dead
olive, the sleeves pushed back off the forearms; a plain leather
belt; soft dark leather boots, worn. No ornament and no metal on him.

He is alone: there is no other figure and no part of one anywhere in
the frame, and no face at the shuttered window above him.

Colour: cool grey stone throughout, with the low sun laying one thin
warm line along the top edge of the sill and along his forearms; his
eyes are pale grey and near white and are the lightest thing in the
face.

Preserve his face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference image.
