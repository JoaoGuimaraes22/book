Image 1: kael-14-full.png — full-figure reference of the boy, for his build and silhouette. His face is not resolved in this picture.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a great stone arena in fog, seen from down on
its floor. Pale raked sand fills the lower part of the frame. Banked
seating rises beyond and above it, packed with people, and goes up
into grey and stops being seats. Flat cold fogged daylight, no sun and
no hard-edged shadow anywhere.

THE FOG IS IN TWO SEPARATE LAYERS AND BOTH ARE VISIBLE: a thin flat
sheet of it lying on the sand at about the height of a man's knee,
level and unbroken across the whole floor — and, far above, a solid
grey lid sitting across the top rail of the building, so that the far
side of the bowl is only a suggestion behind it.

Composition: one small dark figure standing alone on the sand in the
lower third of the frame, well off centre, seen from behind and a
little to one side, both arms hanging at his sides. He is small in
the frame and his face is not visible and not turned toward the
viewer. He is completely still and he is the only person on the sand.

WHAT IS IN THE AIR, EACH IN ITS OWN PLACE: one burning stone high in
the frame near the top rail, just come in over the rim, with fire
streaming backward off it in a long tail. A second burning stone lower
and further off, dropping toward the far seating. A third further away
still and smaller with the distance. Three stones in the air, and no
other flying object anywhere in the picture. Each is a rough block
about as long as three seated people are wide, wrapped and burning
along its whole surface.

A fourth has already gone into the seating on one side: timber broken
downward in three rows and fire burning in the hole it made, and grey
smoke coming up through the boards around it in fifty small separate
threads in neat rows.

The crowd in the seating is standing and moving, drawn as a wash of
small shapes with no face resolved anywhere and nobody drawn in
detail.

The fires are the only saturated colour in the picture — deep orange
and pale yellow — and the only warm light in it. Everything else is
cold grey, pale sand and wet stone.

The boy on the sand is the smallest thing in the frame and the
stillest, and everything else in the picture is in motion.

Preserve his build and clothing as shown in the reference image —
undyed coarse working wool, much mended, in dull oatmeal and dead
olive, with no metal on him. Nothing is in either hand.
