Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of the girl.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Composition: 3:4. A close-up of her face. Head and shoulders only,
the head large in the frame.

Setting: a stone wall at night behind her, unresolved, and off to
one side a low warm glow where something is burning on the ground.

She has just come off a door frame and she has not won.

THE LEFT SIDE OF HER FACE IS BURNED. From the eyebrow down across
the cheekbone to the jaw, red and shining, a smooth shine on the
skin, and minutes old. The right side of her face is clean. Nothing
is bleeding.

VALERIA — the face level, the eyes open and steady on something past
the edge of the frame, the mouth closed, the jaw hard. Not
triumphant. Long black hair, loose and disordered, some of it fallen
across the burned side. Dried red along the line of her jaw, dark
and gone brown, and it is not hers. Dark near-black clothing at the
collar and nothing else of it in frame.

Lighting: the fire on the ground is the only source, low and warm
and off to her left, so the light comes up across the burned side of
her face and the far side goes into shadow.

Her face is the only thing in the picture with a hard edge anywhere
on it, and her eyes are the most saturated point in it.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Her eyes are emerald green.
