Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the boy.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the flat stone working ground of a mountain smallholding at
first light in early spring, as loose wash suggestion — wet stone
underfoot, the yard falling away into cool grey wash behind him. Cold
flat grey daylight, no sun and no shadow with an edge anywhere.

Composition: chest-up and close, one figure. The boy of twelve stands
with his head up and his chin lifted a little. Into the frame at the
height of his throat comes a plain sword, held level and perfectly
still, edge toward him, and the hand and bare forearm of a grown man
holding it; the man's body, shoulder and face are outside the frame
and nothing of him above the elbow is in the picture. The blade is
stopped a finger's width clear of the boy's neck and is touching him
nowhere. Nothing is being cut, no skin is broken, and there is no
blood, no wound and no mark anywhere in the picture.

HIS FACE IS THE SUBJECT AND IT HAS COME OUT THE FAR SIDE OF FRIGHT.
The eyes wide open and very clear, fixed and steady; the mouth closed;
the jaw set; the breath shallow and quick in the throat and the
nostrils. Everything in the face is working and nothing in it is
panicking. Not weeping, not pleading, not defiant, and not blank.

He is looking along the blade to the hand that holds it at the edge of
the frame, and nowhere else — not at the viewer, and his head does not
turn toward the viewer.

Undyed coarse working wool, much mended, dull oatmeal and dead olive,
the collar open at the throat; no ornament and no metal on him
anywhere.

The steel is the one hard bright thing in the picture: it and his face
carry the sharpest step from light to dark and are the two most
finished things in it, and they are a finger's width apart. The wet
stone and the yard behind stay at quiet middle values with soft edges
and no detail.

Preserve him exactly as shown in his reference image — face, hair,
colouring. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face. Dark hair
cut short and uneven. He is twelve years old.
