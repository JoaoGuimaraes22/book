Image 1: severin.png — portrait reference of the old man.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a great stone arena in fog, seen from down on
its floor and looking up at one curve of the seating. Banked rows rise
across the frame, packed with people, going up into grey. A stone
walkway runs across the front of them. Flat cold fogged daylight, no
sun and no hard-edged shadow.

Composition: the old man stands alone on the walkway in the lower part
of the frame, side-on and in three-quarter view, in frame from the
knees up and large enough that his face and his hand read clearly. His
near arm is raised and extended out and up from his body toward the
thing above him, the hand open, the arm clear with nothing crossing
it. His other arm is down at his side. His weight is even and his feet
are still.

ABOVE HIM AND CLEAR OF THE SEATS HANGS A BURNING STONE, and it is
stopped: a rough block about as long as three seated people are wide,
wrapped and burning along its whole surface, sitting motionless in the
air with about the height of a man of empty space between it and the
heads below it. The fire on it is burning steadily and going nowhere —
no tail, no streak, no motion trailing behind it, nothing falling off
it.

THERE IS NOTHING BETWEEN HIS HAND AND THE STONE. The air between them
is empty and plain. No light, no glow, no beam, no rays, no sparks, no
aura, no shimmer and no distortion anywhere in the picture, and
nothing at all is coming off the man.

EVERYTHING IN THE PICTURE HAS STOPPED. The people in the rows above
and behind him are standing still with their faces turned up toward
the stone, drawn as a wash of small shapes with no face resolved and
nobody in detail, and not one of them is moving. Nothing is falling
and nothing is running.

HIS FACE IS AT WORK AND COSTS HIM NOTHING: the eyes up and steady on
the stone, the mouth closed, the brows level, the jaw easy, the whole
face unhurried. Not straining, not grim, not frightened, and not
blank.

He is looking up at the stone and at nothing else, and not at the
viewer.

He is tall and gone lean and still broad across the shoulder, and his
wrists are thick. His hair is fully grey — the whole head, an even
neutral grey with no streak, seam or patch anywhere in it. He wears a
plain grey robe and a heavy chain of office across the chest.

The fire on the stone is the only saturated colour and the only warm
light in the picture; everything else is cold grey and wet stone.

Preserve him exactly as shown in the reference image — face, hair,
colouring, apparent age. His eyes are dark, near black.
