Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a walled yard behind a squat old stone building in a
southern river city, on a hard bright day. Dry swept ground, walls
the colour of the ground with the ground's own grain in them, one
gate in the wall to the street, narrow slits high up in the stone
building. Loose wash suggestion, no detail anywhere. Flat bright
daylight from one side.

Composition: 3:4. KAEL stands near the camera at one side of the
yard, three-quarters toward it, both hands resting on the handles of
a plain wooden barrow. Two strides from him, at the gate, A MAN
stands with his thumbs hooked in his belt, half turned away, talking
to a second man beside him; no features drawn on either of them.
Further back against the far wall: a plain table with a man sitting
at it over a board and a strip of paper, a second man standing beside
the table with his face toward the gate, and on the ground behind
them a heavy wooden chest with a soldier on either side of it. Four
or five men are crossing the yard from the gate toward the table.

NOTHING IS HAPPENING. The two at the gate are talking to each other.
The men crossing the yard are looking at the table. Nobody in the
yard is looking at Kael.

HIS EYES ARE ON THE CHEST. Level across the yard, past the table, at
the chest behind it, the head barely turned — and not on the man two
strides from him. Mouth closed and easy, brows level, shoulders down,
weight settled on both feet.

His left arm is bound from the knuckles to above the elbow in clean
linen with the thumb splinted between two flat pieces of wood, held
in against his chest, and the linen is the whitest thing in the
picture. Undyed coarse working wool, much mended, with the dust of
the yard through all of it.

The six men of the building — two at the gate, two at the table, two
at the chest — are in plain dark military kit. The men crossing the
yard are in loose pale wound cloth with their heads covered. The
military kit belongs only to the six, and Kael wears none of it.

Kael's face and the near half of the barrow carry the finished
drawing; the men at the gate, the table, the chest, the wall and the
stone building stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges
and no hard edge in them.
