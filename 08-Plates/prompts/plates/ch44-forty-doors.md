Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.

Setting: the yard of a village smith at the top end of a narrow fold,
in the middle of the night. A low dry-stone wall along the downhill
side of the yard, and past it the fold going away down: a village of
about forty low stone houses strung out along the water on both sides
of it, roofs and shut doors, and the water a pale thread running
between them. The sides of the fold go up dark and the sky above them
is the lightest thing in the picture.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is in the yard, standing behind them.
The frame stacks away downward. Nearest, low across the bottom third:
the dirt of the yard and the top of the dry-stone wall. Standing at
that wall with their backs to the camera, THREE FIGURES, close
together and not touching, looking down the fold — one taller with
long dark hair, one smaller with the whole of her hair loose and pale
down her back, one boy with one arm held in against his chest. Below
and beyond them the whole village, small and complete. At the top, the
far side of the fold and the night sky.

NOT ONE HOUSE DOWN THERE HAS A LIGHT IN IT. No lamp, no candle, no
fire, no lit window, no open door anywhere in the length of the
village. Every door is shut. The forty roofs and the forty shut doors
are drawn small and exact and they carry the ink drawing.

BEHIND THE VIEWER AND OUT OF FRAME, ONE FORGE IS OPEN AND WORKING.
Its light comes into the picture from behind the camera as one low
warm patch lying across the yard dirt at the bottom of the frame and
up the back of the wall, and it reaches nothing else: it does not
touch the village, it does not touch the fold, and it does not put a
rim on anybody. The three at the wall stand at the far edge of it and
are dark shapes with no faces shown and nothing resolved on them.

Cold night everywhere else. The sky carries bare unpainted paper; the
stone, the roofs, the water and the sides of the fold stay at quiet
low values with open granulation and soft wash-bled edges; the one
patch of warm light on the yard dirt is the only warm thing in the
picture and it is small and low and at the near edge.
