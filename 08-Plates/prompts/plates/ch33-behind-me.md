Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of the girl.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: nothing in this picture is resolved except her. Behind her
head, close and soft, the dark of a stone wall at night, and further
off and lower a low warm glow where something is burning on the
ground.

Composition: 3:4. HER HEAD FILLS THE FRAME. The top of her head is
cut by the top edge, her chin sits about a third of the way up from
the bottom edge, and the distance from her chin to her hairline is
most of the height of the picture. Her face is turned barely off
front-on, with the LEFT side toward the camera. NOTHING ELSE IS IN
THE FRAME: no shoulders, no neck below the throat, no hands, no
blade, no doorway and no ground.

She has just come off a door frame and she has not won.

THE LEFT SIDE OF HER FACE IS BURNED. It runs from the eyebrow down
across the cheekbone to the jaw, red and shining, a smooth shine on
the skin, and it is minutes old. Nothing is broken and nothing is
bleeding, and the right side of her face is clean.

VALERIA — the face level, the eyes open and steady on something past
the edge of the frame, the mouth closed, the jaw hard. Not
triumphant. Long black hair, loose and disordered, some of it fallen
across the burned side and stuck to it. There is dried red along the
line of her jaw and up onto the cheekbone, dark and gone brown, and
it is not hers.

Lighting: the fire on the ground behind her is the only source, low
and warm and off to her left, so the light comes up across the
burned side of her face and the far side of it goes into shadow.

Her face is the only thing in the picture that has a hard edge
anywhere on it, and her eyes are the most saturated point in it.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Her eyes are emerald green.
