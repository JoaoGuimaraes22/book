Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.
Warm living skin, soft ochre in the mid-tones — the warmth belongs to
the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth.

Setting: the dirt of a small yard at night, hard-packed and swept,
with a trough, a woodpile and a shut door somewhere behind in the
dark. Out of an open doorway above and to one side a lamp is being
carried, and its light lies across the dirt in one moving fall and
reaches nothing else.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is down on the dirt itself, at the
height of a face lying on the ground, looking up and along it. TWO
HANDS come down into the light in the upper middle of the frame,
held out flat toward the viewer at about the height of a standing
person's waist, and they are the whole subject. Nearest, the bottom
half of the frame: the swept dirt, in the fall of lamplight, going
out of focus. No face is in the picture and nobody is shown above the
wrists.

THE HANDS ARE OPEN AND EMPTY AND THEY ARE NOT MOVING. Palms turned
up, the fingers spread a little and slack, the wrists relaxed. They
are not reaching, not pointing, not offering anything and not warding
anything off. They are simply held out, in front of a stranger, and
kept there.

They are a girl's hands, small and thin and not worked — the nails
short and broken, the skin dirty, the knuckles grazed. On each wrist,
above them, a plain closed ring of dark metal, and between the two
rings no more chain than the width of one of her own hands, so the
wrists are held close together.

The lamplight is the only light and it falls on the palms. The hands
carry the sharpest step from light to dark in the frame and the most
finished ink drawing in it; the dirt beneath them is soft wash with
open granulation and no hard edge, and the yard behind them goes to
flat dark with nothing resolved in it.

Lighting: one warm lamp out of frame above and to one side, low and
close to the ground because it is being carried by somebody standing.
Nothing else is lit.
