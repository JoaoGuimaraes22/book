Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights. Plus dry-brush drag and flicked spatter
for the movement.

Setting: the trodden yard of a small stone house on a mountain at
night, as loose wash suggestion — the wall of the house close behind
her with a low doorway standing open in it and light coming out, and
beyond the man, further off, a line of dry-stone pens burning along
the slope. Two sources and no others.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is at the side of the two of them,
close, at the height of the girl's head and looking slightly up, so
that the underside of the man's jaw is visible and the tops of his
shoulders are not. VALERIA is on the left, front-on to him and
driven the whole way in against him. THE MAN is on the right, two
heads taller, front-on to her, standing between her and the burning
pens so that he is against the light. THE TWO OF THEM ARE ONE MASS
IN THE MIDDLE OF THE FRAME, overlapping from the hip to the
shoulder: there is no gap of background between their bodies
anywhere below the chin, and her feet are on top of his feet. The
bottom edge crosses her at the hip and him at mid-thigh, so that the
ground, the doorstep and anything lying on either of them are out of
the frame. Nobody else is in the picture.

THE SUBJECT OF THE IMAGE IS THE DISTANCE, WHICH IS NONE. She has
come the whole way in past every range either of them has, and she
is inside his arms and standing on his feet, and he is only now
getting hold of her.

HER BLADE IS IN HIM. One longsword is in his side, low, under the
ribs on the left, going in at a slight upward angle, and about four
inches of it is not showing. Her fist is against his clothing where
it enters. There is red on his clothing around it, dark and wet, and
nowhere else on either of them. Nothing else is bleeding.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
Her right hand holds one longsword and nothing else, and the point
of that blade is the one in his side.
Her left hand holds one longsword and nothing else, held low and
back at her own hip, the point down and behind her, the whole blade
clear of both bodies and touching nothing.
His left hand is shut in her hair at the back of her head, closed.
His right hand is open and coming toward her right wrist and has not
reached it.
Four hands, two blades, nothing else in any hand. Both her scabbards
are empty. No other blade, blade tip or hilt appears anywhere in the
frame.

Each blade is a plain longsword with the blade about as long as she
is from her shoulder to her knee, a straight cross guard, no
ornament on either of them.

VALERIA — the weight all forward and up into him, the near shoulder
driven into his body, the head back so the face is up into his. The
eyes on his face, the mouth closed, the jaw hard. Everything below
her neck is committed and her face is doing arithmetic. Not
triumphant, not frightened, not shouting. Long black hair, no
ornament, pulled hard at the back by the hand shut in it, the rest
of it out and loose with the movement. Dark near-black clothing,
plain and well made, filthy and torn at one shoulder — good cloth
with a bad night on it, not ragged. There is a great deal of dried
red on her forearms and down one side of her, dark and gone brown,
and none of that is hers. There is no mark of any kind on her face.

THE MAN — tall, thin, young, in plain dark clothing with no armour
and no ornament anywhere on him. He is over her: the chin coming
down toward her and the shoulders coming forward. NO FEATURES ARE
DRAWN ON HIS FACE AT ALL — he is between her and the burning pens
and reads as a dark shape against them, and his head is not turned
toward the viewer.

Lighting: the burning pens behind him are the source that does the
work — they put a hard warm edge down the whole outline of him and
light her face and the front of her from beyond his shoulder. The
open doorway behind her puts a second warm edge along her hair and
her near shoulder. Everything else in the yard goes to a deep dark.

Her face and the point of contact where the blade enters carry the
most finished drawing in the picture; the man, the doorway, the pens
and the yard stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in her reference images. Her eyes are emerald green.
