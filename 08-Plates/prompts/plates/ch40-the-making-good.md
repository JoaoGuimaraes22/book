Image 1: portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a plain board box on a cart, at first light,
straw over the floor. Behind the camera, a slot a hand wide with
three bars across it, and the first grey of the day comes through it
in one thin bar.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low, at straw height and close, set
across the width of the box. KAEL lies against the side boards with
his knees up, turned three-quarters toward it, the bar of grey day
across his chest and his hands. The bottom edge crosses him at the
thigh: only the straw and the boards immediately around him are in
frame. Set into the boards behind his shoulder, a plain ring of dark
metal.

EVERYTHING HERE IS ARRANGED TO LOOK UNTOUCHED, and the arrangement
is the subject. A short length of dark linked metal lies out in the
straw in the loose shape such a thing makes when it is doing
nothing. From the ring, a rope tail is laid in a curve on the boards
with a bight in it that from a doorway would read as still tied. The
straw under him is turned over and put back flat. These three carry
the sharpest step from light to dark in the frame and the most
finished ink drawing in it; his face comes next; everything else is
soft wash.

His left hand is folded shut, the thumb flat across the palm, held
in low against his chest and turned away from the camera. A plain
closed ring of dark metal lies loose on that wrist. The back of the
hand, knuckles to wrist, is dark red gone brown where it has dried
and is not resolved, and the same is along the inside of the forearm
below it. His right wrist carries the same ring, with a short run of
links lying away from it along the straw.

KAEL lying still: back to the boards, shoulders let down, breathing
the shallow even way someone asleep breathes, the lashes down and the
eyes open under them, steady on the barred slot behind the viewer.
Nothing in the face admits the hand. Dark hair, short, cut unevenly,
straw in it. A plain undyed light fawn tunic, much mended, no
ornament.

Lighting: cold grey daylight and no other kind. The bar off the slot
is the brightest thing in the frame and carries bare unpainted
paper; the rest of the box sits at quiet middle values with nothing
black in it. No lamp and no flame anywhere.

Preserve him exactly as shown in his reference image — face, hair,
colouring. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face.
