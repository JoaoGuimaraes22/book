Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a small wooden box on a cart, at first light.
Old boards, straw over the floor, the corners in shadow, a dead
lantern hanging cold on a short hook near the roof. Behind the
camera there is a slot in the door, a hand wide with three bars
across it, and the first grey day comes through it in one thin bar.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low, down at straw height, set
across the width of the box. KAEL lies against the side boards with
his knees up, turned three-quarters toward the camera, and the bar
of grey day lies across his chest and his hands. The bottom edge of
the picture crosses him at the thigh, so that no knees, no feet, no
floor beyond him and no other part of the box are in the frame at
all. Set into the boards behind his shoulder, an iron ring.

EVERYTHING IN THIS PICTURE IS ARRANGED TO LOOK UNTOUCHED, and the
arrangement is the subject. The chain lies out in the straw in the
loose shape chain makes when it is doing nothing. From the ring, a
rope tail is laid in a curve on the boards with a bight in it, so
that from a doorway it would read as a rope still tied. The straw
under him is turned over and put back flat.

HIS LEFT HAND IS OUT OF THE IRON AND THE IRON IS BACK OVER IT. The
band — iron a finger thick, closed with a pin whose end is peened
flat — hangs on the wrist where iron hangs, and the hand inside it
is folded into itself, the thumb lying flat across the palm and the
fingers closed over it, so that it is smaller than a hand. The back
of it is skinned from the knuckles to the wrist, the skin turned
back in one piece and caught in a fold at the wrist, and there is
red across the back of the hand and down the inside of the forearm,
dark and drying. He holds it in against his chest and does not move
it. His right wrist carries the same band and eleven short links,
and that one is still on him.

KAEL lying exactly as he has lain for sixty-two days, back against
the boards, shoulders let down, breathing the shallow even way a
sleeping boy breathes. The eyelids almost closed and the lashes
down, the eyes still open under them and steady on the door, the
mouth closed and the jaw set. Nothing in the face admits the hand.
Eyes pale grey, near white. Dark hair, short, cut unevenly, straw in
it. A plain undyed light fawn tunic, much mended, no ornament and no
metal on him but the iron. Two months in the box has taken weight
off him and left the rest.

Lighting: the cold grey bar from the slot is nearly all of it, on
his chest and his hands, and it leaves his face and the boards
behind him in low quiet values.

Preserve his face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image.
