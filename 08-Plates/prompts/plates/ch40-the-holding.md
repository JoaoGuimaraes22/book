Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.
Warm living skin, soft ochre in the mid-tones — the warmth belongs to
the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth.

Setting: the floor of a dark board box at night, straw over the
boards. Out of frame, a slot with three bars in it, and a fire
standing thirty feet outside the box sends one narrow bar of low
light in through the bars. That bar is the only light in the picture.

Composition: 3:4. A close study of two hands and a rope, low down and
near, at straw height. Nobody is in the picture: no face, no head, no
body and nothing above the wrists — two forearms come in from the
near edge of the frame and there is nothing else.

THE LEFT HAND IS SHUT ON THE ROPE AND IT IS THE WRECKED ONE. It is
closed round the standing part of a thick hemp rope and holding it
still. The back of it, from the knuckles to the wrist, is dark red
gone brown and glossed where it is drying, the skin gathered and
caught in a fold at the wrist. Its shape is not a hand's shape any
more: swollen out of its own outline, the knuckles gone under the
swelling, the fingers thickened and lying closed round the rope by
weight rather than by grip. A plain closed ring of dark metal, a
finger thick, hangs loose on that wrist above it.

THE RIGHT HAND IS DOING THE WORK. Whole, filthy, and feeding the tail
of the rope back through a knot a quarter of an inch at a time — the
thumb and two fingers on the tail, the heel of the hand braced on the
standing part. A second ring of the same dark metal is closed on that
wrist, with a short run of links running off it away into the straw.

The left hand and the knot hold the sharpest step from light to dark
in the frame and the most finished ink drawing in it; the straw, the
boards and the rest of the rope stay at quiet middle values with soft
wash-bled edges and no hard edge anywhere in them.

Lighting: the box is dark. The one weak bar off the slot crosses the
straw and falls across the two hands and the knot, and leaves
everything else in low quiet dark. The source is far outside, so
nothing here is brightly lit and there is no rim light anywhere.
