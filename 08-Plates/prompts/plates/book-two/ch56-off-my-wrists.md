Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a slope of bare ground above a valley at night. Loose wash
suggestion — a fire out of frame to one side, and below and far off a
handful of small lamps in a town, unresolved. Nothing else anywhere.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is low and near the ground. IN THE NEAR
FOREGROUND, large and across the bottom third of the frame, a set of
iron lying on bare earth. BEYOND IT, sitting on the ground facing each
other, THE MAN on the left and KAEL on the right, chest-up, close
enough to talk quietly. Nobody else is in the picture.

Age: fourteen. The boy's eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head,
the jaw softly defined and not sharp.

THE IRON: five plain bands and a length of chain, dropped together on
the ground out of a piece of sacking that is open under them. The
bands are dull dark metal, thin, with a pin through each; the inner
edge of each band is rubbed bright and smooth where it has sat on a
wrist, and no other part of them is worn at all.

THE MAN IS LOOKING AT THE IRON AND HAS NOT MOVED. He is leaning
forward a little with his forearms on his knees. The one eye is down
on the bands and has not come off them; the brow is level; the beard
has his mouth. Nothing in his face is deciding anything where it can
be seen.

KAEL IS NOT LOOKING AT THE IRON. His head is turned to the man and his
eyes are on the man's face, waiting. The mouth closed, the brows
level.

Lighting: the fire off to one side is the only light. It comes across
low, catching the top edges of the bands, the near side of each face
and the man's hands, and leaving the slope, the far side of both
figures and everything behind them dark.

The man wears a plain dark coat over a plain collar. Kael wears a
plain dark coat over a tunic, travel-worn. The leather patch belongs
only to the man.

The picture falls away to bare unpainted paper at its edges rather
than being painted out to the border.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference images. Kael's eyes pale grey; the man's uncovered
eye light grey-brown. Do not blend their features.
