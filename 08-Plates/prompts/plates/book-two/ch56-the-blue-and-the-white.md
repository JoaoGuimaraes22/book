Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a camp at night. Loose wash suggestion — nothing behind them
but dark, with the fire itself out of frame beyond their shoulders.

Composition: 3:4. Waist-up, two women sitting side by side on the
ground, close, turned slightly toward each other, both with sewing in
their laps and both hands of each in frame. THE WOMAN is on the left,
THE PRINCESS on the right. Nobody else is in the picture.

Age: the girl fifteen. Her eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head,
the jaw softly defined and not sharp.

Lighting: the fire is behind them and out of frame, so their backs are
dark and their faces are not lit from the front at all. THE CLOTH IN
THEIR LAPS IS THE BRIGHTEST THING IN THE PICTURE and it throws the
light back up onto the undersides of both faces and onto their hands.
Everything above their heads goes into dark.

WHAT IS IN EACH PAIR OF HANDS:
The woman has a wide piece of flat dead white cloth spread across her
knees, her left hand under it and her right holding a needle in it,
half a stitch through.
The girl has a folded dark coat across her knees, and along its edge a
band of deep blue as wide as a finger; her left hand pinches the band
flat against the cloth and her right is drawing the needle away from
the last stitch.

THE WOMAN'S HEAD IS STILL DOWN OVER HER OWN WORK AND HER EYES HAVE
GONE ACROSS ONTO THE GIRL'S STITCHES. Only the eyes have moved.
Nothing in her face has decided anything about it; the mouth is
closed and the brows are level.

THE GIRL IS NOT LOOKING AT HER OWN NEEDLE. Her head is down but her
eyes are along the edge of the coat where the blue has to sit, the
mouth closed, the brows drawn slightly together. She is working
quickly and she is not thinking about her hands.

The white belongs only to the woman and the blue only to the girl.
Both wear coarse pale undyed working cloth, the girl's sleeves down
over both wrists to the base of the hand.

Both women have their heads covered with plain wound cloth and no
hair shows anywhere on either of them.

Preserve each face, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown in the
reference images. The woman's eyes dark brown, the girl's pale blue.
Do not blend their features.
