Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a fold in grey stone high on open country, early on a cold
morning just after sunrise — a slab come away from the seam behind
it, its face cold and flat, and past it long grey backs of country
going away one behind another with thorn low in the hollows. Loose
wash suggestion, no detail anywhere. Flat early daylight from low on
one side.

Composition: 3:4. Waist-up, one figure, near the viewer, sitting back
against the stone face. The camera is a little above her and looking
down, from where somebody standing with one hand on the rock would
be. Her hands are in her lap at the bottom edge of the frame. Nobody
else is in the picture.

SHE IS LOOKING STRAIGHT BACK AT HIM AND SHE IS WAITING. The head up,
the eyes level and straight out at the camera and holding it, the
face still. She has said something and she is waiting to be answered:
not pleading, not apologising, not defiant.

HER FACE IS UNCOVERED FOR THE FIRST TIME. The corners of her mouth
have gone — split into the flesh on both sides, dark and dry, long
healed and split again — and the rest of the mouth is closed and
unhurt. Two months without enough water is in the lips and under the
eyes. Pale untouched skin under it, freckles across the nose and
cheeks.

Her hair is loose down her back and over one shoulder, silver-white
and colourless, and two months in a box are in it: unwashed and
heavy, hanging in ropes rather than waves, darkened with grease at
the parting.

Her hands rest together in her lap, a plain closed ring of dark metal
on each wrist with no more chain between them than the width of her
own hand. She is wearing a man's coat far too big for her, dull dark
wool, the shoulders hanging off her and the cuffs past her hands,
with the ruin of a fine gown under it.

Her face carries the sharpest step from light to dark in the frame
and the most finished drawing in it; the coat is the darkest mass and
the stone and the country behind stay at quiet middle values with
soft wash-bled edges. Her eyes are the only saturated colour
anywhere.

Preserve her face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale blue eyes.
