Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the plank walk along the open side of a rope-walk on a river
bank in a southern city, in the middle of the morning, loose wash
suggestion — posts going down past the edge of the boards, the brown
river below and behind, a low boat lying against the posts with
finished coils of rope already stowed in it. Soft daylight off the
water, bare paper carrying the water and the lit boards.

Composition: 3:4. Waist-up, one figure. KAEL stands on the boards,
turned three-quarters, a heavy coil of pale hemp rope up on his right
shoulder. Behind him and lower, at the edge of the frame, the backs
and hands of two men passing another coil down over the edge of the
walk into the boat; no face is drawn on either of them.

THE ROPE IS HEAVY AND HE HAS TAKEN IT. His right hand is up over the
coil at his shoulder, the fingers hooked into it, and it is holding
the whole of the weight; the shoulder under it is dropped and the
neck is set. His left hand is on the coil lower down, laid flat
against it, steadying it and carrying nothing, and the back of that
hand is marked with old healing scrapes from the knuckles to the
wrist.

His face is level, the mouth closed, the jaw set but easy. His eyes
are down on the boat and the coils going into it, and not on the
viewer.

The rope is pale straw and grey-gold, dry and hairy, the strands and
the lay of them clearly drawn.

Kael wears a plain dark coat over a tunic, travel-worn and dusty, the
sleeves pushed back to the forearm. No weapon anywhere in the frame.

Preserve his face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. His eyes pale grey, the lightest value in his
face.
