Image 1: borren.png — portrait reference of the one-eyed man.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the road outside a small town's gate at first grey, before
sunrise. Loose wash suggestion — the open gate and a length of town
wall behind, wet grass off the road, cold flat light out of the sky
and no sun anywhere. Behind the rider, going away from the gate, the
front of a loaded wagon with canvas roped over it, unresolved.

Composition: 3:4. The camera stands at the shoulder of a second man,
close. IN THE NEAR FOREGROUND, large and seen from behind, THE OTHER
OFFICER's shoulder and his raised right arm, the hand up at the height
of his own head, open, palm out — a greeting already begun. No part of
his face is visible and no features are drawn on him. BEYOND HIM AND
PAST HIS RAISED HAND, at a little distance and slightly higher, THE
ONE-EYED MAN rides by on a horse at a walk, chest-up in the frame,
turned toward the camera.

THE RIDER HAS LIFTED ONE HAND OFF THE REIN, a hand's width and no
more, the fingers loose. His other hand is on the rein. He is
unhurried and entirely at ease: the brow level, the beard keeping his
mouth, the one eye steady on the man in the foreground. He is turned
so that the patch over his right eye is on his far side and his
uncovered left eye is toward the camera. Nothing about him is
performing anything.

He wears an imperial officer's coat, flat dead white with deep blue at
the collar and cuff, and a plain officer's cap with the leather patch
sitting under its brim.

The foreground man wears the same white and blue, creased and grass-
stained from a night on the ground.

The rider's face carries the finished drawing and the sharpest step
from light to dark sits at his lit edge; the raised hand in the
foreground comes next, large and soft; the gate, the wall, the wagon
and the road stay at quiet low values with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve his face, hair, beard, colouring and apparent age exactly as
shown in the reference image, and keep the leather patch over the same
eye. His is the only face in the picture.
