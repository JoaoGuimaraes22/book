Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a river bank in a southern city in the middle of the
morning, loose wash suggestion — behind them a long low building
standing out over the water on posts, open along its side, the brown
river running past it and the flat far bank beyond. Soft daylight.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up, two figures standing close and facing
each other in three-quarter view — the MAN on the left, KAEL on the
right, the boy's head lower in the frame than the man's. The man is
turned so that the patch over his right eye is on his far side and
his uncovered left eye is toward the boy. Between them and a little
behind, a heavy coil of pale hemp rope sits on the top of a mooring
post where it has been set down. Nobody else is in the frame.

Age: thirteen. The boy's eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head,
the jaw softly defined and not sharp.

KAEL IS GRINNING, AND HE IS SPEAKING WHILE HE DOES IT. Both corners
of the mouth are drawn back and up and the mouth is open on a word.
The cheeks are up hard, and they have pushed the lower lids up from
beneath, so both eyes are narrowed and the skin at their outer
corners is folded. The brow has not moved and the forehead is
smooth. His chin is up a little, because he is looking up at the man.

THE MAN GIVES NOTHING BACK YET. The beard has his mouth; the brow is
level and unmoved, and the one eye is steady and unhurried. Nothing
in his face has answered the boy's.

THE TWO ARE LOOKING DIRECTLY AT EACH OTHER, the boy's eyes up and the
man's down, the lines of sight meeting. This eye contact is the
subject of the image.

The man wears a plain dark coat over a plain collar, good cloth, no
ornament on it anywhere. Kael wears a plain dark coat over a tunic,
travel-worn and dusty, with a folded empty grain sack slung over his
back and its corner showing at his shoulder. The leather patch
belongs only to the man and the sack only to the boy.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference images. Kael's eyes pale grey, the lightest value in
his face; the man's uncovered eye light grey-brown. Do not blend
their features. Do not mix their clothing.
