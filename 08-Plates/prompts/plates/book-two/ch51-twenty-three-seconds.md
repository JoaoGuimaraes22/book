Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a small plain room at night, loose wash suggestion — plank
walls, a barred door implied behind them, the background falling into
shadow. One lamp burning low, out of frame to one side — dim warm
light, and it is the only light.

Composition: 3:4. Waist-up, two figures close together and
overlapping. AELIANA has stepped in and put both arms around VALERIA,
holding her, her face down on Valeria's shoulder and mostly hidden —
only the side of her brow, her closed eye and the line of her cheek
visible. VALERIA faces the viewer over her shoulder, her face fully
visible and the focus of the image.

Four hands total: both of Aeliana's are behind Valeria's back, the
hands shut in the cloth of her coat, fingers gripping it. Valeria's
right hand is flat between the girl's shoulder blades, the fingers a
little spread, and it is staying there. Valeria's left hand hangs at
her side, empty.

AELIANA is holding on hard, her weight leaned in, her shoulders
dropped. Long silver-white hair, loose, spilling over both their
shoulders — colourless, rendered mostly as bare paper and the palest
cool greys, no yellow and no gold in it, the lightest mass in the
picture. She wears plain worn cloth, pale and undyed, with nothing
royal anywhere on her.

VALERIA is letting it happen. Standing straight but not braced, the
weight settled. Her eyes are down toward the silver head on her
shoulder, the mouth closed, the corner gone soft. Long black hair
loose well past the shoulders. A plain dark travel coat, the sleeves
down over both wrists; the hilt of a plain sword at her hip, half
hidden by the girl's arm.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as
shown in the reference images. Valeria's eyes green. Do not blend
their features. Do not mix their clothing or their hair: the
silver-white hair belongs only to the girl holding on, and Valeria's
hair is black.
