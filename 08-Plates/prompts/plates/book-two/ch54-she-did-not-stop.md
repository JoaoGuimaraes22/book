Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of Valeria.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights. Plus dry-brush drag and flicked spatter for
the movement.

Setting: a flat of trodden ground below the lip of a fold, at dusk at
the end of autumn. Loose wash suggestion — the ground bare underfoot,
the last of the light in the sky behind the ridge, and further off
and lower the small fires of a camp with nothing resolved in them.

Composition: 3:4. Three-quarter length, one figure, and high on the
ridge behind her a second. VALERIA stands on the flat in the near
frame, side-on to the viewer, at the end of a movement. On the lip of
the fold above and behind her a boy stands watching, small, dark
against the sky, with no features drawn on him.

SHE HAS JUST FINISHED A CUT AND HAS NOT COME OUT OF IT. The weight is
down and forward on the front foot, both feet flat on the ground, the
hips turned through, the shoulders square over them. A plain wooden
stick is in her right hand, held out at the end of the cut and level
with her own shoulder, gripped as a sword is gripped. Her left hand
is empty and open, held in close at her body. There is no blade
anywhere in the picture.

Her face is in the movement and not performing it: the mouth closed,
the brows level, the eyes down the line of the stick at the place the
cut finished, and not on the boy and not on the viewer.

Long black hair, loose, well past the shoulders, thrown by the
movement. Plain dark travel clothes, worn and dust-coloured, the
sleeves down — no armour, no metal and no insignia anywhere on her.

The figure carries the finished drawing, and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits along her lit edge against the sky; the ridge, the
boy on it and the fires below stay at quiet low values with soft
wash-bled edges.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Her eyes green. Hers is the only face in the
picture.
