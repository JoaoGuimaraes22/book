Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the wide gateway of a mule yard in a southern river city, in
the last hour of the light. Mud-brick walls the colour of the ground
with the ground's own grain in them, a water trough and the backs of
two or three mules behind, and beyond the gate a narrow street with
people going along it. Loose wash suggestion, no detail anywhere. Low
daylight from the street side, reaching both faces.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures standing in the gateway, waist-up and a
little more, side by side, neither of them looking at the other.
ZAHIR stands square to the street with his arms folded across his
chest and his face out along it, seen in three-quarter profile and
drawn loosely. KAEL stands half a step behind him and nearer the
camera, turned down toward his own hands.

ZAHIR IS SAYING SOMETHING SHORT AND NOTHING IN HIM IS MOVING. Arms
folded, weight even on both feet, shoulders level, the mouth barely
open, the eyes out on the street. A solid man in middle age, thick
through the middle, a working face, grey coming into the beard. Loose
pale wound cloth, worn and clean, his head covered.

KAEL'S HANDS GO ON WORKING AND HIS HEAD DOES NOT COME UP. His right
hand is running a leather strap through a buckle; his left forearm,
bound from the knuckles to above the elbow in clean linen with the
thumb splinted between two flat pieces of wood, holds the strap
steady against his body. The eyes stay down on the buckle, the mouth
closed. Undyed coarse working wool, much mended, dust through all of
it.

Kael's face and the strap in his hands carry the finished drawing;
Zahir comes next; the gate, the trough, the mules and the street stay
at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges and no hard edge in
them.

Do not mix their clothing: the loose pale wound cloth and the covered
head belong only to Zahir, and Kael wears neither.

Preserve Kael's face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face.
