Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a long low room where soldiers sleep, in the
afternoon. The ceiling is low and close over everything. A big brick
hearth fills the end wall with a low red fire in it; six plain pallets
run along each side wall with a table between them; and high on one
wall there is a single small shuttered window with the shutter open
about the width of a hand.

Composition: 3:4. Waist-up and a little more, one figure, close, at
the left of the frame. KAEL is down on one knee at the hearth with his
back three-quarters to the room, setting split wood down onto the
pile. BEYOND HIM the room runs away: three men asleep on pallets and
four more sitting and standing about the table doing nothing in
particular, all of them at quiet low values, none of them looking at
him, and no face drawn on any of them.

Age: fourteen. The eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head, the jaw
softly defined and not sharp.

Lighting: two sources and neither of them is bright. One flat bar of
cold daylight comes through the gap in the shutter and lies across the
wall and the floor behind him. The low red fire in the hearth lights
his face and his hands from the front and below and reaches nothing
else in the room. The corners and the ceiling stay dark.

HIS RIGHT HAND IS SETTING A SPLIT LOG DOWN ON THE PILE, flat, without
a sound. His left hand rests on top of the load against his own knee,
holding it steady by weight and not by grip.

HE IS DOING EXACTLY WHAT A BOY WITH WOOD DOES AND NOTHING ELSE. His
eyes are down on the pile in front of him and not on the room and not
on the viewer, the mouth closed, the brows level, the face giving
nothing out at all.

Kael wears a plain dark coat over a tunic, travel-worn and dusty, with
wood dust on the front of it.

His face and his two hands carry the finished drawing and the sharpest
step from light to dark sits at the lit edge of his face; the men, the
table, the pallets and the low ceiling stay at quiet low values with
soft wash-bled edges and no hard edge in them.

Preserve his face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. His eyes pale grey, the lightest value in his
face. His is the only face in the picture.
