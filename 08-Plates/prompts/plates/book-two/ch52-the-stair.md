Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the bottom of an outside wooden stair in the corner of a
mule yard in a southern river city, early in the morning. Loose wash
suggestion — a plastered wall the colour of the ground behind her,
the worn treads of the stair coming down past her shoulder, and at
one edge of the frame the opening of the yard onto the street,
standing full of flat daylight with nothing resolved in it.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up, one figure, close, the head large in the
frame. THE PRINCESS stands at the foot of the stair, turned
three-quarters, her shoulders already square to the opening. Nobody
else is in the picture.

Age: fourteen. The eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head, the jaw
softly defined and not sharp.

The chin is level, the jaw set but easy, the mouth all but closed
with the lips barely parted, the brows level and unmoved.

Her eyes are on the flat daylight standing in the opening at the edge
of the frame, and not on the viewer.

A plain cloth is wound over her head and tied at the back, with no
hair showing anywhere. Coarse pale undyed working cloth, well worn
and cut for somebody bigger than her, the sleeves pushed back above
the wrist. Her right hand is up across her body holding a leather
water skin by the neck against her shoulder; her left hand is at her
side.

Her face carries the finished drawing, and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits inside the eye at the edge of the iris; the wall,
the stair and the opening stay at quiet low values with soft
wash-bled edges.

Preserve her face, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale blue eyes, and her hair entirely covered.
