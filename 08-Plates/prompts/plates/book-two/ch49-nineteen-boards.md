Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a small room off the end of a rope-walk by a river, with a
beaten earth floor, one window, and a plain table carrying weighted
piles of paper. A plain door in the side wall, standing open. Loose
wash suggestion, no detail anywhere. Low evening daylight through the
window, gone amber, and it is the only light.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures, waist-up and a little more, facing
each other across a short space, both faces clearly in frame. KAEL
stands on one side, turned three-quarters toward the camera. A WOMAN
of about thirty stands at the open door opposite him, a covered
basket already up on her hip. Nobody else is in the picture.

SHE IS SAYING SOMETHING BACK TO HIM AND SHE HAS IT EXACTLY. Mouth
open mid-word, chin level, the eyes on his face and not on the
viewer, one hand steady under the basket. An ordinary competent
woman, plainly dressed in worn southern cloth with her head covered.

KAEL IS WATCHING HER MOUTH. Head slightly forward, the eyes down a
little and on her mouth rather than her eyes, his own mouth closed.
His left arm is bound from the knuckles to above the elbow in clean
linen with the thumb splinted between two flat pieces of wood, held
in against his chest, and the linen is the whitest thing in the
picture. His right hand is open and empty at his side. Undyed coarse
working wool, worn and dusty. No weapon and no belt anywhere in the
picture.

Kael's face and the woman's mouth carry the finished drawing; the
basket comes next; the table, the paper, the door and the walls stay
at quiet low values with soft wash-bled edges and nothing resolved
in them.

Do not blend their features. Do not mix their clothing: the covered
head and the basket belong only to her.

Preserve his face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face.
