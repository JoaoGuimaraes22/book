Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of Valeria.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights. Plus dry-brush drag and flicked spatter
for the movement.

Setting: open grass on a slope at night, in the middle of a fight
that is already over in this part of it. Loose wash suggestion — a
wagon and the shapes of chests behind, burning grass off to one side
and behind, smoke, and nothing else resolved.

Composition: 3:4. Three-quarter length, all four figures grounded on
the same ground. VALERIA is in the near frame, coming forward and
across the picture with her weight already on the front foot, her
body angled and her head level and forward, past the three men and
going somewhere else. Behind her and to one side, at a little
distance, THREE MEN are down in the ground, seen from behind and in
part, with no features drawn on any of them.

THE GROUND UNDER THE THREE MEN HAS STOPPED BEING GROUND. Around each
of them the earth has gone down and sideways into a shallow bowl of
loose spilled soil with the bottom out of it, and each man is in it
to the knee, upright, stopped, and not getting out. The ground under
her own feet is flat, firm and undisturbed.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
Her right hand holds one plain straight sword and nothing else.
Her left hand holds a second plain straight sword and nothing else.
Two hands, two blades, nothing else in either of them, and no other
blade, blade tip or hilt anywhere in the frame.

Each blade is plain, straight and single-handed, about the length of
her own arm, with a plain crossguard and no ornament of any kind.

SHE IS ALREADY AT THE NEXT THING. Her eyes are level and fixed on
something out of the frame ahead of her, the brows low and straight,
the jaw set, the mouth closed. Everything in the face is set hard and
none of it is moving. She is not looking at the three men and her
head is not turned back toward them.

She wears plain dark travel clothing, close-fitting and worn, with
her long black hair tied back off her face. No armour, no vambraces,
no gorget, no insignia and no metal on her anywhere.

Lighting: burning grass off to one side and behind, low and orange,
is the only light in the picture. It edges her and the three men
from behind and lights the near side of her face and both blades;
the smoke above and the ground beyond go into black.

Her face carries the finished drawing and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits at her eye; the two blades come next; the three
men, the spilled ground, the wagon and the smoke stay at quiet low
values.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Her eyes emerald green, the most saturated
point in the picture. Hers is the only face in the picture.
