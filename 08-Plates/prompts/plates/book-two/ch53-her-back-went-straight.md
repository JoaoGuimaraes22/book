Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a camp at night in a fold of open ground. Loose wash
suggestion — behind her the circle of people sitting round the fire
are unlit shapes with no faces drawn on any of them, and beyond them
nothing at all.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up, one figure, close. THE PRINCESS sits on
the ground beside the fire, seen three-quarters from behind and to
one side so that the whole line of her back and shoulders is in
frame, her head turned forward and a little down toward the fire.

Age: fourteen. The eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head, the jaw
softly defined and not sharp.

Lighting: a low fire in front of her and below the bottom edge of the
frame is the only light in the picture. It comes up from beneath onto
the underside of her jaw, her throat and the front of her face, and
leaves the back of her head, her shoulders and everything behind her
dark. The warm light lands on her face and her hands only.

HER BACK HAS GONE STRAIGHT AND IT IS STAYING THERE. The spine is
vertical, the shoulders carried back and down, the neck long, the
chin level — a body that has been taught to sit like this and has
just remembered how. Nothing else about her has moved.

Her face has not answered her back. The mouth is closed and still,
the brows level and unmoved, and her eyes are steady on the fire
below the frame and not on the viewer.

A plain cloth is wound over her head and tied, with no hair showing
anywhere. Coarse pale undyed working cloth, well worn, the sleeves
pulled down over both wrists to the base of the hand. Her hands are
in her lap, one lying in the other, still.

Her face and the front of her throat carry the finished drawing, and
the sharpest step from light to dark sits inside the eye at the edge
of the iris; the seated shapes behind her stay at quiet low values
with soft wash-bled edges and no hard edge in them. The picture falls
away to bare unpainted paper at its edges rather than being painted
out to the border.

Preserve her face, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale blue eyes, and her hair entirely covered.
