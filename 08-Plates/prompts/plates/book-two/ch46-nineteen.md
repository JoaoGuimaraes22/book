Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a stony track along the bottom of a narrow fold in high
country, fast water on one side and a loose steep rise going up on
the other with dark needle trees along the top of it. Loose wash
suggestion, no detail anywhere. Flat cold winter daylight, no sun in
frame.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up, one figure, close, the head large in the
frame and her folded hands just inside the bottom edge. THE PRINCESS
has come down off the rise and stopped on the track, turned
three-quarters toward the camera. Nobody else is in the picture, and
the track behind her holds nothing but stones, grass and water.

SHE HAS JUST SAID ONE NUMBER AND SHE IS CERTAIN OF IT. Chin level,
shoulders square, standing the way she was taught to stand.

SHE IS WHITE AND ONE CORNER OF HER MOUTH IS NOT HOLDING. No colour in
the face at all — the palest thing in the picture after her hair. The
mouth closed, one corner drawn tight and slightly down, the rest of
the face doing nothing whatever. The eyes wide and steady and level
on somebody just off camera, not on the viewer. Everything in the
face is holding and one small part of it has stopped.

HER TWO HANDS ARE FOLDED IN FRONT OF HER AND THEY ARE COMPLETELY
STILL. Fingers laced, held at her waist, no grip in them and no
movement anywhere — the stillest thing in the picture. Her wrists are
bare and the skin where iron used to be is paler than the rest of her
arm.

A heavy grey wool cloak is folded over her left forearm, carried and
not worn, coarse and dusty and dragging the arm down a little with
its weight.

Silver-white hair, colourless, pulled back and knotted plainly out of
the way, dulled with dust and with needle litter caught in it. A
coarse village dress far too big for her, borrowed, dull oatmeal,
dirty at the hem, the sleeves pushed back off her forearms.

Her face and her folded hands carry the finished drawing, and the
sharpest step from light to dark sits inside the eye at the edge of
the iris; the track, the rise, the trees and the water stay at quiet
middle values with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. Pale blue eyes.
