Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a stone water trough in a lane at the edge of a small town
in winter, in flat cold daylight with no sun in it. Loose wash
suggestion — a board fence and the end of a long shed behind them,
a gate onto the lane, and beyond that the lane running out between
fences with nothing resolved in it at all. The air is cold enough
that their breath shows.

Composition: 3:4. Three-quarter length, three figures standing along
the trough, turned slightly toward each other but not gathered.
THE PRINCESS on the left, KAEL in the middle, VALERIA on the right.
At the far left edge of the frame, half out of it, the back and
shoulder of a WOMAN standing at the trough with her head turned away
and no features drawn on her.

Age: the boy fourteen, the two girls fifteen. Eyes at the vertical
midpoint of the head, jaws softly defined and not sharp, adolescent
and not grown.

THE PRINCESS IS TALKING TO THE WOMAN AND ENJOYING IT. Turned toward
her, mouth open mid-word, chin up, one hand lifted a little from the
handle of a bucket in a small easy gesture. Her eyes are on the
woman. She has been out of doors for months: brown, with freckles
over the nose.

KAEL HAS NOTHING TO SAY AND NOWHERE TO PUT HIS FACE. He stands square
between the two of them, still, not leaning and not turned toward
either. A wooden bucket hangs from his right hand and his left hand
hangs open and empty. The mouth is closed, the brows level. His eyes
are out along the lane at the gate at the end of it, and not on the
princess, not on the woman and not on the viewer.

VALERIA IS FILLING A BUCKET AND SAYING NOTHING. Bent a little at the
waist, both hands on a bucket at the lip of the trough, her eyes down
on the water going into it, her mouth closed. A girl who carries
water and never speaks.

THE PRINCESS wears coarse pale undyed working cloth, the sleeves down
over both wrists to the base of the hand, and a plain cloth wound
over her head and tied with no hair showing anywhere. VALERIA wears
plain dark travel clothes, worn and dust-coloured, her long black
hair loose and uncovered, with no armour, no metal and no insignia
anywhere on her. KAEL wears a plain dark coat over a tunic,
travel-worn. The covered head belongs only to the princess and the
loose black hair only to Valeria.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in their own reference image. Kael's eyes pale grey, the princess's
pale blue, Valeria's green. Do not blend their features. Do not mix
their clothing. The princess's hair is entirely covered.
