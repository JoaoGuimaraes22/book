Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the edge of a shallow stream at first grey, at the end of
autumn — cold flat light out of the sky before sunrise and no other
light anywhere. Loose wash suggestion — running water and wet stones
in the near ground, and a long way behind and above him a camp making
itself for the day, one small fire and a pot, nothing in it resolved.

Composition: 3:4. Waist-up, one figure, close. KAEL kneels at the
water, his body turned three-quarters toward the viewer and his head
down over his own hands, with both hands and the surface of the
stream inside the bottom of the frame.

Age: thirteen. The eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head, the jaw
softly defined and not sharp.

BOTH HANDS ARE JUST OUT OF THE WATER AND HE IS LOOKING AT THEM. They
are held up in front of him clear of the surface, open, the fingers
apart, turned so that the backs of them are toward his face and still
visible to the viewer. Water is running off them. They are clean, and
the skin is wet and cold-reddened at the knuckles. Along the back of
the left hand one thin closed line runs from the knuckles toward the
wrist, dark red and not wet.

His eyes move over his own hands the way they move over a thing being
counted. The head is down, the brows level and unmoved, the mouth
closed. Nothing else about him is doing anything.

Kael wears a plain dark coat over a tunic, travel-worn, the sleeves
pushed back clear of both forearms and wet at the cuff.

His hands carry the finished drawing, and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits at the wet edge of the knuckles; his face comes
next; the water, the stones and the camp behind him stay at quiet
low values with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve his face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image. His eyes pale grey, the lightest value in his
face.
