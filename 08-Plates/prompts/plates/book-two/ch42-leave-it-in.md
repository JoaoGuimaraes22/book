Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the bottom of a wooded fold, late morning. Dark needle trees
standing close with their feet in wet ground, and a stream running
cold and fast and brown over stones. Loose wash suggestion behind
them, the wood going into shadow, no detail anywhere. The only light
is what comes down through the trees onto the water.

Composition: 3:4. Close and low, at the edge of the water, the two of
them side by side and both down on their knees in the wet. KAEL
nearest, turned three-quarters toward the camera, his left arm
straight down into the stream to above the wrist so that the hand
itself is under the moving water and not visible. VALERIA is over him
at his shoulder, both her hands closed round that forearm, holding it
down. Nobody else is in the picture.

SHE IS HOLDING IT UNDER AND HE IS LETTING HER. Nothing is being
fought over. His weight is down and settled, his shoulders let go,
his eyes shut and his head dropped forward; her weight is over the
arm and steady.

VALERIA'S TWO HANDS ARE THE CENTRE OF THE PICTURE. Both of them are
on his forearm together, because they have to be: a plain closed ring
of dark metal on each of her wrists with no more chain between them
than the width of her own hand. Her face is close over his and level
and giving nothing away, her eyes on the water and not on him. Long
black hair, dark clothing, the left side of her face smooth and
shining where it healed.

KAEL is grey and wet through and much too still. Dark red gone brown
dried down one side of his face and into the collar of his shirt, and
none of it is his. Undyed coarse working wool, much mended, a dark
coat too big for him hanging off one shoulder, a plain closed ring of
dark metal on his right wrist with a short run of links off it lying
in the wet.

The water is the brightest thing in the frame and carries bare
unpainted paper where the light is on it; her hands on his forearm
hold the sharpest step from light to dark. The wood behind them is
one soft dark mass with no hard edge in it.

Do not blend their features and do not mix their clothing.

Preserve each face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference images. His eyes pale grey, hers green.
