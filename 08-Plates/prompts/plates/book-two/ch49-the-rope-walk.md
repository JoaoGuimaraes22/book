Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.

Setting: the inside of a rope-walk on a river bank in a southern
city, in the afternoon. It is one building and it is far longer than
it is wide: a low roof on posts, a bare earth floor with no boards in
it, a solid wall down one side, and down the other side no wall at
all — only the posts, with the brown river and the flat far bank
showing between them. Bundles of raw hemp, coils of finished rope,
and a large wooden wheel on a frame at the far end.

Composition: 3:4. The camera stands at the near end of the building
on the floor, looking straight down its whole length.

NEAREST, backs to the viewer and dark against the length: TWO
CHILDREN, standing still, small in the frame, their heads bare and
one of them holding a loose empty grain sack down at his side. No
faces anywhere on them.

BEYOND THEM the building runs away, narrowing hard with distance.
THREE MEN ARE WALKING BACKWARDS down the length of it, spaced well
apart, each of them facing the far end of the building and stepping
backwards toward the viewer, paying a strand of hemp out of a bundle
at his waist so that his line runs away from him up the floor toward
the wheel. A FOURTH man stands at the wheel at the far end with a
hand on it. No face is drawn on any of them.

FURTHEST, the far end of the building goes into shadow and stops
being lit, and the wheel and the last man are the last things visible
in it.

The light comes in sideways between the posts off the water, laying
bright bands across the floor and leaving the solid wall dark. Bare
paper carries the water and the lit bands of floor.

The three long lines of hemp running away up the floor carry the
sharpest step from light to dark and the most finished ink drawing in
the picture; the two children and the posts come next; the roof, the
wall, the bundles and the far end stay at quiet middle values with
soft wash-bled edges.
