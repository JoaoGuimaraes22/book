Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a small upper room at night, loose wash suggestion — plank
walls, a board floor, and a plain plank door directly behind the man,
shut. Lamplight from one side, out of frame, is the only light; it
leaves the corners and the shut door dark, and the warmth lands on the
two faces and her hands.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures, full length, at the door — a grown man
with his back nearly against the shut door, and a girl much smaller
than him facing him, the top of her head at the height of his
shoulder. The boards of the floor are in frame at the bottom.

THE MAN IS OFF THE FLOOR. Both his boots hang clear of the boards,
and a narrow strip of dark shows between his boot soles and the
floor, about the depth of one board's edge — a small gap, clearly
there.

VALERIA holds him there. Her right hand is a fist closed in the front
of his coat at the breastbone and holds nothing else. Her left hand
is closed round his right wrist and holds nothing else. Plain iron
bands sit on both of her wrists with a short chain hanging slack
between them. Her arms are not straining and her body is not braced —
she is standing normally, weight even, and nothing about the pose
says effort. Nothing comes off her: no glow, no dust, nothing lifted
into the air anywhere in the picture.

Her face is turned up to his. The chin is level, the eyes steady on
his face, the jaw set but easy. Long black hair loose well past the
shoulders. Plain dark road clothing, coarse and travel-worn.

THE MAN has stopped moving entirely. His right wrist is caught in her
hand. His left arm is half lifted with the hand open, doing nothing.
There is a knife in his belt and both his hands are nowhere near it.
His eyes are down on her face, wide and fixed, the brows up, the
mouth a little open with nothing coming out of it. A big man in plain
worn clothing, dark-eyed.

Nobody else is in the frame.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
