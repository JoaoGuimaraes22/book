Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the open river side of a long timber building at dusk. Posts
hold the roof up with no wall between them, and past the posts the
river has gone the same colour as the sky. Loose wash suggestion, the
far end of the building falling into shadow. The last of the daylight
comes in low off the water, cool and grey, and it is the only light.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures, waist-up, standing close and turned
toward each other, both heads bowed toward the same point between
them: his right wrist.

VALERIA is fitting an iron band onto KAEL's right wrist. The band is
plain new iron, closed on the wrist, with bright file marks along its
edges. A short chain hangs from it, and the band's twin hangs open at
the end of the chain, on nobody yet. Her left hand is under his
forearm, steadying it, and holds nothing else. Her right thumb rests
on the head of the small pin that locks the band, pressed home, and it
has stopped there.

KAEL's right arm is out between them, the sleeve pushed back clear of
the band. His left hand hangs at his side with the thumb in a small
splint, out of the work. He is looking down at the band on his own
wrist. The mouth closed on the end of something said, the brows level
— not braced, just waiting.

VALERIA's eyes are down on the pin under her thumb, not on his face
and not on the viewer. Her face is level, the jaw set but easy.

Both wear plain dark road clothing, coarse and travel-worn — his a
plain dark coat over a tunic, hers a coarse dark tunic over trousers,
the sleeves down over both wrists. She wears no skirt and no dress. Her long black hair is loose well past the shoulders. She is
lean and athletic, a working build; he is lean and narrow through the
shoulders. No weapon is visible anywhere in the frame, and nobody else
is in it.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference images. His eyes pale grey, hers green. Do not blend
their features. Do not mix their clothing.
