Image 1: portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the floor of a dark board box at night, straw under him and
nothing else visible. Out of frame behind and above, a slot with
three bars in it, and a fire standing thirty feet outside the box
sends one narrow bar of low light in through the bars. That bar is
the only light in the picture and it is weak.

Composition: 3:4. A close-up of his face, the head large in the
frame, lying on its side down in the straw with the cheek flat on it
and turned three-quarters up toward the viewer. Nothing else is in
the picture: no hands, no arms, nothing below the jaw, no objects,
and nothing beyond the straw immediately around his head.

HE IS HOLDING IT DOWN AND NO SOUND IS COMING OUT OF HIM. His mouth
is shut on a fistful of straw, the jaw locked with the muscle
standing at the corner and the lips drawn back off the teeth where
the straw goes in. The eyes are open and wide and fixed on nothing,
the brows driven together and up in the middle, the tendons standing
in the near side of the neck, and the whole face wet through. One
tear track runs down the near cheek and cuts a clean line in the
dirt on it.

His hair is dark, short, cut unevenly, wet at the temple and full of
straw. The straw under his cheek is crushed flat and dark where the
sweat has gone into it.

Lighting: the box is dark. The one weak bar off the slot crosses the
straw and the near edge of his face and leaves the rest in low quiet
dark. The source is far outside and behind, so nothing here is
brightly lit and there is no rim light on him anywhere. Cool dark
everywhere the bar does not reach.

Preserve him exactly as shown in his reference image — face, hair,
colouring. Pale grey eyes, the lightest value in his face.
