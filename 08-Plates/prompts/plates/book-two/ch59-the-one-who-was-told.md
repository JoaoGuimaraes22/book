Image 1: tirzah.png — portrait reference of the woman with the basket.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the driving board of a moving wagon at night, at the top of
a track above a burning fold. Loose wash suggestion — the board, a
rope and the edge of roped canvas near, and at the very edge of the
frame the shoulder and back of the driver beside her, dark and with
no features on him.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up, one figure, the camera close and at her
own level. THE WOMAN is twisted round on the board, looking back
over her own shoulder and down, away from the direction the wagon is
going. Her right hand is up at her mouth with the fingers pressed
flat across it. Her left hand is on the board.

BELOW AND BEHIND HER, a long way down and a long way off, the floor
of a fold is burning — low orange fire spread out across dark ground
and thick smoke standing off it. Standing in the middle of it, very
small, is one man alone with a sword in his hand, a dark upright
shape against the fire with no features drawn on him anywhere.

SHE HAS JUST BEEN TOLD SOMETHING AND SHE IS NOT GOING TO MAKE A
SOUND. Her eyes are wide and unblinking and fixed on the small
standing figure below her; the brow is drawn and the whole face is
held perfectly still, with nothing in it collapsing and nothing in
it straining. Her own hand is across her mouth.

She wears coarse undyed cloth with her head covered, nothing dyed on
her anywhere and no ornament on her.

Lighting: the burning fold is far below and behind her and it is the
only light in the picture. Because it is far, it touches very
little: an orange edge along her jaw, her shoulder and the back of
the hand at her mouth, and nothing else. The night keeps the rest of
her, and the board, the canvas and the man beside her are almost
black. Cool night air above, with the sky pale and open at the top of
the frame.

Her face carries the finished drawing and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits at the lit edge of it; the small standing figure
below is the only thing resolved in the fold; everything between
them is smoke and dark.

Preserve her face, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown in
the reference image. Her dark brown eyes are the most finished
drawing in her face. Hers is the only face in the picture.

Her head is covered: a coarse undyed cloth wound over her head and
once round the throat and knotted at one side, with no hair showing
anywhere.
