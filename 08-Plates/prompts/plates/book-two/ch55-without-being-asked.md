Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than fills.

Setting: inside a long open-sided shed in a carrier's yard in early
spring. Loose wash suggestion — trodden earth underfoot, a plank wall
behind, and cool daylight coming in low and sideways through the open
side, with nothing resolved anywhere beyond arm's length.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is brought down the length of the
subject's own forearms, close in behind the wrists, looking down the
arms at what the hands are doing, so that the reader is inside the
body rather than in front of it. No face and no other person anywhere
in the picture.

A HEAVY COIL OF PALE HEMP ROPE LIES ACROSS THE MIDDLE OF THE FRAME,
dry and hairy, the lay of the strands clearly drawn.

THE LEFT HAND IS THE SUBJECT AND IT IS HOLDING THE ROPE. The fingers
have come round the rope and closed on it, the thumb across, the
grip complete and not loose. It is a boy's hand, lean and hard and
weathered, and it has been broken and mended: the outside of it below
the little finger sits lower and out of line with the rest, the knuckles
are thickened, and one thin closed line runs along the back of it from
the knuckles toward the wrist. Above it the forearm is scarred from
wrist to elbow, the skin there smooth and lighter than the skin
around it. The hand is doing this correctly and easily.

THE RIGHT HAND HAS STOPPED. It rests on the rope a little further
along, open, the fingers slack, not gripping and not working — the
hand of somebody who has broken off in the middle of a job to look
at something.

The left hand carries the sharpest step from light to dark and the
only fully finished ink drawing in the picture; the rope comes next;
the shed, the wall and the ground stay at quiet middle values with
soft wash-bled edges and no hard edge anywhere in them.
