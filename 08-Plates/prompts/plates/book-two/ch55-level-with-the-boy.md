Image 1: borren.png — portrait reference of the one-eyed man.
Image 2: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a camp at night with the fire gone down to almost nothing.
Loose wash suggestion — no other people anywhere, and behind them
only dark, with the edge of a canvas shelter implied a long way back
and unresolved.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up, two figures close and facing each other
in three-quarter view, their two faces at the same height in the
frame and near enough to talk quietly. THE MAN is on the left, down
on his heels in a crouch with his forearms across his knees and his
hands loose. KAEL is on the right, sitting on the ground. The man is
turned so that the patch over his right eye is on his far side and
his uncovered left eye is toward the boy.

Age: fourteen. The boy's eyes at the vertical midpoint of the head,
the jaw softly defined and not sharp.

THE MAN HAS COME DOWN TO THE BOY'S HEIGHT AND HE HAS NEVER DONE IT
BEFORE. Nothing about the crouch is unsteady: the weight is settled,
the back straight, the head level. The beard has his mouth, so the
whole of the face is in the brow and the one eye — the brow level and
unmoved, the eye steady and unhurried and giving nothing out.

KAEL is sitting still with his hands on the ground either side of
him. The mouth closed, the brows level, the face taking something in
and not answering it yet.

THE TWO ARE LOOKING DIRECTLY AT EACH OTHER, level, neither one higher
than the other. This eye contact is the subject of the image.

Lighting: the fire, low and almost out, in front of them and below
the bottom edge of the frame, is the only light in the picture. It
comes up faintly from beneath onto the undersides of both faces and
their hands, and leaves the tops of their heads, their backs and
everything behind them dark.

The man wears a plain dark coat over a plain collar, good cloth, no
ornament on it anywhere. Kael wears a plain dark coat over a tunic,
travel-worn. The leather patch belongs only to the man.

The two faces carry the finished drawing and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits at the lit edge of each; everything behind them
stays at quiet low values with no hard edge in it.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference images. Kael's eyes pale grey; the man's uncovered
eye light grey-brown. Do not blend their features.
