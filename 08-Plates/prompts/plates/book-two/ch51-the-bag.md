Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the angle of two walls in a narrow street at deep dusk,
loose wash suggestion — the last light of the sky above the walls,
the street itself in shadow, no lamp anywhere. Cool ambient light
coming down from the sky.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures, chest-up, standing close and facing
each other in three-quarter view — the man on the left, KAEL on the
right. The man is turned so that the patch over his right eye is on
his far side and his uncovered left eye is toward the boy and toward
the viewer.

Between them, at chest height, a small cloth bag sits in KAEL's two
open hands. It is small, and the hands under it have taken its
weight — they sit a little lower than a light thing would ask. THE
MAN's right hand has just come away from it and is still above it,
open, palm down. His left hand is at his side.

THE MAN's one eye is on the boy's face, steady. The beard has his
mouth; the brow is level; the face gives nothing out. He wears a
plain dark coat.

KAEL is looking down at the bag in his hands, not at the man and not
at the viewer. The mouth closed, the brows level — not braced, just
listening. He wears a plain dark coat over a tunic, travel-worn.

Preserve each face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as
shown in the reference images. Kael's eyes pale grey; the man's
uncovered eye light grey-brown. Do not blend their features. Do not
mix their clothing. The patch belongs only to the man.
