Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael. He is one figure among many here and his face is small in the frame.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a rutted earth road coming down a hill on a bright cold
morning, as loose wash suggestion — dry-stone walls and a ditch along
one side, carts stopped on the verges, the land falling away below
toward open water. Soft daylight from one side.

Composition: 3:4. THE CAMERA IS STANDING IN THE CROWD AT THE SIDE OF
THE ROAD, at a grown person's eye height, side on, so that the road
runs across the picture from left to right and not toward the viewer.
Nearest the camera, at the left and right edges and cut off by them,
the backs and shoulders of two or three bystanders who have stopped
and turned to watch, dark and unresolved, their faces away or out of
frame. Between them, in the middle distance, THE OPEN ROAD, empty for
one clear lane, and KAEL walking through it from left to right, seen
in three-quarter view from behind his shoulder. He is small: his
whole figure stands about a third of the height of the frame, so his
head is a small thing in it and his face is drawn but is not the
largest thing in the picture. Beyond him on the far verge, A MAN with
a coil of rope over his shoulder has stepped back into the ruts and
holds his hat in both hands in front of him, his head turned to
follow the boy; no features are drawn on him. Behind that, a stopped
cart with a whitewashed bed sheet roped along its rail, seen at an
angle, no word on it legible; and along both verges, people standing
in ones and twos, all of them turned toward the road, drawn as
summary shapes with no faces. At the top of the frame the road goes
on down the hill, and open water beyond it.

NOBODY IN THIS PICTURE IS LOOKING AT THE VIEWER, AND THE BOY LEAST OF
ALL. His face is level and in profile, turned along the road ahead of
him; the camera is behind his shoulder and to the side, and no part
of his face is turned toward it.

THE SUBJECT IS THE SHAPE OF THE GROUND: one lane of empty road with a
moving figure in it, closed on both sides by stopped bodies, every
one of them turned his way. The road has opened in front of him and
closed up behind him.

KAEL — walking on, not turning round and not slowing, the mouth
closed and the jaw set. He carries a wrapped bundle across his back
on a cord; both hands are empty and easy at his sides and no weapon
is anywhere in the picture. Undyed coarse working wool in dull
oatmeal and dead olive, much mended, no ornament, badge, colour or
metal on him anywhere, and his left arm bound above the elbow in four
turns of plain cloth.

THE MAN'S HAT IS THE SECOND THING THE EYE SHOULD FIND. It is off his
head and held in both hands at waist height, an ordinary working hat
of dull felt, and it is the only bare-headed man in the picture.

The boy and the man carry the hardest edges and the most finished
drawing; the bystanders at the frame edges are the softest thing in
the picture, and the carts, the sheet, the walls and the far verges
all stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges.
