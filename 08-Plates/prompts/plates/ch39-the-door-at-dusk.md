Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of Aeliana.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a small wooden box on a cart at dusk, with
its one door standing open at the far end. Old boards, straw on the
floor, the corners in shadow, a lantern on a short hook near the
roof. Beyond the open door, low red evening sky over dim ground.

Composition: tall upright frame, taller than it is wide. The camera
is inside the box, down near the straw, looking along its length at
the open door.

At the very bottom of the frame, near straw in shadow, with the back
of a boy's head and one shoulder cut off by the bottom edge, dark
and unresolved, lying down and turned away from the camera. Above
that the floor of the box, with the last of the daylight coming in
low and red through the doorway and lying along the straw in one
long bar that reaches almost to him. Above that, to the right
against the side wall, AELIANA seated upright and facing the door,
taking the red light full on her face and her hair; and to the left
against the far wall a second seated figure in shadow, turned toward
the door, her face away from the camera and unresolved. Then, at the
top, THE MAN filling the open doorway.

THE MAN IS A SHAPE AGAINST THE LIGHT. Not tall, and square through
the body — a man who was strong once and has kept the shape of it.
He stands in the middle of the doorway with the red sky behind him,
drawn as one solid dark mass with no features on him anywhere and
nothing of the face resolved. His clothing reads only as the loose
pale wound cloth of the south catching the last light along its
edges, with one narrow band of imperial blue at the hem. He is
still, and both his hands are empty and down.

AELIANA — she has watched the slot in that door all day and has not
moved, so the door opens onto a girl already facing it. Sitting
upright, back straight, shoulders square, her chin where it was.
Long silver-white hair down her back, colourless, never blonde, no
yellow or gold in it, salt still in it, catching the red light and
staying the lightest mass in the picture. A strip of cloth that was
white once passes across her mouth and is knotted behind her head. A
plain iron band sits round each wrist and her hands rest together in
her lap, the palms marked and mended. What is left of a court gown:
the dark blue gone grey and salt bleached, the gold at the shoulders
dulled and dirty. Her expression is in her eyes and her brows, since
the cloth has her mouth: steady, wide open, giving nothing away. Not
pleading.

HE IS LOOKING AT HER AND SHE IS LOOKING BACK AT HIM. The line of
sight between the dark shape in the doorway and the girl against the
wall runs the length of the box, and it is the subject of the image.

Lighting: the low red daylight through the open door is almost all
of it, coming in level and warm along the floor, lighting her face
and her hair and the near edges of the straw and leaving everything
it does not reach to go dark. The lantern is small and weak against
it and puts one thin warm edge on the boards near the roof.

Preserve her face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown
in the reference image.
