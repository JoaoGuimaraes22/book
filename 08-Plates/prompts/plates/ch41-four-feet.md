Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: underneath a cart at first light, frost laid white and even
over hard dust, the boards close overhead and a wheel to either side.
Out past them, flat open ground going away to low hills with the
light just arriving on them. Flat cold daylight, no sun and no
hard-edged shadow.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is on the ground under the cart, level
with the frost, looking out between the wheels. THREE FACES fill the
near half of the frame, lying flat side by side in the dark under the
boards, all of them turned out toward the light. Beyond them, out in
it, a pair of boots and the lower legs of a man standing with his
back against the boards — nothing above his knee is in the frame, and
he is four feet away.

NOBODY UNDER THE CART IS MOVING. Not braced, not crouched, not about
to go: three people lying still on frozen ground with their weight
already down and their breath held.

KAEL is nearest, on his side, his face turned out and his left arm
folded under him and out of sight. Dark red gone brown down one side
of his face and into the neck of his shirt, and none of it is his.
Undyed coarse working wool, much mended.

THE PRINCESS is in the middle, her feet a hand's width from the boots
beyond her, her eyes open and fixed and no sound coming out of her. A
strip of pale cloth across the lower half of her face, knotted
behind. Silver-white hair, matted. What is left of a fine gown, under
a dead man's coat far too big for her.

VALERIA is at the far side against the boards, a knife lying flat
along her forearm, her hands together with a hand's width of chain
between the wrists, her eyes on the boots and nothing else in her
moving. Long black hair, dark clothing, and the left side of her face
smooth and shining where it healed.

Do not blend their features and do not mix their clothing: the coat
belongs only to the princess, the mended wool only to Kael, the knife
only to Valeria.

The three faces sit in low quiet dark and the frost and the standing
legs beyond them are bright. The sharpest step from light to dark in
the frame lies along the edge of the cart's shadow, right in front of
their faces.

Preserve each face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference images.
