Image 1: aeliana-15.png — portrait reference of the princess.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the inside of a small bare room over a mule yard in a
southern river city, in the late afternoon. Bare plastered walls the
colour of the ground, a plank floor, nothing else in the room at all.
One small square opening in the thick wall with no glass in it and a
fixed wooden grille across it. Loose wash suggestion, no detail
anywhere.

Composition: 3:4. Chest-up and a little more, one figure, close, the
head large in the frame and her hands just inside the bottom edge.
THE PRINCESS sits on the floor with her back to the wall, a little to
one side of the opening, turned three-quarters toward the camera.
The opening is beside her at the edge of the frame.

Lighting: the daylight coming in through the opening is the only
light in the picture. It falls across her face from the side. The
room behind her goes down into shadow with nothing resolved in it.

SHE IS TRYING TO KEEP HER FACE STRAIGHT AND SHE IS NOT MANAGING IT.
Chin a little up, the mouth closed and pressed with both corners
going, the eyes bright and steady on somebody standing just off
camera and not on the viewer. Nothing else in the face is moving.

HER TWO HANDS LIE FLAT ON HER KNEES. Palms down, fingers together,
still.

A plain cloth is tied over her head with no hair showing anywhere. A
coarse village dress far too big for her, borrowed, dull oatmeal,
worn through at one shoulder.

Her face carries the finished drawing and the sharpest step from
light to dark sits inside the eye at the edge of the iris; her hands
come next; the wall, the grille and the room stay at quiet low values
with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve her face, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale blue eyes, and her hair entirely covered.
