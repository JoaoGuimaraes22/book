Image 1: elarine-14.png — portrait reference of the small girl with
bound dark hair.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the corner of a stone courtyard in the evening, as loose wash
suggestion — the round stone head of a cistern close behind them, tables
carried out into the yard further off, a high wall beyond. The last of
the sun is on the top courses of that wall and nowhere else: a narrow
band of warm orange along the top of the stone, with the whole of the
yard below it in cool blue shade. There is no other light in the
picture.

Composition: two figures, close together and near the viewer, the bottom
edge of the picture crossing both at the lower chest. The woman is the
nearer of the two, at the left, seen from behind and to one side, her
head and shoulder cut by the left edge of the frame and her face turned
away from the viewer so that no part of it is drawn. The girl faces her,
turned three-quarters toward the viewer, her face fully visible and
filling the upper right of the frame.

THE WOMAN'S RIGHT HAND IS FLAT AGAINST THE SIDE OF THE GIRL'S HEAD, laid
over the temple and the ear with the fingers spread and still, the wrist
relaxed, resting there and not gripping. Her other hand and arm are out
of the frame. The girl's own hands are down at her sides and empty.
Nothing else is in any hand and there is no object anywhere between
them.

Nothing is happening and neither of them is speaking.

THE GIRL IS LOOKING UP AT THE WOMAN'S FACE: the lids fully raised, the
eyes very dark and steady on her, the mouth closed and even, the jaw
tight, the brows level. She is not looking at the viewer. Nothing in her
face is crying.

Her ink-dark hair is bound close and severe against the head with
nothing loose anywhere. A reddened burn runs along the line of her jaw
on the near side, dry and unbound, with no dressing and no cloth on it.

Her clothing is plain, close-cut and buttoned high to the throat, very
well made, in a flat near-black with no ornament of any kind on it. The
woman wears the same quality in deep blue — a long-sleeved gown closed
high, undecorated. Do not mix their clothing: the deep blue belongs only
to the woman and the near-black only to the girl.

The girl's face and the hand on her head are the most finished things in
the picture and carry the sharpest step from light to dark; the cistern
head, the tables, the yard and the wall stay at quiet middle values with
soft edges.

Preserve the girl's face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as
shown in the reference image. The woman is older, small and finely built
like her, with the same ink-dark hair bound close, and her face is not
drawn.
