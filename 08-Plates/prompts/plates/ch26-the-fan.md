Image 1: elarine-14.png — portrait reference of the girl.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the floor of a great arena on a bright morning, as loose wash
suggestion — pale raked sand underfoot, and behind her the banked
seating rising as a wash of small shapes with no face resolved
anywhere in it. Flat cold daylight.

Composition: one figure, side-on to the viewer, standing in the left
of the frame, the bottom edge of the picture crossing her at the hip.
Her left arm is extended out ahead of her at waist height, the hand
open, palm down and the fingers spread. Her right hand holds a plain
longsword with the point low and angled down and back, away from her
body.

THE FIRE COMING OUT OF HER OPEN LEFT HAND IS THE SUBJECT OF THE IMAGE:
a flat fan of flame leaving the palm and spreading away across the
frame, low and level, travelling just above the sand. It leaves her
hand narrow, is already wider than her own shoulders by the time it
reaches the far edge of the picture, and goes on widening out of
frame. It is a thin sheet seen nearly edge-on, its leading edge ragged
and broken into separate tongues — not a ball, not a column and not a
cloud.

The fire is the only saturated colour anywhere in the picture, deep
orange going to pale yellow where the sheet is thinnest, with the
sand, the seating and her clothing in muted greys and dull browns
around it. It is also the only warm light: it throws warmth upward
onto the underside of her forearm, her jaw and her cheek, and
everything turned away from it stays cold and flat.

SHE IS LOOKING ALONG THE FIRE, out past the edge of the frame in the
direction it is travelling, and not at the viewer.

HER FACE IS AT WORK AND GIVES NOTHING AWAY: the lids fully raised and
the eyes steady along the line of the fan, the brows unmoved, the
mouth closed and soft. Not straining and not fierce.

She is small and finely built, small for her age, with a narrow frame
and a small face. Her hair is ink-dark, a flat neutral black, and it
is BOUND: gathered close against the head in a tight severe knot at
the back with nothing out of it, and none of it falling loose past her
shoulders. Her clothing is finely made — plain dark cloth of very good
quality, close-cut and buttoned high to the throat, with no ornament
of any kind — and it is whole and unmarked, with no tear and no stain
anywhere on it.

Nobody else is in the picture.

Preserve her exactly as shown in the reference image — face, hair,
colouring, apparent age. Her eyes are very dark brown.
