Setting: the floor of a great arena on a bright morning, as loose wash
suggestion — pale raked sand underfoot, and behind her the banked
seating rising as a wash of small shapes with no face resolved
anywhere in it. Flat cold daylight.

Composition: one figure facing the viewer, standing a little to one
side of centre, the bottom edge of the picture crossing her at the
hip. Her left arm is extended straight out toward the viewer at waist
height, foreshortened, the hand open with the palm turned down and the
fingers spread, so that the open hand is the nearest part of her. The
arm is clear of her body with nothing crossing or overlapping it along
its whole length. Her right hand holds a plain longsword with the
point low and angled down and back, away from her body.

THE FIRE IS COMING OFF HER OPEN LEFT HAND STRAIGHT AT THE VIEWER AND
IT IS THE SUBJECT OF THE IMAGE: a flat sheet of flame leaving the palm
and widening as it comes on, low and level and travelling just above
the sand, crossing the lower part of the frame and running out of both
bottom corners. At the hand it is narrower than her own forearm; by
the bottom edge of the picture it is wider than the frame. It is a
thin sheet, its leading edge ragged and broken into separate tongues —
not a ball, not a column and not a cloud.

The fire is the only saturated colour anywhere in the picture, deep
orange going to pale yellow where the sheet is thinnest, with the
sand, the seating and her clothing in muted greys and dull browns
around it. It is also the only warm light, and it is between her and
the viewer: it throws warmth from in front and below onto the
underside of her jaw, her cheekbones and the front of her body, and
everything behind and above her stays cold and flat.

The fire is the brightest and most saturated thing in the picture, and
her face carries the hardest edges and is the most finished drawing in
it; nothing in the flame competes with her face for finish.

SHE IS LOOKING DIRECTLY OUT OF THE PICTURE AT THE VIEWER, along the
line of her own extended arm.

HER FACE IS AT WORK AND GIVES NOTHING AWAY: the lids fully raised and
the eyes steady out at the viewer, the brows unmoved, the mouth closed
and soft. Not straining and not fierce.

She is small and finely built, small for her age, with a narrow frame
and a small face. Her hair is ink-dark, a flat neutral black, and it
is BOUND: gathered close against the head in a tight severe knot at
the back with nothing out of it, and none of it falling loose past her
shoulders. Her clothing is finely made — plain dark cloth of very good
quality, close-cut and buttoned high to the throat, with no ornament
of any kind — and it is whole and unmarked, with no tear and no stain
anywhere on it.

Nobody else is in the picture.
