Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights. Plus dry-brush drag and flicked spatter
for the movement.

Setting: the yard of a small stone house high on a mountain at
night, as loose wash suggestion — the front wall of the house across
the left of the frame with a low doorway standing open in it and
warm light coming out onto the stones, dry-laid pen walls going away
down the slope beyond, grey and empty, and the underside of a low
cloud overhead orange from one side of the frame to the other. Wind
in it.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is out in the yard, off to one side of
the door, low, at about the height of the man's hip and looking
slightly up — the undersides of both jaws are visible and the horizon
of the slope sits below both of them. VASK is already clear of the
doorway, nearest the centre of the frame, driving out to the right,
his body turned that way and his head turned further. KAEL has just
come through the door a stride behind him, to the left and further
back, already breaking the other way toward the near corner. THE TWO
LINES DIVERGE INSIDE THE FRAME: the man's body is going one way and
the boy's is going the other, and there is open ground opening
between them. The bottom edge crosses both at the knee, so that the
stones directly under their feet are in the frame and nothing below
that is.

Both are mid-stride. On each of them the back foot is off the ground
and the front foot is down and taking weight, the cloth is carried
back off the shoulders and the arms are behind the line of the body.
Neither is looking at the other. Neither has said anything.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
The man's right hand holds one longsword, carried down along his leg
with the point back and low, and nothing else.
The man's left hand is empty and open.
The boy's right hand holds one short blade and nothing else.
The boy's left hand holds one short blade and nothing else.
Both of the boy's blades are down at his sides.
Four hands, three blades, nothing else in any hand. No other blade,
blade tip, hilt or scabbard appears anywhere in the frame.

The longsword's blade is about as long as the man is from his
shoulder to his knee. Each of the boy's two blades is about the
length of his own forearm and hand together, and THE TWO DO NOT
MATCH EACH OTHER: one has a plain straight cross guard and the other
has no guard at all, and their grips are wrapped in two different
colours of worn cord.

VASK — moving fast and no hurry anywhere in the face. The whole body
committed out to the right, the shoulders level, the sword hand low
and quiet. The face level, the eyes out into the dark past the edge
of the frame, the mouth closed, the jaw easy. He is not braced and
he is not shouting. Plain village working cloth in undyed oatmeal,
worn soft, no ornament and no metal on him anywhere.

KAEL — a stride behind and turning off the man's line, the near
shoulder already dropped toward the corner he is going to, both
blades down. The mouth closed on a held breath, the brows down, the
eyes on the ground ahead of his own feet and not on his father.
Undyed coarse working wool in dull oatmeal and dead olive, much
mended, no ornament, badge, colour or metal on him anywhere. Dried
red to the elbow on both forearms, dark and gone brown, and none of
it is his; nothing on him is bleeding.

Lighting: the open doorway is the near source and does the work,
raking warm and low across both of them from the left and throwing
two long shadows out across the stones ahead of them. The orange sky
puts a thin light along the tops of the shoulders and through the
wind in their hair. The pens and the slope beyond go to a deep dark.
Nothing is burning anywhere in the picture.

The two faces carry the most finished drawing in the picture and the
sharpest step from light to dark; the house, the doorway, the pens
and the slope stay at quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges.

Preserve each exactly as shown in their reference images — faces,
hair, colouring, apparent age. Do not blend their features. The
man's eyes are iron-dark; the boy's are pale near-white grey.
