Image 1: valeria-14.png — portrait reference of the girl.
Image 2: aurelian-14.png — portrait reference of the boy.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: the open deck of a small dark boat at sea at night, as
loose wash suggestion — worn planking, a thwart, the low gunwale,
black water past it, and nothing at all beyond. A burning sail is
being walked over the side and is the only light in the picture.

Composition: 3:4. The camera is in the boat with them, low, level
with the gunwale. VALERIA is up on the thwart on the left, high in
the frame, her upper body turned out over the side and the LEFT side
of her face toward the camera. AURELIAN is on the right and low, in
the bottom of the boat, side-on, his head turned up and out to the
water. Between and in front of them the burning canvas hangs in a
heavy diagonal from her hands down to his and goes out over the
gunwale. The bottom edge crosses Aurelian at the bottom of the ribs
and cuts the near gunwale, so that no laps, no legs, no feet and no
bottom boards are in the frame at all.

The sail is already across the gunwale and still burning, going down
toward the water and not yet in it. Neither of them is looking at
the other; both are watching the canvas where it crosses the side.
Neither is looking at the viewer.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
Valeria's right hand holds a wet dark coat, bunched, and is pushing
the burning canvas out over the side with it.
Valeria's left arm is carried in against her ribs and her left hand
is empty.
Aurelian's right hand is shut on the bottom corner of the burning
canvas.
Aurelian's left arm is held in against his body and his left hand is
empty.
Four hands, one wet coat, one corner of canvas, nothing else in any
hand.

VALERIA — up on the thwart with her weight all on the forward foot
and the whole of her upper body out over the side, working
one-armed. The face level, the eyes on the canvas, the mouth closed,
the jaw set. Not straining and not afraid. The LEFT side of her face
has come up red and shining from the eyebrow down to the jaw — a
smooth shine on the skin, not a wound, not raw, nothing broken and
nothing bleeding. Long black hair, wet, pulled back off the face and
hanging heavy, no ornament. Dark near-black clothing, plain and well
made, soaked through and dirty and torn at one shoulder — good cloth
with one bad night on it, not ragged and not a beggar's. Twin blades
in their scabbards at her hips, both scabbards full, and neither
blade in her hands.

AURELIAN — down in the bottom of the boat, braced, one-handed, the
good right arm up and out with the corner of the canvas in it, the
left arm held in tight against his body. The face turned up to the
fire, the mouth open on the shape of a word, the brows down.
Dark-gold hair, wet. Heavy well-cut clothing in deep umber, soaked
and filthy, the sleeve gone at one wrist — good cloth ruined, not
rags.

Lighting: the burning canvas is the only source and it is close. It
lights both faces from in front and below, warm and yellow; the
boat, the water and everything past the two of them go to a deep
cold dark. Keep the skin around his eyes cooler than the fire on his
cheek, or the warm light eats the colour out of them.

The two faces carry the most finished drawing in the picture and the
sharpest step from light to dark happens inside the eyes; the sail,
the boat and the water stay at quiet middle values with soft
wash-bled edges.

Preserve each exactly as shown in their reference images — faces,
hair, colouring, apparent age. Do not blend their features. Do not
mix their clothing: the near-black and the twin blades belong only
to the girl, the deep umber only to the boy. Her eyes are emerald
green; his are amber gold-brown.
