Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the dark-haired boy. Image
2: kael-14-full.png — full-figure reference of the same boy, for his
build and clothing.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a narrow village lane as loose wash suggestion — rough stone
house fronts close on both sides, low roofs above them, the far end of
the lane going up into smoke and fog and stopping. A low stone well-head
behind him at one side. Flat cold fogged daylight, no sun and no hard-
edged shadow anywhere, and smoke lying along the lane at about the
height of his knee.

Composition: one boy alone, standing, close to the viewer and turned
three-quarters toward it, the bottom edge of the picture crossing him at
the waist so that no legs are in the frame. His left arm is extended
forward and a little across his body, the elbow nearly straight, the
open hand held out at the height of his own chest, nearer the viewer
than his face and larger in the frame for it.

Nothing is happening. He is completely still.

HIS LEFT HAND IS OPEN AND TOUCHING NOTHING, the fingers spread and
loosely curled, the palm turned up and angled toward the viewer. His
right arm hangs at his side and that hand is open and empty as well.
Nothing is in either hand, and no blade appears anywhere in the picture.

THE SKIN OF THAT FOREARM IS NEW AND PINK from wrist to elbow, smooth and
plainly different from the skin around it; over it, and over the whole
of the open hand front and back, there is red, dark and wet, gone darker
where it has dried in the creases of the fingers and the palm. The
sleeve of that arm is torn away below the shoulder.

Grey dust and soot across the front of him and along both arms, the
cloth torn open at one shoulder, one side of his face reddened below the
ear. There is no red anywhere else on him and nothing else on him is
bleeding.

HIS EYES ARE ON HIS OWN OPEN HAND: the lids fully raised, the pale grey
clear, the mouth open and the jaw slack, the brows level and unmoved. He
is not looking at the viewer.

He is the only figure in the picture and nothing is lying on the ground
anywhere in the frame.

The open hand carries the sharpest step from light to dark in the
picture and his face carries the next; the house fronts, the well-head,
the smoke and the far end of the lane stay at quiet middle values with
soft edges.

Preserve his face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as shown in
the reference images, which are the same boy. Pale grey eyes. He wears
undyed coarse working wool, much mended, in dull oatmeal and dead olive,
with no metal anywhere on him.
