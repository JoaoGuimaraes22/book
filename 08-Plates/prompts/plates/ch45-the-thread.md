Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of Kael.

Same medium and style as the reference image: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: a narrow steep fold in high country at the grey before
sunrise, with no colour in anything yet. A stony track beside fast
water, dark needle trees close on both sides, the sides of the fold
going up out of frame. Loose wash suggestion, no detail anywhere.
Flat grey light from above, no sun, no cast shadows.

Composition: 3:4. Two figures, chest-up and a little more, near the
viewer and side on to each other. KAEL stands on one side, turned
three-quarters toward the camera, holding a plain soldier's sword out
low in his right hand, one-handed, the blade level and across the
frame. A YOUNG MAN of about twenty stands opposite, half a pace
further back than he means to be, facing the blade. Nobody else is in
the picture.

ONE THIN WHITE LINE LIES ALONG THE BLADE AND NOTHING ELSE IN THE
PICTURE IS LIT. It runs the whole length of the steel from the guard
to the point, hairline-thin, perfectly straight and perfectly even,
with hard clean edges. It is the whitest thing in the frame and it is
carried on bare unpainted paper. It does not flicker, spread, branch,
spark or move. There is no glow around it, no haze, no bloom, no
halo. It throws no light: it does not light the blade, the hands, the
faces, the track or the trees, and nothing anywhere in the picture is
lit by it. Everything else stays exactly as grey dawn left it.

KAEL IS NOT PERFORMING IT. He is not braced, not straining and not
looking at the sword. His arm is relaxed, the sword held the way you
hold a tool you have held ten thousand times, and his eyes are on the
young man's face, level and flat and slightly tired. His left arm is
bound from the knuckles to above the elbow in clean linen with the
thumb splinted between two flat pieces of wood, held in against his
chest. Undyed coarse working wool, much mended.

THE YOUNG MAN HAS GONE BACK A STEP AND DID NOT DECIDE TO. Weight on
the back foot, shoulders up, the mouth slightly open, the eyes on the
line of white and not on the boy. Broad through the shoulder, a
working face, plain dark village clothes and a coat.

The white line and Kael's face carry the finished drawing; the young
man's face comes next; the water, the track and the trees stay at
quiet middle values with soft wash-bled edges and no hard edge in
them.

Do not blend their features and do not mix their clothing.

Preserve Kael's face, hair and colouring exactly as shown in the
reference image. Pale grey eyes.
