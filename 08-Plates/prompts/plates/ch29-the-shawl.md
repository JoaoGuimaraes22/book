Image 1: kael-14.png — portrait reference of the dark-haired boy.

Same medium and style as the reference images: loose watercolour and
ink on rough cold-press paper, scratchy ink linework, wash blooms,
bare paper in the lights.

Setting: an open hillside outside a wall, as loose wash suggestion — dry
grass, a low field wall of loose stone close behind them, and past that
a packed mass of standing people falling away into smoke and cold grey.
Flat cold fogged daylight, no sun and no hard-edged shadow anywhere.

Composition: two figures, close together and near the viewer, the bottom
edge of the picture crossing both at the lower chest. The boy sits on
the ground at the right with his back against the low stone wall, turned
three-quarters toward the viewer. The woman is beside and above him at
the left, crouched down on her heels and leaning in, her body turned
toward his right shoulder and away from the viewer, her face in three-
quarter view and fully visible. Both faces are in the upper half of the
frame. Nobody else is drawn near them.

A WOMAN PAST FIFTY, weathered, broad in the face, the skin loose at the
jaw and the eyes small and quick. Grey hair pulled back untidily. A
heavy knitted shawl in undyed grey wool over her shoulders, its near
corner taken up off her shoulder and bunched in her working hand. Coarse
village clothing, mended, in dull brown and dead green. There is nothing
ornamental anywhere on her.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND: her right hand holds the bunched corner of her own
shawl and is pressing it against the boy's right shoulder, quick and not
careful. Her left hand holds a small stoppered clay bottle down at her
knee and nothing else. His right hand rests palm down on his own raised
knee, open and empty. His left hand is flat on the ground beside him.
Four hands, two objects, nothing else in any hand.

HER MOUTH IS OPEN MID-WORD and her eyes are down on the shoulder she is
working on. She is not looking at the viewer.

HE IS LOOKING AT HER FACE: the head turned up to her, the lids fully
raised, the pale grey clear, the mouth closed, the brows level. He is
not looking at the viewer. THE TWO LINES OF SIGHT DO NOT MEET, AND THAT
IS THE SUBJECT OF THE IMAGE.

A fresh red slash through the cloth at his right shoulder, its edges
clean, with the shawl corner against it. Dried dark red up both his
forearms and across the backs of both his hands, older and browner than
the shoulder. Grey dust and soot over the front of him. A cloth-wrapped
bundle is on the ground beside him with the shape of two hilts in it,
the hilts covered.

Both faces are the most finished things in the picture and her two
working hands carry the sharpest step from light to dark after them; the
field wall, the crowd and the smoke stay at quiet middle values with
soft edges.

Preserve the boy's face, hair, colouring and apparent age exactly as
shown in the reference image. Pale grey eyes. He wears undyed coarse
working wool, much mended, in dull oatmeal and dead olive. Do not blend
their features and do not mix their clothing: the knitted shawl belongs
only to her.
