Medium: loose expressive watercolour and ink on rough cold-press
paper, visible paper tooth and pigment granulation, scratchy fine
ink linework, wash blooms with hard irregular edges, generous bare
unpainted paper.

Colour: restrained transparent washes laid over a dominant ink
drawing. Muted, desaturated, earthy. Colour tints rather than
fills. Warm living skin, soft ochre in the mid-tones — the warmth
belongs to the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth.

Eyes: the focal point of the picture.

Composition: three-quarter view with the head turned away from the
viewer, gaze returning to the viewer. Head and shoulders, with
visible shoulder and collar anchoring the lower frame.

Lighting: soft directional light, gentle shadow beneath the
cheekbone and along the jaw, most of the face held in light values.

Age: a man past fifty, thin, and nothing has been taken out of him.
Upright, the neck firm, the skin close at the throat, no hollowing
at the temples and no slackness under the jaw. Thin, not frail.

SUBJECT — cool pale parchment ground.
A man past fifty who has spent his working life with paper.
Eyes light grey-brown, and they carry the only fully resolved
drawing in the picture: a sharp closed line round the lid and a
clean wet dark at the pupil, while every other edge in the face is
loose wash bled soft.
Grey hair, thin, receded well back from the forehead, cut short and
kept. The brows are dark and do not match the hair.
Pale indoor skin, even, with no weathering anywhere in it and no
high colour.
A narrow face, long through the nose and the jaw, regular, with
nothing in the bone that catches.
The face is pleasant and the eyes are not. Mouth closed and easy
with the corners level, the brows unmoved, the head tilted a
courteous half-inch, and the eyes steady and unhurried.
A plain dark coat over a plain collar, good cloth, no ornament on it
anywhere.

Not a scholar, not a priest and not a soldier: nothing in the neck
or the shoulders was ever built by work.
