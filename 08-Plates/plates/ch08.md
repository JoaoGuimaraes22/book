### PLATE: seeing-about-the-goat

*Author-requested (s40).* Anchor: `images/portraits/vask.png` — face, hair and colouring; the body is authored here, and the face is turned away in any case. Ch. 8 canon: *"We do have a fire," Kael said, and his father stood up and went to see about the goat, who did not need seeing about, and was some time in coming back.*

**THE DESIGN: WE ARE DENIED HIS FACE EXACTLY AS KAEL IS.** He has left a lit room to have a feeling where nobody can watch him have it. So the picture is the back of him, and withholding the face is not a workaround here — it is the entire content. Nothing in `03` forbids his face; the moment does.

**THE LIGHT IS THE REMATCH.** `ch01-fallen-stars` proved an in-frame warm source renders beautifully *as* a source and throws nothing onto anything. The lever tried here is to **describe the cast light as an object with a shape and edges** — a long rectangle lying on the ground, his own shadow cut into it — rather than as an effect on a surface. If that lands, it is the technique that unblocks Aurelian's candle; if it does not, the limitation is real and should be written down as one.

**KAEL IS NOT IN THE FRAME AND THE DOORWAY MUST BE EMPTY.** He is at the table behind that door, which is the point — the man went out alone. An unattended run will fill a lit doorway with a figure, so the block bans it by name.

```
Image 1: portrait reference of the man — hair and colouring only.

Setting: the yard of a small stone-and-turf mountain house at night
in autumn, as loose wash suggestion — the house wall behind with one
open door in it, a fenced pen, the mountain lost in deep cool wash.
Wet ground, no snow.

Composition: the man full length, standing still in the middle of the
dark yard with his back to the house and to the viewer, a few steps
short of the pen. THE GOAT in the pen in front of him, side-on. The
open door behind him and to one side, small in the frame. Same ground
plane.

HIS BACK IS TO THE VIEWER AND NO FEATURE OF HIS FACE IS DRAWN — the
back of his head and his shoulders, not turned, not in profile, not
glancing back.

HE IS DOING NOTHING: standing, weight even, arms down at his sides,
both hands empty and open, not reaching for the goat, not working,
not carrying anything. No tool, bucket, rope or lamp anywhere in the
frame.

THE LIGHT COMES FROM THE OPEN DOOR AND IT MUST FALL ON THINGS. On the
wet ground it lies as A LONG BRIGHT RECTANGLE WITH HARD STRAIGHT
EDGES stretching across the yard toward the pen, and THE MAN'S OWN
SHADOW IS CUT INTO IT as a long dark shape running away from the
door. His back and the tops of his shoulders carry a warm rim.
Everything the rectangle does not reach is cold and dark. That
rectangle and the rim on his shoulders are the brightest things in
the picture and the only warm colour in it.

THE DOORWAY IS EMPTY: no person in it, no figure, no silhouette — a
bright empty shape.

THE GOAT NEEDS NOTHING: standing at ease, head down, eating or simply
standing, entirely uninterested in the man, not frightened, not
curious, not approaching.

THE MAN: broad through the shoulders and no further, heavy across the
top of the back with nothing wasted below it. Iron-dark hair of
medium length pushed back off the forehead. Coarse undyed working
cloth, much mended, THE INK CARRYING THE CLOTH at the seams and the
mended places.
```
