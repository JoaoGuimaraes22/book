# Ch. 1 — The Stars That Fell · plate sheet

Three candidates from `candidates.md` built as prompts. **None has been run.** Nothing here is adopted; when one is, the adopted note goes above its block and the image goes in `images/`.

**WHY THESE THREE TOGETHER.** They are three different kinds of picture — a two-figure night exterior, a single-figure gesture, an interior with nobody in it — and **all three are lit from below.** That is an accident of the chapter and it is the reason to run them as one batch: the portrait system's fixed lighting is soft and directional and has never once been asked for a source under the subject. If low warm light works here, Aurelian's candle in Ch. 9 is unblocked.

**ANCHORING COST, and it is the real cost of Part I.** Kael at four and both parents exist as **head-and-shoulders portraits only**; there is no full figure for any of them at any age below fourteen. So in plates 1 and 2 everything below the collar is authored in the prompt and anchored to nothing, and **it will not repeat in the next Part I plate.** Plate 3 has no reference at all. Two of these three pictures share a face with the library and share a body with nothing.

**THE GREY IS FRAMED OUT, not altered.** Kael's pale grey is unspent on the page, so no plate may show it. `kael-4.png` renders it as the lightest value in the face, which is legal in a reference and would be illegal here — so plate 1 puts both faces away from the viewer and says so twice. That is the framing answer, and it costs the picture nothing, because the moment is the direction of her hand.

**THE INHERITED COLOUR PARAGRAPH HAD TO BE OVERRIDDEN IN TWO OF THE THREE.** The house style ends *the warmth belongs to the skin alone, not the paper or the cloth* — written for portraits, where the only warm thing is a face. A plate lit by a fire or by a town has its warmth landing on timber, stone, wool and rock, and one of these three has no skin in it at all. Both blocks now say so in their own words. **This is a general plate problem, not a Ch. 1 problem**, and it is written up in `../scene-prompt-system.md` §Plates.

**DECIDED, so it is not defaulted:** Neris is **not pregnant** in this chapter. Kael is four; the pregnancy belongs to the tournament season nine years later.

### PLATE: fallen-stars

*Candidate 1, the chapter's pick.* Anchors: `images/portraits/kael-4.png`, `images/portraits/neris.png` — faces, hair and colouring only; neither face is visible in the picture.

```
Image 1: portrait reference of the boy — take his hair, colouring and
apparent age from it only.
Image 2: portrait reference of the woman — take her hair, colouring
and apparent age from it only.

Setting: a bare shelf of rock high on a mountainside at night, open
sky above, the ground falling away steeply in front of them. Loose
wash suggestion only — the rock implied in a few dry-brush strokes,
no detail anywhere, everything past the two figures going down into
deep cool wash.

Composition: both figures FULL LENGTH, SEEN FROM BEHIND AND SLIGHTLY
BELOW, small in the frame and set high in it, so the drop and the
water far beneath them fill the lower two thirds of the picture. A
woman stands at the edge of the shelf with a small boy carried on her
hip, wrapped to the chin in a large coarse wool blanket. Both are
facing away from the viewer, out and down over the drop.

NEITHER FACE IS VISIBLE, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. The woman is seen
from directly behind. The boy's face is turned down and away, mostly
lost in the wool and in shadow. NO EYES ARE VISIBLE ANYWHERE IN THE
PICTURE. Do not turn either head toward the viewer.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
Her left arm is under the boy, carrying his weight against her hip.
Her right hand is up at his jaw, fingers turning his face DOWNWARD —
away from the sky, toward the water far below. Both her hands are in
the frame and both are occupied. The boy's hands are inside the
blanket and not visible. Nothing else is in any hand. Neither of them
is carrying a light.

THE LIGHT COMES FROM BELOW, AND IT IS THE SUBJECT. Far down at the
foot of the mountain, where the land meets the sea, a heap of small
warm orange lights lies scattered along the shore — a town's lamps at
night, gathered in one corner where the shore meets the water,
doubled and broken in the black water beneath them, the reflections
trembling and slightly displaced. That heap of light is the brightest
thing in the picture and the only warm thing in it. It reaches the
two figures only faintly: a thin warm rim along the woman's shoulder
and the near edge of the blanket. Everything above them is cold —
rock, cloth, sky. Small cold stars in the upper sky, clearly separate
in colour and character from the warm lights below.

Nothing is happening. Nobody is in danger, nobody is hurrying. It is
a still, mild night and the two of them are standing looking down.

No skin is visible anywhere in this picture. The warmth in it belongs
to the lights below and to the rim they throw, and it falls on cloth,
hair and rock — nothing else in the frame is warm.

THE WOMAN: a grown woman of a mountain village. Tall, straight, and
completely still. Long dark brown hair falling loose past the
shoulders, warm rather than cool, plainly brown and not black. Plain
coarse village working cloth, much mended, in dull grey-brown and
undyed oatmeal. No ornament of any kind.

THE BOY: four years old, small, carried, wrapped to the chin in
coarse undyed wool in dull oatmeal.

No other figures anywhere in the frame. No buildings, no fences, no
animals, no boats. No fire, lamp or torch anywhere near them — every
light in this picture is far below them.
```

### PLATE: the-catch

*Candidate 2.* Anchor: `images/portraits/vask.png` — face, hair and colouring; the body is authored here.

```
Image 1: portrait reference of the man — take his face, hair and
colouring from it.

Setting: the yard of a small stone-and-turf mountain house in a
storm, as loose wash suggestion — a low roof-line behind him with
slates on it, a fence and a goat pen implied in a few strokes, the
mountain beyond lost in grey wash. Rain coming across at a steep
angle: dry-brush drag and flicked spatter through the whole
background.

Composition: the man waist-up, standing, his body and face turned
AWAY from his own raised hand, angled toward a goat in the pen at the
left of the frame. He is mid-sentence, talking to the animal.

WHAT IS IN EACH HAND:
His right arm is raised straight up, above and slightly behind his
head, and his right hand has closed on ONE flat grey roof slate — the
slate held flat in his palm, caught and stopped, not falling and not
being thrown. His left hand is down at his side, empty and open. Two
hands, one slate, nothing else in either hand. No tool, no rope, no
stick, no second slate anywhere in the frame.

HE IS NOT LOOKING AT THE SLATE, AND THIS IS THE SUBJECT OF THE
PICTURE. His face is turned away from his own raised hand, toward the
goat, mouth open mid-word. His expression has not changed for the
catch at all — level, unhurried, entirely unbothered, giving nothing.
The hand has done this without him. Do not have him look up at it. Do
not make him alarmed, braced, straining, heroic or surprised. No
effort anywhere in the body.

THE MAN: a grown man of a mountain village, a herder by every
appearance. Broad through the shoulders and no further — heavy across
the top of the back and the neck, nothing wasted below it, an economy
rather than a bulk. Iron-dark hair of medium length, wet, pushed back
off the forehead. Weathered hard, wind and salt worked deep into the
skin. Coarse undyed working cloth, heavy weave, plain collar closed
to the base of the throat, much mended, soaked dark with rain. No
ornament, no buckle, no badge.

One goat in the pen, ordinary, unsettled by the weather, looking at
him.

Light: flat cold storm light from a low grey sky, with the warm value
in the picture confined to the skin of his face and hand.

No other figures anywhere in the frame. No child, no woman, nobody
watching.
```

### PLATE: room-of-true-things

*Candidate 3.* No anchor at all — the test of whether the house look survives with nothing to hold it.

```
No reference image. Follow the medium and colour description above
exactly.

Subject: the single room of a small, poor, very well-kept mountain
house at night, seen FROM ABOVE — from the height of a sleeping loft,
looking down into the room over the edge of the loft floor, so that a
roof beam and the near edge of rough boards frame the top of the
picture. Interior only, the whole room in one view.

THE LIGHT COMES FROM ONE PLACE AND IT IS BELOW EVERYTHING. In the
middle of the floor is a square hole lined with stone, and in the
hole a small orange fire, low and contained. It is the only light in
the picture. It throws warm light up the walls and up the underside
of the table, the beams and the tools, and it leaves the corners and
the roof in deep cool shadow. The fire is SMALL and deliberately kept
small — a banked hearth fire, not a blaze, not a bonfire, no flames
reaching.

In the room, plainly arranged and readable at a glance:
a heavy table of plain hand-made wood;
two low beds against one wall with folded coarse wool on them;
a wooden chest, closed;
and one whole wall given over to TOOLS — a dozen or more hand tools
hung on pegs in a deliberate order, each with its own place, evenly
spaced, every one of them hanging straight and level and not one
crooked. After the fire, the tool wall is the most important thing in
the picture.

One heavy plank door, shut, with a wooden bar down across it in iron
brackets.

NO PEOPLE ANYWHERE IN THE FRAME. Nobody in the beds, no hands, no
figures, no faces, no silhouettes, nobody at the door. The room is
empty of people and that is deliberate.

Nothing is happening. Nothing is broken, knocked over, spilled or
burning that should not be. It is an ordinary night in a house where
everything has a place and is in it.

Colour: muted, earthy, desaturated — dull grey-brown timber, dead
olive wool, cold grey stone, and the single warm orange source down
in the floor. There is no skin in this picture at all: the warmth
belongs entirely to the firelight, and it falls on timber, stone and
wool.
```
