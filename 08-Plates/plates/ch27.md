### PLATE: the-sky

*Author-requested at s51: the fireballs raining down on the arena.* Anchor: `images/full-figures/kael-14-full.png`. Ch. 27 canon: *Something went over the top of the building. It came in high out of the grey from the seaward side, going fast and going away from him, and it was the size of a cart, and it was burning.* … *It came out of the fog over the rim with fire going backward off it in a tail* … *The third and the fourth came over together.* … *the fire from the fourth stone found the new timber and began to go along it under the seats, and the smoke came up through the boards in fifty places at the same time in neat little rows.* … *Eleven thousand people who had come to watch two children fight got up out of their seats and tried to leave a stone bowl through four gates, and Kael stood on the sand in the middle of it.*

**IT ABSORBS THE PARALYSIS BEAT RATHER THAN COMPETING WITH IT.** A small dark figure standing alone and still on the flattest, most open ground on the headland, with both hands empty, **is** *every school he had was running perfectly, none of them was producing an instruction* — and it does not need a face to say so. **It is also the deliberate inverse of `ch23/empty`**: the identical empty-handed stance on this identical sand, which one chapter ago was the whole of his mastery.

**THE FIRE IS THE ONLY WARM THING IN A GREY PICTURE**, which is what makes the palette do the work: flat fogged daylight everywhere, and four burning objects that are the only saturated colour in the frame.

```
Image 1: kael-14-full.png — full-figure reference of the boy, for his build and silhouette. His face is not resolved in this picture.

Setting: the inside of a great stone arena in fog, seen from down on
its floor. Pale raked sand fills the lower part of the frame. Banked
seating rises beyond and above it, packed with people, and goes up

Composition: one small dark figure standing alone on the sand in the

about as long as three seated people are wide, wrapped and burning

smoke coming up through the boards around it in fifty small separate
threads in neat rows.

The crowd in the seating is standing and moving, drawn as a wash of
small shapes with no face resolved anywhere and nobody drawn in
detail.

The fires are the only saturated colour in the picture — deep orange
and pale yellow — and the only warm light in it. Everything else is
cold grey, pale sand and wet stone.

The boy on the sand is the smallest thing in the frame and the
stillest, and everything else in the picture is in motion.

Preserve his build and clothing as shown in the reference image —

### PLATE: one-hand-out

*Candidate 1.* Anchor: `images/portraits/severin.png`. Ch. 27 canon: *It came down at the north curve. At the children. / And it stopped. / It stopped in the air over the seats with about the length of a man to spare, and it hung there. The fire on it went on burning, going nowhere. People who did not know what they were looking at looked at it anyway, and for one entire second the noise in that building went out like a lamp.* … *Nothing had shown. No light. No sound. An old man in a grey robe on a walkway with one hand out.*

**THE WHOLE PICTURE IS AN ABSENCE, AND THE BLOCK SPENDS ITS ONE BAN THERE.** *Nothing had shown. No light. No sound.* A burning object held in the air by a man with his arm up pulls hard toward every rendering of magic there has ever been — beams, glow, aura, distortion — so §Absence takes exactly that pull and nothing else. **What is between his hand and the stone is empty air, and that is the subject.**

**AND THE SECOND SUBJECT IS THE SILENCE**, which is drawable: *for one entire second the noise in that building went out like a lamp.* Everything in the frame is stopped — the stone, the fire on it, the people. In a chapter where a crowd is a wash of motion, one picture where the wash has frozen is the beat.

**IT IS THE FOLDER'S SECOND ART-WITH-NO-EFFECT**, after `ch26`'s unspent *both hands on her*. Both are Severin, which is the character: the most powerful thing on the island and nothing ever comes off him.

```

Setting: the inside of a great stone arena in fog, seen from down on
