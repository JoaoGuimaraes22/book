# Canon brief — paste at the top of any prompting conversation

A summary of `02` and `03` for a tool that cannot see them. Facts and fences only; if this disagrees with a sheet, the sheet wins and this file is the bug.

## What this file does not carry

**Armament · garment · grime and damage · hair styling · any state a chapter has put on a body.** All of it changes scene to scene, and `02/geography.md` already rules it: **a character reads by WHERE THEY ARE AND WHAT THEY ARE DOING, not by their house.** The prompt authors those from the chapter it is drawing, out of that chapter and `03`'s DYNAMIC and §Body. **What is below is only what is on the person in every picture** — colouring, build, bone, bearing, permanent marks. A FENCE on a changeable channel is still permanent, and those are kept.

## Every image

- Two body-marks of magic exist: **red eyes** and **white hair**. White hair is never styling — never whiten a head for drama, age or contrast. Aeliana's silver is a house trait, warm and bright; the drain register is pure quartz white, cold and hard. Keep them distinct.
- The medium speckles every face. Texture, not freckles; do not fight it and do not read it as canon on anybody.
- Colour lives in the eyes; everything else muted and earthy. Kael's grey wins on value, not saturation.
- **Three visual registers** (`02/geography.md`): the island keeps its own material culture · the capital and the imperial houses are Latin-Byzantine · the south is loose pale cloth wound and hung on men and women alike, heads covered, brown faces and dark eyes, walls the colour of the ground with the ground's own grain in them.
- **Never design "the coat."** It is the book's metaphor for Kael's concealment, not a garment.
- The empire's honourable arms: longsword, rapier, spear, carried as posture.
- **Ages are relative, and the age in a picture is the chapter's.** Aeliana is one year above Kael, Valeria, Aurelian and Elarine, who are of an age; the parents are the same age as each other; Severin is old. Every plate anchors on the fourteen-year-old references whatever the chapter (`README.md`), so the age wanted is written into the prompt or the picture will be fourteen.
- **THE PLATE FOLLOWS THE PAGE** (`README.md`): a plate shows only what its chapter has shown. Where the page is silent, frame — never invent.

## Kael

- Lean, hard, strong without bulk; the strength sits in the forearms and the hands. Narrow through the shoulders — adolescent, not grown.
- **Dark hair**, his mother's, worn short and cut by somebody who was not being careful.
- **Eyes pale grey**, near-white, the lightest value in the face. Free in every image, plates included.
- Handsome, with faintly asymmetrical features — one brow set slightly higher, the nose a fraction off centre. **He is invisible by status and stillness, not by his face**: *plain* does not render and he is not plain.
- Marks, drawable only where the chapter has shown them: burn speckles **wrist to second knuckle** (Ch. 13) · blade scar on the **left forearm** (Ch. 6) · long shallow scar on the outside of the **left arm above the elbow** (Ch. 23) · burn on the **left forearm, wrist to elbow** (Ch. 26), lying over the blade scar — **the first mark he did not spend himself.**
- **Fence:** his full-figure reference wears *plain dark clothing, well-made and well-maintained, chosen to go unnoticed*, and that is wrong. He is not dressed to conceal; he is dressed in whatever his chapter has him in.

## Valeria Vel

- Lean, athletic; a working build that reads as function even at rest.
- **Long black hair**, well past the shoulders, no ornament.
- **Emerald green eyes**, spent at reading distance only.
- Her stillness warns. Gaze level, flat, appraising — unimpressed and faintly tired. Not vulnerable, not wistful, not searching.
- Knuckles older than her face; guard-lineage calluses; old pale scar lines on both forearms, and four newer.
- **From Ch. 36: half her face is burned red and shining down one side**, healed to a shine by Ch. 37. **The page never says which side** — a plate picks one or turns it out of frame, and picking is an invention.
- **Fence: never a one-sword identity.** Twin blades, both hands full, is what she is. The fence is against one sword and not against a count in a given frame: where a hand is occupied with something else, a plate may leave the second blade out of frame or undrawn (author, s51).

## Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine

- Not tall; slight, sheltered, soft — a body that has never worked.
- **Long silver-white hair**, colourless, never blonde, no yellow or gold — the lightest mass in the picture. **The colour is on the page from Ch. 37**, and there is nothing else in the world that colour.
- **Pale blue eyes**, still unspent on any page.
- Pale untouched skin, freckles across the nose and cheeks. Face slightly rounded, small rounded chin, short philtrum, slender neck.
- **Fence:** she is the most beautiful person in the book and in competition with no one — **and her beauty may never arrive as a verdict.** A portrait *plate* of her is illegal; a reference is legal.

## Aurelian Corvane

- Tall for the cohort and built a year ahead of it — broad, heavy through the shoulder, high carriage, the face still soft. A body ahead of its face.
- **Dark-gold hair.** **Amber, gold-brown eyes** — the one warm saturated point, with the skin around the eye kept cool or the face eats it. His ground stays cool; warm parchment is Aeliana's alone.
- Clear skin over good bone, with no weathering worked into it — the station is in the skin, whatever is on top of it.
- Open, easy, pleasant — unspent strength carried high, a boy never once put on the ground. Never sneering, smug or cruel.
- **From Ch. 26 the arm is not all right, and he pretends about it.** The page gives *the bad arm* and *the good arm* and **never names a side.**
- **Fence:** no insignia, badge, house colour or chain, ever. The quality of the cloth is the station; nothing announces the house.

## Elarine Sedrane

- Small, neat, finely built; nothing on her that does not work. Narrow shoulders, a small head, a slender neck.
- **Ink-dark hair**, a flat neutral black with no blue in it, **bound severe, never loose.**
- **Eyes very dark brown, near black** — a portrait may show them, a plate may not name them.
- Pale indoor skin, clear and even, no high colour in it.
- Assessment in progress: attention fully engaged, giving nothing back, no verdict reached. Not cold, haughty, bored or tired — Valeria owns tired.
- **She carries a sword** (author, s49), in the doctrine grip: right hand full, left hand open for the Art.
- **Fence: the binding is the silhouette and it does not come loose to make a picture.** She reads *scholar* where Valeria reads *uniform*; a run that let the binding go returned Valeria's silhouette on her.
- One tell, a state and not a feature: spots of colour high on the cheeks (Ch. 13, Ch. 17). Never in the canonical portrait; available to a plate of those moments only.

## Vask — the father

- Broad through the shoulders and no further; fills a doorway by carriage; moves exactly as much as is required.
- **Iron-dark hair** of medium length, greying early at the temples as diffuse scatter — **never a streak, seam, blaze or band** (that shape is the drain-mark). The adopted reference shows no grey and the plates of him stand; ruled correct (s43). Not re-cut.
- **Iron-dark eyes, and they are empty**: level, giving nothing — not hostile, cold, sad, kind or tired. Both parents are dark-eyed; Kael's grey is no one's.
- A face like used tools, the most weathered in the book. Hands permanently half-curled, as if around something set down.
- Scars, on the page in Ch. 3: a long seam under the **left shoulder**; a round through-wound with its brother on the front; **tally-marks on the forearms**, all one length, all one slant.

## Neris — the mother

- Passes as village until she is still; the stillness is the portrait. Fine bones under worked hands; weather and sun on her, but not the coarsening.
- **Dark brown hair**, long — warm rather than cool, plainly brown and not black, no blue in it. **Dark eyes.**
- **Three quartz-white marks in her hair, and they are permanent** — two arriving in Ch. 32, the third at the front of the hair in Ch. 33. Cold and hard, and distinct in kind from Aeliana's silver.
- Not warm, not maternal, not soft.
- **Her hands are where the book keeps her.** Any full figure or scene puts them in frame and occupied.
- The adopted reference reads early thirties and striking; ruled good (s44). Not re-cut.
- **Ch. 21 to Ch. 33 only:** she is pregnant across that window and **a plate never draws it** — posture, cloth and crop carry it or it is out of frame (`README.md`). From Ch. 33 she is not.

## Severin — the First Master

- Old, worn to what mattered; not frail, not kindly-grandfatherly. Tall, gone lean, still broad across the shoulder. **The wrists are the tell** — too thick for a man whose work was paper. The scholar's stoop is borrowed, over an enforcer's frame; both visible at once.
- **Hair fully grey**, the whole head, a true neutral grey — not quartz white, not warm silver — and even everywhere: no seam, streak or patch.
- **Eyes dark, near black.**
- Courteous, unhurried, attentive; the face gives out nothing it has not priced. Not warm, not kindly, not stern.
- **Fence: what a month at sea or a large working costs him is written on the surface, never as frailty** (s58) — *the skin scoured, and the frame under it sound; the strength still there, nothing taken out of him.*

## Wren — the brother

- A newborn; his mother's dark hair. From crown to heel about the length of a grown man's forearm.
- **NEVER DRAW HIS EYES.** A narrow strip of pale linen is bound across them from Ch. 33 on — level, clear of the nose and clear of the mouth, tucked behind the ear, neatly done and not tight — and the prompt says *no eyes are visible on the child anywhere in the picture.* What is under it is `03`'s and stays off every plate.
