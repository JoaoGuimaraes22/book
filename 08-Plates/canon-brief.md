# Canon brief — paste at the top of any prompting conversation

A summary of `02` and `03` for a tool that cannot see them. Facts and fences only; if this disagrees with a sheet, the sheet wins and this file is the bug.

## Every image

- Two body-marks of magic exist: **red eyes** and **white hair**. White hair is never styling — never whiten a head for drama, age or contrast. Aeliana's silver is a house trait, warm and bright; the drain register is pure quartz white, cold and hard. Keep them distinct.
- The medium speckles every face. Texture, not freckles; do not fight it and do not read it as canon on anybody.
- Colour lives in the eyes; everything else muted and earthy. Kael's grey wins on value, not saturation.
- Two registers: the island keeps its own material culture; the capital and the imperial houses are Latin-Byzantine.
- **Never design "the coat."** It is the book's metaphor for Kael's concealment, not a garment.
- The empire's honourable arms: longsword, rapier, spear, carried as posture.
- Book One ages: Kael 13 · Valeria 13 · Aurelian ~13 · Elarine ~13 · Aeliana 14, reading one year above the rest and never below. The parents and Severin are unpinned; their files carry no age suffix.
- **THE PLATE FOLLOWS THE PAGE** (`README.md`): a plate shows only what its chapter has shown. Where the page is silent, frame — never invent.

## Kael

- Handsome, and filthy with it — not plain (*plain* does not render). Dark hair, his mother's, hacked short by somebody who was not being careful. Lean, hard, strong without bulk; the strength in the forearms and hands. Invisible by status and stillness, not by his face.
- **Eyes pale grey**, near-white, the lightest value in the face. Free in every image, plates included.
- Marks: burn speckles **wrist to second knuckle** (Ch. 13) · blade scar on the **left forearm** (Ch. 6) · long shallow scar on the outside of the **left arm above the elbow** (Ch. 23) · burn on the **left forearm, wrist to elbow** (Ch. 26) — **the first mark he did not spend himself**, lying over the blade scar; mended by Severin inside the chapter and pink and new by its last page. Drawable only where the chapter has shown them.
- Armed: two short blades across his back in a cloth wrap, hilts covered, reading as a bundle — plain, unmatched, one heavier than the other. A knife inside the clothing, not visible. His full-figure sheet carries neither; write the wrap in by hand when the moment carries it.
- Cloth: undyed, coarse, frayed village working cloth. Never "well-made kit chosen to go unnoticed."

## Valeria Vel

- Lean, athletic, a working build that reads as function even at rest. Long black hair worn loose, well past the shoulders, no ornament. **Emerald green eyes**, spent at reading distance only.
- Her stillness warns. Gaze level, flat, appraising — unimpressed and faintly tired. Not vulnerable, not wistful, not searching.
- Knuckles older than her face; guard-lineage calluses; old pale scar lines on both forearms, a few fresher.
- **Twin blades, both hands full — never one sword.** A matched pair, plain, well kept. The fence is against a one-sword identity, not a count in every picture: **where a hand is occupied with something else, a plate may leave the second blade out of frame or undrawn** (author, s51, on `ch27/i-need-you`).
- The capital's grey: uniform-adjacent, austere, undecorated; a bronze collar insignia (canon-by-precedent). Distinct from Kael at thumbnail size: hers long, his short; hers in hand, his bundled.

## Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine

- The most beautiful person in the book, in competition with no one. Not tall; slight, sheltered, soft — a body that has never worked.
- **Long silver-white hair**, colourless, never blonde, no yellow or gold — the lightest mass in the picture. Loose by default; **up off the neck at the tournament** (Ch. 22). **Pale blue eyes.**
- No armour in Book One; court dress only. Ch. 22 gown: a blue so dark it reads black until the light moves on it, gold at the shoulder and the hem in a quantity with no function except to be a quantity. Ch. 24 audience: seated, a lighter dress with less of it, hair down.
- Her hands do nothing: unoccupied, holding nothing, at rest.
- Her beauty never arrives as a verdict: a portrait *plate* of her is illegal. References are exempt.

## Aurelian Corvane

- Beautiful, and knows to the grain what it buys him. Tall for the cohort and built a year ahead of it — broad, heavy through the shoulder, high carriage, the face still soft.
- **Dark-gold hair**, groomed. **Amber, gold-brown eyes.** His ground stays cool; warm parchment is Aeliana's alone.
- Clear well-fed skin with sun on it: no weathering, no grime.
- Open, easy, pleasant — unspent strength carried high, a boy never once put on the ground. Never sneering, smug or cruel.
- Good cloth well cut; the quality is the station. No insignia, badge, house colour or chain.

## Elarine Sedrane

- Small, neat, finely built; nothing on her that does not work.
- **Ink-dark hair bound severe**, never loose; a flat neutral black with no blue in it. **Eyes very dark brown, near black** — a portrait may show them, a plate may not name them.
- Pale untouched indoor skin.
- Assessment in progress: attention fully engaged, giving nothing back, no verdict reached. Not cold, haughty, bored or tired — Valeria owns tired.
- Plain dark clothing of very good quality, close, buttoned high, no ornament of any kind. She reads *scholar*; Valeria reads *uniform*.
- One tell, a state not a feature: spots of colour high on the cheeks (Ch. 13, Ch. 17). Never in the canonical portrait; available to a plate of those moments only.

## Vask — the father

- Broad through the shoulders and no further; fills a doorway by carriage; moves exactly as much as is required.
- Iron-dark hair of medium length, greying early at the temples as diffuse scatter — **never a streak, seam, blaze or band** (that shape is the drain-mark). The adopted reference shows no grey and the plates of him stand; ruled correct (s43). Not re-cut.
- **Iron-dark eyes, and they are empty**: level, giving nothing — not hostile, cold, sad, kind or tired. Both parents are dark-eyed; Kael's grey is no one's.
- A face like used tools, the most weathered in the book. Hands permanently half-curled, as if around something set down.
- Scars, on the page in Ch. 3: a long seam under the **left shoulder**; a round through-wound with its brother on the front; **tally-marks on the forearms**, all one length, all one slant. Out of frame unless the moment needs them.
- Village working cloth, coarse, mended, closed at the throat; no ornament. The parents are the same age.

## Neris — the mother

- Passes as village until she is still; the stillness is the portrait. Fine bones under worked hands; weather and sun on her, but not the coarsening.
- Dark brown hair, long and loose. **Dark eyes.**
- **Her hands are where the book keeps her.** Any full figure or scene puts them in frame and occupied.
- Not warm, not maternal, not soft.
- Eight or nine months pregnant through the tournament season. **A plate never draws it**: no clause naming the term, the carry or the shape, and nothing below her collar authored where the frame can hold it — posture, cloth and crop carry it or it is out of frame (`README.md`).
- The adopted reference reads early thirties and striking; ruled good (s44). Not re-cut.

## Severin — the First Master

- Old, worn to what mattered; not frail, not kindly-grandfatherly. Tall, gone lean, still broad across the shoulder. **The wrists are the tell** — too thick for a man whose work was paper. The scholar's stoop is borrowed, over an enforcer's frame; both visible at once.
- **Hair fully grey**, the whole head, a true neutral grey — not quartz white, not warm silver — and even everywhere: no seam, streak or patch.
- **Eyes dark, near black.**
- Robes and a chain with weight in it at the Temple and the games; the plain coat on the mountain.
- Courteous, unhurried, attentive; the face gives out nothing it has not priced. Not warm, not kindly, not stern.
