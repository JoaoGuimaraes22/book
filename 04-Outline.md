### BOOK TWO'S SHAPE (author, s61) — TWO YEARS, THE TRIO, AND THE ROAD TO SOLVENNA
**Broad only, by destination (author, s61): the specifics are uncovered at design time, chapter by chapter.**
- **The span:** roughly two years from the wake — Kael and Valeria 13→15, Aeliana 14→16. **Only the three of them are on the page**; Neris, Wren, Severin, Aurelian and Elarine stay off it until the end.
- **THE DESTINATION IS ALREADY ON THE PAGE (Ch. 38).** *You do not go to the capital from here — you go to Solvenna, and from Solvenna to the capital. Five months to Solvenna in good weather … The empire begins there.* **SOLVENNA** is the southernmost city of the empire's original borders; **Qeshet is a city-state**, held through a native ruler. Kael carries the number out of that bed. **Both halves of the party are walking to the same place**, which is what makes the ending a promise rather than a coincidence.
- **1 — THE DOOR, AND THE OPEN GROUND.** **THE DOOR IS SPENT — Ch. 41 "The Second":** the morning door, the throat with the teeth, the knife into Valeria's reach, **the princess standing up**, three men dead inside the box with the bar put back on from outside, and out of a camp of fifty unseen. The s62 ruled shape is discharged; its text is in `archive/outline-archive.md`. **THE SEARCH PARTY IS SPENT — Ch. 42 "The Question":** twelve came (Kael's count held), seven in on foot into ground he set, and the three of them killed the party together and kept one man to say where they are — *why fifty men do not catch three children* is answered (the horses are useless on the stone; the party cannot spear into cover where the girl may be; the ground channels them one at a time). **BOTH OWED ITEMS DISCHARGED AT s67.** The pursuit *slower and bigger* is on the page at Ch. 44 — ten horses at least, off them in a yard and not the first yard that day, working the villages with a list, describing all three, and naming a price worth every roof in Doresh and *any house* as the threat. And **ISHARETH is spoken** (Ch. 43, in the smith's mouth): six or seven days east along the south skirt, where the Kharoun stops and the ground goes down to a river — the last of the empire, and past the river not. **The country past the river is still unminted.** The near ground is now named: **the KHAROUN** and the village **DORESH** (`02-World-Bible/geography.md`). Three children loose with a fevered hand, no Art, iron nobody can get off, a soldier's sword each and half a skin of water.
- **1b — THE PURSUIT IS DESTROYED, AND THE ROAD EAST IS THE ONLY ROAD.** Spent at Ch. 45–46. Of the fifty on that road, three died at the box, seven in the wood and **nineteen in the fold above Doresh**; twenty-one are left and the governor is at Ishareth buying more. **Solvenna is not reachable from the Kharoun** — the west road is two months in the open, wells a day apart, no cover for eight days, and every well has had a man at it describing her hair — so the trio turns **east, toward the man hunting them**, and the page never says whether Kael chose it for the map or for the man.
- **2 — QESHET IS GONE.** Learned at distance, never visited: the coalition has struck it, and whatever was there is not there. What follows from it is Kael's own reasoning and the only thread he has — if the others live, they are for Solvenna.
- **3 — THE CONTINENT IS AT WAR AND THE ROADS ARE WATCHED.** Moving hidden is the problem the book is built on; his father's fieldcraft and his mother's languages are what keep three people alive.
- **4 — THE CREW (the main line). FIRST CONTACT IS AT CH. 48 AND THEY ARE *THE HONEST MEN*.** They fall in with outlaws working west toward Solvenna, who know how to move through law. **Passage is paid in work** — jobs, heists, whatever the road asks — and the price of it is that **three identities have to be held for two years**, from the crew and from everybody else. **MET AT CH. 49, AND THE TRADE IS STRUCK.** What the page has (`../02-World-Bible/factions.md` §THE HONEST MEN): the byword and why it is one; the fake-out house and the sacks; a rope-walk that is a going concern; **fifteen of them, none of whom looks like anything**, including a man of sixty in a good coat, two brothers from somewhere else, a woman with a basket who asks the best questions in the room, and four who are of Ishareth; a leader past fifty who is unnamed on the page (BORREN reserved, `../02-World-Bible/naming.md`); a plan complete for a month except the inside of one building; **boats bought before the turn of the month at a price he does not enjoy thinking about**; and the one rule that binds all of them — **nobody who knows how it is going to be done leaves until it is done.** **The terms:** information and two Resonants' hands for **one part in ten**, and Kael inside the building when the door opens. **The first job is not the one Kael brings them** — theirs was ready and he was the missing part of it — and **passage paid in work is still owed**, because nothing about Solvenna has been agreed with anybody.
- **5 — SOLVENNA, AND THE REUNION.** Book Two ends when the group is one again. Who arrives, and what it costs, is not yet written.
- **Unminted / undecided:** the capital's name (unnamed on every page to date) · the crew's leader and its rules · whether the cover story is chosen once or accretes. **The coalition country the border runs against is minted: NALAR** (Ch. 47).
