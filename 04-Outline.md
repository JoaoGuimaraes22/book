# BOOK ONE — THE ISLAND

## Part I — The Mountain (Kael ~4 → 10) · the held breath
1. **First light. — SPENT: Ch. 1 "The Stars That Fell" (Kael = 4).** Earliest consciousness in a small child's prose. The village lights — "the stars that fell down the mountain." His mother: the sea would have to freeze first. He waits every winter.
2. **The rules as nature. — SPENT: Ch. 2 "Part of the Mountain" (Kael = 5).** Never past the split rock; fires only in the hollow hearth; the stillness game when boats come near (he has never beaten his father).
3. **The double curriculum (intense learning as love language). — SPENT: Ch. 3 "Chores" (Kael = 8).** FATHER: the body — weapons, unarmed work, footing, breath, silence, terrain. MOTHER: the mind — languages "for the birds" (four by age 8), herb-lore + reagent-craft (which mushrooms stop a heart, "so you never pick wrong"), far-counting (math), the standing game (mechanics/engineering), "what fire eats" (chemistry — spent Ch. 5), and in time their way of magic (arrives Ch. 4 as a threshold, not one more subject). HISTORY ONLY IN SCRAPS — deliberately rationed; the Temple carries real history education (Part II). Each lesson innocent to Kael, wrong in the reader's head. The parents rebuilt the academy that unmade them, around one boy, out of love and terror — and gave him everything except other people. KAEL'S CURIOSITY canonized here as defining trait: the itch that cannot be out-waited.
4. **The visitor. — SPENT: Ch. 4 "The Visitor" (Kael = 9).** The master's first on-page appearance AND Kael's first meeting — previous visits happened for years as Kael's arranged absences (the books came on those days; he never questioned it). The flicker: Kael does something small and impossible without knowing (his profile — tiny, precise, low-output); the master goes still; the parents exchange the look; Kael misses it. Night: the untaught-language argument (SPENT here — the flicker is the subject), stopped by the floorboard creak. Coda: blade across her knees ("keeping the cold out" — SPENT). Final movement: the mother begins the new school — magic as threshold, perception-first, craft register, no system mechanics. NO father-glimpses (ledger shut).
5. **The year. — SPENT: Ch. 5 "The Year" (Kael = 9; crossing at midwinter).** Magic obsession; all three schools intensify; the new school RATIONED (the leash beneath — 00); the relief-tell spent, deniably. His questions outgrow her answers for the first time. THE PROMISE DIES: "what fire eats" (SPENT — appetite-lore: fire eats, cold eats, salt keeps) teaches him salt and freezing; he works out ALONE that the sea can never freeze — "a door painted on a wall"; the wait was rigged. Her own curriculum kills her cover story (the cultivated inheritance eats its own tail). SILENT: no confrontation; his first secret kept FROM them — the shut-door room; the concealment inheritance begins. Yearning becomes PLANNING: parents' patterns and sleep studied like weather; the night-watch pattern cracked (follows the boats); the door's holding learned in innocent pieces. The master's autumn visit: THE WINTER-FEAST FACT (open valve — liar's-knot family). Ends at the split rock, on the dark of the year, crossing — "The mountain did not notice."
6. **The night. — SPENT: Ch. 6 "The Night" (Kael = 9; continuous from the crossing).** Five movements: the edge of the lit world (wonder and fear in one breath; he never enters the feast); the cold spot (the dark cluster on the night of all lamps); the settlement (the open door; the dead man read through butchery-school; THE WINDOW — the assault witnessed in full through failing categories, the tapping hand; the unfiled faculty — conscience, unlabeled); the untying and the fight (motif spent — below decision, interrupted; intact machinery / broken operator — every exchange won legibly until the count breaks; "not enough" misfiled; THE SCAR — left forearm); the fragments (ledger-compliant rescue; Neris at the rock; the door closing with him inside; the stitching; "He never learned whether the line had held"). Sealed in 00: total kill; Vask completed the untying; the folklore valve; absorbed-state Kynemir rationale. How Vask knew: the empty loft (his waking never in Kael's arithmetic — planted Ch. 3).
7. **The opening. — SPENT: Ch. 7 "The Opening" (Kael = 9→10; PART I CLOSES).** Five movements: the floor (healing; the punishment that never comes; the knife returned; the wasters carved); the audit (design c — exploit AND fight reviewed as work; the misfile fed: "The body held. We will build the rest."; weapons school opens; Neris's doubled watch; the honesty pivot — "I don't know" with both hands); the conversation (the crack — "It was not everything there is"; permission given before asked); the master & THE RULE (persuasion offstage behind the wall — line sealed 00; the eavesdrop declined; the terms; "Home to the temple. The temple to home. Never beyond." — a road cut through the middle of the itch); the walk down (the master at the split rock; "Kael of what?" first strike; the rehearsal — the coat; the village in daylight, settlement roofs unremarked; the carried-water question; the Temple gate — group laughter's first strike, three stacked systems armed; "Come along, charity case"). Part I ends; Kael 10.

## Part III — Valeria (Kael ~12 → 13) · the mirror
12. **She arrives** with the tournament's advance party: Valeria Vel. Flawless, cold, unbeaten. She watches Kael fail to hide something in the yard — and recognizes the SHAPE of his training as real, like hers. First words are a challenge.
13. **The rivalry.** She beats him, repeatedly, at first. He is the first to make her work; she is the first to be his measure. Seasons of matches and silent one-upmanship. Neither calls it friendship; everyone sees it's the only friendship either has ever had. Combat is the one social language he speaks — she is its only other native speaker.
14. **The cracks in her.** Trains before dawn (cannot sleep); letters read by chaperones; the microscopic flinch at her family's revered name. One honest night-conversation: what it costs to be forged. Two weapons discovering they're also children.
15. **The world leaks in.** Continent politics via Temple talk and Valeria's guarded remarks: the rot of the court, provinces bled, honest officials ruined. WAR PRE-SEEDING: rumors reach the Temple; lessons rewritten; older students summoned home; letters grow strange; the princess's visit as imperial confidence-display BECAUSE the frontier cracks. FAR-CONTINENT SEEDS: the forbidden lecture; the chart with a coastline that doesn't exist; the bird-language word that makes a master go pale. AT HOME: the mother is pregnant — nerves fray, drills sharpen, leaving is impossible this year.

## Part IV — The Tournament & The Fire (Kael ~13–14) · the pattern catches fire
16. **The island fills.** The Grand Tournament: a royal princess will attend. Weeks of ships — retinues, banners, and quiet professionals who look at faces. The parents go strange in ways Kael now has the training to notice; they ask him to withdraw. For the first time in his life, KAEL REFUSES.
17. **The birth.** Kael's brother is born. He holds him — perhaps only once. A pure joy planted directly before the catastrophe.
18. **The tournament.** **INTER-SCHOOL (sealed session 8):** the three imperial mage schools convene — hence the island filling, the princess, and the harvest's screening scope (all three schools' best in one place; see 00/02). Trials of arms, wits, structured magic — **amended session 8: first magic seen CLOSE and WHOLE, as spectacle** (the pre-declaration cohort design makes the reserve a POV-access rule — casting existed behind walls all through Part II; "rules still mysterious" now means the tournament's formal dueling rules and the declared arts at full scale, not the minima). Kael rises — seen, publicly, by hundreds; the princess watches. Somewhere in the crowd or the cordon, recognition (vector TBD — see 00 for the harvest). Summit: KAEL VS. VALERIA.
19. **The fire — mid-duel.** Horns and smoke: COALITION FORCES hit the island (their true targets: see 00 — the family is collateral). The match is never finished. Imperial security collapses inward around the princess. Kael fights toward the mountain and arrives into the worst of it. THE PARENTS UNSHEATHED — first and last time; the father's last hour is the most terrifying display of skill in the book; he dies buying time for both sons. The mother and newborn are extracted by the master — separated from Kael in the chaos; no farewell. Fragments of truth reach Kael mid-catastrophe: the insignia, the word, the mother's face — enough to shatter, not enough to understand. Something from the chest ends up in his hands (contents TBD). THIRD-HAND GLIMPSE planted in the chaos (see 00). Kael's first truth, written in fire: the coalition are the enemy.
20. **The flight.** Why Valeria runs WITH him: the princess must be moved, the Temple is falling, eleven generations of Vel training execute — duty first; the deeper choice deferred. A boat, the winter sea, the island burning, the lights going out. **End of Book One:** three children — the mountain boy, the guard's daughter, and the princess of the empire — aimed at the continent.
