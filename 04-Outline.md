## Part IV — The Tournament & The Fire (Kael ~13–14) · the pattern catches fire
**The clock (Kael's own count, Ch. 19):** the turn toward spring · the island filling · the games · the birth, colliding with them · three weeks · a beach in the dark. Ch. 27 stands on day 3 of a printed six with the birth hours or days away, and the games are over.
16. **The island fills.** — HALF SPENT: Ch. 22 "The Names" (the filling, the garrison, the princess arriving, the games opening). **THE ASK AND THE REFUSAL ARE UNSPENT.** Withdrawal has been institutionally impossible since Ch. 18, so the parents' plan — stated in front of Kael in Ch. 19 and never once asked of him — is an EXTRACTION MID-TOURNAMENT: he appears, goes as far as he goes, and on the day the boat is ready they take him off the field, *whatever day it falls on and whatever he is standing in the middle of.* For the first time in his life, KAEL REFUSES — to leave the island with his pregnant mother and newborn brother, because he wants the tournament — and then the island burns. His guilt architecture for the whole series, and 00-safe: the refusal does not cause the raid; it only puts him at the Temple with his family still on the island. Nobody has asked him anything yet, and he is never written as reluctant.
17. **The birth.** Neris goes into labour as the semifinals begin (00 s26) — a violent Kynemir delivery. Severin is above the sand, not at the house, and must be fetched; **he reaches her (author, s32) and she gives birth with his help** — alone for the start of it, not the end. Vask is split three ways (a wife in a house, a son on the sand, the one man who can save her in a box above it) and holds two at most; the order of the legs is unresolved. Kael holds his brother — perhaps only once — a pure joy directly before the catastrophe, with RECOGNITION inside it (the eyes, 00 s20). Neris's survival unconfirmed. The numbering is a list, not a chronology: beat 17's events interleave with 18–19.
18. **The tournament.** — **SPENT WHOLE: Ch. 22–27** (opened at the bracket; Drusane · Sedrane · the semifinal that is never fought). **Tournament structure, the day-map, the bells, martial-prowess-only and the summit-is-the-semifinal** are law in `02-World-Bible/temple.md` §Tournament Structure; the per-chapter record is in `07-Story-Ledger/chapter-records.md`, the clue state in `07-Story-Ledger/planted-clues.md`. **00 s8's first-magic-as-spectacle: OPENED Ch. 23, SPENT Ch. 26** — nothing is reserved.
19. **The fire — AT THE BELL (author, s32).** The duel is never fought: the third bell strikes to begin the semifinal, the judge's hand comes up and never comes down, and the invasion arrives in that instant — not one exchange between Kael and Valeria on any page, ever (SPENT: Ch. 27 "The Fog"). Horns and smoke: COALITION FORCES empty the island (their true targets: 00 — the family is collateral). Imperial security collapses inward around the princess. Kael crosses the island toward the mountain and arrives into the worst of it. THE PARENTS UNSHEATHED — first and last time: THE CHEST IS OPENED and the parents take up their old weapons (contents sealed in 00); the father's last hour is the most terrifying display of skill in the book, spent running the distance between a wife alone in labour, a son on the sand and the First Master in a box above it (00 s26); he dies buying time for both sons. VASK THROWS HIS SWORD TO KAEL as he dies — staged per 00's paternity rule, as the monument, not the bequest. The mother and newborn are extracted by the master — separated from Kael in the chaos; no farewell. Fragments of truth reach Kael mid-catastrophe: the insignia, the word, the mother's face — enough to shatter, not enough to understand. THIRD-HAND GLIMPSE planted in the chaos (00). Kael's first truth, written in fire: the coalition are the enemy. **SPENT SO FAR: Ch. 27 "The Fog"** (the bell, the fleet, the crush, Severin's earth and its limit, the eastern gate) **+ Ch. 28 "Turning Point"** (the crossing inland through the village; Valeria killing; **KAEL'S FIRST KILL**; arrival at the district gate among tens of thousands). **+ Ch. 29 "The Gate"** (the crowd, the wall, Halden taking Valeria through and shutting it on Kael, and the walk east alone) **+ Ch. 30 "The Houses"** (the crossing alone; the fold; the guerrilla through the holdings; the coalition general; **VASK AT TRUE CAPACITY**, and the carry home) **+ Ch. 31 "The Line"** (the district from inside, in VALERIA's head — the manuscript's first interlude, concurrent with Ch. 29–30: the council, the houses electing to hold, and the party made). **STILL UNSPENT IN THE FIRE:** the chest (paid at the house as archaeology — the steel reached the page first, 00/06) · Neris unsheathed · the throw · the insignia · the word and the mother's face · the seed-bread's inversion · the FLICKER · the extraction. The next chapter's design: 05 §NEXT TARGET.
20. **The flight.** Why Valeria runs WITH him: the princess must be moved, the Temple is falling, eleven generations of Vel training execute — duty first; the deeper choice deferred. **PROTECTED AND SHARPENED BY CH. 29:** at the gate she never got to choose at all — she refused, and was taken through by the arm — so the crown-vs-Kael choice is wholly unspent and now costs more. **CH. 31 BUILDS THE ROAD OUT AND DOES NOT WALK IT:** the district evacuates a party over the mountain to Severin's hull, she is posted on the princess's left and wants to be, and she is carrying one fact she has told nobody — the district's north side, off Kael's own reconnaissance. **The choice is still not made**, and the route to the boat now runs across the mountain the house is on. A boat, the winter sea, the island burning, the lights going out. **End of Book One:** three children — the mountain boy, the guard's daughter, and the princess of the empire — aimed at the continent.
