## Part IV — The Tournament & The Fire (Kael ~13–14) · the pattern catches fire
16. **The island fills.** — **HALF SPENT: Ch. 22 "The Names"** (the filling, the garrison, the princess arriving, the games opening). **THE ASK AND THE REFUSAL ARE UNSPENT AND UNTOUCHED** — nobody has asked Kael for anything and he is never once written as reluctant. The Grand Tournament: a royal princess will attend. Weeks of ships — retinues, banners, and quiet professionals who look at faces. The parents go strange in ways Kael now has the training to notice. **RESCOPED (00 s21, refined s22):** withdrawal has been institutionally impossible since Ch. 18, and a boy written down under seal who does not appear is a question asked out loud in the capital — **so he competes.** The parents' plan, stated in front of him in Ch. 19: he appears, he goes as far as he goes, and on the day the boat is ready they take him off the field, *whatever day it falls on and whatever he is standing in the middle of.* **The ask is therefore an EXTRACTION MID-TOURNAMENT, not a withdrawal** — walk off the field in the middle of the thing you are winning, in front of the empire. For the first time in his life, KAEL REFUSES. He refuses to leave the island with his pregnant mother and newborn brother because he wants the tournament, and then the island burns — his guilt architecture for the whole series, and 00-safe: the refusal does not cause the raid, it only puts him at the Temple and his family still on the island. *(Ch. 12 planted the PROHIBITION at 11 and Kael complied, so the refusal here stays the first. The ladder's middle leg is CLOSED — see 13a.)*
17. **The birth.** Kael's brother is born. He holds him — perhaps only once. A pure joy planted directly before the catastrophe. **STAGED s26 — THE BIRTH LANDS ON THE SEMIFINALS, AND THAT IS THE POINT (00 s26).** Neris goes into labour as the semifinals begin — the round the raid lands in — and **Severin is above the sand, not at the house**, because the games are the only thing his chair exists for. She is alone for a violent Kynemir delivery; Vask is split between a wife in a house, a son on the sand, and the one man who could save her sitting above it. Do not resolve who he reaches. **RULED s25 — THE BIRTH COLLIDES WITH THE GAMES.** Neris was ~3 months in the autumn (Ch. 19), so she is eight or nine months when the tournament opens: the birth falls **during or hard against tournament week**, not cleanly before it. **The numbering here is a list, not a chronology** — beat 17 keeps its place on the page and its events interleave with beat 18. This is what makes beat 16's ask work as written (00 s21/s22): the boat becomes ready *mid-games*, which is why the extraction is a walk off the field rather than a withdrawal, and it gives Severin his in-story reason to be in that house when the raid lands. Neris's Ch. 19 line about three weeks *before the sea fills up* is superseded inside its own scene by *whatever day it falls on* — a revision, never to be staged later as a contradiction. Full ruling: 05 §NEXT TARGET.
18. **The tournament.** — **OPENED: Ch. 22 "The Names"** (the bracket standing, the first bout called) · **FIRST BOUT SPENT: Ch. 23 "The Ghost"** (Kael 13, day 1 — he beats Lucan Drusane with no Art and no weapon and knocks him out; **the people name him THE GHOST and the nobles say nothing**; the First Master declares it with his office; **Halden smiles, unseen**; the princess **sends for him**). **00 s8's first-magic-as-spectacle is OPENED, NOT SPENT (s28):** Lucan's air columns are the first unfenced art on the page, and true spectacle is reserved for the top-tier bouts. **DAY-MAP RULED s28 — one round a day: R16 · R8 · SEMIS · break · a final nobody reaches; Kael's road is Drusane → SEDRANE → VEL** (full doctrine in 02 §Tournament Structure). **THE COLLISION STAYS UNANNOUNCED (s28):** the reader holds the shape, never the promise — pre-announcing the summit spends every intervening bout. **INTER-SCHOOL (sealed session 8):** the three imperial Resonant schools convene — hence the island filling, the princess, and the harvest's screening scope (all three schools' best in one place; see 00/02). **MARTIAL PROWESS ONLY (author ruling, s26).** Arms as carried, resonance without limit, one bracket, and no scored discipline beside the fighting — *the games are what the empire takes its own strength to be.* This SUPERSEDES the session-8 wording *"trials of arms, wits, structured magic"*, which implied separate events, never reached any page, and could not stand against a single-elimination bracket of combined bouts (full doctrine in 02 §Tournament Structure). **The session-8 amendment STANDS: first magic seen CLOSE and WHOLE, as spectacle** (the pre-declaration cohort design makes the reserve a POV-access rule — casting existed behind walls all through Part II; "rules still mysterious" now means the tournament's formal dueling rules and the declared arts at full scale, not the minima). Kael rises — an unknown boy with no house and a style nobody has seen, publicly, in front of hundreds. **The people love him; the nobles do not** (s22). The princess does not merely watch — **she takes an active interest** (s22). Somewhere in the crowd or the cordon, recognition (vector TBD — see 00 for the harvest). **STRUCTURE RULED s25 (full canon in 02 §Tournament Structure):** GOLD LEAGUE of 16 — 6 Temple / 5 capital / 5 northwest — running 16 → 8 → **SEMIFINALS** → final, with a Temple-only SILVER league beneath it for a purse of gold. **THE SUMMIT IS THE SEMIFINAL:** KAEL VS. VALERIA is a semifinal, not the final, so **the games are cut off one round short of their own end and no champion is ever crowned.** The bracket must be legible to the reader long before it breaks.
19. **The fire — mid-duel.** Horns and smoke: COALITION FORCES hit the island (their true targets: see 00 — the family is collateral). The match is never finished. Imperial security collapses inward around the princess. Kael fights toward the mountain and arrives into the worst of it. THE PARENTS UNSHEATHED — first and last time; the father's last hour is the most terrifying display of skill in the book; he dies buying time for both sons — **and the geometry is now fixed (00 s26): he spends that hour running the distance between a wife alone in labour, a son on the sand, and the First Master in a box above it.** The mother and newborn are extracted by the master — separated from Kael in the chaos; no farewell. Fragments of truth reach Kael mid-catastrophe: the insignia, the word, the mother's face — enough to shatter, not enough to understand. THE CHEST IS OPENED — the parents take up their old weapons (contents sealed in 00) and "the parents unsheathed" becomes literal. VASK THROWS HIS SWORD TO KAEL as he dies — staged per 00's paternity rule, as the monument, not the bequest. THIRD-HAND GLIMPSE planted in the chaos (see 00). Kael's first truth, written in fire: the coalition are the enemy.
20. **The flight.** Why Valeria runs WITH him: the princess must be moved, the Temple is falling, eleven generations of Vel training execute — duty first; the deeper choice deferred. A boat, the winter sea, the island burning, the lights going out. **End of Book One:** three children — the mountain boy, the guard's daughter, and the princess of the empire — aimed at the continent.
