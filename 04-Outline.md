## Part IV — The Tournament & The Fire (Kael ~13–14) · the pattern catches fire
16. **The island fills.** The Grand Tournament: a royal princess will attend. Weeks of ships — retinues, banners, and quiet professionals who look at faces. The parents go strange in ways Kael now has the training to notice; they ask him to withdraw. For the first time in his life, KAEL REFUSES. *(Ch. 12 planted the PROHIBITION at 11 — Kael complied then, so the refusal here stays the first. The ladder's middle leg — how the prohibition becomes enrollment before this beat — is OPEN and needs a dedicated design session; Severin's pan-away question is the planted door.)*
17. **The birth.** Kael's brother is born. He holds him — perhaps only once. A pure joy planted directly before the catastrophe.
18. **The tournament.** **INTER-SCHOOL (sealed session 8):** the three imperial Resonant schools convene — hence the island filling, the princess, and the harvest's screening scope (all three schools' best in one place; see 00/02). Trials of arms, wits, structured magic — **amended session 8: first magic seen CLOSE and WHOLE, as spectacle** (the pre-declaration cohort design makes the reserve a POV-access rule — casting existed behind walls all through Part II; "rules still mysterious" now means the tournament's formal dueling rules and the declared arts at full scale, not the minima). Kael rises — seen, publicly, by hundreds; the princess watches. Somewhere in the crowd or the cordon, recognition (vector TBD — see 00 for the harvest). Summit: KAEL VS. VALERIA.
19. **The fire — mid-duel.** Horns and smoke: COALITION FORCES hit the island (their true targets: see 00 — the family is collateral). The match is never finished. Imperial security collapses inward around the princess. Kael fights toward the mountain and arrives into the worst of it. THE PARENTS UNSHEATHED — first and last time; the father's last hour is the most terrifying display of skill in the book; he dies buying time for both sons. The mother and newborn are extracted by the master — separated from Kael in the chaos; no farewell. Fragments of truth reach Kael mid-catastrophe: the insignia, the word, the mother's face — enough to shatter, not enough to understand. THE CHEST IS OPENED — the parents take up their old weapons (contents sealed in 00) and "the parents unsheathed" becomes literal. VASK THROWS HIS SWORD TO KAEL as he dies — staged per 00's paternity rule, as the monument, not the bequest. THIRD-HAND GLIMPSE planted in the chaos (see 00). Kael's first truth, written in fire: the coalition are the enemy.
20. **The flight.** Why Valeria runs WITH him: the princess must be moved, the Temple is falling, eleven generations of Vel training execute — duty first; the deeper choice deferred. A boat, the winter sea, the island burning, the lights going out. **End of Book One:** three children — the mountain boy, the guard's daughter, and the princess of the empire — aimed at the continent.
