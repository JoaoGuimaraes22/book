## APPEARANCE REGISTER (rules — the per-character entries live in each character sheet, under STATIC → Appearance)
- **Register (author-amendable):** grounded-specific with strong silhouettes — no heightened palette; memorable through specificity (a feature, a carriage, a habit of the body); each main-cast member owns a distinct silhouette readable at distance. The cast is kept deliberately plain by design — the dark room the story's two loud marks (red eyes, white hair) need.
- **Rendering rule:** filter through KAEL'S EYE (reach, weight, where a body carries, what the hands do) — conventional beauty or ugliness arrives tagged as what OTHER PEOPLE react to, never as narrator verdict. Descriptions accumulate across chapters from the sheets; never improvise against them.
- **Scars accumulate as a record (s28):** Kael now carries two — the Ch. 6 left-forearm blade scar (near Vask's tally-marks in site and length, rhyme unmarked) and a second off Lucan Drusane's lunge at Ch. 23, **long and shallow on the OUTSIDE of the left arm ABOVE the elbow**. Kept deliberately distinct in site so the tally-mark rhyme is not muddied. *"You will keep the scar and it will be a stupid one."*
