## APPEARANCE LEDGER (built session 8 — decided cold, spent gradually)
**Register (author-amendable):** grounded-specific with strong silhouettes — no heightened palette; memorable through specificity (a feature, a carriage, a habit of the body), each main-cast member owning a distinct silhouette readable at distance. **Rendering rule:** filter through KAEL'S EYE (reach, weight, where a body carries, what the hands do) — conventional beauty/ugliness arrives tagged as what OTHER PEOPLE react to, never as narrator verdict. Descriptions accumulate across chapters from this ledger; never improvise against it.

- **KAEL:** village-plain by design — dark hair (his mother's), unremarkable at rest; a wiry, light-through-the-shoulders build that hides its training completely (nothing to look at until he moves, and he is trained not to move). Eyes a pale grey — BELONGING TO NO ONE IN THE HOUSE (sealed session 9, see 00: the bio-father's; the ground the reddening needs). RENDERING RULES: the parents never remark on his eyes, not once, in any register — their silence is the tell that never tells; the grey arrives only through other mouths or other eyes (no mirrors, no self-description); unspent on page as of Ch. 9 — spend when a Temple mouth wants a detail to be cruel or curious about. Left forearm: the blade scar (canon, near his father's tally-marks in site and length). Marked older than his face admits. What others react to: nothing — which is the point, and slowly becomes the thing they react to.
- **VASK:** broad through the shoulders and no further — an instrument's economy, not a soldier's bulk; fills a doorway by carriage rather than size. Iron-dark hair going grey at the temples; eyes iron-dark, near-black (sealed session 9 — BOTH parents dark-eyed; the grey is no one's); a face like used tools, weathered past its years. The scars canon (the seam, the through-wound pair, the forearm tallies). Hands permanently half-curled, as if around something set down. Moves exactly as much as required and no more. PATERNITY (sealed 00, session 9): not Kael's father by blood — father by total choice; "dies buying time for both his sons" carries the truth in *both* — PROTECT.
- **NERIS:** dark-haired, dark-eyed; passes as village until she is still — then the stillness has a quality the village doesn't make (carriage-tell of the register-less name; deniable, never explained). Fine bones under worked hands; the hands are the lie's weak point (they know too many trades). Ages of watching in a face the village would call handsome and unremarkable in the same breath.
- **SEVERIN:** old the way the split rock is old (canon); tall, gone lean, a stoop that reads as a scholar's and once wasn't. Hair fully GREY (canon — the second reading never confirmed). An enforcer's frame under a master's robes: the wrists too thick for the ink they carry. Face: courteous, unhurried, giving out nothing it hasn't priced (where Kael's mother learned it or he did is a question the reader may ask and never answer).
- **WREN:** infant in Book One; ledger entry reserved. Decided facts: his mother's coloring COMPLETE — dark hair, dark eyes; the only face in the family that resolves (sealed 00, session 9: the only child of both by blood). The brother's eyes remain a series door for the other reason.
- **VALERIA VEL (SEALED at her chapter — Ch. 14 design, session 17):** lean, strong, athletic — built the way a tool is built when eleven generations have asked it the same question; nothing spare, nothing ornamental; a working build that reads as function even at rest. BLACK hair, medium length (to the shoulders), kept clear of ornament. EYES: EMERALD GREEN — on-page register "a clear, hard green, the green of deep water over rock" (color spent at reading distance only, Ch. 14 duel — POV-honest; at rank distance she is silhouette and stillness). THE EYES' AFFECT: plain and BORED — worn as display, a boredom that has so far always been right; the day it dies is a beat (SPENT Ch. 14 — it dies against Kael; the awake eyes are the recognition made visible). THE SILHOUETTE: twin blades, both hands full — the one-handed empire's other heresy (her art is internal; both hands free for steel — see 02); perfect stillness that is nothing like Kael's (his disappears; hers WARNS — "the way a drawn blade stands in a room"). Knuckles already older than her face; calluses in the guard-lineage places Kael's eye prices on sight. What others react to: the name before the girl — rooms rearrange around "Vel" before she enters them; reverence draws her one untrained tell, the microscopic flinch (unspent as of Ch. 14 — bank it). What Kael's eye files first: the economy.
- **AELIANA (reserved for the tournament):** imperial register made flesh — direction only: the kind of beauty that is also a uniform. Full block sealed at her chapter.
- **OSTRIAN "OSS" VESSALINE:** soft-built, round-faced, quick-eyed; expressive hands that talk when he does, which is always. THE FEATURE: everything he wears is slightly too new — cloth that hasn't learned him yet (the bought name rendered as wardrobe; deniable, load-bearing). A face built for laughing that watches carefully between laughs.
- **AURELIAN CORVANE:** tall for his cohort, built a year ahead of his age; dark-gold hair (ON PAGE Ch. 8, session 9 revision — harmonized from "bright-haired"), high carriage, unspent strength Kael's eye prices on sight. What others react to: he is beautiful and knows the exact weight of it — the bench behaves differently in his radius (render the radius, not the adjective). Fire's warmth in the coloring without stating it.
- **ELARINE SEDRANE:** ink-dark hair bound severely, pale, economical — nothing on her that doesn't work; dark eyes that price before they blink (Oss's line made flesh). Small; forgettable at a glance and unforgettable the third time you notice she hasn't moved. What others react to: they mostly don't — which she has noticed (her Wound, worn as neatness).
- **PRISCIAN:** heavy, round, stuffed to the seams (canon made body); ink-stained cuffs, chalk dust in the creases, a squint from a life at close text. Moves like a man carrying a library. Delight and irritation use the same furrow.
- **RUTHEN:** built like old rope — a lean, hard, used frame with the yard's weather in it; dark hair with THE SEAM (canon, one clean streak of white). Knuckles like a counting-frame; a cadence-counter's unhurried walk. A face that has priced worse yards than this one.
- **LUCAN DRUSANE:** "the long one" (canon) — tall, narrow, all reach; a careful dresser even in yard clothes; a fencing-master's posture worn like inheritance. A narrow face that does arithmetic badly in public (the Ch. 9 tell — his thinking shows, which among the golden children is a weakness). 
- **HALDEN:** broad and comfortable — weight worn easy, a man built like good furniture; grey at the temples; "a voice like a warm room." Nothing about him hurries. What others react to: they relax — which is the instrument. (Kael's eye files the one wrongness: the warmth runs level, a lamp burning the same for everyone.)
- **THE GARDENER:** reserved entirely (00).
