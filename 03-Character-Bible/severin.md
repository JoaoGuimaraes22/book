## SEVERIN (the master)
**Glance:** head of the Temple — THE FIRST MASTER; the family's secret savior and patron · empire past, his own man now · old, the way the split rock is old · **he delivered the child, took a mountain off its own flank, closed a cut behind them with living wood, and got all eight of them onto his own hull.**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Severin — on-page mostly "the master"; spend the name sparingly so it carries weight.
- **Role:** Head of the Temple; the mountain house's one visitor, exempt from the stillness game, unexplained. Persuaded the parents to let Kael attend; sponsor of "the master's charity case."
- **Origin (00 — never told in Book One):** what he was before the Temple, and what he owes this family, is in 00. On the page it exists only as inference: the exemption from the stillness game, the parents' warmth, small tells.
- **Doctrine:** Reform through education (its origin is in 00). WOUND: what he did before the Temple. LIMIT: believes education can outrun rot; the fire tests this.
- **Abilities:** Resonant — EARTH + NATURE dual proficiency, rare, powerful, experienced (00). His grey's drain-reading grows louder and is never confirmed.
- **THE ART *AND* THE MEDICINE (Ch. 19, ratified s22):** nature resonance AND decades of read medicine. The Art alone is worth little — it needs the knowledge in the same head — and he is the only man on the island with both, which is why Neris will have no one else at the birth. Why an old man learned to mend is in 00. Never remark on it.
- **At the games he is the safety and the arbiter (02/temple):** sharp steel and unlimited resonance under one unenforceable clause, and one old man in a chair to catch what falls — earth to block a directly fatal attack, nature to mend even a fatal wound; he stops bouts, interrupts attacks and declares victors. Everyone knows; nobody says it; he does not remark on it either.
- **Manner:** Kindness in valve-form; doors with nothing written on them; keeps his word as far as we ever witness. THE VALVE FAMILY (do not resolve): the liar's knot — gift or test?; the winter-feast fact — table-talk or the door handed over? Both readings alive, perhaps even to him; they accumulate toward the sealed-room endgame (00) without tipping.
- **Appearance:** Old the way the split rock is old; tall, gone lean, a scholar's stoop that once wasn't. Hair fully grey — the second reading never confirmed. **Eyes dark, near black (author ruling, s34; unspent on any page)** — the one unreadable dark thing in a face gone entirely pale. A soldier's frame under a master's robes: wrists too thick for the ink they carry. A face that is courteous, unhurried, and gives out nothing it hasn't priced.

### DYNAMIC
- **HE STOPPED KAEL FOLLOWING HIS MOTHER AND SAID *"I know what she is."*** — sourced nothing, was never asked to explain it, and it is entirely deniable as *she gave birth an hour ago*. **A NEW VALVE: never resolve it, and never let a later page have him account for it.**
- **NATURE IS NOT ONLY MENDING IN HIS HANDS.** He drove living root and thorn eleven feet deep up through the floor and walls of a cut in half a minute and closed the road behind them, and got up crooked with the nose bleeding again. **The page explains none of it and nobody theorises it.**
- **He is on his own hull with eight people aboard it and has still not explained why he has it.**
- **He is carrying Vask's longsword**, handed up to him by Vask's widow without any expression on her face at all.
- **HE TOOK THE DELIVERY AND WREN LIVED.** He put a princess of the empire on the lamp, and **when the mother's eyes came up red he did not look up, did not react, and did not ask** — and then took the promise out of the girl on the spot. **He tore the strip for the child's eyes and held it out before anybody asked him to.** 00's §(a) who-else-knows valve moved without opening. **Never resolve what he knows.**
- **HE DROPPED THE EASTERN FACE OF A MOUNTAIN.** Not a lifting — a release: he found the lazy place under four hundred feet of terrace and took it away, with everything standing on it. **The Ch. 27 limit is answered without being contradicted: holding is the expensive direction.** He got up crooked with blood coming out of his nose, and no new white can show on a man already grey all through — **the never-confirm rule protects itself and nothing on the page remarks on it.**
- **HE PROMISED VASK AND HE KEPT IT.** *I have them, Vask.* — said twice, because he was made to say it twice.
- **HE SAW WHATEVER WAS IN KAEL'S EYES ON THAT MOUNTAINSIDE AND SAID NOTHING ABOUT IT, THEN OR EVER.** He turned the boy bodily round to look, and then put the mending art the other way round into him and took his legs. **He did not knock him out.** Never explain it, and never let a later page have him raise it.
- **He stopped for Neris on the broken ground when she told him to**, which nobody else on that mountain could have made him do.
- **Now:** he walked what was left of four hundred students off the headland to the only wall on the island, argued the district into evacuating, was given the party, and **went on ahead of it toward the mountain without saying why.** He reaches the house well after dark, at the head of the party, hours behind Vask and Kael (00 s42).
- **THE BOAT IS ON THE PAGE AND HE DOES NOT EXPLAIN IT.** Far side of the mountain, eastern water, *a cut below the goat ground*, takes about forty, his own, since the autumn. Asked why, he gave a true answer that answers nothing — *"You may ask me the rest of it when we are standing on the other side of that water"* — and **nobody in that room could make him.** **It is the family's hull** (03 above: *something of his own, kept dry on their side of the mountain*), and he has just committed it to the empire's children. **Nobody on any page knows this but the reader; do not let anyone say it.**
- **He was right and it was not enough.** He argued the wall down for two hours and the room turned only when a house said it after him. **The second time the reader sees him outreached**, and there is nothing on his face.
- **He is spent and it shows** — grey all the way through, gone thin, the scholar's stoop sitting on him as weight. The drain reading is louder and is still never confirmed.
- **Grey all the way through:** his **dark, near-black eyes are now spent on the page** (ruled s34), read off him by Valeria as the one dark thing left in that face, giving out nothing.
- **NOBODY FETCHED HIM** (author, s42). He came up that mountain at the head of fifteen people, of his own accord, hours after the labour began, because the road to his hull crosses it. He spent the arena morning walking four hundred students toward the northern arch and not looking up again, which is why he was not there when it started, and it was his own decision, made on the page.
- **He put the boy's name in front of the empire's soldiers** — *Kael of the Island*, and then the island's own word for him — to make a captain of the royal guard take a thirteen-year-old's arithmetic seriously. **Reporting the name, not conferring it** (`../02-World-Bible/lexicon.md`, amended s42).
- **He owned it out loud to Vask** — *I have brought all of this onto your mountain. I know what that is* — and got four words back.
- **His EARTH is spent, and its limit was priced in the same minute (Ch. 27):** he stopped one burning stone in the air over the children — nothing showing, no light, no sound — and did not stop the next. He could hold one; he could not hold two.
- **His MENDING is spent at scale (Ch. 26):** two bleeding children in front of the empire, Elarine first, nothing showing; his hands were not entirely steady, Kael saw it, and nobody said a word.
- **He is the second mouth.** Kael has heard the downhill language from exactly two mouths — his mother's and, once, the master's at his own table (Ch. 4) — and the men at the eastern gate speak it. The page names neither and draws no line. **Never explain it.**
- **He saw the smile and said so (Ch. 26).** He is the one man alive who knows what those two people were, and therefore what they did and did not put in the boy — **and he sources NOTHING; the seal is absolute; never explain on the page how he knows.** He refused to moralise (wrong about people for longer than Kael has been alive, and in both directions — never resolve which people or which direction) and gave one instruction — *"You watch it"* — given once, never to be repeated or expanded by anybody. He believes education can outrun rot, and has met something education did not put there. **Never remark on it.**
- **Presides, and Ch. 23 spent his authority and not one grain of his Art:** he ended the first bout with his office and his voice, naming as victor the boy he could not keep out of these games. Nobody remarks on it.
- **Outreached inside his own Temple:** he could do nothing about Kael's entry and told the boy so without softening it. The Temple is his; the tournament is not. Someone is above him and it is not a house.
- **THE PROMISE FAILED AT A WALL AND HE WILL NEVER KNOW (Ch. 29).** Kael quoted it whole through an eighteen-inch gap — *not me, and not my house* — to the one man in the empire it cannot reach, and the answer was *He is not here*, and it was true, because he was six miles off walking children out of a building and then going to take a delivery. **Which of 00's four readings this lands on is untouched; never qualify the promise retroactively.**
- **State:** the protection promise is given, unqualified and slightly too confident — *"you are not standing in it by yourself. Not you, and not your house."* **His read of the flash is SPENT**, paid as a refusal to ask. He reads Halden as no house and nobody's cousin, which is **WRONG, and he does not know it** — never correct him on the page. He reads the blank book at each termly summons; turned back once to a page with very little on it, looked at it twice, and said nothing.
- **Off-page and load-bearing:** he found the hull — nothing with the family's faces in a ledger; something of his own, kept dry on their side of the mountain — and arranged it before anybody asked him to. Neither parent will say why they trust him: Vask offers *"Because he has never once been wrong about us"* and Neris lays a second one beside it, *"He knows us. Truly."* — two reasons, neither explained, neither refused; never resolve what either of them is.
- **Knowledge valves (00):** untouched.

### FORWARD
- **THE CHAIR COSTS HIM THE DELIVERY (00 s26/s32/s42):** he is above the sand when Neris goes into labour, because that is what the chair is for, and **nobody comes for him**; he arrives hours late under his own power, and she gives birth with his help — alone for the start of it, not the end. The old man who learned to mend, held away from the one person who needs the mending by the empire's answer to a rule it could not enforce. **Never remark on it.**
- **Endgame:** open (00).
- **Status:** extracts Neris and the newborn during the fire; vanishes with them. Fate and whereabouts unknown.
