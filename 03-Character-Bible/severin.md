## SEVERIN (the master)
**Glance:** head of the Temple — THE FIRST MASTER; the family's secret savior and patron · empire past, his own man now · old, the way the split rock is old · **as of Ch. 26: above the sand for the whole of the games; he PRESIDES, and his MENDING is now spent in public at scale while his EARTH remains wholly untouched.**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Severin — on-page mostly "the master"; spend the name sparingly so it carries weight.
- **Role:** Head of the Temple; the mountain house's one visitor, exempt from the stillness game, unexplained. Persuaded the parents to let Kael attend; sponsor of "the master's charity case."
- **Origin (00 — never told in Part I):** old empire enforcer sent to hunt the fleeing parents; they spared and saved him; the island placement is the debt repaid. Empire man, NOT program man.
- **Doctrine:** Reform through education — the Temple as the parents' mercy institutionalized. WOUND: what he did as an enforcer. LIMIT: believes education can outrun rot; the fire tests this.
- **Abilities:** Resonant — EARTH + NATURE dual proficiency, rare, powerful, experienced (00). Demonstrations stay candle-scale; his grey's drain-reading grows louder and is never confirmed.
- **THE ART *AND* THE MEDICINE (Ch. 19, ratified s22):** he carries nature resonance AND decades of read medicine. The Art alone is worth little — it needs the knowledge in the same head — and he is the only man on the island with both, which is why Neris will have no one else at the birth. Quietly deepens the atonement doctrine: the old enforcer went and learned to mend. Never remark on it.
- **Manner:** Kindness in valve-form; doors with nothing written on them; keeps his word as far as we ever witness. THE VALVE FAMILY (do not resolve): the liar's knot — gift or test?; the winter-feast fact — table-talk or the door handed over? Both readings alive, perhaps even to him; they accumulate toward the sealed-room endgame (00) without tipping.
- **Appearance:** Old the way the split rock is old; tall, gone lean, a scholar's stoop that once wasn't. Hair fully grey — the second reading never confirmed. An enforcer's frame under a master's robes: wrists too thick for the ink they carry. A face that is courteous, unhurried, and gives out nothing it hasn't priced.

### DYNAMIC
- **THE MENDING, SPENT AT SCALE (Ch. 26).** He came down out of the host's box, crossed the whole floor at a walk, and **went to Elarine first** — mending two bleeding children in front of the empire, with *nothing showing. No light. No sound*, until a building of eleven thousand went quiet on its own. Both halves of the rare dual proficiency are no longer theoretical: **nature is SPENT, in public, at scale. His EARTH remains entirely unspent on the page.** **His hands were not entirely steady**, and Kael saw it, and nobody said one word about it.
- **HE SAW THE SMILE AND HE SAID SO (Ch. 26) — his LIMIT pre-tested in the gentlest register available.** *"Were you aware of it?… You were smiling. While you were cutting her. I do not believe anybody in those seats could see it from where they were sitting. I could."* Then, going down a list: **"Your father and your mother taught you to fight." / "Yes." / "They did not teach you that."** He is the one man alive who knows what those two people were, and therefore what they did and did not put in the boy — **and he sources NOTHING. The seal is absolute; never explain on the page how he knows.** He refuses to moralise (*"I am not going to tell you it is a bad thing. I do not know that it is. I have been wrong about people for longer than you have been alive, and I have been wrong in both directions"* — never resolve which people, or which direction), and offers the only thing his doctrine owns: **"You watch it. The way you watch everything else, which you do better than any child I have ever put in that yard."** He believes education can outrun rot, and has just met something education did not put there. **Never remark on it.**
- **HE PRESIDES, AND CH. 23 SPENT HIS AUTHORITY AND NOT ONE GRAIN OF HIS ART (s28).** He stops bouts, interrupts attacks, and declares victors. He ended the first bout of the games by standing up in his own box — not coming to the rail, not raising his voice, speaking into the one silence that building made all morning — and naming as victor the boy he could not keep out of these games. **Nobody on the page remarks on it. His earth and his mending are both still entirely unspent.** He sat down again before the noise started, and did not stand up for any of the four bouts before midday.
- **AT THE GAMES HE IS THE SAFETY (s25).** The tournament permits sharp steel and unlimited resonance under one unenforceable clause — *no mortal intent* — so he sits above the sand for six days and holds the line himself: **earth to block a directly fatal attack, nature to mend even a fatal wound.** Both halves of the rare dual proficiency, spent in public, all week. Everyone knows; nobody says it, because saying it means saying that the empire wrote a rule it cannot enforce and answered the difficulty by putting one old man in a chair. He does not remark on it either.
- **Now:** First Master, and **outreached inside his own Temple** — he can do nothing about Kael's entry and told the boy so without softening it. The Temple is his; the tournament is not. Someone is above him and it is not a house.
- **State:** the protection promise is given, unqualified and slightly too confident — *"you are not standing in it by yourself. Not you, and not your house."* **His read of the flash is SPENT**, paid as a refusal to ask. He reads Halden as no house and nobody's cousin, which is **WRONG, and he does not know it** — never correct him on the page. He reads the blank book at each termly summons, and turned back once to a page with very little on it, looked at it twice, and said nothing.
- **Off-page and load-bearing:** he will attend the birth (the Art *and* the medicine) and he will find the hull — not one with the family's faces in a ledger, something of his own, kept dry on their own side of the mountain. **He arranged it before anybody asked him to.** Neither parent will say why they trust him: Vask offers *"Because he has never once been wrong about us"* and Neris lays a second one beside it, *"He knows us. Truly."* — two reasons, neither explained, neither refused; never resolve what either of them is.
- **Knowledge valves (00):** untouched.

### FORWARD
- **THE CHAIR COSTS HIM THE DELIVERY (00 s26):** he is above the sand when Neris goes into labour, because that is what the chair is for, and he must be fetched. The old enforcer who went and learned to mend is held away from the one person on this island who needs the mending, by the empire's answer to a rule it could not enforce. **Never remark on it.**
- **Endgame:** open (00) — what he does with Wren, the extraction's true nature, whether the doctrine curdles.
- **Status:** extracts Neris and the newborn during the fire; vanishes with them. Fate and whereabouts unknown.
