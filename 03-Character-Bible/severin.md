## SEVERIN (the master)
**Glance:** head of the Temple — THE FIRST MASTER; the family's secret savior and patron · empire past, his own man now · old, the way the split rock is old · **the hull is gone — it opened on Qeshet's strand with nobody ever having said whose it was; he gave a governor's clerk his name and his lost Temple, heard a princess lie for the family, and said nothing; took the far room alone — *Goodnight*; **on no page from Ch. 39, and nothing of him since: Kael does not know whether he *had got up*, and the deferral can no longer be asked**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Severin — on-page mostly "the master"; spend the name sparingly so it carries weight.
- **Role:** Head of the Temple; the mountain house's one visitor, exempt from the stillness game, unexplained. Persuaded the parents to let Kael attend; sponsor of "the master's charity case."
- **Origin (00 — never told in Book One):** what he was before the Temple, and what he owes this family, is in 00. On the page it exists only as inference: the exemption from the stillness game, the parents' warmth, small tells.
- **Doctrine:** Reform through education (its origin is in 00). WOUND: what he did before the Temple. LIMIT: believes education can outrun rot; the fire tests this.
- **Abilities:** Resonant — EARTH + NATURE dual proficiency, rare, powerful, experienced (00). His grey's drain-reading grows louder and is never confirmed.
- **THE ART *AND* THE MEDICINE (Ch. 19, ratified s22):** nature resonance AND decades of read medicine. The Art alone is worth little — it needs the knowledge in the same head — and he is the only man on the island with both, which is why Neris will have no one else at the birth. Why an old man learned to mend is in 00; the page has not remarked on it.
- **At the games he is the safety and the arbiter (02/temple):** sharp steel and unlimited resonance under one unenforceable clause, and one old man in a chair to catch what falls — earth to block a directly fatal attack, nature to mend even a fatal wound; he stops bouts, interrupts attacks and declares victors. Everyone knows; nobody says it; he does not remark on it either.
- **Manner:** Kindness in valve-form; doors with nothing written on them; keeps his word as far as we ever witness. THE VALVE FAMILY (open by design — author): the liar's knot — gift or test?; the winter-feast fact — table-talk or the door handed over? Both readings alive, perhaps even to him; they accumulate toward the sealed-room endgame (00) without tipping.
- **Appearance:** Old the way the split rock is old; tall, gone lean, a scholar's stoop that once wasn't. Hair fully grey — the second reading never confirmed. **Eyes dark, near black (author ruling, s34).** **SPENT at Ch. 31**, in Valeria's read, as *very dark* — the only dark thing left in that face, giving out precisely nothing; **the exact shade is still unspent** and *near black* has never been on a page. A soldier's frame under a master's robes: wrists too thick for the ink they carry. A face that is courteous, unhurried, and gives out nothing it hasn't priced.

### DYNAMIC
- **ON NO PAGE FROM CH. 39; WHETHER HE GOT UP IS UNANSWERED.** Ten of the sixteen men sent into that house never came out and it was still going on when the governor left the yard. **The only men who could have said are in a city that has changed hands.**
- ***ASK ME THE REST OF IT ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THAT WATER* CAME DUE ON THE SAND AND WAS NOT ASKED.** It stands due and may now be unaskable.
- **THE HULL'S FENCE HELD UNDER DIRECT PRESSURE AND HE HAS NEVER EXPLAINED WHY HE HAD IT.** *"It was not stocked for eight."* — true, and it answers nothing, **and nobody asked him what it had been stocked for.** It was the family's hull, kept dry on their side of the mountain since the autumn and provisioned for four; **nobody on any page knows this but the reader.** The hull is gone.
- **HE SAID *"I know what she is"* AND SOURCED NOTHING**, was never asked to explain it, and it is entirely deniable. **A valve, open by design.**
- **HE SAW WHATEVER WAS IN KAEL'S EYES ON THAT MOUNTAINSIDE AND HAS SAID NOTHING ABOUT IT, THEN OR EVER.**
- **HE SAW NERIS'S EYES COME UP RED AND DID NOT LOOK UP, DID NOT REACT, AND DID NOT ASK** — then took the promise out of the princess on the spot, and tore the strip for the child's eyes before anybody asked him to. **What he knows is unresolved on the page.**
- **HE IS THE SECOND MOUTH.** Kael has heard the downhill language from exactly two mouths before the raiders — his mother's, and once the master's at his own table. **The page names neither and draws no line.**
- **HE SAW THE SMILE, SAID SO, AND SOURCED NOTHING.** He refused to moralise — wrong about people for longer than Kael has been alive, and in both directions — and gave one instruction, *"You watch it"*, once. He believes education can outrun rot, and has met something education did not put there.
- **THE PROTECTION PROMISE STANDS UNQUALIFIED AND HE WILL NEVER KNOW IT FAILED** at a wall. **He reads Halden as no house and nobody's cousin, which is wrong**, and nobody has corrected him.
- **Neither parent ever said why they trusted him** — *"Because he has never once been wrong about us"* / *"He knows us. Truly."* — neither explained, neither refused. **One of the two mouths is now dead.**
- **His Art's spend-state, none of it explained by anybody:** EARTH — one burning stone held and not the next; a mountain's eastern face *released*, not lifted, holding being the expensive direction. NATURE — mending at scale in public; living root and thorn driven eleven feet up through a cut in half a minute. **Each large working leaves him crooked with the nose bleeding**; no new white can show on a man already grey all through, so the never-confirm rule on his grey protects itself.
- **He is spent and it shows:** grey all the way through, gone thin, the scholar's stoop sitting on him as weight; his dark, near-black eyes on the page as *very dark*, giving out nothing; the exact shade unspent.
- **He put the boy's name in front of the empire's soldiers** — *Kael of the Island*, then the island's own word for him — **reporting the name, not conferring it** (`../02-World-Bible/lexicon.md`).
- **Knowledge valves (00):** untouched. The valve family — the liar's knot, the winter-feast fact, *I know what she is* — is open by design and has not tipped.
