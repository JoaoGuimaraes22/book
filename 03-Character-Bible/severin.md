## SEVERIN (the master)
**Glance:** head of the Temple — THE FIRST MASTER; the family's secret savior and patron · empire past, his own man now · old, the way the split rock is old · **the hull is gone — it opened on Qeshet's strand with nobody ever having said whose it was; he gave a governor's clerk his name and his lost Temple, heard a princess lie for the family, and said nothing**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Severin — on-page mostly "the master"; spend the name sparingly so it carries weight.
- **Role:** Head of the Temple; the mountain house's one visitor, exempt from the stillness game, unexplained. Persuaded the parents to let Kael attend; sponsor of "the master's charity case."
- **Origin (00 — never told in Book One):** what he was before the Temple, and what he owes this family, is in 00. On the page it exists only as inference: the exemption from the stillness game, the parents' warmth, small tells.
- **Doctrine:** Reform through education (its origin is in 00). WOUND: what he did before the Temple. LIMIT: believes education can outrun rot; the fire tests this.
- **Abilities:** Resonant — EARTH + NATURE dual proficiency, rare, powerful, experienced (00). His grey's drain-reading grows louder and is never confirmed.
- **THE ART *AND* THE MEDICINE (Ch. 19, ratified s22):** nature resonance AND decades of read medicine. The Art alone is worth little — it needs the knowledge in the same head — and he is the only man on the island with both, which is why Neris will have no one else at the birth. Why an old man learned to mend is in 00; the page has not remarked on it.
- **At the games he is the safety and the arbiter (02/temple):** sharp steel and unlimited resonance under one unenforceable clause, and one old man in a chair to catch what falls — earth to block a directly fatal attack, nature to mend even a fatal wound; he stops bouts, interrupts attacks and declares victors. Everyone knows; nobody says it; he does not remark on it either.
- **Manner:** Kindness in valve-form; doors with nothing written on them; keeps his word as far as we ever witness. THE VALVE FAMILY (open by design — author): the liar's knot — gift or test?; the winter-feast fact — table-talk or the door handed over? Both readings alive, perhaps even to him; they accumulate toward the sealed-room endgame (00) without tipping.
- **Appearance:** Old the way the split rock is old; tall, gone lean, a scholar's stoop that once wasn't. Hair fully grey — the second reading never confirmed. **Eyes dark, near black (author ruling, s34).** **SPENT at Ch. 31**, in Valeria's read, as *very dark* — the only dark thing left in that face, giving out precisely nothing; **the exact shade is still unspent** and *near black* has never been on a page. A soldier's frame under a master's robes: wrists too thick for the ink they carry. A face that is courteous, unhurried, and gives out nothing it hasn't priced.

### DYNAMIC
- **THE HULL IS GONE.** It opened on the sand fifty strides short of Qeshet's strand on the thirtieth day, having sailed them out of range, lost its mast and drifted a month; nobody on any page said whose it was, and now nobody can. *You may ask me the rest of it when we are standing on the other side of that water* **came due on that sand and was not asked.**
- **He taught the boy the boat for thirty days**, twenty words a day, read the drift off the swell and found it agreeing with the boy's stars, chose the sand over the harbour before she went, and gave the number straight again — a day in it, perhaps two. Ashore he gave the clerk *Severin, First Master of the Temple, which is gone.*
- **He heard the princess give Kael a dead boy's name and his mother a house, and said nothing.** At the governor's door, watching the boy count the exits, he had said *You are your mother's. It is not always a compliment* — sourced to nothing.
- **On the sand he put one hand flat on the wet ground and took the child in the other arm** when the watch came down, and took the hand off it when they knelt, and looked at it. He came out of the south's healer crooked, watched her hands, and said *She is good.*
- **He gave the number straight:** four hundred and six that morning, ninety-one in the courtyard by evening, *and I have stopped doing that sum at night, because it does not have an end.*
- **THE HULL'S FENCE HELD UNDER DIRECT PRESSURE, AND HE HAS STILL NOT EXPLAINED WHY HE HAS IT.** Asked how there is so little, he said *"It was not stocked for eight."* — true, and it answers nothing — **and nobody asked him what it had been stocked for.** It is the family's hull, kept dry on their side of the mountain since the autumn, provisioned for four; **nobody on any page knows this but the reader, and nothing joins the two.** His deferral — *"You may ask me the rest of it when we are standing on the other side of that water"* — is unspent, because they are not.
- **He gave the sword back** — told the boy it is far too much sword for him and to keep it where it will not roll — after Vask's widow had handed it up to him without any expression on her face at all.
- **HE STOPPED KAEL FOLLOWING HIS MOTHER AND SAID *"I know what she is."*** — sourced nothing, was never asked to explain it, and it is entirely deniable as *she gave birth an hour ago*. **A valve, open by design: he has not accounted for it, and nobody has asked him to.**
- **HE TOOK THE DELIVERY AND WREN LIVED.** He put a princess of the empire on the lamp, and **when the mother's eyes came up red he did not look up, did not react, and did not ask** — then took the promise out of the girl on the spot, and tore the strip for the child's eyes and held it out before anybody asked him to. 00's who-else-knows valve moved without opening. **What he knows is unresolved on the page.**
- **HE SAW WHATEVER WAS IN KAEL'S EYES ON THAT MOUNTAINSIDE AND SAID NOTHING ABOUT IT, THEN OR EVER.** He turned the boy bodily round to look, put the mending Art the other way round into him and took his legs, and did not knock him out. It is unexplained, and he has not raised it.
- **He promised Vask and he kept it** — *I have them, Vask.* — twice, because he was made to say it twice. He owned it out loud beforehand — *I have brought all of this onto your mountain. I know what that is* — and got four words back.
- **His Art's spend-state on the page, and none of it is explained by anybody:** EARTH — one burning stone stopped in the air over the children and not the next (he could hold one; he could not hold two), and later the eastern face of a mountain *released*, not lifted — the lazy place under four hundred feet of terrace taken away, holding being the expensive direction and releasing the cheap one. NATURE — mending at scale in public with hands not entirely steady; Kael's ribs; Neris; and living root and thorn driven eleven feet up through the floor and walls of a cut in half a minute. Each large working leaves him crooked with the nose bleeding; **no new white can show on a man already grey all through, so the never-confirm rule on his grey protects itself and nobody remarks on it.**
- **Nobody fetched him to the labour.** He spent the arena morning walking four hundred students toward the northern arch and not looking up again, argued the district into evacuating and was outreached until a house said it after him, was given the party, went on ahead of it without saying why, and reached the house hours late, of his own accord, at the head of fifteen people, because the road to his hull crosses that mountain. **His own decision, made on the page and unremarked.**
- **He put the boy's name in front of the empire's soldiers** — *Kael of the Island*, then the island's own word for him — to make a captain of the royal guard take a thirteen-year-old's arithmetic seriously. **Reporting the name, not conferring it** (`../02-World-Bible/lexicon.md`).
- **He is the second mouth.** Kael has heard the downhill language from exactly two mouths in his life before the raiders — his mother's and, once, the master's at his own table. The page names neither and draws no line. **Unexplained.**
- **He saw the smile and said so, and sourced nothing.** He is the one man alive who knows what those two people were, and therefore what they did and did not put in the boy; he refused to moralise — wrong about people for longer than Kael has been alive, and in both directions, which way unresolved — and gave one instruction, *"You watch it"*, once. He believes education can outrun rot, and has met something education did not put there. **Unremarked on the page.**
- **THE PROTECTION PROMISE FAILED AT A WALL AND HE WILL NEVER KNOW.** Given unqualified and slightly too confident — *"you are not standing in it by yourself. Not you, and not your house."* — and quoted whole by Kael through an eighteen-inch gap to the one man in the empire it cannot reach. Which of 00's four readings this lands on is untouched; **the promise stands unqualified.** He reads Halden as no house and nobody's cousin, which is **wrong, and he does not know it** — nobody has corrected him.
- **Neither parent ever said why they trusted him** — *"Because he has never once been wrong about us"* / *"He knows us. Truly."* — two reasons, neither explained, neither refused; what either of them is stays unresolved. One of the two mouths is now dead.
- **He is spent and it shows:** grey all the way through, gone thin, the scholar's stoop sitting on him as weight; his dark, near-black eyes are on the page as *very dark* — the one dark thing left in that face, giving out nothing — and the exact shade is still unspent.
- **Knowledge valves (00):** untouched. The valve family — the liar's knot, the winter-feast fact, *I know what she is* — is open by design and has not tipped.
