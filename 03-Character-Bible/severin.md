## SEVERIN (the master)
**Glance:** head of the Temple — THE FIRST MASTER; the family's secret savior and patron · empire past, his own man now · old, the way the split rock is old · **outreached inside his own Temple; the protection promise given whole — and already keeping it before it was made.**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Severin — on-page mostly "the master"; spend the name sparingly so it carries weight.
- **Role:** Head of the Temple; the mountain house's one visitor, exempt from the stillness game, unexplained. Persuaded the parents to let Kael attend; sponsor of "the master's charity case."
- **Origin (00 — never told in Part I):** old empire enforcer sent to hunt the fleeing parents; they spared and saved him; the island placement is the debt repaid. Empire man, NOT program man.
- **Doctrine:** Reform through education — the Temple as the parents' mercy institutionalized. WOUND: what he did as an enforcer. LIMIT: believes education can outrun rot; the fire tests this.
- **Abilities:** Resonant — EARTH + NATURE dual proficiency, rare, powerful, experienced (00). Demonstrations stay candle-scale; his grey's drain-reading grows louder and is never confirmed.
- **THE ART *AND* THE MEDICINE (Ch. 19, ratified s22):** he carries nature resonance AND decades of read medicine. The art alone is worth little — it needs the knowledge in the same head — and he is the only man on the island with both, which is why Neris will have no one else at the birth. Quietly deepens the atonement doctrine: the old enforcer went and learned to mend. Never remark on it.
- **Manner:** Kindness in valve-form; doors with nothing written on them; keeps his word as far as we ever witness. THE VALVE FAMILY (do not resolve): the liar's knot — gift or test?; the winter-feast fact — table-talk or the door handed over? Both readings alive, perhaps even to him; they accumulate toward the sealed-room endgame (00) without tipping.
- **Appearance:** Old the way the split rock is old; tall, gone lean, a scholar's stoop that once wasn't. Hair fully grey — the second reading never confirmed. An enforcer's frame under a master's robes: wrists too thick for the ink they carry. A face that is courteous, unhurried, and gives out nothing it hasn't priced.

### DYNAMIC
- **As of Ch. 19 (off-page).** The mountain's entire plan is built out of him and he is not in the room: he will attend the birth (the art and the medicine, above), and he will find the hull — not one with the family's faces in a ledger, something of his own, kept dry on their own side of the mountain. **He arranged it before anybody at that table asked him to**, which is what turns Ch. 18's protection promise from a kind thing said to a frightened boy into a description of work already done. Kael, who has thought a great deal of him since he was nine, thinks a great deal more. Neither parent will say why they trust him: Vask offers *"Because he has never once been wrong about us"* and Neris answers *"That is not why"* — two different old reasons, neither spoken in front of their son. Valves untouched.
- **As of Ch. 18.** **HIS FIRST ON-PAGE FAILURE.** Spent a morning learning he can do nothing about the entry, and told the boy so without softening it: the Temple is his, the tournament is not; entries are lodged to the games under seal and he cannot strike a name off a document that is not his. Delivered the jurisdiction canon (02) and "He is the empire, Kael. Not a house." **PAID THE OWED FLASH-READ AS A REFUSAL TO ASK** — carried since the spring, chose every day not to say it, said it now: he will not ask what it was, and somebody in that colonnade did not need an easy answer. **Gave the protection promise unqualified** ("you are not standing in it by yourself. Not you, and not your house."). Reads Halden as no house, nobody's cousin — **WRONG, and does not know it** (03/halden sealed truth); never correct him on the page. Knowledge valves (00) untouched. Previously (Ch. 14): announced the tournament publicly; counseled the parents against sitting still ("how he goes. Held, or loose." — the concession door planted, unresolved); reads Kael's blank book at the termly summons.
- **The exhibition:** he rose at the threading slash and was told "No" by a guest. **His read of the flash is now SPENT (Ch. 18)** — three chapters owed, paid as cause rather than audit.
- **Knowledge valves (00):** untouched.
