## SEVERIN (the master)
- **Name (CANON):** Severin — between registers, as he is between worlds. On-page mostly "the master"; use the name sparingly so it carries weight.
- **Role:** head of the Temple; the family's secret savior and patron; the one visitor the mountain house ever receives — exempt from the stillness game, unexplained. Persuaded the parents to let Kael attend ("You saved him from the empire. Who saves him from the mountain?"); sponsor of "the master's charity case."
- **Origin (SEALED — see 00, never told in Part I):** old empire enforcer sent to hunt the fleeing parents; they spared and saved him; the island placement is the debt repaid. Empire man, NOT program man.
- **RESONANT (canon, session 14 — see 00):** Severin is a Resonant — EARTH + NATURE dual proficiency (rare), very powerful and very experienced. His element/capacity never made explicit on-page in early Part II (Ch. 10 shows the fact of his art — the fern — and seeds the nature flavor deniably: the sill of green pots); earth/nature is what he will mostly use whenever the future shows him in action. Demonstrations stay candle-scale (beat 18's first-magic-as-spectacle reserve untouched). His grey's drain-reading grows louder and is STILL never confirmed.
- **Doctrine:** reform through education — the Temple project as the parents' mercy institutionalized; he believes the empire's next generation can be taught into something better than their parents.
- **Character Law kit:** WOUND — what he did as an enforcer. DOCTRINE — teaching as atonement. LIMIT — believes education can outrun rot; the fire tests this.
- **On-page:** keeps his word as far as we ever witness. To Kael: the best day of the year — the one door that opens, the man who brings new things (books, questions) up the mountain. The parents' WARMTH toward him is itself a planted clue: the two most controlled people in the book become slightly different people for one visitor. **Ch. 5:** the arranged absences are over; the autumn visit delivers THE WINTER-FEAST FACT — offered sideways, "like the knot had been."
