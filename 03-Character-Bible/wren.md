## WREN (the brother)
**Glance:** Kael's brother; second carrier of the bloodline · **BORN — hours old, on the page, with a strip of linen bound across his eyes** · **on no page since the night at Qeshet; his age is no longer counted on the page — *a child of that age takes milk from somebody*, and his brother hoped aloud that somebody is feeding him; his eyes explained to his brother as *something in my blood … in him now***

### STATIC
- **Name:** Wren — the mother teaches languages "for the birds"; her second son is a bird. (Why the name weighs what it weighs: 00.)
- **Role:** Born at the end of Act One; second carrier of the bloodline through Neris — the brother's eyes are a series-long door (00). Kael holds him perhaps once.
- **Appearance (decided; 00 s20):** his mother's dark hair — and **HIS EYES ARE PERMANENTLY RED.** Born with the bloodline OPEN: massive capacity from birth, uncontrolled. His eyes resolve to the BLOODLINE, not to a parent: Kael's grey belongs to nobody, Wren's red to something older than anybody (the rest is in 00). A newborn carrying the devil-eyes of the folklore CANNOT BE HIDDEN — consequences flagged in 00, none of them designed.

### DYNAMIC
- **ON NO PAGE FROM CH. 39; ALIVE OR NOT IS UNKNOWN.** He was not one of the three children carried out of that house; where he went and with whom are on no page (`../07-Story-Ledger/open-threads.md`). **His age is no longer counted on the page** (from Ch. 54); his brother's one hope said aloud is that he is alive and fed (Ch. 55).
- **HIS EYES ARE RED AND THEY ARE THE FIRST RED EYES IN THE MANUSCRIPT.** Kael got ONE of them, for a moment, when the linen rode up, **and nobody in the room was surprised by anything. Nobody has said the bloodline's name.**
- **HIS BROTHER HAS BEEN TOLD WHAT IS UNDER THE LINEN, AS FAR AS THEIR MOTHER WILL SAY IT:** the red is *something in my blood*, old, hers, *it came to him through me. It is in him now* — born with it where hers is hidden by will and control, and she knows little of it.
- **THE LINEN HAS BEEN EXPLAINED TO THE EMPIRE, IN WRITING, AS A LIE:** damage from a bad birth, the light hurting them, bound until she says otherwise — and it is going to the capital under the name Drusane. **The answer to *a newborn with devil-eyes cannot be hidden* is on the page as an ATTEMPT and now a LIE, not a solution**; how long a bound child can be carried through the world is open (00). Nobody has seen his eyes since the mountain.
- **THE DELIVERY WAS A MORTAL EVENT** — a birth of the bloodline is violent, and this child was born extremely potent. It is why Severin had to attend and why the family could not leave the island before the spring. **Kael was told none of that.**
- **His brother has held him properly** — two hands, daylight — counts his breathing until it makes a shape, and the child's hand shuts on his finger and stays shut.
- He cries, he is hot, he weighs about as much as a lamb, and he has a fingernail. **He cried on an open mountain with men hunting the people carrying him, for a hundred and forty seconds, stopped on his own, and not one person said one word about it.**
