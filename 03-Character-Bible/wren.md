## WREN (the brother)
**Glance:** Kael's brother; second carrier of the Kynemir bloodline · the mountain house · not yet born, as of Ch. 21 · off-page.

### STATIC
- **Name:** Wren — the mother teaches languages "for the birds"; her second son is a bird. Chosen with the full weight of the names-as-rebellion canon (00).
- **Role:** Born at the end of Act One; second carrier of the bloodline through Neris — the brother's eyes are a series-long door (00). Kael holds him perhaps once.
- **Appearance (decided; 00 s20):** his mother's dark hair — and **HIS EYES ARE PERMANENTLY RED.** Born with the bloodline OPEN: massive capacity from birth, uncontrolled, unlike Neris, who can switch hers off. He remains the only child of both by blood, but his eyes resolve to the BLOODLINE, not to a parent: Kael's grey belongs to nobody, Wren's red to something older than anybody. A newborn carrying the devil-eyes of the folklore CANNOT BE HIDDEN — consequences flagged in 00, none of them designed.

### DYNAMIC
- Not yet born. **The pregnancy is on-page** (Neris, ~3 months, plainly told); Kael knows a sibling is coming, does not know which, and has asked to hold it.
- **The delivery is a mortal event** (00 s22 — a Kynemir birth is violent and this child will be born extremely potent). It is why Severin must attend and why the family cannot leave the island before the spring. **Kael is told none of that.**

### FORWARD
- **Status:** gone with Neris and Severin. The master will likely rename him; the birth name becomes a cipher only Kael carries — recognition beat banked for the endgame (00). A series-long door.
