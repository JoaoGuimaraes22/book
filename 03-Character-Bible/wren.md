## WREN (the brother)
**Glance:** Kael's brother; second carrier of the bloodline · **BORN — hours old, on the page, with a strip of linen bound across his eyes** · a day and a night old, on a drifting hull, with the linen still across his eyes and his brother's finger in his fist.

### STATIC
- **Name:** Wren — the mother teaches languages "for the birds"; her second son is a bird. (Why the name weighs what it weighs: 00.)
- **Role:** Born at the end of Act One; second carrier of the bloodline through Neris — the brother's eyes are a series-long door (00). Kael holds him perhaps once.
- **Appearance (decided; 00 s20):** his mother's dark hair — and **HIS EYES ARE PERMANENTLY RED.** Born with the bloodline OPEN: massive capacity from birth, uncontrolled. His eyes resolve to the BLOODLINE, not to a parent: Kael's grey belongs to nobody, Wren's red to something older than anybody (the rest is in 00). A newborn carrying the devil-eyes of the folklore CANNOT BE HIDDEN — consequences flagged in 00, none of them designed.

### DYNAMIC
- **A day and a night old, aboard a drifting hull, with a strip of linen bound across his eyes.** Nobody on that boat has touched the linen and nobody has explained it. The answer to *a newborn with devil-eyes cannot be hidden* is on the page as an ATTEMPT, not a solution; how long a bound child can be carried through the world is open (00).
- **His eyes are red and they are the first red eyes in the manuscript.** Kael got ONE of them, for a moment, when the linen rode up, and nobody in the room was surprised by anything. Nobody has said the bloodline's name.
- **Named on the page by his mother:** *"His name is Wren."* Delivered by Severin, with a princess of the empire holding the lamp, in a room with a fight against the wall of it; his mother's own control failed in the delivery, and Kael did not see that and must not.
- **His brother has held him properly now** — two hands, daylight, no barrage — counts his breathing until it makes a shape, and his hand shuts on Kael's finger and stays shut. Before that: one-armed through the whole of a barrage, screaming the length of it and stopping when it stopped; handed to the princess twice, the second time because she asked.
- He cries, he is hot, he weighs about as much as a lamb, and he has a fingernail. He cried on an open mountain with men hunting the people carrying him, for a hundred and forty seconds, stopped on his own, and not one person said one word about it.
- **The delivery was a mortal event** (a birth of the bloodline is violent, and this child was born extremely potent) — it is why Severin had to attend and why the family could not leave the island before the spring. **Kael was told none of that.**

### FORWARD
- **Status:** aboard the drifting hull, eyes still bound. **He is not separated from Kael in Book One** (author, s49). What comes after is in 00; a series-long door.
