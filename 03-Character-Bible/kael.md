## KAEL (protagonist)
**Glance:** POV protagonist · no house — the herald calls him **"of the Island"**, and the island calls him **THE GHOST** to his face · **13** · **in ISHARETH, working a mule yard under his own single name, with the governor inside a stone building he cannot get into and sixty-three bought men round it — and he has found the one thing in that city big enough to open it, and is walking to its door**

### STATIC
- **Role:** POV character; hidden son of Vask (ex-instrument) and Neris (ex-asset) of the program. Carrier of the rare bloodline through his mother (what it is: 00). Surname unknown until the Temple.
- **Want:** Begins with wanting to touch the village lights, understand, and be measured. Evolves into a hunger for success, fame, and acknowledgment — to be noticed, measured, and remarked upon. He never sandbags. The coat hides what he is, never that he is good. Success therefore attracts both noble attention and peer resentment. Valeria eventually becomes the first to be his measure. He wants to learn about the world, go beyond the sea.
- **Wound:** Raised in loving secrecy; the world forbidden; parented through preparation instead of presence. At nine he discovers the founding promise was a lie and absorbs it silently — the first wound he carries alone. The settlement (Ch. 6) is his first impression of humanity: foundational, series-long trauma — distrust of strangers, crowds, touch, and group laughter (rendered as small physical tells, never labeled). It sits under the social blindness: his awkwardness is ignorance plus fear, and combat is his only social language with rules. He misfiles the night's defeat as his own insufficiency when in truth only the operator broke. His conscience is the one faculty nobody built — the series' moral spine. Left-forearm blade scar, stitched by Neris, echoing Vask's tally-marks; never explained.
- **APPETITE WITHOUT DESTINATION (ratified s20 — the arc's engine).** His want (success, measure, acknowledgment) is APPETITE, not purpose. He works the way he breathes, because he has always worked, and it has never once been going anywhere. Everyone around him answers with a *because* — a house, a name, a Line — and where his because should be there is only the work. **This is 01's thesis stated as a wound** (no destiny handed down), (where it goes is in 00). Beginning to make him want to WANT. Explored first with Elarine (full but heavy vs. empty but light — both burdens), later and deeper with Valeria (nothing but purpose, and it is not hers).
- **Trait:** CURIOSITY. Voracious love of learning; the itch that cannot be out-waited is the one urge stillness training cannot defeat. Parent-cultivated, rule-breaking, and exactly what the Temple prizes.
- **Trait:** THE CONCEALMENT INHERITANCE. From age nine he keeps secrets from his parents using their own schools, lovingly and in their voice — the seed of the Temple concealment and the series-long becoming-them thread.
- **Flaw:** SOCIAL BLINDNESS. Cannot read cues, hierarchy, mockery, or alliances; answers rhetorical questions literally. He detects the WHAT (anger, a lie, a turned room) and misreads the WHY (06 §Hard Rules). **Book One ran it as an always; from Book Two it moves (author, s59)** — where it has moved and what still blocks it is state (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md` §THE ROAD EAST); the destination is 00's.
- **Abilities:** Technician, not powerhouse. LIGHTNING proficiency, unusually even generalist spread beneath it; LOW raw capacity; exceptional control, technique, tactics, threading, creativity — small, exact, fast, never bolts. True defining axis: intelligence + technique + fieldcraft + exceptional martial skill; the empire's capacity-worship is loudly wrong about him (emphasis rule in 06). His style is his parents' synthesis — father's economy of motion through mother's economy of power.
- **THE COUNT — WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS (said out loud for the first time at Ch. 47).** Not arithmetic he performs: **he takes in the whole of what is in front of him, involuntarily, and it stays** — *I see what is in front of me. All of it. And it stays.* The numbers are the handle he holds it by, and they are of three kinds in his own account — **amounts** (eight boards, two trees, twenty soldiers), **time** (how long a man took to throw a spear, how long he looked at a face) and **distances** (how long a road is, how much space a room has). **The useless keeps too** — a whole wall he walked past at six, against not knowing his father's mother's name because nobody said it in front of him. **HE CANNOT STOP IT** (*there is nothing to try; it is not a thing I begin*), and **he believes it is ordinary** — nobody had ever asked him, so he had never had to find the words. The page has never named it and there is no term for it anywhere in the world. **It is the mechanism under the what/why split:** total intake, and the wrong conclusions drawn from it.
- **Method:** Runs controlled trials on his own initiative — asking twice with one thing changed. His empiricism against the nobles' capacity-worship is a Part II engine (00 has more). First proof on the page: the earth-step counter (Ch. 16) — he solved Valeria's ground-sense by trial and attacked the MEDIUM rather than the sense, knowledge standing in where he has no capacity; she could not answer it (mechanics: `../02-World-Bible/magic.md` §Blind Spot).
- **Lexicon:** Names his own disciplines and coins terms for discoveries the world lacks; the lexicon grows across the series. Holds "I don't know" as a tool.
- **Voice:** Matures with the prose (06).
- **Appearance:** **A handsome boy, and filthy with it (author-ruled, s33)** — dark hair (his mother's), clean features under village dirt and weather, hair cut by somebody who was not being careful. Sun-weathered and wind-chapped from work at altitude, with **freckles across the nose** under the weather and the grime (ruled s35; unspent on any page). Lean, hard and visibly fit; strong without bulk, the strength sitting in the forearms and the hands. **He is not plain and never was: what makes him invisible is STATUS and STILLNESS, not his face** — no house, no name, working cloth, and a body trained not to move (the ornate royal register is Aeliana's; `appearance-ledger.md`). Eyes a pale grey belonging to no one in the house (00): the parents never remark on them, not once — their silence is the tell that never tells; the grey arrives only through other mouths or other eyes, no mirrors, no self-description; unspent on the page, and it stays so for now — **it comes to be important much later (author, s52), and no Temple mouth is owed**; **IMAGES ARE EXEMPT (author ruling, s42): plates may show the grey, so a mouth that names it CONFIRMS for a reader who has seen them rather than revealing** (`08-Plates/README.md`). Left forearm: the blade scar, near his father's tally-marks in site and length. Marked older than his face admits. What others react to: nothing — which is the point, and slowly becomes the thing they react to.

### DYNAMIC
- **HE IS TRADING, NOT BEGGING, AND HE KNOWS WHAT HE IS ABOUT TO DO.** Four days of work in Ishareth got him a wall — the bridge-house, one door on the street and one onto the bridge, sixty-three men and rising, paid off a table out of a chest fed by the river — *He is not spending a purse. He is spending a river* — and a sum with no door in it. Then three facts that are not about each other gave him the crew. **What he brings them is not a plea:** sixty-two days of the inside of that man's arrangements, a Vel, three Resonants, and a girl whose face is the reason the whole thing exists. ***It was a trade. That was the honest word for it, and he made himself use it.*** **And the part of him that would have argued about handing three lives to strangers did not say one word, and he noticed that it had not** — the second time since the fold, and nothing resolves it.
- **HE EXPLAINED THE COUNT TO SOMEBODY.** The first person in thirteen years to ask him what it is; he had no words ready and found them badly and completely (§STATIC). He thinks everybody does it. She told him they do not, and he did not entirely take it. **The one place it went that he did not follow:** *"Then when you say you have put a thing down, what is it that you have actually done with it." / "Stopped taking it out." / "That is not the same as it being gone." / "No."*
- **HE IS THE ONE WHO GOES OUT, AND THE ONLY NAME HE HAS IS HIS OWN.** *Kael*, and nothing after it, because nobody in that city has anything after it — and the one full name he has ever owned is in the capital's post under a governor's seal and is the most dangerous word in his mouth. He works Zahir's yard for the water, the loads and the feet, is cheap and does not talk, and **never asks a question he wants the answer to** — you ask the one beside it and let the man arrive at it himself, which is his mother's and came to him at nine dressed as something else.
- **HE TAUGHT AGAIN, AND HEARD WHOSE VOICE IT WAS.** Ivrit on the road to both of them, and the food of the ground to the princess in his mother's own words and his mother's method — slowly, then at speed, then not at all, and wait. He gave her the poison procedure whole because she argued for it and was right, and it was the argument he had used on his own mother twice.
- **HE SPENT THE DEAD MEN'S COIN.** Cut out of three hems on his knees in the flat place above Doresh with the light going, carried in the sacking with the iron, **and never mentioned to either of them.** It bought the room and the bread, and the man who took it for the bread put it between his teeth first, and Kael watched him do it.
- **HE CAME IN WITHOUT BEATING ANYTHING.** You do not beat a gate; you arrive inside something it has already decided to let through. Thirty-eight mules, the hoof named before anything was asked for, and a toll gate that counts animals and loads and therefore cannot see people. **He used a small version of the crew's whole method nine days before he had heard of them, and no page says so.**
- **HE CARRIES THE IRON EAST AND HAS NOT SAID WHY.** Five bands, a chain and the coin, on his back every day of eleven, up a road, one-handed.
- **A THING HE PUT DOWN DID NOT STAY DOWN.** He killed nineteen men in a fold and the sum comes out right from every end he takes it from — *He had been right. He was still right* — and they were also **bought men** who sew coins into a hem, one of whom had a roof that had been done before the rain. Both are true and neither moves the other. *He put it down anyway, because putting things down was the only thing he had ever learned to do with them. / It did not stay down.* **The mechanism below has failed him once; nothing on the page resolves it, and nobody knows.**
- **HE KILLED FIVE MEN WHO COULD NOT GET UP, AND THE FIRST ONE WENT WRONG.** The officer on the track with the knife, in under the angle of the jaw; then four in the holes — the first from above at a bad angle, the stroke turned over the collarbone and doing nothing except make him worse and set him screaming, the second attempt weight on the wrist through his own splint and the point pushed up and held; the other three one each. **Making sure is two fingers on the side of the neck and waiting**, and there were two he had to go back for. **The part of him that would have argued did not say one word, and he noticed that it had not.**
- **HE BOUGHT THE MAN WHO WENT TO SELL THEM.** He did not stop Tarim; he agreed with his arithmetic — the same one he had said out loud under that floor — corrected the one term in it, showed him **the Art** as proof at the cost of a hard breath, told him a half-truth about how strong the two girls are, and spent a promise that was not his. He staked forty houses on his own choice of ground to pay a debt he could have walked away from.
- **HE SAYS *VAL*.** Three times now, and **nobody has remarked on it.**
- **HE HAS A PLACE AND A MAN AT THE END OF THE ROAD.** The governor of Qeshet is at **ISHARETH**, buying *as many as the letter he wrote will pay for*, with **one bridge and it is his** — and **he told nobody on that road anything**, which means Kael's oldest question cannot be got from any of them. He is walking toward him, and he said out loud why: the governor is the only thread to his mother, his brother, the old man, **and Elarine and Aurelian**, and *there is not going to be a second one.*
- **THE ART ANSWERS AGAIN.** Two fingers on a smith's anvil, a thread into the iron, and it went — the first answer anything has given him in seventy days, at about what a hard breath costs. The iron is off all three of them; **the black in the grooves is still unexplained and a smith of twenty-two years did not know it either**; the bands are under that forge floor in Doresh, taken as the only fee he had and kept as evidence. **At the end of it he did not count one single thing.**
- **HE BUILT A STORY AND THE WORD IN IT TURNED ON HIM.** *We are orphans, and we were taken* — no name and nobody to send word to, because *a father is a name, and a name has a house on the end of it, and a house is somewhere a letter goes*, which he learned at a governor's table; and the iron is the part a village believes. **Saying it caught him**: his father dead and never a story, his mother the sum he stopped building in the fourth week, and nobody anywhere he can ask. **Nothing was settled and he went on.**
- **HIS FIELDCRAFT WORKED AND HIS BODY DID NOT.** He read the smith off a banked fire and a shed roof and put them over the wall onto ground that takes no print — *the last thing in him that still ran without being asked* — and then the door was under his hand and the ground was against his face with nothing joining them, and *the plan had been him.* **He was carried in.** He holds a double knock on wood and two open hands in iron as fragments he cannot attribute, and *it did not come back to him for a very long time*; Valeria's report leaves both out.
- **HE WAS GIVEN THINGS HE COULD NOT PRICE.** He costed the pot and the sacks against the wall to the day and could not settle the sum; nobody at that table looked at what anybody else was given; and a village that had been offered every roof in it and threatened with *any house* was asked at forty doors and **not one of them opened its mouth** — he counted the three quarters of an hour from under a floor and had the number and nothing to do with it.
- **HE TAUGHT SOMEBODY SOMETHING FOR THE FIRST TIME.** The princess asked to be taught Ivrit and he did it, and heard himself do it the way his mother did — slowly, then at speed, then not at all, and wait. He gave her her own price under the floor when she asked for it, all of it, including *any house*, and told her *I would give us up.*
- **HE GAVE A STRANGER HIS OWN NAME.** A four-year-old asked and he said *Kael*, inside a lie, and she said it back wrong.
- **HE HAS A PLACE AT THE END OF THE ROAD NOW: ISHARETH**, six or seven days east where the ground goes down to a river — the last of the empire, and past the river not — off a smith who has shod what goes up that road for twenty-two years. He took the name and the number.
- **HE READ THE FAMILY RIGHT AND THE PERSON WRONG.** Tarim gave up his bed, carried the healer's water, ran a mile to warn them — and stood at the forge door with a bucket for a second and a half after the scarf came off. Kael priced him off six days of kindness and put it down. **He was not in the forge on the second night, and Kael thought a man who has been up two nights running sends his son to bed.**
- **HIS HAND IS MENDING AND ITS FUTURE IS STILL OPEN.** Opened, drained, packed with honey, splinted at the thumb, and kept up; the healer would not guess at it — *you may get a hand that shuts* — and put the answer at the spring. She wanted ten days and he had six.
- **HE TORTURED A BOUND MAN, AND HIS FATHER'S BODY-SCHOOL WENT OUTWARD FOR THE FIRST TIME.** Feverish, in massive pain, he questioned the prisoner in Ivrit for the ground and the village and **kept the count on the man's own pulse** — forty counts held first, so the truth of it could measure the rest — to stay clinical and stay himself; *he was doing it without deciding to, out of a school with no first day.* Against a first lie he got **the Kharoun** and **Doresh**. Then **the desperation surfaced:** he overstepped the list onto the house and his mother, read the man's flat true pulse saying he knows nothing, **and did it anyway for one second before he stopped** — *I believe you*, two words too late. **The page passes no verdict and resolves it into neither justification nor self-horror.** The what/why holds — he read the body perfectly; the faculty nobody built did not stop him. **Valeria killed the prisoner unasked and he let her**, knowing he had the half-second and *did not use it because it was the right thing and because he could not have made himself, and did not resolve it.*
- **HE SET THE GROUND AND THEY KILLED THE SEARCH PARTY TOGETHER.** Off the road north into trees on the cold side of the Kharoun, he set one crossing, one gap, a bank — as his father taught — and the three of them took twelve men (seven in on foot, five with the horses): Valeria two from cover, Kael the third from the bank (the neck, the jar up the ruined arm), Valeria the rest; **the princess put her knife into the leg of the man they kept, and did not let go.** **Six dead, one kept.** **His Ch. 41 count of twelve held.** The party could not spear into cover where the princess might be — *she is not to be marked* — which is part of why three chained children beat seven.
- **HE HAS A PLAN, IN ORDER:** where they are (the Kharoun) · a village (Doresh, half a day north, down the water, a smith) · **a smith to cut the iron** · a healer. The iron is still on all three of them and nothing in the party's kit opened it.
- **HIS HAND IS A FEVER.** Into cold running water for the first time (the relief turned to white pain, and Valeria held the wrist under and counted him); the back skinned and going a colour that travels, **a red line up the inside of the forearm** — his mother's lamb-lesson, *the body deciding to spend the whole animal to save part of it, and it is nearly always wrong* — hot through his shirt. **He priced it at days without a healer.** Whether it works again is still on no page.
- **HE CARRIES A SOLDIER'S SWORD NOW.** His own mismatched pair gone since the box (Ch. 39), his father's longsword he cannot use — a plain soldier's sword he manages one-handed, belted where it wants no hand; Valeria has one too and is better with an edge than any man it came off (author, s64); three knives kept, the spears left.
- **THE COUNT IS THE ANCHOR.** With the hand and the fever pulling at him he keeps it on strides, on the man's pulse, and at the last on **the princess's steps** going down — *the only number he has that goes up.*
- **HE KILLED THE MAN ON THE ROPE WITH HIS TEETH.** Both hands were spent — the left a ruin, the right in iron on a chain — so the right took the hair and pulled the head back and round and the mouth did the rest. *He had thought about the front of it. It was the side.* He was underneath most of a grown man with nothing to brace with and did not try.
- **HIS WHOLE JOB WAS THE KNIFE INSIDE HER REACH, AND HE DID IT.** Off the dead man's belt with the same hand, into Valeria's two hands — *She had them up and open before it arrived* — and after that he was a boy on a floor: an arm round an ankle and a shoulder into the back of a knee, and then the ruined hand flat over a mouth while Kesir bit through the palm. **He has no illusions about the size of what he is worth now**, and carried the coat and the water and nothing else up the stone.
- **HE WAS NOT THE ONE ANYBODY WATCHED, AND IT IS WHY THEY ARE ALIVE.** Sixty-two days of fifty men correctly identifying the dangerous one. **Nobody on the page says so.**
- **HE DID NOT SEE HER DO IT.** Valeria's three arrived as sound through an open door while he held a mouth shut: the bar going up out of the frame, feet losing the frost, a weight going down flat. *Nine. He had counted it without meaning to.*
- **HE BARRED THE DOOR ON THEM.** Said before he decided to say it, and done with her arms, so that from ten strides there was a black box with its door shut. **Nobody in that camp knew at first light.**
- **HE HAS THE PURSUIT AT TWELVE, AND THE REASON IS THE ONE HE HAS WRONG.** *Not fifty* — ten or twelve, mounted, with somebody who can read ground, behind them before the middle of the day, because a man who watches the road behind him will not strip it. **Nothing has corrected him.**
- **THE IRON IS STILL ON HIM AND ON BOTH OF THEM.** No key, no chisel, no fire; the Art is unanswered and he does not try it. She held her wrists up and let him look, and neither of them said anything about it.
- **HE HAS NOT GOT TO THE PRINCESS.** She stood up and the doorway turned to her and that is the whole reason he was not seen, and he has not assembled it. She is gagged no longer and has not explained it; **the first word out of her in two months was his own name**, not the one in the letter, and he answered the sentence after it and not the name.
- **SIXTY-TWO DAYS IN THE BOX, AND HE HAS NOT LOST THE DAY.** The days off the light in the slot, the moon as an independent second witness, the direction off the sun; east, and up more than down. *A man who has lost the count can be told anything.* He spends the twice-a-day door — *a little over an hour of looking at the world* in two months — on ground, growth, stone, tracks and faces.
- **HE DESTROYED HIS OWN LEFT HAND TO GET IT OUT OF THE IRON.** The thumb broken at the base, the bone below the little finger folded inward against the ring's bracket by pressure because a blow is a noise, a second splintered, and the back of the hand skinned from knuckles to wrist when the last pull was not enough and he pulled harder. **He made no sound.** He has no name for what his body did afterward — *He had a name for every kind of pain his father's school had ever handed him … He had none at all for this* — and Valeria counted him back to about sixty, three times. **Whether the hand works again is on no page.**
- **IT BOUGHT A HAND FOR THE KNOTS, NOT THE ART.** The right band stays on and nothing answers; he does not try it. *A knot wants two hands: one to hold the thing still, one to work it* — his right was in iron on the rope he had to untie, **so the holding was the ruin's**: an hour and a half at the ring, let go four times, out once; the ankles half an hour more. He is armed with an empty band and eleven links on his right wrist.
- **HE DIVIDED THE WATCH AND SHE TOOK IT.** *"Valeria. You should sleep." … I'll keep track of all of it … I'll need your full strength when the time comes to go.* She did not argue. **He filed it, knew that something had happened, and did not have the size of it**, and never once checked on her, and did not put that in words either.
- **HE READS PEOPLE BETTER NOW.** Eight weeks, nine faces out of a column of fifty and no other work: he has the box's eight, he had the ninth voice as the governor's by the second week, and on the eighteenth day he took back his own verdict on Kesir off his own watching — *He could not remember the last time that had happened.* **Two misses stand.** The governor's why needs a fact from a house he was carried out of unconscious. The princess's why was three feet away for two months and never once occurred to him, and the page says it was **not** that he could not see it.
- **WREN IS THREE MONTHS OLD AND HE HAS THAT TO THE DAY.** A baby of three months takes milk from somebody, so either the child is with his mother, or with somebody who found a way, or nowhere at all; the sum on his mother has two ends and no way to choose, and he stopped building it. *The last of him he had was a weight inside his coat in a passage, asleep, with his fists shut.*
- **HE PRICED HIMSELF AND FOUND THE LIE UNDER IT.** The girl is worth more than anything on the road; a Vel is not a thing you come by; and he is worth something because a princess told a governor to write him down a Drusane — *Maybe if she had not, he would not yet be alive.*
- **HE WOKE IN A BOX.** The count was running before he was and two of its numbers were gone; iron on his wrists with black in the grooves, rope to a ring, bare feet, boots and coat and blades taken. He tried the Art and *for the first time in his life nothing answered*; he worked out on the page that *Iron alone does not stop the Art* and that it must be the black paint. He learned the iron and not the way out: *When they take us out. And... it might take a lot to do it.*
- **HE DOES NOT KNOW WHETHER THEY ARE ALIVE.** His mother, the child, the old man — one box on the road and nothing behind it, so *they were not here*; *Then they were in the house, or they were somewhere else, or they were nowhere*, and he could not finish that. The last thing he has of her is *Stay awake, Kael*, five, and his name in her voice. His heart went and *this time it did not finish, and he could not put it anywhere.*
- **HE KNOWS WHO AND NOT WHY.** The governor of Qeshet opened the box's door at dusk a day east and looked at the princess; Kael had priced him in a square and did not need to again. He knows nothing of why he was taken, or why these three (author, s58). Ivrit on the box seat, one voice in the Koine with the coast under it; the count says four mules, two on the box, more than twenty riding and no closer than that yet, east, water tomorrow.
- **HE TOLD THE PRINCESS *DON'T*, AND THEN THE COUNT, UNASKED** — the first things he has ever given her without being asked; *She could not answer; it made it easy. He did not look at that.* He could not read whether she stopped pulling because he was right or because she had given up.
- **HE THINKS AND SAYS THE TRUE NAMES NOW** — *Ivrit*, *the Koine* (author, s58).
- **HE HAS BEEN TOLD WHAT IS UNDER THE LINEN, AS FAR AS HIS MOTHER WILL SAY IT UNDER THAT ROOF:** *something in my blood … something old, and it is mine, and it came to him through me. It is in him now* — that she knows little of it, and that the devil's-eyes story is one that *changes and conforms to what people want to hear rather than the truth*. **And that her own eyes are like Wren's** — *ways, with great will and control, to hide them* — told to him, never seen by him. He swore *Nobody*: not Valeria, not the princess, not Aurelian or Elarine, and nothing added to what the old man knows.
- **WHETHER IT IS IN HIM IS OPEN IN HIS MOTHER'S OWN MOUTH:** *I'm not sure, Kael* — she hoped it was not, prayed to the gods of the East, his eyes were *simply eyes* when he was born, and her blood is *not as strong in you as it is in Wren*, and she is glad of it. He has that and nothing more.
- **HE HAS THE LANGUAGES' TRUE NAMES:** Ivrit (the south's — the family's *Traders*), Avestan (the east's — the downhill one), the Vessantine Koine (the empire's). He understood the healer, the captain and the household; he asked why she taught him the invaders' tongue and was told *it is not theirs* — older than those men, the whole continent's for thousands of years before the empire spread; and why all of them — *so that there would be nowhere on this continent you could not go.* *I was never going to leave the island.* / *No.* — and nothing further; the bundles were not raised.
- **HE SAID THE WRONG ENEMY OUT LOUD, AND HE IS STILL WRONG:** *the people that attacked us at the island, they are the enemies. These people are of the empire, the victims. They'll help.* His mother answered with *my truth* — *There are no enemies. There are no allies. All we can be sure of, is family* — and he had no response to it; the coalition burned his island and there is nothing in it he has turned over. He heard *my truth* from a mother who had always said *the truth*; he was told, aloud, that he is intelligent and that she is proud of him.
- **HE ANSWERED TO *LUCAN* TWO STRIDES LATE** — *Goodnight, Lucan*, from the princess in a passage; *Goodnight, Highness*, to the passage, without turning round.
- **HE LAID HIS FATHER'S SWORD ALONG THE FOOT OF A DOOR WITH NO BAR**, grip toward the bed, and the pair by his head, and slept in the first bed of his life. He felt a weight that was not tiredness and said *Something is wrong* and was answered *I know*; he counted aloud for her and did not reach six; *his mind and body failed him*; the last thing was his name in her voice. **The count failed a fourth way: from inside**, and the page names nothing.
- ***Ask me the rest of it on the other side of that water* came due on the sand, and he did not ask.** It stands due; at Qeshet Severin took the far room alone.
- **HE IS LUCAN DRUSANE IN AN IMPERIAL LETTER.** At the governor's table in Qeshet the princess gave the house as Drusane — his name as Lucan, his mother as the Lady Drusane, his brother as a Drusane child born in the fire — and told the governor to write it so; Aurelian and Elarine backed it with true sentences about the dead boy; a rider took it to the capital that night. He sat under the name and made his face do nothing. **He read that she lied and how she chose the name — for what it would hold — and decided the why was that a princess does not arrive at her father's capital with herders in her train.** He was not in the room for the oath she swore; nobody has corrected him. *Kael* was not said at that table.
- **HE STOOD ON THE CONTINENT ON THE THIRTIETH DAY**, on legs that had forgotten what ground did, and got ready for a fight he did not have: the pair out of his belt and in his hands, **the longsword on his back on the length of cord, where it rides, because it is not a thing he can use**, three strides in front of his mother without remembering getting there. The knife stayed inside the coat. He put the blades away when the watch knelt and had to tell his hands twice.
- **THE STILL WAS HIS MOTHER'S IDEA AND HIS HANDS.** He had known it since he was nine and sat five days on a boat with it inside him without taking it out, and put that away to be ashamed of when there was water for it; he built the hearth, the lid and the catch, and could not be the fire.
- **HE PUT THE HULL ON THE SKY** — position by stars, hers, two nights running — and the old man's swell agreed: north and a little west, a walking pace, a month. His count came back slow for thirty days and returned when there was water in him; at the table it ran the room (ten who could hear, one door, his mother's hands, the rider), and at the end of the meal he did not count anything at all.
- **THE PRINCESS TOLD HIM WHY SHE DID THE CLOTH** — to learn whether his mother's *you will be extremely good at this* was said to make her take the child or because it was true — looking at him for the first time in six days; he took it as an experiment and filed it under things his mother had said. He corrected her wringing once and she did it right.
- **HE HAS BEEN MENDED BY A STRANGER'S HANDS** — the south's healer strapped the ribs his mother mended wrong, found how wrong in four seconds and asked nothing; he watched her hands and had never seen any but his mother's do that. He heard his mother lie about the linen to the empire and said nothing.
- **HE WANTS THREE THINGS, AND IT IS THE FIRST TIME IN HIS LIFE ANYTHING HE WANTED HAD A REASON UNDER IT:** his mother and brother alive; to know what his parents were; and to kill the man who killed his father. The third is not hot — turned over, flat, cold and patient. **No vow was sworn, nothing was said aloud, and he has not named the man on a page (author, s50).**
- **HE HAS SEEN THE MAN WHO KILLED HIS FATHER AT EIGHTY STRIDES** — watched him spend nothing and get smaller, and could do nothing.
- **HE BLAMED A PRINCESS OF THE EMPIRE TO HER FACE AND WENT FOR HER.** Valeria had his wrist before the arm was anywhere, he pushed, and his mother's voice stopped him. **He blames HER for the whole chain and has never once noticed his own hands in it** (00 §True causality): that his reconnaissance put that house in the party's road, that his arithmetic chose the ground. Nobody on that boat has mentioned it to him; nobody confirmed it and nobody denied it. **He has not retracted it, and Book One ends with him wrong (author, s50).**
- **HE HOLDS HIMSELF RESPONSIBLE FOR HIS FATHER'S DEATH**, built only out of what he watched — the crossing that came for him, the sword put into his hands, the order given over his head, his own *don't*, twice, and then nothing. The one thing he said out loud is the one he will carry: *he told the master to take me and I let him.* His mother did not argue with it. **He has not been given the arithmetic** (his father went for the son and nobody held the house), and nobody has corrected him.
- **HE HAD THE QUESTION FINISHED IN HIS MOUTH AND DID NOT ASK IT.** *Ask me on the water* came due; he priced what it would cost her and put it down; she felt him take the breath and offered him nothing. The Phantom is unasked and unanswered.
- **HE WENT, SITTING STILL, AND HIS MOTHER HELD HIM.** The debrief got as far as Oss and stopped being one; she told him none of the useful lies; for about four minutes he was nothing whatever except a person's child.
- **HIS FATHER IS DEAD AND HE WATCHED ALL OF IT** — the last stand, the wounds (he counted, could not stop, was at eleven, and *would have given anything on this earth for it to stop*), and the sword coming off a dead man level at the height of the shoulders. He was not spared one second of it and he was not knocked out. The machine does not switch off, ever, and he now knows exactly what that costs.
- **HE HAS THE SWORD.** Given, not thrown — put into his hand, his fingers closed on it by his father's own, with four words about how to hold it and an order over his head to the man who would carry him. It is far too big for him. His fists opened on it somewhere on the descent and he never noticed when; his mother took it off him, which nobody had managed all night, and the old man has since given it back and told him to keep it where it will not roll. **He woke in the box without it; where it is now is on no page.**
- **HE WENT UP A SLOPE AT FOUR HUNDRED MEN WITH NO DECISION IN IT ANYWHERE**, and then went through the terror into somewhere very cold and very exact, and began to reach for something. **What happened in his eyes is not shown, not named and not resolved on the page** (00). Only Severin saw it. Nothing moved; nothing unlocked.
- **HE HOLDS HIS BROTHER PROPERLY NOW** — two hands, in daylight — and counts his breathing, and the child's hand shuts on his finger and he will not be the one to take it back. Before that: four seconds of complete happiness, then the linen riding up off ONE red eye and nobody in that room surprised by anything, his mother putting it back with her thumb without looking; then an hour one-armed through a barrage, told *do not put him down* and saying it back, both hands full when the boat needed a knife. **The linen is still on, he has not touched it, and nobody has explained one word of it. He knows they knew.**
- **HE HAS WATCHED HIS MOTHER DO SOMETHING HE HAS NO UNIT FOR**, twice, priced her to the grain off the cost doctrine while she did it, and found no bottom. He does not know what she is, he has not asked, and **the archaeology on his mother has started.** He has never seen her face during a working of that size, and has never seen her eyes go red.
- **HE COULD DO NONE OF IT.** Four Resonants held a hillside off eight people and he read every one of the four workings perfectly and performed none of them — a line two fingers wide and nothing in the capacity. **The deficit is real and nothing on the page softened it.**
- **HIS COUNT HAS FAILED THREE WAYS:** it returned nothing at the gate (the wall did not make it so, the wall made it visible); it worked at the house and returned something worse; it worked on the water and returned answers faster than they were worth, losing the terms one at a time, until *he did not know what number he was on.* **None of the three was panic: the machine did not fail, its output stopped being usable.**
- **HE HAS KILLED.** He held out against fifteen grown men because he did not want to do it and was frightened of it — nobody ever put the question to him and nobody built that part of him — and then, disarmed and about to die, he decided, and screamed, and put a threaded hand through a man's chest. **He remembers deciding**, and has never found a version of that morning in which he does otherwise. After the first they were not hard; he noticed the fifth less than the second; four more from cover in an afternoon, alone, quietly, the cold voice counting. He does not count the femoral man — he did not put it near the heart, and his father's school never used the word for what that is — so **the tally he carries is his, and it is wrong.**
- **HE LEARNED THAT HE CAN SET IT DOWN.** The killing has not stopped costing him and he knows exactly what it costs — but it stays where he puts it and has not once asked him anything, **and that is the frightening part, not the lightness.**
- **THE COAT IS OFF THE ART, AND IT COST HIM NOTHING:** he held the threading visible the length of the blade all through that lane because there was no longer anybody on the island he was hiding it from. Four years of putting it away, and no time at all to stop. He fights the only way his ribs allow, which is the way the island named him — he cannot take a blow, cannot trade, cannot spend much, so all of it collapses into not being where the blow is.
- **HIS CONSCIENCE HAS ACTED TWICE ON THE PAGE:** once for nothing — a woman at the gate given the arithmetic, correct to the minute, who thanked him the way you thank a child; once at a price he counted — a woman and two children put in a fold under a terrace, told *not dusk, dark*, at a cost of eleven minutes he carried. He never learns whether it held. It is the one faculty nobody built; and being hunted was very nearly fun, which he is ashamed of.
- **HE WAS REFUSED AT THE GATE, AND HE BEGGED** — called a man *sir* for the first time in his life, asked for passage and not for shelter, and was answered correctly and warmly and without cruelty. **He does not know that the refusal was a decision about him rather than a rule about him.** He played Severin's name at the gap and the card was dead before he played it; he took Valeria through thirty thousand people to a door that opened for her and not for him, and being the instrument of his own exclusion is the shape of it. He begged for his own life twice out loud, in the plain words of the village, to nobody, and was answered gently by the man about to kill him.
- **HIS VERDICT ON ELARINE AND AURELIAN IS BUILT OUT OF ONE FACT AND IS NOT CORRECTABLE:** they were on the wall, he was under it. *Whatever there had been between the three of them, it was weaker than that gate.* Nothing they have done since — carrying his mother off a mountain, comforting him on the boat — has touched it; he is glad they are alive and it changed nothing. **Nobody has corrected him.** **AND HE COUNTS THEM NOW** — asked why Ishareth, he named the governor as the only thread to his mother, his brother, the old man, *and Elarine and Aurelian.* The verdict stands, and he is walking toward a man for them anyway, and the page joins nothing up.
- **HE HAS SEEN HIS FATHER AT TRUE CAPACITY AND UNDERSTOOD HALF OF IT**, and wants the rest; nobody has explained one word of it to him. He heard the coalition call his father THE PHANTOM and could do nothing with it; he joins nothing up and neither does the page. He understood the men at the eastern gate — the downhill language, heard from two mouths in his life before that — and drew no conclusion.
- **THE PRIDE IS GONE AND HE FELT IT GO.** Eleven thousand people made a noise for him and he thought it was the top of something; the page never argued him out of it, the world did. THE GHOST is what the island called him, to his face.
- **HE CRIED, AT A RUN, AND SO DID HIS MOTHER, AND NEITHER OF THEM SAID ONE WORD ABOUT IT.** He has seen his mother cry and been called *my love*; neither had happened in his life. **His mother mended him and he watched what it cost** — three ribs, one sitting on the lung, nature spent out of her own element while in labour, the white coming into her hair beside his face — and he told her to stop and she did not; nobody in that house says what it cost.
- **HE SPOKE INTO A PLAN FOR THE FIRST TIME IN HIS LIFE, AND WAS RIGHT — TWICE.** Told, not asked, as always: the eastern water is not empty, so the family's whole winter arrangement was dead and he was the one who took it off the table; and on the shelf, told *then it is here* and asked nothing, he gave the runnel arithmetic into the back of the old man's head anyway. **There is nobody left alive whose business it is to ask him.** Before that his father said *Ask my son* in the middle of a captain's sentence, and grown professionals took a thirteen-year-old's ground.
- **OSS IS DEAD FORTY FEET FROM HIM AND NOT ONE WORD PASSED BETWEEN THEM, IN A YEAR OR IN THAT MINUTE.** Not repairable, ever. He shouted Lucan Drusane's name and was four seconds late, knelt with the boy until the weight changed, and read exactly why the form killed him — no verdict, no epiphany. He was frightened in a way he had not been before, because everybody he had ever known was standing behind him.
- **HE HAS CROSSED THAT SEA** — thirty days on a bare hull, the boat's small language twenty words a day from the old man until the hull opened on the sand; he has wanted beyond it since he was four, and the other side of it is a brown country with water running loud in stone channels, which he could not walk past.
- **He smiles when a fight turns critical, and knows it** — the instinct is delight; Elarine saw it and will never say so; Severin saw it, said so, sourced nothing, and gave one instruction, *"You watch it"*, once, and nobody has repeated or expanded it. He looked for where it was put into him and found nothing.
- **Never drew his father's knife in the fire**; it stayed inside the coat. Armed: the two shed blades that do not match (his father's answer to the twin-shorts prohibition, and not one word about the arena), his own knife off the belt, and the longsword.
- **Standing kit:** LINE-REINFORCEMENT — earth spent inward into the exact line where force folds, about two fingers wide; the constraint is AREA, not cost, so it costs about what a hard breath costs and repeats (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`); the threading, visible the length of the medium, held or let go at the knock; a threading through the bare hand cuts a working; **two at once empties him in a fifth of a second and one more grain would whiten his hair — it has never once been cheap.** Dual daggers begun and not had. He says *capacity*, not *the purse*; his mother's formal vocabulary is neither the village's nor the Temple's and he has not asked where it comes from.
- Does not sleep well — *not since father—*, his own dating — and says he is not frightened. He slept at Qeshet, and it was not a sleep he chose.
- **Concealments:** the hidden contact (Halden's capital offer) — his parents asked who the man was to his face and got *"a judge of the games"*, and his father died with that hole in him; THE BIND, written in the blank book, told to no one, declined twice, never attempted; the threading, shown to Valeria and stopping with her.
- **Knows:** that a princess of the empire lied for his family to the empire, in writing, and the name she gave him; the men of his night died; his parents are Resonants (lightning / air); the family was hunted, and hunted *because he is theirs*; they were owned and stopped being owned; the coalition called his father the Phantom; his brother's eye is red and the adults were not surprised; the master has a hull he will not explain and *knows what she is*; **the red is in his mother's blood and in Wren's, and her own eyes are like Wren's and hidden by will; the three true names; that the empire spread a few hundred years ago; that his mother prayed to the gods of the East; that the governor of Qeshet is the man at the box's door and stayed with the box for two months; that the iron is painted and the Art does not answer in it; that the governor gives every order on that road, in the Koine; that the governor is at ISHARETH buying men off a letter, that there is one bridge and it is his, and that he has told nobody on that road anything at all.** **Does not know:** anything sealed in 00; what the sleep was; **why the princess stood up; that the reason under his count of twelve is wrong; how iron with a peened pin ever comes off;** **whether his mother, Wren or the old man are alive; why he was taken, or why these three; what the black in the iron is; what the governor turns in the saddle to look for every afternoon**; whether the blood is in him; that Halden is Corvane's man; that Oss was coerced; why the delivery had to be Severin's; what his mother is; what the hull was stocked for; that the gate was a decision about him.
- **Relationships:** mother — the danger admission believed, the history refused, the question unasked, and held by her; **on no page since the wake, alive or not unknown to him**; father — dead, MEMORY, the sword; Wren — his, and on no page; Valeria — the reason he is alive, the rematch never to be had, and the hand she stopped; Elarine and Aurelian — the verdict standing, their comfort taken; Oss — dead, unrepaired; Severin — the boat's one teacher, and a man who says things he does not source; on no page since the wake, the deferral unaskable; Halden — the man who shut the gate, and the hidden contact still hidden; the princess — blamed to her face, and holding his brother.
- **Body:** the settlement blade scar on the left forearm, stitched by Neris; a second scar, long and shallow, on the outside of the left arm above the elbow, off Lucan Drusane's lunge; the burn, wrist to elbow on the left forearm, over the blade scar — the first mark on him he did not spend himself; burn speckle-receipts wrist to second knuckle; three ribs mended badly by his mother; a thigh that stopped answering on a wall; a split lip and a season of real cuts off Valeria; a month of salt (lips, mouth, ears and shoulders opened, a rash the length of both forearms), mended with the south's oils; the ribs strapped at Qeshet; two months in painted iron, thin enough that thumb and finger go past each other round the arm above the elbow; **the left hand destroyed by his own right** — thumb broken at the base, the bone below the little finger folded and a second splintered, the back skinned from the knuckles to the wrist, **and then bitten through the palm by a dying man and gone the colour of a plum and hot**; a cut across the top of the right shoulder and a spear-point drawn across the ribs on the left, down to the bone, in the narrow.
- **Arc position:** the first true "not enough" filed beside the misfiled one; the wall belief broken; the danger self-knowledge live and **ungoverned by any rule but himself**. The first-person slips are spent and their count lives in one place (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md` §DEPARTURE LEDGER).
