## KAEL (protagonist)
**Glance:** POV protagonist · no house — the herald calls him **"of the Island"**, and the island calls him **THE GHOST** to his face · **13** · **carried down his own burning mountain awake, facing the wrong way, with his father's longsword in fists that would not open**

### STATIC
- **Role:** POV character; hidden son of Vask (ex-instrument) and Neris (ex-asset) of the program. Carrier of the rare bloodline through his mother (what it is: 00). Surname unknown until the Temple.
- **Want:** Begins with wanting to touch the village lights, understand, and be measured. Evolves into a hunger for success, fame, and acknowledgment — to be noticed, measured, and remarked upon. He never sandbags. The coat hides what he is, never that he is good. Success therefore attracts both noble attention and peer resentment. Valeria eventually becomes the first to be his measure. He wants to learn about the world, go beyond the sea.
- **Wound:** Raised in loving secrecy; the world forbidden; parented through preparation instead of presence. At nine he discovers the founding promise was a lie and absorbs it silently — the first wound he carries alone. The settlement (Ch. 6) is his first impression of humanity: foundational, series-long trauma — distrust of strangers, crowds, touch, and group laughter (rendered as small physical tells, never labeled). It sits under the social blindness: his awkwardness is ignorance plus fear, and combat is his only social language with rules. He misfiles the night's defeat as his own insufficiency when in truth only the operator broke. His conscience is the one faculty nobody built — the series' moral spine. Left-forearm blade scar, stitched by Neris, echoing Vask's tally-marks; never explained.
- **APPETITE WITHOUT DESTINATION (ratified s20 — the arc's engine).** His want (success, measure, acknowledgment) is APPETITE, not purpose. He works the way he breathes, because he has always worked, and it has never once been going anywhere. Everyone around him answers with a *because* — a house, a name, a Line — and where his because should be there is only the work. **This is 01's thesis stated as a wound** (no destiny handed down), (where it goes is in 00). Beginning to make him want to WANT. Explored first with Elarine (full but heavy vs. empty but light — both burdens), later and deeper with Valeria (nothing but purpose, and it is not hers).
- **Trait:** CURIOSITY. Voracious love of learning; the itch that cannot be out-waited is the one urge stillness training cannot defeat. Parent-cultivated, rule-breaking, and exactly what the Temple prizes.
- **Trait:** THE CONCEALMENT INHERITANCE. From age nine he keeps secrets from his parents using their own schools, lovingly and in their voice — the seed of the Temple concealment and the series-long becoming-them thread.
- **Flaw:** SOCIAL BLINDNESS. Cannot read cues, hierarchy, mockery, or alliances; answers rhetorical questions literally. He detects the WHAT (anger, a lie, a turned room) and misreads the WHY (rule in 06); the gap closes at series scale, and past it his social reading becomes extremely proficient.
- **Abilities:** Technician, not powerhouse. LIGHTNING proficiency, unusually even generalist spread beneath it; LOW raw capacity; exceptional control, technique, tactics, threading, creativity — small, exact, fast, never bolts. True defining axis: intelligence + technique + fieldcraft + exceptional martial skill; the empire's capacity-worship is loudly wrong about him (emphasis rule in 06). His style is his parents' synthesis — father's economy of motion through mother's economy of power.
- **Method:** Runs controlled trials on his own initiative — asking twice with one thing changed. His empiricism against the nobles' capacity-worship is a Part II engine (00 has more). First proof on the page: the earth-step counter (Ch. 16) — he solved Valeria's ground-sense by trial and attacked the MEDIUM rather than the sense, knowledge standing in where he has no capacity; she could not answer it (mechanics: `../02-World-Bible/magic.md` §Blind Spot).
- **Lexicon:** Names his own disciplines and coins terms for discoveries the world lacks; the lexicon grows across the series. Holds "I don't know" as a tool.
- **Voice:** Matures with the prose (06).
- **Appearance:** **A handsome boy, and filthy with it (author-ruled, s33)** — dark hair (his mother's), clean features under village dirt and weather, hair cut by somebody who was not being careful. Sun-weathered and wind-chapped from work at altitude, with **freckles across the nose** under the weather and the grime (ruled s35; unspent on any page). Lean, hard and visibly fit; strong without bulk, the strength sitting in the forearms and the hands. **He is not plain and never was: what makes him invisible is STATUS and STILLNESS, not his face** — no house, no name, working cloth, and a body trained not to move (the ornate royal register is Aeliana's; `appearance-ledger.md`). Eyes a pale grey belonging to no one in the house (00): the parents never remark on them, not once — their silence is the tell that never tells; the grey arrives only through other mouths or other eyes, no mirrors, no self-description; unspent on the page — spend when a Temple mouth wants a detail to be cruel or curious about; **IMAGES ARE EXEMPT (author ruling, s42): plates may show the grey, so the Temple mouth now CONFIRMS for a reader who has seen them rather than revealing — do not write that beat as a reveal** (`08-Plates/README.md`). Left forearm: the blade scar, near his father's tally-marks in site and length. Marked older than his face admits. What others react to: nothing — which is the point, and slowly becomes the thing they react to.

### DYNAMIC
- **HIS FATHER IS DEAD AND HE WATCHED ALL OF IT** — the last stand, the wounds, and the sword coming off a dead man level at the height of the shoulders. He was not spared one second of it and he was not knocked out.
- **HE HAS THE SWORD. It was given, not thrown** — put into his hand, his fingers closed on it by his father's own, with four words about how to hold it and an order over his head to the man who would carry him. **It is far too big for him.**
- **HE COUNTED HIS FATHER'S WOUNDS AND COULD NOT STOP**, and was at eleven, and *would have given anything on this earth for it to stop.* The machine does not switch off, ever, and he now knows exactly what that costs.
- **THE ONE THING HE SAID OUT LOUD IS THE ONE HE WILL CARRY:** *he told the master to take me and I* let him. **His mother did not argue with it.** Never correct him; never resolve it.
- **HE WENT UP A SLOPE AT FOUR HUNDRED MEN WITH NO DECISION IN IT ANYWHERE**, and then went through the terror into somewhere very cold and very exact, and began to reach for something. **What happened in his eyes is never shown, never named and never resolved** (00). Only Severin saw it. Nothing moved; nothing unlocked.
- **HE HELD HIS BROTHER FOR ABOUT FOUR SECONDS AND WAS COMPLETELY HAPPY**, and then the linen rode up off one eye and **it was red**, and nobody in that room was surprised by anything, and his mother put the linen back with her thumb without looking at it. **He knows they knew. Nobody has explained one word of it.**
- **HE WATCHED OSS DIE FORTY FEET AWAY AND NEVER GOT ONE WORD OUT, IN A YEAR OR IN THAT MINUTE.** Not repairable, ever.
- **HE SHOUTED LUCAN DRUSANE'S NAME AND WAS FOUR SECONDS LATE**, knelt with the boy until the weight changed, and read exactly why the form killed him. **No verdict, no epiphany.**
- **HE WAS FRIGHTENED IN A WAY HE HAS NOT BEEN BEFORE** — heart, breath, cold hands — and the reason is that everybody he has ever known was standing behind him.
- **He has seen his mother cry, and been called *my love*.** Neither had happened in his life.
- **Now: 13**, on his own ground, armed, upright, holding two counts at once — the gaps between his mother's pains and the lights coming up the mountain.
- **HIS MOTHER MENDED HIM AND HE WATCHED WHAT IT COST.** Three ribs, one sitting on the lung; she spent nature out of her own element while in labour, and the white came into her hair beside his face while he was looking at it, and he was taught the cost doctrine in a Temple yard and can price it to the grain. **He told her to stop and she did not.** Nobody in that house says what it cost and nobody will.
- **He fights the only way those ribs allow, which is the way the island named him** — he cannot take a blow, cannot trade, cannot spend much, so all of it collapses into not being where the blow is.
- **He gave the ground away and grown professionals took it**, because his father said *Ask my son* in the middle of the captain's own sentence and then did not argue with any of it.
- **He spoke into a plan for the first time in his life.** Told, not asked, as always — and he answered anyway: the eastern water is not empty, so the family's whole winter arrangement is dead, and he is the one who took it off the table.
- **He has not raised the protection promise and now never will.** He had it complete in his mouth and the old man got there first, with *I looked for you*.
- **He has seen Oss alive**, and neither of them got anything out.
- **The purse is nearly empty and has not filled.** The shed blades replace the pair he lost; his father answered the twin-shorts prohibition by handing him steel and saying nothing whatever about eleven thousand people.
- **HE CROSSED IT ALONE AND HIS FATHER'S ENTIRE SCHOOL WORKED.** Wall lines over the fast track, water at the trough, the pace that holds, evasion in dock leaves, room to house to roof. **He killed four men from cover in an afternoon**, alone, quietly, and the cold voice counted them.
- **He learned that he can SET IT DOWN.** The killing has not stopped costing him and he knows exactly what it costs — but it stays where he puts it and has not once asked him anything, **and that is the frightening part, not the lightness.** Do not accelerate this: he is one day from his first one.
- **He acted on the conscience a second time, and paid for it in his own currency:** a woman and two children put in a fold under a terrace, told six hours and *not dusk, dark*, at a cost of **eleven minutes he counted and carried**. He never learns whether it held.
- **He was found; he did not hunt.** Going past the last holding onto the goat ground, exhausted, he gave a yard ninety seconds instead of four minutes and his thigh stopped answering on the wall. **He had not been reading the yard. He had been reading the way out of it.**
- **HE BEGGED A SECOND TIME, OUT LOUD, FOR HIS OWN LIFE**, in the plain words of the village, to nobody — and was answered, gently, by the man about to kill him.
- **He has seen his father at true capacity and understood HALF of it** — and wants the rest. The road is in 02/magic and in FORWARD; nobody has explained one word of it to him.
- **He heard the coalition call his father THE PHANTOM.** He could do nothing with it. He joins nothing up and neither does the page.
- **HE WAS REFUSED AT THE GATE, AND HE BEGGED.** He called a man *sir* for the first time in his life, asked for passage and not for shelter, offered to be escorted through and out the other side, and was answered correctly and warmly and without cruelty. **He does not know that the refusal was a decision about him rather than a rule about him.**
- **He played Severin and the card was dead before he played it.** He quoted the protection promise whole at an eighteen-inch gap — *not me, and not my house* — to the one man in the empire that name cannot reach, and was told *He is not here.*
- **Kael took Valeria through thirty thousand people to a door that opened for her and not for him.** His schools worked; being the instrument of his own exclusion is the shape of it, and nobody on the page says so.
- **His verdict on Elarine and Aurelian is built out of one fact and is not correctable:** they were on the wall, he was under it, no faces, no expressions, no chance for either of them to act. *Whatever there had been between the three of them, it was weaker than that gate.* He is glad they are alive and it changed nothing. Never correct him.
- **He warned a stranger and it was worth nothing.** He gave a woman the arithmetic, correct to the minute and checked twice, and she thanked him the way you thank a child and went back to her place in the queue. His conscience has now acted once, on the page, and failed.
- **His count came back and returned no solution at all.** He worked the ground four times, threw out every assumption, gave himself the whole imperial garrison and eight hours, and there was nothing there — the wall did not make it so, the wall made it visible. Thirty-one thousand, thirty-six at the outside; the cold voice started on the hillside behind him and it is going to have a number.
- **HE HAS KILLED.** He held out against fifteen grown men taking hands, knees and shoulders, because he did not want to do it and was frightened of it — nobody ever put the question to him and nobody built that part of him. Disarmed and about to die, he decided, and screamed, and put a threaded hand through a man's chest. **He remembers deciding**, and has never found a version of that morning in which he does otherwise. After the first, they were not hard, and he noticed the fifth less than the second.
- **He does not count the femoral man** — he did not put it near the heart, and his father's school never used the word for what that is. The tally he carries is his, and it is wrong.
- **The coat came off the Art, and it cost him nothing:** he held the threading visible the length of the blade all through that lane, because there was no longer anybody on the island he was hiding it from. Four years of putting it away, and no time at all to stop.
- **His counting broke once and he forced it back** — it went off in his hands at the top of the rise, and at the wall he made it work, and what it produced was worse than not knowing.
- **Never drew his father's knife.** It stayed inside the coat and he did not once think of it. Armed (the twin shorts across his back), undrawn since the bout.
- **Understood the men at the eastern gate:** they speak the downhill language, and he had heard it from two mouths in his life before this. No conclusion drawn, no minute to draw one in; nobody on the page joins it up.
- **Froze on the sand, and it was NOT fear:** every school returned everything at once, all of it correct, and nothing could be put first. Valeria restarted him open-handed; neither has mentioned it. Never render the freeze as panic or compare it to Ch. 6.
- **Never found the answer to Valeria** — everything he ever took off her he took once — and told her so before the bell. The question is never answered (00).
- **Saw Oss** in the north curve as it went over, turned the wrong way to get a smaller boy off a bench, and does not know what happened to him.
- **Broke Vask's twin-shorts prohibition mid-bout (Ch. 26)** because he wanted to win more than he wanted to obey, and told his parents all of it, blades included. Vask's answer is owed and unwritten.
- **Smiles when a fight turns critical, and knows it** — the instinct is delight. Elarine saw it and will never say so; Severin saw it, said so, sourced nothing, and gave one instruction — *"You watch it"* — never to be repeated or expanded by anybody. Kael looked for where it was put into him and found nothing.
- **Has been to the floor of his capacity in public (Ch. 26):** two at once — earth into one line of his palm, the threading into the hand — emptied him in a fifth of a second; one more grain and the hair would have gone white. Never cheap (02/magic).
- **THE GHOST is the people's name for him** — minted at the first bout (Ch. 23; won with his hands alone, no blade, no thread), said to his face on the road at Ch. 26. The boxes say nothing.
- **Was rude to the princess without knowing it (Ch. 24):** no royal reflex at all — ABSENCE, not defiance; corrected her, asked where her food goes, knelt when told and had no farewell. His instruments return nothing on her at any range, and it is not concealment. The seed of a distaste for royalty is planted and unassembled (07).
- **Said "Val" in public** without noticing; Aurelian and Elarine took it up. Has never said *friend* back to Elarine, and could not read her face when she billed him for four years of *Sedrane*.
- **Tells his parents everything; the hole is only Halden** — the flash, the audience, the bouts, the blades, all told. The hidden contact is at maximum pressure: they asked who the man was to his face and got *"a judge of the games"*.
- **Standing kit:** LINE-REINFORCEMENT — he cannot flood a limb, so he spends earth inward into the exact line where force folds, about two fingers wide. **The constraint is AREA, not cost (author, s37 — retires the s24 *empties him every time / not twice in a day*)**: at that size it costs about what a hard breath costs and he can spend it repeatedly. It is not a weaker version of the Vel's art; it is a different move at a different scale (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`) — the many-element road's second proof. **Dual daggers BEGUN and NOT HAD:** two full hands delete his father's escapes, and two threadings at once are categorically harder than one (02/magic); he has the two shed blades. Says *capacity*, not *the purse* (02/lexicon); his mother's formal vocabulary is neither the village's nor the Temple's, and he has not asked where it comes from.
- **The road:** never the same route or hour twice, never the last stretch in the light; carries his own knife, off the belt. The mountain's schools run on LIVE EDGES — his father kills him five and six times a morning and stops each stroke by deciding to.
- **Does not sleep** — the attention he turned up in the autumn will not turn back down. Not frightened, and says so.
- **Called a friend, and could not say it back** — he lacks the word and took Elarine's apart instead. Suspects Aurelian is one and cannot find the day; does not know whether Oss still is.
- **Gave Valeria a name (Ch. 20)** — it was not answered, and he does not know whether that was permission.
- **Wants:** beyond the sea; the tournament; Valeria's measure, fed and feared. No *because* under any of it — appetite, not purpose.
- **Knows:** the men of his night died; two lightning houses asked after him and his mother; his parents are Resonants (lightning / air); the family is hunted, and hunted *because he is theirs*; they were owned and stopped being owned; a child is hours or days away; he was to compete and be taken off the field mid-tournament — told in front of him, never asked of him, and he said nothing.
- **Does not know:** anything sealed in 00; the third house's man; that Halden is Corvane's man; that Oss was coerced; why the delivery must be Severin's; what is under the third board.
- **Concealments:** the coat; the hidden contact (Halden's capital offer); THE BIND, written in the blank book, told to no one, declined twice, never attempted; the threading, SHOWN to Valeria — spent on her, never handed over, and she is earth and cannot use it — and stopping with her.
- **Relationships:** parents — the danger admission believed, the history refused twice; Valeria — rival, the rematch wanted on both sides, and the reason he is alive; Elarine — collegial ally, crush unlabeled; Aurelian — training ally; Oss — estranged, and lost in the crowd; Severin — sponsor and mentor, protection given whole; Halden — hidden contact, the wrongness filed unexamined.
- **Body:** the Ch. 6 blade scar on the left forearm; a second scar, long and shallow, on the outside of the left arm above the elbow, off Lucan Drusane's lunge (Ch. 23); **the Ch. 26 burn — the first mark on him he did not spend himself — wrist to elbow on the LEFT forearm (author, s33)**, lying over the blade scar (the page never sited that one; the tally-mark rhyme is quieter in a picture than in prose); burn speckle-receipts wrist to second knuckle; proofing-bruises never above the collar or below the wrist; a split lip and a season of real cuts off Valeria.
- **Arc position:** the first true "not enough" filed beside the misfiled one; the wall belief broken; the danger self-knowledge live and **ungoverned by any rule but himself** — and being hunted was very nearly fun, which he is ashamed of. Slips 1–2 spent, slip 3 reserved for the fire.

### FORWARD
- **THE EVOLVED THREADING (author, s39):** he has seen his father's whole-body form once and got half of it. Do not let him try it, approach it, or be taught it inside Book One; the mechanism and the road are in 00.
- **The inheritance:** Vask's sword is in his hands — given, not thrown, at Ch. 33. The rest of the steel is in 00.
- **Arc:** innocence → disillusion → dangerous competence → (00). Aims at the wrong enemy first — the coalition burned his island.
- **End of Book One:** ~13–14; fleeing by sea with Valeria and the princess. Father dead; mother, brother, master lost, fates unknown to him.
