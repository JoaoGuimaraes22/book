## KAEL (protagonist)
**Glance:** POV protagonist · no house — the herald calls him **"of the Island"**, and the island itself now calls him **THE GHOST to his face** · entered to the games by Halden · **13**, as of Ch. 27 · **the semifinal was never fought; the island is being emptied and he is walking inland, away from the mountain**

### STATIC
- **Role:** POV character; hidden son of Vask (ex-instrument) and Neris (ex-asset) of the program. Carrier of the rare KYNEMIR bloodline through his mother; kinetic affinity is dormant and leashed by training. The Ch. 4 knot is its first raw surfacing; he does not know. Surname unknown until the Temple.
- **Want:** Begins with wanting to touch the village lights, understand, and be measured. Evolves into a hunger for success, fame, and acknowledgment — to be noticed, measured, and remarked upon. He never sandbags. The coat hides what he is, never that he is good. Success therefore attracts both noble attention and peer resentment. Valeria eventually becomes the first to be his measure. He wants to learn about the world, go beyond the sea.
- **Wound:** Raised in loving secrecy; the world forbidden; parented through preparation instead of presence. At nine he discovers the founding promise was a lie and absorbs it silently — the first wound he carries alone. The settlement (Ch. 6) is his first impression of humanity: foundational, series-long trauma — distrust of strangers, crowds, touch, and group laughter (rendered as small physical tells, never labeled). It sits under the social blindness: his awkwardness is ignorance plus fear, and combat is his only social language with rules. He misfiles the night's defeat as his own insufficiency when in truth only the operator broke. His conscience is the one faculty nobody built — the series' moral spine. Left-forearm blade scar, stitched by Neris, echoing Vask's tally-marks; never explained.
- **APPETITE WITHOUT DESTINATION (ratified s20 — the arc's engine).** His want (success, measure, acknowledgment) is APPETITE, not purpose. He works the way he breathes, because he has always worked, and it has never once been going anywhere. Everyone around him answers with a *because* — a house, a name, a Line — and where his because should be there is only the work. **This is 01's thesis stated as a wound** (no destiny handed down), and it is the origin of the Requiem: the boy with no destination eventually makes one and pays everything for it. Beginning to make him want to WANT. Explored first with Elarine (full but heavy vs. empty but light — both burdens), later and deeper with Valeria (nothing but purpose, and it is not hers).
- **Trait:** CURIOSITY. Voracious love of learning; the itch that cannot be out-waited is the one urge stillness training cannot defeat. Parent-cultivated, rule-breaking, and exactly what the Temple prizes.
- **Trait:** THE CONCEALMENT INHERITANCE. From age nine he keeps secrets from his parents using their own schools, lovingly and in their voice — the seed of the Temple concealment and the series-long becoming-them thread.
- **Flaw:** SOCIAL BLINDNESS. Cannot read cues, hierarchy, mockery, or alliances; answers rhetorical questions literally. He detects the WHAT (anger, a lie, a turned room) and misreads the WHY (rule in 06); the gap closes at series scale, and past it his social reading becomes extremely proficient.
- **Abilities:** Technician, not powerhouse. LIGHTNING proficiency, unusually even generalist spread beneath it; LOW raw capacity; exceptional control, technique, tactics, threading, creativity — small, exact, fast, never bolts. True defining axis: intelligence + technique + fieldcraft + exceptional martial skill; the empire's capacity-worship is loudly wrong about him (emphasis rule in 06). His style is his parents' synthesis — father's economy of motion through mother's economy of power.
- **Method:** Runs controlled trials on his own initiative — asking twice with one thing changed. **THE EARTH-STEP COUNTER (Ch. 16):** solved her ground-sense by trial (four routes, one variable), understood the mechanism, and attacked the MEDIUM rather than the sense — a thread of earth breaking the stone's continuity under his own boot. Built with Elarine's principle and Aurelian's stupid-correct answer at the cistern court. She could not answer it. His first win off her, and the many-element road's first real proof: knowledge where he has no capacity. His empiricism against the nobles' capacity-worship is a Part II engine; under the deep layer his method is invisibly correct (00).
- **Lexicon:** Names his own disciplines and coins terms for discoveries the world lacks; the lexicon grows across the series. Holds "I don't know" as a tool.
- **Voice:** Matures with the prose (06).
- **Appearance:** Village-plain by design — dark hair (his mother's), unremarkable at rest; a wiry, light-through-the-shoulders build that hides its training completely — nothing to look at until he moves, and he is trained not to move. Eyes a pale grey belonging to no one in the house (00): the parents never remark on them, not once — their silence is the tell that never tells; the grey arrives only through other mouths or other eyes, no mirrors, no self-description; unspent on the page — spend when a Temple mouth wants a detail to be cruel or curious about. Left forearm: the blade scar, near his father's tally-marks in site and length. Marked older than his face admits. What others react to: nothing — which is the point, and slowly becomes the thing they react to.

### DYNAMIC
- **HE NEVER FOUND THE ANSWER.** A year of hunting a way to beat Valeria Vel, and *everything he had ever taken off her, he had taken once.* He walked onto that sand holding nothing, told her so when she asked, and the bell went and the judge's hand never came down. **The question is not answered and never will be.**
- **HE FROZE.** Every school he had ran perfectly and returned everything at once — the reach of the spears, which of the thirty was tired, where the ground went soft, the smoke's forty seconds, his mother six miles away with a child coming down — *and the machine that had produced one clean answer at the end of every problem of his entire life produced ten thousand of them and no way whatsoever to put them in an order.* **It was NOT fear** — *there was nothing to do first — that every single thing was first.* **Valeria got him back**, open-handed, and neither of them has mentioned it.
- **HE UNDERSTOOD THE MEN AT THE GATE.** The soldiers killing in the eastern gateway work in the downhill language, and he has it — *in his whole life Kael had heard that language come out of exactly two mouths.* He draws no conclusion from it. He has not had one minute in which to.
- **HE IS GOING THE WRONG WAY, AND HE KNOWS IT.** He went inland with Valeria toward the walled nobles' district on her orders and her promise that they will help him to the mountain afterwards. *It was the wrong direction. He had worked that out before he took the first step, and he took it anyway.*
- **He is armed and did not draw.** Twin shorts across his back, nobody at the gate said a word about them, and he went out through thirty men without using either.
- **He saw Oss** — three rows up, turned the wrong way against the flow to get a smaller boy off a bench — **and then the crowd took him, and he does not know.**
- **Now: 13**, and standing on the sand of the empire's arena. Temple day-commuter to the last; the mountain's schools now run on LIVE EDGES, and his father kills him five and six times a morning and stops each stroke by deciding to. **He climbed off the autumn sheet into the Temple's GOLD SIX over the winter** — entered under the houseless provision by Halden, and unremovable. He walks the road differently now (never the same route or hour twice, never the last stretch in the light) and carries his own knife, off the belt.
- **The dual-dagger style is BEGUN and NOT HAD.** He derived it himself from the medium law and the folding line, and it fails: two full hands delete every escape his father's school is built on, and holding two threadings at once is categorically harder than one, not doubled. Eight weeks of finding out how it fails. He has the two shed blades and Vask's fence on them.
- **Says *capacity*, not *the purse*.** The yard's craft word is behind him; his mother has begun handing him formal scientific vocabulary, and he knows it is neither the village's nor the Temple's and has not asked where it comes from.
- **HE HAS WON THE ROUND OF 8, AND IT COST HIM EVERYTHING HE HAD.** Against Elarine Sedrane — fire, a treasury, a sword in one hand and the Art in the other — hands were not enough inside ten seconds, and **he drew the twin shorts against his father's standing prohibition**, mid-bout, because he wanted to win more than he wanted to obey. He beat her by never once standing still: every time she moved him he came straight back, so every wall she bought had to be followed by another, **and she was buying all of it out of her own body while he spent his legs.** At the end he did **two at once** for the first time in his life — earth into the one line down the outside of his palm, the threading into the hand the way it goes into steel — and took her whole treasury on it. **The burn from wrist to elbow is the first mark on him he did not spend himself.** Then, with both their Arts gone, he took the length away from her with two short blades against a longsword, **took the wrist and not the sword**, and she knelt from exhaustion and did not get up.
- **HE WAS SMILING AND HE KNEW IT.** When a fight turns critical he follows instinct, and the instinct is delight — *the face of a boy doing the only thing in the world he had never had to be taught to want*, with blood on his teeth. **Elarine saw it across the sand. Severin saw it from the box and said so to his face**, and named the thing nobody had put there: *"Your father and your mother taught you to fight… They did not teach you that."* Kael went looking for the day it was put into him and found **nothing where the answer should have been.** He was given no verdict and one instruction — *"You watch it"* — and said *"All right,"* and meant it.
- **HE TOLD HIS PARENTS ALL OF IT, BLADES INCLUDED.** No new concealment; the hole is still only Halden.
- **THE WHITENING HAS A NUMBER ON IT NOW:** his capacity is a dry hole, and any more spent and his hair would have gone white.
- **HE HAS WON THE FIRST BOUT, and he won it with his hands.** He did not draw against Lucan Drusane and did not spend one thread: no blade, no distance, and a system built to fight at the measure had nothing to say. He knocked him out with one blow after putting him down three times, and waited to see whether he would get up. **The people named him THE GHOST inside a minute; the boxes said nothing at all.** He is in the round of 8 and fights **ELARINE SEDRANE at the third bell**, and he has had the answer since the cistern: everything he knows about fire, she taught him herself.
- **THE AUDIENCE IS SPENT, AND HE WAS RUDE.** His instruments returned nothing at conversational range either, and it is still not concealment. He has no royal reflex at all — ABSENCE, not defiance — so he corrected the daughter of the emperor about Drusane in front of her household, asked what happens to the food on her table and where the ice came from, and left her angry. **He reached no verdict and carried it out whole**, the way he carried Aurelian's shrug. Told to kneel and say his farewells, he knelt and **had no farewell, because nobody ever taught him one.** The seed of a distaste for royalty and nobles is planted and unassembled — 07 governs it.
- **He said "Val" in public**, unweighted, in the middle of a question about a gate, and did not notice until afterward; Aurelian and Elarine both took it up. He has still never said *friend* back to Elarine, and she billed him for four years of *Sedrane* in front of the others, and he could see her face perfectly and could not read it at all.
- **He told his parents the whole of it, un-holed** — the bout, the corridor, the room, the correction, the question about the table. **The hole is still exactly where it was, and it is Halden.**
- **Abilities, new:** LINE-REINFORCEMENT — he cannot flood a limb, so he spends inward into the single inch where a line of force folds. One inch, two seconds, and it empties him to the floor of the purse every time. Permanent kit; the many-element road's second proof.
- **Does not sleep.** The attention he turned all the way up in the autumn will not turn back down; it walks the road all night on its own. He is not frightened, and says so.
- **Has been called a friend and could not say it back** — he does not have the word and took Elarine's apart instead. He suspects Aurelian is one too and cannot find the day. He does not know whether Oss still is, and has no idea how to find out.
- **He has given Valeria a name.** It was not answered. He does not know whether that was permission, and he did not say it twice.
- **Wants:** beyond the sea; the tournament; Valeria's measure, fed and feared. No *because* under any of it — appetite, not purpose.
- **Knows:** the men of his night died; two lightning houses asked after him and his mother; his parents are Resonants (lightning / air); the family is hunted, and hunted *because he is theirs*; they were owned and stopped being owned; a child comes in the spring; **he will compete and be taken off the field mid-tournament** — told in front of him, never asked of him, and he said nothing.
- **Does not know:** anything sealed in 00; the third house's man; that Halden is Corvane's man; that Oss was coerced; why the delivery must be Severin's; what is under the third board.
- **Concealments:** the coat; the hidden contact (Halden's capital offer — **still held from the parents, now at maximum pressure**: they asked who the man was to his face and got *"a judge of the games"*); THE BIND, written in the blank book, told to no one, declined twice; the threading, SHOWN to Valeria — spent on her, never handed over, and she is earth and cannot use it — and stopping with her. The parents know about the flash — he told them himself, un-holed.
- **Relationships:** parents — the danger admission believed, the history refused twice; Valeria — rival, the rematch wanted on both sides; Elarine — collegial ally, crush unlabeled; Aurelian — training ally; Oss — estranged; Severin — sponsor and mentor, protection given whole; Halden — hidden contact, the wrongness filed unexamined.
- **Body:** the forearm scar; **a second scar, long and shallow, on the outside of the left arm above the elbow, off Lucan Drusane's lunge** (Ch. 23 — *"you will keep the scar and it will be a stupid one"*); burn speckle-receipts wrist to second knuckle; proofing-bruises never above the collar or below the wrist; a split lip and a season of real cuts off Valeria.
- **Arc position:** the first true "not enough" filed beside the misfiled one; the wall belief broken; the danger self-knowledge live and **ungoverned by any rule but himself** — and being hunted was very nearly fun, which he is ashamed of. Slips 1–2 spent, slip 3 reserved for the fire.

### FORWARD
- **DUAL THREADING — THE ROUTE HE ACTUALLY TAKES (author direction, s25; UNSPENT — not on any page).** He does not close the gap by acquiring years of control he does not have. He gets there by spending **small, exact, targeted EARTH resonance into his own body to resist the burst** — the Ch. 20 line-reinforcement trick re-aimed: instead of holding the inch where a parry folds, he holds against the discharge coming home. **Valeria's art mimicked at his own scale for the second time**, and the many-element road paying again: earth knowledge standing in where lightning capacity is missing. This is how he skips the *years* Vask names and has a working version of dual threading by the invasion. **Handling:** it must stay expensive and must never become clean — he arrives with something that works, not something mastered, and the cost is paid in his own flesh either way.
- **AT THE GAMES — THE TWIN SHORTS: SPENT (pin).** The s25/s28 forward ruling was *he brings both and does not draw*, with the switch available later in the bracket as a real escalation, made because he has to and never because he planned to. **Ch. 26 executed it exactly that way and the row is closed** — the wrap came off mid-bout against Elarine Sedrane, the blades are LIVE STEEL (*blunted* was never on any page, s28), Vask's prohibition is BROKEN, and Kael told his parents all of it. Full record in DYNAMIC above and `07/planted-clues` §THE FENCE BROKEN. **What is still forward is Vask's answer, which is owed and unwritten.**
- **Endgame kit:** dual daggers, both hands full, lightning threaded through the steel; sheathe-speed faster than most Resonants shape a resonance. Emerges much later (00). The signature is not the whole arsenal — dual daggers · throwing knives · a longsword.
- **The inheritance (00):** his parents' service weapons. Vask's sword is thrown to him as Vask dies; Neris's dagger becomes one of the pair, arriving by a route still open. The banked steel of the weapons school is this chest.
- **Arc:** innocence → disillusion → dangerous competence → the Requiem (00). Aims at the wrong enemy first — the coalition burned his island.
- **End of Book One:** ~13–14; fleeing by sea with Valeria and the princess. Father dead; mother, brother, master lost, fates unknown to him.
