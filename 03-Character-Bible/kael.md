## KAEL (protagonist)
**Glance:** POV protagonist · no house (sponsorless — "the master's charity case") · 12, as of Ch. 17

### STATIC
- **Role:** POV character; hidden son of Vask (ex-instrument) and Neris (ex-asset) of the program. Carrier of the rare KYNEMIR bloodline through his mother; kinetic affinity is dormant and leashed by training. The Ch. 4 knot is its first raw surfacing; he does not know. Surname unknown until the Temple.
- **Want:** Begins with wanting to touch the village lights, understand, and be measured. Evolves into a hunger for success, fame, and acknowledgment — to be noticed, measured, and remarked upon. He never sandbags. The coat hides what he is, never that he is good. Success therefore attracts both noble attention and peer resentment. Valeria eventually becomes the first to be his measure. He wants to learn about the world, go beyond the sea.
- **Wound:** Raised in loving secrecy; the world forbidden; parented through preparation instead of presence. At nine he discovers the founding promise was a lie and absorbs it silently — the first wound he carries alone. The settlement (Ch. 6) is his first impression of humanity: foundational, series-long trauma — distrust of strangers, crowds, touch, and group laughter (rendered as small physical tells, never labeled). It sits under the social blindness: his awkwardness is ignorance plus fear, and combat is his only social language with rules. He misfiles the night's defeat as his own insufficiency when in truth only the operator broke. His conscience is the one faculty nobody built — the series' moral spine. Left-forearm blade scar, stitched by Neris, echoing Vask's tally-marks; never explained.
- **APPETITE WITHOUT DESTINATION (ratified s20 — the arc's engine).** His want (success, measure, acknowledgment) is APPETITE, not purpose. He works the way he breathes, because he has always worked, and it has never once been going anywhere. Everyone around him answers with a *because* — a house, a name, a Line — and where his because should be there is only the work. **This is 01's thesis stated as a wound** (no destiny handed down), and it is the origin of the Requiem: the boy with no destination eventually makes one and pays everything for it. Beginning to make him want to WANT. Explored first with Elarine (full but heavy vs. empty but light — both burdens), later and deeper with Valeria (nothing but purpose, and it is not hers).
- **Trait:** CURIOSITY. Voracious love of learning; the itch that cannot be out-waited is the one urge stillness training cannot defeat. Parent-cultivated, rule-breaking, and exactly what the Temple prizes.
- **Trait:** THE CONCEALMENT INHERITANCE. From age nine he keeps secrets from his parents using their own schools, lovingly and in their voice — the seed of the Temple concealment and the series-long becoming-them thread.
- **Flaw:** SOCIAL BLINDNESS. Cannot read cues, hierarchy, mockery, or alliances; answers rhetorical questions literally. He detects the WHAT (anger, a lie, a turned room) and misreads the WHY (rule in 06); the gap closes at series scale, and past it his social reading becomes extremely proficient.
- **Abilities:** Technician, not powerhouse. LIGHTNING proficiency, unusually even generalist spread beneath it; LOW raw capacity; exceptional control, technique, tactics, threading, creativity — small, exact, fast, never bolts. True defining axis: intelligence + technique + fieldcraft + exceptional martial skill; the empire's capacity-worship is loudly wrong about him (emphasis rule in 06). His style is his parents' synthesis — father's economy of motion through mother's economy of power.
- **Method:** Runs controlled trials on his own initiative — asking twice with one thing changed. **THE EARTH-STEP COUNTER (Ch. 16):** solved her ground-sense by trial (four routes, one variable), understood the mechanism, and attacked the MEDIUM rather than the sense — a thread of earth breaking the stone's continuity under his own boot. Built with Elarine's principle and Aurelian's stupid-correct answer at the cistern court. She could not answer it. His first win off her, and the many-element road's first real proof: knowledge where he has no capacity. His empiricism against the nobles' capacity-worship is a Part II engine; under the deep layer his method is invisibly correct (00).
- **Lexicon:** Names his own disciplines and coins terms for discoveries the world lacks; the lexicon grows across the series. Holds "I don't know" as a tool.
- **Voice:** Matures with the prose (06).
- **Appearance:** Village-plain by design — dark hair (his mother's), unremarkable at rest; a wiry, light-through-the-shoulders build that hides its training completely — nothing to look at until he moves, and he is trained not to move. Eyes a pale grey belonging to no one in the house (00): the parents never remark on them, not once — their silence is the tell that never tells; the grey arrives only through other mouths or other eyes, no mirrors, no self-description; unspent on the page — spend when a Temple mouth wants a detail to be cruel or curious about. Left forearm: the blade scar, near his father's tally-marks in site and length. Marked older than his face admits. What others react to: nothing — which is the point, and slowly becomes the thing they react to.

### DYNAMIC
- **As of Ch. 17.** Kael 12; the autumn; the tournament lists go up tomorrow and his name will not be on them. Temple day-commuter; the mountain's schools in STEEL and into the night; the hour after the yard belongs to Valeria and then to the cistern court with her in it.
- **Goals:** beyond the sea; the tournament (forbidden — complied, devastated); Valeria's measure, fed and feared.
- **Concealments:** the coat (torn once publicly — the flash; benches sealed around "wet light"); the hidden contact (Halden's capital offer); THE BIND, written in the blank book and told to no one — DECLINED a second time in a bind with Valeria, by his own choice. **The threading he GAVE to Valeria, deliberately, without one word of explanation** — the first concealment he has ever spent on purpose, on a person. It stops with her: the cistern court does not get it. He cannot tell anyone why he is not enrolling, so the prohibition reads to his friends as an evasion with nothing in it. The parents KNOW about the flash — he told them himself, un-holed. He never sandbags: at home in Temple steel from the first hour, in front of everyone.
- **Relationships:** parents — prohibition live, anger named, believes the danger admission; Elarine — collegial ally, crush unlabeled; Aurelian — training ally; Oss — estranged; Valeria — rival, coming for the flash; Severin — sponsor and mentor; Halden — hidden contact, the wrongness filed unexamined.
- **Knows:** the men of his night died; two lightning houses asked after him and his mother; his parents are Resonants (lightning / air).
- **Does not know:** anything sealed in 00; the third house's man; Halden is Corvane's man; Oss was coerced.
- **Body:** the forearm scar; burn speckle-receipts wrist to second knuckle (411 more between spring and midsummer); proofing-bruises never above the collar or below the wrist — and now a second source that is not coat-aware at all: a split lip and a season of real cuts taken from Valeria, given and received without either of them ever discussing whether it was allowed to hurt.
- **Arc position:** first true "not enough" filed beside the misfiled one; the wall belief broken; the danger self-knowledge live and now UNGOVERNED BY ANY RULE — nobody forbade him the thread, so the only thing between him and spending it is himself. Withholds it from Valeria by his own decision. Slips 1–2 spent, slip 3 reserved for the fire.

### FORWARD
- **Endgame kit:** dual daggers, both hands full, lightning threaded through the steel; sheathe-speed faster than most Resonants shape a resonance. Emerges much later (00). The signature is not the whole arsenal — dual daggers · throwing knives · a longsword.
- **The inheritance (00):** his parents' service weapons. Vask's sword is thrown to him as Vask dies; Neris's dagger becomes one of the pair, arriving by a route still open. The banked steel of the weapons school is this chest.
- **Arc:** innocence → disillusion → dangerous competence → the Requiem (00). Aims at the wrong enemy first — the coalition burned his island.
- **End of Book One:** ~13–14; fleeing by sea with Valeria and the princess. Father dead; mother, brother, master lost, fates unknown to him.
