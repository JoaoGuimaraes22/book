## KAEL (protagonist)
**Glance:** POV protagonist · no house — the herald calls him **"of the Island"**, and the island calls him **THE GHOST** to his face · **14** — turned in a winter, marked by the narration and by nobody in the story · **the heist done and the crew destroyed for it: he robbed a Resonant-guarded imperial tax-house silent, then watched the fold burn — Selis, the warehouse man, Borren, all of them — and lived, again, because a father-figure spent himself to put him on a wagon. Six out with half the coin. He has now lost two fathers to a fire and does not know how a person carries walking away on somebody else's dying.**

### STATIC
- **Role:** POV character; hidden son of Vask (ex-instrument) and Neris (ex-asset) of the program. Carrier of the rare bloodline through his mother (what it is: 00). Surname unknown until the Temple.
- **Want:** Begins with wanting to touch the village lights, understand, and be measured. Evolves into a hunger for success, fame, and acknowledgment — to be noticed, measured, and remarked upon. He never sandbags. The coat hides what he is, never that he is good. Success therefore attracts both noble attention and peer resentment. Valeria eventually becomes the first to be his measure. He wants to learn about the world, go beyond the sea.
- **Wound:** Raised in loving secrecy; the world forbidden; parented through preparation instead of presence. At nine he discovers the founding promise was a lie and absorbs it silently — the first wound he carries alone. The settlement (Ch. 6) is his first impression of humanity: foundational, series-long trauma — distrust of strangers, crowds, touch, and group laughter (rendered as small physical tells, never labeled). It sits under the social blindness: his awkwardness is ignorance plus fear, and combat is his only social language with rules. He misfiles the night's defeat as his own insufficiency when in truth only the operator broke. His conscience is the one faculty nobody built — the series' moral spine. Left-forearm blade scar, stitched by Neris, echoing Vask's tally-marks; never explained.
- **APPETITE WITHOUT DESTINATION (ratified s20 — the arc's engine).** His want (success, measure, acknowledgment) is APPETITE, not purpose. He works the way he breathes, because he has always worked, and it has never once been going anywhere. Everyone around him answers with a *because* — a house, a name, a Line — and where his because should be there is only the work. **This is 01's thesis stated as a wound** (no destiny handed down), (where it goes is in 00). Beginning to make him want to WANT. Explored first with Elarine (full but heavy vs. empty but light — both burdens), later and deeper with Valeria (nothing but purpose, and it is not hers).
- **Trait:** CURIOSITY. Voracious love of learning; the itch that cannot be out-waited is the one urge stillness training cannot defeat. Parent-cultivated, rule-breaking, and exactly what the Temple prizes.
- **Trait:** DOUBT (author, s76). **He doubts everything, himself first.** Nothing arrives in him as settled — he re-asks what he has already answered, prices his own conclusions as probable rather than true, and can feel that he might be wrong while acting anyway. It is what makes the analysis worth reading and what makes the WHAT/WHY misses land: he reasons thoroughly and still gets there wrong.
- **Trait:** THE DREAMER. Deep and conflicted under the technician. He has wanted past the sea since he was four, with no *because* under the wanting (§APPETITE WITHOUT DESTINATION), and he is growing out of his naivety slowly, into a continent of war and corruption.
- **Trait:** THE CONCEALMENT INHERITANCE. From age nine he keeps secrets from his parents using their own schools, lovingly and in their voice — the seed of the Temple concealment and the series-long becoming-them thread.
- **Flaw:** SOCIAL BLINDNESS. Cannot read cues, hierarchy, mockery, or alliances; answers rhetorical questions literally. He detects the WHAT (anger, a lie, a turned room) and misreads the WHY (06 §Never explain). **Book One ran it as an always; from Book Two it moves (author, s59)** — where it has moved and what still blocks it is state (`../07-Story-Ledger/chapter-records.md`, Ch. 40); the destination is 00's.
- **Abilities:** Technician, not powerhouse. LIGHTNING proficiency, unusually even generalist spread beneath it; LOW raw capacity; exceptional control, technique, tactics, threading, creativity — small, exact, fast, never bolts. True defining axis: intelligence + technique + fieldcraft + exceptional martial skill; the empire's capacity-worship is loudly wrong about him (emphasis rule in 06). His style is his parents' synthesis — father's economy of motion through mother's economy of power.
- **THE COUNT — WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS (said out loud for the first time at Ch. 47).** Not arithmetic he performs: **he takes in the whole of what is in front of him, involuntarily, and it stays** — *I see what is in front of me. All of it. And it stays.* The numbers are the handle he holds it by, and they are of three kinds in his own account — **amounts** (eight boards, two trees, twenty soldiers), **time** (how long a man took to throw a spear, how long he looked at a face) and **distances** (how long a road is, how much space a room has). **The useless keeps too** — a whole wall he walked past at six, against not knowing his father's mother's name because nobody said it in front of him. **HE CANNOT STOP IT** (*there is nothing to try; it is not a thing I begin*), and **he believes it is ordinary** — nobody had ever asked him, so he had never had to find the words. The page has never named it and there is no term for it anywhere in the world. **It is the mechanism under the what/why split:** total intake, and the wrong conclusions drawn from it.
- **Method:** Runs controlled trials on his own initiative — asking twice with one thing changed. His empiricism against the nobles' capacity-worship is a Part II engine (00 has more). First proof on the page: the earth-step counter (Ch. 16) — he solved Valeria's ground-sense by trial and attacked the MEDIUM rather than the sense, knowledge standing in where he has no capacity; she could not answer it (mechanics: `../02-World-Bible/magic.md` §Blind Spot).
- **Lexicon:** Names his own disciplines and coins terms for discoveries the world lacks; the lexicon grows across the series. Holds "I don't know" as a tool.
- **Voice:** **The narration is his mind, not a camera** — the prose thinks the way he thinks, and the pleasure of the book is watching him think (06 §The voice is Kael's). Matures with him.
- **Appearance:** **A handsome boy, and filthy with it (author-ruled, s33)** — dark hair (his mother's), clean features under village dirt and weather, hair cut by somebody who was not being careful. Sun-weathered and wind-chapped from work at altitude, with **freckles across the nose** under the weather and the grime (ruled s35; unspent on any page). Lean, hard and visibly fit; strong without bulk, the strength sitting in the forearms and the hands. **He is not plain and never was: what makes him invisible is STATUS and STILLNESS, not his face** — no house, no name, working cloth, and a body trained not to move (the ornate royal register is Aeliana's; `appearance-ledger.md`). Eyes a pale grey belonging to no one in the house (00): the parents never remark on them, not once — their silence is the tell that never tells; the grey arrives only through other mouths or other eyes, no mirrors, no self-description; unspent on the page, and it stays so for now — **it comes to be important much later (author, s52), and no Temple mouth is owed**; **IMAGES ARE EXEMPT (author ruling, s42): plates may show the grey, so a mouth that names it CONFIRMS for a reader who has seen them rather than revealing** (`08-Plates/README.md`). Left forearm: the blade scar, near his father's tally-marks in site and length. Marked older than his face admits. What others react to: nothing — which is the point, and slowly becomes the thing they react to.

### DYNAMIC
**WHERE HE STANDS**
- **The crew is gone and he is one of six who walked out of the fold** — with Valeria, Aeliana, and Tirzah and the twins, in two wagons with fifteen chests each of the Emperor's coin. **He planned the heist that killed them** — the tax-house counted from a cut, the disguise, his mother's sleeping herb, the painted iron off his own wrists put on the officer's Art hand — and it went perfectly, and then the coalition came up the fold on Ambros's winter of letters and the empire's fire came down it, and everyone but the six died. **He tried to save Borren and could not**, and Borren made him do the count that proved it, and threw him onto the wagon with the last of the air. **The road names burned off** — he said *Aeliana*, Borren said *Valeria* — so the three surviving crew hold the real names now.
- **Inside the honest men, nine months into thirteen of passage paid in work** — started small (distractions, watch, drops) and then not small (a shipment, a lord's document, supplies over a hill, a letter up a stair). *Between Ishareth and Solvenna*; he does not know where, and has stopped building the country. The crew is fifteen; with the three of them, eighteen. **A winter kept in a town where nobody asked**, as a carrier's yard's boy — *he had never in his life been somewhere he was allowed to stay*; the jobs since *the same and faster*, and he is *not started small any more*. **Ahead: the job** — six years of a province's coin, told at the fire, its method unsaid; his two objections (soldiers; bodies) said to Borren alone, and Borren's paper-source said to him alone, read as a true thing meant to stop him looking.
- **He has the trade in pieces:** the seller's walk (Tirzah), the Koine with a town in it (Ambros), a lock with wet twine one-handed (the warehouse man), the drunk who is drunk on purpose (Borren), the road's smell by walking on it. He does what the job needs at the moment it needs it, without being asked, and *he had looked for the wanting … and it was not there.*
- **He is under orders and has obeyed once against his own sum.** At the bridge he had the six soldiers and the ford before Borren did, was told *we go over the bridge … and argue about it*, said nothing, and walked it; it worked, and *there was no way to run it twice with one thing changed.* Borren: *next time you find me one, I might use it … do keep your count up.* His family is on the far side of every slow day. **Borren used his ford** at a river in the fourth month — *Where is your ford* — and neither said a word.
- **His road name is his own** — *Kael*, and nothing after it, because a place that is not there any more is the whole of what else was true. The girls are **TAMAR** and **ADAH**, *my sister and my cousin*, their people dead. Fifteen people hold all three names.
- **The shape of his pay is his own doing:** the girls stay with the camp, their bread out of his share, **and the remainder is his and he takes it** — *a man on a job who is owed nothing is a man with nothing under his feet.*
- **He has Borren's name**, given at the door, held by nobody outside the crew, and he does not use it.
- **He carries the tenth of that chest** — small, heavier than it looks, already turned into something two children can carry through a gate, and **short of thirteen months**. The dead men's coin out of three hems is spent, and he has never mentioned it to either of them.
- **He carries the iron east and has not said why:** five bands and a chain in the sacking, on his back, one-handed.

**THE INSTRUMENT**
- **The count is the anchor and it is running.** He explained it out loud once, to the first person in thirteen years who asked, and still half-believes everybody does it. What *putting a thing down* means, in his own answer: *Stopped taking it out* — and he agreed it is not the same as its being gone.
- **The Art is fully back**, on him and on Valeria. Line-reinforcement and the threading (§Standing kit); **two at once empties him in a fifth of a second**, and it has never once been cheap.
- **He reads bodies extraordinarily and people probabilistically** (author, s74): he is already good and gets better, but he does not pretend to know the inside of a man off two days, and he prices roads rather than declaring them. His physical reading has never failed him; his social reading is a moving arc with bounded misses.
- **He never asks the question he wants** — you ask the one beside it and let the man arrive — which is his mother's, and came to him at nine dressed as something else. **He broke it once**, in the governor's chair, in front of strangers, and nothing on any page answered him.
- **The crew's rule ran ahead of his decision, once.** On the stair *not a body* arrived before he had decided anything — *the first time a rule ever arrived first* — and he opened a man's forearm along, not across, so the man let go and kept the arm. Nobody knows but him.
- **He fights the only way his ribs allow:** he cannot take a blow, cannot trade, cannot spend much, so all of it collapses into not being where the blow is. **He smiles when a fight turns critical, and knows it**; the instinct is delight, and the one instruction he ever got about it was *"You watch it"*, once.
- **He arrives inside things rather than beating them.** You do not beat a gate; you go in inside something it has already decided to let through.

**WHAT IS WRONG IN HIM, AND UNCORRECTED**
- **He named the wrong enemy out loud and has never taken it back**, and nobody has taken it back for him.
- **His verdict on Elarine and Aurelian is built out of one fact and is not correctable:** they were on the wall, he was under it. Nothing since has touched it — **and he counts them anyway** among the people the governor was the only thread to, and the page joins nothing up.
- **He blames the princess for the whole chain and has never once noticed his own hands in it** (00 §True causality). Nobody has confirmed or denied it.
- **He holds himself responsible for his father's death** on what he watched, and **has never been given the arithmetic**. His mother did not argue with it.
- **He does not have the princess.** She stood up and turned a doorway and he has not assembled why; her why was three feet away for two months, and the page says it was not that he could not see it.
- **He does not have the governor's why**, and *thieves* is dead with nothing to replace it.

**WHAT IT IS COSTING HIM**
- **He has watched a father spend himself to save him, twice, and lived both times on it** — Vask, then Borren — and a third man, the warehouse man, died putting him over the lip. He does not know how a person carries being the one who walks away from the fire on the strength of another's dying, and there is no one left on the wagon he can ask. **Borren was something of a son's father to him** (00: *something of a son … in the end*), and he is in the place where the dead fathers go, and there is only the one place and it is full.
- **A thing he put down did not stay down.** The sum on the nineteen comes out right from every end and they were also bought men; both are true and neither moves the other. **The mechanism he has relied on since the island has failed him once, nothing resolves it, and nobody knows.**
- **The part of him that would have argued has not said one word, three times now**, and the third time he had the count on it.
- **The thing that came after somebody else's blood no longer comes.** In his own mouth to the girls: *cut a man, stole from him, and felt nothing*, undated — *I noticed it was gone not too long ago*; he cannot tell whether nothing is what wrong feels like done enough or what right feels like done cleanly, and the two weigh the same. He stopped looking in the winter.
- **He hoped aloud once** — *I hope my brother is alive, and I hope somebody is feeding him* — having refused his mother the one time she asked him to say a thing that was not true; the princess asked, and he did it, and was thanked for what it cost.
- **Under the admiring of Valeria there is a thing with no place, and he thinks it might be envy** — not a word his mother ever put in front of him. She kept what she was made for; *he was not sure what he had been when he walked onto it. He was sure it was not what was sitting on this slope.*
- **He has met his own instrument in another head** — a man who counted thirty-one hulls off his own wall in the dark — and neither of them said so.
- **He lost it once**, standing over a bound man with the chain on the throat, *and he had not decided to do that either*; a stranger said *"Come back"* and he came back.
- **He wants three things, and it is the first time anything he wanted had a reason under it:** his mother and brother alive; to know what his parents were; and to kill the man who killed his father. The third is flat, cold and patient. **No vow, nothing aloud, and the man is not named on any page** (author, s50).

**BODY, AND WHAT IS STILL MENDING**
- **The left hand has shut** — in the spring the fingers came round onto a rope unasked; slow to open; it holds a rope, and a knife *if the knife did not have to do anything clever*; *It would not do what the right one did. Perhaps it would not ever do again.* Destroyed by his own right hand to get it out of the band, bitten through the palm, split along the old line on a stair; it holds by weight, and now by a slow grip.
- **He carries a plain soldier's sword** he can manage one-handed, three knives, and no longsword — his father's went with the box and **where it is now is on no page**; his own mismatched pair went with it.
- **Body:** the settlement blade scar on the left forearm, stitched by Neris; a second scar on the outside of the left arm above the elbow, off Lucan Drusane's lunge; the burn wrist to elbow over the blade scar, and speckle-receipts wrist to second knuckle; three ribs mended badly by his mother and strapped again at Qeshet; a month of salt, mended with the south's oils; two months in painted iron and thin with it; a cut across the top of the right shoulder and a spear-point drawn across the ribs on the left, down to the bone.
- **Does not sleep well** — *not since father—*, his own dating — and says he is not frightened. He slept at Qeshet, and it was not a sleep he chose.

**STANDING KIT**
- LINE-REINFORCEMENT — earth spent inward into the exact line where force folds, about two fingers wide; the constraint is AREA, not cost, so it costs about what a hard breath costs and repeats (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`). The threading, visible the length of the medium, held or let go at the knock; a threading through the bare hand cuts a working. Dual daggers begun and not had. He says *capacity*, not *the purse*.

**CONCEALMENTS**
- The hidden contact (Halden's capital offer) — his parents asked who the man was to his face and got *"a judge of the games"*, and his father died with that hole in him. **THE BIND**, written in the blank book, told to no one, declined twice, never attempted. The threading, shown to Valeria and stopping with her. **Avestan, kept from the crew** — he heard Oran reach for it and let his face do nothing.

**KNOWS**
- **The job as told at the fire — six years of a province's coin under a tax-house, to be moved west before the autumn, nobody paying — and that Borren held a written thing in his hand before its addressee did; five of the crew by name and where each came from (Tirzah, Selis, Oran, Talo, Ambros), and that Borren told them nothing of himself; that the princess knows which house Ambros meant and he does not.** That the governor of Qeshet opened his own coast for three nights and counted thirty-one hulls going past it, and that his reason was the arranging of Qeshet's own fall; **that Qeshet has been taken by the same force that took the island, and that nobody in Ishareth has heard it**; that the continent has been preparing for a great war and the island was the first spark; **that sixteen men went into that house, ten never came out of it, and the people in it were still fighting when the box left the yard.** That a princess of the empire lied for his family to the empire, in writing, and the name she gave him; that his parents are Resonants (lightning / air); that the family was hunted *because he is theirs*, was owned, and stopped being owned; that the coalition called his father the Phantom; that his brother's eye is red and the adults were not surprised; that the master has a hull he will not explain and *knows what she is*; **the red is in his mother's blood and in Wren's, her own eyes are like Wren's and hidden by will; the three true names; that the empire spread a few hundred years ago; that his mother prayed to the gods of the East.** That the iron is painted and the Art does not answer in it.
- **DOES NOT KNOW:** anything sealed in 00; **how a crew that does not leave bodies means to take a convoy, or who wrote what Borren held;** **who the man in the winter was, or what was in the thing that man sent; whether anybody in that house lived; whether his mother, Wren or the old man are alive; why he was taken, or why these three**; what the sleep was; why the princess stood up; that the reason under his count of twelve was wrong; what the black in the iron is; what the governor turned in the saddle to look for; whether the blood is in him; that Halden is Corvane's man; that Oss was coerced; why the delivery had to be Severin's; what his mother is; what the hull was stocked for; **that the gate was a decision about him**; and that the crew's method is the one he used at that toll gate.

**RELATIONSHIPS**
- **Mother** — the danger admission believed, the history refused, the question unasked, and held by her; on no page since the wake, alive or not unknown to him. **Father** — dead, MEMORY, and a sword he no longer has. **Wren** — his, and on no page. **Valeria** — the reason he is alive, the rematch never to be had, the hand she stopped; **he says *Val*, three times now, and nobody has remarked on it.** **Aeliana** — blamed to her face, taught Ivrit, held out of what he was selling, and now his cousin at a fire; she asked him whether it was wrong, asked him to hope, and thanked him for it. **Elarine and Aurelian** — the verdict standing, their comfort taken. **Oss** — dead, unrepaired. **Severin** — the boat's one teacher, a man who says things he does not source; on no page since the wake, and *ask me the rest of it on the other side of that water* still due. **Halden** — the man who shut the gate, and the hidden contact still hidden. **Borren** — the man he is under, who has said *Good* to him once and used his name once; whose road he has obeyed against his own sum; who used his ford once and crouched to his level once. **Tirzah, Ambros, the warehouse man** — the ones who taught him a piece each and never said so.

**ARC POSITION**
- The first true "not enough" filed beside the misfiled one; the wall belief broken; the danger self-knowledge live and **ungoverned by any rule but himself** — and now, for thirteen months, governed by somebody else's word. The first-person slips are spent and their count lives in one place (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md` §DEPARTURE LEDGER).

### FORWARD
- **THE EVOLVED THREADING (author, s39):** he has seen his father's whole-body form once and got half of it; he has not tried it, approached it or been taught it, and the mechanism and the road are in 00.
- **The inheritance:** Vask's sword is in his hands — given, not thrown. The rest of the steel is in 00.
- **Arc:** innocence → disillusion → dangerous competence → (00). Aims at the wrong enemy first — the coalition burned his island.
- **The people-reading (author, s74):** the book leads him into becoming a great reader of people, slowly — he is already good, and his conclusions stay probabilistic (the Ch. 52 council is the model: the read given as a read, the worst case priced, the ruling shared). The what/why split's motion is state (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md`); its destination is 00's.
- **End of Book One:** ~13–14, on a hull with no mast, drifting. Father dead. **His mother, his brother and the master are aboard with him** — the separation is Book Two's (author, s49), and no Book One page may arrange it.
