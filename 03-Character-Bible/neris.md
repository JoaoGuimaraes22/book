## NERIS (the mother)
**Glance:** Kael's mother; teacher of the mind · ex-asset of the program — bearer of the Kynemir bloodline (00) · age unpinned · **the child is days or a week away, as of Ch. 25**; the danger admission given; the birth collides with the games — she goes into labour as the semifinals begin (00 s26).

### STATIC
- **Name:** Chosen, not given (00). Belongs to no naming register; villagers assume it foreign; its true nature is sealed — never explain on the page.
- **Role:** The program's asset, harvested as a child — he was made; she was taken. The Kynemir inheritance is hers to give. Teaches Kael the mind: languages, far-counting, mechanics, herb-lore and reagent-craft, chemistry, and magic as perception — rationed in drops, the leash beneath (00). Her erudition is program-made; teaching Kael unknowingly completes the program's design.
- **Wound/Shame:** As Vask's, and deeper — the program's return aims at her lineage, and now her sons. Watches the sea for a ghost she believes she killed.
- **Flaw:** As Vask's. Not a perfect parent.
- **Abilities:** AIR proficiency (revealed at the declaration — one word, no demonstration); the Kynemir inheritance beneath it, unchanged (00). **She can switch her eyes off** (00 s20) — she always could; her control-first pedagogy is the discipline she survives by. Never explain on the page.
- **Teaching signature:** history rationed, never on asking; shut doors in her stories; the governor — "Smaller. Always smaller than you can." — leash and love in one instruction; her control-first pedagogy IS the leash (00; never explain). The relief-tell is spent — do not respend.
- **Appearance:** Dark-haired, dark-eyed; passes as village until she is still — then the stillness has a quality the village doesn't make. Fine bones under worked hands; the hands know too many trades — the lie's weak point. Handsome and unremarkable in the same breath, with ages of watching in the face.

### DYNAMIC
- **DAYS, OR A WEEK (Ch. 25).** *"He has been sideways since the turn and last night he was not, so he has decided something."* Her hand goes on top of it in front of Kael, which she has never done. *"You keep saying he." / "I do. I have no evidence whatever. Do not write it down."*
- **SHE STOPPED (Ch. 25), AND THE STOPPING IS THE BEAT.** Handed the largest operational event of the year — a royal summons on her son, alone — **she asked three questions and closed the file**: where the guards stood, whether anyone wrote anything down, and what exactly the woman behind the chair was wearing. Then *"That is enough of that for tonight… There is always more. It will be there in the morning."* She has never once stopped. **Kael cannot work out what has just happened to him, and nobody explains it.** The love is in the stopping and is stated by no one.
- **EIGHT OR NINE MONTHS PREGNANT and teaching harder than she ever has.** She works past her hour, which she has never done; she sits side-on to the table and reaches with one arm, having rearranged its whole surface a week before she needed to so that nobody would watch her need it.
- **She has moved Kael into formal scientific vocabulary on purpose** — retiring far-counting, what fire eats and the standing game to his face as the words of a child — and gives no source for the new ones. *"A great many more, and I am going to give them to you quickly, and I am not going to slow down."* / *"It is true, and it is not the reason. - **The pregnancy was TOLD, not deduced** — plainly, to Kael, in answer to *why not now*; not a secret and never was. The birth falls in the spring; **Severin is to take it, and has found the hull** (whether he reaches the house is 00 s26's, not hers — see FORWARD). She watches the sea every night. - **State:** runs every debrief in her built sequence whatever the subject, and does not go to the window when she is frightened. Carries the argument on every operational question — the hulls that talk and the hulls that write, the watched quays, and the hill (*a boy who stops going is a boy worth asking about*). Refuses Kael the history in the same words as last year and says so: *"It is still the only true one."* Holds that the man on the road may be an empire noticing a clever boy rather than their own past, and cannot prove it. Twice this summer she stood in the doorway while her son came in out of the dark, hand on the frame, knuckles pale, and said nothing.
- **Spent, do not respend:** the danger admission; the one shown fear; the relief-tell.
- **Knows:** the escalation (via Severin); the public flash, in full — she debriefed him on it herself. **Does not know:** the hidden contact; the bind idea; that Kael cracked her night-watch pattern; that he knows the sea never freezes; who Halden is — the name meant nothing to her.
