## NERIS (the mother)
**Glance:** Kael's mother; teacher of the mind · ex-asset of the program — bearer of the bloodline (00) · age unpinned, **and the same as Vask's (author, s34)** · **on no page since the night at Qeshet; her son does not know whether she is alive and has stopped building the sum, which has three ends and no way to choose; what she did that night is 00's**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Chosen, not given (00). Belongs to no naming register; villagers assume it foreign; its true nature is sealed (00) and unexplained on the page.
- **Role:** The program's asset, harvested as a child — he was made; she was taken. The bloodline is hers to give. Teaches Kael the mind: languages, far-counting, mechanics, herb-lore and reagent-craft, chemistry, and magic as perception — rationed in drops (00).
- **Wound/Shame:** As Vask's, and deeper — the program's return aims at her lineage, and now her sons. Watches the sea for a ghost she believes she killed.
- **Flaw:** As Vask's. Not a perfect parent.
- **Abilities:** AIR proficiency (revealed at the declaration — one word, no demonstration); the bloodline beneath it, unchanged (00), unexplained on the page.
- **Teaching signature:** history rationed, never on asking; shut doors in her stories; the governor — "Smaller. Always smaller than you can." — and what else that instruction is doing is in 00 (unexplained). The relief-tell is spent.
- **Appearance:** Dark-haired, dark-eyed; passes as village until she is still — then the stillness has a quality the village doesn't make. Fine bones under worked hands; the hands know too many trades — the lie's weak point. Handsome and unremarkable in the same breath, with ages of watching in the face.

### DYNAMIC
- **SHE IS ON NO PAGE FROM CH. 39, AND WHETHER SHE IS ALIVE IS UNKNOWN** to Kael and to the reader. What the page has of that house is not her: sixteen men in, ten who never came out, **and the people in it still fighting when the box went out of the yard** — no name, no outcome, and *there is nobody left alive who knows*, because Qeshet has been taken. MYSTERY begins here (FORWARD); the truth is 00's.
- **HER ART HAS NO BOTTOM THAT HER SON COULD FIND.** Air at mass scale, offensive and then defensive at saturation, priced to the grain by Kael on both occasions and found bottomless. **Her face was turned away for the whole of both and nobody saw it.** What she is is Book Two's (`../07-Story-Ledger/standing-rules.md`). **She has explained nothing and will not.**
- **HER OWN CONTROL FAILED ONCE AND HER EYES CAME UP RED.** Severin and the princess saw it; **Kael did not**; the girl is sworn. **She does not know that she was seen.**
- **SHE ANSWERED HIM, ONCE, AND FENCED THE REST.** The three true names and where each is spoken; that Avestan is the continent's old tongue; that she taught him all of it *so that there would be nowhere on this continent you could not go*; that the red is *something in my blood*, old, hers, and in Wren; that she is **not sure** whether it is in Kael and hoped it was not; **and that her own eyes are like Wren's, hidden by *great will and control*** — told, never shown. She fenced the old man, deferred the rest to another roof, and made her son say *Nobody*.
- **HER TRUTH, SAID ONCE, AS *MY* TRUTH:** *There are no enemies. There are no allies. All we can be sure of, is family.* Said where she had always said *the truth*, to a son who had just named the wrong enemy. She told him aloud that he is intelligent and that she is proud of him.
- ***ASK ME ON THE WATER* STANDS AS A PROMISE INSTEAD OF AN ANSWER.** She felt him take the breath and offered nothing; **she has not volunteered it.**
- **THE CONCEALMENT IS OFF HER.** She cried in front of Kael, which he had never seen, and called him *my love*, which she had never called him. Unexplained, and not walked back.
- **She moved Kael into formal scientific vocabulary on purpose** and gave no source — *"It is true, and it is not the reason. Ask me in a year."* **The fingerprint has stopped being deniable and the seal is untouched:** no name, no source, no doctrine (`../02-World-Bible/curriculum.md`).
- **She takes her son's count as fact and works off it**, and softens nothing for him.
- **What she conceals:** why the delivery had to be Severin's (Kael has the cover reason, which is true and is not the reason); the where, and *the rest*; what the blood is beyond *old, mine, in him*.
- **Knows:** the public flash in full; the bouts and the blades. **Does not know:** the hidden contact; the bind idea; that Kael cracked her night-watch pattern; who Halden is; **that she was seen.**
- **Body:** a herder's hands; marked in three places — two from mending her son's ribs out of her own element while in labour, quartz-clean and permanent, and the third at the front of her hair from the night nobody watched. **Nobody says what any of it cost and nobody will.**
- **Spent:** the danger admission; the one shown fear; the relief-tell.

### FORWARD
- **The chest (00):** her old dagger lay under the floorboards with Vask's sword. Whether the Ch. 4 night-watch blade IS this dagger is a VALVE, open by design (`../04-Outline.md` §Open valves). Where the dagger goes is in 00.
- **Status:** **SEPARATED FROM KAEL (Ch. 38–39):** the no-farewell on the page in the governor's bed, the parting at the wake — he woke a day east in a box without her. **Her fate is unconfirmed on the page from Ch. 39** — a low flame under the whole series; what she did that night is 00's. She becomes MYSTERY as Vask becomes MEMORY: Kael's archaeology of his father ends; his archaeology of his mother never does.
