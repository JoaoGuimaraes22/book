## HALDEN (the judge — the smiling man; introduced Ch. 13)
**Glance:** "a judge of the games," two springs early · **EMPIRE — not Sanctum, not program (00 s21)**; also Corvane's man (off-page canon) · grey at the temples · **he entered Kael in the games and told him so; the fuse is still unlit.**

### STATIC
- **Public role:** A judge arrived early to walk the tournament ground; broad, comfortable, "a voice like a warm room"; a wool-town northerner risen through the empire's ladder, by his own telling. On-page mostly "the judge."
- **The truth (off-page canon — never on any page yet):** **EMPIRE (00 s21, author-ratified): not Sanctum, not program, no knowledge of either.** His warrant is correct and he reaches inside another man's school lawfully — the horror is that all of it is done correctly. AND CORVANE'S MAN — the pan-away's third-house man resolved; coordinating with house Drusane; the pressure on Vessaline ran through this campaign. Mission: build the dossier on the charity case, isolate him, find the lever — found it (the want; the capital offer). Whether he serves only Corvane is open.
- **Neither parent will ever recognise him:** no history with either. The name meant nothing to them.
- **Method:** Never asks anything a coat could catch; contacts with the shape of accidents and the timing of appointments; warmth that runs level for anyone — the tell Kael filed and shut away; asks AROUND the target. The lure is the truth: the capital is real, the ladder is real.
- **Character Law (to develop):** if the wool-town origin is true, a small man who climbed the ladder he sells — a man who believes the offer he weaponizes. Not a cartoon.
- **Appearance:** Broad and comfortable — weight worn easy, built like good furniture; grey at the temples; nothing about him hurries. People relax around him — which is the instrument.

### DYNAMIC
- **Now:** he has ENTERED KAEL TO THE GAMES under the houseless provision, sealed and lodged in the capital nine days before the standings closed — so he knew where the boy would fall before the boy did — and confessed it to Kael's face, warmly, never once cruel: explained the provision, priced his own gain openly, refused to undo it, said he would not if he could, **asked for nothing**, and pointedly did not ask who had forbidden the boy.
- **State:** he reaches above the First Master and Severin cannot touch it. The dossier holds an untraceable technique on a sponsorless boy, witnessed. The lever is still the want. **The fuse is still unlit and the parents do not know he ever reached their son.** He watched the first bout from the judges' enclosure, smiling, and had been for some while; Kael did not see it, the reader did.
- **Open (do not resolve):** why his face spent nothing at the flash — he has seen the thing before, or he was expecting it, or (Ch. 23's third reading) he is a man watching an investment come in.
