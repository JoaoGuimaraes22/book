## HALDEN (the judge — the smiling man; introduced Ch. 13)
**Glance:** "a judge of the games," two springs early · **THE HARVEST — the program's collection arm (00 s38)**, and genuinely Corvane's man · grey at the temples · **he turned the district's council toward evacuation — and then stayed behind on the wall.**

### STATIC
- **Public role:** A judge arrived early to walk the tournament ground; broad, comfortable, "a voice like a warm room"; a wool-town northerner risen through the empire's ladder, by his own telling. On-page mostly "the judge."
- **The truth (off-page canon — never on any page of Book One):** **HE IS THE HARVEST (00 s38).** Post-rebellion generation — the program that rebuilt itself during the parents' years of peace — so nothing Vask or Neris know can reach him. **His imperial standing is real and is not a costume:** a correct warrant, reach above the First Master, genuine work for house Corvane. That is the market layer sheltering the harvest in one body (02), and it is why every horror he commits is done correctly.
- **WHAT HE IS ACTUALLY DOING HERE (00 s38 — do not write him as collecting a child):** the Harvest is **hunting VASK and NERIS.** Kael is the road to them, not the crop. The dossier, the level warmth, the questions asked around the target, the Ch. 18 tail — all of it is that hunt.
- **HE IS GENUINELY STRONG — body AND Art (00 s38). NEVER REMARKED ON** (06 §Staging, the Severin's-fern family). Its one use is Ch. 29; the page keeps it deniable and nothing points at it.
- **Neither parent will ever recognise him:** no history with either. The name meant nothing to them, and that is true, not a lie.
- **Method:** Never asks anything a coat could catch; contacts with the shape of accidents and the timing of appointments; warmth that runs level for anyone — the tell Kael filed and shut away; asks AROUND the target. **The lure is the truth** — the capital is real, the ladder is real, and at the gate the arithmetic is real. His stated reason is always true and is never the reason.
- **Character Law (to develop):** if the wool-town origin is true, a small man who climbed the ladder he sells — a man who believes the offer he weaponizes. Not a cartoon, and never cruel; his register is agricultural, not sadistic.
- **Appearance:** Broad and comfortable — weight worn easy, built like good furniture; grey at the temples; nothing about him hurries. People relax around him — which is the instrument.

### DYNAMIC
- **Now: inside the district, and STAYING.** He turned the council — his word carried where the First Master's did not, because he is a house's man and Severin is not — and then took himself out of the escape party. **His reason is true and is not the reason:** Corvane has men on the eastern stretch and Corvane's business is his to answer for. **Nobody remarks on it, then or ever.** Under 00 s38 a boat going away from the mountain is no use to him; **the page never points at it.**
- **He sat in a room with Valeria Vel for two hours and NOTHING about him reached the page.** He gave a judge's vantage, priced the fleet honestly, and **removed himself from his own count of who could hold that wall** — deniable as modesty, readable only by somebody holding 00 s38.
- The fuse is still unlit and the parents still do not know he ever reached their son.
- **Now:** inside the nobles' district, having come out into an eighteen-inch gap, taken Valeria Vel through it by the arm, and shut it on Kael.
- **He ENTERED KAEL TO THE GAMES** under the houseless provision, sealed and lodged in the capital nine days before the standings closed — so he knew where the boy would fall before the boy did — and confessed it to his face, warmly, never once cruel: explained the provision, priced his own gain openly, refused to undo it, said he would not if he could, **asked for nothing**, and pointedly did not ask who had forbidden the boy.
- **At the gate he does the same thing with the opposite outcome, and neither is cruel and neither is personal.** He states the arithmetic out loud and it is correct — a gap the width of two lets thirty thousand through and the wall falls at noon instead of tonight. He tells Kael the far road closed at the second hour. He refuses to pretend he is sorry in a way that costs him nothing, refuses to ask him for anything, and gives him a true and useful instruction on the way out: get off the road, go high, go east.
- **He is the one man in the empire that Severin's name cannot reach** — he reaches above the First Master, which was established at the entry and is paid here: Kael plays that card at the gap and it was dead before he played it.
- **State:** the dossier holds an untraceable technique on a sponsorless boy, witnessed. **The fuse is still unlit and the parents still do not know he ever reached their son** — the gate advances it hard and defuses nothing. He watched the first bout from the judges' enclosure, smiling, and had been for some while.
- **Resolved by s38:** why his face spent nothing at the flash. Do not re-flag it as open; do not explain it on the page either.
