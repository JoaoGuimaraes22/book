## AELIANA CASSIVARA VESSANTINE (the princess)
**Glance:** royal princess; the raid's prime target · House Vessantine (the ruling dynasty; the empire is the Vessantine Empire) · **14 — one year above Kael and Valeria** · **standing in the yard of a herder's croft on a burning mountain, inside eight guards, in the shoes she wore to dinner.**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine; "Cassivara" is an honor-name in the formal chain.
- **Role:** Attends the Grand Tournament; escapes with Kael and Valeria. Function: the empire at close range — the Trojan horse into the imperial storyline.
- **Naming device (UNSPENT):** her name shortens as her empire recedes — the full ceremonial chain at the tournament; "Aeliana" among the trio; "Liana"/"Ana" reserved as an intimacy beat (parallel to "Val"). On the page so far: the full chain or "the princess" only. The first shortening is a beat; do not spend it casually.
- **Character (author ruling, s29): ARROGANT, PRIDEFUL, NOT EVIL** — the arrogance of someone who has never once been contradicted and does not know that is a condition. Every branch of her arc (00) survives that.
- **Appearance (SEALED at her chapter — Ch. 22):** **unmatched, radiant, royal beauty.** She is the most beautiful person in the book and is never in competition with anyone for it; the cast is kept deliberately plain (appearance-ledger) and she is the exception the plainness exists to frame. Not tall. **Long SILVER hair — the VESSANTINE HOUSE TRAIT, not a drain-mark** (author ruling, s33; the mark is pure quartz white and distinct in kind). **Pale blue eyes.** Both UNSPENT: Ch. 22 gives the gown, the gold and the construction of the hair and never once its colour. Long, and **worn LOOSE by default** (author, s33); the pinned construction is the TOURNAMENT state, not her standing look. *Prose caution: the book already spends the word* silver *on the Silver league; her hair will want another word on the page, or careful placement away from it.* Blue so dark it reads black until the light moves on it; gold at shoulder and hem in a quantity with no function except to be a quantity; hair up off the neck in a construction that took more than one person more than an hour. **Pale untouched skin, with freckles across the nose and cheeks** (ruled s35; unspent on any page). **RENDERING RULE (hard — 03's register): never a narrator verdict.** It arrives two ways only. **(1) THE RADIUS** — what a room does in her presence; at the games eleven thousand people stand, and the sound underneath the standing is not a cheer and not a roar, and is the sound of a very large number of people being glad about a thing they will never be nearer to. **(2) KAEL'S INSTRUMENTS FAILING** — he looks for reach, weight, what the shoulders were taught, where the hands go unoccupied, and gets nothing; and it is explicitly NOT concealment, which he was raised inside and can watch his own mother do across a room. There is nothing under it to get — at arena distance and at conversational range alike. **Both halves are true at once and the paradox is the point:** she is genuinely radiant AND she was built from the ground up to be looked at by exactly that many people at exactly that distance. Kael's verdict — *the kind of beautiful that is also a uniform* — arrives from nowhere he can name, and stays with him for years.

### DYNAMIC
- **Now: in a mountain yard**, six miles walked in court shoes, the gown ruined to the knee, the hair down on one side, inside eight of her twelve. **She does not look at the house.** She looks at the orange on the underside of the cloud and goes on looking at it.
- **Previously: in the district, in the escape party, with her twelve.** **A VEL IS ON HER LEFT** — ordered to give her life for her — and the princess has not been told and would not price it if she had.
- **She spoke once in a war council about her own survival and it was the wrong thing, and nobody corrected her**, because nobody ever has: *"And my guard would be with me the entire way." / "Then I do not see the difficulty."* A room of nineteen turned its head to her when she spoke and **she did not notice a room doing it.**
- **There was nobody in that district to say goodbye to her.** Every other child in that party was held on to; she and Valeria were the two that nobody had hold of.
- **Now:** in the arena as it comes apart, with imperial security collapsing inward around her; her twelve royal guard are dark grey, arranged across the distances, and none of them Vel (02/factions). Last seen forward in her chair with a hand on the rail during the round of 8, watching the thing she could not see at conversational range move, and not answering the woman behind her.
- **The audience is spent (Ch. 24), and she ended it angry.** She sent for Kael to look at a thing up close, found nothing to him and said so to her woman in the third person with him turned toward the lamp, and was looked at instead — corrected about Drusane in front of her household, asked what happens to the food on her table and where the ice came from, which she has no idea of and cannot see why she would. Cold, and fourteen: *"Kneel. Say your farewells. And leave afterwards."* He knelt and had no farewell.
- **What she has of him:** nothing her instruments can price, which is very annoying; a boy with no royal reflex at all, whose rudeness was absence, not defiance.
