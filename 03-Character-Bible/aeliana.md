## AELIANA CASSIVARA VESSANTINE (the princess)
**Glance:** royal princess; the raid's prime target · House Vessantine (the ruling dynasty; the empire is the Vessantine Empire) · **14 — one year above Kael and Valeria**, as of Ch. 22 · arrived on the island for the games; on-page from the opening day.

### STATIC
- **Name:** Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine; "Cassivara" is an honor-name in the formal chain.
- **Role:** Attends the Grand Tournament; escapes with Kael and Valeria. Function: the empire at close range — the Trojan horse into the imperial storyline.
- **Naming device:** her name shortens as her empire recedes — the full ceremonial chain at the tournament; "Aeliana" among the trio; "Liana"/"Ana" reserved as an intimacy beat (parallel to "Val"). The first shortening is a beat; do not spend it casually.
- **Appearance (SEALED at her chapter — Ch. 22):** **unmatched, radiant, royal beauty.** She is the most beautiful person in the book and is never in competition with anyone for it; the cast is kept deliberately plain (appearance-ledger) and she is the exception the plainness exists to frame. Not tall. Blue so dark it reads black until the light moves on it; gold at shoulder and hem in a quantity with no function except to be a quantity; hair up off the neck in a construction that took more than one person more than an hour. **RENDERING RULE (hard — 03's register): never a narrator verdict.** It arrives two ways only. **(1) THE RADIUS** — what a room does in her presence; at the games eleven thousand people stand, and the sound underneath the standing is not a cheer and not a roar, and is the sound of a very large number of people being glad about a thing they will never be nearer to. **(2) KAEL'S INSTRUMENTS FAILING** — he looks for reach, weight, what the shoulders were taught, where the hands go unoccupied, and gets nothing; and it is explicitly NOT concealment, which he was raised inside and can watch his own mother do across a room. There is nothing under it to get. **Both halves are true at once and the paradox is the point:** she is genuinely radiant AND she was built from the ground up to be looked at by exactly that many people at exactly that distance. Kael's verdict — *the kind of beautiful that is also a uniform* — arrives from nowhere he can name, and stays with him for years.

### DYNAMIC
- **ON-PAGE from the opening day of the games (Ch. 22).** Arrives by water so the whole arena watches her arrive; announced by the full ceremonial chain; received at the rail by the First Master, who bows his grey head to her. **Twelve royal guard in dark grey attend her, arranged across the distances rather than in front of her — none of them Vel.** She has not looked at Kael and does not know he exists.
