## AELIANA CASSIVARA VESSANTINE (the princess)
**Glance:** royal princess; the raid's prime target · House Vessantine · **15 — one year above Kael and Valeria; her age is on no page** · **she sewed the empire's blue into the crew's disguise, laughed at the last fire without her hand over it, and drove a wagon of her father's coin out of a massacre when Kael put the reins in her hands and said her real name — *Adah* is off her now, and the crew she laughed with is dead**

### STATIC
- **Name:** Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine; "Cassivara" is an honor-name in the formal chain.
- **Role:** Attends the Grand Tournament; escapes with Kael and Valeria. Function: the empire at close range — the Trojan horse into the imperial storyline.
- **Naming register:** *the princess* by default, the full ceremonial chain where the empire is being invoked, and **the bare given name in narration, on the page from Ch. 34** (author-ruled, s49) — it arrived when she stopped being an institution in that party and became one of eight people on a mountain. The device is in 00 and its later steps are untouched and still reserved.
- **Character (author ruling, s29): ARROGANT, PRIDEFUL, NOT EVIL** — the arrogance of someone who has never once been contradicted and does not know that is a condition. Every branch of her arc (00) survives that.
- **Appearance (SEALED at her chapter — Ch. 22):** **unmatched, radiant, royal beauty.** She is the most beautiful person in the book and is never in competition with anyone for it; the cast is kept deliberately plain (appearance-ledger) and she is the exception the plainness exists to frame. Not tall. **Long SILVER hair — the VESSANTINE HOUSE TRAIT, not a drain-mark** (author ruling, s33; the mark is pure quartz white and distinct in kind). **Pale blue eyes.** Both UNSPENT: Ch. 22 gives the gown, the gold and the construction of the hair and never once its colour. Long, and **worn LOOSE by default** (author, s33); the pinned construction is the TOURNAMENT state, not her standing look. *Prose caution: the book already spends the word* silver *on the Silver league; her hair will want another word on the page, or careful placement away from it.* Blue so dark it reads black until the light moves on it; gold at shoulder and hem in a quantity with no function except to be a quantity; hair up off the neck in a construction that took more than one person more than an hour. **Pale untouched skin, with freckles across the nose and cheeks** (ruled s35; **the freckles spent Ch. 54** — *over the nose*, seen by Kael after two months out of doors, and he cannot say whether they were always there under the paint). **HOW IT REACHES THE PAGE (03's register): not as a narrator verdict.** It has arrived two ways. **(1) THE RADIUS** — what a room does in her presence; at the games eleven thousand people stand, and the sound underneath the standing is not a cheer and not a roar, and is the sound of a very large number of people being glad about a thing they will never be nearer to. **(2) KAEL'S INSTRUMENTS FAILING** — he looks for reach, weight, what the shoulders were taught, where the hands go unoccupied, and gets nothing; and it is explicitly NOT concealment, which he was raised inside and can watch his own mother do across a room. There is nothing under it to get — at arena distance and at conversational range alike. **Both halves are true at once and the paradox is the point:** she is genuinely radiant AND she was built from the ground up to be looked at by exactly that many people at exactly that distance. Kael's verdict — *the kind of beautiful that is also a uniform* — arrives from nowhere he can name, and stays with him for years.

### DYNAMIC
**WHERE SHE STANDS**
- **She drove the escape wagon out of the fold.** Kael put the reins in her hands — *Aeliana. You have carried the water. Now carry it out* — and she took them and set her feet, because she had ruled on harder things in worse rooms. She is one of six out with half the coin.
- **She sewed the empire's blue into the disguise** (Ch. 56) — the blue at the collar and cuff, which must sit exactly or a man who has worn the coat knows, and she has stood next to a thousand: her empire's weakening stitched by her own hand to keep herself alive. At the last feast she laughed properly, without her hand over her mouth, and would buy *blue ribbon* and put her head on Tirzah's shoulder; Ambros's last word was for her, *I am sorry to the girl. She sewed the blue.*
- **The crew she called family are dead** — Selis in the traces, the warehouse man, Ambros, Borren — and her real name is off her in front of the three who lived; at Solvenna she must be the princess again, and does not know if she wants to.
- **She is *ADAH* on the road**, one of the two plain coast names; *our people are dead* was said for her. **Her sleeves stay down over her wrists.** **She has gone brown**, hands hard from daily work; she does camp work nobody told her to, having watched Tirzah once. **She speaks to the crew in the Koine and in Ivrit, and to Selis in something that is neither**, which Selis answers and Kael does not have.
- **She is hidden by a scarf and by ordinariness**, and nobody looks at her twice. **The hair is the one thing that cannot be changed: silver, and nothing else in the world that colour** (author, s55: unique). Her eyes' colour is still unspent.
- **She was asked whether standing on that bank was her own choice and said *"It is. Entirely."*** — the outlaw's own procedure doing what no court ever did. **What she is stays unsaid by everybody**, and the crew's leader enforces the not-saying himself.
- **She knows which house Ambros meant.** *I know which one it was*, said in the dark to two people, in a voice that was not the one she uses for ruling things. **No page says what she does with it.**
- **She asked whether it is wrong** — the water carried for a strongbox, a letter, a copper — and *I find I cannot rule on it. It is the first thing I have not been able to rule on*; what the empire is she had only ever heard *from people who were paid to tell me*.
- **She likes Adah.** *Nobody has ever looked at Adah twice … It is the most restful thing that has ever happened to me in my life. I did not know it was a weight until it came off.* She did the work so nobody would watch and found she *wanted to be a person who did it*.
- **The coin is her father's and she did not stand up.** *It is his and it is wrong to take it. It is his and it was taken. I cannot make those into one thing* — and she carries the water anyway, not knowing what that makes her.
- **She counts what she has not heard** — no princess found dead, no woman with a bound child taken, no First Master anywhere — calls nothing a good sound, and asked Kael to say so knowing he could not.
- **She has never been asked what she wants, only told what she will have**, and has no answer to the first. **At Solvenna she must be the princess again**, and does not know if she wants to.

**WHAT SHE IS**
- **She rules, and the ruling is final** — *Then I have ruled. We go.* She takes the decision *we make together* and closes it, and she reads agreement off faces and says so.
- **She has never been contradicted, and does not know that is a condition.**
- **She spends her rank on other people now.** *I have had twelve royal guard stand beside me and they all died for me, and I won't have it again* — the first time she has ever spent it on somebody's behalf rather than her own.
- **Her one currency is her word, and she knows exactly what it is worth:** their names read at court with their houses standing, *and I shall have it written down.* She prices twelve dead royal guard as correct and refuses to be delicate about it *because that would make it smaller than it was.* **Not evil, and uncorrected on the page.**
- **She refuses the form from Valeria now** — *"Highness," / "Do not."*; still, in the summer tent — *"Highness." / "I have told you."*
- **She is good at intelligence and knows she is.** She built the boats out of four mouths that had not spoken to each other, tracked as one fact moving through a yard and being repeated wrong; told she had done it correctly, **she was extremely pleased with herself and hid it badly.**
- **She does not look away** — the hand, the door, the questioning, from the first lie to the last breath.
- **She asks for the dangerous half of a job.** She took the foraging so Kael could throw, learned the greens in two days, and argued him into the poisons: *I would rather know a thing and not need it.*
- **She speaks Ivrit on the page** and may have a knack for it; about forty words by the fourth day and growing, used on people whether they fit or not. **The first word she wanted was thank you.**
- **She is good with small children and needs no language for it.**
- **She corrects Kael about the things only she can see** — that nobody looks at faces, they look at whoever is being looked after, which is the shape Valeria has been standing in since Qeshet.

**WHAT SHE HAS DONE, AND WHAT IT COST**
- **She lied for them, in writing, to the empire** — *Lucan Drusane, the Lady Drusane, and the child* — and told the governor to write it so. **Nobody at that table named what it was**, and Kael has decided it was pride. **The lie is hers, and she is the one who will not lean on it:** *I would not put weight on that letter.*
- **She stood up at the door**, having been told to do nothing, not to stand, not to run — and it is why they are alive. **She never says why; nobody asks; Kael does not get there.**
- **She stabbed a man to protect Kael and disobeyed her instructions to do it**, and did not let go.
- **She asked for his life with two empty hands** — a deliberate double knock on wood, then her hands out into the lamplight, open and turned up, iron above the wrists. **Kael has both only as fragments he cannot attribute, and nobody has told him.**
- **She was told what she is worth, to her face**, and answered with the child's name.
- **She was told she was not worth it, by a boy who then came at her** — the first contradiction of her life. **What she made of it is unresolved.**
- **She took hold of Valeria with both arms and said nothing** — the second thing she has ever done for her with her hands, after the strip off her own hem.
- **She came apart in the box and put herself back together**, and Kael never got to the truth of it. **The upright is back.**
- **She is sworn, and she does not know what she saw.** *Nothing I have seen in this room leaves it.* **She saw Neris's eyes come up red and has no idea what that was**, and carries it.
- **She apologises when she notices** — *"Three children," she said. "I am sorry. That was badly done."*

**WHAT SHE HAS OF KAEL**
- Nothing her instruments can price, which is very annoying: a boy with no royal reflex at all, whose rudeness was absence and not defiance, who asked where the food on her table comes from, who has blamed her to her face and taught her a language and held her out of a trade she does not know about. **The seed of a distaste for royalty is planted in him and unassembled.** **She has still not been told he held her address all day in front of strangers before he gave it.**

**BODY**
- Hands unwrapped, mended, and held a little open; six miles of goat ground taken on the palms; a month of salt that took the gown's colour, the skin off her hands and the flesh off her face **and did nothing to her hair**. Her guard are all dead behind her, and a Vel is on her left.
