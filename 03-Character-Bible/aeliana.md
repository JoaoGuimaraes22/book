## AELIANA CASSIVARA VESSANTINE (the princess)
- **Name (CANON):** Aeliana Cassivara Vessantine, princess of House Vessantine (the ruling dynasty; the empire is the Vessantine Empire). "Cassivara" is an honor-name in the formal chain.
- **Role:** royal princess attending the Grand Tournament; the raid's prime target; escapes with Kael and Valeria.
- **Function:** the empire at close range — the Trojan horse into the imperial storyline. Long arc undecided (see 00).
- **Naming device:** her name shortens as her empire recedes — the full ceremonial chain at the tournament; "Aeliana" among the trio; "Liana"/"Ana" reserved as an intimacy beat (parallel to "Val"). The first shortening is a beat; do not spend it casually.
- **Status (end of Book One):** in the boat; a symbol learning to boil water.
