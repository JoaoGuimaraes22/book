## THE HONEST MEN — THE NAMED FIVE: TIRZAH · SELIS · ORAN · TALO · AMBROS
**Glance:** five of Borren's fifteen (`../02-World-Bible/factions.md` §THE HONEST MEN) · each from a different corner of the continent, each with the war's story · **named at the first fire, their own mouths, to three children under two false names and one true one.**

### STATIC — the group
- **Role:** the crew's faces. Character Law applies to each; what the page has is a name, a place and one story each, told once. Nothing below is a personality yet.
- **Naming register:** each in the register of where they came from (`../02-World-Bible/naming.md` §THE ROUND). One name each, no house — a steward is not a house.
- **How they were introduced:** Borren's *you should know what they are called*, then a round that nobody announced — *it went round anyway, the way the bread had* — and the not-asking the whole crew does, one face at a time.

### TIRZAH — the woman with the basket
- About thirty; of the coast three days west of Qeshet, a town she will not name; a stall of cloth and thread. The sharpest questioner in the rope-walk (Ch. 49); the one who carried the token up the stair; the one who arms Kael (Ch. 50); greeted by more of the crew than anybody.
- **Her story:** a governor's son took a length of blue ribbon off her stall and did not pay; her husband went after him to be paid, and the watch had him hanging from the gate by evening. *That is the basket off my stall. It is what there was of it that I could carry.*
- **On the road:** sits with the girls in the boat *the way a woman sits with her daughters*; puts them beside her at the fire; gives Kael the one-hand job and looks at the hand once and never mentions it; decides the trio's tent and nobody argues. Speaks the Koine with the coast under it. **Taught Kael the seller's walk** (*Sellers are not going anywhere. They are already there. Slower. No. That is a soldier being slow. Again.*) and **is the noise on a bridge** — met the officer at the third mule with a price in her mouth, three mules short of the princess. The princess does camp work having watched her once.

### SELIS — the younger one
- Eighteen perhaps; thin; hair cut off short at the jaw; says her name to the fire and not to anybody. Of **AMMARETH**.
- **Her story:** her father carried the old lord's cup for thirty years and nothing else in his life; when the empire *took the top off* and *the new top went on*, the men came to every house that had carried anything for the old one. *That is my story. It is a short one.*
- The princess sat beside her through it and did not move. **She and the princess have a tongue between them** that is neither Koine nor Ivrit, and Kael does not have it.

### ORAN and TALO — the twins
- *Plainly not of this country* (Ch. 49); twins, from a valley *across the water, a long way up it*. Oran talks; **Talo does not** — *He did once.* Oran's Koine is a learned thing with Avestan under it where the word will not come; Kael heard it and kept that he has the language. **Oran hears what drivers say from a cut above a road** (*he said her name twice and nobody laughed*), which Kael cannot.
- **Their story:** ahead of the empire's column *our own people came* — grain, animals, every man who could walk (the two of them at fourteen), and fire in the rest so the column would find nothing; their mother would not leave the house and was left in it, *because the sum did not have her in it*. They walked off from the column in the second week and have been walking since.

### AMBROS — the man of sixty in the good coat
- Of the capital, then of a house forty miles north of it, then of nowhere; the empire's tongue *as it is spoken in the empire*. Steward forty-one years — *everything that came into it or went out of it passed under my hand and I wrote it down*. The coat is buttoned to the throat and was the house's: *the only part of that house that is still standing up.*
- **His story:** the house had land; *a cousin of the Emperor* wanted it and asked *by being told no and waiting*; a charge was found, the head went to the wall with his sons, the land went where it was always going, the people with the furniture. *A steward of sixty is not a thing anybody buys.* He names neither the house nor the cousin. **The princess knows which house it was and has said so only in the dark.** **Taught Kael the voice** — the Koine *as it was spoken forty miles north of the capital*, nine words for an hour, *say where you are from as though you were bored of it* — and **sits the box with a made seal in his coat** and pays a toll short by a copper.

### THE OTHER NINE — unnamed
- **The warehouse man of Ishareth** taught Kael the lock: wet twine under the shackle, worked round the tongue, leaned on; not one word, and *a thing worth more than a warehouse* given without saying so. **The talker** drilled Kael's sentence at the bridge-house and made the circle laugh on purpose at the fire.

### FORWARD
- Thirteen months of road with all five of them; what each is for on a job, and what any of them makes of the third child, all open.
