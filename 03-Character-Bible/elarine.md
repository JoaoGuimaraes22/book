## ELARINE SEDRANE (the watcher — pride that learns)
**Glance:** the cohort's real mind; collegial ally · House Sedrane · ~13 · **at Qeshet, inside the princess's lie with four true words; the second fire under the still, and the better one; in Qeshet she gave Aurelian the floor of her room; on no page from Ch. 39 — not in the box, and Kael does not know whether she lives**

### STATIC
- **Role:** Considers herself the cohort's real MIND (Aurelian its capacity, its name, its noise) — and she is right. With Aurelian: the two responses to being out-thought — his world breaks, hers gets bigger. Proof the golden children are not a monolith.
- **Want:** Mastery on her own terms — to be taken as a future power in a Temple that reads her as a future match.
- **Wound:** Her excellence received as ornament; nobody is afraid of her mind, and she has noticed.
- **Flaw:** Pride in intellect as identity — the exact mirror of Aurelian's pride in blood.
- **Limit:** The respect began private — she would not be SEEN learning from a charity case; unlike Oss, she pays the public cost when it matters (the pairing asked for; the joke sat through, spots of color high on her cheeks).
- **Bound to fate (canon):** "I did not choose the Temple. My house chooses everything — the school, then the seat, then the man." She does not pretend the rope is a sash. The marriage-market reading of her excellence — never labeled romantic on the page.
- **Register constraint (sealed):** COLLEGIAL — a working alliance of minds Kael likely never files as friendship; its naming, if it ever comes, is books away. Protects *the only friendship either has ever had* (Valeria, beat 13).
- **The coat-wire (open valve, 00):** the person most likely to breach the coat. She notices HOW he thinks, never WHAT he already knew; whether she someday notices the gap and files it silently — door left cracked, open by design.
- **The crush:** she is the crush-object, unknowing — his side only, never labeled by either. **Which way it runs is UNRULED (s29):** the Ch. 24 teasing is an available reading, not a confirmation. On the page it is confirmed by neither.
- **SHE CARRIES A SWORD (author, s49).** Temple-trained and held in the doctrine grip — right hand full, left hand open for the Art (Ch. 26) — and it came off that mountain with her. She cut a burning mast off a boat with it.
- **Abilities:** FIRE, large capacity + total control — "a treasury with discipline"; her exactness is discipline over a deep purse, not smallness. **The fans are fire, not objects (s30):** a SHAPE she makes off an open hand, spent from the body like any other working (02 §Proximity law), available to any fire Resonant and nobody's instrument.
- **Appearance:** Ink-dark hair bound severely; pale, economical — nothing on her that doesn't work; small; dark eyes that price before they blink. Forgettable at a glance and unforgettable the third time you notice she hasn't moved. What others react to: they mostly don't — which she has noticed, and wears as neatness.
