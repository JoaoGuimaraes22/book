## ELARINE SEDRANE (the watcher — pride that learns)
**Glance:** the cohort's real mind; collegial ally · House Sedrane · ~13 · **at Qeshet, inside the princess's lie with four true words; the second fire under the still, and the better one**

### STATIC
- **Role:** Considers herself the cohort's real MIND (Aurelian its capacity, its name, its noise) — and she is right. With Aurelian: the two responses to being out-thought — his world breaks, hers gets bigger. Proof the golden children are not a monolith.
- **Want:** Mastery on her own terms — to be taken as a future power in a Temple that reads her as a future match.
- **Wound:** Her excellence received as ornament; nobody is afraid of her mind, and she has noticed.
- **Flaw:** Pride in intellect as identity — the exact mirror of Aurelian's pride in blood.
- **Limit:** The respect began private — she would not be SEEN learning from a charity case; unlike Oss, she pays the public cost when it matters (the pairing asked for; the joke sat through, spots of color high on her cheeks).
- **Bound to fate (canon):** "I did not choose the Temple. My house chooses everything — the school, then the seat, then the man." She does not pretend the rope is a sash. The marriage-market reading of her excellence — never labeled romantic on the page.
- **Register constraint (sealed):** COLLEGIAL — a working alliance of minds Kael likely never files as friendship; its naming, if it ever comes, is books away. Protects *the only friendship either has ever had* (Valeria, beat 13).
- **The coat-wire (open valve, 00):** the person most likely to breach the coat. She notices HOW he thinks, never WHAT he already knew; whether she someday notices the gap and files it silently — door left cracked, open by design.
- **The crush:** she is the crush-object, unknowing — his side only, never labeled by either. **Which way it runs is UNRULED (s29):** the Ch. 24 teasing is an available reading, not a confirmation. On the page it is confirmed by neither.
- **SHE CARRIES A SWORD (author, s49).** Temple-trained and held in the doctrine grip — right hand full, left hand open for the Art (Ch. 26) — and it came off that mountain with her. She cut a burning mast off a boat with it.
- **Abilities:** FIRE, large capacity + total control — "a treasury with discipline"; her exactness is discipline over a deep purse, not smallness. **The fans are fire, not objects (s30):** a SHAPE she makes off an open hand, spent from the body like any other working (02 §Proximity law), available to any fire Resonant and nobody's instrument.
- **Appearance:** Ink-dark hair bound severely; pale, economical — nothing on her that doesn't work; small; dark eyes that price before they blink. Forgettable at a glance and unforgettable the third time you notice she hasn't moved. What others react to: they mostly don't — which she has noticed, and wears as neatness.

### DYNAMIC
- **She is inside the lie** — *In my cohort also. Four years.* — true, given after Aurelian's, and nothing more.
- **She was the second fire under the still**, two hours an evening for a month, smaller, steadier and hotter; she tilted the lid and the yield rose; no mark on her. Her sword came up on the sand with the left hand open and nothing in it; after the healer she sat on the ground and looked at her hands.
- **Her house survives and her mother does not, and the machine that disposes of her is untouched:** *my house will go on with me.* She has worked out that the compliment was said because the woman knew she was dying, **and she does not know what to do with it, which has never once happened to her.**
- **Her mother's goodbye answered this sheet's wound:** instructions first, in the house's only dialect; then *"Not one of them has ever been good enough. I have known it since you were four years old, and I have never once said it to you… I was not going to have you softened."* / *"I am not encouraging you. Encouragement is for people who need it."* She has not repeated it or quoted it.
- **She said it out loud when Aurelian made her:** *"They are probably dead."*
- **She comforted Kael and it cost her nothing she noticed** — *I am sorry* — a hand flat on his back for a moment and taken away again.
- **She is empty and still working, and that is how to write her:** she priced herself out loud before the shelf and was exactly right, read three hundred strides of black hillside in ninety seconds and lit every separate thing in it that would burn in the same instant, got a princess up off scree on nothing at all — *"I am not going to be able to do it twice, so please do it now."* — and then got up off the boards with nothing left and cut a burning mast off a boat. **She is not spent-out on the page; she prices herself first and then delivers.**
- **She carries a sword** — Temple-trained, held in the doctrine grip, right hand full and left hand open for the Art — and it came off that mountain with her.
- **She pressed a wound she had not worked out first**, hard, with it coming up between her fingers, **crying and not appearing to know she was doing either.** The mask came off in front of Kael and neither of them has mentioned it.
- **She and Aurelian carried Kael's mother off that mountain.** The two children who could not act at the gate are the reason Neris walked, and Kael has passed no verdict on that.
- **THE COAT-WIRE IS AT ITS HIGHEST PRESSURE YET AND STILL UNBROKEN.** She has seen the house, the yard, the pens, what Kael is, what his mother is and what his father was, **and she has still not learned one thing he knows.** Every earlier link held — she saw the flash and declined to ask; supplied the principle his earth-step counter is built on and never asked what for; heard *"It isn't for me"* and said nothing; took Halden's entry apart herself and found nothing crooked in it, which is what frightened her.
- **She saw Kael from the wall and gave Valeria one fact and no expression** — *I saw him… He was on his own.* She never had a chance to act and does not know he built a verdict on it. **She has not accounted for that morning, and could not have done anything.**
- **She saw his face while he was cutting her** — somebody for whom the fighting was simply the good part — and met him anyway. *She would think about it for a long time and never once say it to him.* She has not raised it.
- **Her *because* was given once and is not repeated:** *"My house chooses the school and the seat and the man. But if I had won it. Perhaps I could have proved to myself that I was still able to write my own destiny."* She wanted it the way Kael did not, and he took it anyway, and she named that too.
- **She has called Kael her friend, out loud, once, and he did not say it back**; she will not raise it. She billed him four years of *Sedrane* and he has not shortened one syllable of her; her name does not shorten. Needling, jealousy and plain enjoyment of his clumsiness all stay live, and which way the crush runs is unruled.
- **She likes the girl she wants to beat.** She wanted Valeria beaten, by her, at the games, and did not get it; she wants Valeria to belong somewhere; she uses *Val* and refused to make Aurelian stop.
- She does not repeat her work and does not ask twice; she tracks the war as paperwork and refuses to draw the conclusion out loud.
