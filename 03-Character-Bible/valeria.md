## VALERIA VEL
**Glance:** rival → first friend; the Witness · Line of Vel (eleven generations; they serve the royal lineage wherever it goes, and **the strongest Vel alive stands over the KING or QUEEN** — the apex, and what being the best of the Line means; a different kind from the ordinary royal guard, not a higher grade; tiers in 02/factions) · 13 (Kael's age) · **the year is up** · **the semifinal was never fought — and she is the reason Kael is still alive.**

### STATIC
- **Role:** Daughter of the Line of Vel. Kael's rival, then first friend. The Witness — she alone will know the truth of him at the end (00).
- **Want:** To be worthy of the Line; later, things she was never permitted to want.
- **CORE (ratified s20 — outranks the flaw below):** DEEP, TOTAL, INDOCTRINATED LOYALTY TO THE ROYAL LINE. She exists to protect it; that is the entire content of her worth, and she has never been given room to hold it any other way. She does not experience being priced by the empire as an injury — being looked at is the only thing that makes any of it worth doing, and a state that reads its children early is a state doing the one thing a state is for. She will not follow others into cynicism about it and does not understand the complaint. **The seam (unspent):** she has never been asked whether she consented to be useful, and cannot construct an answer that is not an insult to her father (her father is the ENFORCER of the mentality and the teaching — the correction arrives from him, by post and in person). **This is the fault line the series opens.**
- **Wound:** Forged from birth; lethality as her family's love language; correspondence read by chaperones; cannot sleep; flinches microscopically at reverence for her name.
- **Flaw:** Coldness as armor; belief in the empire the way one believes in a parent who beats you; mistakes discipline for identity.
- **Abilities:** EARTH at super-advanced level in BOTH directions; internal reinforcement (nothing leaving the body) is her SPECIALTY — a known earth technique that the Line is immensely better at than any other earth user alive (degree, not possession — s24). **THE GROUND-SENSE (02):** reads what the stone she stands on is carrying — short range, intentional only, expensive, dependent on continuous medium. A fighting instrument, not surveillance. Twin blades, both hands full — the one-handed empire's heresy. Unbeaten; total control (a killing stroke stopped at exactly one finger); force and speed beyond Kael's category. The first person on the island to read his formlessness as a FORM — the other native speaker of his one social language.
- **Voice:** Precise, formal, economical; thaws by degrees across books. "Val" is reserved for intimacy — Kael's first use is a beat.
- **Appearance:** Lean, strong, athletic — a working build that reads as function even at rest. Long black hair worn loose well past the shoulders, no ornament (lengthened s33 — the page moved with it, Ch. 14). Emerald green eyes — color spent at reading distance only; worn plain and bored as display (the boredom died against Kael). Her stillness WARNS — nothing like his. Knuckles older than her face; guard-lineage calluses. Rooms rearrange around the name before the girl; reverence draws her one untrained tell, the microscopic flinch — **SPENT at Ch. 27** (see DYNAMIC; the s32 close DE-LISTED it, available not owed).

### DYNAMIC
- **THE FLINCH IS SPENT (Ch. 27).** The herald read the Line of Vel and the building came down on the name, and the one untrained tell she has went across her face and was gone inside half a breath — *what a man's face does when a door shuts behind him in a corridor he has walked down a thousand times.* Kael saw it. Nobody priced it and neither of them has ever mentioned it.
- **SHE ASKED HIM STRAIGHT, AND PAID WHAT SHE OWED (Ch. 27).** *"So. Figured me out?"* — and when he said no: *"You will find it in the first minute or you will not find it… It is the only thing on this island I have been unwilling to guess at."* **The last thing she ever says to him as a rival.**
- **SHE DID NOT GO TO HER POST. SHE CAME FOR HIM.** With the arena coming apart she put Kael into the timber of the judges' enclosure, **hit him open-handed — the grey coats' correction, called and delivered and finished** — and restarted him: *"There," she said. "I need you."* Then she made him be sure of his own number before she moved on it.
- **SHE IS RUNNING TO ORDERS AND ASKING HIM TO COME.** *"The nobles' district… It is the only wall on this island that was built to be a wall. My orders name it."* And, to *my mother's on the mountain*: *"Yes… And the mountain is behind them. We must regroup with the army at the nobles' district. Please, come with me. They'll help you to the mountain afterwards, I'm sure of it."* **She says please twice in one scene, which she has never done.**
- **Blooded.** Blood on her forearm that is not hers, and a burn on her neck she did not report taking.
- **Competes for the CAPITAL**, in its five, though resident on the island all year.
- **Now:** 13, on the island for the year; entered, and intending to win it. Trains alone on the old sea court under adults of the capital's grey who CALIBRATE her — weighted wasters, a number called after each blow, adjust, again; first on court by a quarter-hour, last off, nobody waiting at either end; instructions spoken *at* her; letters read aloud by a chaperone before she is handed them. Fights Kael in the hour after the yard, daily, dangerously, with no adult and no rules, and neither of them has ever discussed whether it is allowed to hurt. Comes to the cistern court now, which surprised her.
- **Wants:** the rematch with Kael in front of the empire — the one personal want she has ever leaked.
- **SHE HAS LEAKED A SECOND TIME, AND IT IS LARGER THAN THE FIRST (Ch. 24).** *"I am going to miss this."* After the games she is sent wherever the Line is needed and is not told in advance; it was always the year; her father wrote in the autumn that she should not become attached to the arrangement and she wrote back that she had not — *"That was true when I wrote it."* A year on the island, and four suppers with other people, all four of them in that court. **Then she smiled — the first smile in the book**, there and gone, doing nothing a smile is supposed to do to a face. Exit line: *"Third bell. Do not lose to Sedrane. I want you."*
- **THREE PEOPLE CALL HER VAL NOW.** Kael said it on the page unweighted, inside a question about a gate, and Aurelian and Elarine both picked it up; she told them to stop and Elarine refused to make him. **She still has not used it herself — her first use back is UNSPENT and is a beat.**
- **She did not laugh** when the other three did, and Kael saw her not laughing, and she caught him seeing it, and neither of them did one thing about it. Two children not laughing for entirely different reasons.
- **SHE STOOD UP FOR THE SECOND ONE (Ch. 26)** — which she had not done once, for anything, the day before. One hand on the rail, watching neither his hands nor his feet. **And she is next:** Elarine's exit line tells Kael and the reader together that tomorrow is the match he really wanted.
- **She was the only person in the north curve who did not stand up for his first bout.** Elbows on knees, chin on fists, **watching his feet and not his hands** from the second column on, and at the end of it she had stopped blinking. She said nothing to anybody about any of it.
- **State:** pushed hard for the first time in her life, and the one person in the yard untroubled by his danger. **Beaten once — by a floor, not a sword** — and cannot answer it; named AIR as the sense she would need and does not have. Has been to the bottom of her purse on his instruction, because nobody had ever asked her to go and look. Her family's care arrives as correction, by post.
- **Has told Kael she is cold all the time, and has never not been** — given as a fact about her body, inside an explicit refusal to have it made into the other thing. There is no word in her house for friendship or for love, and she does not experience the lack as an injury.
- **Refuses the misread to his face:** told that nobody stands beside her, she defends her father without one word against him and answers *"Then do not stand beside me. Stand in front of me. It is worth more."* The rivalry is the only closeness her house has built her to accept.
- Does not sleep. Was pleased with the answer she sat up half a night writing to her father about the arm Kael opened.
- **Kael has called her Val, once. She did not answer it, and said instead that nobody has ever called her anything.** Whether she ever uses it back is unspent.
- Has watched Kael reproduce a fraction of internal reinforcement by a route her line never needed, and said so plainly; she corrected his timing and told him he will never do it the way she does.
- **Knows:** the flash was not wet light. **Does not know:** what it was; anything of the mountain — she has never once asked, then or ever, and that is hers, not leverage.
- **Relationships:** Kael — rival, and the first person she has ever had to read seriously; the rest of the yard — read in passing.
- **Body:** old white lines on the forearms and four new ones; knuckles older than her face.

### FORWARD
- **Arc:** flawless mask → cracks → the choice in the fire (she runs with Kael and the princess — duty first; the crown-vs-Kael choice deferred to when it hurts more) → the Witness.
- **Reveal discipline:** the wound's cracks (the sleeplessness, the flinch, the forged childhood) stay unspent — ration her interiority, keep the mask. Ch. 15 spent only the OBSERVABLE from outside; the confession is beat 14 and is untouched.
- **The one lazy place (Ch. 15, banked):** half a count of lag on the first exchange of each morning, before she is warm. Kael found it; STILL UNSPENT through Ch. 17.
- **The father (Ch. 16/17):** his letter about the cut arm did not mention the boy who made it. It asked how a Vel came to be standing where a waster could reach her, and instructed her to write back with the answer (Ch. 16). His tournament letter said the same thing in fewer words: *they will be pricing you. Do not be cheap.* (Ch. 17). Her family's care arrives as correction, by post.
- **The unfinished duel:** her match with Kael is interrupted by the raid; no winner; unresolved for the entire series.
- **End of Book One:** ~13–14; in the boat.
