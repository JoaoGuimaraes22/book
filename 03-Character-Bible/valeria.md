## VALERIA VEL
**Glance:** rival → first friend · Line of Vel (eleven generations — tiers in `../02-World-Bible/factions.md`) · 13 (Kael's age) · **in a room over a mule yard in Ishareth with her hand on the boards, ordered off the princess's shoulder by the princess herself — and she went**

### STATIC
- **Role:** Daughter of the Line of Vel. Kael's rival, then first friend. The rest of her role is in 00.
- **Want:** To be worthy of the Line; later, things she was never permitted to want.
- **CORE (ratified s20 — outranks the flaw below):** DEEP, TOTAL, INDOCTRINATED LOYALTY TO THE ROYAL LINE. She exists to protect it; that is the entire content of her worth, and she has never been given room to hold it any other way. She does not experience being priced by the empire as an injury — being looked at is the only thing that makes any of it worth doing, and a state that reads its children early is a state doing the one thing a state is for. She will not follow others into cynicism about it and does not understand the complaint. **The seam (unspent):** she has never been asked whether she consented to be useful, and cannot construct an answer that is not an insult to her father (her father is the ENFORCER of the mentality and the teaching — the correction arrives from him, by post and in person). **This is the fault line the series opens.**
- **Wound:** Forged from birth; lethality as her family's love language; correspondence read by chaperones; cannot sleep; flinches microscopically at reverence for her name.
- **THE DEATH-EDUCATION (author, s37).** The Line raises killers by removing the first time: **they take you to WATCH before you are allowed to do**, so that when you can do it there is nothing left about it to find out. **Four years of watching before she was given anything sharp — executions, mostly.** Her father stood behind her at the first with both hands on her shoulders so she could not turn her head, and told her afterward she had done well. **She has no first one and no number.** She holds that this was correct and would change none of it. **On the page:** observational only, in her flat priced register, never from inside; the mask did not come off to deliver it. She can also state the doctrine out loud — *those men were not people* — which is the indoctrination speaking, and it stands uncorrected on the page.
- **Flaw:** Coldness as armor; belief in the empire the way one believes in a parent who beats you; mistakes discipline for identity.
- **Abilities:** EARTH at super-advanced level in BOTH directions; internal reinforcement (nothing leaving the body) is her SPECIALTY — a known earth technique that the Line is immensely better at than any other earth user alive (degree, not possession — s24). **THE GROUND-SENSE (02):** reads what the stone she stands on is carrying — short range, intentional only, expensive, dependent on continuous medium. A fighting instrument, not surveillance. Twin blades, both hands full — the one-handed empire's heresy. Total control (a killing stroke stopped at exactly one finger); force and speed beyond Kael's category. The first person on the island to read his formlessness as a FORM — the other native speaker of his one social language.
- **Voice:** Precise, formal, economical; thaws by degrees across books. "Val" is reserved for intimacy.
- **Appearance:** Lean, strong, athletic — a working build that reads as function even at rest. Long black hair worn loose well past the shoulders, no ornament (lengthened s33 — the page moved with it, Ch. 14). Emerald green eyes — color spent at reading distance only; worn plain and bored as display (the boredom died against Kael). Her stillness WARNS — nothing like his. Knuckles older than her face; guard-lineage calluses. Rooms rearrange around the name before the girl; reverence draws her one untrained tell, the microscopic flinch — **SPENT at Ch. 27** (the s32 close DE-LISTED it: available, not owed).

### DYNAMIC
- **SHE REFUSED HIM, AND THE CROWN OVERRULED HER.** Told she could go out and the princess could not, she would not leave her — *There is one thing I am for. I have stood on her left since Qeshet, and I stood there before I ever met you, and I am not going to be in a street when somebody comes up that stair* — with no edge on it, which is how Kael knew it was not an argument. **Aeliana ordered her**: *I have had twelve royal guard stand beside me and they all died for me, and I won't have it again. Go when he asks you.* / *"Yes, Highness."* **This is not the crown-vs-Kael choice** — the crown pointed her at Kael's work — and it pays inside two days: she walks to the crew's door with him.
- **HER ART GOES INTO THE FLOOR (author, s69).** A hand flat on the boards of a room over a mule yard and *her Art flowed through the floor and walls*: two above them, one heavy and not moving much, the other standing at the top of the stair since they came up it. **Not long, not while asleep, and she would rather nobody in that city knew she could do it at all.** The ground-sense is a projection, not a passive reading (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`).
- **SHE IS THE WORST STUDENT HE HAS EVER HAD.** Every sound in the sentence correct and nothing else about it right — it comes out of her like a report — and the reason is hers and not the language's: *I have never in my life had to want anything out loud in front of a stranger.* The princess laughed at her about it and she took it: *both of them are work.*
- **SHE OUT-THREW HIM ELEVEN TO FOUR** on the road, flat from the shoulder without appearing to have decided to, **and he stopped competing and started giving her the side the wind was on.** She did the cleaning, because he cannot with one hand.
- **SHE ASKED ONE QUESTION ABOUT THE WHOLE PLAN AND IT WAS THE ONLY ONE THAT MATTERED.** *"If they say no, do they let us walk out." / "I do not know." / "That is the answer I expected. I wanted to hear you say it out loud."*
- **HER ART IS BACK AND SHE HAS SAID WHAT IT IS SHORT OF.** *My Art is still returning to me Kael* — three pits properly, or four badly and no use the next day — and *I'm almost embarrassed at my lack of power.* She opens ground by subtraction rather than digging, and it takes a long time and costs her. **What seventy days without the ground-sense was is on the page in her own mouth:** the whole of a wall under her hand at once, every stone and where the weight goes and which one it all stands on, and *As if I had lost a part of the world. And perhaps a part of myself too.*
- **SHE KILLED MOST OF A COMPANY, AND RAN OUT DOING IT.** Off a rise from twelve feet with everything spent inward; four on a strip of grass in a way that did not sound like swordwork; two spears let through the meat of her own left arm to get a hand on the shafts, both men into the rock; one man put head first into a hole to the shoulders. **She visibly slowed** — Kael saw it at sixty strides — and at the end she took the officer's knee with her heel **unasked**, because he was the one to keep, and then sat down and did not get up.
- **KAEL CALLS HER *VAL*, AND SHE HAS ANSWERED TO IT.** *I wish I could see the world as you do Val* / *I'm sure your world is much more interesting.* She has still never used it of herself.
- **SHE WAS LET.** The princess bound her arm with a strip off her own hem, wrong twice and then right, and she sat and let her and said thank you afterward.
- **SHE SAID ONCE THAT SHE DOES NOT LIKE IT.** On Ishareth: *I would go west if there were a way to go west. There is not, and I would rather be in a city than on eight days of open ground with you* … *I do not like the rest of it. I am saying so once and then I will not say it again.* **The map spared her the choice, so the crown-vs-Kael choice is still not made.**
- **THE IRON IS OFF HER AND THE GROUND IS LOUD.** She put her arm on the horn and did not move it by a hair for an hour, and the smith said afterwards that he had never had anything so easy to work on and had never wanted so much to stop. When her second band came away she walked out of the forge into the yard and **did nothing at all for a while**, and Kael went out after her, and **the page gives none of it.** What seventy days without the ground-sense was, and what its return is, are on no page.
- **SHE ASKED HIM A QUESTION.** *Why are they doing this* — the first she has ever asked him about anything, and he had no answer. Her own frame is the one she was raised in: *a house stands for its own* … *These people have nothing, and we are not theirs.* **Neither of them got any further with it, and she did not raise it again.**
- **SHE WAS DEAF FOR SIX DAYS AND SAID SO.** No Ivrit at all: two days of a household talking over the top of her in her own room, an argument she could not follow and a father and son having it out in the yard — *I can tell you how they move. I cannot tell you what they are.* She had the shape of that house and none of its meaning.
- **SHE DID NOT STOP THE PRINCESS.** With Kael down at the door and no plan left, she let a princess of the empire make a deliberate noise in a stranger's yard at night, and **Kael saw her not stop it and could do nothing with it.** Her later report of that night is three flat sentences with the knock and the hands left out of them, as her Ch. 31 report left him out.
- **SHE LET A STRANGER PUT HANDS ON HER.** The old woman strapped her ribs on the first evening; she said thank you afterwards in a language the woman did not have.
- **SHE KILLED MOST OF A SEARCH PARTY IN IRONS.** Into the needle-dark past a gap Kael set, where once she stopped moving he could not find her; she took the first two from cover, both fists together (the second's one sound cut in half), then two loose ones at the gap (*no spear any use in it*), then the runner with a thrown spear at a dozen strides, two-handed, from the shoulder, in irons. Told *"No. That one. Alive"* she took the man's good knee with her heel instead of his throat.
- **SHE KILLED THE PRISONER UNASKED, AND KAEL LET HER.** When Kael had finished with the bound man — *I believe you*, two words too late — she killed him in one movement, the good knife under the jaw, without being asked and without asking. **Kael heard it, had the half-second, and did not use it.** She said nothing about it.
- **SHE CARRIES A SOLDIER'S SWORD NOW** (author, s64) — one of two off the dead; she is better with an edge than any man it came off, and it is worth more to her than the spear she threw. She still fights around a hand's width of chain.
- **SHE HELD THE WRIST UNDER THE WATER AND COUNTED HIM.** When Kael put his ruined hand into the cold stream and the relief turned to white pain, she held the wrist down — *leave it in; the cold is good for it; count* — the same picking-him-up she did through the broken hand (Ch. 40).
- **SHE KILLED THREE MEN IN IRONS AND IT TOOK ABOUT NINE SECONDS.** The rope cut in one draw against the ring's own bracket, the ankles on the way up, **both hands together because they cannot go further apart than the width of a hand**, the knife point-down out of the bottom of her fists. **The kill is withheld on the page** — Kael was holding a mouth shut and got it as sound through a doorway. Her one line after it, about his hand: *"Do not use it again."*
- **SHE TOOK THE BAR ACROSS THE RIBS ON HER WAY PAST AND MADE NO SOUND ABOUT IT AT ALL**, and carried the princess up six shelves of stone afterwards, in irons, with the ribs doing something on the lift that showed in her face and nowhere else.
- **SHE DID NOT ASK.** Told *right* — uphill, backwards, away from the water — she got down and put the girl over her shoulder. She barred the door from outside on his word, in two tries. **She has still never asked him one question about any of it.**
- **THE IRON IS STILL ON HER AND THERE IS NO ART.** At the stop she held her wrists up in front of him without any particular emphasis, let him look, and put them down. **Neither of them said anything about it.**
- **SHE SLEPT ON HIS WORD FOR TWO MONTHS.** Told *You should sleep* in the second week, she did not argue, did not ask about the nights, said *"Very well,"* and lay down — then slept, ate everything she was given, spent nothing on anything, and went thin the slow way instead of the fast one, so that she would be able to fight on the day the door opened. **The page never says what that is.**
- **SHE MADE THE PRINCESS DRINK, TWICE A DAY, FOR THE REST OF THE ROAD** — *"Highness. Drink it."*, and once, when that failed, *"Highness, please. Aeliana. Drink it,"* which she had never done and did not do again. *It did not get easier.* She saw what was happening to the girl and could do nothing about it, and did not once look at her while it was happening.
- **SHE TRIED TO STOP HIM ONCE AND STOPPED.** *"There will be another night." … "You may not be able to use it again."* — then she priced the whole thing in about four seconds, found no fault in his arithmetic, and said nothing else. **Then she counted him back**: *"One."*, to about sixty, three times, picking him up from the last number he had said, without comment.
- **SHE WOKE IN THE BOX AND LOOKED AT THE PRINCESS FIRST**, hair to iron; then her own arms, for a long time. Her report, to the princess and not to him: four in the room, her body weaker than after thirty days on the ship, one in the throat, hit back, something over her face, *from there, my memory goes dark*; *I do not know the rest*; *I saw nothing of your mother … Nor the old man, nor the child, nor Corvane and Sedrane.* **She named no colours and no house.**
- ***I SLEPT.*** Said the way she once said at the water that she did not (Ch. 20) — a fact about her body, not to be made into anything; then held as work. The Art does not answer in the iron — *I thought it was the blow.* She asked him *Can you get loose?* and took *When they take us out* as if he had told her the time.
- **She chose the room** — looked into two and said *This one* — and stood at the princess's shoulder in its door, and said nothing when the princess said *Goodnight, Lucan* to the boy in the passage.
- **At Qeshet, on the princess's left, the only protection left,** and it is her purpose. She gave the governor's clerk two words — *Valeria Vel* — and the pen stopped over them; she stood behind the princess's cushion until the governor asked her to sit; she did not join the lie and did not contradict it.
- **She was in front of the princess before the count finished**, out of the sea with both blades drawn and no hurry left in her to use; when the watch knelt she let the points come down last of anybody and did not sheathe them. She drank first from the watch's skin before it reached the princess, and the captain saw it and said nothing. She carried the princess out of the sea.
- **Thirty days on the hull:** thin the way a blade grows thin; never once sat where she could not reach the girl in one movement; the burn healed to a shine by nothing but time and is now oiled and dressed; the left arm out of the coat at last and bound properly by the south's healer.
- **SHE STOPPED THE HAND AND NOT THE MOUTH.** She did not move while Kael blamed the princess to her face; she was in front of him before the arm was anywhere, said *"Kael, stop it. Please,"* and held while he pushed, and it was his mother's voice that ended it. **This is not the crown-vs-Kael choice** — she stopped the body so as never to have to make it, and nobody can call her on it. **The choice is still not made.** Afterwards she lied for him, mildly and correctly — *"He is tired, Highness."* — and put her feet back where she keeps them.
- *Please* is on the page on two occasions in Book One — Ch. 27 (twice on that page) and Ch. 36.
- **SHE HAS CROSSED THE WHOLE OF THAT MOUNTAIN, STOOD IN ITS YARD, WATCHED THE WHOLE OF IT FALL, AND HAS STILL NOT ASKED ONE QUESTION ABOUT IT.** A year, a treeline, a sea court, a road, the yard, the boat. **That is hers, not leverage.**
- **She apologised** — *I'm sorry, Kael. For everything.* — unprompted, in front of his house: the first time she has apologised to anybody in the manuscript.
- **She held that doorway and did not fight the fight** — the best fighter on the mountain nailed to a door by her own orders for the whole assault. **Her CORE and her seam in one posting**, and nobody points at it.
- **She found the only counter there was to Thiro and it nearly killed her:** the whole way in until she was standing on his feet, one blade four inches under his ribs and a second into his side — and he took her off the ground by the hair and put her into the frame. She did not beat him and knows it, and did not take the step off the threshold after him.
- **Inert twice on the water and she did the work with her hands:** a sweep one-armed, a burning sail over the side — earth on water, with nothing under her to read or take. She priced herself out loud on the mountain: two more terrace-fronts and then she is just blades.
- **SHE HAS BEEN IN A ROOM WITH THE BLOOD AND THE CORE LANDED:** she priced the princess as a body that would have to be carried, and underneath it *she wanted to stand in front of her; she had wanted it her whole life, and nobody had ever had to ask.* The seam is open one millimetre and only the reader can see it.
- **She gave a complete report and left the boy out of it.** Not a decision; she went past the place where he was and kept going. **The first thing she has ever kept from the Line, and the page never says so.** She told Elarine and Aurelian nothing about the gate — only *his mother is having a child, and he has gone east.*
- **She carried rage out of that gate and contained it, and it was about the boy and never about the thirty thousand.** She refused to put both blades through the bar on Halden's own arithmetic, and stopped looking at him for two hours. **The mask never comes off from inside; the containment shows as work.**
- **She has the north side of the district** off Kael's own reconnaissance, asked one flat question about it and filed the answer. Nobody knows what she asked; she has not decided anything.
- **She did not name Kael's one qualification at the wall** — the enemy's tongue in his mouth, which she had ordered him to report — and he watched her not say it. The page joins nothing up, and it has not become leverage.
- **AT THE GATE SHE NEVER GOT TO CHOOSE.** She refused, spent everything she had on it — her name, her orders, herself as the price — was answered correctly at every rung, and was taken through by the arm: **her body could refuse and her Line could not.** The mask came off once, from outside — she was angry, and Kael saw it, the first time he ever had.
- **She is the reason Kael is alive twice over.** She got him out of the arena and restarted him open-handed when he froze (neither has mentioned it); she led across the island, killed first, killed without asking anybody anything, and fifteen grown men could not lay hands on her. She told him the truth about her education when he asked and then handed him the doctrine as comfort — *those men were not people* — and it stands uncorrected. She withdrew the promise about the mountain herself, unasked, because she had made it to a boy who was not standing in his own head.
- **The flinch is spent**; nobody priced it and neither of them has mentioned it. **The rematch in front of the empire — the one personal want she has ever stated — can never be had as designed**; the semifinal was called and never fought.
- **Three people call her Val.** She has never used it herself; her first use back is available, not owed. She leaked once that she would miss this: after the games she is sent wherever the Line is needed and not told in advance; her father wrote that she should not become attached and she wrote back that she had not, which was true when she wrote it.
- **She is cold all the time and has never not been** — a fact about her body, inside an explicit refusal to have it made into the other thing. There is no word in her house for friendship or for love and she does not experience the lack as an injury: *"Then do not stand beside me. Stand in front of me. It is worth more."* Her family's care arrives as correction, by post.
- Does not sleep — and slept at Qeshet, and said so: *I slept.*
- **Knows:** the flash was not wet light; that Kael has the enemy's tongue; what she saw in that yard and on that shelf. **Does not know:** what the flash was; anything of the mountain — she has never once asked, then or ever, and that is hers.
- **Body:** **the ribs, off the bar in the doorway (Ch. 41)** — nothing said about them, and they show only on a lift · **the burn is on the LEFT side of her face (author, s60)** and is mostly healed — healed to a shine, oiled and dressed, a smooth shine on the skin rather than a wound; the left arm bound properly at last; old white lines on the forearms and four newer ones; knuckles older than her face; a bruise black along the bone of the jaw, on the good side (Ch. 39); the left arm opened twice by spears taken deliberately, bound by the princess; no iron on the wrists since Doresh; thin without changing shape.
