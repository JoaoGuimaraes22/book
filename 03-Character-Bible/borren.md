## BORREN (the crew's leader)
**Glance:** leader of the honest men (`../02-World-Bible/factions.md`) · no house — a soldier-worn given name, *given to me; I did not choose it* · past fifty · **DEAD — killed by no one, spent to the last breath in the fold: he was an AIR RESONANT and a master, hidden the whole time, and he held the air, killed the betrayer with understanding, and threw the boy who was a son to him onto the wagon with the last of himself. He loved Tirzah and never said it.**

### STATIC
- **Role:** leader of the fifteen; the trio's passage to Solvenna for thirteen months. Character Law applies; limit, wound and awkwardness are being built from the page and nothing here outruns it.
- **Name:** BORREN — spent at Ch. 52, his own mouth (`../02-World-Bible/naming.md`); unnamed on every page before it ("the man from the rope-walk", "the man at the table"). *Nobody outside this crew has ever had it.* The Ch. 52 narration never uses it after the gift; from Ch. 54 the narration uses it freely.
- **Manner:** the ledger register — everything priced before it is done, everything said *as though it were a thing already written down somewhere and he was reading it out*. Asks *what do you want* and *what have you got* and never *who are you*. Grants correct arguments and refuses or reprices them on his own arithmetic (a fifth offered against an asked tenth; the whole-share forfeit refused and rerouted). Concedes to a child without explaining (the cleared room, Ch. 49; the awake governor, Ch. 50) and has laughed twice, both times genuinely, both times at Kael.
- **Policy lines on the page:** *we do not leave bodies — a body is a hunt, and a hunt has a name in it by the third week* · *nobody who knows how a thing is going to be done leaves until it is done* (extended at Ch. 52: *nobody leaves, not at the first town, not at the worst one*) · a description is worth nothing *until somebody wants it enough to pay for it* · his people come first (*fourteen people of mine in this city, and not one of them came into it for you*).
- **Appearance:** past fifty, thin, grey, going bald from the front, close grey beard; **a plain oval of dark leather over the right eye on a cord that goes up under the hair** (author, s72) — the one member of the crew who can be described; the rest of the face *the sort you are given directions by*. Reference face: `../../08-Plates/prompts/portraits/borren.md`.

### DYNAMIC
- **DEAD (Ch. 59), and it is the largest sacrifice on any page since Vask.** He was an **AIR RESONANT** — the same element as Neris and nothing like her use of it (`../02-World-Bible/magic.md`): he holds his own ten feet of air close against himself, which is why Thiro's suffocating volume did nothing to him, and bends air to a blade to cut and condenses it to block. **He hid it the whole time** — Ishareth, the winter, the job — and showed it only in the fold, to keep a young man who could not be reached from reaching six people on two wagons. **What he was before is sealed (00): a great imperial general; Tirzah knows and will tell Kael at Solvenna.**
- **HE HELD THE AIR SO THE THREE COULD KILL THIRO** (`coalition-generals.md`): he closed to four feet so there was not ten feet to weaponise, an old man doing a young man's work, grey and wet with it, and it cost him everything he had.
- **HE KILLED AMBROS WITH HIS HANDS, WITH UNDERSTANDING.** Ambros betrayed the crew under coercion (his daughters taken; `crew.md`); Borren: *I know what they do. I know it better than you will ever know it. I have done it. But you must understand me too … I am not going to tell you it is all right. It is not. But I know.* Then the crew's four seconds, close, a mercy and a sentence, and set him down gently.
- **HE MADE KAEL DO THE COUNT rather than let the boy die trying to carry him** — *There is no version of this where I am on that wagon. Say it* — and **threw Kael onto the moving wagon with the last of the air.** Kael was *something of a son to him, in the end* (00), and it is the second time Kael has lived on a father spending himself.
- **HE LOVED TIRZAH AND NEVER SAID IT.** He watched her across the last fire the way a man watches a thing he priced long ago and could not afford; at the end he looked up the track at her once, and the thing he never said passed between them, and she was taken over the rise. **Tirzah knows what he was and carries it to Solvenna** (00).
- **HE KNEW THE REAL NAMES.** In the fold he named Valeria to her face (*That's your real name, is it not*); he had worked out the princess at Ishareth (*get her off this river*). He asked the crew *what do you want* and never *who are you*, and knew anyway.

- **HE TOLD THE CREW A JOB, AND THE RULE FIRST.** No cup; the circle looked round one face at a time; an exit offered before he opened his mouth, with the share to date kept, and taken by nobody. Six years of a province's coin under a tax-house, to be moved west before the autumn; *this time the empire is going to do the walking*; men *more than we have ever stood near*, the number not yet his; *Enough that nobody at this fire works again … Enough that there is no honest men, after* — *I have never once said that.* ***"Who is paying us." / "Nobody."*** The rest in pieces. *Go and sleep.*
- **HE TOLD KAEL WHERE THE KNOWLEDGE CAME FROM AND FORBADE THE QUESTIONS.** Crouched to the boy's level, which he had never done: *a thing was written down in the winter by a man who knew, and I had it in my hand for an hour before the man it was written to did. Do not ask me who … Work out for yourself what it means that I was.* Kael is *the one who counts*, and is to bring the job's shape *in order, short* and be told which parts are wrong.
- **HE USED THE FORD.** *Where is your ford*, at a river in the fourth month, without looking round; neither said a word then or afterward.
- **HE NAMED A TOWN IN ADVANCE, ONCE** — *Tomorrow we go in somewhere and we stay … until the roads are roads again* — and kept the winter as a carrier's yard with Ambros over the house.
- **HE TEACHES BY THE PIECE AND PRICES THE LESSON AFTERWARD.** *Between Ishareth and Solvenna* is the whole of where; the next town's name on the day and not before. *Not the drunkest. You want the one who is drunk on purpose … Count how many times he looks at the door.* One *Good*, the first. **At the bridge he overruled the boy's ford** — *we go over the bridge, tomorrow, in the middle of the day; we pay the toll and we argue about it … I told you there would be evenings* — and afterward priced it honestly: *I was right that we are on this side. Whether I was right is a different sum … I would not have found the ford. Next time you find me one, I might use it … do keep your count up, Kael, I'll make sure to make good use of it.* **He used the boy's name, once.** **He is always awake when somebody comes in**; he looked at the hands and the boot and asked about neither, and held out his hand for the letter.
- **HE OPENED THE ROUND AND TOLD NOTHING.** *"You know what they are paid … You should know what they are called"* — to the crew, and then he waited and let the boy do it himself. He sat with his cup through fifteen people's stories and gave none; Kael noticed.
- **HIS CREW MOVES THE WAY HIS ROPE-WALK STOPPED.** Boats before light, a landing with mules nobody claims, tracks instead of the road, a camp that makes itself; fires dug down with stones on the back side, and everyone eating at once out of one pot. Tirzah decides where the trio sleeps and nobody argues.
- **HIS NEVER-AGAIN STOOD ONE NIGHT AND HE PRICED THE FAILURE INSTEAD OF RESENTING IT.** *"It was true when I said it."* Three children walked his hidden place up to his door off a hooded count, and what he did was ask *how*, put six wasted turns on the table, be corrected to eight, and laugh.
- **HE TOOK THEM, AND THE TERMS ARE HIS SHAPE.** Kael's hands for any work; the two girls with the camp; **the whole-share forfeit refused** — *a man on a job who is owed nothing is a man with nothing under his feet* — their bread out of the boy's share and the remainder the boy's, taken. He asked consent of all three, the princess last and straight at her, and **he looked at Valeria while she answered this time.** The standing up was the signature.
- **HE NAMED THE PRICE THAT HURTS, AND OWNED HIS OWN HALF OF IT.** Thirteen months of obedience for a boy who has been the one deciding — *on my road, being right is not a rank* — with the admission made in the room: *some of those evenings I will be wrong. You will do it anyway.*
- **HE KNOWS WHAT THE THIRD CHILD IS AND ENFORCES THE NOT-SAYING HIMSELF.** Worked out at Ch. 51 (*get her off this river*), never said in front of his own people, and Ch. 52 takes her under it unsaid. *"Is it your own choice, to be standing on my bank."*
- **HE KNOWS WHAT KAEL IS FOR.** *You are for one thing and it is not money … It is the most expensive way there is to live* — and he heard the hour of questions about the family that the one thing points at.

### FORWARD
- **DEAD.** MEMORY from here. What he was — a great imperial general — and the whole of his past are sealed (00) and reach the page only through **Tirzah, who will tell Kael at Solvenna**; his air Art, his fatherhood toward Kael and his unspoken love for Tirzah are spent. The job he opened (six years of a province's coin) was taken and cost the crew everything: fifteen of the honest men are dead, three survive.
