## OSTRIAN "OSS" VESSALINE (the almost-friend — warmth that fails)
**Glance:** the almost-friend; the reader's guide to the Temple · House Vessaline (merchant; a bought name) · ~13 · **DEAD, on the lip of the breach, with the estrangement never repaired and not one word ever said.**

### STATIC
- **Role:** Youngest son of an archipelago merchant house that bought its way into the register — new money wearing old ornament; every old-blood child hears the reach in "Vessaline." The first to reach Kael; Temple names, houses, and ranks delivered as characterization.
- **Design (sealed):** the ASYMMETRIC bond — he needs an audience; Kael needs a translator; each gets something, neither is met. Control sample for Valeria's symmetric bond. His instant diminutive ("Everyone says Oss") is intimacy given away free, therefore holding nothing — the inverse of "Val," predicted in his first line.
- **Want:** To belong.
- **Wound:** The bought name — the old houses' contempt predates Kael by years; long practice being laughed at is exactly why he recognizes where Kael stands. Kindness and self-interest braided; both real.
- **Limit:** Courage only in private — true things said to Kael alone, never in a court; when proximity to Kael costs standing he cannot spare, the herd wins.
- **Awkwardness:** Talks too much when nervous, which is constantly.
- **Abilities:** EARTH proficiency; fights "like a sack of flour" by his own pricing; his road is the counting-house.
- **Appearance:** Soft-built, round-faced, quick-eyed — **mid-brown hair, soft, kept short and combed, and warm mid-brown eyes** (author, s64), and his cloth is dyed where the island's is undyed; expressive hands that talk when he does, which is always. Everything he wears slightly too new — cloth that hasn't learned him yet (the bought name as wardrobe). A face built for laughing that watches carefully between laughs.

### DYNAMIC
- **DEAD, on the lip of the breach, doing the one thing this sheet said he could not repeat.** He stopped at the top of the slot while everybody else ran and pulled up a wall of earth four feet high and eleven strides long — *the only working Kael had ever seen him do that was worth anything at all* — and it held four seconds, which was exactly enough, and then they went round it and killed him. **The courage arrived under load, once, and it cost him everything.**
- **Not one word ever passed between them in the last year of his life** — not across three tables in the hall, not in the crowd where Kael found him in a second and a half on both days of the games, not in the yard when he came up the path three behind Aurelian and got half a step forward before the line moved him on, not on the mountain. *There had been no word of any kind between them, not on any day of the last year, and now there was not going to be one.* **The wound he owed Kael was never delivered and the repair is never available.** Kael was forty feet below him, moving toward him, and was picked up off his feet by the front of the coat.
- **His father's last words to him were about the coat and about eating, and never mentioned the bought name** — the name that had cost him most of what he owned and had been the whole subject of his life: *"You were always enough. You were always plenty."* The old houses along that wall all looked at something else at the same moment. Nobody has quoted it back.
- **The truth of the estrangement stays off the page:** Corvane and Drusane pressured Vessaline, Vessaline pressured him, and he obliged — reluctant, hurting his only friend and himself. Kael never learned it and now cannot learn it from him.
- **His forge warning was proved right** and is quoted inside Kael's head by a boy who could never tell him so.
- **Handling:** his hurt was real; the reader's protective-joke theory is productive ambiguity and is not corrected, then or retroactively.

### FORWARD
- **Nothing is owed on him.** The arc this sheet held — *he will eventually wound Kael in earnest; the story will ask him to repeat his one act of courage under load, and he won't manage it* — was inverted at his death: he managed it, once, and died of it, and the wound was never delivered. What Kael does with the year of silence and with having watched is Kael's (`kael.md`).
