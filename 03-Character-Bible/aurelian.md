## AURELIAN CORVANE (the ringleader — pride that breaks)
**Glance:** the cohort's presumptive best; ringleader → training ally · House Corvane · ~13 · **at Qeshet, inside the princess's lie — he backed a dead friend's name on Kael with nothing but true sentences — and the first fire under the still for a month; in Qeshet he shared a room with Elarine Sedrane, and took the floor; on no page from Ch. 39 — not in the box, and Kael does not know whether he lives**

### STATIC
- **Role:** The cohort's best before Kael arrives; capacity-worship made flesh; the face of the science-vs-pride engine. Sealed irony (00): his mockery of Kael's method is the empire failing to recognize its most wanted asset.
- **Want:** To be what his name promises.
- **Wound:** THE SPARE — an elder brother already legendary at court; the Temple is the one place Aurelian gets to be first, which is precisely what Kael takes from him.
- **Limit:** Cannot conceive of merit outside birthright — out-fought he could forgive; out-thought by a charity case reads as the order of the world breaking.
- **Character Law:** Not a strawman. Genuinely gifted — high capacity, real technique, real charm, casual generosity to his own tier; children follow him for reasons. His cruelty is doctrine, not sadism — which is worse. His mass, leadership, and social gravity are real powers the story honors (rule in 06): command that moves six backs as one is a purse of its own kind.
- **Abilities:** FIRE proficiency, high capacity (declared at the dish — expected, cheered).
- **Appearance:** Tall for his cohort, built a year ahead of his age; dark-gold hair; **amber, gold-brown eyes — author ruling s34, unspent on any page**; high carriage; unspent strength Kael prices on sight. Beautiful and knows the exact weight of it — the bench behaves differently in his radius (the page renders the radius, not the adjective). Fire's warmth in the coloring without stating it — it sits in the hair and the eyes together, and is never named as fire.

### DYNAMIC
- **ON NO PAGE FROM CH. 39; ALIVE OR NOT IS UNKNOWN** to Kael and to the reader. What the page has of that house is `../07-Story-Ledger/chapter-records.md` (Ch. 51) and `../07-Story-Ledger/open-threads.md`; what became of him is 00's.
- **KAEL COUNTS HIM AMONG THE PEOPLE HE IS WALKING TOWARD A MAN FOR** — and **his verdict on the gate is untouched and uncorrected.** The page joins the two together nowhere.
- **He is inside the Drusane lie**, in true sentences, said to the governor and not to Kael, gone the colour of the bread with the cup lifted and not drunk from.
- **His house is safe and it is not the smallest comfort to him.** Twenty-nine carry the name; only his father stayed on the wall. **His father's goodbye gave him the thing he has wanted his whole life, too late** — a message for the brother at court, and *"I have two sons. I have never once had a favourite, and I have let a great many people believe otherwise, because it was useful… That was badly done."* **Aurelian, who answers everything, did not answer that, does not raise it, and carries the message without saying so.**
- **He is the one who keeps putting his body between other people and the thing, and nobody has remarked on it.**
- **He spent every grain he had in one wall, off one good hand, and it was genuinely enormous** — and a general drew a line across it and stepped through without breaking stride. **The capacity he was raised to worship, on the page, against real war.** He said out loud what the yard had watched — *He has been telling us for four years that everything is measurable and we have all been extremely funny about it* — and got no laugh and did not want one.
- **He talks the entire way down a mountain**, steadily, in order, correct in every particular, for nobody; told to be quieter he is quieter and does not stop. **He could not find the words for Kael, said so, and then put his shoulder against Kael's shoulder and shut up**, which for him is an enormous undertaking.
- **The arm is not all right and he pretends about it.**
- **Deference as doctrine, the same machine as the old cruelty:** he stands up when Valeria enters a room and cannot sit back down, paid *"She is better than my brother"*, and recruited the loss into the work; his tier would not recognise him. Calls her *Val* because Kael did.
- **The shrug stands:** you take the top off a conquered city and put a new top on. **Not cruelty; the world as it was described to him.**
- **Knows:** something of his house's campaign; that his brother wrote to him about *the eastern roads* and had never once written about a road — **and he does not notice that he has noticed.** **Does not know:** the mountain; Halden's identity, or that Halden is his house's man.
