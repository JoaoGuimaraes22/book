## AURELIAN CORVANE (the ringleader — pride that breaks)
**Glance:** the cohort's presumptive best; ringleader → training ally · House Corvane · ~13, as of Ch. 22 · cistern-court ally; "longer hours."

### STATIC
- **Role:** The cohort's best before Kael arrives; capacity-worship made flesh; the face of the science-vs-pride engine. Sealed irony (00): his mockery of Kael's method is the empire failing to recognize its most wanted asset.
- **Want:** To be what his name promises.
- **Wound:** THE SPARE — an elder brother already legendary at court; the Temple is the one place Aurelian gets to be first, which is precisely what Kael takes from him.
- **Limit:** Cannot conceive of merit outside birthright — out-fought he could forgive; out-thought by a charity case reads as the order of the world breaking.
- **Character Law:** Not a strawman. Genuinely gifted — high capacity, real technique, real charm, casual generosity to his own tier; children follow him for reasons. His cruelty is doctrine, not sadism — which is worse. His mass, leadership, and social gravity are real powers the story honors (rule in 06): command that moves six backs as one is a purse of its own kind.
- **Abilities:** FIRE proficiency, high capacity (declared at the dish — expected, cheered).
- **Appearance:** Tall for his cohort, built a year ahead of his age; dark-gold hair; high carriage; unspent strength Kael prices on sight. Beautiful and knows the exact weight of it — the bench behaves differently in his radius (render the radius, not the adjective). Fire's warmth in the coloring without stating it.

### DYNAMIC
- **In the Temple's Gold six**, and taking bruises off a seventeen-year-old spear four days a week on purpose, cheerfully, expecting to be pulled apart at the highest level available. **His brother has written to him about *the eastern roads*** — and has never once written to him about a road, and he does not notice that he has noticed.
- **Now:** ~12–13; entered, and intending to win. The cistern court is his training ground and he pays for the *why* at full rate — Kael takes his structure apart an inch at a time and he got worse for three weeks before better, and did not stop. "Longer hours": he goes and gets bruises off older boys on purpose.
- **State:** beaten by Valeria in six exchanges and never in one of them; paid the invoice nod and the costliest line of his arc (*"She is better than my brother."*) and recruited the loss into the work. Stands up when she enters a room and cannot sit back down — **deference as doctrine, the same machine as the old cruelty**, and his tier would not recognise him. Lets Kael use his capacity as a magnifying glass. **Houses standing for people is his native arithmetic**, so he understood *"I don't know who it was"* faster than Kael did, and went quiet.
- **The shrug:** asked what happens to the people of a conquered city he explained it without one grain of contempt — you take the top off and put a new top on — and shrugged about the old top. Not cruelty; the world as it was described to him.
- **Knows:** something of his house's campaign — how much is open. **Does not know:** the mountain; Halden's identity.

### FORWARD
- **Arc direction (not sealed):** the unlikely friend — the doctrine cracked, not dissolved; comes to regret the bullying years. Pride intact throughout (Character Law).
