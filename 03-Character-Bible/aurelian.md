## AURELIAN CORVANE (the ringleader — pride that breaks)
**Glance:** the cohort's presumptive best; ringleader → training ally · House Corvane · ~13 · **alive and aboard, with a duty that arrived and a message for a brother who is still at court.**

### STATIC
- **Role:** The cohort's best before Kael arrives; capacity-worship made flesh; the face of the science-vs-pride engine. Sealed irony (00): his mockery of Kael's method is the empire failing to recognize its most wanted asset.
- **Want:** To be what his name promises.
- **Wound:** THE SPARE — an elder brother already legendary at court; the Temple is the one place Aurelian gets to be first, which is precisely what Kael takes from him.
- **Limit:** Cannot conceive of merit outside birthright — out-fought he could forgive; out-thought by a charity case reads as the order of the world breaking.
- **Character Law:** Not a strawman. Genuinely gifted — high capacity, real technique, real charm, casual generosity to his own tier; children follow him for reasons. His cruelty is doctrine, not sadism — which is worse. His mass, leadership, and social gravity are real powers the story honors (rule in 06): command that moves six backs as one is a purse of its own kind.
- **Abilities:** FIRE proficiency, high capacity (declared at the dish — expected, cheered).
- **Appearance:** Tall for his cohort, built a year ahead of his age; dark-gold hair; **amber, gold-brown eyes — author ruling s34, unspent on any page**; high carriage; unspent strength Kael prices on sight. Beautiful and knows the exact weight of it — the bench behaves differently in his radius (render the radius, not the adjective). Fire's warmth in the coloring without stating it — it sits in the hair and the eyes together, and is never named as fire.

### DYNAMIC
- **Aboard, with a duty that arrived and a message for a brother alive at court.** He named the duty and made it everyone's: the princess reaches the capital, *and that is not a duty anybody here was given. It is one that arrived.*
- **His house is safe and it is not the smallest comfort to him.** Twenty-nine carry the name; two were on that island; only his father stayed on the wall. His father's goodbye gave him the thing he has wanted his whole life, too late — a message to carry to the brother, and then *"I have two sons. I have never once had a favourite, and I have let a great many people believe otherwise, because it was useful. It was useful to me. It was not useful to you… That was badly done, and I am saying so now, because there is not going to be a later."* **Aurelian, who answers everything, did not answer that, and does not raise it.** He carries the message and has not said so.
- **He could not find the words for Kael and said so** — *there is not one, so I am saying it badly and you will have to take it as it comes* — and then put his shoulder against Kael's shoulder and shut up, **which for him is an enormous undertaking.**
- **He is the one who keeps putting his body between other people and the thing**, and nobody has remarked on it: a sweep pulled one-handed out of a rock cut; both hands over the back of a princess's head for the length of a barrage, talking to the wood; the princess got up off stone one-handed before anybody else had turned round and carried down the last shelf on his good side; Kael's mother carried off the mountain on his bad side's good arm, with Elarine on the other.
- **He spent every grain he had in one wall, off one good hand, and it was genuinely enormous** — and Fenna drew a line across it and stepped through the middle of it without breaking stride. **The capacity he was raised to worship, on the page, against real war.** He said out loud what the yard had watched — *He has been telling us for four years that everything is measurable and we have all been extremely funny about it* — and got no laugh and did not want one.
- **He said Lucan's name twice and then could not say anything at all**, and left his good hand flat on the boy's chest, *which did nothing for anybody*.
- **He talks the entire way down a mountain** — steadily, in order, correct in every particular, for nobody — and when told to be quieter is quieter and does not stop.
- **The arm** (Meloran, the round of 16) is not all right; he pretends about it, and Valeria made him sit down once.
- **He gave Kael's exclusion at the gate as flat arithmetic and did not spare himself** — *"a house stands for its own. Yes. Of course."* Houses standing for people is his native arithmetic, so he understood the whole of that gate instantly and there was nothing on earth he could do with it. **He never had a chance to act; never write the morning as though he did.**
- **Deference as doctrine, the same machine as the old cruelty:** he stands up when Valeria enters a room and cannot sit back down, paid *"She is better than my brother."* and recruited the loss into the work; his tier would not recognise him. Calls her *Val* because Kael did.
- **The shrug stands:** asked what happens to the people of a conquered city he explained it without one grain of contempt — you take the top off and put a new top on — and shrugged about the old top. Not cruelty; the world as it was described to him.
- **Knows:** something of his house's campaign; that his brother wrote to him about *the eastern roads* and had never once written about a road — and he does not notice that he has noticed. **Does not know:** the mountain; Halden's identity, or that Halden is his house's man.

### FORWARD
- **Arc direction (not sealed):** the unlikely friend — the doctrine cracked, not dissolved; comes to regret the bullying years. Pride intact throughout (Character Law).
